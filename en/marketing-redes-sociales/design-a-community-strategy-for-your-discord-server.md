Context: I'm [creator or brand] and I run a Discord server about [community topic] with about [approximate member count] members. The main problem right now is [current problem] (low participation, scattered conversations, hard-to-manage moderation, etc.).
Goal: I want a community where members talk to each other, not just to me, so the server stays active even when I'm not posting.
Deliverable: 1) A simplified channel structure with the purpose of each channel. 2) A weekly calendar of 4 or 5 recurring rituals (challenges, Q&A sessions, member showcases, etc.) with instructions for running them. 3) A welcome message and a clear first step for new members. 4) Community rules and a moderation protocol with escalating responses.
Criteria: Each ritual should run on little of my time and lean on active members as moderators or hosts. Keep the tone warm and consistent with the community's voice.
Format: Sections with headings; channels and rituals in tables, rules as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
