Context: I'm launching [your product] for [target audience]. I have [budget and resources], and the available channels are [channels] (social, email, ads, physical store, etc.). The launch date is [launch date].
Goal: A memorable campaign that builds buzz before launch and turns that interest into sales or sign-ups.
Deliverable: 1) Three distinct creative concepts, each with a core idea, a campaign line, and why it would resonate with this audience. 2) Recommend one and build it out in three phases (teaser, launch, and sustain) with duration, key message, each channel's role, and the assets needed. 3) Success metrics for each phase and how to measure them.
Criteria: Each channel should play a distinct role (attract, persuade, or convert), and all of them should lead to the same next step. Scale the ambition to my budget: I'd rather have one idea executed well than lots of scattered tactics. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Concepts as a numbered list; the plan as a table with the columns Phase, Dates, Message, Channels and assets, and Metric.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
