Context: I'm going to push [your product or service] on [platform] for 4 weeks, starting [start date]. My marketing goal is [marketing goal] and I can post [posts per week] times a week.
Goal: A calendar that puts the platform's features to work for the goal and moves the audience from discovering the product to buying or trying it.
Deliverable: A 4-week calendar with one stage per week (anticipation, introduction, social proof, and decision). For each post: day, format or platform feature (short video, carousel, story, livestream, product collection, etc.), topic, hook, call to action, and main metric.
Criteria: If you have web search, check which features are currently available on the platform. Mix promotional pieces with value pieces so the audience doesn't burn out: at most a third of the posts should make a direct ask. Social proof must be based on real customers and results.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Day, Format, Topic, Hook, Call to action, and Metric.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
