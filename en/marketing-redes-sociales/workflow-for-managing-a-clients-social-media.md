Context: I'm a social media consultant and I'll be managing the accounts of a client in the [client's industry] industry on [platforms you'll manage]. On the client side I'll be working with [client contacts and decision-makers].
Goal: I want a clear workflow that avoids approval delays, keeps messaging consistent across platforms, and lets me prove results every month.
Deliverable: 1) An onboarding process for the first 2 weeks: access, initial audit, goals, and brand guidelines. 2) The monthly work cycle: planning, production, approval, publishing, community management, and reporting, with an owner and deadline for each step. 3) A tracking dashboard that unifies data from every platform with 6-8 comparable metrics. 4) Rules for escalating sensitive comments or crisis situations to the client.
Criteria: Designed to run several platforms without duplicating work: say what gets adapted per platform and what gets reused. Access should be granted through platform roles or management tools, never by sharing passwords, to protect both the client and you. Metrics should be comparable across platforms even when each one names them differently.
Format: Numbered sections; the monthly cycle as a table with the columns Step, Owner, Deadline, and Suggested tool. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
