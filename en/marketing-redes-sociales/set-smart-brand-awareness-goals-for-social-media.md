Context: I manage social media for [your business] on [platforms]. Where I stand today: [current metrics] (followers, monthly reach, mentions, branded searches), and I have [available resources] (budget, time, team).
Goal: I want to grow brand awareness within [timeframe] with targets I can measure and defend to my team or clients.
Deliverable: 1) Two or three fully written SMART goals. 2) For each goal: the main metric, how to measure it, the baseline, and a midpoint target. 3) Three to 5 tactics that feed directly into each goal.
Criteria: Targets should be ambitious but achievable with my resources; explain in one line why each number is reasonable. Favor awareness metrics (reach among non-followers, mentions, branded searches) over total followers. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Each goal in its own section with a table of Metric, Baseline, Midpoint target, and Final target, followed by the tactics as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
