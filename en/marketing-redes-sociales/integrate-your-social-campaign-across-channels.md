Context: I'm planning a campaign for [your product or service] that will run on [main platform] and on these other channels: [other campaign channels]. My customer segments are [customer segments] and the data and automation tools I have are [available tools].
Goal: Give customers a consistent experience no matter where they come in, with messages tailored by segment and without repeating the same thing on every channel.
Deliverable: 1) The campaign's core message and how it adapts on each channel. 2) A journey per segment: what they see first, what comes next, and what leads them to convert. 3) Sequencing and frequency rules across channels (for example, stop showing ads to people who already bought). 4) How to connect data across channels with my tools and what to measure to evaluate the campaign as a whole.
Criteria: Fit the plan to the tools I already have; if something needs an additional tool, say so and explain why. Respect customer consent and data privacy.
Format: Numbered sections; the journey as a table with the columns Segment, Entry point, Next touchpoint, Conversion, and Channel. 800 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
