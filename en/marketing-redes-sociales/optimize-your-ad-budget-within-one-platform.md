Context: I spend [monthly budget in your currency] a month on [platform] ads for [your business]. My main goal is [campaign goal], and here are my current results by campaign: [current results] (objective, targeting, format, spend, results, and cost per result).
Goal: I want more results from the same budget by moving money from what isn't working to what is.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of my current campaigns: what to scale, adjust, or pause, with the reason. 2) A recommended budget split across campaign objectives, targeting (cold, lookalike, and retargeting audiences), and ad formats. 3) A weekly checklist to prevent wasted spend: high frequency, overlapping audiences, ad fatigue, and low-performing placements.
Criteria: Base your recommendations on my data and tell me when volume is too low to draw conclusions. If you have web search, check the objectives and formats the platform offers today, since they change often.
Format: The diagnosis in a table with the columns Campaign, Cost per result, Verdict, and Action; the split as percentages; the checklist with checkboxes.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
