Context: I have a YouTube channel about [channel topic]. Here are one video's metrics: [video metrics] (impressions, thumbnail CTR, average view duration, retention percentage, traffic sources, subscribers gained) and the average of my last 10 videos: [channel average].
Goal: I want to understand why this video did better or worse than usual and what to change in the next one.
Deliverable: 1) A stage-by-stage diagnosis: whether it stood out or fell short at the click (thumbnail and title), in the first 30 seconds, or in retention across the video. 2) A comparison of each metric against the channel average, with a one-line interpretation. 3) Five concrete changes for the next video, ordered by likely impact.
Criteria: Compare against my own average first and general benchmarks second, because every niche and format behaves differently. If you use external benchmarks, flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Metric, This video, Channel average, and Interpretation; then the diagnosis and changes as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
