Context: My business is [your business], in the [industry] sector (for example, travel and tourism), and I post on [platform]. My audience is mainly in [country or region].
Goal: Get ahead of what my audience searches for at each time of year, so I publish the right content before interest peaks.
Deliverable: 1) A 12-month calendar with the seasons, dates, and events that matter for my industry in that region. 2) For each season, 5-8 keywords and search phrases written the way my audience types them, with their intent (inspiration, comparison, or purchase). 3) How many weeks before the peak to start posting on that platform. 4) One post idea per season using the main keywords. 5) Evergreen terms people search for year-round.
Criteria: Use the phrases people actually type, including regional variations. If you have web search, validate the keywords with search trend data and the platform's own search bar. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Month or season, Event, Keywords, Intent, Post from, and Idea.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
