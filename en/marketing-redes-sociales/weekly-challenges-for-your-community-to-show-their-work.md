Context: I run a community about [your community's topic] on [platform].
Goal: Get members to participate by showing what they make or know how to do, so the community fills up with their contributions and not just my posts.
Deliverable: A calendar of 8 weekly challenges (for example, "Show us this week's project" or "Share your best tip"). For each challenge: name, a 2-3 sentence prompt, a sample response, a suggested hashtag or tag, and how I'll recognize the best contributions.
Criteria: Challenges with different effort levels so newcomers and shy members join in too. Recognition should give visibility and say thanks, asking permission before reposting anyone's content. Warm, inclusive tone.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Challenge, Prompt, Example, Tag, and Recognition. At the end, 3 short community guidelines for the challenges.
