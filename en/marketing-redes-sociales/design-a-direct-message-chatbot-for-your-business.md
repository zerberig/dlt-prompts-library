Context: I run [your business] (for example, a real estate agency, travel agency, or financial services firm) and get a lot of inquiries through [messaging channel]. These are the most frequent questions and their answers: [frequently asked questions and answers]. To qualify a lead I need to know: [lead qualification details].
Goal: I want to set up an AI assistant that answers instantly, filters leads with real interest, and hands off to a person on the team when needed.
Deliverable: 1) Complete system instructions for the assistant: purpose, tone, what it can and can't answer, and how it introduces itself. 2) The conversation flow from greeting to close, with qualification questions in a natural order. 3) Handoff rules for passing to a person (complaints, legal or special-pricing topics, user frustration). 4) Ten test conversations with tricky cases to validate the assistant before turning it on.
Criteria: The assistant must identify itself as an automated assistant and never pretend to be a person. It must not make up prices, availability, or terms: if it doesn't have the information, it offers to connect with the team. It should ask only for the personal data it needs and explain what it will be used for. If I serve several languages, explain how to handle language switches.
Format: The system instructions in a copy-ready block; the flow as a numbered list; the rules and tests in tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
