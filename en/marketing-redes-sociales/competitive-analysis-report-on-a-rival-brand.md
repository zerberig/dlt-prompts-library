Context: I run [your business], which competes with [competitor name] in [market or country]. What I already know about them: [what you already know about the competitor].
Goal: I want a report that helps me decide how to differentiate and which opportunities to pursue, not just a description of the competitor.
Deliverable: A report with these sections: 1) Company overview: stated mission, approximate size, and market position. 2) Product: core features, pricing, and user experience. 3) Marketing: key messages, channels, content, and visible advertising. 4) Social media: platforms, frequency, best-performing formats, and the tone of their community. 5) A SWOT analysis from my perspective. 6) Five actionable takeaways for my business, in priority order.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it and cite the source of every important data point; if not, work with what I gave you and note what still needs research. Distinguish verified facts, observations, and inferences. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Avoid judgments without evidence.
Format: A report with a heading per section; the SWOT as a 2x2 table; the takeaways as a numbered list. 800 to 1,200 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
