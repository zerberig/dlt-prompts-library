Context: Here's the data for my posts from the last [period analyzed] on [platform]: [exported post data] (date, format, topic, reach, engagements, saves, shares, clicks, and conversions if I have them).
Goal: I want to know which content performs best and, above all, why, so I can make more of what gets results.
Deliverable: 1) The top 5 posts by rate (engagements over reach, saves and shares over reach), not by raw numbers, because raw numbers favor whatever was posted when the account had more reach. 2) The patterns the top 5 share compared with the bottom 5: format, topic, hook, length, and posting time. 3) 3 hypotheses for why they work and how to test each one next month. 4) 5 new ideas that apply those patterns.
Criteria: Base your conclusions only on the data I gave you; if the sample is too small to conclude something, say so.
Format: A table for the top 5 and lists for the rest. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
