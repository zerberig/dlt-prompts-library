Context: I'm going to advertise [your product or service] on Facebook and Instagram. My ideal customer is [description of your ideal customer], and I already have this first-party data: [data you already have] (customer list, site visitors, followers, engagers).
Goal: I want audiences that reach people who are genuinely likely to buy, instead of spending the budget on an overly broad crowd.
Deliverable: 1) Three segments: a cold audience defined by demographics, interests, and behaviors; a lookalike audience built from my first-party data; and a retargeting audience. 2) For each segment: setup criteria, estimated size, the core message, and the ad type that works best for it. 3) How to keep the segments from overlapping and competing with each other.
Criteria: If you have web search, check which detailed targeting options are still available, since Meta has removed several in recent years. Leave out any targeting based on health, religion, sexual orientation, or other sensitive data. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Segment, Criteria, Estimated size, Message, and Ad format; then the overlap section as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
