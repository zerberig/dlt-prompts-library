Context: I'll be taking part as [host or guest] in a public conversation on X about [topic], using the hashtag [conversation hashtag] and lasting [length].
Goal: I want the conversation to get real participation, give attendees value, and help me connect with people in my industry.
Deliverable: If I'm hosting: 1) Ten questions in order, from an icebreaker to a closing question, each ready to post with the hashtag. 2) A timed run of show. 3) Three posts to promote it beforehand. 4) A follow-up plan (recap, thank-yous, and spin-off content). If I'm a guest: prepared answers of one or two posts for the likely questions, examples of my own I can bring, and how to engage with other participants.
Criteria: Every post should fit X's character limit and make sense without context. Questions should invite people to share experiences, not answer yes or no.
Format: Sections with headings; questions as a numbered list with the hashtag included.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
