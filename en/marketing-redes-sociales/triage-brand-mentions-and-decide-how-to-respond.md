Context: These are recent mentions of [your brand] on [platform], exported from my tool or copied by hand: [mentions]. They include posts that tag us and others that name us without tagging.
Goal: Know within minutes what needs a reply, what's an opportunity, and what could escalate, so no one important goes unanswered.
Deliverable: 1) Each mention classified by type: question, complaint, praise, comparison with a competitor, partnership opportunity, or reputation risk. 2) Urgency (high, medium, or low) with the reason. 3) Suggested owner (customer care, marketing, or leadership). 4) A short draft reply for high- and medium-urgency mentions. 5) A summary of the 3 most frequent patterns.
Criteria: Base each classification only on the mention's text; if the tone is ambiguous (sarcasm, for example), mark it as unclear instead of guessing. Replies should sound human, acknowledge the person's point, and move the conversation to private messages when personal data is involved.
Format: A table with the columns Mention (summary), Type, Urgency, Owner, and Suggested reply; then the summary as bullets.
