Context: I work at [your brand or organization] and I'm interested in the conversation about [topic] on [platform]. My purpose is [why you need it] (PR, partnerships, countering misinformation, understanding the industry).
Goal: I want to know who actually drives this conversation, not just who has the most followers, so I can decide whom to engage with and how.
Deliverable: 1) A map of players in five groups: experts and specialists, content creators, media and journalists, communities or groups, and critical voices. 2) If you have web search, 3 to 5 examples per group with their handle, stance, and why they're influential; if not, skip the names and describe each group's profile. 3) Signals that separate real influence from popularity (interaction quality, whether others cite them, consistency on the topic). 4) A recommended way to approach each group.
Criteria: Don't make up profiles, accounts, or figures; flag anything you can't verify as an estimate. Include critical voices, because ignoring them leaves blind spots.
Format: A table with the columns Player, Group, Stance, Why they're influential, and How to approach; the rest as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
