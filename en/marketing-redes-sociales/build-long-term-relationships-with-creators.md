Context: My brand, [your brand], has already worked with some creators on [platform] and I want to turn the best collaborations into long-term relationships. Here are the creators and how it went: [creators and past results].
Goal: Build an ongoing relationship program that benefits both sides and where the content feels more authentic over time.
Deliverable: 1) Criteria for choosing who's worth a long-term relationship. 2) A program structure (for example, a 6-12 month ambassador program) with benefits for creators beyond payment: early access, product co-creation, and public recognition. 3) The points the agreement should cover: exclusivity, deliverables, usage rights, payments, ad disclosure, and termination. 4) A follow-up rhythm with feedback, check-ins, and recognition. 5) A message proposing the long-term relationship.
Criteria: Treat creators as partners, not ad slots. Present the agreement points as a topic guide and recommend having a lawyer review it before signing.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
