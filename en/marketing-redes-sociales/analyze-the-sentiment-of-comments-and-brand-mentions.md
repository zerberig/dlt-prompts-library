Context: Here are [comments or mentions] collected from [platform] about [your brand, product, or topic] during [time period].
Goal: I want to understand how people perceive my brand and what's driving positive and negative reactions, so I can decide what to address first.
Deliverable: 1) Each comment classified as positive, negative, neutral, or mixed, with its main topic. 2) The 3 to 5 most recurring topics, with their dominant tone and a verbatim example. 3) A 4-5 sentence summary of overall sentiment. 4) Three recommended actions, ordered by urgency.
Criteria: Catch sarcasm and irony, which are easy to mistake for praise. Base your conclusions only on the comments I gave you, and tell me when the sample is too small to generalize. Separate complaints about the product from complaints about the service, because different teams fix them.
Format: A table with the columns Comment (summarized), Sentiment, and Topic; then the topics, summary, and actions in sections with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
