Context: We're a team of [number of people and roles] managing social media for [your brand or organization] on [platforms you manage]. Right now we work without a clear process and posts, replies, and reports slip through the cracks.
Goal: A weekly management routine covering planning, creation, publishing, community, and analytics, with clear roles and no unnecessary meetings.
Deliverable: 1) The routine day by day: what gets done and who does it. 2) A responsibility matrix (who does it, who approves, who's consulted, and who's informed) for the main tasks. 3) Target response times for comments and messages. 4) A half-page weekly report template. 5) An access and account security checklist: roles, two-step verification, and what to do when someone leaves the team.
Criteria: Scale the workload to the team's real size. Prioritize tools we already have or that are free.
Format: The routine as a table with the columns Day, Task, Owner, and Estimated time; everything else as lists. 650 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
