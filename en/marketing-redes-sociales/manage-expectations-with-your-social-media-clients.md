Context: I'm [freelance consultant or agency] and I'm about to start working with [client type] on a [contracted services] project (strategy, account management, ads, etc.). The client expects [client expectations].
Goal: I want to align expectations from day one to avoid misunderstandings about results, timelines, and scope, which are the most common source of conflict in these projects.
Deliverable: 1) A guide for the kickoff meeting, with the questions I should ask. 2) A one-page scope document: what's included, what's not, deliverables, response times, and number of revisions. 3) How to explain what results are realistic at 30, 90, and 180 days without promising numbers. 4) The structure of the monthly reports. 5) Model replies for tough situations: out-of-scope requests, last-minute changes, and complaints about results.
Criteria: Professional, firm, and empathetic tone. If the client's expectations aren't realistic, help me explain that with reasons and an alternative.
Format: Numbered sections; the scope document in a copy-ready block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
