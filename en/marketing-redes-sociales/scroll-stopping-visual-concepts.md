Context: I want to communicate [post message or topic] to [target audience] on [platform]. My visual identity is [your brand's visual style] (colors, typefaces, photo mood).
Goal: I want visuals that make people stop scrolling and get the message in seconds, to lift engagement without resorting to misleading tricks.
Deliverable: Five distinct visual concepts (for example, before-and-after contrast, a big on-screen stat, an unexpected object, a human close-up, an explanatory illustration). For each: recommended format (image, carousel, or short video), a description of the composition, on-screen text of 6 words max, what appears in the first second, and a short prompt to generate it with an image model.
Criteria: Every concept should be producible with simple resources and stay true to my visual identity. On-screen text has to be readable on a phone. The visual promise must be delivered in the content, because clickbait hurts retention.
Format: A numbered list of concepts with subheadings for each element.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
