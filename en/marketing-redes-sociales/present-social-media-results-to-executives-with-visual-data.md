Context: I have to present the social media results for [your company or team] for [time period] to [presentation audience] (leadership, clients, board). This is the data: [your social media data].
Goal: I want them to understand in a few minutes what happened, why it matters for the business, and what decisions I need from them.
Deliverable: 1) Three to five key findings written as conclusions, not loose metrics (for example, "short videos drive twice as many inquiries as images"). 2) The slide-by-slide structure of the presentation, with each slide's main message. 3) For each finding, the most suitable chart (bar, line, funnel, comparison) and the data it uses. 4) The decisions or resources I'll ask for, with their rationale. 5) Three tough questions they might ask and how to answer them.
Criteria: Connect every metric to a business outcome. Use clear language with no social media jargon. One idea per slide. Base everything on the data I gave you and point out where data is missing.
Format: The structure in a table with the columns Slide, Main message, Chart, and Data; the rest in short sections. 10 slides max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
