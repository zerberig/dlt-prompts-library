Context: I just finished the campaign [campaign name and goal] on [platforms]. These were the targets: [campaign targets]. These are the results: [campaign data] (spend, reach, impressions, engagement, clicks, click-through rate, conversions, cost per result, revenue).
Goal: I want a report that clearly explains whether the campaign worked, what we learned, and what to change next time.
Deliverable: 1) A 4-to-5-sentence executive summary. 2) A results-vs-targets table with the percentage achieved. 3) Key calculations: click-through rate, conversion rate, cost per result, and return on ad spend. 4) A comparison across platforms, ads, or audiences, if there's data. 5) Three wins and three misses, with their likely cause. 6) Five recommendations for the next campaign, ranked by impact.
Criteria: Show the formulas. Distinguish causes the data proves from hypotheses. Don't dress up weak results: explain them and suggest how to improve.
Format: Sections with headings; results and comparisons in tables; recommendations as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
