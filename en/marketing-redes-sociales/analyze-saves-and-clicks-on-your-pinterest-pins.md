Context: I use Pinterest to drive traffic to [your site or shop]. Here are my exported pin metrics for the last [time period]: [your pin data] (pin title, board, topic, impressions, saves, outbound clicks, and date).
Goal: I want to know which pins work for inspiration (saves) and which drive visits (clicks), so I can make more of what moves me toward sales.
Deliverable: 1) The save rate and click-through rate for each pin, with the formula used. 2) The top 5 by saves and the top 5 by clicks, and whether they overlap. 3) Patterns in the best pins: topic, image style, text overlay, board, and season. 4) A comparison with my metrics on [other platform] to understand Pinterest's role in my strategy. 5) Five new pin ideas based on the patterns.
Criteria: Keep in mind that Pinterest calls "saves" what used to be known as repins. Separate new pins from pins that have been accumulating results for a long time, since Pinterest content keeps performing for months. Don't assume missing values.
Format: A table with the columns Pin, Impressions, Save rate, Click-through rate, and Note; then sections with bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
