Context: I run [your local business] (for example, a bakery) in [neighborhood or city] and post on [platform]. This month I want to highlight [product or offer]. My regular customers are [type of customers].
Goal: Get people in the area craving it, commenting, and coming into the shop this week.
Deliverable: Seven posts, one per day, each with a different angle: the process behind the counter, the star product, a regular customer or neighbor (with their permission), a question that invites comments and friend tags, a fun fact about the product, an offer with a real deadline, and a thank-you to the community. For each: a photo or video idea, the caption, the call to action, and the best time of day to post based on my customers' routine.
Criteria: A warm tone, the way someone from the shop would say it. Include neighborhood references so it feels local, plus practical details (hours, location, delivery) when they help people decide to visit. Offers must be real, with clear terms.
Format: A numbered list by day, with the visual idea, caption, and call to action listed separately.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
