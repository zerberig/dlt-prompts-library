Context: I create content about [topic] on [platform] for [target audience]. My content pillars are [your content pillars].
Goal: I want to know what my audience searches for on the platform about my topic, so I can plan content people are already looking for.
Deliverable: 1) Twenty to thirty keywords and search phrases related to my topic, grouped by intent: learn, compare, solve a problem, or buy. 2) For each one: relative volume (high, medium, or low), competition level, and the pillar it belongs to. 3) The 10 with the best opportunity, each with a post idea. 4) How to check volume using the platform's own tools (search autocomplete, suggestions, trend dashboards) and external tools.
Criteria: Use my audience's real language, including regional variations if relevant. If you have web search, use it to identify current terms. Don't present search volumes as exact data: flag them as estimates unless a source backs them up.
Format: A table with the columns Keyword, Intent, Relative volume, Competition, and Pillar; the 10 opportunities as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
