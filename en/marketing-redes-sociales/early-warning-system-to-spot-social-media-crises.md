Context: I work at [your brand or organization] in the [industry] sector, and we're active on [platforms]. The topics that could cause us trouble are [known topics or risks].
Goal: I want to catch the signs of a possible crisis early, so we act while it's still an isolated comment and not a trend.
Deliverable: 1) A list of terms to monitor: brand, products, executives, common misspellings, and combinations with risk words. 2) Warning signals with concrete thresholds (for example, a sudden spike in negative mentions, a high-reach account criticizing us, media coverage). 3) A traffic-light system (green, yellow, red) with what to do and whom to notify at each level. 4) A daily and weekly monitoring routine, with who does it and how long it takes. 5) What kind of listening tool fits my size.
Criteria: Thresholds should be relative to my normal mention volume, not absolute numbers. Include risks that are often missed, such as screenshots of private messages or complaints in closed communities. If you have web search, verify any tools you suggest and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: Terms and signals in tables; the traffic-light system in a table with the columns Level, Signal, Action, and Owner.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
