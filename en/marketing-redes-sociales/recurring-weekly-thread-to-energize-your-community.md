Design a recurring weekly ritual for my [topic or niche] community on [platform], such as a thread or fixed post people look forward to each week. Give it a memorable name, set the day and format, and write the questions or prompts for 8 weeks, rotating between personal experiences, recommendations, opinions, and wins. Each prompt should take about a minute to answer and get members replying to each other, not just to the brand. Add how to highlight the best contributions and a template for the weekly post. Present the 8 weeks in a table.
