Context: Here are my post metrics on [platform] for [time period]: [your post data] (date, topic, format, reach, interactions, saves, shares, clicks). My main goal right now is [current goal] (reach, community, traffic, or sales).
Goal: I want to get more out of what already worked, instead of starting from scratch every week.
Deliverable: 1) The metric that best reflects my goal and why. 2) The top 5 posts by that metric, with an explanation of what made them work. 3) For each one, two ways to repurpose it: a different format (for example, carousel to short video), a different platform, or a follow-up on the topic. 4) A 2-week calendar with the repurposed pieces.
Criteria: Base everything on the data I gave you and don't assume missing values. Each repurposed piece should bring a new angle, not repeat the same text. Adapt the hook to the new format.
Format: The top 5 in a table with the columns Post, Key metric, Why it worked, and Repurposing ideas; the calendar in a separate table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
