Context: I publish content about [your niche] on [platform] for [target audience].
Goal: I want to understand why people share content in my niche and use that to create pieces that get sent in DMs, saved, and shared, not just liked.
Deliverable: 1) The 5 main reasons my audience would share something (usefulness, identity, emotion, conversation, helping someone), explained with examples from my niche. 2) 10 concrete content ideas, each tied to one motivation and in the best-suited format for [platform]. 3) A 6-question checklist to review any post before it goes live.
Criteria: Ideas specific to my niche and doable with limited resources. Prioritize what gives value to the person sharing, because people share what makes them look good or lets them help others.
Format: The motivations as a list; the ideas in a table with the columns Idea, Motivation, Format, and Opening hook; the checklist at the end. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
