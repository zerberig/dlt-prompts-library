Context: I run organic content and ads on Facebook for [your business]. My conversion goal is [target conversion] and this is my data from the last 3 months: [post and ad data].
Goal: Improve reach and conversions with data-based decisions, not hunches.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of the data: which content drives reach, which drives conversions, and where people drop off. 2) 5 hypotheses prioritized by impact and ease. 3) An A/B testing plan for the top 3: the variable being changed, control and test versions, minimum duration or sample size, and success metric. 4) How to document results so learning builds from one test to the next.
Criteria: One variable per test so the result can be attributed. If the data isn't enough for a reliable conclusion, say so and suggest how to get more. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with my data or a source.
Format: Numbered sections; the testing plan as a table. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
