Context: I work at [type of organization and department] and we want to improve how we onboard new people using our internal network or channel: [internal communication tool].
Goal: Help new hires get to know the culture, their coworkers, and what they need to do their job from week one, with short content that also works as reference material.
Deliverable: 1) A 4-week welcome series: each week's theme, the format of each piece (short video, post, poll, live session, coworker introductions), and who produces it. 2) 3 ideas for the current team to welcome people in a participatory way. 3) How to turn the pieces into an easy-to-search training library. 4) Indicators to evaluate whether the program works.
Criteria: Accessible content: captions on every video, alt text on images, plain language, and options for shift workers or people without a computer. Respect privacy and share personal details only with consent.
Format: The series as a table with the columns Week, Theme, Piece, Format, and Owner; everything else as lists. 650 words max.
