Context: My brand is [your brand], and I speak to [target audience] on Instagram in a [brand tone] tone. I want to use this trending meme or format: [meme or trend].
Goal: A post that feels authentic and timely, one people share to their stories or send to friends.
Deliverable: 1) A quick filter: what the meme is about, where it comes from, whether it fits the brand, and what risks it carries (offensive meaning, controversial origin, image rights). 2) Three adaptations with different angles (a customer's everyday situation, behind the scenes with the team, an exaggeration of the problem we solve), each with a description of the image or video and the overlay text. 3) A caption of 2 lines max and a question for the comments. 4) How long you think it will stay relevant.
Criteria: The humor laughs at the situation, never at people or groups. If the meme uses a copyrighted image or audio, suggest recreating the format with our own material. If you don't know the meme or aren't sure what it means, look it up on the web or tell me before proposing anything.
Format: The filter as bullets; the adaptations as a numbered list.
