Context: I run [your brand or profile] on [platform]. My audience is [target audience] and the brand's tone is [your brand tone].
Goal: I want to join current trends that genuinely relate to my brand, to gain reach without looking forced or opportunistic.
Deliverable: 1) If you have web search, 5 to 8 active trends on the platform (topics, formats, sounds, or memes) with a short description and roughly when they started; if not, explain how I can find them and continue with the ones I give you. 2) An assessment of each trend: brand fit, expected lifespan, and reputational risk. 3) For the top 3: a concrete post idea with an opening hook, format, and how to tie it to my topic.
Criteria: Rule out trends tied to tragedies, conflicts, or sensitive issues. Keep in mind trends expire within days, so prioritize ideas that can be produced quickly. Don't present it as fact that a trend will make a post go viral. Flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, Description, Fit, Expected lifespan, and Risk; then the 3 ideas in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
