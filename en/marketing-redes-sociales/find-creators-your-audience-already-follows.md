Context: I want to promote [your product or service] on [platform] with content creators. My current customers are [description of your customers], and I have [partnership budget].
Goal: Find creators my audience already trusts, instead of choosing by follower count.
Deliverable: 1) Five ways to find them starting from my audience: questions for a customer survey, checking who my most active followers follow and engage with, topic and keyword searches, comments on reference accounts in the niche, and organic mentions of my brand. 2) A scorecard with 6 weighted criteria: audience fit, quality of the conversation in their comments, values alignment, consistency, partnership history, and transparency in disclosing partnerships. 3) The columns for a tracking sheet that takes me from the list to first contact.
Criteria: Prioritize the relationship with the audience over account size, and match the creator tier to the budget. Every partnership must be labeled as advertising under the platform's rules.
Format: Methods as a numbered list with steps; the scorecard as a table with the columns Criterion, Weight, and How to verify it.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
