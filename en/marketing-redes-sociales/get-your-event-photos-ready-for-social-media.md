Context: I organized [your event] and have about [number of photos] photos. I want to post them on [platforms] to [goal of the posts] (say thank you, build interest in the next edition, or give sponsors visibility).
Goal: Posts that capture the event's energy and drive engagement, instead of an album of near-identical photos.
Deliverable: 1) Criteria for choosing photos: key moments, faces and emotions, a diverse range of attendees, speakers, and sponsors. 2) A post plan (highlights carousel, one photo per speaker, before-and-after of the setup, a sponsor thank-you) with the suggested image order. 3) Recommended aspect ratios and crops per platform. 4) The caption for each post and who to tag. 5) Descriptive alt text for each type of image.
Criteria: If you have web search, check each platform's current dimensions. Before tagging people or posting close-ups, consider the consent of the people pictured, especially if minors appear. Alt text describes what's visible; it doesn't repeat the caption.
Format: Numbered sections; the plan as a table with the columns Post, Photos, Format, and Caption.
