Context: I run a Facebook group about [group topic] with [number of members] members. Here is the data I have from the group's insights: [group data] (new members, posts, comments, active members).
Goal: Find out whether the community is healthy or just growing in numbers, and what to do to get more members participating.
Deliverable: 1) Five to 7 health metrics with their formulas, such as share of active members, member posts versus admin-team posts, comments per post, time to first reply, and new-member retention. 2) The calculation using my data and a read on each metric (healthy, watch, or weak). 3) For each weak metric, 2 concrete actions, such as conversation prompts, weekly recurring posts, welcomes for new members, or recognition for contributors. 4) A monthly review routine.
Criteria: Calculate only with the data I gave you and flag what's missing. If you have web search, check which metrics Facebook group insights currently offer. Favor actions that put members at the center over ones that depend only on the admin.
Format: A table with the columns Metric, Formula, Result, and Read; then the actions as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
