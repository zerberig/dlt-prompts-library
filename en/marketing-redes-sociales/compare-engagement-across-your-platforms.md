Context: I post for [your brand or profile] on [platforms]. Here are each account's metrics for [time period]: [metrics by platform] (posts, followers, reach or views, likes or favorites, comments, shares, saves, and video plays).
Goal: I want to know which platform gets my content the most real engagement, so I can decide where to focus.
Deliverable: 1) An equivalence table between each platform's metrics, since they're not all named or counted the same way. 2) Per-post engagement rate for each platform, calculated with a common formula you explain. 3) A ranking of platforms with the reason for each position. 4) A comparison by format (video, image, carousel, text) within each platform. 5) A chart suggestion (for example, a scatter of reach vs. engagement by format) and the data to use.
Criteria: Show the formulas and the math. Give more weight to comments, shares, and saves than to likes, and explain the criterion. Point out when the gap between platforms is too small, or the sample too short, to conclude anything.
Format: Tables for equivalences, rates, and ranking; conclusions as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
