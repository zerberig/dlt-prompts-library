Write 3 versions of a humorous post for [platform] about a situation my audience, [target audience], runs into often when it comes to [your brand's topic]. The idea is for them to recognize themselves, laugh, and share their own experience in the comments. Each version should use a different device (exaggeration, a "nobody talks about..." list, and a short dialogue) and end with an easy-to-answer question. Keep the humor kind: laugh at the situation, not at people. 80 words max per version, plus an image or format suggestion for each.
