Context: I want to compare these [platform] accounts: [accounts to compare] (they can be my own accounts by country or product, or mine versus competitors'). Here is their data for [period]: [data for each account] (followers, reach, engagements, number of posts).
Goal: Find out which account actually performs best, accounting for size and posting pace, so I can decide where to put resources.
Deliverable: 1) Normalized metrics with their formulas: engagement rate by reach and by followers, engagements per post, and percentage growth. 2) A comparison table with the calculations. 3) Which account stands out on each metric and possible reasons (formats, frequency, timing, topics). 4) Caveats about what can't be compared with this data. 5) Three recommendations for allocating resources.
Criteria: Use the same period and the same definitions for every account. If data is missing for any account, say so explicitly instead of estimating it. If competitor accounts are included, note that only their public data is available and their true reach is unknown.
Format: Formulas as a list; the comparison as a table; recommendations numbered.
