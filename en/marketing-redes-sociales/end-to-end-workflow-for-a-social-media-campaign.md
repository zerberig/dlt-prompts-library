Context: I'm running a campaign on [platform] to [campaign goal], with a team made up of [team members] and a duration of [campaign length].
Goal: I want a workflow that makes sure everything ships on time, the message stays consistent in every piece, and at the end I can show what worked.
Deliverable: 1) The campaign phases (brief, production, review, launch, monitoring, and wrap-up) with tasks, owners, and dates relative to launch. 2) An approval checklist for each piece: message, brand, legal, accessibility, links, and UTMs. 3) A daily monitoring protocol during the campaign, with thresholds that signal when to adjust. 4) A template for the wrap-up meeting: what we learned and what to repeat.
Criteria: Realistic for the size of my team. Define success metrics before launch so the evaluation doesn't bend to fit the results.
Format: The phases in a table with the columns Phase, Tasks, Owner, and When; everything else as lists. 750 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
