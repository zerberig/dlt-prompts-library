Context: [your brand] went through this crisis on [platforms]: [crisis summary]. This is how we responded: [our response]. The conversation has died down, but [current situation] (recurring negative comments, lower engagement, customers asking questions).
Goal: Regain trust credibly and return to normal communication without looking like we're hiding what happened.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis: what damage remains and with which audiences. 2) A three-stage plan (stabilize, show the changes, rebuild), with the duration, actions, and key messages for each. 3) Criteria for when to resume promotional posts. 4) How to communicate the concrete changes we made, with a sample follow-up post. 5) How to respond to comments that bring the issue back up. 6) Recovery metrics (sentiment, engagement, mentions, and sales), using the pre-crisis baseline as the benchmark.
Criteria: Every message must be backed by real actions; if there are no changes to show yet, say so and propose those changes first. A humble, direct tone that neither dramatizes nor minimizes. If the crisis has legal implications, recommend having the messages reviewed by legal counsel.
Format: Numbered sections; the plan as a table with the columns Stage, Duration, Actions, Key message, and Signal to move on.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
