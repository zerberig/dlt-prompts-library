Context: I publish content about [your industry or niche] on [platform] and my main topics are [your content pillars].
Goal: Build a bank of evergreen keywords, searched all year round, to use consistently so my content shows up in the platform's search.
Deliverable: 30 keywords or phrases grouped by content pillar. For each: the search intent (learn, compare, buy, or get inspired), a sample post title, and where to use it (profile, caption, on-screen text, spoken aloud in the video, or alt text).
Criteria: Phrases the way a real person would type them into the search bar, in my audience's language and way of speaking. If you have web search, check that they're terms people actually search for and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: One table per pillar with the columns Keyword, Intent, Sample title, and Where to use it.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
