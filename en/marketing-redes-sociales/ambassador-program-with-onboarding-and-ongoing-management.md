Context: I want to create an ambassador program on [platform] for [your brand or community], focused on [topic]. Participants would be [ambassador profile] (loyal customers, micro-creators, or specialists), and I can offer them [available perks].
Goal: A long-term relationship with people who recommend the brand out of conviction, producing steady content and strengthening the community.
Deliverable: 1) Selection criteria and a short application form. 2) A welcome message that thanks them for joining and explains perks, expectations, disclosure rules, and how to reach us. 3) An onboarding kit: a condensed brand guide, content ideas, and what to avoid. 4) A quarterly activity calendar: monthly challenges, joint content, live meetups, and recognition. 5) Metrics to evaluate the program and each ambassador. 6) How to end the relationship respectfully when it isn't working.
Criteria: Ambassadors must disclose their relationship with the brand in every post, per the platform's rules and local law. Perks proportional to the effort asked. No mandatory scripts: let them speak in their own voice.
Format: Numbered sections; the welcome message in its own block and the calendar as a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
