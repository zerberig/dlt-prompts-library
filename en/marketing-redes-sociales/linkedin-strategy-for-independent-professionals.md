Context: I'm a [your profession and specialty] and I serve [type of clients]. On LinkedIn I currently have [your current LinkedIn situation], and I can spend [time available per week] a week on it.
Goal: I want my profile and content to position me as a go-to expert in my specialty, to attract relevant followers and work opportunities.
Deliverable: 1) Profile adjustments: headline, About section, and Featured. 2) Three to four content pillars that balance authority, approachability, and services, with the recommended mix. 3) Ten concrete post ideas with a suggested format (text, carousel, document, video). 4) A weekly posting and engagement routine that fits my time. 5) The metrics that show whether it's working.
Criteria: Respect my profession's advertising and ethics rules: no promises of results, no testimonials from confidential cases, and no client data. Prioritize explaining complex topics clearly, because that builds trust. If you have web search, check the rules of my profession's licensing body or regulator in my country and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections; the post ideas in a table with the columns Idea, Pillar, and Format.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
