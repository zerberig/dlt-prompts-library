Context: I spend [monthly budget] on [platform] ads for [your business]. These are my recent results: [your campaign results] (spend, reach, clicks, conversions, sales or revenue). My approximate margin per sale is [margin per sale].
Goal: I want more return from the same budget and to know where I'm wasting money.
Deliverable: 1) My current return calculated from my data (cost per result, return on ad spend, and break-even point based on my margin). 2) Diagnosis: the 3 most likely budget leaks. 3) Six to eight tactics ranked by expected impact and effort, such as consolidating campaigns, adjusting audiences, rotating creatives, improving the landing page, or strengthening retargeting. 4) A 4-week test plan with a single variable per test.
Criteria: Show the formulas so I can check the math. Prioritize what can be done without paid tools. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Calculations in a table; tactics in a table with the columns Tactic, Expected impact, Effort, and How to measure; the test plan as a week-by-week list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
