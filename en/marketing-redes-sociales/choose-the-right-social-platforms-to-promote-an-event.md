Context: I'm promoting [event name and type] to [target audience], with a budget of [budget in your currency] and [weeks available] weeks before the date.
Goal: I want to focus on the 2 or 3 platforms where my audience actually spends time and where I can drive registrations or ticket sales, instead of being half-present everywhere.
Deliverable: 1) A matrix comparing the relevant platforms on audience presence, formats that suit the event, approximate ad cost, and production effort. 2) The recommended platforms and the role of each one (discovery, conversion, community). 3) A posting plan by phase: announcement, countdown, during the event, and after.
Criteria: Justify each choice with my audience's behavior, not the platform's general popularity. If you have web search, check current demographic data for each platform in my region. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A comparison table; then the recommendations and phased plan as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
