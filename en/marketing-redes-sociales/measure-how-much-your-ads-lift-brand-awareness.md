Context: I'm running awareness ads for [your brand] on [platforms]. My audience is [target audience], and the campaign budget is [campaign budget].
Goal: Find out whether the ads actually get more people to know and remember the brand, so I can justify or adjust the spend.
Deliverable: 1) The awareness metrics worth using (ad recall, aided and unaided awareness, branded search, direct traffic, mentions), each with its formula. 2) One measurement design with an exposed group and a control group, and another with a before-and-after comparison, explaining when each makes sense. 3) Questions for a short brand recall survey. 4) The lift formula (difference between groups divided by the control group) with an illustrative numeric example. 5) Which native brand lift studies the platforms offer and what they require.
Criteria: Explain the most common biases (seasonality, other active campaigns, small samples) and how to reduce them. If you have web search, check which studies each platform offers today and their minimum spend. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections, formulas on separate lines, and the example as a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
