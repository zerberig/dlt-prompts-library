Context: I want to set up social listening for [your brand] in [social listening tool]. My key products or services are [key products or services] and my main competitors are [main competitors].
Goal: Have well-built queries that capture what people say about my brand, competitors, and industry without filling up with irrelevant mentions.
Deliverable: 1) The queries to create, grouped into brand, products, competitors, industry, and risks, each with its terms, variants, common misspellings, and exclusions, written with Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT). 2) The alerts to set up and when to notify immediately, such as mention spikes or crisis-related words. 3) A weekly routine to review results and turn them into findings: trends, sentiment, recurring complaints, and product ideas. 4) A one-page monthly report template.
Criteria: Include terms in my audience's language and way of speaking. If you don't know my tool's exact syntax, use generic Boolean syntax and tell me.
Format: The queries as a table with the columns Group, Boolean query, Exclusions, and Purpose; everything else as lists. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
