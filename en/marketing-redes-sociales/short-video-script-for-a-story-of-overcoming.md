Context: I want to tell the story of [person and challenge they overcame] on [platform]. The person agreed to share it, and they're connected to [your brand or cause] like this: [connection to your brand or cause].
Goal: I want a short video that moves people and gets shared because of the story itself, not by presenting the person as an object of pity.
Deliverable: 1) Three different hooks for the first 2 seconds. 2) A 30-to-60-second script with a setup, obstacle, turning point, and outcome, showing what's said and what's seen at each moment. 3) A list of simple shots that can be filmed on a phone. 4) On-screen text and a post caption. 5) A suggestion for the type of music or sound.
Criteria: The person should be the protagonist and tell the story in their own voice whenever possible. Use only real facts they confirm, without exaggerating the drama. The brand, if it appears, comes at the end and stays low-key. Don't promise virality; focus on what makes it shareable.
Format: Hooks as a list; the script in a table with the columns Time, What we see, and What's said; the rest as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
