Context: I manage social media for [your brand] on [platforms], and I want to follow what's being said about [topic or industry] in real time. I use or can use [available tools].
Goal: Catch fast-growing conversations early and decide sensibly whether to take part, without improvising or jumping on topics that aren't ours.
Deliverable: 1) A list of keywords, hashtags, accounts, and sources to watch, grouped by priority. 2) The signals that a topic is taking off (speed of mentions, large accounts joining, presence across several platforms) and suggested alert thresholds. 3) A decision tree for joining or not: brand relevance, risk, the topic's tone, and whether we can add something of our own. 4) Target response times and who approves. 5) A minimal dashboard with the views I should check several times a day.
Criteria: Keep tragedies, divisive political topics, and other brands' crises out of scope, except to share information respectfully. Thresholds are a starting point: tell me how to adjust them after two weeks. If you have web search, check which tools currently offer real-time alerts for those platforms.
Format: Numbered sections; the decision tree as a list of yes-or-no questions.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
