Context: I create content on [platform] for [your brand or profile]. My target audience is [target audience], and what I offer is [your product or service].
Goal: I want an idea bank that lasts a month and connects with my audience's real life, not generic ideas any account could post.
Deliverable: 1) An audience summary in three lists: common problems, interests, and goals. 2) Four content pillars drawn from those lists. 3) Twenty post ideas spread across the pillars, each with a working title, recommended format, and the problem, interest, or goal it addresses.
Criteria: At least half the ideas should deliver value without selling (educate, inspire, or entertain). Include behind-the-scenes ideas or real customer stories where relevant. Each idea should be specific enough to produce without further research.
Format: Audience lists as bullets, pillars one line each, and ideas in a table with the columns Idea, Pillar, Format, and Need addressed.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
