Context: I manage social media for [your business] on [platforms]. These are the goals I set: [your current goals]. My current metrics are [current metrics] and my resources are [available resources].
Goal: I want to know whether my goals are well defined and fix them so I can truly measure progress and explain results clearly.
Deliverable: 1) A short explanation of each SMART criterion applied to social media, with a good and a bad example. 2) An evaluation of each of my goals against the five criteria, pointing out what's missing. 3) A corrected version of each goal, with metric, baseline, target, deadline, and platform. 4) The factors that most often make these goals fail (vanity targets, unrealistic deadlines, metrics outside my control) and how to prevent them.
Criteria: Use my current metrics as the baseline and explain in one line why each target is achievable. Prefer outcome-linked metrics over follower count. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The evaluation in a table with the columns Original goal, Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound, and What's missing; the corrected versions as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
