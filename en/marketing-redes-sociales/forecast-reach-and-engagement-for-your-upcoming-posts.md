Goal: I want to estimate the expected reach and engagement of my upcoming posts, so I can set realistic targets and choose formats, days, and topics more wisely.
Deliverable: 1) A brief exploratory analysis: trends over time, differences by format, day, time, and topic, and outliers. 2) A simple, explainable model (for example, a regression on those variables or trend-adjusted averages) with step-by-step instructions to build it in my tool, including formulas or code. 3) Forecasts for 5 planned posts, with low, expected, and high ranges. 4) How to validate the model against recent posts and how often to update it.
Criteria: Prefer a simple model I can understand over a complex one. Explain the limits: algorithm changes, trends, and viral content can't be predicted from historical data. If the history is too short for a reliable forecast, tell me and suggest how much more data I need. Always present forecasts as estimated ranges.
Format: Numbered sections; forecasts in a table with the columns Post, Format, Day, Low, Expected, and High; code or formulas in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
