Context: I sell [your product or service] through [sales channel] and want to send customers a survey [send timing] (for example, 7 days after delivery).
Goal: I want to know how satisfied they are, whether they'd recommend us, and what to improve in the product, checkout, and shipping, so I can prioritize changes with data.
Deliverable: 1) A 6-8 question survey including overall satisfaction (1-5 scale), Net Promoter Score, an open question about the product, and questions about the buying and delivery experience. 2) The invitation message to send by email or direct message. 3) A conditional follow-up question for low scores. 4) How to analyze the answers and what to do with each group: promoters, passives, and detractors.
Criteria: It should take under 3 minutes to complete. Write neutral questions that don't lead the answer. Ask permission before using a comment as a public testimonial, and offer no incentives in exchange for positive ratings.
Format: Questions as a numbered list with the answer type in parentheses; the invitation in a separate block; the analysis as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
