Context: I want to promote [your product] with a livestream on [platform]. My audience is [target audience], and the special offer for live viewers is [livestream offer].
Goal: I want an entertaining live session that shows the product in action, answers questions in real time, and drives sales, without feeling like a long commercial.
Deliverable: 1) A promotion plan for the 7 days before. 2) A 30-to-45-minute script in blocks: opening hook, demo, use cases, audience Q&A, offer, and close. 3) Three tactics to keep the audience engaged (questions, polls, challenges, or guests). 4) Prepared answers for the 5 most likely objections. 5) What to do afterward: replay, short-video clips, and follow-up with people who asked questions.
Criteria: Show the product in real use and be honest about its limits. The offer must be real, with clear terms. If you have web search, check which live shopping features the platform offers today in my country; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: The script in a table with the columns Minute, Block, What's said or done, and On-screen element; the rest in sections with bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
