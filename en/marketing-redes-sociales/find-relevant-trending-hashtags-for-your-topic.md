Context: I post about [topic] on [platform], in the [industry] industry.
Deliverable: A set of 25 hashtags organized in three tiers: broad (high competition), niche (specific to my topic), and community or location. Add 3 ready-to-use combinations of 3 to 5 hashtags each, and how many hashtags it makes sense to use on [platform] today.
Criteria: If you have web search, check which hashtags are active and trending; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate and don't make up volumes. Drop hashtags that are restricted, spam-heavy, or carry a meaning unrelated to my topic.
Format: A table with the columns Hashtag, Tier, and Why it's relevant; then the combinations.
