Context: I have a YouTube channel about [your channel's topic] and here's last month's data from YouTube Studio: [channel data for the month]. My goal for the channel is [channel goal].
Goal: Understand how the channel is doing beyond views and decide what to do next month.
Deliverable: 1) A read of the key channel metrics: impressions and click-through rate, watch time, average view duration, retention, subscribers gained per video, traffic sources, and returning versus new viewers, explaining what each one says in my case. 2) The 3 videos that contributed most to my goal and why. 3) A diagnosis of what's holding growth back. 4) 3 concrete actions for next month, each with the metric it should improve.
Criteria: Base the analysis only on my data; if a metric is missing to conclude something, tell me which one to export. Separate what depends on the content from what depends on the packaging (thumbnail and title).
Format: Numbered sections; the metrics as a table with the columns Metric, My data, and What it shows. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
