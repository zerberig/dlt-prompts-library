Context: I post on LinkedIn as [your professional profile] in the [industry] industry. My areas of expertise are [your areas of expertise].
Goal: I want to use current news, trends, and conversations in my field to post on time and come across as someone with my own point of view, not someone who repeats headlines.
Deliverable: 1) If you have web search, 5 to 8 recent news items or trends in my industry with date and source; if not, ask me to share the news. 2) For each, a post idea with my angle: what I think, what it means for my audience, and what experience of my own I can add. 3) An opening hook for each idea. 4) A 3-question filter for deciding when not to weigh in on a topic (sensitive, polarizing, or outside my expertise).
Criteria: Prioritize topics where I can offer a perspective of my own that I can back up. Don't overstate a story's significance or present it as confirmed if it isn't. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns News or trend, Source and date, My angle, and Hook; then the filter as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
