Context: I need a tool to schedule posts on [platforms]. We're [team size], and my budget is [monthly budget]. What matters most to me is [main needs] (a free plan, team approvals, analytics, content curation, or a unified inbox).
Goal: Pick a tool that covers what I actually use, without paying for features I don't need or outgrowing it in six months.
Deliverable: 1) Evaluation criteria, weighted by my needs. 2) A comparison of 4-6 currently available tools: free or entry plan, approximate price, supported platforms, team and approval features, analytics, and key limitations. 3) A top recommendation and a runner-up, with the reasoning. 4) Questions to ask during the free trial and red flags.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to confirm pricing, plans, and supported platforms, since they change often, and note the date you checked. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Favor tools with official platform integrations, so the accounts aren't put at risk.
Format: A comparison table with the columns Tool, Free plan, Approximate price, Platforms, Team and approvals, Analytics, and Limitations; then the recommendation.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
