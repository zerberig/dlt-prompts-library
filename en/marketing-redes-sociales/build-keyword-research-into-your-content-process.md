Context: I run a content strategy on [platform] for [your brand or niche] and so far I pick topics by gut feeling. These are the topics and formats I post most: [current topics and formats].
Goal: Build keyword research into my process so my content answers what people search for on the platform and in search engines.
Deliverable: 1) A research method I can repeat monthly: sources (the platform's search suggestions, questions in comments, related searches, tools), how to judge volume and intent, and how to prioritize. 2) A quick audit of my current topics: which match real searches and which should be adjusted. 3) How to turn a keyword into a piece of content: title, hook, caption, subtitles, and tags. 4) What to measure to know whether the change is working, such as impressions from search, saves, and new followers.
Criteria: Balance what people search for with the brand's voice, working keywords in naturally. If you have web search, check how search currently works on [platform].
Format: Numbered sections; the method as a list of steps. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
