Context: I'm launching [your product] on [platform]. It's for [target audience], and what sets it apart is [main differentiator]. The current format or trend I'd like to tap into is [trend or format].
Goal: I want a launch post people want to save or share with someone, not just a "now available" announcement.
Deliverable: 1) Five different hooks for the first line or first seconds (curiosity, problem, fact, humor, contrast). 2) The full post using the hook you recommend. 3) A short explanation of why someone would share it. 4) Three to 5 relevant hashtags and a call to action.
Criteria: The trend should connect naturally to the product; if it doesn't fit, tell me and suggest another angle. Match length and tone to [platform]'s conventions. Don't promise results the product can't deliver.
Format: Hooks as a numbered list, the post in a copy-ready block, then the explanation and hashtags.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
