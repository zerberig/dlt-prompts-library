Context: I want to launch an augmented reality filter or lens for [your brand] on [platform] (for example, Snapchat or TikTok). My audience is [target audience], and I want to attract new followers and get people sharing their results.
Goal: A filter people want to use more than once and post, and that connects naturally with the brand.
Deliverable: 1) Four filter concepts, each with its mechanic (game, transformation, virtual try-on, challenge, or random result), why it would invite sharing, and how the brand appears without getting in the way. 2) For the recommended concept: the full experience from opening to sharing, the visual elements, and suggested sound. 3) Design best practices: length, clear instructions, performance on mid-range phones, and accessibility. 4) A launch plan with creators and our own posts. 5) Metrics to evaluate it: uses, captures, shares, and attributable new followers.
Criteria: If you have web search, check which filter-creation tools are still available on that platform today and their approval requirements, since these change often. Avoid effects that alter physical features in ways that promote unrealistic beauty standards.
Format: Concepts as a numbered list; everything else in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
