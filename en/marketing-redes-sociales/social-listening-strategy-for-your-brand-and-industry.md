Context: I work at [your brand], in the [industry or niche] sector. My main competitors are [competitors], and my audience's conversation happens mostly on [platforms].
Goal: I want a social listening system that flags industry trends early, helps me find customers who already recommend the brand, and shows me what competitors are doing, so I can make content and product decisions.
Deliverable: 1) A query map with brand terms (including common misspellings), product, competitor, industry, and customer-problem terms, with sample Boolean queries. 2) What to monitor on each platform and with what kind of tool (native, free, or paid). 3) Criteria for identifying brand advocates and how to approach them. 4) A weekly routine and a one-page monthly report template with findings and decisions.
Criteria: Prioritize signals that lead to a concrete action over raw mention volume. If you recommend tools, name categories or examples and note that pricing and features should be checked, since they change often.
Format: Sections with headings; the query map in a table with the columns Category, Terms, and Sample query.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
