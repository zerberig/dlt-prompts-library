Context: I run [your brand or business] and want to tell this real story: [real story or case] (a customer, a mistake we fixed, how a product came to be). It will be published as a short video on [platform] for [target audience].
Goal: Get people to watch to the end, see themselves in the main character, and share it with someone going through something similar.
Deliverable: 1) Three different hooks for the first 3 seconds (a question, an awkward situation, an unexpected result). 2) A 30-60 second script in three acts (setup, conflict, resolution) with what's on screen and what's said. 3) On-screen text, the post caption, and a closing question that invites comments.
Criteria: The main character is the person, not the product; the brand shows up as help within the story. Use only the facts I gave you, with no invented testimonials or exaggerated results, because the story has to hold up for people who know it firsthand. Spoken language and short sentences.
Format: Hooks as a numbered list; the script as a table with the columns Time, Visual, and Voice or text; then the caption.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
