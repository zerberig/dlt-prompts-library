Context: I run [your brand or profile] on [platform] and compete for the same audience's attention with [competitors]. Here's what I know about each account: [account descriptions] (topics, formats, tone, posting frequency, what works for them). If you can browse the web, you may review their public profiles.
Goal: I want a clear positioning that makes my account recognizable and the one people choose, instead of copying what others already do.
Deliverable: 1) A comparison chart of the accounts by topics, formats, visual style, voice, community interaction, and value proposition. 2) The gaps: topics, formats, or audience segments nobody serves well. 3) Three possible differentiation angles for me, with pros, cons, and a sample post for each. 4) A final recommendation and the first five concrete changes to put it into practice.
Criteria: Base the differentiation on something I can sustain (expertise, personality, process, community), not format tricks that get copied within a week. Flag which observations are assumptions due to missing data.
Format: The comparison chart as a table; then sections with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
