Context: I create content about [your topic or niche] on [platform] for [target audience]. I want to cross it with a seemingly unrelated field: [unexpected topic] (if I leave it blank, suggest 3 possible crossovers and pick the most promising).
Goal: Ideas that stand out from the niche's repetitive content and that people want to share, save, or comment on.
Deliverable: [number of ideas] ideas. For each: a working title, the format (short video, carousel, thread, poll, or series), the hook for the first line or first seconds, a 2-3 sentence outline, the reason someone would share it (relatability, usefulness, surprise, humor, or debate), and the production effort.
Criteria: No idea should rely on deception, exaggerated data, or provocation for its own sake, because that brings reach but erodes trust. Viral reach is never guaranteed: rank the ideas from most to least potential by your judgment and explain that judgment in one line.
Format: A table with the columns Idea, Format, Hook, Outline, Reason to share, and Effort.
