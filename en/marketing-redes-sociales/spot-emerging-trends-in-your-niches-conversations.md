Context: I work in [niche or industry] and want to catch emerging topics before they go mainstream. Here's a sample of recent conversations from [platform or sources]: [conversation sample] (posts, comments, customer questions, forum threads).
Goal: Find early signals that give me content, product, or service ideas with a head start.
Deliverable: 1) Four to 6 emerging topics, each with its evidence from the sample (short quotes), the need or behavior shift it reveals, and its estimated stage (weak signal, growing, or already established). 2) For each topic: the content opportunity, the business opportunity, and the risk that it's a passing fad. 3) Which terms or searches to monitor to confirm it's growing. 4) The 2 topics you'd bet on first and why.
Criteria: Separate what recurs in the sample from isolated mentions; don't present something that appears only once as a trend. If you have web search, check each topic against recent outside sources and tell me whether they confirm it.
Format: A table with the columns Topic, Evidence, Need, Stage, Opportunity, and Risk; then the recommendations as bullets.
