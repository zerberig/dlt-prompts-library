Context: I represent [your organization]. Here's what's happening: [crisis description]. What we know for certain so far is [confirmed facts], and the conversation is mostly on [platform].
Goal: I need a first public message that acknowledges the situation, shows empathy, and says what we're doing, even though we don't have all the answers yet.
Deliverable: 1) A main statement. 2) A short version adapted to [platform]. 3) Three model replies for the most likely comments: anger, a specific question, and misinformation. 4) A list of things we shouldn't claim yet.
Criteria: Use only confirmed facts and don't speculate about causes or blame, because correcting later makes the crisis worse. Acknowledge the people affected before talking about the organization, avoid defensive or legalistic language, and commit to a next update at a specific time. If there are legal implications, recommend having legal counsel review the text before posting.
Format: Each piece in its own block with a title. Main statement of 120 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
