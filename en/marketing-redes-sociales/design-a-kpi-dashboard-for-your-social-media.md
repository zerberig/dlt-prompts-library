Context: I manage social media for [your business] on [platforms]. My main social goal is [main goal], and I want to build the dashboard in [reporting tool] (a spreadsheet, Looker Studio, or something else).
Goal: I want a dashboard that tells me in two minutes whether we're on track and where to act, instead of a report full of numbers nobody reads.
Deliverable: 1) Six to 10 KPIs organized in three tiers: business outcome, content performance, and community health. 2) For each KPI: definition, formula, data source, and review frequency. 3) A section-by-section dashboard sketch with the chart type for each section. 4) Thresholds that should trigger an alert or review.
Criteria: Every metric should connect to a concrete decision; drop vanity metrics that don't change what I do. Use metric names that exist in each platform's native analytics.
Format: A KPI table with the columns KPI, Tier, Formula, Source, Frequency, and Decision it informs; then the sketch as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
