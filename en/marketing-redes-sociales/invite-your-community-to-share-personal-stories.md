Context: I run the [your brand or community] account on [platform]. What brings my community together is [shared experience] (for example, starting a business, learning a language, or caring for a family member).
Goal: I want followers to tell their own stories in the comments or on their profiles, to spark genuine conversation and community-created content.
Deliverable: 1) Eight storytelling prompts, each with an open question and a short sample answer written as if by me, to go first. 2) A proposed weekly series using the best prompts. 3) A message asking permission before resharing someone's story.
Criteria: Prompts should be answerable in a few lines, specific (a moment, a decision, a mistake), and never intrusive about intimate topics. Avoid yes/no questions and generic lines like "tell us your story," because they get few replies.
Format: A numbered list of prompts with their examples; then the series and the permission message in separate sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
