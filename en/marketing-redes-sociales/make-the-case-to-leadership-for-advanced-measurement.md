Context: I lead social media at my company, and I want to request [amount or resources requested] to improve measurement on [platforms] with [what you want to implement] (for example, a conversions API, brand lift studies, an attribution tool, or an analyst). Today we measure with [current measurement], and annual social spend is [annual social spend].
Goal: A business case that convinces leadership better measurement pays for itself, because it lets us shift budget toward what actually works.
Deliverable: 1) The current problem in business terms: decisions we make blind today and their likely cost. 2) What the proposed measurement unlocks, with examples of decisions it would enable. 3) A return calculation in conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios, with the assumptions shown. 4) Total costs: tools, team time, and implementation. 5) Risks and how to mitigate them. 6) A phased plan with a 90-day pilot and its success criterion.
Criteria: Business language, not technical. Every assumption should be visible so leadership can challenge it. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A document of a page and a half max with headings, opening with a 3-sentence summary; the return calculation as a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
