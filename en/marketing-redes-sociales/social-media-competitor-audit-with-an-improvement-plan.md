Context: My account is [your account or brand] on [platform]. My main competitors are [competitors]. Here's what I've observed about each one over the past few weeks: [observations and metrics] (frequency, formats, topics, visible engagement, standout comments).
Goal: Understand what's working for them and where there are gaps I can fill, so I can adjust my strategy.
Deliverable: 1) A comparison table by account: content pillars, main formats, frequency, tone, type of call to action, and level of conversation in the comments. 2) What's working for them and why you think it works. 3) Gaps: topics, formats, or audience needs no one is serving well. 4) A three-column plan: adopt (adapted to my brand), avoid, and differentiate. 5) The 3 priority actions for next month.
Criteria: Start from the information I gave you; if web search lets you check their public profiles, add to it and note what you added. Separate observed data from hypotheses. No copying content: adapt ideas to my voice.
Format: Tables for the comparison and the plan; everything else as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
