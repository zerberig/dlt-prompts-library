Context: I want to run a contest on [platform] for [your brand], aimed at [target audience]. The prize will be [prize], and it will be open in [country or region].
Goal: I want the contest to spark conversation and valuable content from participants, not just followers who leave when it ends.
Deliverable: 1) Three possible mechanics that use platform features (questions, polls, live streams, user content), with pros and cons. 2) For the recommended mechanic, ten creative questions for participants related to the brand or topic. 3) The post announcing the contest. 4) Draft official rules with dates, eligibility, how the winner is chosen, and how the prize is delivered.
Criteria: Winner selection should be transparent and verifiable. If you have web search, check [platform]'s current promotion rules and the legal requirements for contests in my country, and flag anything you can't confirm. Recommend having a legal professional review the rules when the prize has significant value.
Format: Mechanics in a table; then the questions as a numbered list, and the announcement and rules in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
