Context: I'm running [event name] on [event date], with about [number of attendees] attendees in person or online. We'll use the hashtag [event hashtag] and be active on [platforms].
Goal: Make sure no one talking about the event goes unanswered, catch problems as they happen, and make the most of attendee-generated content.
Deliverable: 1) What to monitor: terms, hashtags, speaker and sponsor accounts, and misspellings of the event name. 2) A plan across three moments (before, during, and after) with listening goals for each. 3) Team roles during the event: who monitors, who replies, who escalates, and who reshares attendee content. 4) Quick replies for common situations: logistics questions, complaints about audio or lines, praise, and technical issues with the stream. 5) Criteria for escalating an issue to event organizers. 6) The structure of the wrap-up report: mention volume, sentiment, most-discussed topics, and lessons learned.
Criteria: Short, human replies, with a target response time during the event. Ask permission before resharing attendee content. If you have web search, suggest current live-monitoring tools.
Format: Numbered sections; roles and quick replies as tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
