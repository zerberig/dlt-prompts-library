Context: I manage the [your business] account on [platform], in the [industry] industry. My average results for the last 3 months are: [your metrics] (engagement rate, reach per post, follower growth, ad CTR, or others).
Goal: I want to know whether my numbers are good for my industry and account size, and which metric has the most room to improve.
Deliverable: 1) Industry benchmarks for each metric, with source and date. 2) A comparison of my results against those benchmarks. 3) The two metrics with the biggest gap and three concrete actions for each. 4) Caveats on how comparable each benchmark is to my case (account size, region, methodology).
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to find recent studies, and prefer sources that explain their methodology. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Don't make up benchmarks.
Format: A table with the columns Metric, My result, Industry benchmark, Source, and Gap; then the actions and caveats as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
