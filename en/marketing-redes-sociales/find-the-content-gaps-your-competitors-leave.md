Context: My brand, [your brand], competes on [platform] with [competitors to analyze]. My audience is [target audience].
Goal: Find gaps competitors aren't covering (topics, formats, audience questions, or tones nobody uses) so I can stand out with my own content.
Deliverable: 1) A map of what each competitor posts: main topics, formats, tone, and approximate frequency. 2) The questions and needs of my audience that none of them answer well, using the comments on their posts as a source. 3) 5 opportunity gaps, each with a content series idea to fill it. 4) What isn't worth copying even if it works for them, and why.
Criteria: If you have web search, review their public profiles and recent posts, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Differentiation should start from my brand's real strengths, not just from doing the opposite of everyone else.
Format: A table for the competitor map; a numbered list for the gaps. 650 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
