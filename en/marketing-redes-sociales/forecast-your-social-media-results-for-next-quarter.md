Context: Here is the monthly [platform] data for [your brand] over the past [historical period]: [historical data]. For next quarter we're planning [planned changes] (more budget, a new posting frequency, a launch, or peak season).
Goal: Set realistic goals for the quarter and know what results to expect before committing to them with my team or client.
Deliverable: 1) A read on the historical trend: average growth, seasonality, and outliers, and whether to exclude them. 2) A monthly forecast of key metrics in three scenarios (conservative, base, and optimistic), with the assumptions behind each. 3) How the planned changes affect the forecast. 4) The method used, explained simply, and how to replicate it in a spreadsheet. 5) Early signals to watch each month to adjust the forecast.
Criteria: Work only with the data I gave you and explain how much confidence the forecast deserves given how much history is available. Present the scenarios as possible ranges, not certainties. If the history is too short to forecast, tell me and suggest another way to set goals.
Format: A forecast table with the columns Month, Metric, Conservative, Base, and Optimistic; everything else in short sections.
