Context: I post about [your niche] on [platform] for [target audience] and I'm planning content for [month to plan].
Goal: Know which trends, dates, and conversations are worth jumping on that month and which to skip.
Deliverable: 1) Relevant dates that month for my niche and my country or region. 2) Format and content trends on the platform (sounds, formats, topics), separating confirmed ones from predictions. 3) Recent changes to the platform's features or policies that affect my strategy. 4) For each trend: whether it fits my brand, how I'd adapt it, and how long it will stay relevant.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and cite the source for each trend; flag anything that's a prediction as an estimate. Drop trends that don't fit my brand or could come across as insensitive.
Format: A table with the columns Trend or date, Type, Source or confidence level, Fits my brand, and How to use it. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
