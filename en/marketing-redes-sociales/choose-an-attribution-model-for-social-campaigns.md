Context: I invest in social media for [your business] and my sales or conversions happen on [where conversions happen]. Today I measure results with [measurement tools you use].
Goal: I want to choose an attribution model that helps me decide which platforms and campaigns to invest in, while understanding how reliable the data is.
Deliverable: 1) A short explanation of first-click, last-click, multi-touch (linear, position-based, and time-decay), and data-driven models, with an example applied to my business. 2) A recommended model for my case and why. 3) Setup steps: UTMs, conversion events, and attribution windows. 4) Current limitations (privacy, cookie restrictions, gaps between what each platform reports and web analytics) and how to offset them with incrementality tests or a "how did you hear about us?" question.
Criteria: Explain it for a marketer who isn't an analyst. If you have web search, check how attribution windows currently work on the platforms I use and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: Sections with headings; the models in a table with the columns Model, How it splits credit, When it fits, and Risk. 800 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
