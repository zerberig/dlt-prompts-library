I'm going to tell the followers of [your brand or community] about this change: [change to your rules or policies] (for example, how we share user content, comment rules, or copyright use), which takes effect on [effective date]. Write a post for X of 280 characters max and a short thread of 3 to 5 posts explaining what's changing, why, and what people need to do, if anything. Use a warm, transparent tone with no legal jargon, and address the most likely question up front. Add a short version to pin to the profile and two model replies for pushback in the comments.
