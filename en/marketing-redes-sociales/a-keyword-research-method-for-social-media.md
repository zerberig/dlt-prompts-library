Context: I want to research keywords for [platform] around [topic or niche], for both organic content and ads. My tools budget is [tools budget].
Goal: A repeatable process to discover how my audience searches inside the platform and to use those words where they matter most.
Deliverable: 1) A 5-7 stage process: search bar autocomplete, related searches, mining comments and frequent questions, analyzing top-ranking results, data from native ad tools, and final validation. 2) The differences between researching for organic content and for ads. 3) Five to 8 tools, free and paid, with what each is for and its approximate price. 4) A keyword log template with columns for intent, relative volume, competition, and where to use it (profile, title, caption, subtitles, or hashtags). 5) Three A/B tests to validate which keywords bring in more searches.
Criteria: If you have web search, confirm the tools are still available and check current pricing. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The process as a numbered list; the tools and the template as tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
