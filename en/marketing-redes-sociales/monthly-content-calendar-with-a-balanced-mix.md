Context: I manage [your brand or business] on [platforms]. My audience is [target audience], and I can post [posting frequency]. This month's key dates or launches are: [key dates this month].
Goal: I want a one-month calendar with a healthy mix of informative, conversational, and promotional content, so I grow without flooding my audience with sales pitches.
Deliverable: 1) The recommended ratio of informative, conversational, thought leadership, and promotional content, with the reasoning. 2) The full calendar with date, platform, format (carousel, short video, live stream, image, or text), topic, goal, and a hook idea. 3) Pieces that can be repurposed across platforms and how to adapt them. 4) A weekly production checklist.
Criteria: Line up promotional posts with the key dates and warm up the audience with value content in the days before. The volume should be realistic for the frequency I gave you, and each topic specific enough to produce without further research.
Format: A table with the columns Date, Platform, Format, Content type, Topic, and Hook; then the other sections as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
