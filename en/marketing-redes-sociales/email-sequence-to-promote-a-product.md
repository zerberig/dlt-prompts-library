Context: I want to promote [your product] with an email campaign aimed at [list segment]. The offer or news is [offer or reason for the campaign].
Goal: Get as many interested subscribers as possible to take this action: [action you want them to take].
Deliverable: A 4-email sequence (announcement, benefits and proof, answers to common objections, and final reminder). For each email: 2 subject line options, preview text, body, call to action, and suggested send day.
Criteria: Each email should add something new and stand on its own. Subject lines of 50 characters max that are clear about the content, because misleading subject lines hurt trust and deliverability. Use urgency only if the deadline is real. Warm, direct tone; 120-200 words of body copy.
Format: Each email in its own block with the headings Subject, Preview, Body, and Call to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
