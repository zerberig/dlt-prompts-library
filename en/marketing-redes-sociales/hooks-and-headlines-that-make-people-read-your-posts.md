Write 11 headlines or opening hooks for a [platform] post about [post topic], aimed at [target audience]. Use a different angle for each: question, surprising fact, common mistake, contradiction, concrete promise, one-line story, list, comparison, before-and-after, strong opinion, and warning. Each hook should be both informative and eye-catching, 15 words max, and deliver on its promise in the content, because clickbait erodes trust. Label the angle of each and mark the 3 you recommend testing first, with the reason. Present everything in a table.
