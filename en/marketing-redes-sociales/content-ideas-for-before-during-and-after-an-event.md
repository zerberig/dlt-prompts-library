Deliverable: 1) Before: 6 ideas to build anticipation (speaker spotlights, sneak peeks, countdowns, audience questions). 2) During: 6 live coverage ideas that can be produced with a phone. 3) After: 6 ideas to keep the conversation going (recaps, key takeaways, talk clips, thank-yous). 4) For each idea: format, platform, and a sample hook. 5) How to gather and share content posted by attendees, with their permission.
Criteria: Adapt the tone and formats to my audience. Each idea should be concrete and doable with a small team. Get permission from speakers and attendees before posting their image.
