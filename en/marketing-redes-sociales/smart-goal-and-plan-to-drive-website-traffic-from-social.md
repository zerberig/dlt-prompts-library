Context: My site is [your website or business], and today I get about [current social traffic] visits a month from [platforms]. I want to grow that by roughly [target increase] within [timeframe].
Goal: Set a realistic goal and a plan so those visits land on pages that help me sell or capture leads.
Deliverable: 1) The goal written in SMART format, with the baseline and how to calculate progress. 2) A UTM parameter scheme to tag links by platform, format, and campaign. 3) Five to 8 tactics that drive clicks (links in stories, posts that point to a full guide on the site, exclusive resources), each with an effort estimate. 4) Metrics that show whether the traffic is good quality: time on page, conversion, and bounce rate.
Criteria: Some platforms may show posts with external links to fewer people; if you have web search, check how it works on each one today and suggest alternatives. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The goal as one paragraph; the UTM scheme as a table; the tactics as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
