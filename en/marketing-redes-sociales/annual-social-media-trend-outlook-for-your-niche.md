Context: I'm planning the social media strategy for [your business], in the [niche] niche, for [planning period]. We work mainly on [platforms] and have [team and budget].
Goal: Decide which trends deserve time and money and which to let pass, with arguments I can present to my team or client.
Deliverable: 1) Six to 8 trends relevant to my niche: formats, audience behavior, in-platform search, AI in content, social commerce, and platform changes. 2) For each: what it is, the evidence behind it, its likely impact on my niche, and the level of certainty. 3) A sort into three groups: adopt now, test at small scale, and ignore for now, each with its reason. 4) For the ones to adopt now, a concrete first action and how to measure it. 5) Signals that would tell me midway through the period that a bet isn't working.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it and cite sources; separate data from predictions. No forecast is certain, so state the level of certainty for each trend. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, Evidence, Impact, Certainty, and Decision; then the actions as a list. 600-900 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
