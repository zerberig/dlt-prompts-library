Context: I'm launching [campaign description] on [platforms] for [your business or organization], running for [campaign length]. The outcome I care about most is [main outcome] (sales, sign-ups, donations, media coverage, etc.).
Goal: I want to know before launch how I'll prove whether the campaign worked, so I'm not improvising the report at the end.
Deliverable: 1) A metrics chain from exposure to outcome (reach, engagement, clicks, conversion), with the key metric and target for each stage. 2) How to track each one: native analytics, UTM parameters, discount codes, forms, or surveys. 3) A UTM naming convention. 4) Checkpoints during the campaign and what to adjust if a stage falls short. 5) The structure of the final report.
Criteria: Separate activity metrics from outcome metrics, and explain how to attribute results that can't be tracked directly. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The metrics chain in a table with the columns Stage, Metric, Target, and How it's tracked; everything else in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
