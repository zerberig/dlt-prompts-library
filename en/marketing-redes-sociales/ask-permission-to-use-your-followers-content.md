Context: My followers post content about [your brand or product] on [platform], and I want to share it on my channels and, in some cases, use it in [intended uses] (ads, website, packaging).
Goal: Use that content with the creator's clear permission, to avoid copyright claims and protect the relationship with the community.
Deliverable: 1) The difference between resharing with credit inside the platform and using the content outside it or in advertising. 2) Two permission-request templates: one for resharing with credit and one for commercial use, spelling out what the creator must explicitly agree to. 3) A pre-publishing checklist: real authorship, identifiable people, minors, third-party music or brands. 4) The columns for a permissions log. 5) What to do if the creator withdraws permission.
Criteria: Clear, friendly messages with no fine print. If you have web search, check the platform's current terms on third-party content. Add a note recommending a lawyer review advertising uses, since this is general guidance.
Format: Numbered sections; templates in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
