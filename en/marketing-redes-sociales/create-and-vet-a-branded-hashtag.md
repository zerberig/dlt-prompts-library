I want to create a branded hashtag for [your brand or campaign] on [platform] to group content about [hashtag topic or purpose]. Suggest 8 short options that are easy to type and remember, and for each one explain what it conveys and whether it could be misread. If you have web search, check whether they're already used for something else or tied to controversial topics, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Recommend the top 2 and add usage rules: where to place it, how to invite the community to use it, and how to credit the content people share. Present the options in a table with the columns Hashtag, What it conveys, Risks, and Recommendation.
