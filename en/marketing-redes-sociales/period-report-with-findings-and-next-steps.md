Context: Here is the [platform] data for [your brand] covering [period]: [period data]. If I have it, I'm also including the previous period's data for comparison. The report is for [report recipient].
Goal: A report that takes three minutes to read and leads to decisions, not a list of numbers.
Deliverable: 1) A 3-4 sentence executive summary. 2) A table of key metrics with the change versus the previous period. 3) The 3 best- and worst-performing posts, with a hypothesis for why. 4) Three actionable findings. 5) Concrete recommendations for the next period, each tied to the metric it should move.
Criteria: Calculate changes only from the data I gave you; if something is missing, say so instead of assuming. Separate what the data shows from your interpretation. Match the technical level to the recipient.
Format: Sections with headings, 400 words max not counting the table.
