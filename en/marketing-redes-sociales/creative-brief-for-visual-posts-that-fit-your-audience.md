Context: My brand is [your brand], and I post on [platform]. My audience is [target audience], and I want a batch of [number of pieces] shareable visual pieces. The design will be done by [who designs] (me with templates, a designer, or an AI tool).
Goal: Give whoever designs everything they need to produce on-brand pieces without rounds of back-and-forth corrections.
Deliverable: 1) A short audience profile: what worries them, what inspires them, what they save and share, and what visual style they consume. 2) The mix of pieces split across three intents: inspire, educate, and entertain. 3) For each piece: goal, concept, exact on-image text, visual direction (composition, colors, and type of image), format and aspect ratio, and the post caption. 4) General guidelines: palette, fonts, logo use, and accessibility (contrast and type size).
Criteria: On-image text of 12 words max. Every piece needs a concrete reason to be shared. If the design will be done with AI, add a scene description for each piece, ready to use in an image generator.
Format: The profile as bullets; the pieces as a table with the columns Piece, Intent, Concept, On-image text, Visual direction, and Format.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
