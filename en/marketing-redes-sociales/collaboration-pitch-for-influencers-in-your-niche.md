Context: I want to work with [creator name or handle], who posts about [creator's topic] on [platform], to raise awareness of [your product]. What I can offer as compensation is [compensation] (payment, product, sales commission, or something else).
Goal: I want the creator to accept a collaboration that works for both of us and that their audience sees as authentic.
Deliverable: 1) A short, personalized first direct message, with 2 variants. 2) A follow-up message in case there's no reply within a week. 3) A one-page collaboration proposal: content idea, deliverables, dates, compensation, the brand's usage rights, and metrics we'll share. 4) Three collaboration ideas that fit their style, beyond a generic sponsored post.
Criteria: Show that I know their content with specific references; mark "to be filled in" wherever I need to add them. State that the collaboration will be disclosed as advertising, per platform rules and local regulations, because that protects both of us. Warm, professional tone without over-the-top flattery.
Format: Each piece in its own block with a title.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
