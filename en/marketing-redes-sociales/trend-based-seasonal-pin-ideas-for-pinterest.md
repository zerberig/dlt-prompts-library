Goal: Publish ahead of time what people will be searching for around those dates, because seasonal searches on Pinterest start weeks or months early.
Deliverable: 15 pin ideas, each with a title (100 characters max), a description with natural keywords (2-3 sentences), pin type (image, video, carousel, or infographic), suggested board, and recommended publishing week.
Criteria: If you have web search, check current Pinterest trends for my topic and season, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Mix trend-driven ideas with evergreen ones that keep working all year. Instead of promising a pin will go viral, prioritize what people search for and save.
Format: A table with the columns Title, Description, Pin type, Board, and Week, sorted by publishing date.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
