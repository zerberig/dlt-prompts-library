Context: I have [your brand or profile] on [platform] and want to connect with these creators or partners: [creators or partners you're interested in]. What I can offer is [what you can bring] (expertise, product, audience, content).
Goal: I want to build genuine relationships that lead to collaborations over time, instead of sending cold pitches nobody answers.
Deliverable: 1) A 30-day plan in four stages: observe, engage with value, first direct contact, and pitch. Include concrete actions per week. 2) Examples of comments that add something and aren't generic. 3) Two first-DM templates, one casual and one professional, that I can personalize. 4) Signs the relationship is ready for a pitch and signs it's better to wait. 5) A simple way to track each relationship.
Criteria: Everything should feel authentic: no empty flattery and no mass automated messages. Respect the creator's boundaries if they don't reply. The templates should be short and explain why I'm reaching out to that specific person.
Format: The plan in a table with the columns Week, Stage, and Actions; the rest in sections with bullets and the templates in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
