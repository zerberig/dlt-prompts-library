Context: My audience is [target audience] and I post about [your niche] on [platform]. These are real phrases, complaints, or questions I've heard from them: [real comments or questions from your audience].
Goal: Create content that connects on a deeper level because it names something my audience feels and almost nobody says, which makes it more likely to be shared.
Deliverable: 1) The 5 underlying tensions or frustrations behind those comments, each explained in one line. 2) 10 post ideas drawn from them, each with an opening hook, format, a 2-3 sentence outline, and why it could get shared. 3) The 2 ideas with the most potential and how to test them.
Criteria: No idea guarantees massive reach, so prioritize relevance and honesty over sensationalism. Keep titles true to the content and avoid manufactured controversy, because it drives reach but erodes trust.
Format: A numbered list for the tensions; a table for the ideas with the columns Idea, Hook, Format, Outline, and Why it would be shared.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
