Context: I have [your brand or product], aimed at [target audience], and I want to work with micro-influencers on [platform]. A creator whose style and audience I'd use as a reference is [reference creator].
Goal: I want a short list of creators with a real, relevant audience, instead of choosing by follower count.
Deliverable: 1) The ideal micro-influencer profile for my brand: follower range, topics, tone, audience type, and region. 2) Search terms, hashtags, and methods to find creators similar to the reference. 3) If you have web search, 5 to 10 candidates with their handle and why they fit; otherwise skip this point. 4) A checklist to spot inflated followers or engagement.
Criteria: Prioritize audience fit, comment quality, and past collaborations with similar brands. Don't make up profiles or numbers: if you can't verify something, flag it as an estimate.
Format: The profile as bullets; candidates in a table with the columns Creator, Niche, Approx. followers, Why they fit, and Red flags; the checklist with checkboxes.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
