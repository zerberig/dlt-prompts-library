Context: I want to learn from successful campaigns on [platform] related to [your industry or campaign type] and apply them to [your brand].
Goal: Understand what made those campaigns work and which parts I can adapt to my situation and budget.
Deliverable: 1) 5 real, documented cases, with brand, year, what they did, and published results. Include at least one that stands out for ethical or transparent practices. 2) For each case, the success factors (idea, format, timing, community, use of the platform), separating what's replicable from what depended on their size or budget. 3) Patterns shared across the cases. 4) 3 concrete ideas to apply those lessons to my brand.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and include the source for each case. If you can't verify a case or its numbers, say so or pick another one instead of filling in the gaps. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: One section per case with bullets; then the patterns and ideas. 800 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
