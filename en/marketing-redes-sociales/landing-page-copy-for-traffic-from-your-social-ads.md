Context: I'm sending traffic from [platform] ads to a landing page for [your product]. My audience is [target audience], and the ad promises [ad promise]. I want visitors to [desired action] (buy, sign up, or book a call).
Goal: Visitors arriving from the ad should immediately find what we promised and take action, with no distractions.
Deliverable: 1) The section structure, in order: headline, subheadline, benefits, how it works, social proof, common objections, offer, and call to action. 2) Ready-to-use copy for each section. 3) Three headline variants to test. 4) Visual direction for each section (which image or video fits). 5) An SEO title and a meta description of 155 characters max. 6) A mobile checklist: load speed, button size, and short forms.
Criteria: The headline should echo the ad's promise in similar words, because that consistency reduces drop-off. Use only real testimonials or figures I give you; if I don't have them, leave the spot marked «to fill in». Write for quick reading on a phone.
Format: Each section with its title and copy; the variants and checklist as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
