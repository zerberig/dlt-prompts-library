Write ad copy for [your product] aimed at [target audience]. Its main features are [main features] and what sets it apart from the alternatives is [your differentiator].
I need three versions: a short one of 125 characters max for an ad, a medium one of 50-80 words for a social post, and a long one of 150-200 words for a product listing or page. Each should open with the customer's problem or desire, turn the features into concrete benefits, and close with a clear call to action. Use claims that can be verified, with no empty superlatives.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
