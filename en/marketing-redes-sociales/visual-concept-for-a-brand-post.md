Context: I need a post for [platform] from [your brand or organization] about [post topic]. My brand's values and visual style are [your brand's values and visual style].
Goal: A visually striking piece that stops the scroll and conveys the brand's values, not just a pretty photo.
Deliverable: 1) 3 distinct visual concepts, each with composition, color palette, type of piece (photo, illustration, short video, or carousel), on-screen text of 6 words max, and the emotion it aims to spark. 2) For the concept you recommend: production notes (framing, lighting, props) and a descriptive prompt to generate it with an image model. 3) The post caption with a call to action, plus alt text for accessibility.
Criteria: Concepts a small brand can produce with its own resources. If real people, animals, or products appear, show them authentically and respectfully, with no staged scenes that could mislead the audience.
Format: A numbered list per concept; then the production notes and the caption. 550 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
