Context: I have [your brand or product], and my Snapchat audience is [target audience]. My production resources are [available resources] (in-house team, agency, budget).
Goal: I want to create an augmented reality experience people want to use and share, to make the brand memorable and gain followers.
Deliverable: 1) Five lens or filter concepts with different mechanics: virtual product try-on, a face-based game, an environment transformation, a filter for a special date, and a personalized experience. 2) For each concept: what the user sees and does, why they'd want to share it, how the brand fits in without overwhelming it, and the production complexity. 3) A plan to promote the lens once it's live. 4) The metrics to evaluate it.
Criteria: If you have web search, check the current tools for building lenses on Snapchat and on other platforms that still allow third-party effects; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Prioritize ideas where the user is the star and the brand plays a supporting role. Avoid mechanics that alter physical appearance in ways that could affect self-esteem.
Format: Concepts as a numbered list with subheadings; promotion plan and metrics as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
