Context: I want to run a giveaway on [platform] for the account of [your brand and niche]. The prize I have is [prize and approximate value].
Goal: Attract followers who genuinely care about what I do, not prize hunters who leave the next day.
Deliverable: 1) The giveaway mechanics, with a prize or entry requirement that filters for people interested in my niche. 2) The official rules: dates, eligibility, how the winner is chosen, prize delivery, and exclusions. 3) Copy for the announcement post and for the winner announcement. 4) A 2-week post-giveaway plan so new followers stick around.
Criteria: The mechanics must follow the platform's promotion rules and local contest laws; if you have web search, check the current rules for [platform] and point out what I should confirm with a professional. Avoid mechanics like tagging 10 friends or buying followers, because they inflate numbers without real interest.
Format: Numbered sections, the rules as bullets. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
