Context: I use [platform] for [your business], which sells [your product or service] with a buying cycle of [buying cycle length]. My boss or client wants proof that social media drives business.
Goal: I want a short set of metrics that shows the link between social activity and leads and revenue, so I can justify the investment.
Deliverable: 1) Six to 8 key metrics, each with its definition, how to get it, and why it matters in my case. 2) How to connect platform data to my CRM, store, or form. 3) A sample calculation of cost per lead and return, using made-up numbers clearly labeled as such. 4) The metrics I should stop reporting and why.
Criteria: Order the metrics from closest to furthest from revenue. In long buying cycles, impact shows up with a delay; explain how to reflect that in the report.
Format: A table with the columns Metric, Definition, Source, and Why it matters; then the remaining sections as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
