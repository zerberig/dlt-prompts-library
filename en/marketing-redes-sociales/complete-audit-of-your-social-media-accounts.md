Context: I manage the accounts for [your brand or business] on [platforms]. My main goal on social is [main goal], and this is the information for each account: [your account data] (bio, posting frequency, formats you use, metrics from the last 3 months, sample posts).
Goal: I want an honest diagnosis of where my accounts stand and a clear list of improvements, so I stop posting on autopilot.
Deliverable: 1) A 1-to-5 score per account in six areas: profile and bio, visual and tonal consistency, content mix, frequency and consistency, community interaction, and results against the goal. 2) The top 3 strengths and top 3 weaknesses of each account. 3) Accounts worth pausing or doubling down on, with the reason. 4) A 90-day improvement plan prioritized by impact and effort.
Criteria: Back each score with evidence from the data I gave you. Point out what you couldn't evaluate due to missing information. Prefer concrete recommendations, like "change the first line of the bio to…", over general advice.
Format: Scores in a table with one column per account; strengths and weaknesses as bullets; the plan in a table with the columns Action, Account, Impact, Effort, and Timeline.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
