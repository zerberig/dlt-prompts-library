Context: I use [AI assistant] to generate content ideas for campaigns on [platform] aimed at businesses in the [industry] sector. Right now my prompts are one-liners and the results come out generic.
Goal: A prompt template I can reuse for every campaign by changing only a few details, and that produces specific, actionable ideas.
Deliverable: 1) The full template, organized into context, goal, deliverable, criteria, and format sections, with the changing details marked as «to fill in» (product, audience, campaign goal, tone, and constraints). 2) An example of the template filled in for a case in my industry. 3) Three short variants: one for ad keywords, one for creator collaborations, and one for questions in a live Q&A session. 4) Five tips for iterating on the AI's response (asking for more options, adjusting tone, cutting anything generic).
Criteria: The template should describe what a good result looks like instead of dictating a procedure, and end with a line asking the AI to ask questions if information is missing.
Format: The template and variants in code blocks; everything else as bullets.
