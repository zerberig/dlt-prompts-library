Context: I have a community around [your brand or topic] on [platform]. My followers are usually [description of your community], and what brings them together is [shared interest or value].
Goal: I want an activity where the community works toward a common goal, for example, reaching a certain number of contributions, completing a collective project, or supporting a cause, so the achievement belongs to everyone rather than a few winners.
Deliverable: 1) Three collective challenge concepts with their shared goal and how progress is measured. 2) For the recommended concept: the step-by-step participation mechanic, the type of content each person creates, how to show progress publicly, how to celebrate reaching the goal, and how to recognize participants. 3) A 2-to-4-week calendar. 4) Rules for using participants' content.
Criteria: Joining should be easy, under 5 minutes and doable on a phone. Ask for explicit permission before reposting user content and always give credit. If there are prizes, check the platform's contest rules.
Format: Concepts as a numbered list; the mechanic and calendar in tables; the rules as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
