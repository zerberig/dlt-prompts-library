Context: I'm responsible for social media at [your organization] on [platforms], and I want to be ready to monitor a crisis such as [likely crisis type]. The monitoring tools we have are [available tools].
Goal: I want a protocol for collecting and analyzing data during a crisis that gives leadership clear information to decide, instead of scattered screenshots.
Deliverable: 1) The signals to watch (mention volume, growth speed, sentiment, reach of the accounts spreading it, media coverage, customer service inquiries) and how to measure each. 2) Monitoring frequency by severity level. 3) Thresholds that trigger escalation or a change in response. 4) A one-page situation report template to send every few hours. 5) Good practices for preserving evidence and protecting users' personal data.
Criteria: Prioritize indicators that change a decision. Distinguish noise from a few very active accounts from widespread concern. Thresholds should be proportional to the usual volume of conversation about my brand; ask me for that number if you don't have it.
Format: Signals and thresholds in a table with the columns Signal, How to measure, Frequency, and Escalation threshold; the report template in a separate block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
