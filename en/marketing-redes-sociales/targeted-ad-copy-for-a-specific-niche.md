Context: I'm advertising [your product or service] on [platform] to people interested in [niche or interest]. The offer is [offer], and this audience's biggest concern when buying is [main objection].
Goal: I want ad copy that speaks the niche's language and that I can test against each other to find the best converter.
Deliverable: Three ad groups: cold audience (discovery), warm audience (consideration), and retargeting (conversion). For each group, 3 variants with headline, primary text, short description, and call to action, plus a suggested image or video.
Criteria: Use references, vocabulary, and situations specific to the niche. Each variant should test a different angle (benefit, objection, real social proof, or curiosity). Stick to the platform's current character limits and tell me if you're unsure of any. Base urgency only on the offer's real conditions, and avoid claims that break ad policies, such as promising results or referring to the user's personal attributes.
Format: One table per group with the columns Variant, Angle, Headline, Primary text, Call to action, and Suggested visual.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
