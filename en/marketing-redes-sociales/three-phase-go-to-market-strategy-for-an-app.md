Context: I'm launching [your app's name], an app that [value proposition in one sentence], aimed at [target audience]. My goal for the first 3 months is [launch goal] and I have [available budget and team].
Goal: A three-phase launch strategy that tells me what to do each month, on which channels, and how to tell whether it's working.
Deliverable: 1) Pre-launch (month 1): how to build anticipation and a waitlist. 2) Launch (month 2): priority channels, launch-day tactics, and app store listing optimization. 3) Post-launch (month 3): activation, retention, and gathering user feedback. For each activity: channel, suggested owner, approximate cost, and main KPI. 4) The plan's top 3 risks and how to mitigate them.
Criteria: Scale the ambition to the budget and team I have; if my goal looks unrealistic for those resources, say so and propose an alternative. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: One headed section per phase, with activities in a table (columns Activity, Channel, Owner, Approximate cost, and KPI); risks at the end. 900 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
