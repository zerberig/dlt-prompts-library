Create a 5-episode Snapchat story series about [topic or niche] for [target audience], designed so people come back every day and follow the account so they don't miss the next episode. For each episode, give the topic, 4 to 6 snaps with what's shown and the on-screen text, one interactive element (poll, question, quiz, or an invitation to reply), and an ending that sparks curiosity for the next one. Each episode should teach something useful on its own. Add 3 ideas for promoting the series on other platforms and the metrics that show whether it worked. Present the episodes in a table and the rest as bullets.
