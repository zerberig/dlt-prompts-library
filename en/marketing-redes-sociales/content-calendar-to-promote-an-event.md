Context: I'm organizing [your event and its date] and will start promoting it [lead time] ahead on [platforms]. The audience is [target audience], and the goal is [registration or attendance goal].
Goal: A calendar that builds interest right up to event day and uses post-event content to set up the next edition.
Deliverable: 1) The phases: announcement, speaker or activity reveals, countdown, event day, and post-event. 2) A day-by-day or week-by-week calendar with date, platform, format, post topic, and call to action. 3) Key moments for reminders (early-bird deadline, final week, the day before). 4) Content speakers and sponsors can share, with suggested copy. 5) A column for logging results and adjusting the promotion as you go.
Criteria: Alternate value content (previews of what attendees will learn, short interviews) with registration reminders, so it doesn't turn into nonstop selling. Deadlines and capacity limits must be real.
Format: A table with the columns Date, Phase, Platform, Format, Topic, Call to action, and Result.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
