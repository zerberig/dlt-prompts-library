Context: I'm hosting a live Q&A stream on [platform] about [session topic] for [target audience] (for example, small business owners in my niche), lasting [length].
Goal: I want attendees to leave with skills they can apply that same day, and I want the session to help me gain followers and leads.
Deliverable: 1) A run of show in timed blocks: welcome, short opening lesson, audience questions, a live case study, and a close with a call to action. 2) Twelve backup questions in case participation is low, ordered from basic to advanced. 3) Three posts to promote the session and a day-of reminder. 4) A plan to repurpose the recording into clips, posts, and an email.
Criteria: Prioritize practical, current advice; if you mention trends or algorithm changes, verify them with web search if you have it and flag what's an estimate. Include how to handle off-topic questions and negative comments live.
Format: The run of show in a table with the columns Minute, Block, and What to say or do; everything else in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
