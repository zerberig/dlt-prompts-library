Context: I manage social media for [your brand or business] on [platforms], and we mostly post [content types] (videos with music, carousels with stock images, memes, AI-generated content). We operate mainly in [country or region].
Goal: Avoid claims, takedowns, or account penalties for using other people's material without the rights.
Deliverable: 1) A checklist by type of material: music and audio, photos and illustrations, third-party videos, fonts, memes and templates, brands and logos, and AI-generated content. For each: the question to check, the risk, and the safe alternative (commercially licensed libraries, original material, or written permission). 2) The difference between the personal-account audio library and the commercial-use library inside the platforms. 3) Common myths, such as "if I give credit, I can use it" or "under 10 seconds is fine", and why they're wrong. 4) What to do if we receive a claim.
Criteria: If you have web search, check each platform's current policies and the general copyright rules in that region, and flag anything you can't confirm. Include a note recommending a lawyer for unclear cases, since this is general guidance.
Format: The checklist as a table with the columns Material, Question to check, Risk, and Safe alternative; everything else as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
