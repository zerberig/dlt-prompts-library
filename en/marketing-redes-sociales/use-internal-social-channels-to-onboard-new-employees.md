Context: I work at [your company], with [team size and work model] (for example, 80 people on a hybrid schedule). For internal communication we use [internal tools].
Goal: I want new hires to feel welcome, get to know their team, and learn faster, using social dynamics in our internal channels.
Deliverable: 1) A plan for a new hire's first 30 days with posts, activities, and training content per week. 2) Templates for the welcome post, the new-hire introduction, and recognition of their first win. 3) Ideas for bite-sized training content (short videos, question of the week, quick guides). 4) How to involve managers and teammates without making it a burden. 5) Indicators that show whether it's working.
Criteria: Participation should be optional and respect privacy: ask permission before sharing photos or personal details. Keep confidential company information separate from anything posted on public social media. The plan should work equally well for remote and in-office people.
Format: The plan in a table with the columns Week, Action, Channel, and Owner; the templates in separate blocks; the rest as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
