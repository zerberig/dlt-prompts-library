Context: I'm a [your role: agency, consultant, or freelancer] and I'm going to propose a social media strategy to [client name and industry]. Their marketing goals are [client goals], their audience is [client audience], and their brand image is described as: [client brand image].
Goal: I want a strategy the client understands and approves, where every social action clearly ties back to their business goals.
Deliverable: 1) A one-paragraph summary of the diagnosis and the opportunity. 2) An alignment matrix: business goal → social goal → key indicator → target. 3) Recommended platforms and the role of each. 4) Content pillars with examples that reflect the brand image. 5) Tone and visual style guidelines. 6) A phased plan for the first 90 days. 7) What I need from the client (access, approvals, materials, response times). 8) How and how often I'll report results.
Criteria: Write for a client who isn't a social media expert: clear and jargon-free. Targets should be realistic; flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Make clear what depends on the agency and what depends on the client, because that prevents misunderstandings later.
Format: A document with numbered sections; the matrix as a table; the 90-day plan as a table by phase.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
