Context: I want to advertise [your product or service] to people interested in [niche]. Their main objection to buying is usually [main objection], and my offer is [your offer].
Goal: I want creatives that stop the scroll and resonate with this niche, so I can test which one works best before spending more.
Deliverable: Three ad concepts with different angles: one emotional, one rational that answers the objection, and one using humor. For each concept: a description of the image or short video (scene, subject, framing, and on-image text of 6 words max), primary text in two lengths (short and long), headline, call to action, and the hypothesis it tests.
Criteria: The visuals should feel native to the feed, not like a catalog ad. The humor shouldn't mock the audience or their problems. Avoid outcome claims I can't prove, because the platform may reject the ad and the audience loses trust.
Format: Each concept in its own section with subheadings; at the end, a table with the columns Concept, Angle, Hypothesis, and Metric to pick the winner.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
