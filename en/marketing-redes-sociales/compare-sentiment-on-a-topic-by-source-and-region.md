Context: I want to understand the public reaction on [platform] to [topic or news story]. Here's a sample of posts, comments, or articles with their source and, when I have it, their region: [content sample].
Goal: A neutral read on how the topic is perceived across sources and regions, so I can decide whether my organization should say something and in what tone.
Deliverable: 1) Each item classified as positive, negative, neutral, or mixed, with the phrase that supports the call. 2) A sentiment summary by source and by region, with percentages calculated on the sample. 3) The 3-5 main arguments on each side, stated fairly. 4) Notable differences between regions or sources and possible explanations. 5) The analysis's limitations: sample size and biases.
Criteria: Describe each position without taking sides, especially on political or social topics. If a text is ironic or ambiguous, mark it as unclear. Don't extrapolate to the whole population: the results represent only this sample.
Format: A table with the columns Source, Region, Sentiment, and Evidence; then the summary in sections.
