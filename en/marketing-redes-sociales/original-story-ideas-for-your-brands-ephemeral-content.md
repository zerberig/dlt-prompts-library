Context: I post ephemeral stories on [platform] (Instagram Stories, Snapchat, or another) for [your brand or profile], which targets [target audience].
Goal: I want stories people watch to the end and reply to, different from what everyone in my niche posts.
Deliverable: First, 3 bullets on what's already overdone in my niche. Then, ten story-sequence ideas; for each: concept, number of frames, what each frame shows, the interactive feature to use (poll, question box, quiz, slider), and the response I'm hoping for.
Criteria: No idea should repeat what's overdone. Each sequence should be shootable on a phone in under 20 minutes. Use only features that exist on the platform today; if you're unsure about one, say so.
Format: The 3 bullets, then a numbered list of the 10 ideas.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
