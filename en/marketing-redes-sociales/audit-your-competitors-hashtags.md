Context: I'm in the [your industry] industry and I want to review how these competitors use hashtags on [platform]: [competitors to analyze].
Goal: Find out which hashtags work for them and which ones I could adopt, avoid, or replace with my own.
Deliverable: 1) A method to collect the hashtags from their last 20-30 posts and match them with each post's visible engagement. 2) A classification of the hashtags found: branded, campaign, community, broad topic, and niche. 3) Which ones seem linked to better performance, noting it's correlation, not causation. 4) A proposed hashtag set for my account, including a branded one of my own.
Criteria: If you have web search, review these accounts' recent public usage and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Leave competitors' branded hashtags out of the proposal, because using them confuses the audience and can look like piggybacking on their community.
Format: A table with the columns Hashtag, Type, Who uses it, Observed performance, and Recommendation. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
