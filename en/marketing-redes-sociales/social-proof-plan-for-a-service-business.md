Context: I offer [your service] to [target audience] and promote myself on [platform]. Right now I have this backing: [available proof] (testimonials, case studies, certifications, number of clients served, media mentions, partnerships).
Goal: Get a potential client who doesn't know me to trust me enough to reach out, without my profile looking like a wall of self-praise.
Deliverable: 1) The types of social proof that apply to a service (client results, visible process, experience and credentials, third-party endorsement, community and volume), noting which I already have and which I could get. 2) Eight post ideas that show social proof naturally, such as a before-and-after case told as a story, behind the scenes of a project, or real client questions answered. 3) A template for requesting specific testimonials that address the most common objections. 4) Where to place social proof on the profile: bio, highlights, and pinned posts.
Criteria: Only real, verifiable proof, with the client's permission to mention them; made-up testimonials destroy trust and can carry legal consequences. If my industry has rules on testimonials (health, finance, legal services), point that out and recommend reviewing them with a professional.
Format: Numbered sections; the ideas as a table with the columns Idea, Type of proof, Format, and Objection it addresses.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
