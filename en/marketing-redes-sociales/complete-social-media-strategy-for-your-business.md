Context: I run [your business and what you sell], aimed at [target audience]. Today I'm on [current platforms] with these results: [current results]. My social media team and budget are [available resources].
Goal: I want a complete strategy I can execute over the next 6 months to build a loyal community and turn followers into customers.
Deliverable: 1) Audience profile: needs, objections, and which platforms and moments they consume content. 2) Priority platforms and each one's role in the customer journey. 3) Three to five content pillars with mix, formats, and examples. 4) Realistic posting frequency per platform and a baseline weekly calendar. 5) Engagement tactics: replying to comments and messages, community content, collaborations. 6) How to move from engagement to conversion. 7) Key indicators, a monthly review, and criteria for adjusting.
Criteria: Everything has to fit the resources I gave you; if something doesn't fit, suggest what to cut. Prioritize a few well-run platforms over many half-done ones. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A document with numbered sections; the pillars and baseline calendar in tables. 900 to 1,400 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
