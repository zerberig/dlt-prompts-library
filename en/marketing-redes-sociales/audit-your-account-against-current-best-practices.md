Context: I manage the [your brand] account on [platform] in the [your industry] industry. Here's how I work today: [description of your current strategy] (frequency, formats, content types, comment replies, ad use).
Goal: Find out how well my account lines up with the platform's and my industry's current best practices, and what to change first.
Deliverable: 1) Current best practices for [platform] in my industry, grouped into profile, content, community, advertising, and compliance (including sector-specific regulations, if any). 2) An assessment of how I work in each group: meets, partial, or doesn't meet, with the reasoning. 3) The 5 highest-impact improvements, ordered from least to most effort.
Criteria: If you have web search, rely on recent sources from the platform and the industry, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Distinguish official rules from popular advice.
Format: A table with the columns Area, Best practice, My status, and Recommended change; then the list of improvements. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
