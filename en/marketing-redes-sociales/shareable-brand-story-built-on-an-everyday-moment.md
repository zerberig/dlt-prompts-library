Context: My brand is [your brand] and the message I want to get across is [key message]. I'll post it on [platform] for [target audience].
Goal: I want a short, relatable story where the brand message shows up naturally, so people see themselves in it and share it without feeling it's an ad.
Deliverable: 1) Three everyday situations from my audience's life where the message fits, one line each. 2) The full story using the situation you recommend, adapted to the platform's format (text, carousel, or short-video script). 3) An ending that ties back to the brand without breaking the tone. 4) Two alternative opening lines.
Criteria: The story needs a recognizable character, a small conflict, and a twist or punchline. Keep the humor light and never at the expense of a group of people. The brand appears at the end or in the background; the situation is the star.
Format: Situations as a numbered list; the story in a copy-ready block (split into slides or scenes for a carousel or video); then the ending and the variants.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
