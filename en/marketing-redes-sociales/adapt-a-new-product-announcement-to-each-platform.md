Context: I'm launching [your new product or service] for [target audience]. The most important thing people need to know is [key message and launch date]. I'll announce it on [platforms].
Goal: I want a consistent announcement across platforms that still feels native on each one and uses its format, instead of copy-pasting the same text.
Deliverable: For each platform: the announcement copy, the recommended format (carousel, short video, text post, story), an image idea or first-second video idea, 3 to 5 hashtags where relevant, and a call to action. Also, one message line that stays the same everywhere to build recall.
Criteria: Adjust length and tone to each platform: more professional on LinkedIn, direct and brief on X, visual and warm on Instagram, with a spoken hook on TikTok. Lead with the customer benefit, not the product name. Use only real product facts, with no invented figures or testimonials.
Format: One section per platform with subheadings; at the end, the shared line highlighted.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
