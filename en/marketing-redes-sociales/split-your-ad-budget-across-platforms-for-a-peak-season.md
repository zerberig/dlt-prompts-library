Context: I run [your business and what you sell] and want to maximize [desired outcome] (sales, bookings, sign-ups) during [target season]. My total budget is [budget in your currency], and I'm considering [platforms]. If I have results from past campaigns, I'll share them below.
Goal: I want a budget split across platforms and weeks that captures demand peaks and leaves room to shift money toward what works best.
Deliverable: 1) An initial allocation by platform and phase (before, during, and at the end of the season), with the reasoning behind each percentage. 2) Each platform's role in the funnel. 3) A testing reserve and clear rules for reallocating budget based on weekly results. 4) Three scenarios (conservative, base, and aggressive) with estimated results.
Criteria: If I have no historical data, explain the assumptions you're using. If you have web search, check current benchmark costs per platform for my region and season. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: An allocation table with the columns Platform, Phase, Percentage, Amount, and Funnel role; then the rules and scenarios in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
