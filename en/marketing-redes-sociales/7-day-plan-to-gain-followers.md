Context: I run [your brand or profile] on [platform]. I talk about [topic or niche] for [target audience] and can spend [time available per day] a day on it.
Goal: I want an intensive week focused on getting new people to find me and decide to follow, so I can then repeat what works.
Deliverable: 1) A 7-day plan with one post a day: format, topic, opening hook, and a call to action to follow the account. 2) One daily engagement task (commenting on related accounts, replying, collaborating, or joining niche conversations). 3) An end-of-week review: which metrics to compare and how to decide what to repeat.
Criteria: Prioritize formats with high discovery potential on the platform and topics that give a clear reason to follow me. Each post should deliver value on its own. The workload has to fit the time I gave you. Avoid artificial tactics like follow-for-follow or buying followers, because they hurt reach and credibility.
Format: A table with the columns Day, Format, Topic, Hook, Call to action, and Engagement task; the review as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
