Context: I just wrapped a [campaign type] campaign on [platform]. The goal was [campaign goals]. These are the results: [campaign results]. This is what we did: [campaign description].
Goal: Understand what worked, what didn't, and why, so the next campaign is better and we don't repeat mistakes.
Deliverable: 1) Results versus goals, with the percentage achieved. 2) An analysis by element: audience, message, format, posting time, budget, and call to action. 3) The 3 factors that most influenced the outcome, separating evidence from hypotheses. 4) Lessons learned written as rules for the next campaign. 5) What to measure differently next time.
Criteria: Be direct about what didn't work; the point is to learn, not to justify. Calculate only from the data I gave you and point out where data is missing to draw firm conclusions.
Format: A goals-versus-results table; everything else as sections with bullets. 500 words max.
