Context: I sell [your products] on [store or sales platform] and promote them on [platform]. Right now I have [available customer content] (reviews, photos, videos, mentions).
Goal: Use real customer content to ease the doubts of people about to buy and improve conversion.
Deliverable: 1) Which types of customer content to collect for each buying stage (discovery, consideration, and decision). 2) A system for asking: the best moment after delivery, the request message, and how to make it easy to respond. 3) Where to place each type: product page, shoppable posts, story highlights, ads, and emails. 4) Criteria for choosing which pieces to feature. 5) Metrics to measure the effect on sales.
Criteria: Real content only, with the creator's explicit permission for commercial use and without changing its meaning. Show some reviews with constructive criticism too, because a profile with nothing but five stars raises suspicion. No incentives in exchange for positive reviews, because that breaks most platforms' policies.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets; the request message in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
