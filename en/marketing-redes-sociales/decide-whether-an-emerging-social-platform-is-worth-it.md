Context: I run [your business or project], aimed at [target audience]. I'm currently on [current platforms] and I'm considering [emerging platform].
Goal: I want to make a reasoned decision about investing time there now, because every new platform takes hours away from the ones already working.
Deliverable: 1) An overview of the platform: what it's used for, who uses it, and which formats it rewards. 2) A 1-5 score on my audience's presence, early-mover advantage, production effort, fit with my current content, monetization or sales options, and the risk that the platform doesn't take off. 3) A recommendation (join now, test for 30 days, or wait) with a low-cost test plan if relevant. 4) Two other emerging platforms that might fit me better, one line each.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it for current user, growth, and feature data, and say when available information is thin. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The overview as a paragraph, the evaluation in a table with the columns Criterion, Score, and Rationale, and the recommendation at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
