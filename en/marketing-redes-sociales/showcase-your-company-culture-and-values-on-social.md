Context: I work at [company name], a [industry] company with [team size] employees. Our values are: [company values].
Goal: I want our social channels to show what it's like to work here, to attract like-minded talent and build customer trust, without it feeling like corporate advertising.
Deliverable: 1) Three or four content pillars that turn each value into situations people can see day to day. 2) Twelve post ideas spread across those pillars, with a suggested format and who should appear. 3) Short guidelines for employees to take part voluntarily: what they can share, what they shouldn't, and how to get approval.
Criteria: Favor real, verifiable stories (projects, team rituals, lessons learned) over inspirational quotes, because people distrust a culture that is only declared. Employee participation should be optional, with their consent to use their image.
Format: Pillars as a list; ideas in a table with the columns Idea, Pillar, Format, and Featured person; guidelines as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
