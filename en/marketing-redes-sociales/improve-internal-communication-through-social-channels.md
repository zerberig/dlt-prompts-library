Context: I work at [type and size of organization] and we use [internal communication tools] to talk with the team. The problems we see today are [current problems].
Goal: Make internal channels a place to inform, recognize good work, and build a sense of belonging, not just to push announcements nobody reads.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of the most common challenges in my situation (message overload, low participation, information that doesn't reach everyone, conversations that go off track), with a concrete fix for each. 2) A per-channel plan: what gets posted, who posts it, and how often. 3) Short usage guidelines for the team: tone, privacy, and what not to share. 4) 5 internal content ideas that encourage participation.
Criteria: Proposals that respect the team's time and also work for people who don't sit at a computer. Guidelines written in clear, positive language.
Format: Sections with headings; the diagnosis as a table with the columns Challenge and Fix. 650 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
