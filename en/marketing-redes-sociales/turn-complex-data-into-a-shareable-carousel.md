Context: I have this data or these findings: [data or information]. I want to publish them on [platform] for [target audience], who aren't necessarily experts on the topic.
Goal: Turn the information into a carousel people can understand without reading the original report, and that they'll want to save or share.
Deliverable: A script for a 6-10 slide carousel. For each slide: the main message, the exact on-image text, the type of visual (bar chart, side-by-side comparison, icon with a number, timeline), and a short note for the designer. Also the post caption and 3 headline options for the cover slide.
Criteria: One idea per slide. Round numbers so they read at a glance without changing their meaning, and cite the source on the last slide. If a figure can be read more than one way, pick the most cautious reading and tell me. Keep on-image text to 20 words max so it reads well on a phone.
Format: A table with the columns Slide, Message, On-image text, Visual, and Design note; then the caption and headlines.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
