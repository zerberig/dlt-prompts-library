Context: Here's my current profile on [platform]: [your profile content] (name, bio, link, pinned or highlighted posts, and a description of the photo and cover). I work in [your work or service] and want to attract [target audience].
Goal: I want a first-time visitor to understand in 5 seconds what I do, who it's for, and why to trust me, and then take the next step.
Deliverable: 1) A brief diagnosis of each profile element: what works and what's missing. 2) Three bio versions with different angles (outcome, authority, personality) within the platform's character limit. 3) Recommendations for the photo, cover, link, and pinned or highlighted posts. 4) Social proof elements I could add with what I already have (achievements, clients, press, real numbers).
Criteria: Use only the true information I gave you; if something would boost my credibility and I don't have it, suggest it as a goal, not as made-up copy. Clear, specific language without empty words like "passionate" or "innovative."
Format: The diagnosis in a table with the columns Element, Current state, and Improvement; then the bios and recommendations in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
