Context: I'm launching a collaboration with content creators to promote [your product or service] to [target audience]. My main goal is [campaign goal] and I have [budget and number of creators].
Goal: I need a brief I can send to creators so they understand what we expect without losing their own style, because content that feels authentic outperforms an imposed script.
Deliverable: A shareable brief with these sections: brand summary, campaign goal, the creator profile we're looking for, key messages (3 max), what they can and can't say, deliverables per platform (format, quantity, length), timeline with draft, approval, and publishing dates, content usage rights, metrics they'll need to report, and payment terms.
Criteria: Include the requirement to disclose the content as advertising using each platform's paid partnership tools, because hiding it erodes trust and can lead to penalties. Leave explicit room for the creator's creativity. Clear, friendly language with no corporate jargon.
Format: A document with headings and bullets, 1 to 2 pages long. At the end, a short invitation message to send along with the brief.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
