I have [your brand or product] and want to collaborate with content creators on [platform] to achieve [collaboration goal] (awareness, sales, reusable content, community). Give me 10 collaboration ideas in different formats, such as account takeovers, product co-creation, a content series, a joint livestream, an affiliate program, or a shared challenge. For each idea, explain what it involves, which kind of creator fits, the relative cost (low, medium, or high), the effort for my team, and the metric to evaluate it. Prioritize ideas that feel authentic to the creator's audience, and remember that every paid collaboration must be disclosed as an ad. Present everything in a table.
