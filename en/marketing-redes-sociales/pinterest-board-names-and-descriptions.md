Context: I have a Pinterest profile about [main topic] aimed at [target audience].
Deliverable: Suggest 8 boards that break that topic into useful subtopics. For each, give a clear name (5 words max) and a 2-3 sentence description explaining what people will find and why it's worth following.
Criteria: Names should use the terms people type into Pinterest search, not hard-to-find wordplay. Work keywords into the descriptions naturally, without hashtag lists.
Format: A table with the columns Board, Description, and Main keywords.
