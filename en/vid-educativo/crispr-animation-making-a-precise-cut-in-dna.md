Camera traveling at molecular scale inside a cell nucleus, following, in 3D scientific animation, a violet Cas9 protein carrying a bright green strand of guide RNA. The protein moves along a blue DNA double helix, stops where the sequence matches the guide, the helix opens, and both strands are cut cleanly in a white flash; then the cell's enzymes repair the site. Volumetric lighting, soft depth of field, floating particles, realistic biomedical visualization style. Mood of wonder and precision.
A narrator says: "CRISPR uses a guide RNA to lead the Cas9 protein to the exact spot in the DNA to be cut."
SFX: a crystalline snap at the moment of the cut.
Ambient: a low cellular hum and minimalist electronic music.
