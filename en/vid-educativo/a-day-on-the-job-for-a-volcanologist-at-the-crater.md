Handheld camera following a volcanologist in her mid-thirties, wearing a helmet, safety glasses, and a filtered respirator, as she walks along the rim of a steaming crater at sunrise. She kneels, points a portable gas meter at a yellow sulfur fumarole, then pulls the respirator down to her neck and looks at the camera to explain. Photorealistic, science-communication documentary style, low golden light, white steam against a blue sky. Mood of adventure and focus.
The volcanologist says: "We measure the volcano's gases because they can change before an eruption."
SFX: the hiss of the fumarole, the meter beeping, and gravel crunching under her boots.
Ambient: strong summit wind and a low, tense musical pulse.
