Close-up with a very gentle handheld camera inside a torchlit cave. A prehistoric woman presses her open hand against the rock wall, raises a hollow bone to her lips, and blows red ochre pigment around her fingers. When she pulls her hand away, its outline remains in negative, beside other ancient hands and paintings of bison and horses that emerge as the light moves. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the whole wall. Photorealistic recreation, flickering firelight, mineral textures, palette of ochres, reds, and blacks. Ancestral, intimate mood.
A narrator says: "Some of the first artworks were hands: pigment blown onto rock tens of thousands of years ago."
SFX: a puff of breath, the crackle of the torch, and dripping water.
Ambient: the deep echo of the cave and a distant drum.
