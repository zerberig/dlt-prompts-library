Medium shot with a slow tracking move around a workbench in a small furniture workshop. A white collaborative robotic arm sands a piece of wood and hands it to a carpenter in her forties, who fits it into a chair. When she reaches into the work area and brushes against the robot, it stops instantly and a light on its base changes from green to amber; she smiles and restarts it with a tap on a tablet. Photorealistic, natural skylight, floating sawdust, warm wood tones. Mood of collaboration and trust.
A female narrator says: "Collaborative robots are designed to work alongside people and stop when they detect contact."
