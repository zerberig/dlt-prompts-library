Side-on tracking shot moving alongside a team of Egyptian workers as they haul a massive limestone block on a wooden sledge up a sand ramp with ropes, the pyramid under construction in the background. In front of the sledge, a man pours water from a clay jar onto the sand and the block slides forward more easily; a foreman sets the pace with a chant. Photorealistic historical recreation, harsh afternoon sun, golden dust in the air, cinematic documentary style. Mood of collective effort.
A narrator says: "Wetting the sand in front of the sledge reduced friction and made it easier to drag multi-ton blocks."
SFX: ropes pulling taut, creaking wood, and water hitting sand.
Ambient: rhythmic work songs and desert wind.
