Medium shot with a slow lateral tracking move in a high school classroom: a group of students surrounds a first-aid instructor kneeling beside a CPR manikin on a blue mat. She places the heel of one hand on the center of the chest, interlocks the other on top, locks her arms, and starts firm, fast compressions while a student copies her on a second manikin. The instructor says: "Center of the chest, straight arms, hard and fast: one hundred to one hundred twenty a minute."
