Very slow forward tracking shot at tatami level in a traditional Kyoto room, its paper sliding doors open to a garden. A maiko, an apprentice geiko, in a long-sleeved silk kimono and traditional hairstyle, rehearses an unhurried dance: she opens a golden fan, turns her wrist, and lowers her gaze with precision. Across from her, an older teacher kneels and keeps time with a light tap of her closed fan against her palm. Photorealistic, soft afternoon light diffused through paper, muted colors with red and gold accents, contemplative cinematic style. Mood of discipline and elegance.
SFX: silk brushing the tatami and the snap of the fan opening.
Ambient: a shamisen plays slow notes, and water trickles from a garden fountain.
