Wide, locked-off timelapse of the dirt plaza of a rural Andean village. 0–3 s: women and children cross carrying buckets of water as shadows sweep and days pass. 3–6 s: neighbors dig a trench, lay a pipe, and build a small concrete community fountain; the landscape turns from dry to green. 6–8 s: time returns to normal speed and a girl opens the tap and fills her cup while her grandmother smiles. Photorealistic, documentary style, warm afternoon light, earthy palette. Hopeful mood.
