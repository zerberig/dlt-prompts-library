Paper-cut animation with amate bark-paper texture and a camera panning sideways as if across an illustrated map. 0–3 s: in a Mexica market in Tenochtitlan, a vendor offers red tomatoes and paper letters float from her mouth to spell "tomatl". 3–6 s: the letters ride a sailing ship across a rippling blue ocean and, on reaching Europe, rearrange into "tomate". 6–8 s: the letters split and also form "tomato" above a plate of modern food. Warm palette of reds, ochres, and turquoise, soft paper shadows. Playful, curious mood.
A narrator says: "The word 'tomato' comes from the Nahuatl 'tomatl', and it traveled the world along with the fruit."
SFX: rustling paper and lapping waves.
