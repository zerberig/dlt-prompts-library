Continuous forward zoom starting from the reference image, keeping its style, palette, and technique. The camera closes in on a small detail of the scene, such as a window, an eye, or a drop of water, and as it enters, a new setting in the same style appears; the zoom keeps moving toward another detail and repeats the effect once more, with no cuts. Transitions between settings are fluid and organic. Constant speed, hypnotic effect. Dreamlike, surprising mood.
SFX: a deep, sustained whoosh that swells as it passes through each setting.
Ambient: an ambient electronic track with a rising arpeggio.
