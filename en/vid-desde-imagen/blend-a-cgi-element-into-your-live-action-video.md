Edit the reference video to integrate [digital element to add] into the real scene, as if it had been filmed there. Keep the original camera movement, framing, people, and background. The element moves in sync with the camera, is grounded with contact shadows, takes on the same light direction and color temperature as the scene, and interacts with its surroundings: it kicks up dust, reflects lights, or brushes the foliage as it passes. Photorealistic film-grade visual effects, with the same grain and blur as the footage. A believable sense of wonder.
SFX: sounds from the digital element, such as footsteps, hums, or impacts, with the room's acoustics.
Ambient: keep the video's original ambient sound.
