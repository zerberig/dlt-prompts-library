Take the reference animation, hand-drawn or made in stop motion, and generate in-between frames so the motion looks fluid. Keep the art style, line weight and texture, colors, the imperfections of the paper or clay, and the composition of every shot intact. Characters keep their proportions and design in every new frame, with no distortion or invented features. The timing and duration of each action match the original; only the transitions between poses are smoothed. Handcrafted mood, now more fluid.
SFX: keep the original sound effects, synced to the new motion.
Ambient: keep the original music.
