Restore the reference video, recorded at low resolution or on old tape. Keep exactly the same people, faces, gestures, clothing, framing, and motion; do not invent details or change identities. Reduce noise and interference lines, remove flicker, stabilize the image, sharpen it naturally, and correct faded color toward tones true to the era, without making it look shot today. Keep a light grain texture so the result feels authentic. Nostalgic, respectful mood.
SFX: clean up the hiss and crackle in the original audio.
Ambient: keep the original voices and sounds, with more clarity.
