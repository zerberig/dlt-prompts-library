Completely locked-off camera on the reference image, turned into a perfectly looping cinemagraph. Keep the subject, framing, colors, and style of the image exactly the same; only [moving element] moves, softly and continuously, like steam rising from a cup, water in a fountain, or hair in the breeze. The rest of the scene stays frozen like a photograph. The last frame matches the first so the clip repeats with no visible cut. Hypnotic, serene mood.
SFX: a soft, cyclical sound from the moving element.
Ambient: a minimal ambient track that can loop seamlessly.
