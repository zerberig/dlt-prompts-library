Use the last frame of the first scene as the starting image and the first frame of the second scene as the ending image. Generate one continuous bridge shot between them: the camera whip-pans with motion blur out of the first location, passes through an in-between element that fits the story, such as a doorway, a street, or the sky, and settles smoothly into the composition of the second scene. Keep the characters, lighting, and color style of both clips so the cut is invisible. Fluid, narrative mood.
SFX: the whoosh of the whip pan.
Ambient: a smooth crossfade from the first scene's ambient sound to the second's.
