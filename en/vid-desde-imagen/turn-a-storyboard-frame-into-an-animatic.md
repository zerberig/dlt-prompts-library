Animate the storyboard panel from the reference image as an animatic. Keep the pencil-sketch style, loose lines, paper background, and characters exactly as drawn; do not turn it into a realistic image. The camera makes the move the composition suggests, such as a lateral tracking shot following the character, and the characters perform a simple, readable action: walking, turning, or pointing at something off-screen. Limited animation at a low frame rate, like a studio previs. Work-in-progress creative mood.
SFX: temp sounds for the action, such as footsteps or a door.
Ambient: simple temp piano music.
