Re-edit the reference video with speed ramps, keeping the same subject, framing, and visual style.
0–3 s: normal speed while the action builds.
3–5 s: extreme slow motion right at the peak moment, such as a jump, a spin, or a dive, with a slight camera push.
5–8 s: a sharp ramp back to normal speed through the end of the move.
Every speed change lands on a beat of the music. Energetic sports-video style for social media.
SFX: a deep whoosh going into slow motion and a sharp hit on the speed-up.
Ambient: an electronic track with a heavy bass line and breaks at each tempo change.
