Turn the reference image, a photo from a family gathering, wedding, or trip, into a memory shot on Super 8 film. Keep the people, their faces, their clothes, and the place exactly as in the photo; only add natural, subtle motion: hair and fabric move in the breeze, the light flickers, and people breathe and sway slightly. Handheld home-movie camera with a gentle sway and a slow zoom. Heavy grain, warm faded colors, vignette, light leaks, and rounded frame edges. Nostalgic, heartwarming mood.
SFX: the clatter of a home-movie projector.
Ambient: soft acoustic guitar and distant laughter.
