Remove [object to remove] from the reference video in every frame and rebuild the background behind it naturally, continuing the textures, lines, and shadows of the surroundings. Keep everything else exactly the same: people, camera movement, framing, lighting, and color. The patched area does not flicker, warp, or leave halos when the camera moves or someone walks in front of it. Professional, invisible post-production finish. Mood identical to the original.
SFX: also remove any sound coming from the removed object.
Ambient: keep the rest of the original audio.
