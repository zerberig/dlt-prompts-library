Convert the horizontal reference video into a 9:16 vertical video for social media. Reframe intelligently: the virtual camera follows the main subject with a smooth pan and keeps them in the upper third, leaving room at the bottom for captions. When the subject moves, the frame follows without abrupt jumps. If image is missing at the top or bottom, extend the background naturally and consistently with the scene. Keep the original content, light, color, and style. Mood true to the original video.
Ambient: keep the full original audio, in sync with the picture.
