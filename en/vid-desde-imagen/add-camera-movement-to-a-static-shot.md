Reinterpret the reference video, shot on a locked-off camera, as if it had been filmed on a dolly: a slow, steady push toward the main subject that ends in a medium close-up. Keep the original action, people, set, and lighting. As the camera moves in, the background falls out of focus with cinematic depth of field and the subject takes center stage; the composition follows the rule of thirds throughout the move. Understated cinematic style. Intimate, focused mood.
SFX: keep the sounds of the original action.
Ambient: add a soft string track under the original sound.
