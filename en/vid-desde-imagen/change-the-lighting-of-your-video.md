Relight the reference video so it looks shot in [lighting condition], for example golden sunset, night under streetlights, an overcast day, or dramatic studio light. Keep the same content, people, faces, movements, and camera move. Consistently adjust the light's direction and color, shadow length, reflections on surfaces, and the sky, and add logical light sources, such as lit windows or streetlights, when the new condition calls for them. Photorealistic, stable result with no flicker between frames. Mood that matches the new light.
SFX: keep the sounds of the original action.
Ambient: adapt the soundscape to the new time of day, such as night crickets or dawn birds.
