Starting from the reference image, create a 2.5D parallax effect: a slow lateral tracking move combined with a slight forward push, so the foreground moves faster than the background and the scene gains depth. Keep the composition, light, and style of the photo; only add natural life: grass and leaves sway in the wind, clouds drift slowly, and mist moves between the layers of the landscape. No people or new elements. Immersive, calm mood.
SFX: wind through the trees and a distant bird.
Ambient: a soft atmospheric pad with sustained strings.
