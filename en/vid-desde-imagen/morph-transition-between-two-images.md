Use the first reference image as the starting frame and the second as the ending frame. The camera pushes slowly toward a key shape in the first image, such as a moon, a plate, or a window, which flows into a similar shape in the second image through an organic morph, like a match cut. The camera then pulls back to reveal the full second scene. Keep the subjects and style of both images free of distortion outside the moment of transition. Creative, surprising mood.
SFX: a rising whoosh that ends in a soft shimmer as the transformation completes.
Ambient: a light electronic track with an accent on the moment of change.
