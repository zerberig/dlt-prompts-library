Transform the reference video, shot on a phone, into a shot with a cinematic finish. Keep exactly the same people, actions, overall framing, and duration. Stabilize the motion so it feels like a smooth gimbal, correct the exposure, recover detail in skies and shadows, and apply a film-style color grade with natural skin tones, slightly cool shadows, and warm highlights. Add subtle depth of field to separate the subject from the background and a fine film grain. Polished mood that stays true to the original moment.
SFX: clean up wind noise and microphone bumps.
Ambient: keep the original ambient sound, with balanced levels.
