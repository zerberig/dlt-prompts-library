Turn the reference video into an 8-second vertical teaser of its most impactful moments, keeping the original people, places, and visual style.
0–3 s: the most striking moment of the video as the hook, with a quick camera push.
3–6 s: two or three fast cuts of the key actions, each on a beat of the music.
6–8 s: a slow-motion closing shot that leaves empty space at the top for a title added in editing.
Clean cuts, consistent color, building pace. A mood of anticipation.
SFX: impact hits on every cut.
Ambient: an epic track that builds and ends abruptly.
