Use the three reference images, in order, as the three shots of a micro story. Keep the same character, wardrobe, color palette, and visual style from the images throughout.
0–3 s: wide shot based on the first image, with a slow forward tracking move that introduces the place and the character.
3–6 s: medium shot based on the second image; a handheld camera follows the character through the main action.
6–8 s: close-up based on the third image, with a gentle push toward their face at the closing moment.
Consistent cinematic style, clean cuts between shots.
SFX: footsteps and action sounds synced to each shot.
Ambient: a piano melody that builds toward the end.
