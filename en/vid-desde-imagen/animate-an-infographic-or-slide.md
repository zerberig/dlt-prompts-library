Animate the infographic or slide from the reference image as an explainer video. Locked-off camera with a slow zoom toward the center. Elements appear in reading order, top to bottom: first the title, then the icons with a small bounce, then the chart bars or lines grow to their final values, and at the end the key figure lights up. Keep all text, numbers, colors, and typography exactly as in the image, with no new text added. Clean corporate motion-graphics style. Clear, professional mood.
SFX: soft clicks and a subtle pop as each element appears.
Ambient: light corporate music with an upbeat pulse.
