Continue the reference video from its last frame, with no visible cut. Keep the same subject, wardrobe, set, lighting, color grade, and pacing. The camera carries on with the move it was making, at the same speed, and the action progresses logically: [next action]. Avoid lighting jumps, lens changes, or elements that weren't in the original. Style identical to the reference video. Continuous mood, as if it were the same take.
SFX: the sounds of the new action, with the same room acoustics.
Ambient: carry on the original ambient sound and music without interruption.
