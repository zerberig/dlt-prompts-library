Slow 180-degree camera orbit around the product from the reference image, which stays still on a pedestal in a minimal studio set. Keep the product's shape, colors, labels, and materials exactly as they appear in the image; add no new text or logos. A rim light sweeps along its edges as the camera turns, revealing reflections and textures, and the move ends on a slightly low-angle front view. Premium commercial style, clean gradient background, shallow depth of field. Elegant, aspirational mood.
SFX: a soft whoosh as the orbit starts and a deep hit as it stops.
Ambient: minimal electronic music with a slow pulse.
