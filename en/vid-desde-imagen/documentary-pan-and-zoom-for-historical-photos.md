Documentary-style camera move over the reference image: a slow left-to-right pan combined with a gentle zoom toward the most important detail in the photo. Keep the photograph exactly as it is, with its colors, grain, scratches, and paper texture; do not animate the people or alter their faces, only the camera moves. A light vignette darkens the edges and a subtle projector flicker adds an archival feel. Historical documentary style, unhurried pacing. Nostalgic, solemn mood.
SFX: the faint whir of an old projector.
