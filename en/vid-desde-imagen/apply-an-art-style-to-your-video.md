Reimagine the entire reference video in [desired style], for example watercolor, stylized 3D animation with soft lighting, or hand-drawn anime. Keep the same camera movement, composition, pacing, and every subject's actions, frame by frame. Apply the style consistently across the whole clip with no flicker between frames: the same strokes, palette, and texture from start to finish. People keep their recognizable features adapted to the style, without changing their identity. Artistic, cohesive mood.
SFX: keep the original sound effects.
Ambient: soft music that fits the chosen style.
