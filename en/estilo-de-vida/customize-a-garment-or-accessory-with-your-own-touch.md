Context: I want to customize [garment or accessory] so it reflects my style, which I'd describe as [your style]. I identify with [colors, symbols, or interests that represent you], and my crafting level is [your level].
Goal: Turn an ordinary piece into something that feels like mine, without ruining it.
Deliverable: 6 customization ideas using different techniques (for example, embroidery, fabric paint, patches, dyeing, appliqués, or swapping buttons and hardware), each with materials, difficulty, time, and how to care for it in the wash. Then step-by-step instructions for the idea that best matches my style.
Criteria: Order the ideas from least to most permanent. Suggest testing first on a hidden spot or scrap fabric. The ideas should work together so the piece doesn't look overloaded if I combine several.
Format: A table with the columns Idea, Technique, Materials, Difficulty, Time, and Care; then the numbered steps.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
