Context: I want to publish an infographic about [cooking method] (for example, air frying, sous vide, fermentation, or smoking) for [target audience].
Goal: Someone who has never tried it should understand in one minute what it's good for, how to do it right, and what to watch out for, and the information should be trustworthy.
Deliverable: The full infographic copy split into 5 or 6 panels: what it is, real benefits, basic technique in steps, common mistakes, risks and safety measures, and one key takeaway to close. For each panel, also suggest an icon or visual element.
Criteria: Short lines that fit a design (25 words max per block). Present health benefits carefully and without hype; if you mention temperatures, times, or nutrition data, rely on recognized food-safety references. Flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate.
Format: A title of 8 words max, then each panel with its heading, copy, and suggested icon, and at the end a list of the sources you used.
