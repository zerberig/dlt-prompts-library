Context: I work at [type of workplace] (office, store, workshop, hybrid work) with about [number of people] people. My role is [your role] and I can influence [what you can decide or propose].
Goal: Reduce my workplace's carbon footprint with actions I can drive from my position and that my team will get behind.
Deliverable:
1. The main likely emission sources in a workplace like mine (energy, commuting, travel, purchasing, waste, digital equipment).
2. 10 prioritized actions, separating what I can do myself, what I can propose to my team, and what needs leadership.
3. How to track progress with simple data (power bills, commute surveys, kilos of waste).
4. A short message to pitch the initiative to my team or manager.
Criteria: Prioritize by impact and ease, and be realistic about cost. Flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate.
Format: The actions in a table with the columns Action, Who drives it, Impact (high, medium, or low), Effort, and How to measure it; everything else in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
