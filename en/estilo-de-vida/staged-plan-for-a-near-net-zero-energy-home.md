Context: I live in [location and climate] in a home with these characteristics: [home characteristics] (size, age, materials, orientation). My approximate current usage is [electricity and gas usage], and my budget for upgrades is [available budget].
Goal: I want to cut my energy use as far as possible and move toward a net-zero home, investing first where the return is highest.
Deliverable:
1) Diagnosis: where a home like mine loses or wastes the most energy and how I can check it myself.
2) A staged plan: passive and low-cost improvements first (sealing, insulation, shading, lighting), then efficient equipment, and finally renewable generation (solar panels, solar water heater).
3) For each measure: estimated cost, estimated annual savings, payback period, and whether I can do it myself or need a professional.
4) Recommended sustainable materials for each upgrade.
Criteria: Tailor the recommendations to my climate. If you have web search, check current prices and the incentives or financing programs available in my country, and flag any figure you can't back up with a source as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections and a summary table with the columns Measure, Stage, Cost, Annual savings, Payback, and Who does it.
For electrical, structural, or solar installation work, recommend that I hire a certified professional.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
