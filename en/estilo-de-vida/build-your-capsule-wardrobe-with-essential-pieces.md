Context: I want to build a capsule wardrobe of [number of pieces] pieces for [season or climate]. My day-to-day is [your lifestyle] (office, remote work, travel, young kids, etc.) and my style is [your style].
Goal: Dress well with fewer clothes, decide quickly every morning, and shop with intention.
Deliverable:
1. A palette of 3 base colors and 2 accent colors, with the reason for each.
2. The exact list of pieces by category (tops, bottoms, layers, shoes, and accessories), with suggested color and cut.
3. 12 outfits that show how far the capsule goes.
4. What to keep from my current wardrobe and what to buy first, if I share what I own.
Criteria: Every piece should go with at least 3 others. Favor comfortable fabrics suited to my climate. Fit the count per category to my real lifestyle, not a fixed formula.
Format: The piece list in a table with the columns Category, Item, Color, and Goes with; the outfits as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
