Context: I want to strengthen [muscle group] (for example, glutes, abs, back, or arms). My level is [your training level], and I have [available equipment].
Goal: A 40 to 50 minute session that works that group from different angles, that I can repeat 1 or 2 times a week, and that complements the rest of my training.
Deliverable: A targeted warm-up, 5 or 6 exercises (from compound to isolation), a cool-down with stretches, and how to progress over the next 4 weeks.
Criteria: For each exercise, state which part of the muscle it emphasizes, the main technique cue, the most common mistake, and one easier and one harder variation. Balance the work to avoid imbalances (for example, if it's abs, include the lower back).
Format: A table with the columns Exercise, Sets x reps, Rest, Technique cue, and Easier or harder variation. Then the progression as a list.
If I feel joint pain or have an injury, recommend that I see a physical therapist before continuing.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
