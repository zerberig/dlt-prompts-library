Context: These are the clothes I own and wear: [list of pieces with colors]. My typical week includes [occasions in your week] (office, working from home, going out, weekends) and my style is [your style].
Goal: Get more combinations out of what I already own before buying anything new.
Deliverable: 10 outfits built only from my pieces, each with the occasion it suits and one detail that makes it look intentional (how to tuck, roll sleeves, layer). Then the 3 pieces or accessories that would unlock the most new combinations if I bought them, and why.
Criteria: Explain the rule behind each outfit (color contrast, texture mixing, proportions) so I can apply it on my own. Prioritize the pieces I rarely wear.
Format: A table with the columns Outfit, Pieces, Occasion, and Why it works; then the suggested shopping list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
