Context: I run a business selling [type of handmade products] and I'm launching [new product or collection]. My audience is [target audience], and I mainly post on [social platforms you use].
Goal: Build anticipation before launch, drive sales in the first week, and spark community content that keeps going afterward.
Deliverable:
1) The core launch message and 3 content pillars (making process, the story behind it, real-life use).
2) A 3-phase calendar: 2 weeks of teasers, launch week, and 2 weeks of follow-up, with format (reel, carousel, story, live), idea, and call to action for each post.
3) 2 user-generated content activations (for example, a challenge or photo contest) with clear rules.
4) 5 sample captions for the key posts.
5) Metrics to track in each phase.
Criteria: Show the real making process, because it builds trust in handmade products. Contests must follow each platform's promotion rules; if you have web search, check them and flag anything you can't confirm. Don't use invented reviews or sales numbers.
Format: Numbered sections; the calendar as a table with the columns Relative date, Platform, Format, Idea, and Call to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
