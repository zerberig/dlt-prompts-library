Context: I'm hosting a [occasion or theme] dinner for [number of guests] people. Some guests have these restrictions: [guests' dietary restrictions]. My cooking level is [your cooking level].
Goal: I want a memorable menu that I can prepare almost entirely ahead of time, so I can enjoy the evening with my guests instead of spending it in the kitchen.
Deliverable: 2 menu options with a starter or appetizer, main course, 2 sides, dessert, and drink pairings (including a non-alcoholic option). For the option I pick: a shopping list with quantities and a prep timeline from 2 days before until serving.
Criteria: Dishes that complement each other in flavor, color, and texture, and that accommodate the restrictions without a separate menu. At least two courses should be doable a day ahead.
Format: Each menu as a list with a one-line description of each dish; the timeline as a table with the columns When, Task, and Duration.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
