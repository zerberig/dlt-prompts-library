Context: I make [your product] for [target audience]. Right now it's made with [current materials and process] and sold like this: [packaging and sales channel].
Goal: I want to improve the product so it helps my customers live more sustainably, and communicate that without greenwashing.
Deliverable: 1) A quick life-cycle review: materials, manufacturing, packaging, shipping, use, and end of life. 2) 8 possible improvements ranked by impact and difficulty. 3) For the top 3, how to implement them and how they affect cost. 4) How to communicate the changes to customers with verifiable claims.
Criteria: Realistic proposals for a business my size. Avoid vague claims like "eco-friendly" or "planet-friendly" without data to back them up, because they erode trust and can break advertising rules. Flag any impact figure you can't back up with a source as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections; the improvements in a matrix with the columns Improvement, Life-cycle stage, Impact, Difficulty, and Cost.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
