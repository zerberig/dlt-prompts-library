Deliverable:
1. The 6-8 plants best suited to my space and climate, with planting season and companion-planting pairs that help each other.
2. Soil or potting mix preparation and a composting system that fits my space.
3. An efficient watering strategy (timing, mulching, rainwater capture if it applies).
4. Natural control for the pests most likely in my area.
5. A week-by-week calendar for the first 3 months.
Criteria: Favor cheap, easy-to-find options. If you have web search, check planting seasons for my region.
Format: Plants in a table with the columns Plant, When to sow, Light, Watering, and Good companions; everything else in sections with bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
