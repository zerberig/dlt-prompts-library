Context: I want to keep training, but I have [injury or physical limitation]. My doctor's or physical therapist's guidance is: [professional guidance you've received]. My current routine includes [exercises in your routine].
Goal: Stay active and keep my progress without making the limitation worse.
Deliverable: For each exercise in my routine: whether to keep, modify, or replace it; the specific modification or replacement; and why it's friendlier for my case. Then the warning signs that mean I should stop an exercise.
Criteria: Offer a progression of variations from least to most demanding for each movement. Put the professional guidance I shared ahead of any general advice. Don't diagnose or suggest treatments.
Format: A table with the columns Original exercise, Decision, Recommended variation, Why, and How to progress; then the warning signs as a list.
Remind me to confirm these changes with my doctor or physical therapist before applying them, especially if I feel pain.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
