Context: I run [your business] and want to launch or redesign [product] for [target audience]. I produce roughly [production volume] and my per-unit budget is [target cost per unit].
Goal: A product that genuinely cuts waste and has a positive social impact, and that I can talk about without overstating it.
Deliverable:
1. 3 distinct design concepts, each with proposed materials, manufacturing process, and packaging.
2. For the recommended concept: a short life-cycle analysis (raw materials, production, use, end of life) and where the biggest impacts are.
3. Social-responsibility practices that apply to my supply chain.
4. Honest marketing messages about the environmental benefits and certifications worth pursuing.
Criteria: Avoid vague claims like "100% eco-friendly", because they're greenwashing and can create legal trouble; every benefit should be verifiable. Consider suppliers and options available in my region. Flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate.
Format: The concepts in a comparison table (Materials, Estimated cost, Waste reduction, Risks); everything else in numbered sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
