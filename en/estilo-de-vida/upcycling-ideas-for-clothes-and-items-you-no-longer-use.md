Context: I have these clothes and items I no longer use: [clothes and items you want to transform]. I have [tools or sewing machine available] and my experience is [your level].
Goal: Give them a second life as something useful or decorative instead of throwing them out.
Deliverable: 8 upcycling projects based on my list (for example, T-shirts into bags or rugs, jeans into organizers, an old chair reupholstered or painted). For each: which item it uses, what it becomes, main technique (sewing, painting, reupholstering, new hardware), difficulty, time, and key steps. Fully develop the project with the best impact-to-ease ratio.
Criteria: Say which finishing materials are safe for indoor use and how to properly prep the surface or fabric, since that's what most determines whether the result lasts. Suggest what to do with whatever can't be transformed (donate, textile recycling).
Format: The projects in a table with the columns Item, Becomes, Technique, Difficulty, and Time; then the detailed project with numbered steps.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
