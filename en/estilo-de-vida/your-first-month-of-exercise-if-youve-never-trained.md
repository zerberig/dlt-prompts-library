Context: I've never had an exercise routine. I'm [age] years old and want to start because [your motivation]. What intimidates me most is [what's holding you back].
Goal: I want a first month that makes me feel capable and leaves me wanting more, not sore or frustrated.
Deliverable: A 4-week plan with 3 sessions a week of 20 to 30 minutes, combining basic strength, light cardio, mobility, and one fun activity (dancing, a walking challenge, a game).
Criteria: Every session has a warm-up, a main block, and a cool-down. Explain exercises in plain language with the single most important technique cue. Progress gently week to week and give an easy version of each exercise for low-energy days. Each week should include a small win to celebrate.
Format: A table per week (day, exercises, sets or time, rest) and a list of signs that tell me when to raise or lower the intensity.
Recommend a medical checkup before I start if I have any health condition.
