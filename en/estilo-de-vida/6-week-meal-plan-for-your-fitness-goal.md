Context: My goal is [your fitness goal] (for example, losing fat, building muscle, or performing better). I train [training frequency and type]. I have these restrictions or allergies: [dietary restrictions], and these preferences: [foods you like and dislike].
Goal: I want a plan I can follow for 6 weeks without getting bored, that supports my training and recovery, and that teaches me to build my own meals afterward.
Deliverable:
1) A guide to building my plate on training days and rest days.
2) A 2-week template menu that rotates across the 6 weeks, with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and 1 or 2 snacks.
3) Adjustments for weeks 3-4 and 5-6 based on how I feel (energy, hunger, performance).
4) 5 quick base recipes and a list of swaps.
Criteria: Built on real, accessible foods, with enough protein at every meal and no food groups cut out except for my restrictions. Give approximate portions in everyday measures, not just grams. Don't promise results in pounds, kilos, or timelines; explain which signs show the plan is working.
Format: Numbered sections, the menu as a table (days as rows, meals as columns), and the swaps as a list.
This plan is a guide only: remind me to review it with a registered dietitian, especially if I have a medical condition.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
