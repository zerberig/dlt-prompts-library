Context: My style is [your style] and the colors I wear most are [main colors in your wardrobe]. The accessories I already own are: [accessories you already own]. My total budget is [budget in your currency].
Goal: Have a small set of accessories that elevates almost any outfit, instead of buying one-off pieces that don't go together.
Deliverable: A collection of 8-10 accessories (bag, belt, jewelry, sunglasses, scarf, watch, key shoes, etc.), listing for each: exact characteristics (color, material, size), why it fits my style, and what kind of outfit I'd wear it with. Then the order in which to buy them so each purchase adds the most combinations.
Criteria: Build on what I already own and don't duplicate roles. Favor durable pieces over passing trends, except for one statement piece with more personality.
Format: A table with the columns Accessory, Characteristics, Why, and Wear it with; then the numbered buying-priority list with an estimated cost for each piece.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
