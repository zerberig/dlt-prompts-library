Context: I have a lot of [star ingredient] (bought in bulk, in season, or from my garden) and want to use it over a week for [number of people] people. My preferences or restrictions are: [preferences or restrictions].
Goal: Use it in every meal without getting bored or wasting any.
Deliverable:
1. A 7-day plan (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) where the ingredient shows up in different ways: raw, roasted, in a sauce, in a dessert, preserved, etc.
2. The 5 standout recipes in full, with servings, ingredients, steps, and time.
3. Approximate nutrition per serving for those 5 recipes (calories, protein, carbs, fat).
4. How to store the ingredient so it lasts all week.
Criteria: Balance each day with other food groups so it doesn't feel repetitive. Flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate. If I have a health condition, recommend I validate the plan with a dietitian.
Format: The plan in a table with the columns Day, Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner; recipes in sections; nutrition in a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
