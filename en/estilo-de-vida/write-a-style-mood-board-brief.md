Context: I want to put together a mood board around [theme or concept] for [what the mood board is for] (for example, planning a season's outfits, the look of an event, or a business's image). The words that describe the feeling I'm after are [3 to 5 keywords].
Goal: Have a cohesive visual guide I can build in my favorite design tool or on paper and use as a reference when making decisions.
Deliverable: 1) The concept in 2 or 3 sentences. 2) A palette of 5 or 6 colors with name, HEX code, and role (base, accent, neutral). 3) Textures, materials, and patterns. 4) 10 image descriptions to search for or generate with AI (clothing, objects, spaces, details). 5) Suggested typography or graphic style, if relevant. 6) What's out of bounds for the concept.
Criteria: Every element should fit with the others and with the keywords. Describe the aesthetic through techniques and materials, without naming brands or designers.
Format: Numbered sections; the palette in a table with the columns Color, HEX, and Use.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
