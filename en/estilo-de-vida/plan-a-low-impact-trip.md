Context: I'm traveling to [destination] for [trip length], from [home city]. It's a [type of trip] trip (family, adventure, work, relaxation).
Goal: Enjoy the trip while reducing my environmental impact and leaving more benefit with the local community.
Deliverable:
1. A comparison of transport options for getting there and getting around, with their relative impact.
2. What to look for in responsible lodging and what to ask before booking.
3. Activities and spending that support local businesses and communities, and practices to avoid (for example, attractions with captive animals or overcrowded fragile areas).
4. A packing list for producing less waste.
5. Environmental rules or permits at the destination I should know about.
Criteria: Suggest realistic changes, not a perfect trip. If you have web search, check the destination's current regulations. Flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets; the transport comparison in a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
