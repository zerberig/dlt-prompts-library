Goal: Have a core pantry that lets me make many dishes from that cuisine by buying only fresh ingredients each week.
Deliverable: A list of essential ingredients divided into dry goods and grains, sauces and condiments, spices, and refrigerated or fresh items worth always having. Then 5 dishes I could make with that pantry plus 1 to 3 fresh ingredients.
Criteria: Prioritize the ingredients that show up in the most recipes. For each one, state its shelf life, how to store it, and an easy-to-find local substitute if it's hard to source.
Format: A table with the columns Ingredient, What it's used for, Shelf life and storage, and Local substitute. Then the list of dishes.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
