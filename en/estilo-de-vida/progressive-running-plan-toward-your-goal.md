Context: Right now I can run [your current level] (for example, 20 minutes nonstop or 5K in 35 minutes). I want to reach [your goal] (finish a 5K, cut my 10K time, run a half marathon) in [weeks available] weeks, and I can run [days per week] days a week.
Goal: Reach my goal by building load gradually, without getting injured or burning out.
Deliverable:
1. A week-by-week plan with each session: type (easy, intervals, tempo, long run, or rest), duration or distance, and intensity.
2. Scheduled deload weeks.
3. How to gauge intensity without devices (talk test, perceived effort) and, if I have them, with heart rate or pace.
4. Supporting strength work in 2 short sessions a week.
5. What to do if I miss a week or feel pain.
Criteria: Increase weekly volume moderately and explain the rule you're using. Adjust the plan if the goal isn't realistic for the time available, and tell me. Include a note recommending a medical checkup before starting if I have a health condition or haven't exercised in a while.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Day, Session, Duration or distance, and Intensity; everything else in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
