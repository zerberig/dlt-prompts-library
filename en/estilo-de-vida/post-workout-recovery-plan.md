Context: I do [type of training] [weekly frequency] times a week and afterward I usually feel [how you feel after training] (next-day soreness, fatigue, stiffness).
Goal: Recover better between sessions so I perform better and lower my injury risk.
Deliverable:
1. A 10-minute post-workout cool-down.
2. What to do on active-rest days.
3. Recovery techniques (foam rolling, stretching, contrast temperature, massage) with what the evidence says about each.
4. The sleep, hydration, and nutrition habits that matter most for recovery.
5. How to tell normal muscle soreness from a sign of injury.
Criteria: Be honest about which techniques have strong support and which have little; flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate. Include a note recommending I see a doctor or physical therapist if pain is sharp, lasts more than a few days, or limits movement.
Format: The techniques in a table with the columns Technique, How to do it, When, and Strength of evidence; everything else as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
