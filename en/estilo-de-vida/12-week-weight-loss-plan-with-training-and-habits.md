Context: I'm [age] years old, my current activity level is [current activity level], and I want to lose weight. I can train [days and minutes available], and I have access to [equipment or training location].
Goal: I want a realistic plan I can stick with for 12 weeks and that keeps working afterward, not a quick fix.
Deliverable:
1) A training plan in 3 four-week phases combining strength and cardio, with clear progression between phases.
2) Daily activity targets outside of workouts (steps, movement breaks) that increase gradually.
3) 3 to 5 supporting habits (sleep, hydration, meal planning) with a simple way to track each one.
4) Progress markers beyond the scale: measurements, strength, energy, and how my clothes fit.
Criteria: Favor consistency over intensity: a plan I can follow 80% of the weeks beats a perfect one I abandon. Include a deload week and what to do if I miss days. Don't promise specific pounds or kilos; focus on the process.
Format: A weekly table for each phase (day, session, exercises, sets and reps or duration), followed by sections on daily activity, habits, and progress markers.
Recommend that I see a doctor before starting, especially if I have a health condition, and a registered dietitian for the nutrition side.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
