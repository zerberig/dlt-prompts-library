Surreal illustration of a calm sea at sunset, so still it works like a mirror. Three rocky islands float above the water, with roots and small waterfalls hanging from them and pouring up toward the sky; on the central island grows a bare tree, and from its branches hangs a soft pocket watch melting like wax. Another clock drapes over the edge of a stone staircase that rises out of the sea and leads nowhere. Digital painting with a smooth oil finish, long crisp shadows, and a palette of warm sand, peach, powder blue, and lavender. Quiet, dreamlike mood, low horizon with plenty of sky, no text.
