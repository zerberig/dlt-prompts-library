UI element kit for an arcade video game, laid out as an asset sheet on a plain black background. It includes health and energy bars, three rounded buttons in normal, pressed, and disabled states, power-up icons (heart, shield, lightning bolt, star, and coin), a score counter with large digits, a circular minimap, and a reward chest. Glossy, bold 2D style with thick outlines, candy-like gradients, specular highlights, and saturated colors: red, yellow, cyan, and lime green. Each element is separate and aligned in rows, ready to cut out.
