Concept art of a futuristic city in the middle of the desert at noon, seen from the top of a dune. Stepped towers of adobe and solar glass rise like giant termite mounds, linked by shaded bridges and fabric sails that cast stripes of shadow; the rooftops hold vertical gardens and wind turbines. A sandstorm approaches on the horizon while a caravan of six-wheeled vehicles heads toward the main gate. Digital matte painting with harsh sunlight and heat haze, in a palette of ochre, sienna, sand, and teal. An arid, imposing, resilient mood.
