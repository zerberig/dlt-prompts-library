Ink-and-watercolor urban sketch of a busy downtown avenue on a rainy afternoon. Tall buildings from different eras line the street in one-point perspective, with lit windows and signs with no legible text. Pedestrians with colorful umbrellas cross a zebra crossing, and car headlights reflect on the wet asphalt in long streaks of yellow and red. Quick, slightly loose ink lines, blue-gray washes with warm color accents. Vibrant, melancholic mood.
