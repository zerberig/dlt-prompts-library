Eerie watercolor of an abandoned city avenue at nightfall. Empty buildings with broken windows, dark signs, and a rusted streetcar stopped on its tracks fade into thick fog. Translucent ghostly figures walk through the mist, painted only with the white of the paper and soft edges, some with a pale green glow. A single streetlight flickers with yellowish light. Muted palette of greenish gray, sepia, and slate blue, with vertical paint drips that heighten the sense of abandonment. Cold, silent, spectral mood.
