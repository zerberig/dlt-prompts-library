Serene watercolor of a calm lake painted from the height of the water surface at dawn. In the foreground, round lily pads and three open blossoms in white and pale pink; a small green frog with dewdrops on its back rests on one of the pads. Behind, a low milky mist covers the water, and on the far shore reeds and willows appear in bluish grays. The water is painted with soft horizontal washes and blurred vertical reflections. Palette of jade greens, pinks, and lavender. Still, fresh mood, no text.
