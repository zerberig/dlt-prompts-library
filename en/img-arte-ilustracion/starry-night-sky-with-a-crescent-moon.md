Vertical watercolor of a night sky that fills almost the entire frame, with a wet gradient from deep indigo at the top to violet blue and a faint turquoise glow near the horizon. A thin, luminous crescent moon in the upper right with a soft halo. Hundreds of stars made with white gouache splatters, some larger ones with four-pointed sparkles, and a faint band of the Milky Way running diagonally. At the base, the black silhouette of a pine forest with a small lake reflecting the moon. Serene, magical mood. No text.
