Abstract geometric painting on bone-white cotton paper, conceived as a visual musical score. A large vermilion circle rests in the left third; around it, concentric cobalt blue arcs, a mustard yellow triangle, small black squares that work like notes, and fine graphite lines that cross the composition like staves. Each shape balances another in weight and color, with plenty of empty space to let it breathe. Matte gouache with crisp edges, slight paper texture visible, no gradients. Harmonious, rhythmic, serene mood, no text.
