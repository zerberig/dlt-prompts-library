Vertical watercolor of a scarlet macaw perched on a jacaranda branch covered in lilac blossoms, with a few petals falling. The bird looks back over its shoulder in profile; its red, yellow, and blue feathers are built up in overlapping transparent layers that reveal each feather's texture, and the curved beak has a white highlight left as bare paper. The background is a soft sky-blue wash fading into the white of the page. Loose brushwork on the flowers, precise detail on the eye and talons. Bright tropical mood. No text.
