Abstract fluid acrylic artwork poured in a spiral on a square canvas, seen straight on. A vortex of color swirls from the center with bands of magenta, orange, lemon yellow, electric blue, and black that stretch and curve as if in motion. Organic cells and bubbles appear within the bands, and the textures contrast: smooth resin-gloss areas next to matte impasto ridges and fine gold metallic veins. Even studio lighting that reveals the relief. Intense energy and a strong sense of motion.
