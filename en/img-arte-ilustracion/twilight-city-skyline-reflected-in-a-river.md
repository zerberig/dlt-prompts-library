Panoramic skyline of a large city at dusk, seen from the far bank of a wide river. Glass skyscrapers of different heights stand out against a sky shifting from peach to indigo, with the first windows lit and an illuminated arched bridge. The whole skyline is mirrored in the water as trembling vertical streaks. Wet-on-wet watercolor: buildings as silhouettes of blue and violet washes that melt into the sky, with lights added as small dots of white and yellow gouache. Elongated composition with a low horizon. Calm, urban mood.
