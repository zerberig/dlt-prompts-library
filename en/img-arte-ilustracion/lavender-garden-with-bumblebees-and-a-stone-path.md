Watercolor of a cottage garden in full bloom. An irregular stone path winds between beds of lavender, pink foxgloves, daisies, and poppies, toward a small stream with a wooden bridge in the background. In the foreground, three chubby bumblebees buzz over the lavender, their wings barely suggested with fine strokes. Loose floral brushwork, blending patches of color, a palette of violet, pink, fresh green, and butter yellow, with mid-morning light. Cheerful, lively mood.
