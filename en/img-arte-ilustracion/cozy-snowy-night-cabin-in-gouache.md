Interior of a wooden cabin in the forest on a winter night. A stone fireplace with a crackling fire lights up a plaid wool armchair with a knitted blanket, a steaming mug on a side table, and a cat asleep on a thick rug. Through the large window, snow falls on bluish pines and a frozen lake. Gouache illustration with visible matte brushstrokes, contrast between the orange firelight and the cold blue outside, and textures of wood and knitwear. Cozy, quiet mood.
