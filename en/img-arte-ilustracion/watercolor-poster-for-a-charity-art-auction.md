Vertical poster for a charity art auction, on textured white paper. In the upper half, an explosion of watercolor blooms in turquoise, coral, and mustard yellow melting into one another, with three brushes and a stained palette peeking in from the corner, as if the piece had just been painted. In the center, the text "ART AUCTION" in elegant black serif letters, and below it, in a smaller line of brush calligraphy, "for charity". Lower half left as clean white space to add the date and venue later. Creative, warm, community mood. No additional text.
