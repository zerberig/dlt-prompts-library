Square abstract watercolor composition inspired by the inside of a seashell. A large spiral of curved color bands opens from the center outward, in coral, sand, pearly pink, pearl gray, and a touch of turquoise. The bands have textures made with salt on wet paint, pigment granulation, and hard edges where the water dried. Fine wavy white lines recall the shell's ridges. Plenty of white paper around it. Calm, organic, coastal mood, ideal as decorative art. No figurative elements, no text.
