Panoramic watercolor landscape of a sunset over a desert of dunes. The sky is a broad, continuous gradient from golden yellow at the horizon to orange, coral pink, and lavender at the top, painted wet-on-wet with no hard edges. The foreground dunes have soft crests with shadows in violet and burnt ochre. In the distance, a caravan of camels and travelers in dark silhouette moves in a line along a ridge, casting long shadows. The half-hidden sun is reserved as white paper. Warm, still, contemplative mood.
