Ink-and-watercolor urban sketch, at eye level, of a Mexican open-air street market on a narrow street on a Saturday morning. Hot pink, blue, and orange awnings line both sides; beneath them, stalls of stacked fruit, dried chiles, marigold flowers, and clay pots. A vendor weighs tomatoes on a hanging scale while a shopper with a woven market bag haggles, and a boy eats corn on the cob. Overhead, strings of colorful papel picado cross the street. Quick, loose ink lines with bright washes spilling past the outlines and blue-violet shadows. Bustling, cheerful mood. Signs with no legible text.
