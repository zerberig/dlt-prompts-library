Vibrant watercolor of a gray wolf standing on a snowy rock, muzzle raised, howling at a huge full moon. Behind it, curtains of aurora borealis in emerald green, turquoise, and violet ripple over a mountain range and a forest of pines silhouetted in indigo blue. The moon is reserved as pure white paper; the aurora is painted wet-on-wet with blending edges, and the wolf's fur with fine strokes in gray and silver. White spatter for stars. Lonely, magical mood.
