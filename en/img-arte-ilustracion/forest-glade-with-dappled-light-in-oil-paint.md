Oil landscape painting of a clearing inside an old oak and beech forest. In the center, a fallen moss-covered log crosses a floor of ferns and dry leaves; sunlight filters through the canopy and leaves bright, flickering patches on the moss, while the background fades into blue-green shadows. Visible impasto brushstrokes in the lit areas, transparent glazes in the shadows, a palette of deep greens, golden ochres, and touches of lemon yellow. Wide horizontal composition with the sunlit clearing in the central third. Serene mid-morning mood, no figures and no text.
