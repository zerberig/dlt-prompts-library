Interior of a quiet stone chapel at dusk, seen from the central aisle. High on the wall, a circular stained-glass rose window with a celestial design: a golden sun at the center, a crescent moon, planets, and constellations in cobalt blue, violet, and amber, separated by thick dark lead lines. Rays of light pass through the glass and leave pools of color on the flagstone floor and wooden pews. Transparent watercolor with ink for the lead lines and reserved white paper for the glow. Serene, sacred atmosphere.
