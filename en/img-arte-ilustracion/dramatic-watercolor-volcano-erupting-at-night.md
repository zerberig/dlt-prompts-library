Volcano erupting at night, seen from a distant valley: the dark cone shoots fountains of glowing orange lava and a column of ash that mushrooms into a deep blue sky. Lava rivers run down the slope and their reflection turns a foreground lake red, ringed by silhouetted pines. Dramatic wet-on-wet watercolor: the orange bleeds into the gray ash, with pigment splatters like sparks and white reserved for the brightest points. Strong contrast between fire and night. Awe-inspiring mood.
