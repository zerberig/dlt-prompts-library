Half-length watercolor portrait of a boy around eight years old, with messy hair, freckles across his nose, and a lopsided grin showing a missing tooth. Skin painted in warm transparent layers, with highlights in the eyes left as white paper; background with a loose sky-blue wash and yellow splatters. Fun, endearing mood. No text.
