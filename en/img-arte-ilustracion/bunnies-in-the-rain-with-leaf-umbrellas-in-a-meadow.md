Whimsical watercolor for a children's book: a family of four fluffy rabbits crosses a meadow of wildflowers during a spring rain. Each one shelters under a large leaf like an umbrella; the smallest one trails behind with a clover leaf that is far too small and soaked ears, hopping over a puddle. Raindrops drawn with fine lines, flowers in pink, yellow, and lilac dotted across the meadow, and a soft gray sky with a ray of sunshine peeking through the corner. Colored pencil outlines, pastel colors. Sweet, funny mood, no text.
