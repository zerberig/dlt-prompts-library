Dynamic watercolor of a major city avenue at night in the rain, in diagonal perspective with a vanishing point on the right. Yellow taxis and buses leave red and white light trails that reflect on the wet asphalt; pedestrians with colorful umbrellas hurry across the crosswalk. Tall buildings only suggested with slate-blue washes and windows lit in amber. Wet-on-wet technique with vertical drips, energetic splatters, and quick black ink strokes in the foreground. Vibrant, fast-paced mood. Signs with no legible text.
