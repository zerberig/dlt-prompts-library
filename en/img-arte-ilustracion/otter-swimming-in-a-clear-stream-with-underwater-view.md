Split-view watercolor of a crystal-clear stream: the top half shows the surface with fern-lined banks and sunlight, the bottom half the transparent water. A playful otter floats on its back at the waterline holding a pebble between its paws, while silver bubbles rise from its submerged body. On the bottom, rounded stones in ochre and gray, and small fish. Turquoise and light-green washes, reflections left as white paper, and wavy lines of light dancing on the stones. Joyful, fresh mood.
