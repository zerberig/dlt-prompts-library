Surreal landscape in a peach-colored sand desert under an aqua-green sky: a gold pocket watch melts over the pad of a prickly pear cactus and drips into a puddle where the numbers float like leaves. In the distance, a staircase climbs into nothing and two more clocks curve over smooth rocks. Long shadows and a slightly impossible perspective. Surreal watercolor with soft transitions, transparent violet shadows, and fine pen details on the clock mechanisms. Silent, strange, dreamlike mood.
