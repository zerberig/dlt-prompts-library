Whimsical close-up watercolor of a raccoon peeking out from behind a large fern frond covered in dewdrops. Only its head, one paw pushing the leaf aside, and its black mask are visible; its eyes sparkle with curiosity and its mouth forms a mischievous grin. The gray and cream fur is painted with dry brushstrokes that suggest texture, the ferns in fresh greens with crisp edges, and the background is a blurred forest in greens and golden morning light. Dewdrops highlighted with white gouache. Playful, sweet mood, no text.
