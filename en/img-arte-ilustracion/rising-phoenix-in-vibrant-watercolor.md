A phoenix rises vertically from a swirl of embers, wings spread wide and a long tail of feathers that turn into flames. Its body shifts from deep crimson at the base to orange, gold, and finally an almost white yellow at the head. Vibrant watercolor on white paper: saturated pigments mixing wet-into-wet, splatters and droplets flying off the wingtips like sparks, and minimal ink lines on the eyes and beak. White background with a soft, warm halo. Mood of rebirth and energy.
