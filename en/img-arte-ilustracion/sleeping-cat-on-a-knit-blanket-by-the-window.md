Loose watercolor of an orange tabby cat asleep and curled up on a chunky knit blanket on a cushioned window seat. Outside, raindrops slide down the glass in blue-gray tones. The cat's fur is painted with a few brushstrokes that melt into the white paper at the edges, its tail covering its nose and paws tucked in. The blanket has knitted cables in cream and mustard. Warm indoor light against the cold outside. Cozy, quiet mood.
