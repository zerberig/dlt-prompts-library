Abstract watercolor interpreting the movement of rushing water. Curved bands of turquoise, ultramarine, and magenta weave from left to right like crossing currents, blending at the edges with a wet-on-wet technique. Pigment splatters and loose drops follow the direction of the flow, salt textures create small pale stars within the color, and a few fine lines of gold ink trace the path of the main current. Visible rough-grain paper, reserved white areas for breathing room. Fluid, energetic, fresh mood, no figures and no text.
