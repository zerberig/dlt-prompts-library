Vertical ink-and-watercolor painting of a cherry tree in full bloom leaning over a narrow river, beside a small arched wooden bridge. The trunk and branches are drawn with expressive black ink brushstrokes with varied pressure, in the manner of traditional brush painting. The blossoms are loose dabs of pale pink and white, and dozens of petals fly on the wind and float on the water. Plenty of white space and a soft mist in the background. Poetic, fleeting mood.
