Expressive half-length portrait of a saxophonist mid-solo, leaning back with eyes closed and cheeks puffed out. Acrylic painting in a loose style with thick, visible brushstrokes, palette-knife impasto, and intense color: the saxophone in bright yellows and oranges, the skin in ochres and violets, and a background bursting with electric blue, magenta, and turquoise. Curved strokes of color pour from the instrument's bell to represent the sound and blend into the background. Warm overhead stage light. Passionate, rhythmic mood full of energy. No text.
