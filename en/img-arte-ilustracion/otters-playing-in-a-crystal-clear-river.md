Top-down watercolor of a clear, shallow river, with round colorful pebbles on the bottom and dancing light patterns over them. Two river otters swim in a circle around each other, one floating on its back holding a pebble on its belly and the other doing a somersault, leaving trails of bubbles and splashes on the surface. Glossy, wet brown fur with highlights left as white paper. Water painted in transparent washes of turquoise and light green, ripples marked with dry brush. Cheerful, fresh, mischievous mood.
