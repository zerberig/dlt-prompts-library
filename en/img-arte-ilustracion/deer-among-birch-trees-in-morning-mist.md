Vertical watercolor of a birch forest wrapped in mist at dawn. White trunks with dark markings line up and fade into the background in ever-paler grays. Between two foreground trunks, a young deer peeks out with alert ears, looking at the viewer; it is the only patch of warm color, in sienna and cinnamon. Lots of negative space, very diluted washes in blue-gray and sage green, and a faint pink glow of sun behind the mist. Quiet, delicate mood.
