Watercolor of a small, cozy living room on a winter night. On the left, a brick fireplace with a lit fire bathing everything in orange and amber tones; in front of it, a plush mustard armchair with a knitted throw and a steaming mug on the side table. A tabby cat sleeps curled up on a round rug. Shelves full of books, hanging plants, and a window with snow falling outside in cold blue that contrasts with the warmth inside. Fine ink outlines and warm washes with plenty of detail in the objects. Homey, peaceful mood. No text.
