Vertical watercolor of a peacock seen from the front with its tail fully spread in a fan, filling almost the entire frame. Each feather ends in a concentric eyespot layered in cobalt blue, turquoise, emerald green, and bronze, with delicate touches of metallic gold ink. The deep blue neck and small feather crest are painted in fine detail, while the edges of the tail dissolve into loose washes on the white paper. Very light cream background. Near-perfect symmetry. Elegant, opulent mood, ideal as a decorative print. No text.
