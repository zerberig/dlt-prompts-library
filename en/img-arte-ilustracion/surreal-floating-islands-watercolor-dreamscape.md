Surreal watercolor of an endless sky in a gradient of peach, lavender, and turquoise, with no ground in sight. Rock islands of different sizes float with roots dangling beneath them; a waterfall pours from one but, instead of falling, curves upward and turns into clouds. Unsupported stone stairs link two islands, and a small figure in a red cloak carrying a lantern walks across them. Translucent whales and winged fish swim among the clouds. Soft wet washes with crisp ink detail on the rocks and the figure. Dreamlike, lonely, awe-inspiring mood. No text.
