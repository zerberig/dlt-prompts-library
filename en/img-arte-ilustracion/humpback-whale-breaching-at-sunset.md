Horizontal watercolor of the open sea at sunset, with a low horizon and a sky in peach and lilac washes. A humpback whale bursts almost entirely out of the water, twisting onto its side with one long pectoral fin stretched toward the sky. The water explodes around it in splashes and foam painted with reserved white paper and drops of white gouache. Its blue-gray skin shows barnacle textures and pale throat grooves. Loose waves in navy and turquoise. Epic yet serene mood. No text.
