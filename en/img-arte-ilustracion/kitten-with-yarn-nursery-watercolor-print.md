Nursery wall print in pastel watercolor, isolated on a plain white background and centered. A cream-colored tabby kitten lies on its side, hugging a pale pink ball of yarn with all four paws while a loose strand curls around it in a playful spiral. Big round eyes, rosy cheeks, fine whiskers. Soft, wet brushstrokes with blurred edges, a palette of cream, pale pink, mint, and pearl gray, no ink outlines. Plenty of white space around it so it breathes in a frame. Sweet, calm mood. No text.
