Oil painting of a mountain lake at dawn, the water so still it mirrors the snowy granite peaks and pink sky. Along the shore, smooth rocks, purple and yellow alpine wildflowers, and dark pines on both sides framing the view. A band of mist floats over the water's surface. Realistic plein air landscape with soft brushwork in the reflection, impasto in the light on the summits, and atmospheric perspective in cool tones. Still, clean, contemplative mood.
