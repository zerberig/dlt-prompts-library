Vertical watercolor of a night sky filling almost the entire frame, above a low hill in silhouette. A small person with a telescope looks up beside a lone tree. The Milky Way crosses diagonally in clouds of violet, blue, and a hint of pink, over a deep indigo background painted wet-on-wet. Stars spattered in white gouache and several recognizable constellations joined by fine gold ink lines. The hillside grass in blue-black with dry brushstrokes. A mood of wonder and calm.
