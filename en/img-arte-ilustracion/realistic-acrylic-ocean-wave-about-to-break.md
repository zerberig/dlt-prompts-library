Realistic acrylic painting of a large ocean wave about to break, seen from water level. The wall of water is translucent at the crest, with sunlight shining through it in emerald and turquoise, while white foam bursts into spray and suspended droplets. Below, the sea darkens to deep blue with swirls of foam. Visible impasto strokes in the foam, soft glazes for the water's transparency, and a silvery gray overcast sky. Dynamic horizontal composition. Powerful, energetic mood.
