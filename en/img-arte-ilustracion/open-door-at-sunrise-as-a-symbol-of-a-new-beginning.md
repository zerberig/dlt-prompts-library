Conceptual watercolor about the hope of a new beginning. In the middle of a grassy field still in blue-gray shadow stands a lone wooden door, with no walls, slightly ajar. On the other side of the door a bright sunrise in peach, gold, and pink is visible, and its light spills through the opening onto the grass like a path. A flock of small birds flies out through the door toward the sky. Strong contrast between the cool tones on this side and the warm ones beyond, wet washes and soft edges. Optimistic, emotional mood, no text.
