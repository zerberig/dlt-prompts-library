Realistic oil painting of a family portrait: three generations gathered on a green velvet sofa in a warm living room. The grandmother sits in the center holding a baby; beside her, a couple in their thirties and an eight-year-old boy leaning on the armrest with a mischievous grin. Each face has its own features and expression, natural skin tones, and well-resolved hands. Warm side window light, ochre wall behind with a blurred framed painting. Visible brushstrokes in clothing and background, finer work in the faces. Palette of ochres, greens, and earthy reds. Cozy, heartfelt mood.
