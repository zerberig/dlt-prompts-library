Science communication print that tells how water travels inside a tree. An oak is shown in vertical cross-section: underground, the roots absorb glowing blue droplets; up the trunk, thin channels carry water like threads of light; in the leaves, magnifying circles show stomata releasing vapor toward a cloudy sky. Curved painted arrows guide the eye from bottom to top. Watercolor and ink illustration with a naturalist sketchbook look, in greens, earth tones, and cyan blue. Clear, educational vertical composition, no text or labels.
