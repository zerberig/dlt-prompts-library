Scientific botanical illustration in watercolor and graphite on off-white paper, laid out like a museum plate. In the center, a complete slipper orchid with its stem, two mottled leaves, and an open flower with striped petals in burgundy and lime green, a pouch-shaped lip, and fine hairs along the edges. Around it, four enlarged studies arranged neatly: the lip in cross-section, the column seen from the front, a petal with its venation, and a closed bud.
