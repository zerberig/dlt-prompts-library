Sumi ink and color wash painting of a Japanese garden in autumn, seen from the edge of a polished wooden veranda that fills the foreground. A still pond with orange koi beneath the surface stretches ahead; on the left, a moss-covered stone lantern; behind, maples with crimson leaves and cloud-pruned pines, with gravel raked in parallel lines. Black ink outlines with varied brush pressure, color applied in transparent layers, and lots of empty space in the sky. Quiet, contemplative mood, no text.
