Vintage naturalist plate on ivory paper: a monarch butterfly with open wings perched on a cluster of pink milkweed flowers, with the stem and two long leaves. Precise layered watercolor over a graphite underdrawing visible at the edges, crisp black veins on the orange wings, white dots along the margins, and suggested pollen. To one side, a small sketch of the striped caterpillar and the green chrysalis with gold dots. Below, the text "Danaus plexippus" in sepia handwritten italics. Delicate, scientific mood.
