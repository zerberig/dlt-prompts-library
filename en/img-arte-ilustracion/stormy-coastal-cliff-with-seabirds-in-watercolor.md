Watercolor seascape of a rugged cliff dropping into the ocean under a stormy sky. Huge waves crash against the dark rocks and throw white spray into the air; gulls and gannets nest on the cliff ledges, and a few glide against the wind. Wet-on-wet technique for the slate-gray clouds and teal sea, sprinkled salt for the texture of the foam, and dry brushstrokes on the rock. A band of silver light breaks through on the horizon. Wild, powerful mood.
