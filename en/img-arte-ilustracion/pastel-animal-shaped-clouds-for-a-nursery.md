Dreamy watercolor of a lavender and pale-pink sky where the clouds form animal silhouettes: a big whale floating in the center, an elephant with its trunk raised, and a little bunny curled up in a corner. The clouds have soft, fluffy edges, painted wet-on-wet in white, cream, and lilac, with very faint sky-blue shadows. A couple of tiny birds and a few small stars complete the scene. Pastel palette, lots of light, and visible paper texture. Calm, tender mood, ideal for a nursery, no text.
