Vertical watercolor of a golden eagle seen from below in full flight, wings fully extended and primary feathers spread like fingers. Behind it, a stormy sky starting to clear: blue-gray clouds at the bottom and a bright opening of sky blue and pale gold above. The feathers are painted with dry brushstrokes in chocolate brown and ochre, with fine detail on the head and talons, while the sky is handled with broad washes and plenty of white paper. A feeling of freedom and height.
