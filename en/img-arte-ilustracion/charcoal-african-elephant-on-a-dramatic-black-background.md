Realistic charcoal drawing of an African elephant head-on, walking toward the viewer with ears spread and trunk slightly curled. The deep wrinkles of the skin, worn tusks, and long eyelashes are rendered with compressed charcoal and a blending stump, and a cloud of dust rises around its feet. The elephant emerges from a deep black background, lit by hard side light that defines its volume. Highlights lifted with a kneaded eraser and white pencil, on fine-grain white paper.
