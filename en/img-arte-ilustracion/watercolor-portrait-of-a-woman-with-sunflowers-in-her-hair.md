Watercolor portrait of a young woman in three-quarter profile, with a soft smile and her gaze turned toward the light. Her long wavy hair falls loose and dissolves into wet washes of sienna, ochre, and amber that spread toward the edge of the paper; three sunflowers painted in more detail are tucked among the strands. The face is built with transparent layers and a few precise strokes on the eyes and lips, using the white of the paper as the main highlight on the cheek. Nearly white background with yellow splatters. Warm, serene, summery mood, no text.
