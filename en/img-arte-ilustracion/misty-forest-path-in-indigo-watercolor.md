Vertical, nearly monochrome watercolor in indigo and blue-gray: a dirt path winds from the foreground into the depths of a forest of tall, slender trees. The trunks are built in layers: the nearest dark and crisp, the middle ones lighter, and the farthest just a smudge dissolving into the fog. A shaft of pale light falls on the path in the distance. Graded washes and wet-on-wet technique, lots of white paper left for the fog. Quiet, contemplative, slightly mysterious mood. No people, no text.
