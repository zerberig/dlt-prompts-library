Spy thriller scene in noir-style watercolor, seen from a low angle on the street. At night in the rain, a secret agent dressed in black rappels down the facade of an art deco building with a case strapped to his back; above, a searchlight beam sweeps the sky and barely grazes him. Lit windows form an amber grid and the wet street reflects the lights. Nearly monochrome palette of indigo, slate gray, and black, with a single red accent in his coat lining. Wet washes with splatters that simulate rain. Tense, cinematic mood, no text.
