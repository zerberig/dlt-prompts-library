Photograph of a street art mural painted on a long brick wall in a Latin American neighborhood street. The mural shows faces of people of different ages and backgrounds intertwined with flowers, hummingbirds, and hands holding one another, over bands of turquoise, yellow, magenta, and orange. Spray paint with stencils for the silhouettes and brushwork for the details, with drips and soft edges. In the foreground, the sidewalk and a bicycle leaning against the wall. 35mm lens, morning side light. Festive, community mood.
