Large-format abstract expressionist painting on raw canvas, seen head-on. Wide gestural brushstrokes in cadmium red and charcoal black cross the canvas in violent diagonals; layers of yellow ochre and off-white show through underneath. There are vertical drips of thinned paint, areas of thick impasto applied with a palette knife that leave ridges and grooves, and splatters flung hard in the upper corner. The rhythm of the painter's body shows in every stroke. Acrylic and oil texture with uneven sheen. Intense, raw, visceral mood, no text and no signature.
