Pointillist painting of an avenue in the historic center of a Latin American city at dusk, seen from the sidewalk at eye level. Colonial stone buildings and facades in ochre and hot pink frame the street; hurried pedestrians, a corn vendor with a steaming cart, cyclists, and an approaching trolleybus with its headlights on. Everything is built from thousands of dots of pure color: oranges and violets in the sky, blues in the shadows, yellows where shop lights begin to glow. Dot density increases in areas of motion to convey energy. Vibrant, bustling mood, no legible text.
