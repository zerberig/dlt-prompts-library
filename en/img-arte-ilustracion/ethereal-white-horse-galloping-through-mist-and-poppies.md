Ethereal watercolor of an otherworldly landscape at dawn: a field of red and lilac poppies that fades into pearly mist, hills floating without a base on the horizon, and two pale moons in a pearlescent pink sky. A nearly translucent white horse gallops diagonally to the right; its mane and tail unravel into threads of iridescent light that leave a trail in the air. Very diluted layers, lost edges, the white of the paper as the light source, and small touches of pearlescent pigment. Dreamlike, silent, luminous mood, no text.
