Watercolor study of an amethyst cluster centered on white cotton paper. A dozen pointed crystals of different sizes rise from a gray rock base; each has clearly defined flat facets with crisp edges, from deep violet in shadow to almost transparent lilac on the lit faces. Touches of blue and pink in the internal reflections and white highlights left as bare paper. Transparent layering with hard edges, no ink outlines, and a soft cast shadow to the right. Precise, luminous mood, like a naturalist's notebook plate. No text.
