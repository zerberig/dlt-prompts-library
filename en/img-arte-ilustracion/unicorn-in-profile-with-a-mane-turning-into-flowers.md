Watercolor portrait of a white unicorn in profile, head slightly raised and eyes half-closed. Its long mane and tail gradually turn into a cascade of flowers: peonies, wisteria, and small roses in lilac, pink, and peach, which in turn dissolve into brushstrokes and drops of color. The horn is a pearly spiral with iridescent highlights. The white body is defined only by very soft lavender-blue shadows. White background with a faint halo of color. Ethereal, magical mood.
