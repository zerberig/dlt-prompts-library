Delicate watercolor of a small country cottage with whitewashed walls and a crimson tile roof, surrounded by a lush garden. Pink and white climbing roses cover the facade and frame the blue wooden door; a flagstone path winds from a low gate through lavender, hollyhocks, and foxgloves. A cat sleeps on the front step. Loose watercolor technique with edges that fade into white paper at the corners, more precise detail on the house, a palette of pinks, sage greens, and lilacs. Golden afternoon light. Nostalgic, peaceful mood, ideal for a print or card, no text.
