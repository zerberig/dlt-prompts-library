Ancient forest at dusk, with huge moss-covered trunks and roots wrapped around the ruins of a stone arch. Barely visible creatures hide among the trees: a deer with antlers of flowering branches, small lantern-shaped light spirits, and a pair of eyes glowing inside a hollow log. A brook crosses the foreground. Mystical watercolor in deep greens and violets, with glazes that create haze and pale cyan magical lights reserved from the paper and touched up with gouache. Mysterious, enchanted mood that invites you to search for the creatures.
