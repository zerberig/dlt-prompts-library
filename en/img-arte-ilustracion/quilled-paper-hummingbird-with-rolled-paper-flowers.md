Macro photograph of a quilling artwork on cream cardstock: a hummingbird in flight in front of a trumpet flower, surrounded by fern leaves and small blossoms. Every shape is made from thin paper strips coiled into spirals, teardrops, and eyes; the hummingbird's feathers combine emerald, turquoise, and violet strips, and the flower shifts from magenta to orange. Soft side lighting casts fine shadows and reveals the edge of each strip. 100 mm macro lens at f/8, centered framing, real paper texture. Delicate, handcrafted mood.
