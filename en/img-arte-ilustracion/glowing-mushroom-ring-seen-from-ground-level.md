Watercolor and ink illustration of a forest at night seen from ground level, as if you were an insect. A ring of mushrooms with translucent caps glows turquoise and amber on a carpet of moss; tiny creatures peek out between them: a snail with a glass shell, moths whose wings shed luminous dust, and a small leaf-faced forest spirit holding a dewdrop like a lantern. The background fades into dark, blurred indigo tree trunks. White gouache accents for the sparkles. Mysterious, enchanting mood, no text.
