Watercolor still life of a 1960s suitcase record player open on a wooden cabinet, its needle resting on a spinning record. Beside it, several records scattered on the floor and leaning against the cabinet, in worn cardboard sleeves with faded colors and no legible text, a cup of coffee, and a plant in a clay pot. Afternoon light coming through a side window casts long shadows. Warm, limited palette of sepia, ochre, mustard, and olive green, with soft washes and visible fine pencil outlines.
