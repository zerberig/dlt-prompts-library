Nursery print on a warm white background with plenty of empty space. A sleeping baby koala hugs a eucalyptus branch, eyes closed and cheeks rosy, while its mother wraps one arm around it. Long sage-green eucalyptus leaves hang around them and frame the scene. Delicate watercolor in transparent layers, soft gray fur built with dry-brush touches, and nearly invisible outlines. Limited palette of grays, sage green, and pale pink. Calm, tender mood. No text.
