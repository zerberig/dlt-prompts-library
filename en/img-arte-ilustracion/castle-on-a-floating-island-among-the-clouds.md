A castle of white spires rises from a chunk of floating rock suspended among swirling clouds at sunset. Waterfalls pour from the base of the rock and dissolve into mist, roots dangle into the void, and golden windows glow in the towers. A stone staircase spirals around the island and a pair of white birds fly nearby. Surreal watercolor with wet-on-wet clouds in pink, gold, and lavender, edges that fade into the sky, and fine architectural details in ink. Vertical composition with the castle in the upper third. Ethereal, dreamlike mood.
