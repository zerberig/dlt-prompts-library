Overhead watercolor view of a calm garden pond. Round lily pads float in clusters on jade-green water, with two open pink lotus flowers and one bud. Beneath the surface, three orange, white, and black koi swim in a curve, slightly blurred by the water, and a blue dragonfly rests on a leaf. Transparent layers give the water depth, sky reflections are left as white paper, and soft rings mark where the surface moves. Balanced square composition. Serene, meditative mood.
