Vertical gouache and watercolor painting of a monumental waterfall dropping from the top of dark rock cliffs into an emerald green pool. The falling water fills the center like a white curtain; mist rising from the base forms a faint diagonal rainbow. Dense vegetation hangs from the rock walls, and a low cloud cuts across the top of the cliff. Below, on a rock beside the pool, a tiny hiker with a red backpack gives the scale. Contrast between opaque rock and luminous water, low-angle perspective. Epic, majestic mood, no text.
