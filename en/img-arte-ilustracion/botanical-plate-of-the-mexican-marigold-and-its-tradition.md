Scientific-style watercolor botanical plate on ivory paper: a complete Mexican marigold (cempasúchil) plant in the center, with root, stem, serrated leaves, and three flowers at different stages, from bud to full bloom, in intense orange and yellow. On the sides, smaller studies of a petal, a seed, and a cross-section of a flower. At the bottom, as a nod to its role in the Day of the Dead tradition, a path of loose petals leading to a lit candle and a small piece of purple cut-paper banner. Fine sepia ink line and precise washes. Warm, respectful mood, no text.
