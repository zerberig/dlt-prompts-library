River winding through a lush summer forest, seen from a slight rise: clear water runs between round moss-covered stones and disappears around a bend in the distance. Sunbeams filter through the canopy and cast patches of light on the water and the ferns along the bank. Landscape watercolor in transparent layers from yellow greens to blue greens, light rays lifted out with a damp brush, and reflections in horizontal strokes. Vertical composition that leads the eye along the river. Fresh, peaceful mood, ideal as a decorative print.
