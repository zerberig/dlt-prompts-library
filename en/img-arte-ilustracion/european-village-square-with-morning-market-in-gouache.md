Gouache illustration of a European village square seen from a high balcony in the early morning. Cobblestone streets converge on a stone fountain; around it, three-story houses with green shutters and pots of geraniums, cafe tables with striped umbrellas, and market stalls under canvas awnings full of fruit, cheese, and flowers. Small figures stroll with baskets, a cyclist rides by, and pigeons take off from the fountain. Warm flat colors in terracotta, cream, sage green, and sky blue, long soft shadows. Cozy, lively mood, no legible text on the signs.
