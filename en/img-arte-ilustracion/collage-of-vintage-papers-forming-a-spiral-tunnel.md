Analog collage photographed head-on, made of cut and torn paper fragments overlapping in concentric rings to form a spiral tunnel sinking toward the center. The outer layers use yellowed vintage maps and sheet music; the middle layers, botanical and astronomical engravings in sepia ink; the center, tissue papers in deep blue and gold that suggest a distant night sky. Real shadows between layers, torn edges with visible fibers, a few strokes of white acrylic, and aged tape. Mood of wonder and discovery, no legible text.
