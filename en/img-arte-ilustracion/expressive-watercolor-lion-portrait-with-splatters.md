Close-up front-facing portrait of an adult lion, staring straight at the viewer with a piercing gaze. Its amber eyes are painted precisely with crisp highlights, while the mane opens into loose locks that dissolve into pigment splatters and drips toward the edges of the paper. Expressive watercolor on white paper: ochres, burnt sienna, and unexpected touches of ultramarine and magenta in the mane's shadows. Contrast between the detailed face and the free-flowing mane. White background with minimal blooms. Majestic, intense mood.
