Horizontal watercolor of a floating market on a canal at sunrise, seen from above on a bridge. Dozens of narrow wooden boats move past one another, loaded with mangoes, pineapples, bananas, coconuts, and bunches of flowers; the vendors wear conical palm-leaf hats, and one passes a bunch of fruit to another boat. The olive-green water reflects the colors and the golden haze of the low sun. Wooden stilt houses on both sides with potted plants. Transparent washes with fine ink lines for the boats, dense detail in the center loosening toward the edges. Lively, warm mood. No text.
