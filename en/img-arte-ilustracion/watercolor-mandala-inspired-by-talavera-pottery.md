Circular mandala painted in watercolor on white cotton paper, seen head-on and centered, inspired by Mexican talavera pottery. From a center with an eight-petal flower, concentric rings of leaves, scrolls, stylized birds, and small dots open outward in perfect radial symmetry. Palette of intense cobalt blue, yellow ochre, emerald green, and touches of orange, with fine brushstrokes and the slight irregularity of hand painting. Plain white background around the mandala. Meditative, orderly, luminous mood, no text.
