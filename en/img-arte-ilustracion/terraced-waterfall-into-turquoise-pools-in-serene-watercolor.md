Serene watercolor of a mountain river stepping down over limestone terraces, forming small cascades and pools of milky turquoise water. Ferns, wild banana plants, and moss-covered trees grow all around and reflect in the still water of the pools. The falling water is painted with fine vertical strokes and reserved white paper for the foam; the foliage is built with wet-on-wet layers in deep greens and pale yellows. Soft midday light filtering through the vegetation. Fresh, quiet, contemplative mood, no text.
