Panoramic watercolor landscape of a lake hidden among tall mountains, early in the morning. The peaks are reflected almost perfectly in the still water, separated by a band of white mist covering the shore and a pine forest. A limited palette of only three colors, ultramarine blue, burnt sienna, and yellow ochre, mixed into soft grays and greens. Distant mountains in flat, pale washes, foreground with darker strokes and a small wooden canoe on the shore. Still, contemplative mood.
