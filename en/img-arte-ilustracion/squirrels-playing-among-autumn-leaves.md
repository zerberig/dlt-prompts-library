Watercolor of a moss-covered oak trunk in full autumn, with red, orange, and gold leaves swirling down. Two russet squirrels chase each other around the trunk: one spirals down with its bushy tail held high while the other leaps from a branch with its legs outstretched. At the base of the tree, a pile of dry leaves and an acorn. Quick, loose brushstrokes with ochre and crimson splatters that suggest leaves in the air, fur painted with dry brush. Warm afternoon light coming in from the left. Playful, cozy mood. No text.
