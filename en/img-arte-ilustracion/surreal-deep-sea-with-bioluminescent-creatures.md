Surreal watercolor of the deep ocean at night, over a background of midnight blue and indigo black painted wet-on-wet. Huge translucent jellyfish float at different heights with tentacles that turn into ribbons of cyan and magenta light; a lanternfish with an amber lure lights its own way, and clouds of plankton glow like constellations. At the bottom, a garden of candelabra-shaped corals and anemones giving off a soft glow. Dots and splatters of white gouache and fluorescent pigment. Mysterious, dreamlike, silent mood, no text.
