Dense jungle in mid-morning, with giant ferns, vines, and banana leaves overlapping in layers of deep green. A Bengal tiger prowls low toward the viewer through the foliage, one front paw lifted and its gaze locked forward. Its blazing orange fur with black stripes is painted in wet layers, then sharpened with dry brush; the shafts of light cutting through the leaves are left as white paper. Green and ochre splatters at the edges. Horizontal composition with the tiger slightly off-center. Tense, majestic mood. No text.
