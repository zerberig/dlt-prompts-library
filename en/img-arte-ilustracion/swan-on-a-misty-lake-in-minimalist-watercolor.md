Minimalist watercolor of a white swan gliding across a lake wrapped in mist at dawn. The swan sits in the lower right third, its neck in an elegant curve and a thin wake behind it; its reflection stretches downward in trembling vertical strokes. The rest of the frame is mist: very diluted washes of bluish gray that melt into the white paper, and just a few ink reeds in the left corner. A single touch of peach in the sky and the swan's orange beak. Lots of negative space. Serene, elegant, silent mood, no text.
