Double-page spread for a picture book: a city park in spring where five dogs of different breeds and sizes play together. A dachshund chases a red ball, a Labrador leaps to catch a flying disc, a greyhound runs in circles, a pug rolls in the grass, and a brown mixed-breed dog carries a huge stick. Blossoming trees, a bench with two people laughing, and a pond with ducks in the background. Cheerful watercolor with loose pencil outlines, fresh colors, and expressive movement. Horizontal composition with clear sky at the top as open space for text. Fun, energetic mood.
