Vertical watercolor of a deep ocean pierced by shafts of light falling from the surface. A mermaid swims downward on a diagonal, her long copper hair drifting like seaweed, wearing a top of pearly shells, with a scaled tail shifting from jade green to violet. Around her, small bubbles, a school of silver fish, and translucent jellyfish. Wet-on-wet technique with transparent layers of deep blue, turquoise, and sea green, highlights left as white paper, and a faint pearly shimmer on the scales. Ethereal, silent, dreamlike mood.
