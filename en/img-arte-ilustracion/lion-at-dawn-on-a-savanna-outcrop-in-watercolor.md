Watercolor landscape of the African savanna at dawn. On a rocky outcrop to the left, an adult lion stands surveying the horizon, its golden mane stirred by the wind and the low sun lighting its profile. Below, the plain of golden grass stretches out with a silhouetted acacia tree and a distant herd of zebras barely suggested. Sky in washes of peach, gold, and pale blue; the lion's shadows in burnt sienna and violet. Loose brushwork in the landscape and more detail on the head. Majestic, calm mood.
