Quiet beach at sunset: gentle waves break into white foam on wet sand that mirrors the sky, while the golden sun touches the sea horizon. Three seagulls glide diagonally and dunes with beach grass rise on the right. Soft pastel painting on textured paper, with visible strokes and finger-blended layers in the orange, pink, and lilac sky, and crisper marks in the foam. The paper's tooth shows through in the sand. Horizontal composition with the horizon in the lower third. Serene, nostalgic mood.
