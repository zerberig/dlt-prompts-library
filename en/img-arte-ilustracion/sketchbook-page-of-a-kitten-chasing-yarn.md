Sketchbook page on textured ivory paper, seen from above. Three quick studies of the same gray kitten with white paws chasing a blue ball of yarn: in one it crouches with its rear end up, in another it leaps with paws outstretched, and in the last it lies tangled on its back biting the strand. Drawn with loose, gestural ink lines, with watercolor patches spilling past the outlines and a single continuous blue thread connecting all three sketches. Some pencil construction lines visible. Spontaneous, fun mood. No text or notes.
