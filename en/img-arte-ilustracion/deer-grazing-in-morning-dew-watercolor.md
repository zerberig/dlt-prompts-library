Vignette watercolor on white paper: a white-tailed deer, in medium shot and in profile, lowers its head to graze among tall grasses and wildflowers at sunrise, with dewdrops glinting on the leaves. Its ears are alert, and the pink morning light draws a glowing rim along its back. The cinnamon-brown coat is painted in warm layers, with fine strokes on the face. All around, the scene fades toward the edges in soft patches of sage green, lilac, and yellow until it melts into the white of the paper. Still, tender, delicate mood. No text.
