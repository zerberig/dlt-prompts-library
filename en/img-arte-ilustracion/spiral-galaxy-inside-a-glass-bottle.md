Watercolor of an antique glass bottle with a cork stopper, standing on a dark wooden table. Inside, a spiral galaxy swirls with nebulas in violet, magenta, and turquoise, tiny stars splattered in white gouache, and two small ringed planets. A little stardust escapes through a crack in the cork and floats in the air. Crisp reflections on the glass and a shadow with flecks of color on the table. Dark ink-blue, nearly black background so the bottle glows. Magical, mysterious mood, no text.
