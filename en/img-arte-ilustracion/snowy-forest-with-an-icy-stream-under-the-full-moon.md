Vertical watercolor of a birch forest on a winter night, after a snowfall. A partly frozen stream winds from the foreground into the distance, with cracked ice plates and dark water reflecting the full moon, high and bright in a navy sky. Cool palette of blues, violets, and silver, with a faint warm halo around the moon. Silent, magical mood, no text.
