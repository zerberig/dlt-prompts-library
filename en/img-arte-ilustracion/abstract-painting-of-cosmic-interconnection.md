Meditative abstract painting in a square format, with fluid ink and gold leaf on a canvas with a deep midnight-blue ground. Irregular concentric circles expand from the center like ripples in water, and over them a network of very fine gold lines connects dozens of luminous points, reading as roots, neurons, and constellations all at once. At the edges, the ink dissolves into organic blooms of teal and violet. Visible texture of flowing ink and tiny cracks in the gold leaf. Balanced, breathable composition. Contemplative mood of deep calm. No text.
