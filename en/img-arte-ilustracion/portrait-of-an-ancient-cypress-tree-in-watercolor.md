Watercolor treated as a portrait: an ancient Montezuma cypress fills almost the entire frame, with a massive, twisted trunk full of knots and folds that hint at a wise face without becoming one. Its thick roots spread across the ground, gripping moss-covered stones; the leafy crown opens upward in masses of deep green with Spanish moss hanging from the branches. Warm afternoon light filters through the leaves, casting golden patches on the bark. Bark built up with dry brush and layers of ochre, sepia, and gray. Light wash background. Wise, ancient, protective mood. No text.
