Digital collage on a sheet of aged kraft paper with coffee stains. In the center, a cut-out sepia photograph of a grandmother smiling in her 1950s kitchen, surrounded by fragments of handwritten recipes in faded blue ink, postage stamps, yellowed tape, and pressed dried flowers. Modern elements cross over the vintage layers: flat-color circles in turquoise and mustard and a thin neon-pink line framing the photo. Torn edges, subtle shadows that give each piece depth, and soft photographic grain. Warm, nostalgic mood, with no additional legible text.
