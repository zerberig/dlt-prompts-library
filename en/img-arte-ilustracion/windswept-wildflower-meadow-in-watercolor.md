Panoramic watercolor of a spring meadow, with gentle hills in the distance and a clear sky with long, stretched clouds. In the foreground, a dense band of wildflowers —red poppies, daisies, blue cornflowers, and purple lupines— leans to the right, pushed by the breeze along with the tall grasses. Loose petals fly through the air. Long, directional brushstrokes that convey the wind, foreground flowers in detail and background ones dissolved into patches of color. Clear mid-morning light. Fresh, free, luminous mood. No animals or people, no text.
