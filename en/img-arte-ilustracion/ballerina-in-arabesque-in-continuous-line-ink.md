Minimalist drawing on plain white paper of a ballet dancer in arabesque, traced with a single continuous line of black ink that never leaves the paper: the leg extended behind her, the arms elongated, and the neck curved. Line weight varies with brush pressure, thinner at the hands and feet. A single splash of pale pink watercolor suggests the tutu and spills slightly outside the line, with a light gradient toward peach. Centered composition with ample empty space around it. Light, elegant, fluid mood, no text.
