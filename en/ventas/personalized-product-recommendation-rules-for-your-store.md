Goal: Recommend relevant products to each customer on the site and by email to increase repeat purchases and average order value, without needing an engineering team.

Criteria: Use only data customers agreed to share and explain transparently how it's used. If you have web search, check the current apps for my platform and flag anything you can't confirm.
Format: A rules table with the columns Rule, Logic, Where it appears, and Metric; then the rest in sections.
