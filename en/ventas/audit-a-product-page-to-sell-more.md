Deliverable: 1) A 1-5 score for each element: title, images, description, price and offer, reviews, shipping and returns, call to action, and mobile version. 2) The 5 highest-impact changes, ranked by impact and effort. 3) A rewritten description. 4) The list of photos that should accompany the product.
Criteria: Explain each recommendation in terms of the buyer question it answers. Show only real customer reviews.
Format: An audit table with the columns Element, Score, Problem, and Recommendation; then the prioritized changes and the new description.
