Context: I sell [your product or service] in the [industry] industry and run discovery calls with prospects who hold the role of [prospect's role].
Goal: Fully understand the prospect's situation, problems, and goals before presenting my solution, so I only recommend what actually helps them and move the deal forward with solid information.
Deliverable: 15 to 20 open-ended questions organized into 5 blocks: current situation, problems and their impact, goals and priorities, decision process (who decides, how, and when), and budget or resources. For each question, a follow-up question to dig deeper and the signal to listen for in the answer.
Criteria: Questions that invite a story rather than a yes or no, and that sound natural to someone in that role. Include 2 questions that quantify the cost of the problem, because that makes the value of solving it tangible.
Format: One table per block with the columns Question, Follow-up, and What to listen for. Close with 3 tips for running the call.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
