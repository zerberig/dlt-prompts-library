Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience] and have these resources for social media: [available time and budget].
Goal: Decide which 1 or 2 networks to focus on first to drive sales, instead of being half-present everywhere.
Deliverable: 1) An evaluation of the relevant networks (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn, X, and WhatsApp) based on where my audience is, how well the product fits the format, production effort, and in-platform selling options. 2) A recommendation of 1 or 2 main networks with the reasoning. 3) For each: content type, posting frequency, and a ready-to-use sample post, such as a launch announcement. 4) The signals that will tell me in 3 months whether to stay the course or switch.
Criteria: If you have web search, check current usage data by country and the selling features available today, and flag anything that is an estimate.
Format: An evaluation table with a 1-5 score per criterion, followed by the recommendations in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
