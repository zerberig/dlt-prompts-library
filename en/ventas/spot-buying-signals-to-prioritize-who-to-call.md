Context: I prospect by phone to sell [your product or service] to companies in [industry]. My prospect list is big and I don't know where to start.
Goal: Call the prospects most likely to buy and most valuable first, using industry data and public signals.
Deliverable: 1) 10 buying signals relevant to my industry (for example, hiring, expansion, leadership changes, new regulations, or product launches), with where to spot them. 2) A scoring model to prioritize my list using those signals and account size. 3) For the 3 strongest signals, a call opener that mentions the signal naturally. 4) A 30-minute weekly process to keep the list updated.
Criteria: Use only public, legitimate sources. If you have web search, check which data sources exist for my industry and country.
Format: A signals table with the columns Signal, Why it indicates interest, Where to find it, and Weight; then the remaining sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
