Context: I run an online store selling [your product] on [your store platform], and I'm at this stage: [business stage] (just launched, growing, or established).
Goal: I want a simple dashboard I can review every week to know whether the business is on track and where to act, without drowning in metrics.
Deliverable: 8 to 12 KPIs grouped into acquisition, conversion, customer value, retention, and operations. For each one: what it measures, the formula, where I get the data, how often to review it, and what decision I make if it goes up or down. At the end, the 3 KPIs I should watch first at my stage and why.
Criteria: Favor actionable indicators over vanity metrics like followers or total visits. If you mention benchmark ranges for my industry, cite the source or flag them as estimates.
Format: A table with the columns KPI, What it measures, Formula, Data source, Frequency, and Action if it changes; then a numbered list with the 3 priority KPIs.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
