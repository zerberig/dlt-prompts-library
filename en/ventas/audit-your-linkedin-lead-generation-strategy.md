Context: I use LinkedIn to win clients for [your product or service]. Here is what I do today and my results: [your current activity and results] (posting frequency, content type, messages sent, reply rate, leads per month, and ads if any).
Goal: Find out what's holding my results back and what to change first.
Deliverable: 1) An audit across 5 areas: profile, content, network, messaging, and measurement, with a 1-5 score and the main problem in each. 2) The obstacles to measuring my LinkedIn results accurately and how to solve them. 3) 5 two-week experiments, each with a hypothesis, a concrete change, and a success metric. 4) What to stop doing.
Criteria: Base the diagnosis on my data and point out what is an assumption. Don't present assumptions about the algorithm as facts.
Format: An audit table with the columns Area, Score, Problem, and Action; then the experiments as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
