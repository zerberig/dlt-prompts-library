Context: Here is the data from my online store selling [your product] for the last period: [your store data] (visits by source, conversion rate, carts created and abandoned, orders, average order value, returns, and support response times).
Goal: Identify which part of the funnel is holding sales back and where to invest first to improve returns.
Deliverable: 1) A stage-by-stage read of the funnel with the rates calculated. 2) The 3 priority improvement areas with evidence from the data (for example, conversion, cart recovery, customer support, or acquisition channel). 3) Concrete actions for each area with their estimated impact. 4) What data is missing for a better diagnosis.
Criteria: Base every conclusion on my data and keep facts separate from hypotheses. If you compare against industry averages, cite the source or flag them as estimates.
Format: A funnel table, followed by findings and actions in sections with bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
