Context: I sell [your product or service] to [target audience] and want [social network] to be my main source of customers.
Goal: A funnel that turns strangers into followers, then into leads who share their contact details, and then into buyers, with clear content and messaging at every stage.
Deliverable: 1) The funnel stages: discovery, interest, consideration, conversion, and loyalty. 2) For each stage: goal, type of content or ad, a concrete example, call to action, and key metric. 3) The lead magnet (guide, class, free assessment, or sample) and how it's delivered. 4) The follow-up sequence via direct messages or email. 5) How to use the platform's analytics to find the stage where most people drop off. 6) A sample post that explains to my audience why my solution is worth it.
Criteria: If you have web search, check the network's current features (formats, ads, shop, and automated messaging). Any automation must respect the platform's policies and users' consent.
Format: A table by stage with the columns Stage, Goal, Content, Example, Call to action, and Metric; then sections 3 to 6.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
