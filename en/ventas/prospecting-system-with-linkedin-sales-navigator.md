Context: I sell [your product or service] to companies with this profile: [ideal customer]. I have, or am about to get, LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
Goal: Build a prospecting system that gives me a short list of relevant prospects every week, plus real reasons to reach out.
Deliverable: 1) The account and lead searches I should save, with suggested filters. 2) How to organize lists by priority. 3) Which alerts or signals to use as a reason to reach out (job changes, recent posts, company growth). 4) A 5-step weekly cadence combining content engagement, connection request, message, and follow-up. 5) 3 sample messages based on different signals.
Criteria: If you have web search, confirm which filters and features Sales Navigator offers today and flag anything you can't verify, because the tool changes often. Personalized messages and no automation that breaks LinkedIn's terms.
Format: Numbered sections; the filters in a table with the columns Filter, Suggested value, and Why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
