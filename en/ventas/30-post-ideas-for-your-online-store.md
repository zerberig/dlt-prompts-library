Context: I run an online store selling [your product] to [target audience]. I mainly post on [social network].
Goal: I want to stop improvising what to post and have ideas that keep my community engaged, show the product without feeling like a nonstop ad, and help me capture interested leads.
Deliverable: 30 post ideas split across four types: product in use, customer lifestyle, educational or value content, and lead capture (for example, a downloadable guide, a giveaway with sign-up, or a waitlist).
Criteria: Each idea should be concrete and tied to my product (not "share a tip"), use the format that works best on that network, and end with a clear action. Keep most of them value-driven and a minority as direct promotion, because an account that only sells loses interest and followers.
Format: A table with the columns No., Type, Idea, Format (carousel, short video, story, etc.), Hook for the first line or first seconds, and Call to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
