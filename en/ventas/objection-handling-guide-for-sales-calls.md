Context: I sell [your product or service] by phone to [type of customer]. Our value proposition is [your value proposition], and the objections I hear most are: [common objections].
Goal: Help my team handle objections with confidence and empathy, understanding what's behind them, so they can move the deal forward or walk away in time.
Deliverable: For each objection (mine plus 3 more that typically come up in my type of sale): what it usually really means, a question to understand it better, a 2-4 sentence model answer tied to the value proposition, and the next step. At the end, a post-call follow-up email that addresses the main objection.
Criteria: Conversational answers, with no pressure and no dismissing the customer. If an objection shows we're not a good fit for them, say so and suggest how to close respectfully.
Format: A table with the columns Objection, What's behind it, Question, Model answer, and Next step; then the email.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
