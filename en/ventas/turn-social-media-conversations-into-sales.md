Context: My business [your business name] sells [your product]. I get comments and direct messages on [social network], but few conversations end in a purchase.
Goal: Build relationships with people who engage and naturally guide interested ones toward buying, without coming across as pushy.
Deliverable: 1) A 20-minute daily routine for replying to and starting conversations. 2) Reply templates for: a price question, a product question, a positive comment, a public complaint, and someone who stopped replying. 3) 5 ideas to spark conversation from posts and stories. 4) How to keep track of interested people for follow-up.
Criteria: Short, personal, helpful replies. No unsolicited mass messages, because they read as spam and can hurt the account. Every template should be adaptable in seconds.
Format: Sections with bullets and each template in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
