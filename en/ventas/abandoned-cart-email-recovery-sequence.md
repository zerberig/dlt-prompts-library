Context: My store [your store] sells [your product]. As an incentive, I can offer [available incentive] (a discount, free shipping, a gift, or none).
Goal: Recover abandoned purchases with helpful reminders, without training customers to wait for discounts.
Deliverable: 3 automated emails: 1) a friendly reminder after one hour, 2) answers to common questions plus social proof at 24 hours, and 3) a final incentive at 48 to 72 hours. For each: subject line (2 options), preview text, short body, how to display the cart items, call to action, and expiry. Also include a thank-you email for customers who completed a large order, with a small perk for their next purchase.
Criteria: A service tone, not a pressure tone. Use urgency only when it's real (actual stock levels or deadlines). Always include an unsubscribe option.
Format: Each email in its own block with a heading, plus a summary table with the send timing and goal of each.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
