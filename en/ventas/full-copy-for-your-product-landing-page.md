Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. Its main features and benefits are [features and benefits], and I have this social proof: [available social proof] (reviews, customer count, case studies, or press mentions).
Goal: A landing page that turns visitors from ads and social media into buyers by quickly explaining why it's worth it.
Deliverable: Full copy by section: headline and subheadline (3 options), a benefits block, how it works in 3 steps, social proof, answers to the 5 most likely objections as FAQs, offer and guarantee, and calls to action throughout the page.
Criteria: Concrete language focused on what the customer gains. Use only the social proof I gave you, with no invented testimonials or numbers, because that destroys trust and can be illegal. Every call to action should lead to the same next step.
Format: Sections in page order, with brief design notes in parentheses (for example, where an image or button goes).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
