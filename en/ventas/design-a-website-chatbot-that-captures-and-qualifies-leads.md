Context: My company offers [your product or service] to [target audience]. I want to add an AI chatbot to my website that greets visitors and turns interested ones into calls booked with my sales team.
Goal: The chatbot should answer common questions, identify who is a real prospect, and make it easy for them to book a call, without feeling like a form in disguise.
Deliverable: 1) The conversation flow: a greeting based on the page the visitor is on, qualifying questions, answers to the 5 most likely questions or objections, and a close with a booking link. 2) The criteria for classifying each visitor as hot, warm, or cold, and what the bot does in each case. 3) The system prompt, ready to configure the bot. 4) The data that should flow into the CRM and how the sales rep gets notified.
Criteria: No more than 3 or 4 questions before offering value or a booking slot, because every extra question increases drop-off. The bot should say it's an automated assistant, ask for consent before storing contact details, and hand off to a person when it can't answer.
Format: A text flowchart with numbered branches, followed by sections 2 to 4 with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
