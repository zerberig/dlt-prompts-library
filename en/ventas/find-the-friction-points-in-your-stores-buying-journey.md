Context: I run an online store selling [your product]. Here is the current process from the moment someone lands on the site until they receive their order: [description of your buying process] (browsing, cart, payment, shipping, support, and returns).
Goal: Remove the obstacles that make people abandon or not come back, and improve customer satisfaction.
Deliverable: 1) A journey map in 6 stages (discovery, evaluation, cart, checkout, delivery, and post-purchase) with the likely friction points at each. 2) Concrete fixes for each friction point, including a simpler checkout and personalized support. 3) A post-purchase follow-up plan: confirmations, shipping updates, feedback requests, and issue handling. 4) The metrics to track improvement (abandonment rate, satisfaction, and repeat purchase rate).
Criteria: Rank fixes by impact and effort, starting with the ones that don't require development work.
Format: A table by stage with the columns Stage, Friction, Fix, Impact, and Effort; then the post-purchase plan and the metrics.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
