Context: I'm running an outbound calling campaign to sell [your product or service] in [industry or market]. Here is the data from the last few weeks: [campaign data] (calls made, live contacts, conversations, meetings booked, sales, and cost; by rep, time slot, or list if you have it).
Goal: Understand where prospects drop off and what to change first to improve the campaign's return.
Deliverable: 1) The key funnel metrics calculated from my data: contact rate, conversation-to-meeting rate, meeting-to-sale rate, cost per meeting, and cost per sale. 2) A diagnosis of the 3 main bottlenecks, with evidence from the data. 3) An optimization plan with 3 to 5 experiments, each with a hypothesis, a concrete change, the metric to watch, and a duration. 4) What data I should start tracking if it's missing.
Criteria: Base every conclusion on the numbers I gave you and separate what the data shows from what is a hypothesis. If the sample is too small to conclude something, say so.
Format: A metrics table, followed by the diagnosis and plan in sections with bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
