Context: I'm about to send a sales email about [email topic] to [target audience].
Goal: Test which type of subject line drives more opens and clicks, so I learn what works with my list.
Deliverable: 5 subject line pairs for A/B testing. In each pair, A is direct and benefit-led, and B leans on curiosity or a story. For each pair, include the preview text, the hypothesis being tested, and the metric that decides the winner.
Criteria: Subject lines under 50 characters, honest about what the email contains, and free of words that commonly trigger spam filters.
Format: A table with the columns Pair, Subject A, Subject B, Preview text, Hypothesis, and Metric.
