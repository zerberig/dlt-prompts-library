Context: I run [your business], which sells [your product] online in [country or region].
Goal: Know which e-commerce trends over the next 12 to 24 months actually affect me and which ones I can take advantage of with my resources.
Deliverable: 8 to 10 trends (payments, logistics, social commerce, AI, search, consumer habits, regulation), each with what it is, evidence, likely impact on my business (high, medium, or low), and one concrete action. End with the 3 you'd prioritize and a 90-day plan for the first one.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and cite recent sources; flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. Separate established trends from passing fads.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, Evidence, Impact, and Action; then the priorities and the plan.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
