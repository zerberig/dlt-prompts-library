Context: My business [your business] sells [your product] online. My main competitors are [competitors] (names or websites).
Goal: Find gaps and opportunities to stand out on what customers value, instead of competing on price alone.
Deliverable: 1) A comparison of each competitor on value proposition, price range, assortment, shopping experience, shipping and returns, content, and reviews. 2) What customers say about them (recurring praise and complaints). 3) 3 to 5 differentiation opportunities for me, each with a concrete action for the next 30 days.
Criteria: If you have web search, review their sites and public reviews and cite where each data point comes from; flag as an estimate anything you can't verify. Keep facts separate from your interpretations.
Format: A comparison table with one competitor per column, then the opportunities as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
