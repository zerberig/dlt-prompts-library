Context: I'm launching [your product] in my online store [your business]. My audience is [target audience], my launch budget is [budget], and the planned date is [launch date].
Goal: A complete launch plan that drives sales from the first month and builds a customer base to grow from.
Deliverable: 1) Audience definition and value proposition versus 2 or 3 alternatives customers already use. 2) Measurable goals for the first month and first quarter. 3) Pre-launch, launch, and post-launch strategy by channel (social, email, ads, partnerships, and PR). 4) Conversion and retention tactics. 5) Budget by channel. 6) A weekly timeline. 7) A metrics dashboard and criteria for adjusting course.
Criteria: Fit the tactics to the real budget and explain what you'd cut if it were halved. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source.
Format: A document with numbered sections, with the timeline and budget in tables, 900 to 1,300 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
