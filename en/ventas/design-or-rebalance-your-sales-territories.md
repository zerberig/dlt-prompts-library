Context: I have a team of [number of reps] reps covering [region or market]. Here is the account and rep data: [account and rep data] (location, current revenue, estimated potential, visit frequency, and each rep's experience or specialty).
Goal: Split territories so workload and opportunity are balanced, travel is reduced, and every rep has an achievable quota.
Deliverable: 1) Design criteria and their weights (potential, number of accounts, travel time, specialty). 2) A territory proposal with the accounts or regions assigned to each rep and their resulting workload. 3) A comparison with the current setup. 4) Transition risks (customer relationships, commissions) and how to handle them. 5) How to review the balance every quarter.
Criteria: Justify each assignment with the data and point out where you made assumptions. If the data isn't enough for a reliable proposal, tell me what's missing before proposing anything.
Format: A territory table with the columns Rep, Regions or accounts, Current revenue, Potential, and Estimated workload; then the remaining sections in bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
