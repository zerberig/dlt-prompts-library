Context: My company [your company] sells [your product or service], and leads come in through calls, forms, and social media. Right now each rep handles them their own way and many go cold.
Goal: A documented process that takes every lead from arrival to purchase or disqualification, with clear owners and deadlines, so we convert more.
Deliverable: 1) A process map by stage: capture, first contact, qualification, nurturing, proposal, close, and disqualification. 2) For each stage: owner, maximum response time, action, tool, and criteria to advance. 3) A simple lead-scoring model with 5 or 6 criteria. 4) The follow-up cadence for leads who don't respond. 5) Conversion indicators by stage and how to review them weekly.
Criteria: Design it for a team of [team size] people, with the minimum needed so it actually gets followed. Prioritize speed to first contact, because leads go cold in hours, not days.
Format: A table by stage with the columns Stage, Owner, Max time, Action, Tool, and Criteria to advance; then sections 3 to 5.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
