Context: I generate leads for [your product or service] across several channels. Here is the data for the last [time period] by channel: [data by channel] (LinkedIn and the others: spend, leads, qualified leads, opportunities, sales, revenue, and time to close).
Goal: Decide how much to invest in LinkedIn versus my other channels based on business results, not just lead volume.
Deliverable: 1) A comparison table by channel with cost per lead, cost per qualified lead, lead-to-opportunity rate, close rate, cost per customer, average deal size, and return. 2) LinkedIn's strengths and weaknesses against each channel. 3) A recommended budget split for the next period. 4) The design of a monthly dashboard: metrics, a suggested visualization for each, and the data source.
Criteria: Show your calculations. Point out attribution limitations (for example, customers who touched several channels) and how they affect the conclusion.
Format: Tables for the comparison and the dashboard; analysis and recommendation as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
