Context: I sell [your product or service] to [target audience]. I have leads who came in through [lead source] (a guide download, a call, or a webinar), but they're not ready to buy yet.
Goal: Stay top of mind, answer their questions, and bring them to a sales conversation when they're ready, without overwhelming them.
Deliverable: A sequence of 6 emails over 4 weeks, each with a subject line, goal, 120-180 word body, call to action, and send day. Include value content, a success story, an answer to a common objection, and a final invitation. Add rules for personalizing by behavior (opened, clicked, replied, didn't open) and when to hand the lead to sales.
Criteria: Warm tone and one goal per email. Send only to people who gave consent and always include a clear way to unsubscribe.
Format: A summary table of the sequence, then each full email.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
