Context: I want to post on LinkedIn about this milestone: [your milestone] (my business anniversary, a customer count, or a professional achievement). I work as a [your profession or role].
Goal: Thank my network genuinely and spark conversation without sounding like I'm bragging.
Deliverable: 3 versions of the post with different angles: a story about what I learned, a thank-you that mentions people who helped, and an open question to the community. Each 120-200 words, with a first line that makes people keep reading and a closing line that invites comments.
Criteria: Human, concrete tone with at least one real detail from the journey. Few emojis and no more than 3 hashtags.
Format: The 3 versions, numbered and separated.
