Context: My online store [your store] sells [your product]. My current conversion rate is [current conversion rate], and the problems I see or suspect are: [identified problems].
Goal: A prioritized plan to improve the user experience and conversion rate across the whole site over the next 90 days.
Deliverable: 1) An audit by area: homepage, navigation and search, category pages, product discovery, cart, checkout, mobile, and speed. 2) 10 to 15 improvements, each with a hypothesis, a priority based on impact, confidence, and ease, and the metric that will validate it. 3) Which ones to A/B test and which to ship directly. 4) A 3-month timeline. 5) How to avoid false conclusions if my traffic is low.
Criteria: Explain the reasoning behind each priority. If you mention industry benchmarks, cite the source or flag them as estimates.
Format: An improvements table with the columns Improvement, Hypothesis, Impact, Confidence, Ease, Score, and Metric; then the timeline.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
