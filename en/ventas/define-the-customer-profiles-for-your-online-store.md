Context: I sell [your product] online. What I know about my customers today is: [what you know about your customers] (sales data, comments, messages, surveys, or just gut feeling).
Goal: Be clear about who I'm talking to so I can focus ads, content, and email campaigns on the segments most likely to buy.
Deliverable: 2 or 3 customer profiles, each with: situation and context, the problem or desire that leads them to buy, objections, where they look for information, what makes them decide, and one key message that speaks directly to them. Then, how to segment my customer list with the data I already have and which campaign I'd run for each segment next peak season.
Criteria: Separate what comes from my data from what is a hypothesis, and suggest a low-effort way to validate each hypothesis (for example, a short survey or reviewing customer reviews). Avoid stereotypes and demographic details that don't affect the buying decision.
Format: One profile card per persona with bullets, then a table with the columns Segment, How to identify it, and Suggested campaign.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
