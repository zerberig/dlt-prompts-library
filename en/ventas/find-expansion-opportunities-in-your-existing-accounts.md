Context: I work in sales or customer success at a company that offers [your product or service]. Here is a customer's current usage: [customer's current usage] (plan, features they use and don't use, number of users, results, and complaints). Our catalog also includes [other products or plans].
Goal: Determine whether there's a genuine upsell or cross-sell opportunity that adds value for the customer, and raise it at the right moment.
Deliverable: 1) An analysis of usage patterns: signs the customer is ready to grow and signs of risk. 2) The 2 or 3 opportunities that make the most sense, each with the problem it solves, the value to the customer, and the best moment to propose it. 3) A short email to open the conversation. 4) Questions for the call.
Criteria: If the customer shows churn risk or isn't using what they already have, recommend improving adoption before selling more, because a forced sale increases cancellations.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets; the email in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
