Context: My brand [your brand] sells [your product] online and hardly anyone knows it yet. My monthly promotion budget is [budget] and my audience is [target audience].
Goal: Run a 4-week campaign that builds awareness and drives qualified traffic to the store, so I can later turn it into sales with retargeting and email.
Deliverable: 1) A creative concept for the campaign with one core message and an internal name. 2) A week-by-week plan combining organic content, micro-creator collaborations, and paid ads, with a suggested budget split. 3) A one-page brief for the creators. 4) The metrics that will tell me whether the campaign worked.
Criteria: Favor small creators with a closely matched audience over mass reach, because they usually earn more trust per dollar spent. Every paid collaboration must be disclosed as advertising, as platforms and consumer protection laws require. Flag as an estimate any cost or performance figure you can't back with a source.
Format: Sections with headings; the weekly plan as a table with the columns Week, Goal, Actions, Channel, and Budget.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
