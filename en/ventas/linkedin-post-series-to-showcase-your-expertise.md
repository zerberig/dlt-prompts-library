Context: I'm a [your profession or role] and I help [ideal client] to [result you deliver]. Some cases or experiences I can share: [cases or experiences].
Goal: A post series that demonstrates my expertise with real examples and gets potential clients to reach out.
Deliverable: 5 ready-to-use posts, each in a different format: a client case with before and after, a common mistake in my industry and how to avoid it, an opinion on an industry practice, a step-by-step practical guide, and a behind-the-scenes look at my work. Each 150-250 words, with a strong first line and a closing line that invites comments or a message. Add an image or carousel suggestion for each post.
Criteria: Use only the cases I gave you, with no invented results or client names. An approachable expert tone, without unnecessary jargon.
Format: Numbered posts in the suggested publishing order.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
