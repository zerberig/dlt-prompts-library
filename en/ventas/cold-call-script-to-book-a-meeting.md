Context: I sell [your product or service] to [type of customer] and make cold calls to book meetings. What sets us apart most is [your value proposition].
Goal: A script that lets me, in about 2 minutes, get attention, earn the right to keep talking, and close with a meeting on the calendar.
Deliverable: A script with: an opener saying who I am and why I'm calling, a request for 30 seconds of their time, a value proposition centered on a customer problem, 2 qualifying questions, a close proposing a specific date, and short replies to "just send me some info", "I don't have time", and "we already have a provider". Include a voicemail version under 30 seconds.
Criteria: Natural spoken language with short sentences, no pressure tactics or fake urgency. The script should sound like a conversation, not a reading, so mark where to pause and where to adapt based on the answer. If you have web search, remind me to check the telemarketing rules and do-not-call registries that apply in my country.
Format: Blocks by stage, with the script text in quotes and a short note on why each part works.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
