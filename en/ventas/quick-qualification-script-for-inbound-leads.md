Context: I take or return calls from people interested in [your product or service]. My ideal customer is [ideal customer].
Goal: Know within 5 to 7 minutes whether the lead is worth a proposal, without the call feeling like an interrogation.
Deliverable: 8 to 10 qualifying questions covering need, urgency, decision-maker, budget, and fit with my ideal customer; a 0-3 scoring table per criterion with the rule for classifying the lead as advance, nurture, or disqualify; and a closing line for each case.
Criteria: Open, conversational questions in an order that flows naturally; the budget question asked tactfully.
Format: A numbered list of questions with the criterion each one assesses, followed by the scoring table and the closing lines.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
