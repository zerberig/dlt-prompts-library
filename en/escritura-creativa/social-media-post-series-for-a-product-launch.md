Context: I'm launching [your product] on [launch date]. My audience is [target audience], and I'll post on [social networks you use] (for example, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, or X).
Goal: Build anticipation before launch, convert on launch day, and keep the conversation going afterward.
Deliverable: A series of 8 posts across three phases: teaser (3 posts with hints or sneak peeks), launch (2 posts), and follow-up (3 posts with real use, FAQs, or behind the scenes). For each post: suggested day, network, format (carousel, short video, image, or text), full copy adapted to that network, a concrete visual idea, and a call to action.
Criteria: The series should tell a continuous story, not a set of disconnected posts. Use urgency only with real data (dates, spots, or launch pricing I give you). Adapt length and tone to each network.
Format: A table with the columns Day, Phase, Network, Format, Copy, Visual idea, and CTA.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
