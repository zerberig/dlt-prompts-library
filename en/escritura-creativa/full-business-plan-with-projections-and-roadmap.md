Context: I want to launch [your business] in [city or market], aimed at [ideal customer]. My starting budget is [starting budget in your currency]. The plan is for [who the plan is for, e.g. partners, investors, or a bank].
Goal: A business plan that convinces that reader and helps me make decisions during the first 12 months.
Deliverable:
1. Executive summary, half a page max.
2. Problem, solution, and value proposition.
3. Market analysis: estimated size, trends, and customer profile.
4. Competitive landscape: 4-6 competitors or alternatives and how I stand apart.
5. Revenue model and pricing.
6. Marketing and sales strategy for year one.
7. 3-year financial projections with explicit assumptions: revenue, costs, break-even, and cash flow.
8. Quarterly roadmap with measurable goals, plus the main risks and how to mitigate them.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it for market and competitor data and cite the source. Flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate. Show the assumptions behind the projections so I can adjust them. Recommend that I validate tax, legal, and financial points with an accountant or advisor.
Format: A document with the 8 sections as headings; competitors and projections in tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
