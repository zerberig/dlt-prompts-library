Write a satirical poem of 16-20 lines about [a modern-life absurdity], for example meetings that could have been an email. I'll share it on [where you'll publish it]. Use exaggeration, irony, and wordplay to show how ridiculous the situation is through the eyes of someone who lives it every day. Aim the humor at the habit or the system, not at specific people or groups, so the ones laughing are the ones who recognize themselves. Use rhymed quatrains for a punchline rhythm and end on the line that lands the hardest. Add 3 alternative titles.
