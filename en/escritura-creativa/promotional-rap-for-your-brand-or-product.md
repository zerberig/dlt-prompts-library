Context: I want a rap to promote [your product or brand] to [target audience]. What I most want to highlight is [main benefit].
Goal: A song people remember and repeat, for short-form video, radio, or social media.
Deliverable: 1) Full lyrics structured as intro, verse 1, hook, verse 2, hook, and outro. 2) A 30-second version (hook plus 4 lines) for ads. 3) Production notes: approximate tempo in BPM, beat style, and which parts the vocals should punch.
Criteria: A hook of 4 lines max, including the product name and easy to sing along to. Internal rhymes and clever wordplay with a natural flow. Talk about the benefit through images from the audience's real life, not a feature list. No claims the product can't back up and no comparisons with competing brands.
Format: Lyrics with each section labeled; production notes as bullets at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
