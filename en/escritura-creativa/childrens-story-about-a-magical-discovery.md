Context: I want a story to read aloud to children aged [children's age]. The main character is [main character], who discovers [magical object or place] that leads to a world full of talking creatures.
Goal: A story that entertains, sparks imagination, and leaves a meaningful idea without preaching.
Deliverable: A 700 to 1,000-word story with a title.
Criteria: Age-appropriate vocabulary, short sentences, and a repeated phrase or sound kids can say along. The main character solves the problem through their own courage or wit; adults or magical creatures only help. An adventure with some tension, but nothing truly scary before bedtime. A warm ending that closes the story.
Format: Text split into 6 to 8 short blocks, like the pages of a picture book, each with an illustration suggestion in parentheses.
