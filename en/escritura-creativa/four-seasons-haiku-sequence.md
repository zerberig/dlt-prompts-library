Write a sequence of 4 haiku about [a place you know well], one for each season. Follow the traditional form: 3 lines of 5, 7, and 5 syllables, a word or image that signals the season, and a single concrete moment observed in the present tense, like a photograph. Let nature carry the emotion instead of naming it, and avoid adjectives like "beautiful" or "magical". If seasons barely change in that place, use its real climate shifts (rainy and dry spells, blooms). Head each haiku with the season's name and show the syllable count for each line.
