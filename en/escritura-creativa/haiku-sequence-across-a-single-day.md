Write a sequence of 5 haiku about [topic], each set at a different moment of the same day: dawn, midday, afternoon, dusk, and night.
Follow the 5-7-5 syllable structure and, in each haiku, include a reference to the season, which here is [season]. Each haiku should capture one concrete moment, with images from nature or daily life, no explained metaphors or abstract reflections. Aim for a small cut or contrast between the second and third lines.
Deliver the haiku numbered, with a title for the sequence, and the syllable count per line under each one.
