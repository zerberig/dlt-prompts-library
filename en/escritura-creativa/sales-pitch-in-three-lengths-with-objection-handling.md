Context: I run [your business] and sell to [ideal customer]. What sets us apart is [your differentiators]. Customers usually compare us with [competitors or alternatives].
Goal: A pitch I can say out loud in meetings, networking events, and calls, and that leads to a concrete next step.
Deliverable:
1. A 30-second, a 1-minute, and a 2-minute pitch.
2. The 5 most likely objections from my ideal customer, each with a short answer.
3. 3 opening questions to uncover the customer's problem before I present.
Criteria: Start with the customer's problem, not the company's history. Use short sentences that are easy to say. Stand out with concrete facts instead of knocking competitors, because that erodes trust. Each version ends with a clear invitation (book a demo, a trial, or a visit). Don't invent numbers or customer results; if a figure would help, leave a placeholder marked "to fill in".
Format: Each pitch under its own heading with an estimated speaking time; objections in a table with the columns Objection and Response.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
