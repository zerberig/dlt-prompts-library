Write a short ode to [everyday object], inspired by the elemental odes that celebrate simple things. I want readers to look at that object as if for the first time.
Build the poem from concrete, observable details: texture, sound, smell, wear, who uses it, and at what time of day. Choose surprising but clear metaphors over generic adjectives like "beautiful" or "magical", which tell the reader nothing. The tone can be [desired tone].
Deliver the poem in free verse, 20 to 30 lines, with its own title, followed by two lines explaining the central image that holds the poem together.
