Write 5 limericks about [situation, person, or business you want to portray]. I'll use them in [where you'll use them], for example an internal newsletter, a toast, or a presentation. Keep the form: 5 lines rhyming AABBA, lines 1, 2, and 5 longer, lines 3 and 4 short and quick, with the punchline in the last line. Draw the humor from recognizable, absurd details of the situation, in an affectionate tone that doesn't expose or ridicule anyone. Give each limerick a short title and point out which one works best read aloud.
