Context: I'm doing a stand-up set of about 5 minutes on [set topic] for [venue and type of audience]. These are real anecdotes of mine you can use: [your personal anecdotes].
Goal: A set that sounds like me, gets frequent laughs, and feels like a story rather than loose jokes.
Deliverable: The full set in 3 or 4 bits, with an opener that introduces me fast, clear punchlines, at least one callback near the end, and a closing line.
Criteria: Build the humor on observation and my anecdotes; you can exaggerate details but keep the true core. If the topic is political or current events, aim at situations, contradictions, and public figures in their public role, not at groups for who they are. Short sentences that are easy to say out loud.
Format: Script-style text with stage notes in parentheses for pauses, gestures, or voice changes; at the end, the 3 strongest jokes in case I need to cut to 3 minutes.
