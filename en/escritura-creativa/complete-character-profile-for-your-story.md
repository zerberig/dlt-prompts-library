Context: I'm writing [type of work] set in [setting], and I need to develop [character type and their role in the story].
Goal: A character with enough depth that their choices feel inevitable rather than dictated by the plot.
Deliverable: A profile with: appearance and one memorable physical detail; personality, including an internal contradiction; backstory with 2 or 3 defining moments; what they want (external goal) versus what they need (internal need); their core fear and a flaw that costs them dearly; skills; key relationships; quirks and way of speaking, with 3 sample lines of dialogue; and how they might change over the story.
Criteria: Avoid flat archetypes; every trait should have a reason in their past. Flaws should have real consequences in the plot, not be merely endearing.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets, 500 to 700 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
