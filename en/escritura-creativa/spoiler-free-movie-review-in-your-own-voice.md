Context: I want to publish a review of [movie title] on [where you'll publish it]. Here's how it hit me: [your personal take: what moved you, what bothered you, which scene stuck].
Goal: A review that helps people decide whether to watch it and sounds like my opinion, not a fact sheet.
Deliverable: A 500-700 word review with a strong title and a 1-10 rating with its reasoning.
Criteria: Open with the emotional experience of watching it. Cover plot, themes, tone, acting, direction, cinematography, score, and pacing only where they add to the opinion; you can be critical as long as you back it up. No spoilers: don't reveal twists or the ending. If you have web search, check facts like year, director, and cast; don't make any up.
Format: Title, body in paragraphs, and a final line saying who the movie is for.
