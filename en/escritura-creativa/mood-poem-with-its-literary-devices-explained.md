Write a poem of 14-20 lines that describes [a specific scene], for example a market at dawn or a storm over the city, and conveys a mood of [mood you want]. Build that mood with concrete devices: at least 3 metaphors, 2 similes, alliteration or assonance that echoes the sounds of the scene, and one personification. The devices should serve the scene and not read like an exercise. After the poem, give me an annotated version as a table with the columns Line, Device, and Intended effect, so I can use it as study or workshop material.
