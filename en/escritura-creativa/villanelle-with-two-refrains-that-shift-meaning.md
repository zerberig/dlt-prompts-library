Write a villanelle about [central theme or emotion]; it works especially well for loss, longing, or resilience. Keep the form: 19 lines in five tercets and a closing quatrain, only two rhymes (ABA in the tercets, ABAA in the quatrain), and two refrains: line 1 returns at the end of the second and fourth tercets, line 3 at the end of the third and fifth, and both close the quatrain together. Make each repetition shift meaning based on what surrounds it, so the refrain feels like an evolving obsession rather than an echo. Before the poem, propose 3 refrain pairs and pick the strongest.
