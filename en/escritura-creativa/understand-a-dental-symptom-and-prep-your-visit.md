Context: I have this dental issue: [your symptom] (for example, sensitivity to cold). It started [how long ago], and these are my hygiene habits: [your brushing and flossing habits].
Goal: I want to understand what usually causes this symptom and show up better prepared for my dentist appointment; I'm not looking for a diagnosis.
Deliverable: 1) The most common causes of this kind of issue, explained in plain language. 2) Reasonable care and habit changes until my appointment (brushing technique, flossing, type of toothpaste). 3) Warning signs that mean I should get care soon. 4) A list of 5 questions to ask my dentist.
Criteria: Present causes as possibilities, not conclusions, and remind me that only a dentist can diagnose after examining me. Don't recommend medications with doses.
Format: Four sections with headings and bullets, 400 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
