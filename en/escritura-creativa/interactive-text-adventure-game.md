I want to play an interactive text adventure set in [game setting], with a [adventure tone] tone.
How it works: you narrate and I type what my character does (for example, "open the door" or "talk to the guard"). Start by describing where my character wakes up and what they see, hear, and smell, in 80 to 150 words. After each action, narrate only the consequences and what the character perceives; don't make choices for me or move the story forward without my action.
Game rules: keep an inventory and a health status, shown on one line at the end of each turn. Include puzzles and dangers that can be solved with wit, and let my choices have real consequences, including the possibility of losing. If I write something in curly braces, like {this}, it's an out-of-game comment and you answer out of character.
Turn format: the narration and, at the end, the inventory and health line. Start now.
