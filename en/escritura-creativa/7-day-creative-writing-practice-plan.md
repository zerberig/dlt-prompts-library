Context: I usually write [what you normally write], and I feel like I always use the same tone and themes. I have about [minutes available per day] to write.
Goal: A week of practice that forces me to try other perspectives, emotions, and styles.
Deliverable: 7 prompts, one per day, each in a different genre: science fiction, romance, mystery, personal nonfiction, a free verse poem, flash fiction, and one of your choice. For each day: the prompt, a technical constraint (narrator, tense, length, or a banned word), the core emotion to work with, and one question to review my piece when I'm done.
Criteria: Each prompt should be doable in the time I have. Difficulty should rise across the week, and each day should take me a bit further from my usual style.
Format: One section per day with a heading and bullets.
