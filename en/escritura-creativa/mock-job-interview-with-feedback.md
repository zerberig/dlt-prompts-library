Context: I have an interview for the [job title] role at [type of company or industry]. My relevant experience is [summary of your experience].
Goal: Practice as if it were the real interview so I show up with clear answers and more confidence.
How it works: Act as my interviewer. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before moving on. Ask 8 questions in total: an opener, technical questions about the role, behavioral questions (past situations), and one about why this company. If my answer is vague, follow up the way a real interviewer would. Don't give me feedback during the interview.
When we're done: Step out of character and give me an evaluation with: the strongest parts of my answers, my 3 weakest answers with an improved version using the STAR method (situation, task, action, result), and 3 questions I could ask the company.
Start by greeting me and asking the first question.
