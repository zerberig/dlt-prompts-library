Context: I want to do this project at home: [project you want to do] (for example, an outdoor seating area for having guests over). My space is [space dimensions and features], my budget is [budget in your currency], and my DIY experience is [experience level].
Goal: Be able to do it myself with a result that looks good and is safe.
Deliverable: 1) 2 or 3 design options for my budget, with pros and cons. 2) For the recommended option: a materials list with quantities and approximate cost, the tools I'll need, and numbered steps in plain language. 3) Common beginner mistakes and how to avoid them. 4) Safety precautions.
Criteria: Realistic steps for someone at my level. Flag prices as estimates, since they vary by area. Point out which parts are best left to a professional (electrical work, load-bearing structures, or gas).
Format: Sections with headings; materials in a table with the columns Material, Quantity, and Approximate cost.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
