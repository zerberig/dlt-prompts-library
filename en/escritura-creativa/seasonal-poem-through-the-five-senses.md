Write a poem that captures [season] as it's lived in [place where you experience it] (for example, rainy season in Mexico City or winter in Patagonia).
Organize it into five stanzas, one per sense: what you see, hear, smell, taste, and touch at that time of year. Use figurative language (metaphor, synesthesia, personification) so each image feels physical and close. In the final stanza, bring all five senses together in a single scene and let the emotion that season stirs show through without naming it.
Deliver the poem with a title, 20 to 30 lines, and avoid each season's clichés (golden leaves, white snow) unless you transform them.
