Context: I want a short story that explores the themes of [theme 1] and [theme 2], with a protagonist who is [character type].
Goal: A surprise ending that feels fair on a reread, not like a trick.
Deliverable: A 1,000 to 1,500-word story with a title and, separately, a list of the clues you planted.
Criteria: The twist should change the meaning of what the reader has already read, not just add new information. Plant at least 3 clues that look like throwaway details on first read. Avoid the most worn twists (it was all a dream, the narrator was dead, a secret twin) unless you give them a real spin. The themes should play out in the protagonist's choices, not be explained.
Format: Narration with dialogue; after the story, a numbered list of the clues with the exact sentence where each appears.
