Context: I have these symptoms: [your symptoms and since when]. Details that may matter: [age, existing conditions, and current medications].
Goal: Walk into my doctor's appointment organized and understand what's going on a bit better, without replacing a professional's evaluation.
Deliverable:
1. A chronological summary of my symptoms I can walk my doctor through in one minute.
2. General information on causes commonly associated with these symptoms, in plain language and without giving me a diagnosis.
3. Warning signs that would mean I should seek urgent care.
4. Questions worth asking at the appointment and things to track until then.
Criteria: Be clear about what can't be known without an exam or tests. Don't recommend medications or doses. Recommend that I see a doctor and, if anything I describe sounds urgent, say so first.
Format: Sections with headings and short lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
