Context: I'm producing a [length] minute educational video for kids aged [kids' age] about [video topic], for example how volcanoes work or the history of writing.
Goal: Help them understand the main idea, remember it, and want to learn more.
Deliverable: A full script with a host character, an opening built on a question or mystery, 3 or 4 key moments on the topic, a short 4-8 line rhyme to remember the core concept, and a closing challenge to try at home.
Criteria: Age-appropriate language, short sentences, comparisons to things kids already know, and gentle humor. Check that the facts are correct and simplify without saying anything false. Note which images, animations, or effects go with each part.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Narration or dialogue, and What's on screen.
