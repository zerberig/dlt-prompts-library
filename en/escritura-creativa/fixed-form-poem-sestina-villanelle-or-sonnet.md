Context: I want a poem about [topic] that explores its nuances and contradictions, not just one side.
Goal: Use a fixed form so repetition and structure do part of the work: the theme should come back and shift meaning with each turn.
Deliverable: A poem in the form of a [poetic form] (sestina, villanelle, or sonnet), with a title. Before the poem, the scheme you'll follow (end words and their rotation in a sestina, refrains in a villanelle, rhyme and meter in a sonnet).
Criteria: Follow the form rigorously; if you loosen it anywhere, say so. Repeated words or lines should take on a different meaning each time they appear, because that's the point of the form. Clear, contemporary language, with no archaic filler to pad the meter.
Format: Scheme, poem, and at the end an 80 to 120-word commentary on how the form reinforces the theme and how the key words shift through the poem.
