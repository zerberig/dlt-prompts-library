Context: I have [your business or product] for [target audience]. The brand values are [your brand values], and the campaign goal is [campaign goal] over [duration].
Goal: A creative concept that connects the brand's values with what customers truly care about, and that the team can execute across channels.
Deliverable: 1) The big idea in one sentence and the story behind it (who the hero is, what problem they face, and how the brand helps). 2) 5 tagline options. 3) Key messages tailored to 2 or 3 audience segments. 4) A distribution plan by channel with content format, frequency, and sample copy. 5) 3 metrics to measure results and how each would be calculated.
Criteria: The customer is the hero of the story; the brand is the guide. Only make promises the product can keep. If you have web search, check current trends and references in the industry and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections with headings; the channel plan as a table with the columns Channel, Format, Frequency, and Example.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
