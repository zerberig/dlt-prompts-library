Context: My current routine looks like this: [your daily routine and schedule]. What stresses me most is [main sources of stress] and I'd like to achieve this: [your goal, e.g. sleep better or disconnect from work].
Goal: Build sustainable habits for managing stress, starting with small changes that fit into my day.
Deliverable:
1. A short read on the patterns in my routine that may be adding stress.
2. 3-5 concrete habits, each with when to do it, how long it takes, and how to start in week one.
3. A 4-week plan that adds habits gradually.
4. A simple way to track progress and what to do if I skip days.
Criteria: Favor broadly supported strategies (sleep, movement, breaks, boundaries with work, breathing) and fit them to my real life, not an ideal routine. If stress is seriously affecting my sleep, health, or mood, recommend that I talk to a health or mental health professional.
Format: Sections with headings; the weekly plan in a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
