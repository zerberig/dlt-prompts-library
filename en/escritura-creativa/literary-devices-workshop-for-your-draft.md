Context: I wrote this draft about [topic of the piece]: [paste your draft here]. It can be a poem or a prose passage I want to turn into a poem.
Goal: Improve my piece while learning to use literary devices, without losing my voice.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis: what works and where the language feels flat. 2) 6 to 8 targeted suggestions, each with the original line or phrase, the device you propose (alliteration, metaphor, personification, anaphora, enjambment, or another), the rewritten version, and why it's better. 3) A full revised version that integrates only the best suggestions.
Criteria: Respect my tone and vocabulary; suggest, don't rewrite everything. Prefer a few well-used devices over many that clutter the piece.
Format: The diagnosis in one paragraph, suggestions in a table with the columns Original, Device, Proposal, and Why, and the revised version at the end.
