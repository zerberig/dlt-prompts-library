Context: I run [type of group, e.g. workshop, class, or book club] and want exercises about ethical dilemmas in [field of the dilemma], for example artificial intelligence, medicine, or family businesses.
Goal: Have each participant write a short piece where no option is clearly right and they have to explore the gray areas.
Deliverable: 8 writing prompts. Each with a concrete situation (character, place, and moment), the dilemma framed as 2 or 3 options with real costs, a creative constraint (point of view, length, or narrative time), and a question the story should leave open.
Criteria: Vary the tone and genre (realist, science fiction, thriller, dark humor) and the scale of the dilemma, from the personal to the social. Avoid scenarios where one option is obviously the good one, because they kill the tension.
Format: A numbered list with a short title for each prompt.
