Context: I want a poem about [topic] that incorporates elements of [culture or tradition] (for example, Mexica mythology, Andean tradition, or Caribbean culture).
Goal: Put the culture at the heart of the poem (its images, myths, symbols, and ways of naming the world), not as exotic decoration.
Deliverable: A 20 to 32-line poem with a title and, underneath, a short glossary of the terms, mythic figures, or symbols you use.
Criteria: Use precise, verifiable references; if you're unsure what a symbol or myth means, leave it out. If the tradition has its own poetic forms or devices (repetition, parallelism, chants), bring them into the rhythm. Avoid stereotypes and the tourist's gaze. If a word comes from an Indigenous language, use its accepted spelling.
Format: A free verse poem or the traditional form you choose, followed by the glossary in bullets.
