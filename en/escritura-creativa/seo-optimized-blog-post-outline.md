Context: I run a blog about [your blog's topic] and want to publish a post on [article topic]. The main keyword is "[keyword]" and my readers are [target audience].
Goal: An outline that answers what searchers want better than the competition, so I or a writer can develop it.
Deliverable: An H1 title (plus 2 alternatives), a meta description of 155 characters max, an H2 and H3 structure with 1–2 lines on what each section covers and its suggested length, 3 FAQs for a closing section, and 2 internal linking ideas.
Criteria: First identify the search intent (informational, comparison, or purchase) and order the sections around it. Include natural keyword variations without forcing them. If you have web search, review the current top results to spot gaps the article can fill; flag any volumes or data you can't confirm as estimates.
Format: A hierarchical bulleted outline, with the recommended total length at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
