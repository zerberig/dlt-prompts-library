Context: I want to give my group of friends an adventure story for [occasion]. They're the main characters: [your friends' names with one trait and one skill each].
Goal: A fun story where everyone recognizes themselves and gets a moment to shine.
Deliverable: A 1,500-2,500 word story in 3 chapters, where the group hunts for a treasure or lost object in [adventure setting].
Criteria: Each friend gets past at least one obstacle thanks to their skill or trait, and at least one causes a problem because of that same trait. Include surprises, a rival or a trap, a false lead, and an ending where the real treasure has to do with the group, without getting sappy. Use inside jokes only if I give them to you, and keep the humor affectionate.
Format: Title, 3 chapters with subheadings, and a short epilogue.
