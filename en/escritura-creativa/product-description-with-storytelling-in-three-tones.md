Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. Its main features are [main features], and the problem it solves is [problem it solves].
Goal: Test different tones to find out which one connects best with my customers in my online store and on social media.
Deliverable: Three descriptions of the same product, 100 to 150 words each: 1) Narrative: opens with a short scene of a customer before and after using the product. 2) Direct: clear benefits in bullets for people comparing options quickly. 3) Playful: light humor and personality without losing the key information. For each, a headline and a call to action.
Criteria: Each version should explain what makes the product unique and why it matters in the customer's real life. Only verifiable claims; no "best on the market" without backing.
Format: Three labeled blocks and, at the end, a recommendation on which version to use on which channel.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
