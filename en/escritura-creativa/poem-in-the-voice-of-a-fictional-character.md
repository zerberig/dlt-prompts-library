Write a first-person poem in the voice of [your own character or one from a public domain work] at this point in their story: [character's moment or situation]. Capture how they speak, their obsessions, what they hide, and what they want, as if they were writing only for themselves. Use details from their world and era, and let an inner contradiction show. If the character is mine, use only what I describe and don't invent major facts about their story. Length: 16-28 lines, with a title. At the end, add 2 lines on what the poem reveals about the character that could help me write them in prose.
