Context: I'm writing a novel set in [time and place of the story]. My protagonist is [your protagonist and what they firmly believe].
Goal: An opening scene that hooks from the first line and sets up a moral dilemma that tests those beliefs.
Deliverable:
1. The opening scene, 1,000-1,500 words.
2. Three possible paths for the story depending on the choice they make, each in 3-4 lines.
Criteria: The dilemma comes from a concrete, urgent situation, with a real cost on every side. Show the inner conflict through action, dialogue, and details of the setting instead of explaining it. End the scene right before or right after the decision, so the reader has to keep going.
Format: The scene in prose with a chapter title; the paths as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
