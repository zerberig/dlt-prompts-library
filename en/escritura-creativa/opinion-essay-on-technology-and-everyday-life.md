Context: I want to publish an essay in [where it will be published] on how [technology] is changing [aspect of human life] (for example, friendship, memory, or creative work). My readers are [target audience].
Goal: A piece that makes people think, not a pamphlet for or against.
Deliverable: A 900 to 1,200-word essay with a title and subtitle.
Criteria: Open with a concrete scene or anecdote, not a definition. Present real benefits and costs, include the strongest argument against your position, and answer it honestly. Use current examples; if you have web search, verify data and studies, and flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate. Close with a question or proposal readers can take with them.
Format: Prose with 3 or 4 subheadings and short paragraphs.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
