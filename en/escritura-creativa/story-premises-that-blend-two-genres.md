Context: I want to write a story on the theme of [theme], and I'm drawn to combining [genre 1] with [genre 2].
Goal: Several solid premises to pick from so I can start writing today.
Deliverable: 6 premises. Each with: a working title, a specific setting (place, period, and one detail that makes it unique), a protagonist with a concrete goal, the central conflict, what's at stake if they fail, and the scene the story could open with.
Criteria: Both genres should be truly blended in the conflict, not just in the backdrop. Make the 6 premises very different from one another in tone (comic to dark) and scale (intimate to epic). Avoid each genre's most obvious clichés, or use them on purpose to subvert them.
Format: A numbered list with nested bullets. At the end, tell me which premise you recommend for a short first draft and why.
