Context: I want to write a [genre] novel with this premise: [your premise in 1-2 sentences]. I'm picturing a length of [approximate length in words or chapters].
Goal: A solid structure that lets me write without getting lost, with good pacing and well-placed twists.
Deliverable:
1. A one-sentence logline and 3 central themes.
2. Profiles for 4-6 main characters: want, fear, contradiction, and transformation arc.
3. A three-act structure with the main turning points: inciting incident, midpoint, crisis, climax, and resolution.
4. A chapter-by-chapter outline: what happens, what changes, and what question is left open at the end of each chapter.
5. 3 possible plot twists and where to place them.
Criteria: Every chapter moves the plot or a character arc, because filler chapters kill momentum. Plant the clues for each twist before it happens. Respect the genre's conventions and point out where it's worth breaking them on purpose.
Format: Numbered sections; characters and outline in tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
