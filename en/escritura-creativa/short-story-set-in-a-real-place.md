Context: I want a short story set in [real place: neighborhood, town, or city]. The theme or conflict I'm interested in is [story theme or conflict].
Deliverable: A 1,500-2,500 word story with a title.
Criteria: The place is almost another character: use sensory details, customs, ways of speaking, and some trace of its history, without turning the text into a travel guide. If you have web search, check the facts about the place; if not, prefer plausible, general details over made-up facts. Two or three characters with clear wants, an unexpected twist that still fits what was set up earlier, and an ending that leaves the atmosphere lingering.
Format: Prose with dialogue; at the end, a short list of the real details about the place you used.
