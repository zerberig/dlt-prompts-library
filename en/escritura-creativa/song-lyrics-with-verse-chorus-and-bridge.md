Context: I want a song about [story or emotion of the song] in the [music genre] genre. I'll use it to [what you'll use it for, e.g. sing it myself, set it to music with AI, or perform at an event].
Deliverable: Complete lyrics with this structure: Verse 1, Pre-chorus, Chorus, Verse 2, Pre-chorus, Chorus, Bridge, and Final chorus.
Criteria: The chorus sticks on first listen: short lines, a repeated hook, and one clear central idea. The verses tell the story with concrete details and move it forward; the bridge shifts perspective or energy. Match vocabulary, rhyme, and meter to the genre's conventions, and avoid worn-out lines from well-known songs.
Format: Each section under its name as a heading; at the end, one line with an approximate tempo in BPM and the suggested musical mood.
