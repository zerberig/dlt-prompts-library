Context: I want a poem about [topic] inspired by the way [poet or literary movement] writes.
Goal: Understand and practice that style through a new poem, not a copy or a collage of famous lines.
Deliverable: 1) A short description of 4 or 5 technical traits of the style (line type, recurring images, syntax, tone, themes). 2) An original 16 to 28-line poem applying those traits to the topic. 3) Notes showing where each trait appears in the poem.
Criteria: Don't quote or paraphrase the author's actual lines; the goal is the technique, not literal imitation. If the author is contemporary, draw only on general devices of their movement. Use current language and references where the topic calls for it.
Format: Three sections with headings; notes as a numbered list.
