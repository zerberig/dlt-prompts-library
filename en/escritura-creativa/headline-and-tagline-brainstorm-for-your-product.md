Context: My product or service is [your product or service], for [target audience]. Its main benefit is [main benefit] and the brand tone is [brand tone].
Goal: Have options for ads, the homepage, and social media, and quickly choose which ones to test.
Deliverable: 10 headlines and 5 taglines.
Criteria: Vary the angles: direct benefit, problem solved, curiosity, proof or data point, and emotion. Keep each headline to 10 words max and each tagline to 6, because they need to land at a glance. Favor clarity over cleverness: if a pun confuses, drop it. Avoid claims I can't back up, like "number one".
Format: A table with the columns Headline, Angle, and Where to use it; taglines as a numbered list; at the end, your 2 favorites and why.
