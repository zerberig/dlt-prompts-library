I want a free verse poem about the connection between [personal theme] and [related concept] in my life (for example, my grandmother's kitchen and the idea of home).
Before writing, ask me 5 questions, one at a time, to gather concrete details: a specific moment, objects, smells and sounds, something someone said, and how I feel remembering it today. Wait for each answer before asking the next question.
Using my answers, write an 18 to 30-line poem that uses my own images and words where possible, with lines of varying length and line breaks that mark the emotional pauses. Intimate and honest in tone, without explaining the emotion or ending on a moral. After the poem, offer me 2 alternative titles.
