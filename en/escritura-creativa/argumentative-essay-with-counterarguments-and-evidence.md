Context: I need an argumentative essay defending this position: [your position on the topic]. It's for [audience or context, e.g. a college class or an op-ed] and should run about [length in words] words.
Goal: Win over a skeptical reader, not just people who already agree.
Deliverable: The full essay and, separately, a list of the sources or types of evidence you used.
Criteria: A clear thesis in the first paragraph and every argument backed by concrete evidence (data, studies, examples, or cases). Give a section to the 2 strongest counterarguments and answer them honestly, acknowledging what's valid in them. Mix logic with at least one human example that connects. If you have web search, use verifiable sources and cite them; flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate.
Format: Title, introduction, 3 arguments, counterarguments, and conclusion, in paragraphs; source list at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
