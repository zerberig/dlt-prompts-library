Context: I lead [type of group] (a class, a workshop, or a book club), and I want to open a conversation about [topic] with a poem.
Goal: A poem that raises questions instead of handing out answers, so the group can use it as a starting point for reflection.
Deliverable: 1) An original 18 to 28-line poem that poses a tension or dilemma about the topic and ends with an open question or an ambiguous image. 2) A guide with 6 discussion questions moving from the literal (what the poem says) to the personal (what it has to do with me). 3) A short 10-minute writing activity to close the session.
Criteria: The poem shouldn't moralize or draw conclusions for the reader. Accessible language for the group, with at least one powerful image everyone can discuss.
Format: Three sections with headings; questions as a numbered list.
