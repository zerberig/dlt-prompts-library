Design a writing challenge about [theme or setting] (for example, a futuristic city where technology has changed how people relate to each other). My level as a writer is [your level], and I want to practice [skill you want to practice], such as dialogue, character development, or plot twists.
The challenge should include: a setting with 2 or 3 very specific details, a character with an emotional conflict, a concrete opening situation, and a technical constraint that forces me to experiment (for example, writing without adjectives, telling the story backward, or using only dialogue). Add a recommended length and 3 questions to review my piece when I'm done.
Deliver the challenge in under 200 words, with short headings.
