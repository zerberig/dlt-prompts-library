Context: I'm going to perform a poem about [social or environmental cause] at [event or format, e.g. school forum, march, or social video]. The audience is [audience].
Goal: Raise awareness and move people toward one concrete action: [what you want the audience to do].
Deliverable: A spoken word poem of 2-3 minutes, with a title.
Criteria: Start from one concrete human story or image before widening to the big picture, because people connect with someone before they connect with a statistic. If you include a fact, use only one and make it verifiable; if you have web search, confirm it and give the source separately. Use repetition and shifts in rhythm that work out loud. Keep the tone firm and hopeful, without blaming the audience.
Format: The poem with a slash (/) at each long pause and, separately, the source for any fact you used.
