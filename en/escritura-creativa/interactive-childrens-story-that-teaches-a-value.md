Context: I want a story for children aged [readers' age] that teaches [value or lesson] and takes place in [story setting]. An adult will read it aloud, and I want the kids to take part.
Deliverable: A 10-12 page story. For each page: the text (2-4 sentences) and a short note on what the illustration should show. At 3 or 4 points, add an interactive pause: a question, a sound to imitate, or a choice the kids can weigh in on.
Criteria: The main character learns the value through what they do and its consequences, not because an adult explains it. Include a diverse cast without making diversity the topic. Use age-appropriate vocabulary, rhythmic repetition, and a warm ending.
Format: Title and numbered pages; at the end, 3 questions to talk about after reading.
