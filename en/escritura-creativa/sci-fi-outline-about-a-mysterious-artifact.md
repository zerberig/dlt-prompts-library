Context: I want to develop a science fiction story in which [group of characters] find a mysterious artifact on [place of discovery]. The artifact grants them [ability it grants], but it comes with unexpected consequences.
Goal: A solid foundation for writing a long story or novella, with clear rules and a real cost.
Deliverable: 1) A one-sentence logline. 2) The artifact's rules: what it does, what it takes in return, and what it can't do. 3) A short profile of each character and how the power changes them. 4) A beat outline of 10 to 12 key moments across three acts. 5) The opening scene written out, 400 to 600 words.
Criteria: Consequences should grow out of the artifact's rules, not arbitrary twists. Each character should face a different dilemma with the power. Blend wonder with rising tension that can edge toward horror. The science can be speculative but must stay consistent within the story.
Format: Numbered sections with headings; the outline as a numbered list grouped by act.
