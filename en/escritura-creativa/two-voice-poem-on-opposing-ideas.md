Context: I want to explore the relationship between [concept 1] and [concept 2], which may look like opposites or complements.
Goal: A poem that doesn't take sides right away, but lets each idea speak so the reader discovers where they intersect.
Deliverable: A poem for two voices: one block for each concept, answering each other, and a final stanza where both voices speak together.
Criteria: Each voice gets its own vocabulary, rhythm, and imagery, so they're distinct without labels. The answers should truly talk to each other (one picks up a word or image from the other and transforms it). The ending should reveal common ground or an unresolved tension, not a moral.
Format: The two voices alternating in short stanzas, one in italics and one in roman type, 24 to 36 lines in total.
