Write a poem about [landscape or natural place] (for example, a cloud forest, a desert at dawn, or a Pacific beach) that feels like being there.
Use real, specific elements of that place: names of plants, birds, or stones native to the region, the kind of light and the sounds at that time of day. Blend all five senses and let the poem's rhythm echo the place's (slow and wide, or clipped). Avoid worn formulas like "mother nature" or "birdsong" without saying which birds.
Deliver a 16 to 24-line poem with a title and, underneath, a 4-line version to use as a photo caption.
