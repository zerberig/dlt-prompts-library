Write a classic sonnet about [sonnet theme]. Use 14 lines of iambic pentameter, either Shakespearean (ABAB CDCD EFEF GG) or Petrarchan (ABBA ABBA CDE CDE); pick the one that suits the theme and tell me why. Open with the theme through a concrete image, then bring in a turn: a contradiction, a shift in perspective, or a revelation that shows another side of the theme. Keep full rhymes without bending the natural word order. Give me the sonnet with a title and, below it, a short table showing each line's syllable count and stressed syllables, so I can check it.
