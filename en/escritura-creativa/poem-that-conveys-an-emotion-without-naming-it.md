Write a poem that makes the reader feel [emotion] without ever using that word or its direct synonyms.
Convey it through a concrete situation, gestures, objects, and sensory details, the way a good short story would: show, don't tell. If it helps, start from this moment: [starting situation or memory]. Use fresh metaphors and steer clear of the most worn associations (rain for sadness, sunshine for joy).
Deliver two versions of the same poem: one in free verse, 12 to 20 lines, and one condensed into a haiku. At the end, explain in 2 lines which image carries the emotion in each version.
