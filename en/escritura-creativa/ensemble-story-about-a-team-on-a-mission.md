Context: I want a story about [group of people] who come together to [mission they must complete], set in [place and time period].
Goal: Tell a story where cooperation costs something, not an automatic happy ending.
Deliverable: A 1,200 to 1,500-word short story with a title.
Criteria: 3 to 5 characters, each with a personal aspiration that sometimes clashes with the mission. At least two obstacles: one external (the environment or a rival) and one internal (a disagreement or betrayal within the group). Each character should have a distinct voice so readers know who's speaking without dialogue tags. The climax should turn on a skill or choice that earlier looked like a flaw. Skip explicit motivational speeches; let the value of the team show through actions.
Format: Third-person narration with dialogue, split into 3 or 4 scenes separated by a line break.
