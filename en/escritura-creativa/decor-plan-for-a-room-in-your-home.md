Context: I want to redecorate [space, e.g. living room, bedroom, or home office] of about [approximate dimensions]. It's used by [who uses the space and for what]. My budget is [budget in your currency] and I want to keep [furniture or items that stay].
Goal: A more comfortable, better-looking space with a consistent style I can put together in stages.
Deliverable:
1. 2 distinct style concepts, each with a 3-5 color palette and the feeling it's going for.
2. For the best-fitting concept: furniture layout by zone, lighting (ambient, task, and accent), textiles, and decor.
3. A shopping list ranked by priority and impact, with an estimated price range.
Criteria: Respect the room's traffic flow and natural light. Prioritize high-impact, low-cost changes (paint, lighting, textiles) before new furniture. Flag prices as estimates.
Format: Sections with headings; the shopping list in a table with the columns Item, Priority, Estimated price, and Why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
