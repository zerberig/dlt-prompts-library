Context: I'm launching [your project or business] for [target audience]. I want the name to convey [brand personality].
Goal: Find a short, easy-to-remember name that works as a web domain and as a social media handle.
Deliverable: 15 name ideas grouped into three styles: invented words, blends of real words, and metaphors. For each, the suggested domain with 2 possible extensions and one sentence on why it fits.
Criteria: 8 letters max per name where possible, easy to pronounce and spell in both Spanish and English, no hyphens or numbers. Avoid names that resemble well-known brands, to stay clear of legal trouble. You can't confirm availability, so if you have web search, use it to rule out names clearly in use and mark the rest as "to verify".
Format: A table with the columns Name, Style, Suggested domain, and Why it works. At the end, your top 3 with a one-line justification.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
