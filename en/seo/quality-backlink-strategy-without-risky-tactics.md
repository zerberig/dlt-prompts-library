Context: My site [your site] promotes [your product or service] in the [industry or niche] space. Assets I can use to attract links: [available assets and resources] (original data, guides, free tools, in-house experts, partnerships).
Goal: I want to earn links from relevant sites with real audiences that build my authority without exposing me to penalties.
Deliverable: 1) 5 tactics ranked by effort-to-result for my case (for example, citable content, collaborations, broken link recovery, acting as an expert source, industry directories). 2) For each: what kind of site to look for, how to find it, and an example applied to my business. 3) A short, customizable outreach email template. 4) Criteria for judging whether a site is worth pursuing.
Criteria: No buying links, private blog networks, or mass link swaps, because they violate search engine guidelines. Favor topical relevance over third-party authority scores.
Format: Sections with headings; the template in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
