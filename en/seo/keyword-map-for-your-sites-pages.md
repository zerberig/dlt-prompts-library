Context: My business is [your business] and I offer [products or services] to [target audience]. These are the current pages on my site: [list of current pages].
Goal: I want every page to have one clear topic and keyword, so pages don't compete with each other and I know what content is missing.
Deliverable: 1) Seed keywords based on how my customers describe their need (not how I describe myself). 2) A map that assigns each current page a primary keyword, 2–4 secondary keywords, and its intent. 3) Pages that compete for the same thing (cannibalization) and what to do about them. 4) New pages worth creating to cover unmet intents.
Criteria: One main intent per page. Don't make up search volumes; point out which keywords I should validate in a tool and which metric to check. Favor terms close to purchase if my goal is sales.
Format: A table with the columns Page, Primary keyword, Secondary, Intent, and Action (keep, adjust, merge, or create).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
