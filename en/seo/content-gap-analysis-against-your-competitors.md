Context: My site is [your site] and I offer [products or services] to [target audience]. My search competitors are [competitors]. If I have it, here's keyword or page data for each site: [competitor data].
Goal: I want to know which topics and intents my competitors cover that I don't, and where I can beat them, so I can decide what content to create or improve.
Deliverable: 1) A grouped map of the topics each competitor covers. 2) Gaps: topics where they have content and I don't. 3) Topics we both cover where theirs is clearly better, and why (depth, format, freshness, demonstrated experience). 4) Opportunities nobody covers well. 5) The 10 pieces I should create or improve first, with the angle that sets me apart.
Criteria: If you have web search, review the sites and their results; if not, work only from the data I gave you and tell me what's missing. Prioritize by relevance to my business and a real chance to compete, not by volume. Don't suggest copying content; propose original angles.
Format: Sections with headings; the 10 pieces in a table with the columns Piece, Type (new or update), Gap covered, Differentiating angle, and Priority.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
