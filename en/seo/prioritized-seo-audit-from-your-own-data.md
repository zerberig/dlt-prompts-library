Context: My site is [your site and platform] and its goal is [site goal]. Here's the data I have: [site data] (Search Console exports, crawler results, a speed report, a list of key pages).
Goal: I want a clear diagnosis of what's holding back my organic visibility and a work plan my team can execute in order.
Deliverable: 1) A 5-sentence executive summary of the overall state. 2) Findings in four areas: indexing and crawling, technical and speed, content and on-page, authority and links. For each finding: evidence in the data, impact, and recommendation. 3) An action plan on three horizons: this week, this month, and this quarter.
Criteria: Base every finding on the data I gave you and cite the number or URL that supports it. If something requires information I don't have, mark it "to verify" instead of assuming. Prioritize with an impact-effort matrix, because I'd rather have 5 changes done than 40 pending.
Format: Sections with headings, a findings table with Area, Issue, Evidence, Impact, Effort, and Recommendation, and the plan as a numbered list. 600-1000 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
