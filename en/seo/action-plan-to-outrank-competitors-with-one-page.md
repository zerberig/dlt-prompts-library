Context: I want to improve the ranking of this page: [your page or its content] for the search [target keyword]. It currently sits at position [current position]. These are the pages ranking at the top: [top-ranking pages] (URL or a summary of their content).
Goal: Understand why those pages beat mine and which specific changes would make my page answer the search better.
Deliverable: 1) The main intent behind the search and whether my page covers it. 2) A comparison of my page against the top results: topics covered, depth, format, demonstrated experience, internal links, and user experience. 3) A list of 8-12 concrete actions, ordered by impact, with the text or section to add or change.
Criteria: Base the comparison on what the pages actually contain; if you can browse the web, review them, and if not, use only what I gave you. Don't recommend repeating the keyword more often; recommend covering what the searcher needs better. Be honest if the search is dominated by sites with far more authority.
Format: A comparison table, then the numbered list of actions with Impact (high, medium, low).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
