Context: I have this piece that already gets organic traffic: [content title]. It covers: [content summary]. It's read mostly by [target audience] and ranks for [main keyword].
Goal: I want to turn those readers into subscribers by offering a bonus resource tightly tied to this piece, not a generic site-wide lead magnet.
Deliverable: 5 content upgrade ideas. For each one: resource title, what it includes in 2 sentences, format (PDF checklist, template, spreadsheet, email mini-course, quiz, etc.), why it fits what the reader was looking for, a call to action, and where in the post to place it.
Criteria: Each resource should save the reader time applying what they just read and be something I can create in under a day. Concrete calls to action with no fake urgency. Tell me which of the 5 you would build first and why.
Format: A numbered list with a subheading per field; at the end, your recommendation in 2-3 sentences.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
