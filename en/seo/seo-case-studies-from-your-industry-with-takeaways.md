Context: I run a business in [your industry or niche] and want to improve my search rankings. My current situation is: [your site's current situation] (new site, flat traffic, strong competition, etc.).
Goal: I want to learn from companies in my sector that have already gotten results from SEO, so I can decide which strategies are worth copying and which don't fit my size.
Deliverable: 4 to 6 documented cases. For each: company and context, what they did, the reported result, the source, and the specific lesson I could apply in the next 30 days.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to find published cases and cite the source for each; if a case has no verifiable source, drop it instead of making it up. Prioritize small and mid-sized companies, because they look more like my situation. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Company, What they did, Result, Source, and Lesson for me; then a closing paragraph on the pattern shared across the cases.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
