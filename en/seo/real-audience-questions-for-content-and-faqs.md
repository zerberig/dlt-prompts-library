My primary keyword is [keyword] and my audience is [target audience]. List 20 questions that person would ask a search engine or an AI assistant about this topic, grouped by stage: understanding the problem, evaluating options, and deciding. If you have web search, check related-question boxes and real forums and mark which ones you found there; mark the ones you came up with yourself as suggested. For each question, say whether it belongs in an FAQ section or deserves its own article, and write a 40–50 word model answer for the 5 most important ones. Deliver a table with the columns Question, Stage, Source, and Where to answer it.
