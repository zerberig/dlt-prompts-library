I run [your business] with a team of [team size] and almost no SEO budget. I've already covered the basics (an optimized site, Search Console, some blogging) and I want less obvious ideas to gain visibility.
Give me 10 creative, low-cost tactics: for example, original data or surveys others will want to cite, free tools or templates, collaborations with complementary businesses, team members as spokespeople, appearances on podcasts or local media, and repurposing one piece of content into several formats. For each one: what it is, an example applied to my business, the approximate time cost, and the SEO benefit it brings (links, mentions, branded searches, content).
Format: A numbered list; at the end, the 3 you'd do first.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
