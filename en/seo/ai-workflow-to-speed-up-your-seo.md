Context: I handle SEO for [your business] in the [your industry] industry. My team is [your team size], and right now we spend the most time on [most time-consuming tasks].
Goal: I want to use AI assistants to get more done in less time without publishing generic content or making mistakes that hurt trust in my site.
Deliverable: 1) 6–8 specific SEO tasks where AI helps (for example, clustering keywords, drafting metadata, analyzing exports, generating FAQ ideas, reviewing internal links). 2) For each task: how to fit it into the workflow, a short sample prompt, and the approximate time saved. 3) What a person should always review, and why. 4) Tasks where AI isn't a good fit.
Criteria: Focus on improving quality and speed, not on mass-producing pages, because search engine guidelines penalize content produced at scale without added value. Flag time savings as estimates.
Format: A table with the columns Task, How to use AI, Sample prompt, Human review, and Estimated savings; then the tasks to avoid as bullets.
