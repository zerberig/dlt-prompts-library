Context: I just launched [your business], which offers [products or services] to [target audience] in [city or market]. The site is new and has little to no authority.
Goal: I want to show up for the first relevant searches as soon as possible and build a foundation that keeps paying off.
Deliverable: An 8-week plan with tasks per week: setting up Search Console and analytics, Google Business Profile if relevant, essential pages (home, services or products, about, contact), 5-10 low-competition keywords to start with, first content pieces, and first mentions or links from directories and local associations. Also include an optimized description for the home page.
Criteria: Focus on specific or local searches where a new site can actually compete, and explain why. Be realistic about timing: SEO usually takes months to show results.
Format: A table per week with Tasks, Estimated time, and Expected result; then the home page description.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
