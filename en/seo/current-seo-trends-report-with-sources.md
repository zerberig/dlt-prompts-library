Context: I handle SEO for [your business or industry], and my audience mostly searches in [markets or countries].
Goal: I want to understand what has changed over the last 6–12 months in how people search and how search engines display results, so I can adjust my strategy based on evidence rather than rumors.
Deliverable: 1) 5 to 7 relevant trends (for example, AI-generated answers, conversational search, content quality updates, video and social search, changes to local results). 2) For each: what's happening, source and date, how well confirmed it is, and what it means for a business like mine. 3) Three specific adjustments I'd make this quarter.
Criteria: Use web search and prioritize official search engine sources and recognized industry publications. Distinguish between confirmed changes, industry observations, and speculation. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. If you don't have web search, tell me before you start and say how recent your information is.
Format: One section per trend with the same subheadings; then the 3 adjustments as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
