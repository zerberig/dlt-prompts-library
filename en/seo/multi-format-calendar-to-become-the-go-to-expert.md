Context: I'm [your role or company] in the [your industry] industry, and I want to be recognized as a go-to expert. I can produce [formats you can produce], and my channels are [your channels].
Goal: I want every search-optimized article to become several pieces for other channels, multiplying its reach and drawing searches for my name or brand.
Deliverable: A 4-week calendar with one anchor article per week and its spin-offs: short video, a post for LinkedIn or my main network, a carousel or infographic, and a newsletter section. For each anchor article: topic, guide keyword, and the distinct point of view that sets it apart from what's already out there.
Criteria: Each spin-off should add something native to its format rather than copy the article, and point back to the original when it makes sense. Prioritize topics where I can contribute first-hand experience or original data, because that's what builds authority.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Anchor article, Keyword, Spin-offs, and Channel; then 3 distinct-angle ideas as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
