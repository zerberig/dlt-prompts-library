Context: I run [your business or site], aimed at [target audience], in [country or market]. I want to rank around [main topic]. If I have them, here is volume or Search Console data: [keyword data].
Goal: I want to know what my customers search for at each buying stage and which page on my site should answer each search, so I stop writing content at random.
Deliverable: 1) 40-60 keywords grouped into 6-10 topic clusters, including long-tail variations and questions. 2) For each cluster: intent (informational, commercial, transactional, local), customer stage, main keyword, and recommended page (existing or new). 3) An order of attack: which clusters to tackle first based on potential and difficulty. 4) How to validate and expand the list with free tools.
Criteria: Use terms the way a real person in my market would search them, including local phrasing. One page per intent so my pages don't compete with each other. If you don't have real volume or difficulty data, give a relative estimate (high, medium, low) instead of inventing numbers. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with Cluster, Keyword, Intent, Stage, Estimated volume, and Target page; then the order of attack as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
