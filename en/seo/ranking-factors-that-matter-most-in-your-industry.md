Context: My business is in the [your industry] industry and I compete in [country or market]. My most important searches are [type of searches] (local, comparison, informational, purchase).
Goal: I want to know where to invest effort first by understanding which factors matter most in my sector and which are myths.
Deliverable: The 10-12 most relevant factors for my industry, grouped into content, experience and trust (E-E-A-T), technical, links, and local or user signals. For each one: why it matters in my sector, the level of evidence, and one concrete action to improve it.
Criteria: Classify the evidence on three levels: confirmed by Google in its documentation, consistently observed in industry studies, or speculative. Google doesn't publish exact weights, so don't invent percentages. If my industry touches health, money, or legal topics, explain how trust standards change. Use web search if you have it. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with Factor, Category, Evidence level, Why it matters here, and Action; at the end, the top 3 priorities for me.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
