Context: I handle SEO for [your site or business] and spend about [hours per week] hours a week on it. I already use these tools: [tools you already use].
Goal: I want to lean on AI assistants to save time on keyword research, content optimization, and monitoring, without publishing anything a person hasn't reviewed.
Deliverable: A weekly and monthly workflow organized by task. For each task: what the AI does, what I review, which tool to use, a 2-3 sentence sample prompt, and the estimated time. Include how to cluster keywords, create content briefs, review existing articles, and summarize Search Console data.
Criteria: Point out the tasks where AI tends to get things wrong (search volumes, current data, unsourced claims) and how to check them. The final content should bring first-hand experience, because generic mass-produced content rarely ranks.
Format: A table with Task, Frequency, AI's role, Human review, Tool, and Time; below it, the numbered sample prompts.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
