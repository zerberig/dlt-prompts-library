Context: My site is [your site URL], in the [industry or niche] sector, and its main goal is [site goal]. Here is my data: [exported data] (for example, a crawler export with status codes, titles, metas, and depth; Search Console pages and queries for the last 3 months; the indexing report).
Goal: I want a diagnosis based on my real data, not a generic checklist, so I can decide where to invest the next few weeks.
Deliverable: 1) A 5-line executive summary. 2) Findings grouped into crawling and indexing, architecture and internal linking, content and on-page, and performance and experience. 3) For each finding: evidence in the data, likely impact, fix, and effort. 4) A 30-day plan with the 10 most important actions.
Criteria: Every finding should point to specific rows or URLs in the data. Separate what you see in the data from what you only suspect, and tell me which extra data would confirm it. Prioritize by impact on traffic and conversions, not by error count. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Sections with headings; findings in a table with the columns Finding, Evidence, Impact, Fix, Effort, and Priority.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
