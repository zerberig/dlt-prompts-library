Context: I handle SEO for [business or client] and present results to [report audience] every month. I have access to [available data tools] (for example, Search Console, Google Analytics 4, a rank tracker).
Goal: I want a report that shows whether SEO contributes to the business and can be read in 5 minutes, not a dump of charts.
Deliverable: 1) The report structure by section. 2) The 6–8 metrics you'd include, with their definition, source, and why they matter to the reader. 3) A suggested dashboard layout (which chart goes in each block). 4) An executive summary template with "to complete" slots for wins, problems, and next steps.
Criteria: Connect visibility metrics (impressions, clicks, rankings) to business metrics (leads, sales, revenue) whenever possible. Compare against the previous month and the same month last year so seasonality isn't mistaken for results. Jargon-free language in the summary.
Format: Sections with headings; metrics in a table with the columns Metric, Definition, Source, and Why it matters.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
