Context: I have content about [topic] for [target audience]. My business [serves locally or online] and this is the page I want to adapt: [your content or URL].
Goal: I want my content to be easy to quote when someone asks their phone, a smart speaker, or an AI assistant out loud.
Deliverable: 1) 12 questions phrased the way a person would say them out loud, grouped by intent (what is, how to, where, how much, which is best). 2) A direct 40-60 word answer for each, ready to paste into the page. 3) Structure tweaks: where to place the answers, question-style headings, structured data worth reviewing, and, if relevant, optimization for local "near me" searches.
Criteria: Answers that make sense read aloud: short sentences, the answer in the first sentence, and no reliance on tables or images. If you have web search, confirm which FAQ-style rich results Google still shows before recommending them.
Format: A numbered question-and-answer list, followed by a short list of structure tweaks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
