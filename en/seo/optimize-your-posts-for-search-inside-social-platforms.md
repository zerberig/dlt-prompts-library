Context: I post content about [topic] for [target audience] on [social platforms]. Here's a post or idea I want to optimize: [your post].
Goal: I want my content to show up when someone searches my topic inside these platforms, and I want those posts to send visits to my site too.
Deliverable: 1) 10 keywords and phrases people would use to search this topic inside each platform. 2) For the post I gave you, an optimized version per platform: on-screen hook text, description or caption, title if relevant, hashtags (few and specific), and alt text. 3) How to drive traffic to my site on each platform without the post losing reach. 4) How to find more searches using each platform's search suggestions.
Criteria: Write the way people who search actually talk, not like an ad. Use web search if you have it to confirm each platform's current features, and don't present assumptions about their algorithms as facts.
Format: One section per platform with its elements ready to copy.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
