Context: I run a [type of business] in [city or area] that serves customers [service mode] (in-store, on-site, or both). My main local competitors are [local competitors].
Goal: I want to show up when someone nearby searches for what I offer, especially in maps and "near me" searches, to get more visits, calls, or requests.
Deliverable: 1) Google Business Profile optimization: categories, description, services, hours, photos, posts, and Q&A. 2) A system for asking every customer for real reviews, plus templates for replying to positive and negative reviews. 3) Site changes: a page per location or service area, visible contact details, and content about my area. 4) Local presence actions (partnerships, events, local media). 5) How to measure calls, direction requests, and visits.
Criteria: Ask for reviews neutrally and without incentives, because tying perks to positive reviews violates platform policies. If you have web search, confirm the profile's current features, since they change often.
Format: Sections with headings; review templates in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
