Context: My site is in [your business or industry]. Here's a list of pages with their URL, current SEO title, current meta description, and target keyword: [list of pages].
Goal: I want a higher click-through rate from search results without losing relevance for the keywords I already target.
Deliverable: For each page, a new SEO title and a new meta description, with their character counts and one line on what you improved.
Criteria: Titles of 50–60 characters with the keyword near the start; meta descriptions of 140–155 characters that say what the visitor will find and why they should click. Every title must be unique across the site, because duplicates compete with each other. Avoid promises the page doesn't deliver, since they raise bounce rates and search engines may rewrite the snippet. If a current title is already good, keep it and explain why.
Format: A table with the columns URL, New title, Characters, New meta description, Characters, and What improved.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
