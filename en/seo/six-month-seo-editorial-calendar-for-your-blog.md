Context: I run a blog about [blog topic] for [target audience]. I can publish [publishing frequency], and the blog's goal is [blog goal] (traffic, leads, sales, authority).
Goal: I want a 6-month plan that builds topical authority instead of publishing scattered ideas, so that every article reinforces the others over time.
Deliverable: 1) 3 topic clusters, each with its pillar article. 2) A month-by-month calendar with working title, guide keyword, search intent, format, and cluster. 3) Which articles link to each other. 4) Dates or seasons relevant to my topic worth planning around. 5) A monthly slot for refreshing older articles.
Criteria: Publish pillars first and supporting articles after, so internal linking works from day one. Balance high-volume competitive topics with specific topics where I can rank sooner. Validate volumes in a tool; here, just give me your judgment and flag anything that's an estimate.
Format: A table with the columns Month, Week, Title, Keyword, Intent, Format, and Cluster; then the linking and seasonality notes as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
