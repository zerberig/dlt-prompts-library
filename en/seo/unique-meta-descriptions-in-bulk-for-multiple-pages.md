Context: My site is [your site or business] and these pages have no meta description or a repeated generic one: [list of pages] (URL, topic, and keyword for each).
Goal: I want every page to have its own description that explains what it offers and invites a click, instead of letting the search engine pick a random snippet.
Deliverable: One meta description per page, 120-155 characters, plus a short note on the angle used.
Criteria: Each description should differ from the rest, even between similar pages, highlighting what's specific to each one. Include the keyword naturally, a concrete benefit, and a varied call to action. Keep the [brand tone] tone. Flag pages that look duplicated or share the same intent, because merging them may make sense.
Format: A table with URL, Keyword, Meta description, Characters, and Angle, ready to copy into a spreadsheet.
