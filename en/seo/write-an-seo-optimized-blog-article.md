Context: I write for the blog of [your business or site]. The article is about [article topic], targeting the primary keyword [primary keyword]. My ideal reader is [target audience] and the brand voice is [brand tone].
Goal: I want an article that answers what that person is looking for better than the competition, so it ranks and also moves the reader to take the next step with my business.
Deliverable: 1) The search intent you identify and 5–8 related terms worth covering. 2) An outline with H2s and H3s. 3) The full article, 1,200 to 1,800 words. 4) SEO title (60 characters max), meta description (155 characters max), and slug. 5) 3 internal link ideas and 2 types of external sources to cite.
Criteria: Answer the main question in the first two paragraphs, because readers and AI-powered search both reward direct answers. Use the keyword naturally; favor concrete examples, actionable steps, and an FAQ section. Don't invent statistics: if you need a figure, leave it as "figure to confirm". Short paragraphs and subheadings that make sense on their own.
Format: The five parts in that order, separated by headings; the article in Markdown.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
