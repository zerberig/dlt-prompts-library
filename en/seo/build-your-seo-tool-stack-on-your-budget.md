Context: I handle SEO for [your business or site], my level is [your SEO level], and my monthly tool budget is [budget in your currency].
Goal: I want a minimal tool set that covers the essentials without paying for features I won't use.
Deliverable: For each task (keyword research, technical audit, performance and speed, rank tracking, link analysis, and traffic measurement), one free option and one paid option, with what I'd use it for and its main limitation. At the end, the combination you recommend for my budget.
Criteria: If you have web search, verify current prices and plans and note the date you checked; if not, flag prices as estimates. Favor the search engines' own official tools, because they're free and use first-party data.
Format: A table with the columns Task, Free option, Paid option, Approximate price, and Limitation; then the recommendation in 3–4 lines.
