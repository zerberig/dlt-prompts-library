Context: My site is about [site topic]. These are my most important pages, the ones I want to rank: [pillar pages]. And these are the supporting pages or articles: [supporting pages].
Goal: I want my internal links to reinforce the topical authority of my main pages and help readers find the next thing they need.
Deliverable: 1) The pages grouped into clusters, each with its pillar page. 2) A link map: from which page, to which page, with what anchor text, and where in the content. 3) The gaps: topics missing from each cluster to cover it well. 4) Orphan pages or pages without a clear cluster, and what to do with them.
Criteria: Descriptive, varied anchor text, never "click here". Each supporting page links to its pillar and to 1-2 relevant sibling pages; the pillar links to all its supporting pages. Don't force links between unrelated topics.
Format: One table per cluster with From, To, Anchor text, and Placement; then lists of gaps and orphan pages.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
