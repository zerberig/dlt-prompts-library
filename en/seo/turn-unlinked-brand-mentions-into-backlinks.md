My brand is [your brand name] and my site is [your domain]. Help me find pages that mention my brand, products, or key people without linking to my site. Give me: 1) 5–8 ready-to-copy searches using operators (for example, combining the name in quotes with -site: to exclude my domain and social profiles), plus how to set up free alerts for new mentions. 2) How to prioritize the mentions you find (relevance, audience, whether the author can be reached). 3) A short, friendly email asking for the link that thanks them for the mention and gives the exact URL. Keep the tone grateful and low-pressure, since the author has no obligation to link. Deliver each part under a heading, with the email in its own block.
