Context: My site about [your site topic] has these main pages and current URLs: [list of pages and URLs]. The pages I most want to rank are [priority pages].
Goal: I want a clear structure that channels authority toward my priority pages and makes the whole site easy for search engines to crawl.
Deliverable: 1) A proposed hierarchy map (home, categories, subpages). 2) URL format recommendations with examples applied to my pages, and which ones to change or keep. 3) An internal link matrix: which page links to which, and with what anchor text. 4) A 301 redirect plan if you suggest changing any URL.
Criteria: Change URLs only when the benefit outweighs the risk of losing rankings during the transition, and explain that trade-off. Descriptive, varied anchor text rather than the same exact-match keyword. No important page more than 3 clicks from the home page.
Format: The map as an indented list; the matrix and redirects as tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
