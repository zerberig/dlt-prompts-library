Context: My site is in [your business or industry]. Here's a Search Console export covering [analysis period] with queries, pages, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position: [Search Console data].
Goal: I want to know where I'm leaving clicks on the table and what to do first to win them back.
Deliverable: 1) Queries with high impressions and low CTR, with a hypothesis for why and an improved title or meta. 2) Pages in positions 5 to 20 that could climb with content or internal linking improvements. 3) Queries that trigger the wrong page, or several pages at once. 4) Topics that show up in queries but have no dedicated page. 5) The 5 highest-potential actions, ranked.
Criteria: Work only from the data I gave you and cite the rows you're relying on. Keep in mind that average position and CTR vary by result type and device, so present your conclusions as hypotheses to test.
Format: One section per point, each with a short table; then the 5 actions as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
