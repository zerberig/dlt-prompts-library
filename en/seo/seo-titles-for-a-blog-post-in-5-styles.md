Write 10 SEO titles for a post about [article topic] aimed at [target audience], using the keyword [keyword].
Use 5 styles, two titles each: numbered list, question, how-to guide, comparison, and direct benefit. Each title should be under 60 characters so it doesn't get cut off in search results, place the keyword as early as possible, and promise only what the post actually delivers, because a misleading title drives up bounces.
Format: A table with Style, Title, and Character count. At the end, recommend the 2 you'd test and why.
