Context: My site is built on [platform or CMS], has about [number of pages] pages, and is a [type of site].
Goal: I want to make sure Google can crawl, understand, and index my site without technical obstacles, and explain to my team why each item matters.
Deliverable: A technical checklist in six blocks: crawling (robots.txt, crawlable internal links), indexing (XML sitemap, canonicals, noindex, status codes and redirects), speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile experience, security (HTTPS, mixed content), and structured data. For each item: what to check, how to verify it with free tools, a one-sentence explanation of why it matters, and the typical fix on my platform.
Criteria: Adapt the items to my CMS and point out which ones are usually handled by default and which aren't. Leave out anything that doesn't apply to a site my size.
Format: A table per block with Item, How to check, Why it matters, and Fix on my platform.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
