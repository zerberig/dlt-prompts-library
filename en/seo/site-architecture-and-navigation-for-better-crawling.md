Context: My site is [your site or business] and it's currently organized like this: [current structure] (main menu, categories, sections, and sample URLs). The most important topics or products for the business are: [priority topics].
Goal: I want a structure where every important page is 3 clicks or fewer from the home page, Google understands the topic hierarchy, and visitors find what they need effortlessly.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of the current structure: pages buried too deep, confusing or duplicate categories, and inconsistent URLs. 2) A proposed site tree with levels and category names based on how people search. 3) A recommended URL pattern. 4) Proposed main menu, footer, and breadcrumbs. 5) The 301 redirects needed if URLs change.
Criteria: Name categories the way customers would search for them, not with internal jargon. Minimize URL changes on pages that already have traffic or links, because every change carries risk.
Format: Diagnosis in bullets, the site tree with indentation, and a redirect table with Current URL and New URL.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
