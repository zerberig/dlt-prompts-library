Context: This is the page I care about most: [URL or page type], built on [platform or CMS]. Here are the mobile results from PageSpeed Insights or the Search Console Core Web Vitals report: [report results].
Goal: I want the mobile experience to be fast and comfortable, because most of my visits come from phones and Google indexes the mobile version.
Deliverable: 1) A plain-language summary of what's wrong and how serious it is (LCP, INP, CLS). 2) Fixes ordered by impact and effort, each with what to do and who should do it (me, a designer, or a developer). 3) Mobile usability issues the report doesn't measure: button size, pop-ups, forms, readable text.
Criteria: Explain each metric in one sentence without jargon. Give platform-specific fixes where possible. If the report data isn't enough to conclude something, say so instead of guessing.
Format: A 3-4 sentence summary, a table with Issue, Fix, Impact, Effort, and Owner, and an 8-point mobile checklist.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
