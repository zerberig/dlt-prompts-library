Context: I run [your business or site] and the goal of SEO is [business goal] (sales, leads, subscribers, brand visibility). Reports go to [who you report to].
Goal: I want to measure SEO with a few metrics that connect to business results, instead of reporting only rankings and visits.
Deliverable: 1) A metric tree: 1 main business metric, 3-4 outcome indicators (organic clicks, conversions, revenue or leads from organic), and 4-6 health indicators (indexed pages, CTR, rankings for priority keywords, speed). 2) For each metric: definition, data source, frequency, and how to set a realistic target from my historical data. 3) A one-page dashboard layout: which charts go in and in what order.
Criteria: Every metric should inform a decision someone will make with it; otherwise drop it. Separate leadership metrics from operational ones.
Format: A table with Metric, Type, Definition, Source, Frequency, and Suggested target; then a text sketch of the dashboard.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
