Context: I handle SEO for [your business or niche] and my main organic traffic channels are [current channels].
Goal: I want to understand what's changing in search (Google updates, AI-generated answers, search on social platforms and assistants) so I can adjust my strategy for the coming months.
Deliverable: The 6-8 trends most relevant to my niche. For each one: what's happening, evidence or source, how it could affect my traffic, and 2-3 concrete actions I can take.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and cite each source's date, because this topic moves fast. Distinguish between what Google or the platform has officially confirmed, what industry studies have observed, and what is speculative. Don't present assumptions about the algorithm as facts. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A summary table with Trend, Evidence level, Impact for me (high, medium, low); then a short section per trend and a closing list of the 3 actions you'd take this quarter.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
