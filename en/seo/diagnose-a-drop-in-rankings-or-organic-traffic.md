Context: My site [your site] lost organic traffic or rankings starting around [approximate date of the drop]. Here's the data I have: [drop data] (rankings before and after by keyword, affected pages, weekly Search Console clicks). Recent changes to the site: [recent changes] (redesign, migration, new or removed content, platform changes).
Goal: I want to understand what caused the drop and what to do, without reacting with rushed changes that make things worse.
Deliverable: 1) The pattern of the drop: which pages, query types, and devices were affected. 2) Likely causes ranked by probability: technical, my own changes, a search engine update, competition, seasonality, or changes to the results page (for example, more AI answers). 3) For each cause: what evidence would confirm or rule it out, and where to check. 4) An action plan for the next 2 weeks.
Criteria: Separate what my data shows from what's a hypothesis. If you have web search, check whether there were confirmed search engine updates around that date and cite the source. Advise against major changes until the cause is confirmed.
Format: Sections with headings; causes in a table with the columns Cause, Likelihood, Evidence to look for, and Where to check.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
