Context: My site is [your site and topic] and I compete with [competitors]. Here is an export of those competitors' inbound links (linking domain, page, anchor text, and estimated authority if I have it): [backlink data].
Goal: I want to find which sites link to my competitors and could link to me too, so I can earn links that build authority without risking a penalty.
Deliverable: 1) Domains that link to two or more competitors but not to me. 2) Opportunities grouped by type: resource pages, unlinked mentions, broken links I could replace, collaborations or guest articles, industry directories or associations. 3) For the top 10, which of my content (existing or to be created) I'd offer and the angle for the outreach message. 4) A short, customizable outreach email template.
Criteria: Prioritize topical relevance over authority metrics, because a link from a site in my niche is worth more than a big unrelated one. Rule out link farms, unlabeled paid links, and private networks, which violate Google's guidelines. Don't invent URLs or metrics that aren't in the data. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with Domain, Opportunity type, Why it's relevant, Content to offer, and Priority (high, medium, low); then the template.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
