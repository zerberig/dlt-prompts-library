Context: I run [your product or business] aimed at [target audience]. The current SEO situation is: [current situation] (traffic, rankings, content, team). We have [available resources] (people, hours, budget in [currency]).
Goal: I want a 12-month SEO strategy I can present to leadership to get approval and resources, tied to business goals.
Deliverable: 1) A half-page executive summary. 2) A short diagnosis: strengths, gaps, and opportunities versus competitors. 3) Measurable 12-month goals tied to the business. 4) 3-4 strategic pillars (for example, technical, content, authority, conversion) with concrete initiatives. 5) A quarterly calendar. 6) Resources and budget per pillar. 7) Risks and assumptions. 8) Metrics and reporting cadence.
Criteria: Write for a non-specialist executive: explain each initiative by its business impact. Goals should be realistic for the available resources; if they aren't enough, say so and propose what to prioritize. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A document with headings per section, with tables for the calendar and budget. 900-1400 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
