Context: I handle SEO for [your site or business] and use [measurement tools]. My main goal for organic traffic is [business goal].
Goal: I want a fixed review routine to catch problems early and know whether my efforts are working, without spending hours in dashboards.
Deliverable: A review schedule on three levels: weekly (15 minutes), monthly (1 hour), and quarterly (half a day). For each review: which data to look at, which report it's in, what counts as a normal change versus a warning sign, and the action to take if the warning appears.
Criteria: Include indexing errors, click drops by page, new queries, position changes, and conversions from organic. Remind me to compare against the same period last year when there's seasonality, so I don't mistake a normal cycle for a problem.
Format: Three sections with one table each (What to check, Where, Alert if, Action) and a short notes template to log each review.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
