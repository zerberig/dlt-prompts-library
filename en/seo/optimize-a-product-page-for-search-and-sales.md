Context: I sell [your product] on [your online store] to [target audience]. This is the page's current content: [current page content] (title, description, features, images, and price).
Goal: I want the page to show up when people search for this kind of product, and to give them what they need to buy once they land.
Deliverable: 1) 3 primary keyword options with their purchase intent. 2) SEO title, meta description, and H1. 3) A rewritten description with benefits, specs, and use cases. 4) File names and alt text for the images. 5) 4–6 FAQs with answers. 6) Which product structured data to add.
Criteria: Write for the buyer first; the keyword should read naturally. Address the usual objections (size, shipping, returns, compatibility), because those are what stall a purchase. Use only the details I gave you; mark anything missing as "to complete".
Format: The six parts with headings, ready to paste into my platform.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
