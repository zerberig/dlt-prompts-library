Context: My business is [business name and type], located in [city and country]. Official details: [name, address, phone, and website]. I'm already listed on: [directories you're already on].
Goal: I want my details to be identical everywhere I appear and to be listed in the directories my customers actually use, to strengthen my visibility in local search.
Deliverable: 1) The official name, address, and phone format I should always use. 2) A list of 15–20 directories and platforms by priority: general, maps, industry-specific, and city or country-specific. 3) A process for finding and fixing duplicate or outdated listings. 4) LocalBusiness JSON-LD code with my details. 5) A tracking template.
Criteria: If you have web search, confirm that each directory is still active and relevant in my country; if not, mark it as to verify. Favor quality and relevance over volume. Don't invent reviews or business details.
Format: Sections with headings; directories in a table with the columns Directory, Type, Priority, and Status; the code in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
