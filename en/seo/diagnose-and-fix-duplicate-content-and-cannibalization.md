Context: My site is [your site and platform]. I suspect duplicate content or pages competing for the same search. Here's what I have: [available data] (a list of URLs with their titles, Search Console queries with multiple pages, crawler output, or plagiarism checker results).
Goal: I want every important search to have a single strong page, and I don't want Google wasting time crawling repeated versions.
Deliverable: 1) The cases found, classified by type: URL variations (parameters, capitalization, trailing slash, http and https), near-identical pages, content copied on other sites, and cannibalization between articles. 2) For each case, the recommended fix (merge and 301 redirect, canonical tag, noindex, rewrite with a different angle, or leave as is) and why. 3) Steps to apply it on my platform and how to verify afterward in Search Console.
Criteria: Explain the difference between duplication and plagiarism, and between a penalty and simple result filtering, so I don't overreact. Don't remove pages with traffic or links without redirecting them. If the data isn't enough to decide, tell me what to check.
Format: A table with URL or group, Issue type, Fix, Priority, and Notes; then the numbered implementation steps.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
