Context: I run an online store selling [your products] to [target audience]. My main competitors are [competitors], and right now I publish [current content frequency and type].
Goal: I want content that attracts buyers at different stages (people researching a problem, people comparing options, and people ready to buy) and sends them to my categories and products.
Deliverable: 1) 3–4 pillar topics, each with 5–6 supporting articles and a working keyword. 2) Which category or product page each cluster links to. 3) Recommended formats by stage (guides, comparisons, tutorials, FAQs). 4) A 90-day promotion plan using owned channels. 5) 3 indicators that tell me whether the strategy is working.
Criteria: Every piece should answer a real buyer question and connect clearly to a product, because traffic that doesn't buy doesn't justify the effort. Prioritize topics where a store my size can compete. Validate volumes and difficulty in a tool before committing resources; here, just give me your judgment.
Format: One table per pillar with the columns Article, Keyword, Stage, and Target page; everything else in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
