Context: I'm considering trying to rank my site [your site] for the keyword [keyword]. My site's situation: [your site's situation] (age, published content, rough link count, topics I already rank for).
Goal: I want to know whether competing for that keyword is realistic in the next 6 months, or whether I should aim for a more accessible variant.
Deliverable: 1) If you have web search, an analysis of the top 10 results: type of site, type of content, how well they meet the intent, and visible authority signals. 2) A difficulty rating from 1 to 10 for my specific site, with the reasoning. 3) What I'd need to create to compete. 4) 3–5 less competitive variants with the same intent.
Criteria: Base the rating on what you observe in the results and on my situation, not on a generic score. If you don't have web search, say so and give me a guide to run the analysis myself. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table for the results with the columns Position, Site, Content type, and Strength; then the rating and recommendations as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
