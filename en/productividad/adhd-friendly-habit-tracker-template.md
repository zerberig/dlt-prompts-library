Context: I have ADHD and I want to build these habits: [the habits you want to build]. I'd rather use the tracker in [tracker format] (spreadsheet, printed notebook, or notes app). My main obstacles are [your main obstacles] (forgetting, losing interest after a few days, quitting once the streak breaks).
Goal: I want a template that keeps me motivated without punishing me when a day doesn't work out, because rigid trackers usually end up abandoned.
Deliverable: A complete template with these sections:
1) Habit list: each with the concrete action, the frequency, and a minimum version for hard days.
2) Daily and weekly log: a clear visual system (checkboxes, colors, or a 1-3 scale) with an "excused day" mark that doesn't break the streak.
3) Reward system: points per completed habit, with small, medium, and big rewards, and examples that don't undermine the habits themselves.
4) Reminders: a mix of visual cues, alarms, and support (body doubling or a trusted person who checks in on me), with ideas for rotating them so I don't tune them out.
5) Weekly review: 4 or 5 questions about progress, obstacles, and adjustments, plus a rule for when to simplify a habit.
Criteria: Short, kind language with no guilt. Start with 3 active habits max even if I listed more, and tell me which to save for later and why. Adapt the design to the format I chose; if it's a spreadsheet, include columns and formulas for points and streaks. If any habit involves medication or treatment, remind me to follow my doctor's instructions instead of suggesting changes.
Format: Each section with a heading and the template ready to copy; at the end, usage instructions in 5 bullets max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
