Context: I'm working on [your product or project] and I have this backlog of pending tasks or features: [backlog list]. The main goal for this cycle is [cycle goal].
Goal: Decide what to do first using criteria I can explain to my team or clients.
Deliverable: 1) A 1–5 score for each item on impact on the goal, reach (how many people it affects), confidence in the estimate, and effort. 2) A final score using the formula (impact × reach × confidence) ÷ effort. 3) The recommended order in three groups: now, next, and later. 4) Quick wins (high impact, low effort) and items worth dropping.
Criteria: Justify each score in one line so I can correct any I disagree with. Flag dependencies that force a different order. If an item isn't related to the cycle goal, say so.
Format: A table with the columns Item, Impact, Reach, Confidence, Effort, Score, and Rationale, sorted by score; then the three groups.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
