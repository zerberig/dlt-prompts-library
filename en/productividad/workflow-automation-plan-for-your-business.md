Context: I run [your business] with a team of [team size]. We use these tools: [your current tools]. These are manual tasks we repeat every week, with roughly how long they take: [manual tasks you repeat].
Goal: I want to win back hours of repetitive work for clients and growth, starting with quick, low-risk wins.
Deliverable:
1) Prioritized inventory: each task with hours per month, frequency, error risk, and automation potential (high, medium, or low).
2) For the top 5 tasks: the proposed flow (trigger → steps → output), the suggested tool (built-in features, no-code automation platforms, or AI assistants and agents), and estimated setup time and monthly savings.
3) What isn't worth automating yet and why.
4) A 30-day roadmap by week, with an owner for each step.
5) Safeguards: how to check each automation is working and what to do if it fails.
Criteria: Use the features of my current tools before adding subscriptions. If you have web search, check current integrations and pricing; flag any savings or costs you can't back up as estimates. Point out automations that handle customers' personal data and what protecting it involves.
Format: A table for the inventory, a short card per automation, and the roadmap as a weekly table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
