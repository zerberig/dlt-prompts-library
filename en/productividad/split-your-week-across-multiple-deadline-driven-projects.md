Context: I'm working on these projects at the same time: [projects with deadlines and remaining hours]. I have [available hours per week] real working hours per week, after fixed meetings.
Goal: Hit every deadline without working nights and without leaving any project to the last minute.
Deliverable: 1) A capacity check: hours needed vs. hours available, week by week, up to the last deadline. 2) A weekly allocation of hours per project, using theme days or fixed blocks to reduce context switching. 3) The conflicts you find and concrete options: what to cut, what to move, and which deadline to renegotiate, with a short message asking for that extension.
Criteria: Assume my estimates are optimistic and add a 20% buffer. Prioritize by the consequences of missing, not by who pushes hardest. Batch similar tasks across projects when it saves time.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Project, Hours, and Deliverable that week; then the conflicts as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
