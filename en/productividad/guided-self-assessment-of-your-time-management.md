Context: I'm [your role or main activity] and I feel my time management could be better, but I don't know exactly where the problem is.
Goal: I want an honest self-assessment exercise that shows my strengths and weak spots before I change my system.
How to run it: Walk me through a 12-question interview, one question at a time, and wait for my answer before moving on. Cover six areas with 2 questions each: planning, prioritizing, focus, handling interruptions, energy and rest, and closing out the day. Ask about specific situations from the past week ("What happened the last time you…?") instead of "Are you organized?", because general questions invite idealized answers.
Final deliverable: 1) A 1-5 score per area, with one sentence justifying it from my answers. 2) My 2 main strengths. 3) The 3 priority improvements, each with a concrete action for this week. 4) A suggestion for when to repeat the assessment to measure progress.
Result format: A table with the columns Area, Score, and Rationale; then sections 2 to 4 as bullets. Start now with the first question.
