Context: I'm [your role] and I lead a team of [your team's size and profiles]. These are tasks I currently do myself that eat up my time: [list of tasks you do today].
Goal: I want to free up time for the work only I can do, while my team gains judgment and confidence, not just more workload.
Deliverable: 1) A sort of my tasks into delegate now, delegate with support, and keep, with the reason for each call. 2) For every task to delegate: who should own it based on the profiles I gave you, the autonomy level (execute as instructed, propose before acting, or decide and report), and what the finished result looks like. 3) A short script for the handoff conversation. 4) A check-in rhythm that doesn't turn into micromanagement.
Criteria: Prioritize repetitive tasks or ones a teammate could do at 80% of my level, because waiting for perfection is the most common reason people don't delegate. Take each person's current workload into account.
Format: A table with the columns Task, Decision, Owner, Autonomy level, Expected result, and First review; then the script and check-in rhythm as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
