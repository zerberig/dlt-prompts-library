Context: My main goals for the next few months are [your main goals]. I work in [your type of work] and I use [spreadsheet tool] (Google Sheets, Excel, or another).
Goal: I want a tracker I can fill in under 5 minutes a day that shows where I'm making progress and where I'm stuck, so I can adjust every week.
Deliverable: 1) 4 to 6 metrics tied to my goals (at least one outcome metric and one effort metric), with an exact definition of how each is logged. 2) The sheet structure: tabs, columns, data types, and validation (dropdowns, checkboxes). 3) Formulas for weekly totals, averages, and completion rate, written for my tool. 4) Suggested conditional formatting to spot patterns at a glance. 5) Three questions for a 10-minute weekly review.
Criteria: A few well-defined metrics beat many, because a heavy tracker gets abandoned within two weeks. Include 2 customization options (changing a goal, adding a habit) that don't break the formulas.
Format: Numbered sections; the column structure as a table and formulas in code blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
