Context: I work as [your role] and I feel my time disappears into [where you feel you lose time]. I'd rather log my time with [your preferred way of logging].
Goal: Measure for 2 weeks where my time actually goes so I can make decisions based on data.
Deliverable: 1) 6 to 8 activity categories tailored to my role, with examples. 2) The simplest way to log with my preferred method and, as an alternative, 2 or 3 time-tracking apps, noting whether they have a free plan. 3) A logging routine that takes me under 5 minutes a day. 4) A weekly analysis template: hours per category, comparison with what I planned, and 3 questions to decide what to change.
Criteria: Avoid a level of detail that would make me quit logging by day three. If you have web search, confirm the apps' current features and pricing, and flag anything you can't verify as an estimate.
Format: A list of categories, an app comparison table, and an analysis template ready for a spreadsheet.
