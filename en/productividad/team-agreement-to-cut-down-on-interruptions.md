Context: I lead a team of [team size and type] working [in-office, remote, or hybrid]. We use [communication tools]. The main problem is [how the interruptions show up].
Goal: Give the team time to concentrate without losing the ability to handle what's truly urgent.
Deliverable: 1) A one-page team agreement: protected focus hours, which channel to use by urgency, expected response time per channel, how to show availability, and meeting rules (agenda in advance, length, no-meeting days). 2) What counts as a real emergency, with examples. 3) A message to introduce the agreement and invite the team's input. 4) How to evaluate it after a month.
Criteria: Start with a few norms the team can follow from week one. Collaborative wording, not a rulebook. Account for roles that need to respond fast, like support or sales, and define clear exceptions for them.
Format: The agreement as bullets under headings, ready to share; then the message and the evaluation plan.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
