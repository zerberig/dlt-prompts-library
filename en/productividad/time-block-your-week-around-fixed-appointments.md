Context: These are my fixed commitments for next week: [meetings and appointments with day and time]. My priorities for the week are: [this week's priorities]. I focus best [your best focus hours].
Goal: Reserve real time for my priorities before the week fills up with other people's requests.
Deliverable: 1) A Monday-to-Friday calendar with the fixed commitments and focus blocks assigned to each priority, using specific names ("Write the client proposal" instead of "work"). 2) Windows for email and messages, one for small tasks, and a daily buffer. 3) Rules for defending the blocks: what to do when someone proposes a meeting on top of one. 4) A 10-minute Friday review.
Criteria: Put focus blocks in my best hours and don't split them into chunks shorter than 60 minutes. Don't fill more than 75% of the open time: the rest is slack for the unexpected.
Format: One table per day with the columns Time, Block, and Related priority, ready to move into my digital calendar.
