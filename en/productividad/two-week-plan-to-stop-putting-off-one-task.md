Context: I've been putting off [the task or project you keep delaying] for a while. When I try to start, this is what happens: [what you feel or think when you try to start]. I can give it [time available per day] a day.
Goal: Understand what's behind the procrastination in this case and make steady progress.
Deliverable: 1) The most likely causes based on what I told you (unclear task, fear of doing it badly, boredom, distant payoff, or sheer size), each with a one-line explanation. 2) A specific strategy for each cause. 3) A 14-day plan with one 10–30 minute action per day, from easiest to most demanding. 4) What to do if I miss a day, so the plan doesn't collapse.
Criteria: Concrete, measurable actions with a verb and an outcome. Understanding but firm tone, no guilt. If what I describe includes persistent distress, recommend that I talk to a mental health professional.
Format: Causes and strategies in a table; then the day-by-day plan as a numbered list.
