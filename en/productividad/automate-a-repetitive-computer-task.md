Context: Every [frequency] I do this manual task: [description of the repetitive task]. These apps are involved: [apps involved]. My technical level is [your technical level].
Goal: Win that time back and reduce errors with a solution I can maintain on my own.
Deliverable: 1) 2 to 3 ways to automate it, from simplest to most powerful: built-in app features, no-code platforms like Zapier, Make, or n8n, a script, or an AI assistant. 2) For the recommended option, the full workflow (trigger, actions, and conditions) and what to configure at each step. 3) Estimated setup time, monthly time saved, and approximate cost. 4) Risks and how to test the automation before relying on it.
Criteria: Match the solution to my technical level. Flag any step that handles sensitive data and how to protect it. If you have web search, check that the integrations and pricing are current, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: A short comparison of options in a table; then the recommended workflow as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
