Context: I work at [your company or team] and want to measure the cost of our meetings in [currency]. These are our recurring meetings, with attendees, length, and frequency: [your recurring meetings]. We use [spreadsheet tool].
Goal: I want to put numbers on what meetings cost so we can better decide which to keep, shorten, move to async, or cancel.
Deliverable:
1) The methodology: how to turn monthly or annual salaries into an hourly cost (with benefits or employer costs as an adjustable percentage) and which costs to add beyond time in the room (prep, travel, and the time it takes to get back into work).
2) The calculator's structure: input fields, editable assumptions, and outputs (cost per meeting, per month, and per year).
3) Formulas ready for my spreadsheet, in code blocks.
4) The calculation applied to my recurring meetings, using salary ranges by level instead of individual salaries to protect privacy.
5) An assessment of each meeting with a recommendation (keep, shorten, trim attendees, move to async, or cancel) and the estimated savings.
6) Five best practices for shorter, more useful meetings.
Criteria: If I don't give you salaries, ask me for ranges or use explicit assumptions flagged as estimates. Frame the results as a tool for improvement, not for blaming anyone.
Format: Numbered sections, with tables for inputs and outputs.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
