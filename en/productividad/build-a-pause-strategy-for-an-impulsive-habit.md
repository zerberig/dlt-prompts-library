Context: I have ADHD and want to work on this impulsive behavior: [impulsive behavior]. For example, interrupting, unplanned purchases, or firing off angry emails. It usually happens when [usual situation or trigger].
Goal: Create a moment between the impulse and the action so I can consciously choose how to respond.
Deliverable: 1) A short breakdown of the sequence: trigger, feeling, urge, action, and consequence. 2) A "pause and plan" strategy with an anchor phrase or gesture, the concrete pause (breathe, count, step away, write the message without sending it), and the alternative response. 3) External supports that don't rely on willpower: settings, reminders, or deliberate friction. 4) A simple 2-week log to see what works.
Criteria: Practical, non-judgmental tone. Offer self-management strategies, not diagnoses or treatment. End with one line recommending I talk to a mental health professional if the impulse is causing serious problems at work, with money, or in my relationships.
Format: Short sections with bullets, and the log as a table with the columns Date, Trigger, Did I pause?, and What happened.
