Context: I work in [your type of work or context] and my main time challenges are [your main challenges].
Goal: Find out which techniques fit the way I work and test them with a short experiment, instead of trying methods at random.
Deliverable: 1) 5 to 7 techniques relevant to my case (for example: Pomodoro, time blocking, batching similar tasks, the two-minute rule, doing the hardest task first, limiting work in progress), with the problem each one solves and when it's a poor fit. 2) The 2 techniques you recommend most for me and why. 3) A 7-day experiment: how to apply them, what to measure, and how to decide whether to keep them.
Criteria: Examples applied to my actual work, not generic ones. Explain each technique in 3 lines max.
Format: A table with the columns Technique, What it solves, How to apply it in your case, and When not to use it; then the recommendations and the day-by-day experiment.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
