Context: I work in [your field or type of work], I use [devices and operating system], and my current tools are [current tools]. What I struggle with most is [your main problem].
Goal: Pick a few tools that integrate with each other and solve my problem, without drowning in apps.
Deliverable: 1) The categories I actually need (tasks, notes, calendar, automation, focus) and which ones I already cover. 2) For each missing category, 2 or 3 options with pros, limitations, whether there's a free plan, and what they integrate with. 3) My recommended stack and a one-week migration plan.
Criteria: Before suggesting anything new, check whether a tool I already use can do the job. Prefer options available in my language and region when possible. If you have web search, verify current pricing, plans, and features, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: A comparison table per category with the columns Option, What it's for, Pros, Limitations, Free plan, and Integrations; then the final recommendation in 3–5 bullets.
