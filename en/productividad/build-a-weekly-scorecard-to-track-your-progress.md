Context: I want to get better at [area you want to improve], and my goal for [timeframe] is [your goal]. I have about [minutes per week] minutes a week to log and review.
Goal: Know from data, not gut feeling, whether I'm making progress and where to adjust.
Deliverable: 1) 3 to 5 metrics: at least 2 effort metrics (things I control, like practice hours) and 1 or 2 outcome metrics. 2) For each: how to measure it, how often, and a realistic weekly target. 3) A weekly log template. 4) Decision rules: what I do if I miss a target two weeks in a row.
Criteria: Metrics I can log in under 2 minutes a day. If something is hard to measure, propose a proxy and explain its limits. Skip vanity metrics that go up without real progress.
Format: A metrics table with the columns Metric, Type, How to measure, Frequency, and Weekly target; then the template, ready to paste into a spreadsheet.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
