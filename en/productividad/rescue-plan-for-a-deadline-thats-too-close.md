Context: I need to deliver [what you need to deliver] and I have [working hours left] working hours before the deadline. Here's what I already have: [current progress]. The person receiving it expects [what the recipient expects].
Goal: Deliver something of good quality on time, even if it isn't the ideal version.
Deliverable: 1) What's essential, what's nice to have, and what can wait for a second version. 2) The minimum acceptable version, described in 3–5 bullets. 3) A time-block plan with the output of each block and checkpoints to decide whether to cut further. 4) A short message letting the recipient know what the delivery will include, in case expectations need adjusting.
Criteria: Save the last 15% of the time for review. Put the riskiest parts first. Be blunt: if something doesn't fit, tell me and suggest what to cut.
Format: Numbered sections; the plan as a table with the columns Block, Time, Task, and Output.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
