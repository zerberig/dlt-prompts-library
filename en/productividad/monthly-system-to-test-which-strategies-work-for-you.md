Context: These are the productivity strategies I use today: [the strategies you use today]. My goals are [your productivity goals], and this is how I measure progress now: [how you measure progress] (or I don't).
Goal: I want to stop switching methods on impulse and evaluate each strategy with evidence, so I keep only what actually works for me.
Deliverable: 1) An evaluation framework with 3 or 4 indicators: at least one outcome indicator (what I get done), one process indicator (whether I actually apply the strategy), and one well-being indicator (energy or stress). 2) A 2-week experiment template: hypothesis, what I'm changing, how I'll measure it, and the success criterion. 3) A 20-minute monthly review with guiding questions and a rule for deciding whether to keep, adjust, or drop each strategy. 4) A full worked example using one of the strategies I already use.
Criteria: Indicators I can log in under 2 minutes a day. One variable per experiment, because if I change several at once I won't know which one worked.
Format: Numbered sections; the experiment template and monthly review as tables ready to copy.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
