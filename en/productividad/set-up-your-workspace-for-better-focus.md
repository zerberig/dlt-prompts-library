Context: I work in [description of your workspace], and what distracts me most there is [your main distractions in that space]. My budget for improvements is [budget in your currency].
Goal: A space that helps me focus without relying only on willpower.
Deliverable: 8 to 10 concrete changes (layout, light, noise, what's in view, ergonomics, and signals that let others know I'm focusing), each with an approximate cost and expected impact, starting with the free ones. Include a 3-minute routine to prep the space before each work block.
Criteria: Fit everything to my actual space; if I share it with other people, include how we can agree on it.
Format: A table with the columns Change, Cost, Impact, and How to do it; the routine in 3 or 4 bullets.
