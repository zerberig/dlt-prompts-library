Context: For one week I logged my energy and focus levels from 1 to 5 every 1-2 hours, plus what I was working on. Here's my log: [your weekly log]. My usual schedule is [your usual schedule].
Goal: I want to see at a glance when I perform best and worst, so I can fit my tasks to my energy instead of the other way around.
Deliverable: 1) A heatmap-style visualization with days as columns and time slots as rows; if you can generate charts, create it, and if not, use a table with values or colored squares that reads like a heatmap. 2) The 3 to 5 clearest patterns: peaks, dips, unusual days, and the types of task that line up with each level. 3) A proposed schedule that puts deep work in my peaks and operational tasks in my dips. 4) Two experiments for next week.
Criteria: Separate solid patterns from one-day coincidences, because a week of data is limited. Don't add data that isn't in my log.
Format: The visualization first, then patterns as bullets, the schedule as a table, and the experiments as a numbered list.
