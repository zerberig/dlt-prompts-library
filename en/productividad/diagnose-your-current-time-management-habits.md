Context: Here's what a typical week looks like for me, with approximate times: [description of your typical week]. My main responsibilities are [your main responsibilities], and what I feel isn't working is [what you feel isn't working].
Goal: Understand which habits cost me the most time and energy, so I change what actually matters.
Deliverable: 1) An assessment across 6 areas (planning, prioritization, focus, meetings and communication, energy and rest, end-of-day shutdown), each with a 1–5 score and the part of my description that supports it. 2) Recurring patterns. 3) The 3 highest-impact changes, each with a first step for this week. 4) What I'm doing well and should keep.
Criteria: Be direct and specific. Base every observation on what I told you, not generic advice; if something in my description doesn't add up or is missing, say so.
Format: A table with the columns Area, Score, Evidence, and Recommendation; then the 3 changes as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
