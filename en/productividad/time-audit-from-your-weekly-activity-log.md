Context: This is my log of how I spent my time over the last 5-7 days (activity, start time, and end time): [your activity log]. My current priorities are [your priorities], and I work with [your current tools].
Goal: I want hard data on where my time goes, compared with what I say matters, so I can decide what to change.
Deliverable: 1) A summary of hours by category (deep work, meetings, email and messages, operational tasks, commuting, personal, and unlogged time), with the percentage of the total. 2) A comparison between time spent on my priorities and time that went elsewhere. 3) The 5 biggest sources of lost or fragmented time, with evidence from the log. 4) A concrete recommendation for each (eliminate, delegate, batch, automate, or reduce), including how to make better use of my current tools. 5) A realistic target for hours recovered next week.
Criteria: Stick to the data in my log; if something is ambiguous, say so instead of guessing. If you can generate charts, add a bar chart by category.
Format: Tables for sections 1 and 2, bullets for the rest. 600 words max.
