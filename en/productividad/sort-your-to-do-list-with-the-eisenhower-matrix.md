Context: This is my to-do list for [period you want to plan]: [your tasks with deadlines]. I have [available hours] hours available in that period.
Goal: Stop reacting to whatever is loudest and spend my time on what actually matters.
Deliverable: Place each task in one of the four quadrants (do now, schedule, delegate, drop) with one line explaining why, and propose the order for the "do now" quadrant based on my available hours.
Criteria: Treat as important whatever moves me toward my goals or has real consequences if it doesn't get done; treat as urgent whatever has a near deadline. If a task is ambiguous, ask me instead of guessing. Tell me if the "do now" quadrant is overloaded and what you would renegotiate.
Format: A table with the columns Task, Quadrant, Reason, and Next action; then the ordered list.
