Context: I work in [your type of work]. Right now I use [the apps you already use], and my main organization problem is [your main organization problem]. I can spend up to [your monthly budget] a month on tools.
Goal: I want a small set of tools that work well together and solve my problem, not a long list of trendy apps.
Deliverable: 1) A recommended stack of 3 to 5 tools covering tasks, calendar, notes or documents, and automation or AI assistants, explaining the role each one plays in my day. 2) A free alternative for each. 3) Which of my current apps to keep and which to drop. 4) A 2-week adoption plan so I don't switch everything at once.
Criteria: If you have web search, check current pricing, plans, and features, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Prioritize integrations between tools.
Format: A table with the columns Tool, Role, Approximate price, Free alternative, and Why it fits me; then sections 3 and 4 as bullets.
