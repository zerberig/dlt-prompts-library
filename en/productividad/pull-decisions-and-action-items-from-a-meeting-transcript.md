Context: This is the transcript or notes from a meeting about [meeting topic]: [transcript or notes].
Goal: Make sure no one leaves the meeting unsure about what was decided and who's doing what.
Deliverable: 1) A 3–5 line summary. 2) The decisions made, each with minimal context. 3) Action items with owner, due date, and dependencies. 4) Open questions or items still awaiting a decision. 5) A follow-up email ready to send to [email recipients].
Criteria: Include only what's in the text; if an action item has no owner or date, mark it "TBD" instead of making one up. Distinguish between what was decided and what was only proposed. Use names exactly as they appear.
Format: Sections with headings; action items in a table with the columns Task, Owner, Due date, and Notes; the email at the end, brief and in a friendly professional tone.
