Context: I have ADHD (or I really struggle to start big tasks) and I've been avoiding [the task that overwhelms you] for days. Today I have [time available today] and my energy level is [your energy level today].
Goal: Start today without feeling overwhelmed and see visible progress fast.
Deliverable: 1) A very easy first step that takes 2 minutes or less. 2) The task broken into 5–15 minute micro-steps, each with a concrete verb and a visible result. 3) An energy label for each step (low, medium, or high) so I can pick based on how I feel. 4) Natural stopping points and a small reward after each group of steps.
Criteria: Short, kind language, no lecturing. No step should force me to make a new decision: if something needs deciding, make it its own step with 2 options. If the task is too big for today, tell me where it's reasonable to stop.
Format: A numbered checklist with "☐" boxes, grouped into 3 blocks max. At the end, add one line reminding me that a health professional experienced with ADHD can help me find strategies that fit me.
