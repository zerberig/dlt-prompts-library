Context: I want to make [area or type of tasks you put off] more bearable. What motivates me most is [what motivates you most], and I'll keep track in [where you'll keep track].
Goal: Make progress feel visible and rewarding instead of like an obligation.
Deliverable: 1) A points system by task type based on effort. 2) Levels with real, proportionate rewards. 3) Streaks with "wildcards" for bad days, so one miss doesn't break everything. 4) A weekly challenge and a bigger monthly reward. 5) How to set it up in my tracking tool in 15 minutes.
Criteria: Rewards that don't sabotage the goal (for example, no rewarding saving money with big purchases). Rules simple enough to fit on an index card. Tune the difficulty so I win something in the first week.
Format: The game rules in a table, then the setup as a numbered list.
