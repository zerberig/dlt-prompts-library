Context: I have ADHD and my day-to-day includes [your main responsibilities]. The signs of exhaustion I've noticed lately are [signs you've noticed].
Goal: Catch overload early and have a plan to slow down before I burn out.
Deliverable: 1) Common burnout drivers for people with ADHD (overcommitting, perfectionism, hyperfocus without breaks, neglecting self-care) and which ones seem to apply to me. 2) A personal traffic light with green, yellow, and red signals, and what to do at each level. 3) Concrete strategies: how to say no, realistic expectations, delegating, scheduled breaks, and a minimum rest routine. 4) A 5-question weekly check-in.
Criteria: Compassionate, practical tone. General information and planning, not a diagnosis. If my signs include persistent low mood, anxiety, or sleep problems, recommend that I see a mental health professional.
Format: Sections with bullets and the traffic light as a table with the columns Level, Signals, and What I do.
