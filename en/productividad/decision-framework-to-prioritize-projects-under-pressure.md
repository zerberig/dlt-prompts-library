Context: I lead [your department or business]. Right now these projects and tasks are competing for time and resources: [competing projects and tasks]. We have [available resources] (people, hours, budget), and this quarter's objectives are [quarterly objectives].
Goal: I want a framework to decide quickly, with clear criteria, what gets done, what waits, and what gets cut, and to be able to explain those calls to my team and leadership.
Deliverable:
1) A scoring model with 4 or 5 criteria (for example, impact on objectives, real urgency, effort, risk of not doing it, and dependencies), with each one's scale and weight and the reasoning behind the weights.
2) The model applied to my list: a table with scores per criterion, total, and ranking.
3) A resource allocation for the next 4 weeks based on the ranking, flagging where there's overload.
4) A rule for when a new urgent request arrives: which questions to answer before accepting it and what moves if it comes in.
5) A short message to communicate the priorities and what's on hold.
Criteria: Flag scores that rely on assumptions and what data would confirm them. Show at least one hard call (something valuable that gets cut) and why.
Format: Tables for the model and ranking, bullets for the rest, and the message in a separate block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
