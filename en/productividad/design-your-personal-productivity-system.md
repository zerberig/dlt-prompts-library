Context: My work involves [your role and type of work]. Today I organize my tasks with [tools you use today], and what fails me most is [your main organization problem]. My goals for the next few months are [your main goals].
Goal: A reliable system where nothing slips, I always know what's next, and I can maintain it without it becoming another job.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis of why my current method fails. 2) The proposed system in four parts: capture (where everything goes first), organization (lists, projects, tags), planning (daily and weekly), and review. 3) How to set it up with my current tools, changing tools only if truly necessary. 4) A 2-week rollout plan that adds one new habit at a time.
Criteria: The simplest version that solves my problem; every piece has to earn its place. Connect weekly planning to my goals so urgent work doesn't always crowd out important work. Include how to restart the system after I drop it for a few days, because that will happen.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets, 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
