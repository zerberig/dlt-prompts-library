Context: I want to spend [number of hours] hours of deep work on [demanding task]. I'll be working in [your workspace] and my available window is [start and end time].
Goal: Make meaningful progress on that task without interruptions and finish the session knowing exactly what comes next.
Deliverable:
1. A 5–10 minute startup ritual: define the concrete outcome for the session, set up materials, and dump open loops into a note.
2. The full schedule with start and end times for every focus block and break.
3. The sub-task for each block, from most to least demanding as energy drops.
4. Distraction countermeasures tailored to my space: devices, notifications, and people around me.
5. A short screen-free activity for each break.
6. A 5-minute shutdown: what I got done, where I stopped, and the first step for next session.
Criteria: Blocks of 50–90 minutes depending on difficulty, with a longer break after the second block. Every sub-task needs a visible output ("draft of section 2"), not just "work on the project". Keep a 10–15% buffer for the unexpected.
Format: Plain text with 24-hour times, easy to copy into a calendar. 450 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
