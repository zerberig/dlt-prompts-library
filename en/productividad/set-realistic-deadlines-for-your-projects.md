Context: I'm planning [your project], with this scope and these deliverables: [scope and deliverables]. I can put [your available hours per week] a week into it, given my other responsibilities. The proposed due date is [proposed deadline].
Goal: I want to know whether that date is realistic and, if not, set one I can hit without stress or late nights, and know how to negotiate it.
Deliverable: 1) A breakdown of the project into tasks of no more than one workday each, with optimistic, likely, and pessimistic estimates for each. 2) The total time calculation with a buffer for the unexpected, explaining why people tend to underestimate and how you're compensating. 3) A verdict on the proposed date and a recommended date, with milestones. 4) What to cut or simplify if the date can't move. 5) A short message to propose the new date, or confirm the current one, to whoever needs it.
Criteria: Include time for reviews, feedback, and waiting on others, which usually get forgotten. Flag the tasks with the most uncertainty.
Format: A table with the columns Task, Optimistic, Likely, Pessimistic, and Dependencies; then sections 2 to 5 as bullets and the message in a separate block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
