Context: I have ADHD and I struggle to keep my home in order. I live in [your type of home], with [who you live with], and I can spend about [time available per day] a day cleaning.
Goal: I want a routine I can keep up for weeks, not a deep clean that wears me out and gets dropped.
Before building the plan, ask me in a single message what my biggest obstacles are (starting, knowing where to begin, getting bored halfway, forgetting) and which areas stress me out the most. Wait for my answer.
Deliverable: 1) A weekly plan of 5-15 minute micro-tasks, one area per day. 2) A "bare minimum" of 3 tasks for bad days. 3) Concrete visual cues and triggers: where things live and which task to chain to something I already do (like wiping the sink right after brushing my teeth). 4) Ideas to make it less boring: music, timers, small rewards.
Criteria: Every task starts with a verb and is small enough to look easy. No guilt language: if I miss a day, the plan just picks up the next one.
Format: A weekly table (Day, Area, Micro-tasks, Time) and sections 2 to 4 as bullets. If my struggles seem to be seriously affecting daily life, briefly suggest I talk to a health professional.
