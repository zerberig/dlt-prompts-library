Context: I want to do a weekly review every [day and time of your review]. I organize my tasks in [tools you use] and my current goals are [your current goals].
Goal: End each week knowing what worked, what didn't, and what to adjust, so I manage my time better week after week.
Deliverable: 1) A 30-minute agenda in 4 steps: clear and sort open tasks, compare results with the plan, learn (what worked, what held me back, where I lost time), and plan next week. 2) 8 to 10 guiding questions. 3) A template to record the answers and see how things change month to month. 4) A 10-minute express version for heavy weeks.
Criteria: Concrete questions that lead to decisions, not just reflection. Include one question about my energy and one about what I'll stop doing.
Format: An agenda with times, numbered questions, and the template as a table.
