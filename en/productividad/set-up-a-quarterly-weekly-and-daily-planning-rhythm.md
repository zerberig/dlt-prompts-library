Context: I'm [your role] and I work with [your team or collaborators]. My goals for this quarter are [quarterly goals]. I use [planning tools] to stay organized.
Goal: Make what I do every day move my quarterly goals forward instead of just putting out fires.
Deliverable:
1. A 60–90 minute quarterly ritual to turn goals into 3–5 key results and their projects.
2. A 30-minute weekly ritual to review, prioritize, decide what to delegate, and block time on the calendar.
3. A 10-minute daily ritual to pick the day's top 3 priorities.
4. For each ritual: when to do it, guiding questions, what gets recorded, and where in my tools.
5. Warning signs that the system is drifting away from the goals.
Criteria: Each level feeds the next: no daily priority without a link to a goal, except essential operations. Make delegation an explicit step of the weekly review. Keep total planning time under 2 hours a week, because heavy systems get abandoned.
Format: One section per ritual with its agenda as bullets and its guiding questions; at the end, a sample month with the rituals on the calendar.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
