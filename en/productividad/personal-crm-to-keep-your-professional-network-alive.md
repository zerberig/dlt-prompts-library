Context: These are important professional contacts, with the last time we spoke and one piece of context for each: [list of contacts]. My current career goal is [your career goal], and I can put [time you can give your network] into my network.
Goal: I want to keep genuine relationships with these people without relying on memory and without only reaching out when I need something.
Deliverable: 1) A CRM-style table with each contact's priority (high, medium, or low), how often to reach out, the suggested next contact date, and a message idea that adds value (a relevant article, congrats on a win, a specific question, or a useful introduction). 2) The 5 people to contact first and why. 3) A dated list of reminders I can copy into my calendar or task app.
Criteria: Message ideas should sound natural and specific to each person, never "just checking in". Respect the time I can give, even if that means some contacts get a lower frequency.
Format: A table with the columns Contact, Priority, Last conversation, Frequency, Next contact, and Message idea; then sections 2 and 3 as lists.
