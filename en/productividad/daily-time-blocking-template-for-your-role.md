Context: I work as [your role] with a workday of [working hours]. My most common types of work are [types of tasks you do].
Goal: A base structure for each day that I only need to tweak, instead of planning from scratch.
Deliverable: 1) A typical-day template with blocks by category (focus, communication, meetings, admin, and buffer) and suggested times. 2) Two variants: a meeting-heavy day and a production day. 3) Instructions for adapting it each morning in 5 minutes. 4) How to use Pomodoro inside the focus blocks.
Criteria: Put the most demanding work in the first hours unless I say otherwise. Batch email and messages into 2 or 3 fixed windows. Include at least one buffer block for surprises, because a plan with no slack breaks by mid-morning.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Block, and What goes here, easy to copy into a calendar, a doc, or a notes app.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
