Context: Tomorrow I have these commitments: [meetings and appointments with times]. These are my open tasks: [task list]. My workday runs [working hours].
Goal: Start tomorrow knowing exactly what to do and in what order, and end the day with the important things done.
Deliverable: An hour-by-hour plan that fits the tasks into the gaps between commitments, with the day's top 3 priorities marked, small tasks batched together, and a list of what doesn't fit and should move to another day.
Criteria: Estimate how long each task takes and add a 25% buffer. Put priorities in the longest gaps. If the day isn't long enough, tell me plainly.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Activity, and Priority; then the list of tasks to move.
