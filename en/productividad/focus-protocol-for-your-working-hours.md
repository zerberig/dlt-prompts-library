Context: I work in [your type of work] and my main distractions during the day are: [your main distractions] (messages, coworkers dropping by, social media, noise, task-switching). My working hours are [your working hours].
Goal: I want a protocol that gets me at least 2 focus blocks a day without cutting me off from my team.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis that splits my distractions into external (people, tools) and internal (boredom, anxiety, unclear tasks), with the likely cause of each. 2) One concrete countermeasure per distraction. 3) The rules for my focus blocks: length, timing, what's allowed, what isn't, and how I respond if someone interrupts. 4) A short message telling my team when I'm in focus mode and how to reach me for something urgent.
Criteria: Measures I can apply tomorrow without buying anything. For internal distractions, address the cause, not just the symptom, because blocking an app doesn't fix why I open it.
Format: A diagnosis table (Distraction, Type, Likely cause, Countermeasure), the rules as a numbered list, and the message in a separate block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
