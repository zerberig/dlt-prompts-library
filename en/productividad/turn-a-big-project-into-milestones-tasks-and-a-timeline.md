Context: My project is [project description] and it's due on [deadline]. I can put [hours per week] hours a week into it, and I have [available resources or people].
Goal: Go from a big, fuzzy goal to a realistic plan that tells me what to do each week.
Deliverable: 1) A definition of the final outcome and what "done" means. 2) 4 to 6 dated milestones. 3) The tasks for each milestone with estimated duration, owner, and dependencies. 4) A week-by-week timeline in text, Gantt-style. 5) The top 3 risks to the deadline and how to mitigate them.
Criteria: No task should take more than one working day; if it does, split it. Leave a 15–20% buffer before the deadline. If the plan doesn't fit the available time, tell me and suggest what to cut.
Format: A task table with the columns Milestone, Task, Duration, Owner, and Depends on; then the timeline with text bars, for example "Week 1 ████".
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
