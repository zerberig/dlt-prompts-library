Context: I lead a team of [your team's size and roles]. These are my current tasks and responsibilities: [list of your tasks].
Goal: I want to free up time for the work only I can do, while giving my team tasks that help them grow.
Deliverable: 1) A decision for each task: keep, delegate now, delegate with support, or drop. 2) For each task to delegate: to whom (by role), what that person would learn, how much autonomy they get, and how I'll check progress. 3) A short script for the handoff conversation of the most important task.
Criteria: Keep what depends on my judgment, key relationships, or confidential information. Delegate what is repeatable or what someone else can do at 80% of my level, because perfectionism isn't a reason for me to keep doing it. Flag the main risk of each delegation.
Format: A table with the columns Task, Decision, Suggested owner, Autonomy level, and Follow-up; then the script.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
