Edit the photo I upload: remove the harsh shadows falling on the main object, including the shadow of my phone or hand from shooting overhead. Rebuild the surface underneath with the same texture, color, and sharpness as the rest, with no smudges or blurry patches. Leave even lighting, like the diffused light of an overcast day, and keep a very soft contact shadow under the object so it doesn't look like it's floating. Keep everything else the same: colors, printed text, object positions, and framing.
