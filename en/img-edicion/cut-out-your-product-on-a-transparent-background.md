Edit the product photo I upload: remove the background completely and leave only the isolated product on a transparent background (or pure white if your tool can't export transparency). Cut it out with sharp, clean edges, no halos or leftover background, and respect interior gaps such as handles or spaces between parts. If the product is glass or has translucent parts, keep its natural transparency. Center the product with an even margin around it. Keep its shape, colors, texture, reflections, and labels unchanged.
