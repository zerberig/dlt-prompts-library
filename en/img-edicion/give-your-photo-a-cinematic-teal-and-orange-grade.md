Edit the image I upload with a cinematic color grade: shadows and backgrounds in a cool teal tone, skin tones and highlights in a natural warm orange, slightly lifted blacks, and a gentle S-curve contrast. Slightly desaturate distracting colors and let the skin be the warmest point in the image. Add a subtle bloom in the highlights, like a cinema lens. Keep everything else the same: content, face, pose, sharpness, and framing. Skin must still look natural, without exaggerated orange tones.
