I'm uploading two images: the first shows the person and the second is the background I want to use. Cut the person out of the first image and place them naturally into the second. Match their size and position to the scene's perspective, make the direction and color of the light match the background, add a contact shadow on the ground, and keep a clean edge around the hair with no halos. Match the grain and sharpness of both photos so they look shot with the same camera. Keep the person unchanged: face, pose, clothing, and proportions. Don't change any elements of the background.
