Transform the image I upload into a black pen-and-ink drawing on ivory cotton paper. Define the outlines with confident lines of varying weight and build the shadows only with crosshatching: spaced parallel lines in the midtones and dense crossed lines in the dark areas, with no gray fills or gradients. Leave the highlights as bare paper. The result should look like an antique book illustration or an editorial engraving. Keep the composition, proportions, and recognizable features of the original image. No text.
