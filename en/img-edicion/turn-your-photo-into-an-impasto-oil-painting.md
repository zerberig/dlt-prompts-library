Transform the image I upload into an oil painting on canvas. Use thick, visible brushstrokes with impasto in the highlights, thinner and more transparent layers in the shadows, and a barely noticeable canvas texture in the smooth areas. A rich, warm palette of ochres, siennas, deep greens, and creamy whites, with soft chiaroscuro lighting like classic portraits. Simplify the background into loose patches of color so the subject stands out. Keep the composition, pose, and recognizable features of the original. No signature or text.
