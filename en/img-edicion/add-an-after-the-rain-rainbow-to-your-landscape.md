Edit the landscape photo I upload: add a full rainbow arcing across the sky from behind the hills or buildings, with soft, semi-transparent colors that are stronger near the horizon, plus a faint second rainbow above it, the way it happens in real life. Adjust the sky so it looks freshly cleared after a storm: gray clouds retreating to one side and sunlight hitting the foreground with warm light. If there are wet surfaces, let them shine slightly. Photorealistic result. Keep everything else the same: terrain, buildings, people, and framing.
