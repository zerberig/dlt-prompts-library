Edit the photo I upload so it looks like it was taken at golden hour, about 30 minutes before sunset. Change the light to a low, warm sun coming from the side and slightly behind, with golden rim light on the hair, shoulders, and edges of objects. Lengthen the shadows on the ground in the correct direction and soften the contrast. Give the sky a peach-to-pale-blue gradient. Shaded areas stay slightly cooler. Keep everything else the same: people, faces, pose, objects, and framing.
