Edit the image I upload so the subject appears submerged in clear ocean water. Replace the background with a deep blue that lightens toward the surface, with sun rays piercing the water and rippling light patterns across the skin and clothing. Let the hair and fabric float gently, add small bubbles rising from the nose and the edges of the clothing, and a few suspended particles. Apply an overall blue-green tint with less contrast in the distance. Photorealistic result. Keep the face, expression, pose, and framing the same.
