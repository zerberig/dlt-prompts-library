Edit my photo that I upload: change my hair color to [desired hair color], as if it had been dyed at a professional salon. Apply the color with natural variation: slightly lighter strands where the light hits, deeper tones in the inner layers, and roots just a bit darker. Keep every strand, the volume, the hairstyle, the frizz, and the real shine of the hair. Adjust the eyebrows only if the contrast looks unnatural. Keep everything else the same: face, skin tone, makeup, clothing, background, lighting, and framing.
