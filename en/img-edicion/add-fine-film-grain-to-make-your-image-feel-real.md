Edit the image I upload to remove its overly clean, digital look. Add fine, even film grain that is more visible in the midtones and shadows, similar to a medium-speed film stock. Add subtle lens imperfections: a very slight vignette, minimal chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, and highlights that roll off softly instead of clipping. Slightly reduce excessive sharpening. The change should be felt, not noticed. Keep everything else the same: content, colors, faces, composition, and framing.
