Edit the city photo I upload so it looks like it was taken during a rainy night. Add fine diagonal raindrops, sharper in the foreground and blurred in the distance. The pavement and sidewalks are wet, with puddles showing upside-down reflections of streetlights, lit windows, and car lights. Metal and glass surfaces show droplets and streaks. Shift the light to a cool night with bluish tones and warm accents from the streetlights. Photorealistic result. Keep everything else the same: buildings, people, composition, and framing.
