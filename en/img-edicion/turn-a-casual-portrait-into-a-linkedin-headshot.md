Edit my photo that I upload into a professional LinkedIn profile picture. Crop to a head-and-shoulders close-up with the head slightly above center. Replace the background with a plain warm gray with a soft gradient. Apply flattering studio lighting: a soft key light at 45 degrees, light fill, and a touch of rim light. Correct the white balance and exposure. Keep my features, skin tone, glasses if I wear them, and real skin texture intact, with no plastic smoothing. Keep the clothing, hairstyle, and expression the same.
