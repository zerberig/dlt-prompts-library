Edit the portrait I upload with a natural skin retouch, like a serious magazine would do. Remove pimples, temporary redness, small marks, and stray lint, and slightly even out the tone on the cheeks, forehead, and chin. Soften under-eye circles only a little. Keep the real skin texture with its pores, expression lines, freckles, and moles, because they're part of the person. No plastic-skin effect and no changes to face shape. Keep everything else the same: features, makeup, hair, clothing, background, lighting, and framing.
