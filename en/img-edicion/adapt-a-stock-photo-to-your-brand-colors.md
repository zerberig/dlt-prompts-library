Edit the stock photo I upload so it matches my brand's visual identity, whose colors are [your brand colors]. Recolor the secondary elements (clothing, cushions, mugs, background walls, decor) using that palette, with one dominant main color and the others as accents. Adjust the overall grade so the neutrals lean slightly toward the main color and the image feels cohesive. Keep the real textures, shadows, and reflections on every recolored object. Keep the people, their skin tones, faces, pose, and framing the same.
