Edit the product photo I upload: replace the surface it sits on with a glossy black glass plate that mirrors the product, with an inverted reflection that is fainter and fades out toward the bottom. Change the background to a charcoal-to-black gradient, with a soft strip of light behind the product that outlines its silhouette. Add a thin contact shadow exactly where the product touches the glass. Premium catalog look. Keep the product's shape, colors, texture, and labels unchanged.
