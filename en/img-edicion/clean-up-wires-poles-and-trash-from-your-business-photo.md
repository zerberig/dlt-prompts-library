Edit the photo of my business exterior that I upload: remove overhead wires, power or phone poles, traffic cones, trash cans, litter on the ground, and parked cars blocking the entrance. Rebuild what was behind them (sky, walls, sidewalk, windows) following the location's perspective, lines, and textures, with no blurry or repeated patches. Don't change the architecture, facade colors, signs, or the business's own plants, so the photo stays faithful to the real place. Keep the lighting and framing the same.
