Edit the photo I upload so it looks like it was taken during a gentle snowfall. Add falling snowflakes with depth of field: large, blurred flakes in the foreground, sharp ones around the subject, and small ones in the distance. Cover horizontal surfaces (roofs, branches, railings, the ground) with a thin layer of snow and add frost and a few small icicles on metal or glass edges. Cool the light slightly with a bluish tone and let warm lights from windows or streetlamps contrast with it. Photorealistic result. Keep everything else the same: subject, face, pose, and framing.
