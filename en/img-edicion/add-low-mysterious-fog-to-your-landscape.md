Edit the landscape photo I upload: add low fog that creeps along the ground and pools in the low areas, between trees, and around rocks or buildings. Build layers of mist that grow denser with distance, so faraway elements look pale and diffused while nearby ones keep their contrast. If there's sunlight, let it pierce the fog in soft rays. Shift the palette to cool, desaturated tones, like a quiet dawn. Photorealistic result. Keep everything else the same: landscape elements, people, and framing.
