Transform the image I upload into a chalk drawing on a matte black chalkboard. Trace the outlines in white chalk with slightly uneven lines and fill the main areas with soft colored chalk (ochre, pink, mint green, and light blue), applied with visible strokes and a dusty texture. Leave smudges from erasing and white dust around it, like a real café chalkboard. Simplify the fine details but keep the composition and recognizable elements of the photo. Leave empty space on one side for writing later. No text.
