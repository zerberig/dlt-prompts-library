Edit the photo I upload to simulate a panning shot, as if the camera were tracking the moving subject. Keep the runner, cyclist, or vehicle sharp, and apply a horizontal motion blur to the background in the direction of travel, with streaked lights. Add a slight blur to the fastest-moving parts, such as wheels, feet, or hands, plus a few dust particles or droplets kicked up behind. The result should convey speed and look like a real sports photo. Keep the subject, their face, the pose, and the framing the same.
