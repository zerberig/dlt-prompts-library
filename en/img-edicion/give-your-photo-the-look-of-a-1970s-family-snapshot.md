Edit the photo I upload so it looks like a 1970s family photo print. Faded colors with a warm yellow-orange cast, lifted blacks that read as brown, greens that lean olive, and pale blue skies. Add visible film grain, a slight loss of sharpness at the edges, and a soft vignette. The paper shows a thin white border and slight color fading, like a photo kept in an album for decades. Keep everything else the same: people, faces, pose, composition, and framing.
