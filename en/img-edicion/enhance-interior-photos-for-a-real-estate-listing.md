Edit the interior photo I upload for a real estate listing. Increase the overall brightness so the space looks spacious and inviting, with warm natural light coming through the windows and the outside view visible, without blowing out the sky. Correct the white balance so walls and ceilings show their real color, straighten the vertical lines of walls and frames, and remove temporary personal items such as clothes, loose cables, or dishes. Don't add or remove permanent features of the property (walls, floors, windows, finishes, or water stains), so the listing stays honest. Keep the furniture and framing the same.
