Edit the image I upload: add dense stage smoke curling around the subject and rising behind them. Light the smoke with a hard cool-white backlight so its swirls and texture show, and let it form a glowing outline around the head and shoulders. Darken the background toward black and increase the contrast for a movie-poster look. The smoke drifts in front of the body in a few places, subtly, without covering the face. Keep everything else the same: face, expression, pose, clothing, and framing.
