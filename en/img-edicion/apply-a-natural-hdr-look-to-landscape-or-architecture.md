Edit the photo I upload with a natural HDR effect. Recover detail in the overexposed sky so the clouds and their texture show, and lift the shadows to reveal detail in walls, rocks, or foliage without them looking gray or washed out. Enhance the micro-texture of stone, wood, and metal, with rich but realistic colors. Avoid halos along edges against the sky and keep deep contrast in the blacks. Keep everything else the same: composition, scene elements, perspective, and framing.
