Edit the landscape photo I upload: add a flock of birds flying across the sky, 15 to 25 silhouettes in a loose formation heading toward the horizon. The closest ones are larger and show wing detail at different stages of the wingbeat, with a slight motion blur; the distant ones are small, almost dots. Match their color and contrast to the sky's light (dark against the light if the sun is in front). Photorealistic result that adds scale and movement. Keep everything else the same: sky, terrain, people, and framing.
