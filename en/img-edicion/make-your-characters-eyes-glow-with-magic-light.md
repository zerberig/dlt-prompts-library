Edit the image I upload: make the character's eyes glow with a magical [glow color] light. The irises become luminous with an almost white center and the most intense color toward the edge, with the pupil barely visible. Let the light spill a little and form a soft halo around each eye, lighting the eyelids, lashes, cheekbones, and bridge of the nose with a reflection of the same color. Slightly darken the rest of the scene so the effect stands out. Keep everything else the same: face, expression, pose, hair, clothing, and framing.
