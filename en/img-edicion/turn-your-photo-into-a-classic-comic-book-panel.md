Transform the image I upload into a classic 1960s comic book illustration. Thick black inked outlines, solid black shadows, saturated flat colors (red, yellow, cyan blue), and visible halftone dots in the midtones, as if printed on cheap magazine paper. Add subtle speed lines behind the subject for energy and frame the scene in a panel with a black border. Keep the composition, the pose, and the recognizable features of the people and objects in the original photo. No speech bubbles.
