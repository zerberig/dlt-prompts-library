I'm uploading two images from my own brand: the first is the product photo with the old label and the second is the new label design. Replace the label in the photo with the new design, adapting it to the container's shape: it should follow the curvature, warp with the perspective, and sit at the same height and size. Apply the same lighting, shadows, and reflections the original label had, plus the material's texture (matte paper, glossy plastic, or glass). Keep the new design's text exactly as it is. Keep everything else the same: container, cap, background, and framing.
