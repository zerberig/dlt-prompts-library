Edit my travel photo that I upload: remove every stranger in the background and on the sides, including people partly cut off at the edges. Rebuild what was behind them (floor, walls, steps, plants, sky) following the location's lines, perspective, texture, and light, with no blurry patches or repeated patterns. Keep shadows only if they're cast by elements that stay. Keep everything else the same: me and my companions in the foreground, faces, pose, colors, and framing.
