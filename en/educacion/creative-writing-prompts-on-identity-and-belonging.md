Context: I teach writing to [grade or level] students and want to explore the themes of identity and belonging. My class is into [students' interests].
Goal: Prompts that get students writing in their own voice and work for students with different levels of writing confidence.
Deliverable: 6 creative writing prompts across three challenge levels (2 accessible, 2 intermediate, 2 challenging), mixing short story, poem, letter, and flash fiction. For each: the prompt addressed to the student in second person, one question to spark ideas before writing, and a suggested length.
Criteria: Prompts allow students to write through fiction, so no one feels pushed to expose their personal life. They start from concrete situations (an inherited object, a move, a language spoken at home) rather than abstract questions. Include a range of cultural contexts.
Format: A numbered list grouped by level. At the end, 3 questions for sharing and discussing the pieces respectfully as a group.
