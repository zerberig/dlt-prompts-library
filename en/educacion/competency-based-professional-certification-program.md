Context: I want to design a certification program in [certification field] for [candidate profile], lasting about [program length].
Goal: A program that proves graduates can perform the real job and that rests on a solid foundation if we later seek endorsement or accreditation.
Deliverable: Graduate profile; competency map (knowledge, skills, and attitudes); modules with objectives and hours; assessment for each competency with passing criteria and required evidence; entry and recertification requirements; and applicable endorsement or accreditation options.
Criteria: Assess with authentic tasks (cases, simulations, projects) in addition to exams, because that's how performance is demonstrated. If you have web search, use it to identify real accreditation bodies and competency standards for the field and country; mark as an estimate anything you can't confirm with a source.
Format: Sections with headings; the competency map and assessment in tables.
