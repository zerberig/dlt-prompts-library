Context: I teach [topic] to [grade or level] in a [teaching mode] setting (in person, online, or hybrid), with a class of [class size] students.
Goal: Have students learn by explaining things to each other, through structured activities where everyone contributes.
Deliverable: 6 peer learning activities (for example, jigsaw, think-pair-share, peer instruction with concept questions, peer tutoring, guided peer review, and expert rotation) applied to my topic. For each: how it works, time, how to form groups, an example using real content from the topic, how to keep one person from doing all the work, and how to run it online with breakout rooms or shared documents.
Criteria: Every activity builds in individual accountability (each student submits or explains something). Instructions ready to give the class.
Format: One block per activity; at the end, a table showing when each one works best.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
