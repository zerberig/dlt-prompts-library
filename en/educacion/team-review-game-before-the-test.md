Context: I need to review [topic] with my [grade or level] class of [number of students] students, in a 40-50 minute session, using materials any classroom has.
Goal: I want the class to reach the test having practiced the most important content in a fun way.
Deliverable: A team game with a name, objective, materials, rules written so I can read them aloud to students, a scoring system, and a bank of 20 questions with answers, increasing in difficulty.
Criteria: Everyone should participate, not just the fastest students (for example, rotating turns or team-agreed answers). Include questions that ask students to recall, apply, and explain. Add one variation for large groups and one for playing online.
Format: Teacher instructions, student rules as a numbered list, and the question bank in a table with the columns Question, Answer, and Difficulty.
