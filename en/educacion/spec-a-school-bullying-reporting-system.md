Context: I work at [type of institution or project], and we want a system to report and follow up on bullying cases in [education level]. Current resources and constraints: [resources and constraints] (budget, staff, tools we already use).
Goal: A spec that school leadership and a developer can use to decide what to build or buy, and that makes intervention faster and more reliable.
Deliverable: 1) System overview and roles (students, families, teachers, counselors, administrators). 2) A student reporting flow with an anonymous option and access channels, described screen by screen. 3) Teacher and counselor views: intake, severity triage, follow-up, and intervention logging. 4) An administrator dashboard with trends and indicators. 5) A response protocol with maximum response times per severity level. 6) Privacy and security for minors' data. 7) A phased rollout plan.
Criteria: Design for a scared student reporting from a phone in under 2 minutes. Protect the reporter's identity and limit who can see each case. State that the protocol must be validated with the school's counseling or psychology team and with local data protection and school safety rules; if you have web search, check the regulations in [country] and flag anything you can't confirm.
Format: Seven sections with headings, bullets, and tables where they help. 900–1,400 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
