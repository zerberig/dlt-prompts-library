Context: I teach a course or training on [topic] to [target audience] in a [delivery mode] format, with about [number of learners] learners. Right now I only know who completed lessons, not who actually learned.
Goal: A system to measure each learner's real progress and catch anyone falling behind in time.
Deliverable: 1) Checkpoints per module, each using a method suited to what it checks (diagnostic quiz, hands-on exercise, peer-reviewed submission, live demo). 2) A mastery criterion for each checkpoint: what "they've got it" means. 3) A simple tracking dashboard (spreadsheet columns) with early-warning flags. 4) Support actions for each type of flag. 5) How to show learners their own progress.
Criteria: Distinguish completion from mastery. Methods that are workable for the number of learners I have.
Format: A checkpoint table; the dashboard structure; actions as bullets.
