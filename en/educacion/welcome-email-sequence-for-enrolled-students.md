Context: I teach an online course on [course topic] on [course platform]. It starts on [start date] and runs for [duration]. The students are [target audience].
Goal: Every enrollee shows up on day one knowing what to do, with clear expectations and eager to start, to reduce first-week dropout.
Deliverable: A 4-email sequence: 1) immediate confirmation and welcome; 2) how the course works (access, schedule, recommended weekly time, how to get help); 3) a pre-start reminder with one small prep task; 4) a first-day email. For each: when it's sent, subject line, preview text, and body.
Criteria: Short emails (100–180 words), each with a single main action. Warm, professional tone. Include where to find support and what happens if someone falls behind.
Format: Each email in its own block with its send timing.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
