Context: I teach [subject] to [grade or level], and we're currently on [topic]. My students live in [your students' context] (city, rural area, shared interests).
Goal: Give a convincing answer to "why do I need this?" and make the topic more relevant to them.
Deliverable: 1) 8 real-life connections, each with the concrete situation, how the topic shows up there, and a question to pose in class. 2) 2 connections to other subjects. 3) A 15-minute mini activity built on the strongest connection.
Criteria: Everyday situations for students that age (getting around, money, social media, sports, their family's work), not generic textbook examples. Explain the link precisely without forcing it.
Format: A table with the columns Situation, Connection to the topic, and Class question; then the cross-subject connections and the mini activity.
