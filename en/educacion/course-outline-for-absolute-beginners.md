Context: I want to create a self-paced course on [course topic] for people starting from zero, such as [beginner profile].
Goal: Make sure no one gets lost or quits in the first lessons, and that by the end they've made something concrete they can show.
Deliverable: The course's end result; required prior knowledge (ideally none); an outline of 4-6 modules moving from simplest to applied, each with its lessons and a quick win; a glossary of the terms that intimidate beginners most; the 5 most common beginner mistakes and which lesson prevents each; and a simple final project.
Criteria: One new idea at a time, with examples before definitions. Explain in one line why each module comes in that order.
Format: A numbered list of modules with lessons and quick win; the other sections as bullets.
