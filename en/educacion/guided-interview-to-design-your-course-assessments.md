Context: I'm building an online course on [course topic] in a [course format] format, and I'm not sure how to assess my students.
Goal: Design assessments and assignments that genuinely measure whether students learned, based on my specific situation.
Deliverable: First, interview me: ask one question at a time, up to 8 total, about the outcomes I expect, my students' profile, how much time I have for grading, my platform's tools, and whether the course grants a certificate. When you're done, give me an assessment plan with assessment types by module, sample assignments, grading criteria, how to give feedback at scale, and how to design submissions that discourage dishonest AI use.
Criteria: Tailor each question to my previous answers and explain in one line why you're asking it.
Format: During the interview, only the question and its reason; at the end, the plan with one table per module.
