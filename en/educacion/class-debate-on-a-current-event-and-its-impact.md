Context: I teach social studies to [grade or level]. I want to spend [session length] analyzing [news story or current issue] and how it affects society.
Goal: I want students to discuss in teams using arguments and evidence, listen to views different from their own, and connect the story to concepts from the course.
Deliverable: A neutral summary of the event to read in class, 3 or 4 perspectives from different affected stakeholders, a team activity with roles (moderator, note-taker, fact-checker, devil's advocate), 6 guiding questions, and an individual reflection to close.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to confirm the facts and cite your sources; flag anything you can't verify. Present the perspectives fairly and without taking sides, because the point is for students to build their own position. Language suited to the group's age.
Format: Sections with headings and suggested timing for each part of the session.
