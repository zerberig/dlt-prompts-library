Context: I teach [subject] to [grade or level], in a class of [class size] students. The main challenges are [class challenges] (interruptions, phone use, low participation, conflicts between students).
Goal: A classroom management plan that creates a positive, predictable climate so I spend more time teaching and less time correcting behavior.
Deliverable: 1) 3–5 class agreements phrased positively and how to build them with the class. 2) Routines for the start of class, transitions, and the end of class. 3) Prevention strategies for the challenges I mentioned, with the benefits and possible drawbacks of each. 4) A tiered response to disruptive behavior, from most discreet to most formal. 5) How to reinforce positive behavior without relying on rewards.
Criteria: Respectful strategies that protect the relationship with students and never call anyone out in front of the class. If a case involves violence, bullying, or a serious personal situation, recommend referring it to the counselor or administration according to school protocol.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets; the tiered response as a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
