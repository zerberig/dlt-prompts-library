Context: I teach a course on [course topic] for [target audience]. In [module or lesson], I want learners to practice [skill to practice] in a realistic situation.
Goal: A case study or role-play activity that improves retention because it forces learners to apply what they learned and make decisions.
Deliverable: 1) A 300–400 word case with characters, context, relevant data, and a dilemma with no single right answer. 2) A role-play version with 2 or 3 roles, each with a private brief. 3) Analysis questions in increasing order of difficulty. 4) A facilitator guide: timings, how to run it online, and key points for the debrief.
Criteria: A situation that feels believable to the audience, with enough data to decide but some ambiguous information, as in real life. Fictional characters and companies.
Format: Sections with headings; the roles in separate blocks so they can be handed out.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
