Context: I teach an online course on [course topic] for [target audience] on [platform or community space]. Forum participation is low, and learners barely interact with each other.
Goal: Turn the course into a community where learners learn from each other and feel supported.
Deliverable: 1) 6 discussion prompts, one per week, that ask learners to share their own experience or solve something, not just give an opinion. 2) Short participation guidelines (how to reply to a peer in a way that adds value). 3) 4 peer collaboration activities (peer review, group challenges, Q&A sessions, accountability groups). 4) A moderation plan: what I do each week in 30 minutes.
Criteria: Each prompt produces different answers across learners, so reading others is worth it. Account for learners moving at different paces.
Format: Numbered sections; prompts ready to copy.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
