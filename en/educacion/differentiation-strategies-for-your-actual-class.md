Context: I teach [subject and topic] to a class with this profile: [class description] (mastery levels, specific needs, English language learners, different paces). I have [available resources].
Goal: Adjust my teaching so everyone moves toward the same objective without me having to prepare three separate lessons.
Deliverable: 6 concrete strategies organized by what they differentiate (content, process, product, or environment), each with an example applied to my topic, the prep time it takes, and which students it helps most. At the end, the 2 you recommend I start this week and why.
Criteria: Strategies that are realistic for a busy teacher. Talk about needs and mastery levels rather than sorting students by "learning styles," because the evidence doesn't support that approach. If a need calls for specialized support, suggest coordinating with the support team or a professional.
Format: A table with the columns Strategy, What it differentiates, Example in my topic, Prep, and Best for; then the recommendation in one paragraph.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
