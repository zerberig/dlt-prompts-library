Context: I teach [subject] to [grade or level], and we're going to discuss [topic]. The class's core material is [source text or resource].
Goal: Lead a discussion that moves from understanding to analysis, evaluation, and creation instead of staying at recall.
Deliverable: 12 open-ended questions grouped into 4 levels (understand, analyze, evaluate, create), each with 1 or 2 follow-up questions and a note on what kind of answer shows strong reasoning.
Criteria: Questions with no single right answer, tied to the material and to students' lives. Include 2 questions that force students to consider the opposing view.
Format: A table with the columns Level, Question, Follow-up, and Sign of strong reasoning.
