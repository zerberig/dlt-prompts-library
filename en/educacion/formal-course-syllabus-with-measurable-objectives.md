Context: I'm going to teach a [course length] course (for example, 12 weeks) on [course topic] for [course audience].
Goal: A formal syllabus I can submit to my institution or hand to students on day one.
Deliverable: Course description and purpose; overall goals; units and modules with measurable learning objectives (written with observable verbs), content, suggested activities, and resources; a week-by-week breakdown; a grading scheme with percentages; and course policies (attendance, submissions, AI use, and academic integrity).
Criteria: Clear alignment between objectives, activities, and assessment: every objective is practiced and assessed at some point. A realistic weekly workload.
Format: A document with headings; the weekly breakdown and grading scheme as tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
