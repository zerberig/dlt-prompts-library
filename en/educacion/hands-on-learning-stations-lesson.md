Context: I teach [topic] to [grade or level] in a class of [class size] students. I have [class length] and access to [available materials].
Goal: A station-rotation lesson where students move around and apply the topic through hands-on activities, simulations, and challenges.
Deliverable: 4–5 stations; for each: objective, hands-on activity or simulation, materials, student instructions on a card (6 steps max), the evidence they leave behind, and time. Also: how to set up the room and the rotation, a backup station for teams that finish early, and a 10-minute wrap-up connecting what they saw at each station.
Criteria: Stations run without me being there constantly, since I'll be circulating. Vary the type of activity: hands-on manipulation, simulation (digital or on paper), solving a case, and creating something. Low-cost materials.
Format: One printable card per station; logistics as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
