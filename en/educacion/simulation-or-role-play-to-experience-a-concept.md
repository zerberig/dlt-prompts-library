Context: I teach [topic] to [grade or level], and I want students to experience the concept instead of just hearing about it. I have [available resources].
Goal: Help them understand how the system or process works by living it from the inside, so they can explain it in their own words afterward.
Deliverable: 5 clearly different simulation or role-play ideas (for example, students acting as parts of a system, a mock market, or a negotiation), compared in a table. Then develop the best one: setup, roles, step-by-step flow, debrief questions, and how to tell whether they learned.
Criteria: Each idea must model the concept accurately. Point out where the simulation simplifies reality so I can clarify it in the debrief, because that prevents misconceptions.
Format: A table with the columns Idea, Concept it models, Time, Materials, and Prep difficulty; then the chosen idea developed in sections.
