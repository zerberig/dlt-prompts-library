Context: I teach [topic or unit] to [grade or level] and want to run a [session length] virtual field trip using virtual tours, online museums, simulations, or interactive maps.
Goal: Give the trip a clear learning purpose that goes beyond watching a video and aligns with the unit's objectives.
Deliverable: 3 or 4 virtual destinations that fit the topic; an itinerary with timing and what to observe at each stop; activities before (prediction questions), during (an observation guide or scavenger hunt), and after (a product or reflection); team roles; a way to assess; and a backup plan if the connection fails.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to confirm each destination exists and is free; if you can't, flag it as needing verification. Adapt everything to the age group and available technology, with clear instructions so students can navigate alone or in pairs.
Format: The itinerary in a table with the columns Stop, Time, What to observe, and Question or task; the activities in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
