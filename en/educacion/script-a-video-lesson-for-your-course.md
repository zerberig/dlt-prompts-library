Context: I'm producing a recorded course on [course topic] for [target audience]. The outline is done, and now I'm recording the lesson [lesson title], whose goal is for learners to be able to [lesson objective].
Goal: A short, clear video lesson people watch all the way through.
Deliverable: 1) A 5–8 minute script split into hook, explanation, demo or example, practice, and wrap-up, with what I say and what's on screen in each part. 2) Visual cues (screen share, diagram, on-screen text). 3) An exercise to do after the video. 4) A production checklist: audio, lighting, captions, and length.
Criteria: One main idea per video; if the objective doesn't fit, suggest splitting it. Natural spoken language, no reading slides.
Format: The script as a two-column table (What I say / What's on screen); then the exercise and checklist.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
