Context: I have a course on [course topic] for [target audience]. The main result learners get is [result learners achieve], and what sets it apart from other courses is [what makes it different].
Goal: Nail down a clear name and value proposition before any promotion, so my audience can tell in seconds whether the course is for them.
Deliverable: 1) 10 names in different styles (descriptive, outcome-driven, creative), each with a subtitle. 2) The top 3 and why. 3) A one-sentence value proposition and a 3-sentence version. 4) The building blocks of a short course description: who it's for, who it's not for, what they'll be able to do afterward, and format. 5) 3 key messages to repeat across all promotion.
Criteria: Specific, achievable promises, with no income guarantees or results that don't depend on the course. Names that are easy to say and search for.
Format: A numbered list of names; the rest in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
