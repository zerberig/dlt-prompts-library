Context: I'm starting a [subject or topic] course with a [grade or level] group in a [delivery mode] format (in person, online, or hybrid). The group is diverse in background, experience, pace, and needs.
Goal: From the first week, everyone should feel welcome, safe to participate, and held to high expectations.
Deliverable: A first-week plan with a subject-related icebreaker, co-created class agreements, an intake survey to learn students' interests and needs, and equitable participation routines (turn-taking, think time, multiple ways to respond). Then practices for the rest of the course: inclusive language, materials that reflect different realities, and how to respond to exclusionary comments.
Criteria: Activities that never force anyone to share personal information. For mental health situations or special needs, suggest referring to the counseling team or a professional.
Format: A day-by-day plan and, at the end, a list of ongoing practices.
