Context: I'm launching an online course on [course topic] for [target audience]. Price: [price and currency]. Resources for promotion: [available resources] (current audience, budget, hours per week, partners).
Goal: Pick the channels that can actually bring me students with what I have, instead of trying to be everywhere.
Deliverable: 1) An assessment of 6–8 possible channels (social media, email, partnerships, free webinars, communities, paid ads, SEO, referrals) with effort, cost, and potential for my case. 2) The top 3 channels with concrete actions for the 6 weeks before launch. 3) Metrics to tell whether each channel is working and when to change tactics.
Criteria: Prioritize based on my real resources. If you have web search, use it for current cost or conversion benchmarks, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. No fake scarcity or invented testimonials, because they destroy trust.
Format: A channel comparison table (Channel, Effort, Cost, Potential, Why); then the week-by-week plan and a metrics table with alert thresholds.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
