Context: I want to teach [topic] to [grade or level] through inquiry-based learning, in a [lesson length] lesson.
Goal: Have students discover the concept by investigating and reasoning, rather than having it explained up front.
Deliverable: A driving question that sparks curiosity; a warm-up to activate prior knowledge; an exploration phase with materials or data students analyze; questions that guide without giving away the answer; a whole-class discussion where the concept is formalized; a follow-up application activity; and a short assessment.
Criteria: The teacher guides rather than explains first. Anticipate 3 misconceptions that might come up and how to redirect them with questions.
Format: A phase-by-phase plan with timing and the teacher's and students' roles in each phase.
