Context: I'm launching the course [course name and topic] for [target audience], priced at [price], with enrollment opening on [launch date]. I have an email list of people interested in the topic.
Goal: Turn subscribers into students without overwhelming them or pressuring them with deceptive tactics.
Deliverable: A sequence of 7 emails (warm-up, enrollment open, value content, FAQs and objections, social proof, last call, and close), each with send day, goal, subject line plus 2 variants, full body copy, and call to action. At the end, one idea for repurposing each email on social media.
Criteria: A warm, helpful tone; every email should deliver something even if the reader doesn't buy. Use testimonials and deadlines only if they're real, and write "real testimonial to add" wherever one goes, because invented urgency or results destroy trust.
Format: Each email as a block with headings; body copy of 120-200 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
