Context: I'm running [type of session] (webinar, workshop, or internal training) lasting [length] on [topic] for [target audience]. When it ends, I want attendees to [desired action] (apply a technique, sign up for a course, implement something at work).
Goal: A live session that holds attention from start to finish and leaves attendees with a concrete result.
Deliverable: 1) A minute-by-minute structure with blocks of no more than 10 minutes of presenting, each followed by an interaction (poll, chat question, quick exercise). 2) An opening hook for the first 2 minutes. 3) A hands-on exercise attendees do during the session. 4) A plan for Q&A. 5) Follow-up material to send afterward (summary, template, or assignment).
Criteria: All content serves the desired action; leave out anything that doesn't. Write each interaction's instructions so I can read them out live as is.
Format: An agenda table with the columns Minute, Block, What I say or do, and Interaction; the rest in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
