Context: I'm reviewing [topic] with [grade or level] before [test or end of unit]. I have [available resources] (whiteboard, projector, phones, or no tech at all).
Goal: Review key concepts with games that get the whole class involved, not just the fastest students.
Deliverable: 5 different review games (team-based, movement, quiz show, card-based, or digital). For each: name, how to play in 4–6 steps, time, materials, ready-written sample questions on the topic, and how to make sure everyone participates.
Criteria: At least two games with no tech. Rules that can be explained in 2 minutes. Friendly, team-based competition so no one is put on the spot.
Format: One block per game with a heading; at the end, a comparison table with the columns Game, Time, Materials, and Best for.
