Context: I teach [topic] to [grade or level] and want study material students fill in while I explain or while they read.
Goal: Help them identify and organize the key concepts instead of copying everything or writing nothing down.
Deliverable: 1) 1–2 pages of guided notes: a heading per concept, sentences with fill-in blanks marked by a line (____), a comparison chart, and space for their own example. 2) The completed version for the teacher. 3) An outline for a one-page infographic summarizing the topic: title, 4–6 blocks with short text, the icon or diagram for each block, and the reading order. 4) 3 self-check questions at the end.
Criteria: Blanks land on the key ideas, not on trivial words. Clear language for the level.
Format: Student notes, teacher key, and infographic outline in separate sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
