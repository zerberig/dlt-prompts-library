Context: My [grade or level] students have already covered [topic]. I want a short challenge of [challenge length] (for example, 2 weeks) where they apply it in teams to a real problem at school or in the community.
Goal: Have them use what they learned to propose a concrete solution and learn to collaborate with clear responsibilities.
Deliverable: 3 real problem options related to the topic for the class to choose from; team roles and their responsibilities; stages with interim deliverables; the final proposal format and who to present it to (school leaders, families, or a local organization); a rubric for teamwork and solution quality; and a collaboration self-assessment.
Criteria: Problems that are close to home and manageable for the age group, with different final product options so each team can contribute from its strengths. Include agreements for resolving conflicts within teams.
Format: Sections with headings and the rubric as a table.
