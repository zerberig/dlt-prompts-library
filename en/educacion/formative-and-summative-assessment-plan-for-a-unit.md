Context: I'm teaching a [number of weeks]-week unit on [topic] to [grade or level]. My school requires [assessment requirements] (percentages, number of grades, format).
Goal: An assessment plan that tells me during the unit who's on track and who isn't, so I can adjust in time, as well as grade at the end.
Deliverable: 1) The unit's expected learning outcomes. 2) A week-by-week formative assessment calendar with concrete techniques (exit tickets, hinge questions, self-assessment, peer assessment) and the decision I make with each result. 3) Summative assessment with its weighting. 4) Moments for student self-reflection. 5) How to communicate results to students and families.
Criteria: Every instrument measures a specific learning outcome; none are redundant. A realistic grading load for one teacher. Follow my school's requirements.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Outcome, Instrument, Type (formative or summative), Weight, and What I do with the result; the rest in short sections.
