Context: I want to create and sell an online course on [course topic]. My monthly tool budget is [budget and currency], and my technical skill level is [technical skill level].
Goal: Pick the minimum set of tools to produce, host, sell, and follow up on the course without overpaying or overcomplicating things.
Deliverable: 1) Recommended tools by stage: planning, recording and editing, materials design, course platform, payments, email, and community, including useful AI tools at each stage. 2) For each stage, one free or low-cost option and one full-featured option, and who each one suits. 3) A minimal starter stack and when it makes sense to upgrade. 4) Accessibility considerations (captions, contrast, mobile use).
Criteria: If you have web search, check current pricing and features, since they change often; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Account for payments in local currencies if I sell internationally.
Format: A table by stage with the columns Stage, Budget option, Full-featured option, and Best for; then the starter stack as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
