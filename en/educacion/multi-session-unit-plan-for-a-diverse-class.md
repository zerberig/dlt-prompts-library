Context: I'm teaching a unit on [topic] to [grade or level] over [number of sessions] sessions. The class is diverse: [class characteristics] (skill levels, interests, support needs).
Goal: A complete unit sequence where every student has several ways to access the content, practice it, and show what they learned.
Deliverable: 1) Unit objective and final product. 2) A per-session plan with objective, main activity, and closing. 3) Across the unit, a variety of representations (visual, spoken, written, hands-on) and ways of working (individual, pairs, teams). 4) At least two options for students to demonstrate learning in the final product, with shared criteria.
Criteria: Vary representations and activities for the whole class instead of assigning tasks by "learning styles", since that classification lacks solid research support. Each session builds on the last. Mark where formative assessment happens, and include an estimated time for each activity so I can check the plan fits the sessions.
Format: A table with the columns Session, Objective, Main activity, Representation and grouping, and Assessment; then the final product with its options and criteria.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
