Context: I want to run an extracurricular activity on [topic or field] for [grade or level] students. We have [available time and resources] (sessions per week, space, budget).
Goal: A club or challenge that builds creativity, collaboration, and critical thinking, and that students keep coming back to because they want to.
Deliverable: 1) 3 possible formats (weekly club, one-day intensive challenge, fair or competition) with pros and cons. 2) For the best-fitting format, an 8-session or 8-stage plan with the objective and activity for each. 3) A central challenge with a final product they can show the school community. 4) Roles for students and the supervising teacher. 5) How to recruit students and keep attendance up.
Criteria: Students make real decisions about their project. No grades: motivation comes from achievement and recognition. Resources in line with what's available.
Format: A format comparison table, then the detailed plan in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
