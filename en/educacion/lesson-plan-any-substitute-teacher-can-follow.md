Context: I need a [subject] lesson plan for [grade or level] on [topic], lasting [lesson length]. Objectives: [learning objectives].
Goal: A plan so clear that another teacher, even a substitute who doesn't know the class, could teach it without further prep.
Deliverable: A plan with these sections: objectives, materials, opening, explanation with key concepts and examples, guided practice, independent practice, assessment, and adaptations for students who need support and for early finishers. Include what the teacher should say at key moments and the answers students are expected to give.
Criteria: Realistic timing that adds up to the stated length. Instructions in order and free of jargon.
Format: Plain text with headings, ready to print on one or two pages.
