Context: I sell the course [course name] to [target audience]. By the end, students achieve [main outcome]. Price: [price and payment options].
Goal: A clear sales page that helps the right person decide to enroll.
Deliverable: Full copy by section: headline and subheadline (3 options), problem and promise, who it's for and who it's not for, curriculum summarized as benefits, instructor bio, testimonials section, price and guarantee, 6 FAQs, and a call to action repeated throughout the page. Also an SEO title and meta description.
Criteria: Realistic, verifiable promises, with no guaranteed income or results. Wherever a testimonial goes, write "real testimonial to add," because inventing them is deceptive. Short sentences that read well on a phone.
Format: Sections in the order they'll appear on the page, with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
