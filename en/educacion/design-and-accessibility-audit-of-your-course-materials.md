Context: I'm preparing [material type] (slides, PDFs, videos, or online modules) for a course on [course topic] aimed at [course audience]. I'll paste the material or its description at the end of this message.
Goal: Make it clear, appealing, and accessible to everyone, including people who use screen readers, have low vision or hearing difficulties, or study on their phones.
Deliverable: An audit covering what already works, the issues found ranked by impact, and a concrete fix for each; then a reusable checklist for my future materials.
Criteria: Review visual hierarchy, readability, contrast, amount of text per screen, image alt text, captions and transcripts, heading structure, and plain language. Use the WCAG guidelines as a reference and explain each recommendation in practical terms, without technical jargon.
Format: A table with the columns Issue, Why it matters, Fix, and Priority (high, medium, or low), followed by the checklist with checkboxes.
