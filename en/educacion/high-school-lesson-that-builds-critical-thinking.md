Context: I teach [subject] in high school and need a [lesson length] lesson on [topic].
Goal: Have students analyze, question, and argue, and see why the topic matters to them today.
Deliverable: Learning objectives; materials; an opening hook tied to teenagers' lives (a situation, a surprising fact, or a dilemma); a main activity analyzing sources or cases; a structured discussion; a closing with a short product (written argument, concept map, or justified position); and an assessment with clear criteria.
Criteria: Activities that require backing claims with evidence. Language that respects teenagers rather than talking down to them.
Format: A plan with sections and timing for each part.
