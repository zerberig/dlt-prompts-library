Context: I'm putting together a course on [course topic] for [target audience], with a total length of [total duration] and a [course format] format (in person, live online, self-paced, or blended).
Goal: An ordered outline I can use to produce the course and to show learners before they enroll.
Deliverable: The full outline: overall objective, prerequisites, and 4 to 8 modules in logical order. For each module: learning objectives, lessons, hands-on activity, assessment method, and recommended resources (readings, tools, examples).
Criteria: Each module builds on the previous one; explain in one line why it sits where it does. Objectives start with an observable verb (apply, design, compare) so they can be assessed. Fit the workload to the duration I gave you. Only recommend resources that exist; if you're unsure about one, say so.
Format: A header with overall objective and prerequisites; one section per module with bullets; at the end, a table showing time allocation per module.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
