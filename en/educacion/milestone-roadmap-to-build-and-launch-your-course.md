Goal: A realistic roadmap with milestones and deliverables so the course actually gets finished and ships on time.

Criteria: If the deadline doesn't work with my hours, tell me and propose a minimum viable version of the course. Start by validating with potential learners before producing anything.
Format: A milestone table (Phase, Deliverables, Definition of done, Weeks); a checkbox checklist; risks as bullets.
