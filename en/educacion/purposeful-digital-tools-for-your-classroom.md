Context: I teach [topic] to [grade or level], and in my classroom I have [available technology] (for example, a projector, students' phones, a computer lab, or limited connectivity).
Goal: Use technology where it genuinely improves learning and collaboration, not as decoration.
Deliverable: 5 concrete uses for my topic organized by purpose (collaborate, create, practice with instant feedback, research, assess); each with the type of tool, examples of current free tools, what it looks like in class step by step, and an offline alternative. Include one that uses AI assistants, with responsible-use guidelines for students.
Criteria: If you have web search, confirm the tools are still available, and prioritize free ones. Protect privacy: avoid collecting students' personal data or creating accounts for minors without permission.
Format: A table with the columns Purpose, Activity, Tool, Step by step, and Offline alternative; the AI guidelines as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
