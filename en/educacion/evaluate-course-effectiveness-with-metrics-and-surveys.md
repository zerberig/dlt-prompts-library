Context: I teach a course on [course topic] for [target audience]. I have this data available: [available data] (completion rate, grades, comments, sales).
Goal: Find out whether the course works, meaning whether learners were satisfied, learned, applied what they learned, and got results, and decide what to improve in the next run.
Deliverable: 1) A four-level evaluation framework (reaction, learning, application, results) with 2 metrics per level and how to collect them. 2) A 10-question end-of-course survey (scaled and open-ended). 3) A 30-day follow-up survey on applying what they learned. 4) A template for analyzing results and turning them into at most 5 prioritized improvements.
Criteria: Few metrics, but ones that drive decisions. Neutral, short questions that don't lead the answer, so people respond honestly.
Format: A framework table (Level, Metric, How to measure it, When); numbered surveys; an analysis template as a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
