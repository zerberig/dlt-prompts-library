Context: I teach [subject] to [grade or level] and want my students to learn to reflect on how they learn, not just on what they learned.
Goal: A bank of metacognitive reflection questions to use routinely in lessons, assignments, and tests.
Deliverable: 1) 5 questions for each stage: before a task (planning), during (monitoring), and after (evaluating). 2) 4 exit tickets with 2–3 questions each. 3) A post-test reflection for analyzing mistakes and planning how to study differently. 4) A template for students to set a learning goal and review their progress after 2 weeks.
Criteria: Specific questions that can't be answered with "yes" or "fine". Age-appropriate language. Include how to model answers the first few times, because without examples students reflect superficially.
Format: Sections by resource type, with questions numbered and ready to copy.
