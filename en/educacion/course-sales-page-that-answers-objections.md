Context: I'm selling my course [course name] on [course topic] to [target audience]. Real details: [course details] (length, format, modules, price, bonuses, guarantee, real student results or testimonials).
Goal: A clear, persuasive sales page that helps the right person decide to enroll, without pressure or misleading tactics.
Deliverable: Full page copy: headline and subheadline, the reader's problem, the transformation the course offers, benefits by module, who it's for and who it's not for, about the instructor, social proof, price and what's included, guarantee, answers to the 5 most likely objections as an FAQ, and calls to action. Plus 3 alternative headlines.
Criteria: Use only the details and testimonials I gave you; if social proof is missing, write "to be added" instead of inventing it. Concrete benefits, no income promises or guaranteed results. Sound like the audience talks, not like an ad. Short, scannable paragraphs, since most readers will be on a phone.
Format: Sections with headings in page order. 900–1,300 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
