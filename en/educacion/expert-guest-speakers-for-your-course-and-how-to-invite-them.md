Context: I'm building a course on [course topic] and want to add sessions with expert guests for these modules: [key modules or topics]. The format will be [session format] (for example, a 45-minute live interview).
Goal: Choose the right guests, invite them professionally, and get the most out of their session.
Deliverable: The ideal guest profile for each module (experience, perspective they bring, type of organization); where to find these people; if you have web search, 2 or 3 real candidates per profile with the source where you found their work; a short, customizable invitation message; and 8 interview questions.
Criteria: Don't invent contact details or attribute roles you can't confirm; flag anything uncertain as needing verification. Aim for a diversity of perspectives, backgrounds, and regions.
Format: A table with the columns Module, Profile, What they bring, Where to look, and Candidates; then the message and the questions.
