Context: I want to demonstrate [concept] to [grade or level] students with an experiment or hands-on activity. Available materials: [available materials].
Goal: Have students see the concept in action, make predictions, and explain what happens.
Deliverable: Experiment name, the question it investigates, materials with quantities, safety measures, numbered steps for students, what they should observe and why (the science explained for the teacher), prediction questions before and analysis questions after, a results data sheet, and a variation to extend the investigation.
Criteria: Safe for the stated age; clearly flag any step that needs adult supervision, heat, sharp objects, or irritating substances. Cheap, easy-to-find materials. Explain what unexpected results might come up and what they teach.
Format: Sections with headings; the data sheet as a table.
