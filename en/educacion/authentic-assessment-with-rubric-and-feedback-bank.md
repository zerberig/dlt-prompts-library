Context: I teach [subject or topic] to [grade or level] and want to assess whether my students can [learning to assess], not just whether they memorized.
Goal: An authentic assessment: a task similar to what people do outside the classroom, with feedback that tells students exactly how to improve.
Deliverable: 1) A performance task with a realistic context, product to deliver, and conditions. 2) An analytic rubric with 4 criteria and 4 levels, using observable descriptors. 3) A bank of feedback comments by criterion and level, each naming what the student achieved and the concrete next step. 4) A short activity where students use that feedback to submit an improved version.
Criteria: Descriptors distinguish levels by quality, not quantity. Language students can understand.
Format: Task in one block; rubric as a table; comment bank grouped by criterion.
