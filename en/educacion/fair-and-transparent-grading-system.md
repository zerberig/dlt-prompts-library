Context: I teach [subject] to [grade or level], and I want to redesign how I grade the term. Right now I assess with [current assessment tools]. My school requires [school requirements].
Goal: A system that reflects what students actually learned, that they understand from day one, and that gives them useful feedback to improve.
Deliverable: A weighting for each component with its rationale; an overall academic performance rubric with 4 levels; a separate record of work habits and behavior (punctuality, collaboration, submissions) with its own indicator; a policy for late work and second chances; and a one-page explanation for students and families.
Criteria: Keep academic learning separate from behavior, because mixing them distorts what the grade communicates. It must work with large classes and meet the school's requirements.
Format: A weighting table, the rubric as a table, and the family explainer at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
