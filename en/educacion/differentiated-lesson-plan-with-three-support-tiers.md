Context: I teach [topic] to [grade or level] in a [lesson length] session. The class includes [class needs] (for example, students with reading difficulties, English language learners, or students who have already mastered the topic).
Goal: A lesson plan where everyone works toward the same objective with different entry and exit points.
Deliverable: A single learning objective; an opening activity everyone can access; the core task in three tiers (supported, on-level, and challenge) with student-facing instructions for each tier; specific accommodations for each need I mentioned; suggested groupings; and an exit ticket with an adapted version.
Criteria: All three tiers reach the same essential learning, and the challenge tier goes deeper rather than just adding more problems. Group by in-the-moment need rather than fixed groups, so no one gets labeled. For formal accommodations, recommend validating them with the special education team or a professional.
Format: A timed, phase-by-phase plan and a table for the core task with the columns Tier, Instructions, Supports, and Expected product.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
