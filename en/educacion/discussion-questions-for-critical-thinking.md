Context: We're going to discuss [topic or reading] with [grade or level] in a [discussion length] session.
Goal: A discussion where students think critically, listen, build on each other's ideas, and form their own position.
Deliverable: 12 questions across three levels (comprehension and interpretation, analysis and connection, evaluation and personal stance), with one follow-up question per level to go deeper. Plus an opening question everyone can answer, a closing question, and 4 sentence starters for responding to classmates (for example, "I agree with… and I'd add…").
Criteria: Open-ended questions with no single right answer that require evidence from the topic or reading. At least one question connects to students' own lives.
Format: A numbered list grouped by level; at the end, a brief suggestion for how to run the discussion (circle, fishbowl, or pairs).
