Context: I have [your business or product], and I want to create a short, free course on [course topic] that's useful on its own and attracts potential customers.
Goal: Have the course build trust and a natural transition to what I sell, without feeling like a disguised ad.
Deliverable: 5 possible creative formats (email mini-course, 5-day challenge, live workshop, short video series, template with lessons) with pros and cons. Then develop the best one: lesson structure, when and how to present my offer, 3 ways to promote it through collaborations, communities, or affiliates, and indicators to measure whether it brings in customers.
Criteria: The content must solve a real problem even if the person never buys. The offer comes at the end and is presented transparently.
Format: A comparison table of formats, then the plan in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
