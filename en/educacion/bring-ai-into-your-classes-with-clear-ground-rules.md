Context: I teach [subject or topic] to [grade or level]. My students already use AI tools on their own, and at school I have access to [available tools].
Goal: Integrate AI and other technology so students learn more instead of outsourcing their thinking, and set clear rules of use.
Deliverable: 1) 5 activities for my subject where technology adds something you couldn't get the same way without it (for example, AI as a debate opponent, as a generator of examples to critique, or as a tutor that asks questions), each with objective, student instructions, and time. 2) A plain-language AI use policy for the class: what's allowed, what isn't, and how to disclose use. 3) 3 ideas for designing assignments that still measure real learning even with AI around.
Criteria: Every activity requires students to evaluate, correct, or justify, not just copy an answer. Account for minors' data privacy and for students without devices at home.
Format: Activities as a numbered list; policy in 10 bullets max; close with the assignment design ideas.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
