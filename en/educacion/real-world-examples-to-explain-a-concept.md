Context: I teach [concept or topic] to [audience or level], and I struggle to make them see why it's useful.
Deliverable: 8 real-world examples from different contexts (home, work, news, technology, sports, nature), each with 2 or 3 sentences explaining how it relates to the concept, a question to pose to the group, and a difficulty level. Then 2 common misconceptions the examples might create and how to clear them up.
Criteria: Accurate examples drawn from everyday life; if an example simplifies the concept, say so.
Format: A numbered list.
