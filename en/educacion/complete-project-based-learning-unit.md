Context: I want to design a [number of weeks]-week project-based learning unit on [topic] for [grade or level].
Goal: Have students learn the unit's content while creating a final product for a real audience.
Deliverable: The project's driving question; final product and audience; learning objectives and content covered; a weekly calendar with milestones, research activities, mini-lessons to teach what they need along the way, and check-in points; peer feedback strategies; a rubric for the final product; and a public showcase plan.
Criteria: Balance student autonomy with explicit teaching of key content. Every milestone produces evidence that lets me spot struggling teams early.
Format: A unit summary sheet, a calendar table with the columns Week, Milestone, Activities, and Evidence, and sections for the rest.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
