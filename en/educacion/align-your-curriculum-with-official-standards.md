Context: I teach [subject] in [grade or level] in [country or education system]. I need to align my plan with [official curriculum or standards]. Here's my current plan: [your plan or list of units].
Goal: Confirm my plan covers what the official curriculum requires, spot gaps or overlaps, and adjust before the school year starts.
Deliverable: 1) An alignment matrix: each learning outcome or standard mapped to the units or lessons where it's covered. 2) Outcomes not covered or covered only superficially. 3) Content in my plan that doesn't map to any outcome, with a keep, adjust, or cut recommendation. 4) Concrete adjustments in suggested order. 5) A one-page summary to present to my department or school leadership.
Criteria: If you have web search, check the current official text and cite the source; if you can't confirm it, work from what I give you and flag what I need to verify. Don't invent standard codes or wording.
Format: The matrix as a table (Outcome or standard, Where it's covered, Coverage level); the rest in short sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
