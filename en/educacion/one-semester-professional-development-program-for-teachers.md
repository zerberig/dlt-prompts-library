Context: I coordinate training for [teacher profile] at my school. I need a program on [training topic], and we have [available time] (for example, 2 hours every other week for one semester).
Goal: I want teachers not just to learn about the topic but to apply it in their classrooms, so we can see the change in practice.
Deliverable: Program objectives; a session calendar with the purpose, hands-on activities, and a classroom application task for each session; peer collaboration structures (peer observation, professional learning communities, or looking at student work together); recommended resources; and a plan to measure impact at the start, midpoint, and end.
Criteria: Ground the practices in educational research and say where each one comes from; mark as an estimate anything you can't back with a source. Favor active, applicable sessions over long lectures, because training that isn't practiced rarely reaches the classroom. Account for teachers' real workload.
Format: A calendar table with the columns Session, Purpose, Activities, Classroom application, and Evidence; then the remaining sections as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
