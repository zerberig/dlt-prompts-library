Goal: Understand who I'm selling to and where to focus my effort, instead of doing every channel halfway.
Deliverable: An ideal student profile (situation, problem they want to solve, objections to buying, where they look for information); 2 secondary segments; the 4 most promising organic or paid channels, ranked, with concrete tactics, a key message per segment, and a primary metric; and what to set aside for now.
Criteria: Justify each channel by how the audience actually behaves, not by trends. If you have web search, use it for platform usage data and ad costs in my country; mark as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source.
Format: The profile as bullets and the ranking in a table with the columns Channel, Segment, Tactic, Message, Suggested spend, and Metric.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
