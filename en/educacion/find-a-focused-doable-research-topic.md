Context: I'm a [academic level] student and need to write a research paper on [field or subject]. I'm especially interested in [your interests within the field], and I have [time available] to complete it.
Goal: Find a specific topic that's researchable in that time and has enough literature, so I can then write the paper myself.
Deliverable: 5 possible topics. For each: working title, why it matters today, 2–3 research questions, the kinds of sources or data I'd need, a possible methodological approach, and difficulty level. At the end, recommend the most viable one and explain why.
Criteria: Narrow topics, not broad ones. If you have web search, suggest 2–3 real authors, journals, or databases per topic; if not, name only source types, because a made-up reference would hurt me. Remind me to verify every source before citing it.
Format: One section per topic with bullets; final recommendation in one paragraph.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
