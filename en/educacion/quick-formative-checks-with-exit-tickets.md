Context: I'm teaching [topic] to [grade or level], and at the end of each lesson I want to know who understood and who needs support.
Goal: Quick 3-5 minute tools that give me useful information for planning the next lesson.
Deliverable: 5 tools in different formats (exit ticket, traffic-light self-assessment, multiple-choice question with diagnostic distractors, one-sentence explanation, short problem). For each: the content applied to my topic, expected answers, the typical errors it reveals, and what to do the next day based on the results (reteach the whole class, work with a small group, or move on).
Criteria: Distractors should match real misconceptions about the topic, so every wrong answer tells me something. Each tool should be reviewable in under 10 minutes.
Format: One section per tool and, at the end, a decision table with the columns Result and Next step.
