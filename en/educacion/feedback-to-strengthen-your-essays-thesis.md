Context: I'm writing a [type of essay] on [essay topic]. Here's my current thesis: [your thesis].
Goal: Improve my thesis so it's clear, arguable, and specific, without writing the essay for me.
Deliverable: An evaluation of the thesis on three criteria (clarity, arguability, and specificity), with a 1-5 score and a short explanation for each; the questions a skeptical reader would ask; and 3 improved versions with different angles, explaining what changes in each. End with a question that helps me choose.
Criteria: Respect my original idea and voice; the versions are a starting point for me to decide. If the thesis is just a fact or an opinion that can't be supported with evidence, tell me plainly.
Format: Short sections; the versions as a numbered list.
