Context: I teach a course on [topic] for [target audience], in a [delivery mode] format, lasting [duration]. I want to boost participation and consistency.
Goal: A gamification system that motivates learners to practice and progress without turning learning into a race for points.
Deliverable: 1) A storyline or theme that gives the course meaning (mission, expedition, season). 2) Mechanics: points for learning actions, levels, badges with their criteria, and weekly individual and team challenges. 3) How each mechanic ties to the learning objectives. 4) Simple tools to run it (a spreadsheet or the platform I already use). 5) Risks (demotivating learners who fall behind, excessive competition) and how to avoid them.
Criteria: Reward effort, improvement, and collaboration, not just results. Manageable in under 30 minutes a week.
Format: Numbered sections; a badge table with the columns Name, Criterion, and Learning it reinforces.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
