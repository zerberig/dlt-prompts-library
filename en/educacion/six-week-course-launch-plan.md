Context: I'm launching a course on [course topic] for [target audience]. My current channels are [channels and audience size], and I have [launch budget] for promotion.
Goal: A week-by-week plan to reach opening day with an engaged audience and close enrollment against a clear target.
Deliverable: An enrollment goal and the assumptions behind it; pre-launch, launch, and post-launch phases in a 6-week calendar; content ideas by channel; paid actions if they make sense; tasks by week; metrics to review weekly with thresholds for adjusting; and what to do after enrollment closes (feedback, real testimonials, next cohort).
Criteria: A plan one person or a small team can actually execute. Base the goal on my audience numbers and mark any conversion rates you use as estimates; if you have web search, draw on current benchmarks.
Format: A calendar table with the columns Week, Phase, Actions, Channel, Owner, and Metric; short sections for the rest.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
