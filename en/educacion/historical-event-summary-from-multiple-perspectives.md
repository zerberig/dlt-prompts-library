Context: I need to explain [historical event] to [audience] (high school students, college students, or a general audience).
Goal: A clear, rigorous summary that also shows how history is experienced differently depending on who tells it.
Deliverable: 1) An introduction on why the event matters. 2) Main causes and contributing factors. 3) How it unfolded, with the turning points in chronological order. 4) Key figures and groups. 5) The same event seen from 3 different perspectives (social groups, regions, or sides), with their motivations and experiences. 6) Short- and long-term consequences and its legacy today. 7) 3 discussion questions.
Criteria: Objective, engaging tone. Separate documented facts from interpretation, and note where historians disagree. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets, plus a short timeline table. 600–900 words.
