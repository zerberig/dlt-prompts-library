Context: My [grade or level] students will give presentations on [topic], [presentation length] minutes each, with visual aids.
Goal: Grade fairly and quickly while I listen, and let students know in advance what's expected.
Deliverable: An analytic rubric with 5 criteria (content and accuracy, organization, delivery, visuals and use of technology, handling questions) and 4 performance levels with observable descriptors; a short student-friendly version; a peer review sheet so the class can give feedback; and 3 model feedback phrases per criterion.
Criteria: Descriptors that name observable behaviors instead of vague adjectives like "good" or "excellent." A suggested weight for each criterion with its rationale.
Format: The rubric in a table with the columns Criterion, Weight, and the 4 levels; then the student version and the peer review sheet.
