Context: I'm [your role] (for example, an instructional designer or training coordinator), and I'm developing a course on [course topic] with subject matter experts who have little time. Project deadline: [deadline].
Goal: Capture the experts' knowledge efficiently and turn it into high-quality learning content without rework.
Deliverable: Roles and responsibilities in a simple RACI matrix; a kickoff meeting questionnaire; an interview guide to draw out tacit knowledge (cases, common mistakes, expert shortcuts); a staged production workflow with time-boxed review rounds; a template for experts to review content; and tips for resolving disagreements about how much to include.
Criteria: Respect the expert's time with short sessions and specific questions. Prioritize what learners need to do over everything the expert knows.
Format: Sections with headings; the workflow as a numbered list with the duration of each stage.
