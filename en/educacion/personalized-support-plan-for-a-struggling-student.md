Context: I have a [grade or level] student who is struggling with [topic or skill]. What I've observed: [what you've observed] (frequent mistakes, what they do master, how they respond to different activities, interests).
Goal: A 4-week support plan that helps them catch up with the class without feeling singled out.
Deliverable: 1) A hypothesis about where the gap is (which prerequisite is missing), with a short diagnostic activity to confirm it. 2) A 4-week path with measurable weekly goals, activities, resources, and estimated time. 3) Strategies I can use within regular class. 4) How to involve the family with a simple task. 5) Signs of progress and signs that a different approach is needed.
Criteria: Base everything on what I observed, without diagnosing any condition. If difficulties persist or I suspect something more, recommend referring the student to the school counselor, a learning specialist, or another professional. Short, realistic activities for a teacher managing a full class.
Format: Numbered sections; the path as a table with the columns Week, Goal, Activities, and How I know they progressed.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
