Context: I publish a newsletter about [newsletter topic] for [target audience], from [your brand name]. People subscribe through [how they subscribe] (for example, a free resource, the website, social media).
Goal: Get new subscribers opening my emails from the start, understanding what they'll receive, and trusting the brand before they see any offer.
Deliverable: 4 emails: 1) an immediate welcome with what was promised and what to expect; 2) my best content or the brand story; 3) a question to learn their interests and invite them to hit reply; 4) recommendations based on their interest and a soft invitation to the next step. For each one: send day, subject line, preheader, and body.
Criteria: The first email delivers what was promised at signup right away. Asking for a reply in email 3 helps deliverability and helps me get to know the audience. Warm, person-to-person tone. No hard selling in this series.
Format: Each email in its own block with a heading; bodies of 100-180 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
