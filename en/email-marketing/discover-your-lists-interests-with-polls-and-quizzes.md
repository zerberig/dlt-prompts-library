Context: I run an email list for a [type of business], and my subscribers care about different aspects of [main topic]. Right now everyone gets the same email.
Goal: I want subscribers to tell me what they're interested in, so I can segment the list with data they chose to share and send them more relevant content.
Deliverable: 1) 3 ways to collect interests inside the email (for example, a one-click poll, a short quiz, a preference center), each with the exact email copy and answer options. 2) The 4-6 interest segments that would result and the tag you'd assign to each answer. 3) One campaign idea per segment.
Criteria: Each mechanism should take under 30 seconds and give something back (tailored content, a resource, fewer irrelevant emails), because that's what gets more people to respond. Ask only for data I'll actually use. Not every email client renders interactive elements, so every idea must also work with plain links.
Format: Numbered sections; the segments in a table with the columns Segment, Triggering answer, Tag, and Suggested campaign.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
