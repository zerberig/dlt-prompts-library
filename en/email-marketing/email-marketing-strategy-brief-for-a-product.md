Context: I need an email marketing strategy for [your product], aimed at [target audience]. The current situation is: [current situation] (list size, recent results, team resources). The available budget is [budget in your currency].
Goal: Have a document I can present to my team or a client to get the strategy approved and start executing.
Deliverable: 1) Measurable, time-bound goals. 2) Audience and 3-5 segments with each one's main need. 3) Content pillars and email types for each segment. 4) Priority automations and campaigns. 5) Required resources and budget allocation. 6) KPIs with targets and review frequency. 7) Risks and how to mitigate them.
Criteria: Every recommendation ties back to a goal. Realistic targets for my situation. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Prioritize what a small team can sustain over time.
Format: A document with numbered headings and tables for segments and KPIs, 600-900 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
