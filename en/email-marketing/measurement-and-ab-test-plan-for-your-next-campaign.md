Context: I'm about to send a campaign to [campaign goal] to a list of roughly [list size] contacts, using [email platform].
Goal: I want to know clearly whether the campaign worked and learn something useful for the next one, instead of staring at loose numbers after the send.
Deliverable: 1) The primary metric and 2-3 supporting metrics, with the formula for each and why they matter for this goal. 2) One A/B test (subject line, content, or call to action) with a hypothesis, both variants, a suggested sample size, duration, and the rule for declaring a winner. 3) What I do with the result in each scenario: A wins, B wins, or no clear difference.
Criteria: Privacy features like Apple Mail's inflate open rates, so don't use opens as the primary metric if the goal is clicks or sales. Test one variable at a time so I know what caused the change. If the list is too small for a reliable test, tell me and suggest an alternative. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A metrics table with the columns Metric, Formula, Why it matters, and Suggested target; the test plan as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
