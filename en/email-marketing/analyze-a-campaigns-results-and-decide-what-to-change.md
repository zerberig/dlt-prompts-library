Context: Here are the results of my email campaign about [campaign topic], sent to [segment or list]: [campaign data] (sends, opens, clicks, conversions, unsubscribes, complaints, revenue and, if I have them, results by segment, device, or link).
Goal: Understand what worked, what didn't, and why, so I can improve the next campaign with concrete decisions.
Deliverable: 1) A 3-4 sentence summary. 2) A diagnosis by stage: delivery, open, click, conversion, and list health. 3) The top 5 improvement actions, prioritized by impact and effort. 4) One concrete test to validate the most important action.
Criteria: Separate what the data shows from what's a hypothesis. Keep in mind open rates may be inflated by privacy features in some email clients. If you compare against industry averages, use web search if you have it and cite the source. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Sections with headings; the actions in a table with the columns Action, Problem it addresses, Impact, and Effort.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
