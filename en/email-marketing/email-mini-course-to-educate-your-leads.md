Context: My prospects need to understand [topic to teach] before buying [your product or service]. My audience is [target audience], and their current knowledge of the topic is [knowledge level].
Goal: Create an email mini-course that works as a subscriber magnet and, by the end, leaves leads ready to consider my offer.
Deliverable: 1) Course name and its promise in one sentence. 2) 5 lessons, one per day, each with subject line, learning goal, main content of 150-250 words, and a quick exercise or action. 3) A wrap-up email that recaps what they learned and presents the offer as the natural next step.
Criteria: Each lesson teaches a single idea they can apply that same day. Clear language with examples from my audience's life. The offer appears only in the wrap-up email, so the course feels valuable on its own.
Format: First the course outline, then each lesson in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
