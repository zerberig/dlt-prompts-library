Context: I sell [type of products or services] to [target audience] and want to improve how I write my sales emails.
Goal: Understand what makes an email convert and apply it to the next ones I write, without relying on templates.
Deliverable: 8 email copywriting principles (for example, one idea per email, benefit before feature, specificity, handling objections, social proof, a clear call to action, reading on a phone, the P.S.). For each one: a 2-3 sentence explanation, a before-and-after example applied to my niche, and a common mistake. Close with an 8-point checklist for reviewing any email before sending it.
Criteria: Realistic examples for my type of product, not generic ones. Honest persuasion techniques: no fake urgency and no promises the product can't keep.
Format: A numbered list with subheadings; the before and after on separate lines.
