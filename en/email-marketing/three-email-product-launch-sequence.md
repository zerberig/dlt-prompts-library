Context: I'm launching [your product] on [launch date] for [target audience]. The main problem it solves is [problem it solves].
Goal: Build anticipation before launch, concentrate sales on launch day, and win back people who didn't open or didn't buy.
Deliverable: 3 emails: 1) a teaser 3-5 days before that names the problem, hints at the solution, and invites people to an early-access list; 2) the launch-day announcement; 3) a reminder 2-3 days later, only for people who didn't buy. For each one: 2 subject line options, preheader, body, and call to action.
Criteria: Each email does one job and has one main call to action. Lead with the outcome the customer gets before the features. Use exclusivity or urgency only if it's real (early access, limited spots, or a dated launch price), because invented urgency wears down your list's trust.
Format: Each email in its own block, with its send timing and target segment. Bodies of 80-150 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
