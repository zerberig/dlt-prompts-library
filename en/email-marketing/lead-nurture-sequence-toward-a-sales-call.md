Context: I sell [your service or solution] to [ideal customer profile]. Leads enter the sequence when [how leads come in] (they download a resource, request information, attend a webinar). My sales cycle usually lasts [sales cycle length].
Goal: Help leads understand their problem, trust my approach, and book a call when they're ready, without feeling chased.
Deliverable: 5 emails, each with send day, goal, subject line, body, and call to action: the problem, a common mistake, a case or example, the main objection, and a direct invitation to the call. Add the exit rules (books, replies, stops opening) and what to do with leads who finish the sequence without booking.
Criteria: Each email is useful on its own, even if the lead doesn't move forward. Use only real cases and numbers I give you; if I don't have them, leave the spot marked "to be completed". Low-commitment calls to action early, more direct ones at the end.
Format: A summary table of the sequence with the columns Day, Goal, Subject line, and Call to action; then each full email. Bodies of 90-160 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
