Context: My product [your product] has new features: [new features]. They're available on [higher plan], and I want to announce them to users on [current plan].
Goal: Help users see how the new features save them time or get them better results, and get a share of them to upgrade.
Deliverable: 1) One email with 2 subject line options, preheader, body, and call to action. 2) An FAQ block with the 3 most likely objections (price, switching, complexity) and their answers. 3) A follow-up email for people who clicked but didn't upgrade.
Criteria: Show each feature through a concrete use case, not a technical list. Be transparent about price and terms. Acknowledge the value of what they already have on their current plan, without making them feel they're missing out.
Format: Main email of 120-200 words, the FAQ as bullets, and a follow-up of 50-90 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
