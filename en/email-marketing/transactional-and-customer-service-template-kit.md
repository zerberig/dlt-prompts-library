Context: I run a [type of business], and my team answers customer emails about [main products or services]. My brand's tone is [brand tone].
Goal: Make every operational and support email sound the same, resolve issues fast, and cut down on back-and-forth.
Deliverable: 6 templates: order or appointment confirmation, shipping or next-step notice, reply to a common question, reply to a complaint, request for missing information, and follow-up after resolving a case. For each one: subject line, body, and the fields the team fills in, written as {{customer_name}} or {{order_number}}.
Criteria: The information the customer needs comes first, everything else after. Short sentences, one clear next step, and visible contact details. In the complaint reply, acknowledge the problem without blaming the customer or promising what the team can't deliver. Transactional emails carry no pushy promotions, because their job is to inform.
Format: Each template in its own titled block; bodies of 50-120 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
