Context: I'm launching [your new product or service] and want my existing customers to see it before anyone else. I'll offer them [exclusive perk] (special price, early access, a gift) until [deadline].
Goal: Make my customers feel valued, buy first, and become the launch's first reviews and referrals.
Deliverable: One email with 3 subject line options, preheader, body, and call to action, plus a short reminder for people who didn't open it.
Criteria: Thank them for the relationship before selling. Connect the new release to what they already bought or use, so it feels like a natural next step. Explain the perk's terms clearly. If you invite a review after purchase, keep it an open invitation, with no incentives in exchange for positive ratings.
Format: Main email of 120-180 words and a reminder of 40-70 words, in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
