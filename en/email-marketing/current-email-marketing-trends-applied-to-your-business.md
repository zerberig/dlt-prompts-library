Goal: Know which email marketing trends are worth adopting now and which are noise for a business like mine.
Deliverable: 6-8 current trends (for example, AI for personalization, metric changes driven by privacy, new mailbox provider requirements, interactive content, integration with messaging apps). For each one: what it is, the evidence or source, its relevance to my business (high, medium, or low), and one concrete first action.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and cite recent sources. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Separate trends backed by evidence from those that are just hype, and take my size and resources into account.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, What it is, Source, Relevance, and First action; close with the 2 priorities you recommend.
