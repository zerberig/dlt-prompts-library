Context: I'm running a promotion on [your product or offer] with [offer details] (discount, gift, free shipping), valid from [start date] to [end date], for [target audience].
Goal: Concentrate sales within the offer window, using the real deadline as the reason to act.
Deliverable: 4 emails: 1) an early heads-up to the list; 2) the offer opening; 3) a mid-period reminder with social proof or answers to common questions; 4) the final hours. For each one: send timing, subject line, preheader, body, and call to action. Say which emails should exclude people who already bought.
Criteria: Urgency comes from the real date and terms; don't invent stock levels or fake countdowns, because besides hurting trust it can break consumer protection laws. Restate the offer terms clearly in every email. Each email brings a new angle, not just the days remaining.
Format: Each email in its own block; bodies of 60-130 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
