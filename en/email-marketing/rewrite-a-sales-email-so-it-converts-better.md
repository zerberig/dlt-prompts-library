Context: Here's a draft email selling [your product] to [target audience]: [your draft]. The action I want readers to take is [desired action].
Goal: Improve the email so more readers take that action, while keeping my voice and what already works.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis of the 3-5 things holding back conversions most (hook, offer clarity, benefits, objections, proof, call to action). 2) The rewritten version. 3) 2 new subject lines. 4) A list of the main changes and the reason for each.
Criteria: Keep the original's facts and claims; if something needs proof I don't have, flag it instead of inventing it. One main call to action, repeated twice at most. Same length as the original or shorter.
Format: Numbered sections, with the rewritten version in its own block.
