Context: I want to promote [your product] during [season or key date] (for example, the holidays, Black Friday, Mother's Day, or back to school) to [target audience].
Goal: Stand out in a crowded inbox with original campaigns that connect with the season and sell.
Deliverable: 5 distinct campaign concepts. For each one: concept name, core idea in one sentence, the story behind it, the mechanic (gift guide, challenge, advent-style surprise calendar, interactive content, collaboration), how many emails and when to send them, 2 sample subject lines, and how to measure success.
Criteria: A small team must be able to run each concept. Real variety between them: not 5 discounts with different names. Offers and urgency only with real terms.
Format: Each concept in its own block with a subheading; at the end, the concept you'd recommend as most feasible and why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
