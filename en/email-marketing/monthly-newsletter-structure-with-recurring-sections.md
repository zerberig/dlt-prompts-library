Context: I send a monthly newsletter from [your brand name] to [target audience]. I want it to combine product news, updates from [your industry], customer testimonials, and a promotion. This is this month's material: [this month's news].
Goal: Have a format my readers recognize every month, that's easy for my team to produce and balances value with selling.
Deliverable: 1) A structure of 4-6 recurring sections, with name, goal, length, and order. 2) This month's issue written in that format, with subject line and preheader. 3) A checklist for producing each issue.
Criteria: The value section comes before the promotion. Testimonials must be real and published with permission; if I don't have one this month, leave the spot marked "to be completed". Verify industry news with web search if you have it and cite the source.
Format: The structure as a table with the columns Section, Goal, and Length; the full issue in its own block; the checklist as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
