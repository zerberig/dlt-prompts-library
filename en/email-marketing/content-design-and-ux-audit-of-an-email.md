Review this email the way a busy subscriber would, opening it on their phone between two meetings.
Context: I regularly send this email to [target audience] with the goal of [email goal]: [email content or code]. If I have them, these are its results: [email metrics].
Goal: Find what's costing it clarity, interest, or clicks, and fix it.
Deliverable: An audit by area: subject line and preheader, first screen, structure and readability, message and value proposition, calls to action, design and mobile version, accessibility (contrast, alt text, font size), and elements that could affect deliverability. For each area: a 1-5 score, the problem found, and a concrete fix. Close with the 3 highest-impact fixes.
Criteria: Base every observation on what's actually in the email. Propose specific changes (rewritten copy, new button placement), not general advice.
Format: A table with the columns Area, Score, Problem, and Fix; the top 3 fixes as a numbered list.
