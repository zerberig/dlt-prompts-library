Context: I'm sending a campaign to promote [your product] to my customers. For each contact I have this data: [available data] (age, city, interests, purchase history, stated preferences).
Goal: Give each segment the version most relevant to them instead of a generic email, to lift clicks and sales.
Deliverable: 1) 3-5 segments I can build with my data, with the exact criteria for each. 2) For each segment: personalized subject line, message angle, benefit to highlight, suggested image or product, and call to action. 3) A base version for anyone who doesn't fit a segment. 4) How to compare results across segments.
Criteria: Personalize from real data, not stereotypes: favor stated interests and past purchases over assumptions based on age or gender. Don't use data customers don't know they shared, because it feels invasive.
Format: A table with the columns Segment, Criteria, Subject line, Angle, Benefit, and Call to action; the base version and measurement as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
