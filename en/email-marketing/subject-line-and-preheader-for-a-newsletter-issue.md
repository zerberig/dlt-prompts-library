Context: This issue of my newsletter about [newsletter topic] includes: [issue contents]. My readers are [target audience].
Goal: Pick a subject line and preheader that work together and make my readers want to open this specific issue.
Deliverable: 8 subject line and preheader pairings with different angles (the strongest piece, curiosity, usefulness, a number, a question, a personal tone). Mark your favorite and why.
Criteria: Subject lines of 50 characters max and preheaders of 40-90 characters that complement the subject line instead of repeating it. Both reflect what the issue actually contains. If my newsletter has a recognizable section name or format, use it.
Format: A table with the columns Subject line, Preheader, Angle, and Characters.
