Context: I want to feature in my newsletter the story of [customer or type of customer], who used [your product or service] to solve [the problem they had]. These are the data points and quotes I have: [real results and testimonials].
Goal: Show the value of what I offer through a verifiable case my readers can see themselves in, without it reading like an ad.
Deliverable: An article with a headline and 2 alternatives, an intro with the customer's challenge, the solution and how they applied it, the results, one practical lesson any reader can use, and a soft call to action at the end.
Criteria: Use only the data and quotes I gave you, without inventing or rounding numbers; if something is missing, mark it "to be completed". Tell it as a story (before, during, and after) with concrete details. Add a closing note reminding me to confirm the customer approved publishing their name and results.
Format: Short subheadings and short paragraphs, 400-600 words, with the main quote highlighted in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
