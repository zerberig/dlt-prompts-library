Context: I'm sending a campaign about [campaign topic] to [target audience]. The core message is: [main message].
Goal: Have plenty of genuinely different subject line options to choose from and test, not variations on one idea.
Deliverable: 20 subject lines grouped into 5 styles (direct benefit, curiosity, question, number or data point, personalization), 4 per style. Then the 3 you'd test first, with the reason for each.
Criteria: 50 characters max per subject line so it shows in full on a phone. Every subject line delivers on what the email contains, because misleading subject lines drive complaints and hurt deliverability. Use emojis in only 2-3 options, as a test. Avoid all-caps words and repeated punctuation.
Format: One table per style with the columns Subject line and Characters; the recommendation as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
