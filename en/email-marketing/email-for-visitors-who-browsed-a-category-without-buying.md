Context: I run an online store selling [type of products]. My platform detects when a subscriber views products in a category but doesn't add anything to the cart. The category for this email is [product category].
Goal: Help that shopper decide with useful recommendations, without making them feel watched.
Deliverable: One email with 3 subject line options, preheader, body, a block of 3-4 recommended products (say which rule to pick them by: best sellers, top rated, or new arrivals), and a call to action. Plus a variant for people who viewed the category more than once.
Criteria: An advisor's tone: "you might like these" rather than "we saw you were here". Include one doubt-reducing element (size guide, comparison, shipping and returns). No discount in this email, so customers don't learn to wait for offers.
Format: Email and variant in separate blocks; bodies of 60-120 words plus the product block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
