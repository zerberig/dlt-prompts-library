Context: I run a [type of business] and email [target audience] regularly. My most common email types are: [email types] (for example, newsletter, promotions, launches, reminders).
Goal: I want a resource my team can check every time they write a subject line, so they don't start from scratch and they try different approaches.
Deliverable: 12 subject line formulas. For each one: the structure with the blanks in quotes (for example, "How to ___ without ___"), 2 examples applied to my business, the email type it works best for, and one risk to avoid.
Criteria: No formula guarantees opens; present each one as a hypothesis to test with my own list. Include variety: curiosity, benefit, real urgency, social proof, question, list, and personalization. Keep examples under 50 characters.
Format: A table with the columns Formula, Example 1, Example 2, Best for, and Risk.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
