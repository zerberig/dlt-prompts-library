Context: I offer [your service] to [ideal customer profile]. The main problems I solve for them are: [customer pain points]. What sets me apart is [differentiator].
Goal: Write emails a busy professional reads to the end because they recognize their problem, and that lead them to reply or book a conversation.
Deliverable: 3 versions of the email, each centered on a different problem. For each one: subject line, opening line, body following a problem, consequence, and solution structure, one credibility element, and a call to action.
Criteria: Use the customer's language, not my industry's jargon. Include numbers or cases only if I give them to you. Low-commitment call to action (a question, a short reply, or a 15-minute conversation). If the email goes to contacts who didn't subscribe, include a clear way to stop receiving messages and follow my country's commercial email laws.
Format: Each version in its own block, with a body of 120 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
