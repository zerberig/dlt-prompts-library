Context: I want to introduce [your new product or service] to [target audience]. Its 3 main benefits are: [main benefits]. Most of my subscribers open email on their phones.
Goal: Get readers to understand what's new and click within 10 seconds of reading.
Deliverable: 1) 5 subject lines of 40 characters max and 3 preheaders. 2) The full email: headline, 1-2 opening sentences, the 3 benefits in skimmable blocks, a button with action-oriented text, and a P.S. 3) Mobile formatting guidance: button placement, font size, paragraph length, and hero image.
Criteria: Paragraphs of 2 lines max on a phone screen. The call to action visible without scrolling and repeated at the end. Each benefit framed as an outcome for the reader.
Format: Subject lines and preheaders as a list; the email in its own block of 80-130 words; the guidance as bullets.
