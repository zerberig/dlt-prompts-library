Context: I want to introduce [your product] to [recipient's name and role] at [recipient's company]. Here's what I know about their situation: [customer context] (challenges, past purchases, previous conversations).
Goal: Make the email feel written for this person and lead them to a concrete next step, like booking a call or trying the product.
Deliverable: One email with 2 subject line options, body, and call to action, plus a short follow-up version to send if they don't reply within a week.
Criteria: Pick only the 2-3 features that address their challenges and frame each as a benefit for their case. Open with something about their situation, not about my company. One easy-to-accept call to action. Professional, friendly tone with no pressure.
Format: Main email of 100-160 words and a follow-up of 50-80 words, each in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
