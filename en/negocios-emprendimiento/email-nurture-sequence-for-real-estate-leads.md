Context: I'm a [your real estate role] in [your area]. Leads join my list through [how leads come in] and are usually [lead type] (first-time buyers, investors, people looking to sell, etc.).
Goal: I want an automated sequence that guides each lead from the moment they share their details until they're ready to book a viewing or meeting, adding value in every email.
Deliverable: A 7-email sequence over 6 weeks, organized by stage (welcome, education, trust, decision). For each email: send day, goal, 2 subject line options, full copy of 120 to 200 words, and a call to action. Also include: which tag or signal moves a lead into a more advanced branch (for example, clicking on listings) and a re-engagement email for anyone who hasn't opened in 30 days.
Criteria: Personalize with fields like name and area of interest. Every email should offer something useful (a guide, a local market insight, a checklist, a real client story I provide) rather than just asking for the meeting. Include a clear unsubscribe option in every email. If you mention market data, ask me to verify it or flag it as an estimate.
Format: One block per email with Day, Goal, Subject lines, Copy, and Call to action; then the branching rules as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
