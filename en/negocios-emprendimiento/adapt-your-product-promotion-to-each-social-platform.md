Context: I want to promote [your product] to [target audience]. What sets it apart is [differentiator]. I'm active on [social platforms you use].
Goal: I want a post built for each platform, not the same caption pasted everywhere, because each platform rewards different formats and tones.
Deliverable: For each platform I use, one post idea with: format (carousel, short video, text post, story, etc.), the hook for the first line or first 3 seconds, full ready-to-post copy, a short description of the visual, and a call to action.
Criteria: Every idea should show the product in use or solve a real problem for the audience, not just announce it. Match length and tone to the platform (more professional on LinkedIn, punchier on X, for example). If you give advice about formats or algorithms, present it as best practice, not guaranteed fact.
Format: One section per platform with a heading and the elements as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
