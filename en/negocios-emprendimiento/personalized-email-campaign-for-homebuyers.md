Context: I'm a real estate agent in [city or area]. I have a list of prospects who opted in to receive information, grouped into these profiles: [buyer profiles and their criteria] (for example, first-time buyers on a mid-range budget, a family looking for a house with a yard, or an investor). My available properties are: [available properties and their features].
Goal: An email campaign personalized by profile that shows each buyer properties they'll actually care about and gets them to book a showing.
Deliverable: For each profile: 1) A 3-email sequence: a property selection, useful content about the area or the buying process, and an invitation to a showing. 2) For each email: a subject line with 2 variants, preview text, body, and call to action. 3) Which properties from my list to include and why they fit that profile.
Criteria: Describe properties with concrete benefits for that profile (commutes, schools, space) without exaggerating or promising appreciation. Include an unsubscribe option. Warm, professional tone; emails of 120 to 180 words.
Format: One block per profile, with each email separated inside it.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
