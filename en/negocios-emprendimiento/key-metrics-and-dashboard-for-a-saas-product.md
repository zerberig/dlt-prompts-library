Context: I have [your SaaS product] with a [business model] model (self-serve, sales-led, or freemium), at the [stage] stage. This quarter's goal is [quarterly goal].
Goal: I want to know what to measure, how to calculate it, and what to look at each week to make decisions, without drowning in data.
Deliverable: 1) One north star metric and why it reflects the value customers get. 2) 8 to 12 KPIs grouped into acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, and efficiency, each with its formula, data source, and a reference range for my stage. 3) A one-page dashboard design: what goes at the top, which charts, and which alerts. 4) The review cadence (weekly, monthly, quarterly) and who reviews what.
Criteria: Pick metrics that lead to action; drop vanity metrics. If you have web search, use recent benchmarks for the ranges and cite the source. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns KPI, Formula, Source, Reference range, and What to do if it drifts; then the dashboard and cadence as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
