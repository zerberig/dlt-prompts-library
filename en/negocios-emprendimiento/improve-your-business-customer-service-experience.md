Context: I run [your business]. Customers contact me through [support channels], and the most common complaints or friction points are [most common complaints].
Goal: I want customers to feel well looked after at every contact, so they come back, refer others, and the same complaints stop coming in.
Deliverable: 1) A customer journey map showing where friction happens today and how to fix each point. 2) Concrete service standards: response times per channel, tone, and what the team can resolve without asking for approval. 3) Template replies for the 5 most frequent situations, including a difficult complaint. 4) A simple system to request and log customer feedback and turn it into improvements every month. 5) 3 low-cost touches that exceed customer expectations.
Criteria: Everything should be doable by a small team. Template replies should sound human and adaptable, not robotic.
Format: The map as a table with Stage, Current friction, and Improvement; the rest as sections with bullets, and template replies in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
