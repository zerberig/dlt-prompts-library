Context: I run [your business], which offers [what you sell] in [market or city]. Current situation: [key facts about your business] (sales, team, customers, and recent market changes).
Goal: A SWOT analysis that doesn't stop at a list but helps me decide what to do over the next 12 months.
Deliverable: 1) A SWOT matrix with 4-6 points per quadrant, all specific to my business. 2) Cross strategies: SO (use strengths to seize opportunities), WO, ST, and WT, with 2 strategies per pairing. 3) The 3 recommended priorities, each with its first action.
Criteria: Strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats are external; keep that separation. If you have web search, ground opportunities and threats in current trends in my industry and cite the source. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The SWOT as a 2x2 table, cross strategies as a table, and priorities as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
