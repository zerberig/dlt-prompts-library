Context: I'm a real estate agent in [your area] and I specialize in [client or property type]. My leads mostly come from [lead source].
Goal: I want to turn a first contact into a trusting relationship: understand what the person is looking for, give them value, and book a meeting without sounding pushy.
Deliverable: 1) A first-call script: opening, 5 questions to understand needs, budget, and timing, a two-sentence value proposition, answers to 3 common objections, and a close that books a specific meeting. 2) 4 WhatsApp messages for the next 14 days, each offering something useful (a matching listing, a neighborhood insight, a short guide) rather than just "still interested?".
Criteria: Natural, warm language that's easy to say out loud. Respect the prospect's choice if they don't want to stay in touch and offer an easy way to pause messages.
Format: The script with headings for each stage and sample lines; the messages numbered with the day each one is sent. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
