Context: I run [your business]. My current situation is [current situation] (sales, customers, team, main challenges), and what I most want to achieve next year is [top priority].
Goal: I want a clear set of objectives to align my team and decide where to put time and money.
Deliverable: 10 business objectives spread across sales, customers, operations, team, and finance. For each one: the objective written in SMART format, why it matters for my top priority, the metric I'll track it with, and a first action to get started.
Criteria: Targets should be ambitious but reachable from where I am today; if you propose a number, explain where it comes from. Rank them by impact and point out which ones depend on others.
Format: A table with the columns Area, SMART objective, Why it matters, Metric, and First action. Close with a short paragraph on the 3 objectives I should tackle first.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
