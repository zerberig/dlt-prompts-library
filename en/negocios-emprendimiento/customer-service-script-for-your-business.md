Context: I run [your business] and we handle customers through [support channels]. The most frequent questions or complaints are: [most common questions or complaints]. Our return or warranty policy is: [return or warranty policy].
Goal: I want anyone on the team to deliver the same level of service, solve things fast, and leave customers wanting to come back.
Deliverable: 1) A short tone guide with examples of how to say things and how not to. 2) A greeting and closing for each channel. 3) A script for each frequent question, with variations depending on the customer's response. 4) A protocol for upset customers: listen, acknowledge, resolve, and follow up. 5) When and how to escalate a case. 6) Ten phrases to avoid with an alternative for each.
Criteria: The scripts should sound human and leave room to personalize, not read like a robot. Never promise anything the policy doesn't allow, because a broken promise creates a second complaint.
Format: Numbered sections; the scripts as short dialogues and the phrases to avoid as a two-column table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
