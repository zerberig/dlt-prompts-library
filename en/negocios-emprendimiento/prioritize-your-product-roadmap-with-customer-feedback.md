Context: I run [your product] for [ideal customer]. My business goal for the next 6 months is [six-month goal]. Here's the customer feedback I've collected: [customer feedback] (requests, complaints, support comments, surveys, or interviews).
Goal: I want to decide what to build first using clear criteria, rather than just serving whoever asks the loudest.
Deliverable: 1) Group the feedback into underlying themes or needs (not the literal features requested) and show how many mentions each theme has and which type of customer is asking. 2) For each theme, one or two possible solutions. 3) A RICE score (reach, impact, confidence, effort) with the reasoning behind each value. 4) A three-column roadmap: Now, Next, and Later. 5) What to tell customers whose requests won't make it in for now.
Criteria: Align the prioritization with my six-month goal. Point out where the feedback is thin and what to validate before building. Effort is an estimate my team needs to confirm.
Format: A themes table with Theme, Mentions, Customer type, and Possible solution; a RICE table sorted by score; the roadmap as a three-column table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
