Context: Here is the transcript or slides of [document type] from [company name]: [transcript or text]. I'm mainly interested in [your focus] (financial performance, strategy, competition, industry outlook).
Goal: I want to grasp what matters in the presentation in 5 minutes without reading all of it, to use in my analysis or a conversation with my team.
Deliverable: 1) A 5-line summary. 2) Key reported figures and how they compare with the prior period, if the text includes it. 3) Guidance or outlook given by management. 4) Strategic announcements (products, investments, acquisitions, leadership changes). 5) Risks and red flags, including evasive answers in the Q&A. 6) 3 open questions worth researching.
Criteria: Use only what the document says and quote the line when tone matters; don't fill in figures that aren't there. Separate what the company claims from your interpretation. This is an informational summary, not investment advice; if I use it to invest, remind me to consult a financial advisor.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets; figures in a table with Metric, Value, Prior period, and Comment.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
