Context: I run [your business], at [business size or approximate sales], and I manage my finances with [how you handle your finances] (outside accountant, spreadsheet, or software).
Goal: A financial routine I can follow without being an expert, to keep my numbers in order and catch problems early.
Deliverable: A financial management checklist split into weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual tasks. Each task with: what to do, why it matters, how long it takes, and whether I or my accountant handles it. Include bank reconciliation, collections, separating personal and business finances, setting aside money for taxes, reviewing margins, and an emergency fund. End with 5 financial warning signs.
Criteria: Plain language and concrete tasks. Tax obligations vary by country, so mention them in general terms and recommend I confirm dates and requirements with an accountant.
Format: Checkbox lists grouped by frequency, ready to print.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
