Context: I offer [your product or service] and want to reach out to [type of prospect]. The main problem I solve for them is [problem you solve], and here is my proof of results: [real proof or case].
Goal: I want a first-contact email that can be read in under 30 seconds and earns a reply or a short call, not an immediate sale.
Deliverable: 3 versions of the email with different angles (the problem, a concrete result, a thought-provoking question). Each with 2 subject line options, the body, and a low-commitment call to action. Add a short follow-up to send 4 days later if there's no reply.
Criteria: 120 words max per email. Talk about the recipient more than about me, and leave a placeholder marked "to be completed" for a personalized line about their company. Use only the proof I gave you, without inventing numbers or clients. Include an easy way to opt out of further emails.
Format: Each version in its own block with Subject and Body; the follow-up at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
