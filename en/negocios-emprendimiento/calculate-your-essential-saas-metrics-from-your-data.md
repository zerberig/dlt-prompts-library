Context: I run [your SaaS business]. Here is my data for the last period: [your period data] (customers at start and end, new customers, cancellations, recurring revenue, expansions, sales and marketing spend, gross margin).
Goal: I want to understand the real health of my business through the 5 essential SaaS metrics and know which one to work on first.
Deliverable: For MRR, churn rate (customer and revenue), net revenue retention (NRR), CAC, LTV, and CAC payback period: a one-sentence definition, the formula, a step-by-step calculation with my data, and what the result means for my business. At the end, the LTV:CAC ratio and a short diagnosis.
Criteria: Show the math so I can check it. If a data point is missing, tell me which one instead of making it up. If you compare against industry benchmarks, note that they vary by segment and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with Metric, Formula, My result, and Interpretation; then the detailed calculations and a paragraph on the metric I should improve first and why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
