Context: I run [your SaaS product] for [ideal customer]. The main value they get is [core value], and right now many users drop off at [where they drop off].
Goal: I want every new customer to reach their first meaningful result as fast as possible, because that decides whether they stay or cancel.
Deliverable: 1) The activation event you suggest I track and why. 2) A map of the first 30 days by moment (signup, first session, first week, day 14, day 30) with the goal of each moment, the touchpoint (in-app, email, call, help content), and the key message. 3) In-product changes to reduce friction (checklist, templates, sample data, empty states). 4) What to do when a user gets stuck: signals and actions. 5) Metrics to evaluate onboarding.
Criteria: Separate self-serve customers from accounts that deserve hands-on help. Every touchpoint should push toward the next concrete action, not just say welcome.
Format: The map as a table with Moment, Goal, Channel, Key message, and Metric; the rest as sections with bullets. 400 to 700 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
