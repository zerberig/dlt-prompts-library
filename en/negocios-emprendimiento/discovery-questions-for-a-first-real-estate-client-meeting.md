Context: I'm a real estate agent with a first meeting coming up with a client who wants to [buy, sell, or rent] [property type and area].
Goal: Fully understand their needs, situation, and priorities so I don't waste time on properties that don't fit, and build trust from the start.
Deliverable: 20 to 25 questions grouped into: motivation and timeline; budget and payment method; must-haves and nice-to-haves; location and lifestyle; and decision process (who else decides). For each group, a short note on what to listen for in the answers. At the end, 5 questions the client should ask me to judge whether I'm the right agent.
Criteria: Open, conversational, respectful questions; money questions phrased so they don't feel awkward. Avoid questions that could be discriminatory (origin, religion, marital status), because beyond being unethical they may be illegal.
Format: Numbered lists by group, ready to print or use in the meeting.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
