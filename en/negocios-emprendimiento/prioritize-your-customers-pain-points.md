Context: I run [your product or service] and serve [type of customer]. What I know today about their problems is: [what customers or your team have told you].
Goal: I want to know which customer problems to tackle first to improve adoption and satisfaction, and to validate them before investing in development.
Deliverable: 1) 8 to 12 likely pain points, grouped into before buying, during implementation, and in daily use. 2) A score for each by frequency, intensity, and revenue impact. 3) A proposed solution for the top 3. 4) A script of 8 open-ended questions to interview customers and confirm or rule out each hypothesis.
Criteria: Separate what comes from my data from what is your hypothesis. Interview questions should ask about past events ("when was the last time you…?") rather than opinions or intentions, because that yields more reliable answers.
Format: A table with the columns Pain point, Stage, Frequency, Intensity, Impact, and Data or hypothesis; then solutions and the script as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
