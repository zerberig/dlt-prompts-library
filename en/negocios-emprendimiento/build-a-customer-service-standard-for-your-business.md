Context: I run [your business], which serves [type of customers] through [service channels] (in store, WhatsApp, phone, or email). The most common complaints are [frequent complaints].
Goal: Raise the quality of our service so customers come back and recommend us.
Deliverable: 1) A map of the key moments customers have with us, from first contact through after-sales, with what should happen at each one. 2) 12 concrete improvements, ordered from easiest to hardest. 3) Measurable standards: response time, follow-up, and case resolution. 4) Model replies for 4 difficult situations: a delay, a defective product, an upset customer in public, and a request we can't fulfill.
Criteria: The actions should work with my current team. Replies should sound human, acknowledge the problem, and offer a concrete fix.
Format: The map as a table with the columns Moment, What the customer expects, What we do today, and Improvement; the rest as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
