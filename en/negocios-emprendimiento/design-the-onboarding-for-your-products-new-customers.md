Context: I have [your product]. The first moment of value for a new customer is [first value moment]. Right now new users get stuck or drop off at [where new users drop off today], and I have [available resources] (team, email or in-product tools).
Goal: I want more new customers to reach their first win quickly, because people who get there early are far more likely to stay.
Deliverable: 1) A definition of an activated customer and how to measure it. 2) A day 0 to day 30 flow with each step, its channel (in-product, email, human touch), and its goal. 3) How to remove or simplify the steps where people drop off today. 4) Drafts of the 3 most important emails. 5) Metrics to track: activation rate, time to first value, and 30-day retention, with a reasonable starting target.
Criteria: Ask customers only for what's essential at the start and leave the rest for later. Personalize by customer type when what they need to achieve changes. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The flow as a table with Day, Step, Channel, Goal, and Metric; the emails in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
