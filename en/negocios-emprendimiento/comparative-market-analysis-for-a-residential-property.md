Context: I'm a real estate agent and need a comparative market analysis (CMA) for [property description] (type, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, age, condition, and extras), located in [location]. These are the comparables I have: [available comparables] (address or area, price, sale or listing date, features).
Goal: I want a well-supported price range to recommend to the owner, and to explain why, so the property lists at a competitive price.
Deliverable: 1) A comparables table showing how each differs from the subject property. 2) Adjustments for size, condition, age, amenities, and location, explaining the reasoning for each. 3) Adjusted price per square meter or square foot. 4) A suggested price range: quick-sale price, market price, and stretch price. 5) Local market factors that could move the price. 6) 3 talking points for presenting the range to the owner.
Criteria: Use the comparables I gave you; if you have web search, add recent listings from the area and cite the source. Clearly separate closed sale prices from asking prices. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Note that this doesn't replace a professional appraisal.
Format: Numbered sections with tables for comparables and adjustments, and a 5-line summary for the client at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
