Context: I sell [your product or service]. Here's what I know about my customers: [your customer data] (who buys, what they buy, how often, through which channel, what they ask about or complain about).
Goal: I want to stop talking to everyone the same way and focus my marketing and offer on the customer groups that bring me the most value.
Deliverable: 1) 3 to 5 segments based on needs and behavior (not just age or gender), each with a descriptive name, profile, main problem, what they value when buying, typical objections, and estimated value to the business. 2) For each segment: the key message, the best-fit offer, and the 2 channels to reach it. 3) Which one to prioritize first and why. 4) How to tell which segment a new customer belongs to, and what data to start collecting to refine the segmentation.
Criteria: Base the segments on my data; if something is an assumption, mark it as a hypothesis to validate. Segments should be distinct from each other and large enough to justify their own effort.
Format: A summary table with one column per segment; then one section per segment with bullets, and a closing on priority and next steps.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
