Context: I run [your business], which offers [what you sell] to [target audience]. My main competitors are [competitors], and what sets us apart is [your differentiator].
Goal: Define a clear, consistent brand identity that helps me become a recognized reference in my industry and say the same thing across every channel.
Deliverable: 1) A one-sentence positioning statement. 2) Purpose, 3 values, and a brand personality with concrete traits. 3) Tone of voice with "we sound like this" and "we don't sound like this" examples. 4) Three key messages and 5 tagline options. 5) A 90-day plan to build authority: content, partnerships, and appearances at events or in media.
Criteria: Everything should rest on what I actually deliver; avoid promises I can't keep, because a brand is built on consistency. Prefer concrete words over abstractions like "innovation" or "quality".
Format: Sections with headings and bullets. 400-700 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
