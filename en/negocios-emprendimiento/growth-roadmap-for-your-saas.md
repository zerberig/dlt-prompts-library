Context: I have [your SaaS product]. Here's where we are today: [current situation] (recurring revenue, number of customers, team). The target is [growth target and timeframe].
Goal: I want a roadmap that aligns the team on what to do each quarter to hit the target, and what to leave out.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis: the strongest growth lever and the main bottleneck. 2) Initiatives per quarter across product, acquisition, retention, and team, each with a milestone, owner, and dependencies. 3) Leading indicators that show whether we're on track before revenue does. 4) The top 5 risks with a mitigation plan. 5) A list of what we won't do and why.
Criteria: Break the target into intermediate numbers (new customers, retention, average price) to check it's achievable; if it isn't with my resources, tell me and propose an adjusted target. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: The roadmap as a table with Quarter, Initiative, Milestone, Owner, and Indicator; the rest in numbered sections. 500-800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
