Context: I run [your agency] in [city or country] and I'm bringing on new agents with [agents' experience level]. In their first month I want them to master [priority skills] (for example, professional ethics, negotiation, market analysis, and lead generation).
Goal: A training program that makes them productive quickly while upholding the industry's ethics and regulations.
Deliverable: 1) A 4-week program split into modules, each with its learning objective. 2) For each module: key content, a hands-on activity (role-play, case analysis, or a shadowed showing), and how to assess it. 3) A negotiation exercise with briefs for the buyer role and the seller role. 4) A checklist of what the agent should be able to do by the end. 5) A coaching plan for months 2 and 3.
Criteria: Balance theory and practice, with at least half the time spent on exercises. For legal and regulatory topics, list what must be covered and recommend that a lawyer or the local real estate association teach it, because rules vary by country.
Format: The program as a table with the columns Week, Module, Objective, Activity, and Assessment; the rest in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
