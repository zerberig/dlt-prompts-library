Context: I run [your business], and here are my profiles and where they stand: [your profiles and current results] (platforms, followers, posting frequency, content type, what works and what doesn't). My ideal customer is [ideal customer].
Goal: I want to know what's holding my social presence back and what to change first to attract more potential customers, not just followers.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of each profile: bio, profile and cover images, links, highlights or pinned posts, visual consistency, content type, frequency, and community interaction. 2) 10 actionable improvements ranked by impact and ease. 3) A proposed new bio for each platform. 4) A 2-hour weekly routine to keep the presence active.
Criteria: Recommendations specific to my case, not generic advice. If you talk about algorithms or best posting times, present it as recommended practice rather than guaranteed fact, and suggest how to test it with my own analytics.
Format: The diagnosis as a table with Element, Current state, and Improvement; the 10 improvements as a numbered list; bios and routine in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
