Context: My business sells [your product or service]. Today I sell [current monthly sales] and I want to grow sales by [growth percentage] within [timeframe]. This is the data I have: [your customer, average order value, and conversion data].
Goal: A realistic plan that shows where that growth can come from and what to do each month.
Deliverable: 1) The target broken into levers: more leads, a better conversion rate, a higher average order value, more frequent purchases, and fewer lost customers, with how much each should contribute. 2) 2 to 3 actions per lever, with estimated cost and effort. 3) A pricing and packaging review: opportunities for bundles, tiers, or upgrades. 4) A month-by-month plan with interim targets. 5) Indicators to check every week.
Criteria: If the target isn't realistic given my data, tell me and propose an alternative. Show your math. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A levers table with targets and actions, a monthly plan table, and indicators as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
