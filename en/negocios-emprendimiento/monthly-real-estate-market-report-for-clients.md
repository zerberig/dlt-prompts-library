Context: I'm a real estate advisor and every month I send my clients a market report on [city or area] covering [property type] for [period]. Here's the data I have: [data you have].
Goal: I want a report my clients actually read, and that shows I know the area better than a listings portal does.
Deliverable: A report with: 1) A 3-line summary of what matters most. 2) Sale prices and how they changed. 3) Transaction volume and available inventory. 4) Rents and estimated yield. 5) What this means for buyers, sellers, and investors. 6) One interesting local fact or trend. 7) A closing invitation to book a consultation.
Criteria: Use my data first; if you have web search, fill gaps with recent public sources and cite each one. Don't make up figures: if a number is missing, write «to be completed» in its place. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Plain language, no unnecessary jargon.
Format: A title, sections with headings, and a summary table with Indicator, Value, Change, and Source. 500-700 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
