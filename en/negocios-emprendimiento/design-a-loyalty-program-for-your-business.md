Context: I run [your business], I sell [what you sell and average ticket], and my customers buy on average [current purchase frequency]. I can put [budget or available margin] toward rewards.
Goal: I want existing customers to come back more often and refer others, without giving away margin to people who would have bought anyway.
Deliverable: 1) The recommended mechanic (points, tiers, membership, or punch card) and why it fits my business. 2) A rewards table with the estimated cost to me. 3) A simple referral system. 4) Launch messages for the store and for WhatsApp or email. 5) Three metrics that tell me within 90 days whether it's working.
Criteria: Favor rewards with high perceived value and low real cost (early access, extra services, experiences) over discounts, because discounts train customers to buy only on promotion. Everything should be runnable with the tools a small business already has. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections; the table with the columns Reward, How it's earned, Estimated cost, and Why it motivates. 500 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
