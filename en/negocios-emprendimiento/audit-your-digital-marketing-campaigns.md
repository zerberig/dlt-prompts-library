Context: I run [your business] and these are my digital marketing campaign metrics for the last period, by channel: [your campaign metrics] (spend, reach, clicks, conversions, sales, or whatever you have).
Goal: Know which campaigns work, which don't, and where to move budget next month.
Deliverable: 1) Key metrics per channel where the data allows: cost per click, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, and return on ad spend. 2) A verdict for each channel: scale, adjust, or pause, with the reason. 3) Five concrete optimization recommendations. 4) Which metrics I'm not tracking yet and how to start.
Criteria: Show how you calculated each figure. If you compare against industry averages, use web search if you have it. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. If the period is short or volumes are low, warn me that conclusions are unreliable.
Format: A table per channel; then the verdicts and recommendations as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
