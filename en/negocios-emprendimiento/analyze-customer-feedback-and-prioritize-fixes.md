Context: Here is real feedback from customers of [your product or service] (reviews, surveys, support tickets, or messages): [paste the feedback here].
Goal: Find what to improve first, based on what customers actually say rather than assumptions.
Deliverable: 1) Recurring themes with the number of mentions and one verbatim sample quote each. 2) Overall sentiment per theme. 3) The top 5 improvements, ranked by impact and ease. 4) What customers value and should stay as is. 5) What data I'm missing to confirm the conclusions.
Criteria: Base everything on the feedback I pasted; don't invent quotes or percentages. Tell an isolated complaint apart from a pattern. If a comment is ambiguous, say so instead of reading it the convenient way.
Format: A table with the columns Theme, Mentions, Sentiment, and Sample quote; then numbered lists. 500 words max.
