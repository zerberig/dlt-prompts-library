Context: At [your business] we're about to run [project name and goal]. We have [available team and budget] and the deadline is [deadline].
Goal: A project plan the whole team understands and that lets me catch delays early.
Deliverable: 1) Scope: what's in and what's out. 2) A breakdown into phases and tasks, with estimated duration and dependencies. 3) A timeline with milestones and deliverables. 4) A responsibility matrix: who does the work, who approves, who is consulted, and who is informed. 5) Budget by phase. 6) The top 5 risks with their mitigation. 7) A tracking cadence: meetings, progress reports, and indicators.
Criteria: Build in a time buffer for surprises and highlight the critical path. If the deadline isn't realistic with the resources, tell me and suggest what to cut or adjust.
Format: Tables for tasks, timeline, responsibilities, and budget; the rest as bullets. At the end, how to move the timeline into a spreadsheet or project management tool.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
