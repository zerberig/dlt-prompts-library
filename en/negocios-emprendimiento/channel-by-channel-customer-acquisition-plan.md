Context: I run [your business] and my ideal customer is [ideal customer]. Each sale brings an average ticket of [average ticket], and I can invest [monthly marketing budget] per month in acquiring customers.
Goal: I want a predictable system for generating qualified leads and turning them into customers, instead of relying on occasional referrals.
Deliverable: 1) The 3 or 4 channels with the most potential for my case, mixing online and offline, with the reason for each. 2) For each channel, a concrete lead offer (free assessment, guide, demo, visit) and the core message. 3) Criteria for qualifying a lead before investing time in it. 4) A 14-day follow-up sequence. 5) The maximum cost per customer I can afford given my ticket, and a budget split by channel. 6) A 60-day test plan with what to measure and when to drop a channel.
Criteria: Fit everything to the real budget and a small team. If you have web search, check current advertising costs in my industry. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections, one table per channel with Offer, Message, Estimated cost, and Key metric. 500-800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
