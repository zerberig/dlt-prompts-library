Context: I want to implement [practice, strategy, or technology] at [your business], which operates in [your industry].
Goal: Learn from companies that have already done it, so I can repeat what worked and avoid their mistakes.
Deliverable: 1) 4 to 6 real cases across different industries and company sizes, favoring small and midsize businesses and Latin American examples where they exist. 2) For each case: company, context, what they did, measurable results, timeframe, and source. 3) Common patterns among the successful cases and frequent mistakes. 4) The 5 lessons most relevant to my business, each with a first step.
Criteria: Use web search and cite the source and date of each case. Include only documented cases; if you can't find enough, tell me rather than inventing them or filling in with hypothetical examples. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: One profile per case with fixed subheadings; then patterns and lessons as lists.
