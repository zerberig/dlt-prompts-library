Goal: I want to know who it makes sense to ask for money at my stage, and to show up with a proposal that gets taken seriously.
Deliverable: 1) Which funding sources fit me (friends and family, angel investors, accelerators, venture capital funds, debt, public programs) and why, with what each one usually expects. 2) How to find specific investors of that type in my region. 3) An outreach sequence, from warm intro to follow-up. 4) A short first-contact email. 5) A 10-slide pitch structure tailored to my case. 6) The 10 tough questions I'll get and how to prepare for them.
Criteria: Don't invent investor or fund names; if you have web search, only suggest ones you can verify and cite the source. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Point out where legal and accounting advice is worth getting, for example when negotiating valuation or signing an agreement.
Format: Numbered sections; funding sources as a table with Type, Typical amount, What they expect, and Fit with my case.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
