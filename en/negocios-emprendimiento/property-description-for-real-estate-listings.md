Context: I'm listing [property details] (type, location, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking, amenities, condition, and price). The most special thing about this property is [what makes it special], and the ideal buyer is [ideal buyer].
Goal: I want a listing that makes the ideal buyer picture themselves living there and request a viewing.
Deliverable: 1) A listing-portal description: a title of up to 70 characters, an opening paragraph that paints life in the home, features as bullets, and a closing call to action (150 to 250 words). 2) A social media version of up to 80 words with an opening hook. 3) A short WhatsApp message to send to interested prospects.
Criteria: Stick precisely to the details I gave you and don't exaggerate or invent features, because mismatches at the viewing destroy trust. Describe the property and its benefits, not who should or shouldn't buy it, to avoid discriminatory language. Skip clichés like "once-in-a-lifetime opportunity" or "don't miss out".
Format: The 3 versions in separate blocks, each with a heading.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
