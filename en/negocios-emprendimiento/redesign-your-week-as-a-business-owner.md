Context: I own [your business]. In a typical week I split my time like this: [how you spend your week]. What I struggle with most is [your main time problem].
Goal: Win back hours for the work that grows the business and stop putting out fires all day.
Deliverable: 1) My activities sorted into eliminate, automate, delegate, or keep, with the reason for each. 2) An ideal-week template with time blocks. 3) Five concrete rules or habits, such as set times for email and messages. 4) A 15-minute weekly review with guiding questions.
Criteria: Suggest realistic changes for someone who also handles customers and operations; start with the ones that free up the most hours for the least effort.
Format: One table for the sorting, another for the ideal week, and lists for the rest.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
