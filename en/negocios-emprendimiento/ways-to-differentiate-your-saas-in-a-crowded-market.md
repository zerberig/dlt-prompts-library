Context: I run [your SaaS product] in a crowded category: [product category]. Today customers mainly choose me because [why customers choose you], and my team has [team size] people.
Goal: I want ways to stand out that don't depend on adding more features, because the biggest budget wins that race.
Deliverable: 12 ideas grouped into 6 levers: niche or industry specialization, pricing and packaging, service and hands-on support, community and content, integrations and ecosystem, and user experience. For each idea: what it involves, why it would be hard to copy, estimated effort, and a cheap experiment to validate it in 2 to 4 weeks. At the end, the 3 ideas that best fit my situation.
Criteria: Concrete ideas for my category, not textbook advice. Factor in my team size when estimating effort.
Format: One section per lever with its ideas as bullets; the top 3 in a table with Idea, Why it fits, and Experiment.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
