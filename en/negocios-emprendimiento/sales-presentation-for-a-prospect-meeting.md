Context: I sell [your product] to [type of customer or company]. Its value proposition is [value proposition] and my main competitors are [competitors]. I'll be presenting to [who will be in the meeting].
Goal: A sales presentation that convinces this prospect to take the next step: a trial, a formal proposal, or a purchase.
Deliverable: 1) A 10-12 slide structure: the customer's problem, the cost of not solving it, the solution, how it works, differentiators versus alternatives, results or proof, market opportunity, pricing or model, and next steps. 2) For each slide: title, key content as bullets, a visual suggestion, and speaker notes. 3) Answers to the 5 most likely objections. 4) A one-paragraph recap to email after the meeting.
Criteria: Talk about the customer before the product. Use only the data and results I give you; where a figure or real case is needed, leave a "TBD" placeholder. Clear calls to action, no artificial pressure.
Format: One section per slide; objections as a table with the columns Objection, Response, and Supporting proof.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
