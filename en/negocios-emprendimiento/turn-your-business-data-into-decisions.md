Context: I run [your business] and here is my data: [paste your data or attach your file] (sales, customers, inventory, web traffic, or other). The decision I need to make is [decision you want to make].
Goal: Use my own data to make better decisions instead of relying on gut feeling alone.
Deliverable: 1) A quick data quality check: gaps, errors, or biases. 2) The 5 findings most relevant to my decision, each with the calculation or comparison behind it. 3) A recommendation with its confidence level and what would change it. 4) A simple dashboard to keep tracking this: 5 to 7 indicators, the source of each, update frequency, and a suggested chart type.
Criteria: Don't extrapolate beyond what the data shows, and keep correlation separate from causation. If you can run code or analyze files, do it and show me the key charts.
Format: Sections with headings; findings as a numbered list and the dashboard as a table.
