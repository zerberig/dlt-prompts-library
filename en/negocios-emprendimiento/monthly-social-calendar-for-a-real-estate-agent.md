Deliverable: A 4-week calendar mixing listings, local market insight, tips for buyers and sellers, behind-the-scenes, and client stories (with their permission). For each post: format (carousel, reel, story, feed post), first-line hook, short caption, call to action, and 3 to 5 local hashtags.
Criteria: No more than 1 in 4 posts should be a direct listing pitch, because useful content is what builds trust. On timing, don't invent "best times to post": tell me how to find them in my own account's analytics.
Format: A table with the columns Date, Platform, Format, Topic, Hook, Caption, CTA, and Hashtags.
