Context: I run [your business] and my ideal customer is [your ideal customer]. I'm looking for a partnership to [partnership goal], and I can offer [what you can offer] in return.
Goal: I want to grow market share by teaming up with businesses that already have my ideal customers' trust.
Deliverable: 1) Ten types of potential partners (complementary services, suppliers, communities, media, platforms), with examples of the kind of company. 2) A matrix to evaluate them on value alignment, shared audience, effort, mutual benefit, and risk. 3) The top 3 with a concrete collaboration proposal: what each side does and how success is measured. 4) A short, customizable first-contact message.
Criteria: If you suggest specific companies, use web search to confirm they exist and fit, and flag anything you can't confirm as a suggestion to verify. The proposal should be clearly beneficial to the partner, not just to me, because that makes a yes more likely.
Format: The matrix as a table scored 1 to 5; proposals and message in separate sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
