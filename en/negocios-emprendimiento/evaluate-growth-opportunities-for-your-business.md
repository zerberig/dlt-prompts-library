Context: I run [your business] in the [your industry] industry, with these current products and customers: [current products and customers]. My main capabilities are [key capabilities or assets].
Goal: Identify and compare growth opportunities so I can pick 1 or 2 to invest in next year.
Deliverable: 1) Current industry trends that open up opportunities. 2) Opportunities organized into 4 paths: selling more to current customers, new products for them, current products in new segments or markets, and diversification. 3) An assessment of each opportunity by potential size, required investment, fit with my capabilities, risk, and time to results. 4) A recommendation of the top 2, each with a low-cost experiment to validate it before overinvesting.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it for the trends and cite dated sources. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Favor opportunities that build on what I already have.
Format: An assessment table scoring each criterion from 1 to 5; the rest in sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
