Context: I run [your business] and my typical customer is [type of customer]. They find me and buy through [channels where they find and buy from you]. The complaints or friction points I know about are: [known complaints or friction points].
Goal: I want to see the full experience through the customer's eyes so I can decide what to improve first.
Deliverable: 1) A map with the stages discovery, consideration, purchase, use or delivery, after-sale, and referral. 2) For each stage: what the customer wants to achieve, what they do, touchpoints, how they feel, and where they get stuck. 3) The 5 moments with the most impact on the decision to buy again. 4) Five quick wins (under 2 weeks) and three deeper fixes, with the metric to track each one.
Criteria: Base the map on what I gave you and mark anything you're assuming as a hypothesis. Also suggest 3 questions to validate the map with real customers, because an unvalidated map reflects the business's view, not the customer's.
Format: A table with the columns Stage, Customer goal, Actions, Touchpoints, Emotion, Friction, and Opportunity; then the fixes as a prioritized list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
