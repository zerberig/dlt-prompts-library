Context: I'm designing [your digital product] for [main users]. The most important action I want them to take is [key action]. Here's what I already know about my users: [what you already know about your users].
Goal: I want a clear UX foundation to hand off to design and development, and to validate with real users before building.
Deliverable: 1) Two or three user personas based on what I gave you, marking what's an assumption and what's data. 2) The main step-by-step flow to the key action, plus one secondary flow. 3) Text wireframes of the 3 critical screens: elements, hierarchy, and content. 4) The main expected friction points and how to reduce them. 5) A usability test plan with 5 users: tasks, questions, and what to watch for.
Criteria: Prioritize clarity and the fewest possible steps to the key action. Build in basic accessibility (contrast, text size, keyboard navigation) from the start, because fixing it later costs more.
Format: Numbered sections; each wireframe as a top-to-bottom hierarchical list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
