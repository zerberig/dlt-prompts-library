Context: I'm launching [your new product] for [target audience] on [launch date]. Its main benefit is [main benefit], and I have [campaign budget] for the campaign.
Goal: I want to build anticipation before launch, land the first sales during launch week, and keep the momentum afterward.
Deliverable: 1) 2 creative concepts for the campaign, each with a name, core idea, main message, and tone; recommend one. 2) A 3-phase plan (3 to 4 weeks of pre-launch, launch week, and 4 weeks after) with goals, actions per channel, and the content pieces needed. 3) An honest launch offer with a real deadline. 4) Ideas for collaborating with relevant creators or partners, with criteria for choosing them. 5) Goals and metrics per phase. 6) Budget split.
Criteria: Each phase should move the audience to the next step: waitlist, first purchase, referral. If you involve creators, keep in mind that paid posts must be disclosed as ads. Scale the actions to my budget.
Format: Concepts in separate blocks; the plan as a table by phase with Goal, Actions, Channel, and Metric; the budget as a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
