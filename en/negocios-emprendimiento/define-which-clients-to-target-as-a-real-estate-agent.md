Context: I'm a real estate agent specializing in [type of properties] in [city or area]. My best clients so far have been: [description of your best clients].
Goal: Know exactly which kinds of clients to go after and how to reach them, instead of prospecting everyone.
Deliverable: 1) 4 to 6 potential client profiles (for example, investors, growing families, or downsizers), each with their need, the life event that triggers the search, and their main objection. 2) Where to find each profile: digital channels, partnerships (notaries, developers, property managers), and events. 3) A first-contact message for each profile. 4) Three social ad ideas that show the benefit of working with an agent.
Criteria: Messages should offer something useful (a market insight or a guide) and avoid pressure. Prospect only with data collected with consent and in line with my country's data protection laws.
Format: A table of the profiles; then messages and ads as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
