Context: I own [your business] and work about [weekly working hours] hours a week. Here's how my time is split today: [how you spend your time] (customer service, operations, sales, admin, social media, meetings, etc.). My team is [your team].
Goal: I want to stop firefighting all day and free up at least one fixed block each week to work on growing the business, not just running it.
Deliverable: 1) A breakdown of my activities into: do it myself, delegate, automate, or eliminate, with the reason for each call. 2) For what can be automated, the type of tool that would help, including AI options where relevant. 3) A typical week with time blocks: focused work, customer time, admin, and a weekly review. 4) 3 personal rules to protect my time (for example, response windows or meeting limits). 5) A 4-week plan to make the transition without neglecting the business.
Criteria: Realistic for my team size and budget. Prioritize the changes that free up the most hours for the least effort.
Format: The breakdown as a table with Activity, Decision, and Why; the typical week as a table by day; the rest as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
