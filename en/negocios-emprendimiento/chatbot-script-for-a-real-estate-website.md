Context: I run [your agency name], which sells and rents [type of properties] in [city or area]. I want a chat assistant on my website that helps visitors, including after hours.
Goal: Answer common questions instantly and turn interested visitors into qualified leads my team can follow up with.
Deliverable: 1) A welcome message and opening menu. 2) Conversation flows for buying, renting, selling a property, and general questions. 3) Qualifying questions (budget, area, timeline, payment method) in the order they work best. 4) 10 FAQs about the home-buying process with short answers. 5) Rules for handing the conversation to a human agent, plus a closing message that asks for contact details with consent.
Criteria: Friendly and brief, 2-3 sentences per message at most, because people abandon long blocks of text in chat. The bot gives no legal or tax advice and never promises prices or appreciation; in those cases it offers to connect with an agent. Answers about paperwork stay general and note that requirements vary by country.
Format: Each flow as a dialogue labeled Bot and User, with branches marked as "If the user answers…".
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
