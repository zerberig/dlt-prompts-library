Context: At [your business] we're about to implement [initiative or project] (a new system, an expansion, a process change, etc.). We have [available resources] and the timeline is [timeline].
Goal: Identify what could go wrong before we start and have a plan for every significant risk.
Deliverable: A register of 10 to 15 risks covering technical, financial, operational, legal, and people risks. For each: description, cause, likelihood (1-5), impact (1-5), score, mitigation action, contingency plan, warning sign, and suggested owner. Then a summary of the 3 critical risks and what to do about each this week.
Criteria: Write concrete risks specific to my initiative, not generic categories. Mitigations should be actions I can carry out with my resources. For legal or regulatory risks, recommend validating them with a specialist.
Format: A table sorted by score from highest to lowest; then the summary as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
