Context: I run [your business] and my main competitors are [your main competitors]. My customers have told me they value [what your customers value].
Goal: I want to stand out in a market where everyone offers roughly the same thing, and attract customers who don't even consider me today.
Deliverable: 1) A short map of how each competitor presents itself (promise, price, tone) and the space nobody occupies. 2) Seven out-of-the-box ideas to differentiate, mixing positioning, customer experience, guarantees, partnerships, and service format. 3) For each idea: cost, difficulty, and how to test it in 2 weeks before investing more.
Criteria: The ideas should rest on something I can sustain, not on competing only on price, because a price war is usually won by whoever has more capital. If you have web search, review what my competitors say on their websites and social media.
Format: The map as a table; the ideas as a numbered list with their three data points. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
