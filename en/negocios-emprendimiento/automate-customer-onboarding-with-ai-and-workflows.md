Context: I run [your digital product or service]. Today, when someone signs up or buys, this is what happens: [your current onboarding process]. Customers usually get stuck at [where your customers get stuck].
Goal: Use automation and AI so every new customer reaches their first result quickly, with a more personal experience and less manual work for my team.
Deliverable: 1) A map of the customer journey in their first 30 days, with the critical moments. 2) 8 to 10 automation ideas (behavior-based messages, in-product guides, an AI assistant, reminders, or short surveys), each with the problem it solves, its trigger, and a suggested tool. 3) Where it's worth keeping a human touch. 4) Three indicators to measure whether the experience improved.
Criteria: Rank the ideas by impact and ease. Automations should feel helpful, not intrusive, and respect customers' contact preferences and privacy.
Format: A table with the columns Idea, Problem, Trigger, Tool, Impact, and Effort; the rest as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
