Context: I have [your SaaS product], which solves [problem it solves]. The person who makes the buying decision is usually [buyer profile]. The campaign will run for [campaign length and budget].
Goal: I want my target market to know the product and for that to turn into free trials or booked demos, not just impressions.
Deliverable: 1) A central campaign idea with its main line and 2 alternatives. 2) Messaging tailored to the person who will use it and to the person who approves the budget. 3) Recommended channels with each one's role (discovery, consideration, conversion). 4) The list of assets to produce (ads, landing page, emails, LinkedIn content, webinar). 5) A week-by-week calendar. 6) Metrics per stage with a reasonable target for each.
Criteria: The messaging should start from the customer's pain and a measurable outcome, not the feature list. Avoid promises the product can't back up. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Sections with headings, the calendar as a table with Week, Action, Channel, and Asset. 600-900 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
