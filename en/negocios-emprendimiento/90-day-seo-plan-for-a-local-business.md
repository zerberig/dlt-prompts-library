Context: I run [your business] and serve customers in [city or area you serve]. My site has these pages: [main pages on your site]. I can spend [weekly time available] on this each week.
Goal: I want people searching for what I sell in my area to find me, both in search engines and in AI-generated answers, so I get more inquiries without paying for ads.
Deliverable: 1) The 10 searches my customers most likely use, grouped by intent. 2) Which page should answer each one and what it's missing. 3) A basic technical checklist (speed, mobile, titles, structured data). 4) Local SEO actions: business profile, genuine reviews, directories. 5) A 90-day week-by-week plan ordered by impact.
Criteria: Everything should be doable by one non-developer. If you have web search, check the search engines' current guidelines, and don't present assumptions about the algorithm as facts. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections, the plan as a table with Week, Task, Estimated time, and Expected impact.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
