Context: I want to implement [the initiative] (for example, a new CRM, a sales process, or a training program) at [your organization and team size]. The target date is [target date] and I have [available resources].
Goal: I want a plan the team can follow without getting lost, and that reduces resistance to the change.
Deliverable: 1) The project goal and 3 measurable success criteria. 2) Phases with milestones, deliverables, and dates. 3) A responsibility matrix: who decides, who does the work, who is consulted, and who is informed. 4) Resources needed per phase. 5) The top 5 risks with their mitigation. 6) A communication and training plan for the people who will live with the change. 7) How progress is reviewed each week.
Criteria: Realistic dates for a team that is also doing its day-to-day work. Include a pilot before the full rollout, because it surfaces problems while they're still cheap to fix.
Format: Numbered sections, a timeline table with Phase, Milestone, Owner, Date, and Deliverable. 500-800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
