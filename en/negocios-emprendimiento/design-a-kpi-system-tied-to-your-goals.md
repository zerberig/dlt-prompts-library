Context: I run [your business or product]. My strategic goals for this year are [your main goals]. Today I track [what you currently measure].
Goal: An indicator system that connects day-to-day work to my goals, so I know whether I'm on track and can act in time.
Deliverable: 1) One north-star metric that sums up the business's success. 2) 8 to 12 KPIs linked to each goal, split into leading (predict results) and lagging (confirm them). 3) For each KPI: definition, formula, data source, frequency, owner, starting target, and market benchmark. 4) Which metrics to stop tracking because they don't drive any decision.
Criteria: A few well-chosen indicators beat a cluttered dashboard. If you have web search, use benchmarks from my industry and cite the source. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns KPI, Goal, Type, Formula, Frequency, Owner, Target, and Benchmark.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
