Context: I run [your product or business] and want to attract [target audience]. Today I publish content on [current channels] and my capacity is [hours or people available for content].
Goal: A content strategy that establishes me as a go-to voice, drives organic traffic, and turns readers into leads I can nurture all the way to a sale.
Deliverable: 1) 3 to 4 content pillars, each with the customer question it answers. 2) Content by funnel stage (awareness, consideration, and decision) with suggested formats: articles, downloadable guides, webinars, case studies, or a newsletter. 3) A downloadable resource to capture emails and a 4-email sequence to nurture people who download it. 4) A plan for collaborating with creators or industry experts. 5) An 8-week calendar and metrics per stage.
Criteria: Match the volume to my real capacity; publishing consistently beats posting a lot at first and then dropping off. Every piece should give the reader a clear next step.
Format: Sections with headings; the calendar as a table with the columns Week, Pillar, Piece, Format, Stage, and Call to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
