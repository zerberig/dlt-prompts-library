Context: I run [your product or business] for [target audience]. My main competitors are [main competitors], and here's what I know about them: [what you know about your competitors] (price, audience, messaging, strengths, and weaknesses).
Goal: I want to find a market position that's credible, sets me apart, and is hard for competitors to copy.
Deliverable: 1) A comparison of each competitor on price, audience, value proposition, strengths, and weaknesses. 2) A positioning map using the 2 axes my customers care about most, explaining where each player sits. 3) Gaps or needs nobody is serving well. 4) 3 positioning options for me, each with a value proposition statement, what I'd need to change or reinforce, and its main risk. 5) Your recommendation.
Criteria: Base the analysis on what I gave you; if you have web search, add recent public information and cite the source. Separate facts from your assumptions. Avoid generic differentiators like "better quality" or "better service", because everyone claims them.
Format: A competitor comparison table, the map described in text or as a table, and the 3 options as sections with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
