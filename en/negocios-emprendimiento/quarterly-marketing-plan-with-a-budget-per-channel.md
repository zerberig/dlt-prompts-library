Context: I run [your business] and want to grow [product or service to promote] with [target audience]. My budget for the quarter is [quarterly budget], and today most customers find me through [current channels].
Goal: I want a 90-day marketing plan that tells me what to spend on, when, and how to know it's working.
Deliverable: 1) 2 or 3 measurable goals for the quarter. 2) Strategy per channel (social media, email, search, ads, partnerships, events, or whatever applies): what to do, why that channel, and what result I should expect. 3) Budget split by channel, with a 10–15% reserve to double down on what works. 4) A week-by-week calendar with activities and owners. 5) Metrics per channel and a mid-quarter checkpoint.
Criteria: Concentrate the budget on the channels most likely to work for my audience instead of splitting it evenly. If you give per-channel costs or benchmark conversion rates, flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Activities should be realistic for a small team.
Format: Sections with headings; the budget as a table with Channel, Amount, Percentage, and Expected result; the calendar as a table by week.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
