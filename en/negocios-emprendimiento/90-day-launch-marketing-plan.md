Context: I'm launching [your product, service, or development] for [target audience] in [market or city]. My total marketing budget is [budget in your currency] and the launch date is [launch date].
Goal: I want a plan my team can execute that tells me where to spend first, so we build demand before launch and drive sales in the first 90 days.
Deliverable: 1) Two or three prioritized audience segments, each with its main motivation and most common objection. 2) One core message and 3 supporting messages. 3) Recommended channels and why each one. 4) Budget split by channel and by phase (pre-launch, launch, and consolidation). 5) A week-by-week calendar. 6) Metrics and a target for each phase.
Criteria: Favor a few well-executed channels over many half-done ones, because the budget is limited. Justify every budget allocation. If you have web search, check benchmark costs per channel in my market. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Sections with headings; budget and calendar as tables. 600-900 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
