Context: I want to launch [your product or business] in [market or country] for [ideal customer]. My revenue model is [revenue model], and I have [available resources] (capital, team, experience).
Goal: I need a business plan that helps me make decisions and, if needed, can be presented to a bank, partner, or investor.
Deliverable: A plan with: 1) Executive summary. 2) Problem and solution. 3) Market analysis: estimated size, trends, and ideal customer. 4) Competitive landscape and differentiating advantage. 5) Business model and pricing. 6) Marketing and sales strategy. 7) Operations and team plan. 8) 3-year financial projections: revenue, costs, break-even, and funding needs, with explicit assumptions. 9) Milestones for the first 12 months. 10) Main risks and how to mitigate them.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it for market data and cite sources; flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Projections should be conservative and consistent with my resources. Recommend that I review the financial and legal sections with an accountant or advisor before presenting it.
Format: A document with one section per item, tables for projections, and bullets for milestones and risks. 1,500 to 2,500 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
