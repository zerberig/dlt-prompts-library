Context: I run [your business]. The situation I'm facing is [economic situation] (falling sales, inflation, rising costs, etc.). My approximate numbers are: [revenue, fixed costs, and cash on hand].
Goal: A plan to protect cash and keep the business stable over the next 6 months without sacrificing what drives growth.
Deliverable: 1) Three scenarios (moderate, hard, and severe) showing how many months of runway my cash gives me in each. 2) Expenses sorted into essential, cuttable, and revenue-generating, with cuts prioritized. 3) Actions to protect revenue: retaining customers, adjusting prices, and collecting faster. 4) Financing options with their pros and risks. 5) Signals that tell me when to move from one scenario to the next.
Criteria: Show your math and flag assumptions. Avoid cuts that hurt quality or relationships with key customers. Recommend I review decisions about debt, staffing, and taxes with an accountant or financial advisor.
Format: A scenarios table, an expenses table, and lists for the rest. 800 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
