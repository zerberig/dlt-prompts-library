Context: I run [your SaaS product]. My monthly churn is [current churn] and the most common cancellation reasons are [cancellation reasons]. These are the plans or add-ons a customer can buy: [plans and add-ons].
Goal: I want customers to stay longer and grow with me, to raise customer lifetime value (LTV) without relying only on new customer acquisition.
Deliverable: 1) A customer health score with 4 to 6 signals (usage, adoption of key features, tickets, payments, engagement) and how to combine them into green, yellow, and red. 2) Playbooks for each state: what to do, who does it, and by when. 3) A flow for customers trying to cancel: questions to understand why and alternatives (pause, plan change, help) without putting obstacles in the way of leaving. 4) Moments and signals for offering an upgrade or add-on in a way that's genuinely useful to the customer. 5) Metrics to measure the plan's impact.
Criteria: Cancellation should be clear and simple, because making it hard erodes trust and is illegal in many places. If you give churn or LTV benchmarks, flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table for the health score with Signal, How it's measured, and Thresholds; the rest as numbered sections with bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
