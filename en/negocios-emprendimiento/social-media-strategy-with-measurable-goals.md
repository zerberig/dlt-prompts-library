Context: I run [your business] and want to reach [target audience]. The business goal social media should support is [business goal]. I use or am considering [platforms you use or are considering], and I have [available time and team].
Goal: I want a social media strategy I can sustain with my resources and that shows up in the business, not just in follower counts.
Deliverable: 1) Which 2 or 3 platforms to focus on and why, and which to skip for now (consider Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, and X based on my audience). 2) 3 to 4 content pillars with a percentage for each. 3) Formats and a realistic cadence per platform. 4) How to engage the community: replies, DMs, and collaborations. 5) KPIs tied to my goal and how to measure them. 6) A 90-day review plan.
Criteria: Favor consistency over volume. Don't present assumptions about the algorithms as facts; if you have web search, check each platform's recent changes. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections; KPIs as a table with KPI, How it's measured, 90-day target, and What decision it informs. 500-800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
