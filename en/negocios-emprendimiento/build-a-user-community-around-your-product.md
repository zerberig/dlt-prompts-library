Context: I run [your product] with roughly [number of customers or users] customers. What my users have in common is [what your users share].
Goal: Build a community where users help each other, learn to get more out of the product, and feel part of the brand, to improve retention and referrals.
Deliverable: 1) The community's value proposition: why a user would want to join. 2) A recommended platform, with pros and cons of 2-3 options. 3) A 90-day launch plan: how to invite the first members, community guidelines, and first activities. 4) Recurring rituals (live sessions, challenges, member recognition) and an ambassador program. 5) Community health metrics.
Criteria: Design it to run on little of my team's time and account for moderation effort. The community should deliver real value to users, not serve only as a promotion channel.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets; the 90-day plan as a month-by-month table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
