Context: At [your business] there's a process that eats up a lot of time: [process to improve]. Today it works like this: [current steps and who does them]. We use these tools: [current tools].
Goal: Cut time, errors, and costs in that process so the team can focus on higher-value work.
Deliverable: 1) The current process as numbered steps, flagging bottlenecks, rework, and steps that add no value. 2) The redesigned process. 3) What to eliminate, simplify, standardize, or automate, with suggested tools (including AI options) and their approximate cost. 4) An estimate of hours saved per month. 5) A 3-phase rollout plan and how to measure the improvement.
Criteria: Favor low-cost, quick-to-adopt changes over complex systems. Flag as estimates any savings and costs you can't back up.
Format: A before-and-after comparison table; the rest as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
