Context: I run [your business], a local business in [your city or area] that sells [what you sell]. My customers are [target audience] and I'm active on [social networks you use].
Goal: A 4-week social campaign that gets people to comment, share, and visit or buy, not just scroll past my posts.
Deliverable: 1) A creative campaign concept with a name and its own hashtag. 2) A participation mechanic (challenge, poll, customer-created content, or similar) with clear rules. 3) A 4-week calendar with 3-4 posts per week: format, idea, short caption, and call to action. 4) Ideas for teaming up with neighboring businesses or local creators. 5) Metrics to tell whether it worked.
Criteria: Ideas I can produce with a phone and a small budget. If you include a giveaway or contest, remind me to check each platform's current rules and local legal requirements. Favor mechanics that give customers something of value over asking for comments in exchange for nothing, because they generate better-quality engagement.
Format: Concept and mechanic in short paragraphs; the calendar as a table with the columns Week, Day, Format, Idea, Caption, and Call to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
