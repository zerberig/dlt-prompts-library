Context: I run [your business] and here are my numbers: current bank balance, expected income, and fixed and variable payments for the coming weeks: [your income and expense figures]. My customers usually pay me in [collection terms] and I pay suppliers in [payment terms].
Goal: See in advance which weeks I might run short of cash and have a plan to prevent it before it happens.
Deliverable: 1) A 13-week cash flow forecast. 2) The at-risk weeks flagged. 3) Actions to close each gap, ranked by speed and cost: collections, supplier negotiation, inventory, expenses, and financing. 4) Three indicators to review every week.
Criteria: Use only my figures and clearly flag every assumption you add. Favor actions that don't hurt relationships with customers or suppliers. Recommend that I check with an accountant or financial advisor before taking on financing or making tax decisions.
Format: A weekly table with the columns Week, Opening balance, Inflows, Outflows, and Closing balance; then the plan as a numbered list. At the end, the formulas to rebuild the table in a spreadsheet.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
