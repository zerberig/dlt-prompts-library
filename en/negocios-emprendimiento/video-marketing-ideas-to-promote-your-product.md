Context: I want to promote [your product] to [target audience]. For filming I have [filming resources] (phone or camera, budget, who can be on camera).
Goal: I want videos that reach new people and move them closer to buying, not just rack up views.
Deliverable: 1) Five clearly different video ideas (demo, before and after, behind the scenes, FAQ, customer story, or another), each with the ideal platform (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or YouTube), the hook for the first 3 seconds, the structure in 3 or 4 beats, and the call to action. 2) A full script for the idea with the most potential, with on-screen text, spoken lines, and shots. 3) How to repurpose one video across several platforms.
Criteria: Everything should be shootable with my resources in one afternoon. The ideas should show the product solving something real, because that persuades more than expensive production.
Format: The ideas as a table with Idea, Platform, Hook, Structure, and CTA; the script as a table with Second, Shot, Dialogue, and On-screen text.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
