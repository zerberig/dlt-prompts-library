Context: I have [your SaaS product] with a churn rate of [current churn rate]. The reasons I know about are: [cancellation reasons you know]. My customers are mainly [customer segment].
Goal: I want to understand what's driving cancellations and reduce them with actions I can measure.
Deliverable: 1) Hypotheses about the causes, ranked by likelihood, separating voluntary from involuntary churn (failed payments). 2) Early warning signals in product usage that indicate risk. 3) Interventions by stage: first 30 days, ongoing use, and renewal. 4) A cancellation flow with an exit survey and an honest alternative (pause or plan change). 5) Three experiments with their metric and the result that would confirm each hypothesis.
Criteria: The cancellation flow should be easy and transparent; hiding the button or adding hurdles hurts the brand and can break consumer protection laws. If you cite real companies as examples, make sure they're verifiable. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets, the experiments as a table with Hypothesis, Action, Metric, and Duration.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
