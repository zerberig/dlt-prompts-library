I run [your business] and want to win more customers among [target audience]. My monthly marketing budget is [monthly budget] and I've already tried [what you've already tried].
Give me 10 creative ideas for attracting new customers that go beyond the obvious (more ads or more posts). Mix digital channels, partnerships with other businesses, events, referral programs, LinkedIn or other professional platforms, and in-store experiences. For each idea, say what it involves, why it would work with my audience, approximate cost, effort (low, medium, or high), and the first step to test it this week.
Put everything in a table and finish by recommending the 3 ideas with the best impact-to-effort ratio.
