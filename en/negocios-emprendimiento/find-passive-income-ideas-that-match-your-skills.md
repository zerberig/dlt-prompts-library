Context: My main skills and knowledge are [your skills]. I can put [hours per week] into a side project and I have [available capital] to invest up front.
Goal: I want to build an income stream that, after the initial effort, needs little upkeep. I'm looking for realistic options for my profile, not a generic list.
Deliverable: 1) 8 passive or semi-passive income ideas that fit my skills (digital products, content, licensing, affiliate, templates, etc.). 2) The 3 most promising, each with a 30-day validation plan before I invest more time or money.
Criteria: Be honest about the real setup work and ongoing maintenance of each option, because almost no income is 100% passive. Drop anything that needs more capital than I have. If you mention potential earnings, flag them as estimates. If an idea involves investing money, recommend that I talk to a financial advisor.
Format: A table with Idea, Upfront investment, Hours to launch, Monthly maintenance, and Fit with my profile (high/medium/low); then the validation plan for the top 3 as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
