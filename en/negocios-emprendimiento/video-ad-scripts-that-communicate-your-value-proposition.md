Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. Its main benefit is [main benefit] and what sets it apart from alternatives is [differentiator]. The ad will run on [ad platform].
Goal: An ad that makes it clear within seconds why my product is worth it and drives action.
Deliverable: 1) The value proposition in one sentence. 2) Three video ad scripts of 15, 30, and 60 seconds, each with a different angle: problem-solution, demo, and a real customer testimonial. 3) For each script: a hook for the first 3 seconds, on-screen text, voiceover or dialogue, visual directions, and a call to action.
Criteria: The hook should show the problem or the result, not the logo. Use verifiable claims. In the testimonial script, mark where a real customer's quote goes instead of making one up.
Format: Each script as a table with the columns Time, Visual, On-screen text, and Audio.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
