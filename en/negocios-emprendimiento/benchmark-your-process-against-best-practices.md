Context: At [your business], this is how we currently handle [area or process] (for example, onboarding new customers, collections, or after-sales support): [description of your current process].
Goal: Know how far I am from best practice and what to change first to gain profitability and efficiency.
Deliverable: 1) The 8 to 10 most widely accepted best practices for this process in a business like mine, each with a short explanation of why it works. 2) A practice-by-practice comparison: I do it, I partly do it, or I don't, based on what I described. 3) The 3 gaps worth closing first and a 30-day plan to close them. 4) Common mistakes to avoid when rolling out the changes.
Criteria: Adapt the practices to my size and resources; if a practice is meant for large companies, suggest a simple version. If you have web search, rely on recent sources and cite them.
Format: A table with the columns Practice, Why it works, My situation, and Priority; then the plan as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
