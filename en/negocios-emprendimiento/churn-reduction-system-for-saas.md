Goal: A system to spot at-risk customers before they cancel, understand why they leave, and reduce churn sustainably.
Deliverable: 1) Risk signals based on my usage data, with suggested starting thresholds. 2) A process for understanding causes: exit survey, interviews, and cohort analysis. 3) A retention playbook with actions by risk type (low adoption, low perceived value, price, or support), owner, and timing. 4) Onboarding improvements to prevent early churn. 5) Metrics to track (logo churn, revenue churn, and net revenue retention) and how to calculate them.
Criteria: Separate voluntary from involuntary churn (failed payments), because they're solved differently. Retention offers should fix the customer's real problem, not just hand out discounts. Flag as an estimate any market benchmark you can't back up with a source.
