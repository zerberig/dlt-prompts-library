Context: I run [your business], my ideal customers are [ideal customer], and I have [monthly budget] per month and [hours per week] for marketing.
Goal: I want a simple plan I can pin on the wall and follow every week, instead of a long document I'll never reread.
Deliverable: A one-page marketing plan with: 1) Ideal customer and their main problem. 2) Core message in one sentence. 3) An entry offer to attract new customers. 4) 2 or 3 chosen channels, with the reason for each. 5) How I turn interested people into customers. 6) How I get them to come back and refer others. 7) Budget per channel. 8) 3 measurable goals for the next 90 days.
Criteria: Pick a few well-justified channels based on my audience and available time, because spreading too thin is the most common mistake on a small budget. One person should be able to execute all of it.
Format: 8 short sections with headings, 1 to 3 lines each. Close with a list of the first 5 tasks for this week.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
