Context: My product is [your product], it competes in [market or category], and it targets [ideal customer]. The competitors I know of are [known competitors].
Goal: Understand how I stack up against competitors so I can define my positioning and decide where to differentiate.
Deliverable: 1) A list of direct and indirect competitors, adding relevant ones I didn't mention. 2) A comparison matrix: value proposition, audience, pricing, key features, channels, and customer perception. 3) Strengths and weaknesses of each. 4) Gaps in the market that nobody serves well. 5) Three positioning options for me, with the upside and risks of each.
Criteria: If you have web search, research websites, published pricing, and current reviews, and give the source and date for each data point. Flag as an estimate anything you can't confirm, such as market share or revenue. Be honest about where competitors beat me.
Format: The matrix as a table; everything else in sections with bullets. 600-1,000 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
