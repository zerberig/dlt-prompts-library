Context: I offer [your service] and I'm sending a proposal to [client], whose main problem or need is [client's need]. My pricing and terms are [pricing and terms].
Goal: I want a proposal the client can read in 5 minutes, that shows I understand their problem, and that has everything needed to say yes or pass it to the decision-maker.
Deliverable: A proposal with these sections: 1) Executive summary. 2) Diagnosis of the client's situation. 3) Proposed solution and approach. 4) Scope and deliverables (including what's out of scope). 5) Phased timeline. 6) Investment and payment terms, with 2 or 3 package options if it makes sense. 7) Expected outcomes and how they'll be measured. 8) Why us. 9) Next steps.
Criteria: Talk about the client before talking about my company. Expected outcomes should be realistic and measurable; don't promise numbers I can't guarantee, because that causes disputes later. If case studies or testimonials would help, leave a placeholder marked "to be completed" so I add only real ones. Professional, direct tone.
Format: A document with section headings, short paragraphs, and bullets. 700 to 1,000 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
