Goal: I want a planned month of content that educates, entertains, and sells in the right mix, so I stop improvising every day.
Deliverable: 1) 4 content pillars with their goal and the share of the calendar each one takes. 2) The full calendar with date, platform, pillar, format (carousel, short video, text post, story, live), idea, hook, and call to action. 3) Full copy for the 3 most important posts of the month.
Criteria: Vary the formats and don't repeat ideas. Include dates relevant to my audience and country only if they fit naturally. At most 1 in 5 posts should be a direct sales post, because the rest builds the trust that makes those sales work.
Format: Pillars as bullets, the calendar as a table, and the 3 posts at the end, each in its own block.
