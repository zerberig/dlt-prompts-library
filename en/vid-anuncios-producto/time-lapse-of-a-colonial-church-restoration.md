Locked-off time-lapse facing the carved stone facade of a colonial church on a Latin American plaza.
0–3 s: scaffolding and tarps go up over blackened, cracked stone; workers in hard hats move like flickers.
3–6 s: shadows shift day after day as cleaning reveals carved reliefs and the stone's original pinkish tone.
6–8 s: the scaffolding comes down and the restored facade glows under an orange sunset as pigeons take flight.
Photorealistic, documentary style, fast-moving clouds. Mood of pride and renewal.
