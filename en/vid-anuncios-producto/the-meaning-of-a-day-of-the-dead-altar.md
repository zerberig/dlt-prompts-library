Very slow eye-level lateral tracking shot along a multi-tiered Day of the Dead ofrenda in a village home in Mexico at night. The camera passes lit candles, marigold flowers, swaying papel picado, pan de muerto, sugar skulls with names written on them, and framed family photos; it ends on a girl placing a plate of mole next to her grandfather's photo. Photorealistic, warm candlelight, copal smoke drifting through the air, intense orange and purple tones, cultural-documentary style. Warm, respectful, nostalgic mood.
A narrator says: "Every element of the ofrenda guides those who return home."
Ambient: crickets and a softly strummed jarana.
