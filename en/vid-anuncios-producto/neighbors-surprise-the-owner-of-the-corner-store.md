Static wide shot facing the freshly painted yellow and turquoise facade of a small corner grocery store, with the name "Lupita's Market" hand-lettered above the door. About twenty neighbors holding brushes and wearing paint-splattered overalls wait in silence. The metal shutter rolls up and the owner, a woman in her seventies in an apron, steps out, stops, covers her mouth with her hands, and cries as the neighbors applaud and a child hands her a bouquet. Photorealistic, soft morning light, community-documentary style. Generous, touching mood.
