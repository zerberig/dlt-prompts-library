Locked-off high-angle time-lapse from a rooftop looking down on an empty lot between houses in a neighborhood.
0–3 s: neighbors of all ages zip around building raised wooden beds and filling them with soil.
3–6 s: days pass with shifting light and rain; plants sprout and spread until the lot is covered in green, with tomatoes, squash, and sunflowers.
6–8 s: the footage returns to normal speed as neighbors lift baskets full of harvest.
Photorealistic, colors moving from brown to deep green, documentary style. Community-minded, hopeful mood.
SFX: sped-up laughter and tools thudding.
Ambient: birdsong and a cheerful folk song with guitar.
