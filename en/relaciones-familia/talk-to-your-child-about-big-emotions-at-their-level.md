Context: My child is [your child's age] years old and has been struggling lately with [emotion]. A recent situation: [recent situation].
Goal: Talk calmly about what they feel so they learn to name the emotion, know it's valid, and have healthy ways to express it.
Deliverable: 1) A model dialogue between parent and child about that situation, 10 to 15 exchanges, with notes on why each response works. 2) 3 phrases to avoid and what to say instead. 3) 3 activities of 5–10 minutes to practice during the week (a game, drawing, breathing, or a story). 4) What to do in the moment if the situation happens again.
Criteria: Age-appropriate language, short sentences, and examples from their world. Grounded in respectful parenting and emotional-regulation principles: connect first, then guide. Include when it would be a good idea to see the pediatrician or a child mental health professional.
Format: The dialogue labeled "Parent" and "Child" with notes in parentheses; everything else as lists. 600 words max.
