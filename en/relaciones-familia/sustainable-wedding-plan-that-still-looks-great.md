Deliverable: For each area (venue, invitations, decor and flowers, food and drinks, attire, transportation, favors, and waste management): 2–3 sustainable alternatives, their estimated impact (high, medium, or low), whether they cost more or less than the conventional option, and how visible they are to guests. Format: One table per area with the columns Alternative, Impact, Cost vs. conventional, and Visibility; then the vendor questions and a top 5 of actions with the best impact-to-cost ratio.
