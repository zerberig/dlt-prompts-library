Context: I'm a wedding planner offering [type of service you offer] (full planning, day-of coordination, or destination weddings). Clients usually book me [typical planning lead time] ahead, and I work mainly in [city or region].
Goal: A standard checklist to use for every wedding so nothing slips through, which I can partly share with clients.
Deliverable: 1) A checklist by phase (booking, design, vendors, logistics, final month, final week, wedding day, and wrap-up) with dates relative to the wedding (for example, "−90 days"), owner (planner, couple, or vendor), and status. 2) The documents and confirmations I should have in writing at each phase. 3) The day-of emergency kit. 4) Post-wedding wrap-up tasks (payments, returns, review request, and delivery of materials).
Criteria: Adapted to my region: if you have web search, check common permits or paperwork and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Concrete, actionable tasks that can be copied into a spreadsheet or task manager.
Format: One table per phase with the columns Task, Relative date, Owner, and Notes; the kit and wrap-up as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
