Goal: See a concrete example of how that same conversation could go with better communication skills, so we can practice it ourselves.
Deliverable: 1) The "before" version, rebuilt as a dialogue, with notes marking where communication breaks down (criticism, defensiveness, assumptions, interruptions). 2) The "after" version: same topic, same people, and a constructive ending, with notes naming the technique used in each turn (reflective listening, "I" statements, asking instead of complaining, validating). 3) 4 bridge phrases to use in real life. 4) A 10-minute exercise to practice the dialogue out loud.
Criteria: Natural dialogue, the way people actually talk, not a self-help book. In the "after" version both people improve, not just one. If the situation involves serious disrespect, control, or fear, say so clearly and suggest professional support.
Format: Dialogues labeled "Person A" and "Person B" with notes in parentheses; everything else as lists. 700 words max.
