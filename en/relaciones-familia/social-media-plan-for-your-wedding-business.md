Context: I run a [type of business] business (photography, wedding planning, catering, bridal wear, venue, music, etc.) in [city or region]. My ideal client is [ideal client] and I mainly use [social platforms you use].
Goal: Couples planning their wedding discover me, trust my work, and request a quote.
Deliverable: 1) 4 content pillars with the reasoning for each (for example, portfolio, behind the scenes, tips for couples, and real testimonials). 2) A 4-week calendar with a realistic posting frequency per platform, format (reel, carousel, story, or short video), a concrete idea, and a call to action. 3) 10 content ideas that answer couples' real questions. 4) How to ask couples for permission to use their photos and how to ask them for genuine reviews. 5) 3 metrics to review each month.
Criteria: Ideas I can produce with my current team. If you have web search, check current wedding-industry trends and formats on my platforms and flag any claim about reach or algorithms as an estimate. Testimonials only from real clients, with their permission.
Format: The pillars as a list; the calendar in a table with the columns Week, Day, Platform, Format, Idea, and Call to action; the rest as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
