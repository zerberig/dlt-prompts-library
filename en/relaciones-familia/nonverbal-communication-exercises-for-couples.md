Context: My partner and I sometimes misread each other's gestures, tone, or silences, especially in [situations where we misread each other]. We have [available time] a week to practice.
Goal: Read each other's signals better and make our body language and tone match what we mean.
Deliverable: 1) A short guide to key nonverbal cues (eye contact, posture, tone, distance, facial expressions) and why the same cue can mean different things depending on the person and culture. 2) 6 exercises to do together, each with duration, instructions, and questions to discuss afterward (for example, mirroring, talking through tone only, or revisiting a past argument). 3) A simple agreement to ask "what did you mean?" instead of assuming.
Criteria: Present body language as a clue rather than an exact science, because treating it as certain leads to misreadings; the point is to ask and check. Keep the exercises light and even fun.
Format: Sections with headings; the exercises as a numbered list.
