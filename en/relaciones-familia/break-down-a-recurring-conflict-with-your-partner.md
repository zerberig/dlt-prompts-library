Context: My partner and I keep arguing about [the topic of the argument]. It usually goes like this: [how it usually unfolds] (who starts, what gets said, how it ends). What I think each of us needs: [what you think each of you needs].
Goal: Stop having the same fight and reach an agreement that respects what matters to each of us, without hurting the relationship.
Deliverable: 1) The pattern that keeps repeating, explained without blaming anyone (trigger, each person's reaction, and escalation). 2) The underlying needs behind each position, including my partner's perspective as they might experience it. 3) 3 options for an agreement or compromise, with pros and cons. 4) How to bring up the conversation: the timing, the opening line, and 3 repair phrases if it gets tense. 5) The communication mistakes we're most likely to make and how to avoid them.
Criteria: Balanced: if my version seems one-sided, point it out tactfully. Techniques grounded in recognized principles of couple conflict management. If the conflict involves control, threats, or violence, say so clearly and recommend professional help instead of looking for a compromise. Mention when couples therapy would make sense.
Format: Numbered sections, bullets, and sample phrases in quotes. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
