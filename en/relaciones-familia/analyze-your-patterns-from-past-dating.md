Context: These are my recent dating and relationship experiences: [summary of my experiences] (how we met, what attracted me, how it developed, how it ended, and how I felt). What I'm looking for now: [what I'm looking for now].
Goal: Identify the patterns that repeat on my side, so I can make better choices and not repeat what didn't work.
Deliverable: 1) Patterns you see in the kind of people I choose, how I communicate, how quickly I get invested, the signals I missed, and how things end. 2) For each pattern, the concrete evidence from my examples. 3) The strengths that also show up. 4) Three reflection questions to answer in writing. 5) Five practical changes for my next dates, ordered by impact.
Criteria: Be honest and direct, even if I won't like reading some of it, but don't judge. Separate what's visible in my examples from what's a hypothesis. Don't make psychological diagnoses; if the patterns point to deeper issues, suggest I work on them with a therapist.
Format: Sections with headings; the patterns in a table with the columns Pattern, Evidence, and What to try differently.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
