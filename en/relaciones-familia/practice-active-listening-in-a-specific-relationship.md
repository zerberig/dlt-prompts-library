Goal: Help that person feel heard and understood, and improve our conversations.
Deliverable: 1) The 6 most useful active listening techniques for my case, each with what it is, an example phrase, and the common mistake it fixes. 2) Three realistic scenarios with that person showing a typical unhelpful response and one with active listening. 3) A 14-day challenge with a short daily practice and how to notice my progress.
Criteria: Natural examples, with phrases that sound like real conversation rather than a textbook. Adapt the tone to the type of relationship.
Format: Sections with headings; the scenarios in a table with the columns Situation, Unhelpful response, and Active listening response.
