Context: We're [couple's names] and we're getting married on [date and venue]. The wedding style is [wedding style], and for invitations we prefer [print, digital, or both].
Goal: A consistent visual identity for every piece that we can hand to a designer or build ourselves in a design tool.
Deliverable: 1) 2 distinct visual concepts, each with a name, a 4–5 color palette in HEX, a pairing of 2 fonts (with free alternatives), an illustration or ornament style, and a monogram idea using our initials. 2) The list of pieces in the suite (save the date, invitation, details card, RSVP, a digital version for messaging apps, and the website) with suggested format and dimensions. 3) The main invitation wording in a formal tone and a relaxed one.
Criteria: The concept should reflect the wedding style and look good both in print and on a phone screen. The practical information (date, time, venue, dress code, and RSVP) should be easy to find at a glance.
Format: Each concept as a section with bullets; the pieces in a table with the columns Piece, Format, Dimensions, and Content; the wording in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
