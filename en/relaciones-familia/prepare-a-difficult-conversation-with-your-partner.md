Context: I need to talk with my partner about [topic to discuss]. What I need or want to achieve: [what I need]. When we get into topics like this, my partner tends to [how my partner usually reacts].
Goal: Have the conversation without it becoming a fight, express how I feel, and find common ground.
Deliverable: 1) Preparation: how to clarify my need, choose the time and place, and define what's non-negotiable for me. 2) An opening line and 3 "I" statements to express how I feel without blaming. 3) What to do if things heat up: agreed-upon breaks, how to pick it back up, and techniques to calm myself down. 4) Questions to understand their point of view and reach concrete agreements. 5) How to wrap up and follow up over the next few days.
Criteria: A respectful approach that also sets clear boundaries. If the topic involves violence, fear, or control, prioritize my safety and recommend I seek professional support before the conversation.
Format: A 5-phase guide with bullets and example phrases in quotes.
