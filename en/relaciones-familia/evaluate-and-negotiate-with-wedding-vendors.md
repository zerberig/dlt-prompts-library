Goal: I want to choose using clear criteria and negotiate terms that let me hire the vendor I like most without going over budget.
Deliverable: 1) An evaluation table with 6-8 weighted criteria for comparing quotes. 2) The 10 questions I should ask before signing. 3) Red flags in contracts and quotes (hidden fees, cancellation policies, deposits). 4) Realistic negotiation tactics for this type of vendor: adjusting the package, date, or time slot, or paying in installments. 5) A short, friendly email asking for a discount or payment plan from a vendor I love who is over my budget.
Criteria: Favor tactics that keep a good relationship with the vendor, since they'll be working on the wedding day. If you have web search, use current price ranges for my area and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections with headings; the table with the columns Criterion, Weight, and What to check.
