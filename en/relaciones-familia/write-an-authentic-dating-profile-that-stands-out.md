Context: I'm putting together my profile on [dating app]. Here's what I want you to know about me: [about you] (age, what I do, hobbies, endearing quirks, what a normal Sunday looks like). What I'm looking for: [what you're looking for].
Goal: I want a profile that sounds like me, attracts compatible people, and gives them something to talk about from the first message.
Deliverable: 3 bio versions in different tones (playful, warm, and direct), 5 answers to typical profile prompts, and a list of 6 recommended photos with what each one should show.
Criteria: Use concrete details instead of clichés like "I love to travel and laugh", because details invite people to write. Everything must be true and based on what I told you, with no exaggeration. Respect the app's character limit if you know it.
Format: A heading for each section; each bio 300 characters max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
