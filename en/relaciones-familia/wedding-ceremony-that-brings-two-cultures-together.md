Context: We're getting married and come from different cultures or traditions: [each partner's culture or tradition]. The ceremony will be [type of ceremony] (civil, religious, or symbolic), and what we most want to convey is [what matters most to you to convey].
Goal: A ceremony that honors both families, that guests from both sides understand and enjoy, and that feels like ours.
Deliverable: 1) 4–6 rituals or traditions from each culture that could be included, with their origin, meaning, and how they're performed. 2) A proposed ceremony order that combines them, with the length of each moment. 3) Ideas for explaining the rituals to guests (printed program, officiant narration, a bilingual version if relevant). 4) New symbolic rituals we could create with elements from both cultures.
Criteria: Explain each tradition accurately and respectfully; if a ritual has religious meaning or special requirements, say so and suggest checking with family or the officiant. Flag anything you can't back up with a source. The full ceremony should run 30 to 45 minutes.
Format: The rituals in a table with the columns Culture, Ritual, Meaning, and How it's done; the ceremony order as a numbered list with minutes.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
