Context: We're hosting a [wedding style or theme] wedding and want a [number of courses]-course menu. Our guests have these restrictions: [your guests' restrictions] (for example, vegetarian, vegan, celiac, nut allergy, halal or kosher, kids). Budget per person: [budget per person].
Goal: Everyone eats well and feels included, without the menu turning into a separate list of special plates.
Deliverable: 1) A main menu where as many dishes as possible work for several diets at once. 2) Specific substitutions for each restriction. 3) An allergen matrix by dish. 4) Drink options with and without alcohol. 5) 8 questions for the caterer about cross-contamination, labeling, and service.
Criteria: Dishes that match the wedding's style and are realistic to serve to many guests at the same time. Include a reminder that severe allergies must be confirmed with the caterer and with each guest.
Format: The menu by course with a name and short description for each dish; an allergen table with the columns Dish, Gluten, Dairy, Nuts, Egg, Shellfish, and Suitable for; then the drinks and the questions as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
