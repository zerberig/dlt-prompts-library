Context: My partner and I have been together for [time together]. What works well between us: [what works well]. What we want to improve: [what you want to improve] (communication, handling arguments, quality time, sharing chores).
Goal: A practical plan we can follow together to communicate better and work through disagreements without hurting each other.
Deliverable: A 4-week plan with one focus per week (listening, expressing needs, resolving conflict, and quality time). Each week includes a 20-30 minute exercise to do together, a 5-minute daily habit, a short role-play script based on a common conflict, and questions to review how it went.
Criteria: Grounded in research-backed couples therapy approaches, explained in everyday language. Exercises that don't cost money. Include a note on when it makes sense to see a couples therapist.
Format: One section per week with bullets.
