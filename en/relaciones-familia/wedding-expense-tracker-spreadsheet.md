Context: We've already set aside [total budget and currency] for the wedding, and I need to track actual spending in [tool] (Google Sheets or Excel).
Goal: Know at any moment how much we've spent, how much is left to pay, and when each payment is due, so there are no surprises in the final month.
Deliverable: 1) An expenses tab with the columns category, item, vendor, estimated, actual, difference, deposit paid, balance, payment due date, status, and who pays. 2) A suggested list of categories and subcategories (venue, catering, attire, music, photography, decor, etc.). 3) A summary tab with totals by category, percentage of budget used, and an alert when a category goes over its estimate. 4) The exact formulas and conditional formatting rules for overdue or upcoming payments.
Criteria: It should take less than 10 minutes a week to keep up to date.
Format: Describe each tab with a column table and put the formulas in code blocks.
