Context: My partner and I want to communicate better. My usual communication style: [how you communicate]. My partner's: [how your partner communicates]. The situations where we clash most: [situations you struggle with].
Goal: We want a practical plan to follow together for 4 weeks to achieve this: [what you want to achieve]. We'll both read it, so it needs to be fair to each of us and not single anyone out as the problem.
Deliverable: 1) A short read on where our styles fit together and where they collide. 2) A 4-week plan: each week with a focus, one concrete agreement, a 15–20 minute exercise to do together, and a question for a weekly check-in. 3) A "pause plan" for when a conversation heats up: an agreed signal, how long to pause, and how to come back to it.
Criteria: Exercises that are realistic for a week full of work and fatigue. Sample phrases in natural language, not textbook lines. Ground the techniques in recognized couple-communication principles and explain in one line why each works. If anything I describe suggests control, violence, or fear, say so clearly and recommend professional help instead of communication techniques. Note when couples therapy would be worth considering.
Format: The read in one paragraph, a table with the columns Week, Focus, Agreement, Exercise, and Check-in question, and the pause plan as bullets. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
