Context: I'm setting up my profile on [dating app]. About me: [facts about you] (work, hobbies, values, what my ideal weekend looks like). Some concrete details that describe me: [anecdotes or specific details]. I'm looking for: [what you want in a partner].
Goal: An honest profile that shows my personality and sense of humor, attracts people with similar values, and makes it easy for them to message me.
Deliverable: 1) A main bio in 2 versions (one more playful, one warmer). 2) Answers to 5 typical app prompts ("a perfect Sunday", "you'll win me over if…", etc.), each with a hook to start a conversation. 3) Recommendations for choosing and ordering 5–6 photos based on what I told you.
Criteria: Show, don't list: concrete details instead of adjectives ("I learned to bake bread and I'm still losing fights with my sourdough starter" instead of "I'm creative"). Everything true and nothing inflated, because the profile has to hold up on the first date. Positive tone, no lists of what I don't want. Respect the app's character limits; if you don't know them, keep the bio under 300 characters.
Format: Sections with headings; bios and answers ready to copy.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
