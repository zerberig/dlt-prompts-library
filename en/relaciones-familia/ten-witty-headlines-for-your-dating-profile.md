Context: I use [dating app] and want a headline or opening line for my profile. Some traits and fun facts about me: [traits or fun facts about you]. I'm looking for: [what you're looking for].
Goal: Make my profile stand out among many similar ones and attract people who are actually compatible with me, not just anyone.
Deliverable: 10 headlines in 5 tones (witty, warm, direct, self-deprecating, and intriguing), 2 per tone. For each: the impression it gives, who it tends to attract, and an easy question it invites people to answer.
Criteria: Based only on what I told you, because a headline that exaggerates sets up false expectations for the first date. 15 words max each. No worn-out lines like "love to travel and laugh" and no negativity ("no drama"). Respect the app's character limit for that field; if you don't know it, say so.
Format: A table with the columns Tone, Headline, Impression, Attracts, and Question it invites. Close with your top 3 and why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
