Context: I have a first date in [your city]. I'm into [your interests], and I know the other person likes [what you know about their interests]. Budget: [budget].
Goal: Suggest a plan that feels thoughtful, lets us talk, and is comfortable for someone who doesn't know me yet.
Deliverable: 5 date ideas, each with: the plan in one sentence, why it works for a first date, approximate length, estimated cost, a plan B if it rains or the place is closed, and a short message to suggest it.
Criteria: Mix low-key and active options. All in public places, easy to get to, with a natural exit if the date isn't clicking (for example, coffee that can turn into a walk). Avoid plans where you can't talk, like the movies. If you have web search, suggest real types of venues or events in my city and flag any prices or hours you can't confirm as estimates.
Format: A numbered list with each idea's fields as bullets. 450 words max.
