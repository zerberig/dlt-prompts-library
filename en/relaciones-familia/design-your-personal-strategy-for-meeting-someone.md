Context: I'm looking for [what I'm looking for] (a serious relationship, something casual, not sure yet). My situation: [my current situation] (age, city, work, kids, whether I just moved, etc.). My strengths and challenges when meeting people: [my strengths and challenges]. I have [available time] a week for this.
Goal: Stop relying on chance and have a realistic plan to meet compatible people without burning out.
Deliverable: 1) My non-negotiable and nice-to-have values, written from what I told you. 2) A recommended mix of channels (apps, activities, social circle, events) with the reason for each based on my profile. 3) An 8-week plan with concrete weekly actions and a time limit for apps. 4) How to work on two of the challenges I mentioned, with practical exercises. 5) Three signals to assess whether the plan is working and when to adjust it.
Criteria: It should fit my schedule and energy; prioritize the quality of encounters over quantity. The approach is about being authentic, so leave out manipulation techniques or seduction "games". If I mention intense anxiety or recent grief, suggest professional support.
Format: Sections with headings; the plan as a table with the columns Week, Action, and Goal.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
