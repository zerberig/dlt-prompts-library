Context: I lead or belong to [type of team] of [size and setup] (in-person, remote, or hybrid). Current problems: [current problems] (misunderstandings, unproductive meetings, lost information, friction between people). We use [tools you use].
Goal: Improve collaboration and reduce friction with clear communication agreements the team will actually adopt.
Deliverable: 1) A brief diagnosis with the likely causes of each problem. 2) Team agreements: which channel to use for what, expected response times, and how to document decisions. 3) Meeting rituals with purpose, length, and format (weekly check-in, retrospective, one-on-ones). 4) A protocol for interpersonal conflict, with example phrases for giving and receiving feedback. 5) A 30-day rollout plan and 3 simple indicators to tell whether it's working.
Criteria: Proposals proportional to the team's size, without unnecessary bureaucracy. Include how to present the agreements so the team builds them together instead of feeling they're imposed.
Format: Sections with headings; the channel agreements in a table with the columns Message type, Channel, and Response time.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
