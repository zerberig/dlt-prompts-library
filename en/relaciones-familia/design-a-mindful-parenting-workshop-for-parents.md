Context: I'm going to run a mindful parenting workshop for [workshop audience]. It will be [number and length of sessions], for parents of children aged [children's ages], delivered [in person or online].
Goal: Participants should leave with concrete practices for managing their own stress and responding more calmly to their kids, not just theory.
Deliverable: 1) The workshop's learning objectives. 2) An agenda for each session with timings: opening, key concept, guided exercise, case study, and take-home practice. 3) 3 realistic cases (for example, a public meltdown, a fight between siblings, or the morning routine) with group discussion questions. 4) A simple way to measure progress at the start and the end.
Criteria: Short exercises a busy parent can repeat at home in 5 minutes. Grounded in recognized mindfulness and parenting approaches; flag any figure you can't back up with a source as an estimate. Make clear that the workshop is educational and doesn't replace psychological care, and include how to refer anyone who needs it to a professional.
Format: Numbered sections; each session's agenda in a table with the columns Block, Minutes, Activity, and Materials. 900 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
