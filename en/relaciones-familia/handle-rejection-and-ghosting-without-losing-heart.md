Context: I've been dating for a while and this happened: [what happened] (they stopped replying, I was turned down after several dates, nothing seems to move forward). I'm feeling [how I feel].
Goal: Process it in a healthy way and keep meeting people without shutting down or taking it personally.
Deliverable: 1) A realistic read on what happened, with possible explanations that have nothing to do with me. 2) What to do in the next 48 hours so I don't keep ruminating. 3) An optional message to close the loop with dignity, if it applies, and when it's better not to send it. 4) Habits to protect my self-esteem and mood while dating (app breaks, boundaries, activities outside of dating). 5) How to say "no, thanks" respectfully myself, so I don't do to others what I don't like.
Criteria: Empathetic, direct tone, no empty motivational lines. If what I describe sounds like persistent sadness or anxiety that affects my daily life, suggest I talk to a mental health professional.
Format: Numbered sections, 500 words max.
