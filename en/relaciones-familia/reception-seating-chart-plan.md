Context: We have [guest count] guests and [available tables] (number, shape, and capacity of each table). Here's the list by group: [guest list by group] (each side of the family, friends, work, children). Considerations: [special considerations] (people who shouldn't sit together, older guests, reduced mobility, parents with babies).
Goal: A layout where everyone is comfortable, the head table and dance floor work well, and there's no tension.
Deliverable: 1) Table-by-table assignment with names. 2) Suggested placement of each table relative to the head table, dance floor, speakers, restrooms, and exit. 3) Brief reasoning for the most delicate decisions. 4) A plan B for last-minute cancellations or added guests.
Criteria: Mix groups with shared interests so they'll talk, keep couples and families with children together, and seat guests who need quiet away from the speakers.
Format: A table with the columns Table, Guests, Location, and Note; then the lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
