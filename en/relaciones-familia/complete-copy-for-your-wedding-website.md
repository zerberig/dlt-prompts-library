Context: We're building our wedding website with a site builder or template. We are [couple's names]. Event details: [event details] (date, times, venues, dress code, accommodations, registry, RSVP deadline). Our story in a few lines: [our story]. Desired tone: [tone].
Goal: Guests should find everything they need without having to message us with questions.
Deliverable: Copy for each page: Home, Our Story, Schedule, Travel and Accommodations, Registry, RSVP (with the form questions: attendance, plus-one, meal choice, dietary restrictions, and a song request), and FAQ (8-10 questions about kids, parking, weather, timing, and photos during the ceremony).
Criteria: Short copy that's easy to read on a phone, with practical information first. Mention gifts or contributions naturally, without sounding demanding.
Format: A heading for each page with its copy below; the form fields as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
