Context: My partner and I have been together [time together and current situation]. What's going well: [what's going well]. What worries me: [what worries you].
Goal: Understand where our relationship stands and what to focus on first, based on what research on couples has found.
Deliverable: 1) A short 12-statement questionnaire rated 1 to 5, grouped by area (connection and affection, handling conflict, trust, mutual support, and shared goals). 2) How to interpret the results for each area. 3) Based on what I told you, the 3 habits most worth working on, each with the psychological principle behind it, how to practice it this week, and how to tell if it's working.
Criteria: Cite the principle or line of research behind each habit and flag anything you can't back up with a source as an estimate. Warm tone, no diagnoses or labels about my partner. If anything I describe suggests violence or control, say so clearly and recommend professional help. Note when couples therapy would be worth it.
Format: The questionnaire in a table with the columns Area and Statement, the interpretation as bullets, and the 3 habits as short sections. 650 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
