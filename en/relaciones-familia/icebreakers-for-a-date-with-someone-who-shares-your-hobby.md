Context: I have a first date with someone who shares my love of [shared hobby]. What I know about them: [what you know about them].
Goal: Start with what we have in common to break the ice, then take the conversation beyond the hobby so we actually get to know each other.
Deliverable: 9 questions on three levels: 3 openers (light, about the hobby), 3 bridges (from the hobby to their life: why they started, what it gives them), and 3 deeper ones (values, dreams, what matters to them). For each, a follow-up question and a quick idea of what I could share so it doesn't feel like an interview.
Criteria: Open, natural questions that don't sound like a questionnaire. Leave out topics that put people on the defensive on a first date (exes, income, plans for kids). Include 2 signals that it's time to change the subject.
Format: Three sections with headings and a numbered list in each. 400 words max.
