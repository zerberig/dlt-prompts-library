Context: We have [total budget and currency] for our wedding with [guest count] guests in [city or region]. Our priorities, from most to least important, are: [priorities] (for example: food, photography, music, venue).
Goal: Decide how much to allocate to each category before requesting quotes, so we don't commit the budget to the first vendor we like.
Deliverable: 1) Allocation by category (venue, catering, drinks, photo and video, music, attire, flowers and decor, invitations, ceremony, transportation, favors, and contingency) as a percentage and amount. 2) The resulting cost per guest. 3) Three scenarios: base, tight (15% less), and one with a priority category boosted, showing where the money comes from. 4) An 8-10% contingency fund with clear rules for using it. 5) The 10 highest-impact savings ideas for our case, with the estimated savings for each.
Criteria: Respect our priorities: cut what matters least to us first. If you have web search, use current reference prices for our region. Flag any figure you can't back up with a source as an estimate.
Format: A main table with the columns Category, %, Amount, and Notes; then the scenarios and savings ideas as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
