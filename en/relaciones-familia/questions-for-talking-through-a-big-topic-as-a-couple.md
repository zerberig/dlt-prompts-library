Context: My partner and I want to talk in depth about [topic to discuss] (money, kids, where to live, in-laws, careers, or emotional intimacy). We want to do it now because [why now].
Goal: Truly understand each other's perspective, experiences, and fears about the topic before making decisions.
Deliverable: 1) 15 open-ended questions on three levels: personal history (where each person's view comes from), present (how we experience it today), and future (what we want and what worries us). 2) Simple ground rules for the conversation (taking turns, listening without interrupting, pausing if it gets tense). 3) A ritual for repeating it: length, frequency, and how to close each session. 4) How to note down agreements without turning it into a negotiation.
Criteria: Questions that invite stories and feelings, not yes-or-no answers. No questions that sound like a reproach. If the topic sparks serious conflict or involves health or legal matters, suggest professional support.
Format: The questions as numbered lists by level; everything else as bullets. 500 words max.
