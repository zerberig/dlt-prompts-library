Context: I'm marrying [your partner's name] and want to write my own vows, far from the traditional formulas. Moments in our story that matter: [moments from your story]. What I admire most about my partner: [what you admire about your partner]. The tone I'm after: [tone you want] (emotional, funny, understated, or a mix).
Goal: To read them at the ceremony in front of our guests and have them sound like me, not a template.
Deliverable: A complete vow draft, 2 alternative endings, and 5 extra concrete promises I can pick from or swap in.
Criteria: Three-part structure: one specific memory, what this person means to me, and the promises. Mix deep promises with a few everyday or funny ones ("I promise to always leave you the last cookie"). Skip clichés like "soulmate" or "my everything". Short sentences that are easy to say out loud, and no jokes that would embarrass my partner in front of their family.
Format: The draft in short paragraphs, 1 to 2 minutes when read aloud (about 150–250 words), with pause cues in parentheses; then the endings and promises as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
