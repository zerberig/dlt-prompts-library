Context: We're getting married in [city or region] on [wedding date], with a total budget of [total budget and currency]. We need vendors for: [vendor categories] (photography, video, florist, catering, music, cake, makeup).
Goal: Build a comparison base to decide whom to ask for a quote first.
Deliverable: 1) A spreadsheet ready to copy, with the columns category, vendor, contact, website or social profile, price range, what's included, notable reviews, availability, status (to contact, quoted, meeting, booked), and notes. 2) If you have web search, 3-5 vendors per category in our area with public information and the source for each data point; if not, leave the rows as a template with one example of how to fill them in. 3) A reference price range per category based on our budget.
Criteria: Include only names, contacts, and reviews that come from a source you can cite, because made-up data would waste our time. Flag any price you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: A table I can paste into Google Sheets or Excel, followed by the price ranges as a list.
