Context: I want to offer [type of clients] (online stores, marketplace sellers, small brands) an AI-powered product photo enhancement and production service. My tools: [tools you use]. My market is in [country or region].
Goal: A clear offer I can sell and deliver at consistent quality.
Deliverable: 1) Three packages (basic, standard, premium) with what each includes: cleanup and white background, generated lifestyle scenes, social media variations, number of images, and turnaround. 2) Price ranges in [currency], flagged as estimates, with the factors that move them. 3) The end-to-end workflow: brief, photos the client must send, editing in Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2, review, and delivery. 4) A quality-control checklist. 5) A short blurb to pitch the service.
Criteria: The product must look exactly as it is (accurate color, shape, size, and labels), because a misleading photo drives returns and can break marketplace rules. If you have web search, check the current image requirements of the main marketplaces in my region and benchmark prices, and flag anything that's an estimate.
Format: A packages table, then sections with bullets. 600 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
