Context: I want to turn a photo of [who is in the photo] into an illustrated character in a [illustration style] style (stylized 3D animation, clean-line cartoon, fine-line anime, children's watercolor, or other) to use for [use of the character].
Goal: A character people recognize as that person, which I can reuse across several images with the same look.
Deliverable: 1) An instruction for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 that transforms the uploaded photo into the chosen style, describing the technique (line type, shading, head-to-body proportion, palette) and asking to keep the features that make the person recognizable. 2) A sheet for the resulting character: exaggerated key features, hairstyle, signature outfit, and palette. 3) Five pose or scene prompts (waving, working, celebrating, thoughtful, full body on a plain white background) that use the character image as reference.
Criteria: Describe the style through techniques, not studio names, franchises, or copyrighted characters, because models may refuse it and you couldn't use it commercially. Use only photos of yourself or of people who've given permission.
Format: Numbered sections and prompts in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
