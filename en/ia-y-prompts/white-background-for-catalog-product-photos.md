Context: I sell [type of products] on [marketplace or store] and my photos were taken in [photo conditions] (kitchen table, window light, cluttered background).
Goal: Pure white background photos, consistent across the whole catalog and true to the real product.
Deliverable: 1) The marketplace's main-image requirements (background, how much of the frame the product fills, minimum size); if you have web search, verify them and flag anything you can't confirm. 2) Manual method: cutout with the pen tool or automatic selection, pure white background, a soft resting shadow, centering, and even margins. 3) An instruction for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 that replaces the background with plain pure white and a soft contact shadow under the product, ending with "keep the product exactly the same: shape, color, labels, and proportions". 4) How to batch-process the whole catalog to the same size and framing.
Criteria: Keep the product's color and details identical, because any difference leads to returns and negative reviews.
Format: Numbered sections with steps; the AI instruction in one block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
