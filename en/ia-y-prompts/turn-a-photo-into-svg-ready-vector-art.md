Context: I have a photo of [what the photo shows] and need to turn it into vector art for [final use] (logo, T-shirt, sticker, signage). I want to use [number of colors] colors at most.
Goal: A scalable, clean vector file that's easy to print.
Deliverable: 1) An instruction for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 that converts the uploaded photo into a flat-color illustration with clean outlines, simplified shapes, and a limited color count, isolated on a plain white background with a centered composition. 2) Steps to vectorize it with auto-trace in a vector program or an online tool, with the key settings (threshold, number of colors, curve smoothing). 3) Cleanup afterward: joining paths, removing extra nodes, checking gaps and minimum line weights for the print method. 4) When it's better to redraw by hand instead of tracing.
Criteria: Large shapes that stay readable at small sizes, because fine detail gets lost in tracing and printing. If the photo includes third-party logos or characters, warn me that I can't use them commercially.
Format: Sections with numbered steps; the AI instruction in one block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
