Context: I want to apply a [creative effect] (selective color, double exposure, particle dispersion, vignette, or radial filter) to this photo: [photo description]. I edit in [your editing software].
Goal: The effect should guide the eye to the subject and strengthen the image's story, not just decorate it.
Deliverable: 1) In 2-3 sentences, what the effect does and whether it suits this photo; if not, suggest a better alternative. 2) Manual steps with non-destructive adjustment layers and masks, with starting values. 3) An instruction for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 that applies the same effect to the uploaded photo, precise about which area changes, ending with "keep everything else the same (face, pose, framing, lighting)". 4) Three common mistakes that give the effect away and how to avoid them.
Criteria: Plain language for an intermediate user. Use the tool names from my software; if you don't know them, use generic terms.
Format: Numbered sections with headings; the AI instruction in a separate block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
