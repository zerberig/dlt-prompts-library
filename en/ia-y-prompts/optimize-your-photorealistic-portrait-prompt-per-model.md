Context: Here's my draft prompt for a photorealistic portrait: [your portrait prompt or idea]. I'll use it for [use of the portrait].
Goal: The portrait should look like a real photo taken by a professional, not a generic AI image.
Deliverable: 1) A 3-5 point diagnosis of what the draft is missing. 2) An improved version for Nano Banana 2 and GPT Image 2: one flowing 60-120 word paragraph with scene and background, subject, wardrobe, expression, a specific lens and aperture (for example, 85mm at f/1.8), light type and direction, and real textures (pores, flyaway hair, creased fabric). 3) The Midjourney V8 version ending with --ar and --raw. 4) Three variations that each change a single factor (light, lens, or mood) so I can compare results.
Criteria: Describe the scene in natural language instead of piling on words like "8K, ultra realistic, masterpiece", because current models respond better to concrete descriptions. Phrase exclusions positively ("naturally textured skin" instead of "no plastic skin"). If the portrait is of a real person, remind me to use only photos they've agreed to.
Format: Diagnosis as bullets and each prompt in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
