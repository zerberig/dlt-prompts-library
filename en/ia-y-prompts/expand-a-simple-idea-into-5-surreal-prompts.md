Context: Here's my seed idea: [seed idea]. I want to explore surreal versions for [use of the images].
Goal: Get five clearly different directions to pick the strongest, not five variations of the same thing.
Deliverable: Five prompts, each built on a different surreal strategy: scale shift, material transformation, impossible physics, fusion of two worlds, and a visual metaphor for an emotion. For each: the strategy name, the prompt, one line on what makes it memorable, and the model you'd use (Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, or Midjourney V8).
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Each prompt has one clear central image and a defined composition (framing, point of view, light), because surrealism works best when the impossible looks believable. Describe the technique (hyper-detailed dreamlike oil painting, hard-lit studio photography, cut-paper collage). For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: Five numbered blocks with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
