Context: I'm designing a creature for [type of project] (video game, tabletop RPG, novel, short film). Starting point: a [type of creature] tied to [element or theme].
Goal: An original, coherent creature whose design stays the same across artists or across generated images.
Deliverable: 1) Sheet: a readable silhouette, size compared to a person, anatomy and how it moves, skin or scales, a 3-4 color palette, abilities and how they look, habitat and behavior, and a 3-sentence backstory. 2) Concept-art prompts: a front, side, and back turnaround on a neutral gray background; a close-up of the head and textures; and the creature in its habitat with a human figure for scale. 3) A video prompt for Veo 3.1 or Kling 3.0, about 8 seconds, where the creature performs a signature action, with SFX and Ambient lines.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. The shape should read from the silhouette alone. Base the anatomy on real animals and name the reference, because that makes it believable. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: Sheet as a list and prompts in numbered blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
