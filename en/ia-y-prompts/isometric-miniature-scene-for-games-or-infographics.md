Context: I need an isometric scene of [scene theme] (futuristic neighborhood, fantasy village, office, factory) for [use of the scene] (game background, infographic, web illustration).
Goal: A miniature scene full of little stories that still reads well at small sizes.
Deliverable: 1) A scene plan: 4-6 zones with buildings or spaces, vehicles, characters, and one action in each zone. 2) A main prompt: an isometric diorama seen from about 30 degrees, on a floating base with cut-away edges or isolated on a plain color background, with its style (stylized 3D render with matte materials, pixel art, or flat vector illustration), palette, and soft light. 3) Two variants: daytime and nighttime with the lights on. 4) If it's for an infographic, how to leave clean space for labels I'll add later.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Ask for strict isometric perspective and consistent scale across elements, because that's the first thing to break. Keep the image free of text; labels get added later in an editor. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: Plan as a list and prompts in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
