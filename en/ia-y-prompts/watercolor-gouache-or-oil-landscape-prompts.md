Context: I want to generate a painting of [type of landscape] at [time of day] that looks like [painting medium] (watercolor, gouache, oil, pastel, or other). I'll use it for [use of the image].
Goal: The image should look genuinely hand-painted, not like a filtered photo.
Deliverable: 1) Three prompts with different compositions (wide shot with a low horizon, close-up with one hero element, elevated view), each with its dominant palette and atmosphere. 2) A list of 6-8 medium-specific terms that image models understand well (for watercolor, for example: wet-on-wet, granulation, blooming edges, visible cold-press cotton paper texture), with what each one adds.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Mention the surface and how the pigment behaves, because that is what makes a painting convincing. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: The three prompts in numbered blocks with a short title; then the list of terms.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
