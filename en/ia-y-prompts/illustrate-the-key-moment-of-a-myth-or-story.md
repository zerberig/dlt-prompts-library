Context: I want to illustrate [myth or story passage] for [use of the illustration] (children's book, cover, class, poster) in a [illustration style] style.
Goal: An image that tells the story on its own and conveys its tension.
Deliverable: 1) Three candidate moments from the story, plus which one you'd illustrate and why. 2) For that moment: characters, what each one is doing, setting, composition (where the eye goes, lines of tension, point of view), and the core emotion. 3) An image prompt with that composition. 4) A video prompt for Veo 3.1 or Kling 3.0, about 8 seconds, showing the instant before the climax, with a single camera move and SFX and Ambient lines.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Respect the essential elements of the version I gave you; if the myth has several versions, tell me which one you used. For kids, keep the tension exciting without being violent. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: Numbered sections; prompts in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
