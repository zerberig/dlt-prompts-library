Context: I run social media for [your brand or project], which talks to [target audience] on [platform]. The brand's tone is [brand tone].
Goal: Post memes that spark conversation and feel like the brand's own, without relying on viral templates built on other people's images.
Deliverable: Six meme concepts based on real situations my audience lives. For each: the situation it captures, the meme text (12 words max, in one or two blocks), a prompt for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 that creates an original image with the text built in, and the recommended aspect ratio for the platform.
Criteria: Humor that laughs at the situation, not at people or groups. Original scenes instead of stills from movies, shows, or existing memes, because they may be copyrighted. Describe each scene as a natural paragraph and put the literal text in quotes with typeface, color, and position; short text renders better. If you have web search, you can check current humor formats on the platform, without presenting trends as hard facts.
Format: A table with the columns Situation, Text, and Aspect ratio; then the six prompts numbered in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
