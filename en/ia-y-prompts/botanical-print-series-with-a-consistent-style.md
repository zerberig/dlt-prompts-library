Context: I want a series of botanical plates of these plants: [list of plants], in a [illustration style] style (scientific watercolor, hand-tinted ink engraving, flat gouache), for [use of the series] (prints to sell, decor, educational material).
Goal: Every plate should look like part of the same collection.
Deliverable: 1) A fixed style template: paper and texture, technique, palette, composition (plant centered with root, flower, leaf, and seed details), margins, and label position. 2) One prompt per plant that repeats the template word for word and changes only the botanical description: leaf shape, flower color and structure, fruit, and distinguishing traits. 3) The label for each plate with the common name in English and the scientific name, in quotes, in a small serif typeface centered below the plant.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Check that the botanical traits are accurate for each species and flag any doubt, because mistakes show in educational material and in products for sale. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize. Suggested ratio: 3:4 or 2:3 for framing.
Format: Template as a list and prompts numbered in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
