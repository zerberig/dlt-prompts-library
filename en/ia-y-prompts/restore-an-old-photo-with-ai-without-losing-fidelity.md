Context: I have an old photo of [photo description] with this damage: [visible damage] (scratches, tears, stains, fading, low resolution). I want to [desired result] (just repair it, repair and colorize it, or prep it for a large print).
Goal: Recover the image without the AI changing faces or inventing details that never existed.
Deliverable: 1) How to digitize it properly: scan resolution, how to photograph it if I don't have a scanner, and how to avoid glare. 2) A staged restoration order, with an instruction for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 at each stage: repair physical damage, recover contrast and tone, and colorize if I asked, using colors plausible for the era. Each instruction ends with "keep the facial features, expression, and original composition exactly as they are". 3) What to check after each stage (eyes, hands, text, backgrounds) and when manual retouching is the better call. 4) How to archive the original and the restored version.
Criteria: One stage per instruction, because asking for everything at once increases alterations. Make clear that added color is an interpretation.
Format: Numbered sections; instructions in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
