Context: I'm creating a sci-fi city for [project] (novel, game, cover, video). Starting point: [city idea] (for example, a floating city over a toxic sea, or a vertical metropolis inside a canyon).
Goal: Images of a city that feels lived-in and coherent, not a generic pile of neon skyscrapers.
Deliverable: 1) A mini world bible: climate, energy source, who lives up top and who lives below, transportation, building materials, and one unique everyday detail. 2) Three image prompts from different viewpoints: aerial view at dawn, street level at night, and a view from inside a home looking out at the city. 3) A video prompt for Veo 3.1 or Kling 3.0, about 8 seconds: a drone shot descending between the buildings, with SFX and Ambient lines.
Criteria: Every visual element should come from the world bible, because that's what makes the city feel real. Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: Bible as a short list and prompts in numbered blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
