Context: I want to design a fictional object that's used for [object's function], with a [aesthetic] look (steampunk, retro-futurist, solarpunk, biomechanical), for [project].
Goal: The object should look like it could actually work: every visible part needs a purpose.
Deliverable: 1) Design sheet: name, how it works in 3 sentences, main parts and materials (brass, leather, glass, wood, ceramic), size, how it's held or used, and wear from use. 2) Three image prompts: a studio product shot on a neutral background, an exploded view with parts floating in alignment, and the object in use within its world. 3) A video prompt for Veo 3.1 or Kling 3.0, about 8 seconds, where the object powers on as the camera slowly pushes in, with mechanical SFX and Ambient lines.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Describe each material with its finish (for example, polished brass with green patina at the joints), because that's what makes the design believable. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: Sheet as a list and prompts in numbered blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
