Context: I need images of [dish] for [use of the image] (printed menu, delivery app, social media). The mood I'm after is [mood or style] (rustic, elegant, fresh, homestyle).
Goal: An appetizing image that shows the dish as it's actually served.
Deliverable: 1) A styling concept: tableware, surface, props, visible garnishes, camera angle (overhead, 45 degrees, or eye level), and why it suits this dish. 2) Two photorealistic photography prompts with scene, dish, texture details (sauce sheen, steam, crumbs, condensation droplets), lens and aperture (for example, 100mm macro at f/4), light direction, and mood. 3) The recommended aspect ratio for each use. 4) If I have a real photo of the dish, an instruction for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 that improves the plating and light and ends with "keep the dish, portions, and ingredients the same".
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Portion and ingredients must match what's served, because misleading photos lead to complaints and can break delivery platforms' rules. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize.
Format: Concept as bullets and prompts in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
