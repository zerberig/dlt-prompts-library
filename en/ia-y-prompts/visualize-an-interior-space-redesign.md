Context: I want to redesign [type of space] of roughly [approximate dimensions] in a [desired style] style. I want to keep: [elements to keep]. I can upload a photo of the current space.
Goal: See how it would look before buying or hiring anything, with a proposal that can actually be built.
Deliverable: 1) A 3-4 sentence concept and a materials palette (floors, walls, textiles, woods, metals, and plants) with descriptive color names. 2) A furniture layout with approximate measurements that fit the space. 3) If I upload a photo, an instruction for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 that redesigns the space and ends with "keep the architecture the same: windows, doors, ceiling, and camera angle". If not, a photorealistic interior photography prompt with a 24mm wide-angle lens, natural window light, and real textures. 4) A prompt for a mood board with material samples on a neutral background.
Criteria: Proposals that work in my space; flag any measurement or price as an estimate. Write the prompts as descriptive paragraphs, not keyword lists.
Format: Numbered sections; prompts in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
