Context: I work at [your company or plant], where we make [product]. The most common defects are [common defects]. Today inspection works like this: [how inspection is done today]. We have [available resources] (budget, cameras, IT team, historical data).
Goal: A plan for an AI visual inspection pilot that shows whether it's worth scaling, with clear success criteria.
Deliverable: 1) Problem statement and measurable success criteria: detection rate, acceptable false rejects, line speed. 2) Image capture: camera, lens, controlled lighting, positioning, and how to capture reference images. 3) Data: how many good and defective images to collect, how to label them, and what to do if defects are rare (anomaly detection trained only on good parts). 4) Solution options: a no-code commercial platform, a cloud service, or an in-house model, with pros and cons for my resources. 5) An 8-12 week timeline with owners. 6) Risks and how to integrate the system with operators without taking the final call away from them.
Criteria: Favor practical decisions over theory. Flag as an estimate any cost, timeline, or expected performance you can't back up with a source.
Format: Sections with headings, a comparison table of options, and the timeline as a table. 600-900 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
