Context: I sell [your digital product] (prompt pack, templates, course, guide) to [target audience] on [sales platform]. I have [available resources] (budget, weekly time, current audience).
Goal: A [plan length] plan that gets me to steady sales and is realistic for my resources.
Deliverable: 1) A one-sentence value proposition and three messaging angles. 2) A staged plan (pre-launch, launch, and ongoing sales) with weekly actions per channel: organic content, email, collaborations, and ads if the budget allows. 3) A free sample or lead magnet that proves the product's value. 4) Pricing and offer structure, with ranges flagged as estimates. 5) 3-5 metrics to review weekly and what decision to make based on each.
Criteria: Use real social proof (customer results shared with permission, product samples); no invented testimonials or fake scarcity, because they destroy trust and break many platforms' rules. If you have web search, check the platform's current fees and policies.
Format: A table for the weekly plan and short sections for the rest. 700 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
