Context: I'm working on [type of project] (video game, 3D scene, textile print, or other) and need textures of [material or surface] with a [visual style] finish.
Goal: I want textures that repeat with no visible seams, generated with Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, or Midjourney V8, and ready to prep in my software.
Deliverable: 1) Five prompts with useful variations (clean, worn, wet, with moss or grime, stylized), each with a flat top-down view, even diffuse lighting, and an explicit seamless repeating pattern. 2) A short procedure to check and fix the seams (offset the image 50% on each axis and repair with cloning or an AI edit). 3) Which maps are worth deriving (normal, roughness, height) and with what kind of tool.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Explain in one line why flat lighting matters: directional shadows give the repetition away. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar 1:1.
Format: Numbered prompts in blocks; then the procedure as numbered steps and the maps as a short list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
