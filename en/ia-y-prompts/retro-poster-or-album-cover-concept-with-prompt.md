Context: I need to design [type of piece] (poster, album cover, flyer, or other) with a retro look about [theme or message]. The text that must appear is: [text on the piece].
Goal: I want a clear design concept and a prompt I can paste straight into an image generator to get a usable first version.
Deliverable: 1) Three short concepts, each inspired by a different era (for example, 1930s travel poster, 1960s psychedelia, 1980s sci-fi), with composition, type hierarchy, a 4-5 color palette, and print textures (halftones, grain, aged paper). 2) For the concept you think is strongest, a prompt for Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2 and its Midjourney V8 version.
Criteria: Write each image prompt as one flowing paragraph (scene, subject, details, technique or camera and lighting, mood), not a keyword list, which current models handle worse; phrase exclusions positively and describe techniques instead of naming living artists, brands, or copyrighted characters. Any text inside the image goes in quotes, kept short (1-4 words per block), with typeface, color, and position. For Midjourney V8, append only --ar and, when useful, --raw or --stylize. Recommend an aspect ratio based on how the piece will be used.
Format: The three concepts in a table (Era, Composition, Typography, Palette, Textures), then the prompts in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
