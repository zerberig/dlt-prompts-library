Context: I run an Etsy shop selling [your niche or type of products] with about [number of products] items. These are my current sections: [your current sections].
Goal: I want visitors to quickly find what they're looking for in my shop, and I want section names to use terms my customers actually type.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis of my current sections: duplicates, vague names, and sections with too few or too many items. 2) A new structure of 5 to 10 sections, with the recommended organizing principle (by product type, occasion, collection, or recipient) and why it fits my shop. 3) For each section: a short name, 2 alternatives, which items go in it, and the order it should appear in. 4) How to reflect this structure in each listing's category and title.
Criteria: Short, descriptive, easy-to-scan names; skip clever names nobody would search for. If you have web search, check Etsy's current limits on section count and characters.
Format: The diagnosis in bullets and the new structure in a table with the columns Section, Alternatives, Items included, and Order.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
