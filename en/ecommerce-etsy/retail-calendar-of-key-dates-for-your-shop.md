Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform], and my customers are mainly in [countries you sell to]. My production and shipping time is [production and shipping time].
Goal: I want to plan ahead for the dates that drive sales in my category so inventory, designs, photos, and ads are ready on time.
Deliverable: 1) A 12-month calendar of the dates relevant to my product and markets: holidays, gift-giving seasons, national and international sales events, and seasons specific to my niche. 2) For each date: relevance to my product (high, medium, or low), the deadline to publish listings, the last order date for on-time delivery, and a promotion or collection idea. 3) The 3 periods where I should focus my effort, and why.
Criteria: Keep in mind that some dates differ by country (Mother's Day, for example) or from year to year; use web search if you have it to confirm next year's dates, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Work the deadlines backward from my production and shipping time.
Format: A table with the columns Month, Date or event, Countries, Relevance, Publish by, Last order date, and Promotion idea; then the 3 priority periods as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
