Context: I sell [your product], and I want the description to convey what it feels like to own and use it. These are its real details: [materials, textures, colors, scents, or sounds].
Goal: Help buyers picture themselves with the product and feel a personal connection before they buy.
Deliverable: A 120-200 word description and 3 short titles in the same voice.
Criteria: Engage at least three senses with concrete, true details about the product; skip empty adjectives like "amazing" or "magical". If you add urgency, base it only on real facts (season, production time, gift shipping cutoff), because fake urgency costs trust. Close with the practical details: dimensions, materials, and care.
Format: The description in short paragraphs, followed by the practical details as bullets and the 3 titles as a numbered list.
