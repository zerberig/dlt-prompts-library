Context: I run an Etsy shop selling [type of products]. These are the search terms from my stats for the last [time period]: [search terms with visits and sales]. These are the current tags on my main listings: [current tags].
Goal: Find out which tags bring visitors who buy, which add nothing, and what to replace them with.
Deliverable: 1) My tags sorted into keep, tweak, or replace, with the reason for each decision. 2) Patterns between the terms that convert and those that don't. 3) New tags based on terms that already bring me traffic but that I don't have as tags. 4) A plan to test the changes and measure them over 30 days.
Criteria: Base every recommendation on the data I gave you and tell me when the sample is too small to conclude anything. Keep in mind that Etsy listings don't use hashtags: tags, the title, and attributes are what connect with searches. Don't claim how Etsy weighs each element.
Format: A table with the columns Tag, Visits, Sales, Decision, and Reason; then bullets for points 2 to 4.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
