Context: I'm going to collaborate with [content creators] to promote [your product or shop]. The campaign's main goal is [campaign goal] (sales, followers, shop traffic, or a launch) and the budget is [budget].
Goal: Know, with data, which collaborations worked, what each sale cost me, and who is worth working with again.
Deliverable: 1) The metrics to track, grouped into reach, engagement, traffic, and sales, with the formula for each. 2) How to attribute results to each creator: unique discount codes, tracked links, or UTM parameters. 3) A weekly tracking template. 4) Criteria for deciding whether to repeat, adjust, or end each collaboration.
Criteria: Prioritize metrics tied to the goal and explain why vanity metrics (followers, likes) aren't enough. Include how to calculate cost per sale and return on investment. Remember that paid posts must be disclosed as advertising under each social network's and country's rules.
Format: Numbered sections; the template as a table with the columns Creator, Date, Reach, Clicks, Attributed sales, Cost, and Cost per sale.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
