Context: I sell [your product], and these are its real sustainable attributes: [sustainable attributes and certifications] (recycled or natural materials, origin, durability, packaging, whether it's vintage or secondhand, current certifications).
Goal: I want to attract buyers who care about responsible shopping without making vague or exaggerated claims that cost me credibility.
Deliverable: 1) An 80-120 word description paragraph focused on sustainability. 2) 5 attribute bullets, each with the concrete fact that backs it up. 3) A list of phrases I should avoid given my current information, and why (for example, "100% eco-friendly" or "zero impact" without evidence). 4) A short post announcing the product or line on social media.
Criteria: Every claim should be specific and verifiable ("60% recycled cotton" rather than "planet-friendly"). Mention certifications only if they're in my details. If the product is vintage or secondhand, explain the benefit of extending its life without invented impact figures, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Four numbered sections with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
