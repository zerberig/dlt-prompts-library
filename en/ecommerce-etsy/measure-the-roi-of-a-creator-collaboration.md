Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform] and I'm planning a collaboration with [type of content creator] that will cost me [collaboration cost] between fees, product sent, and my time.
Goal: I want to know whether the collaboration actually pays for itself and decide with data whether to repeat it, instead of going by likes.
Deliverable:
1. How to set up tracking before the post goes live: an exclusive discount code, links with tracking parameters, and what to check in the shop's stats.
2. The metrics to log at three levels: reach, interest (visits, favorites, new followers), and sales.
3. A return formula based on net margin that includes every cost, with an illustrative numeric example.
4. How to estimate the indirect effect (sales without the code, followers who buy later) without overstating it.
5. Criteria for deciding whether to repeat, adjust, or drop the collaboration.
Criteria: The post must be clearly disclosed as paid or gifted according to each platform's and country's rules; if you have web search, confirm those rules. Flag any benchmark figure you can't back with a source as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections; a tracking table with the columns Metric, How it's measured, Result, and Notes.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
