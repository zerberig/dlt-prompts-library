Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy. I think different profiles buy it: [customer segments] (for example, gift buyers, people buying for themselves, businesses, or international buyers).
Goal: I want my listings to show up in each segment's own searches, because each one describes the same product in different words.
Deliverable: For each segment: 1) how they'd search for the product (8 to 10 likely search phrases), 2) 2 title variants that speak their language, 3) the tags that cover their searches, 4) an opening line for the description, and 5) a tailored call to action. At the end, say whether one listing that combines segments or separate listings works better, and why.
Criteria: Search phrases should sound like something a person would actually type, including regional differences if I sell internationally. Don't invent search volumes; if you have web search, use it to validate terms and flag the rest as estimates.
Format: One section per segment with the five parts as bullets; the final recommendation in one paragraph.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
