Context: I sell handmade [type of material or supply] on Etsy (for example, hand-dyed yarn, beads, handmade paper, or kits). This is the data: [product details] (quantity, dimensions, colors, materials, technique, origin).
Goal: I want a listing that inspires crafters and also gives them the technical information to know whether the material works for their project.
Deliverable: 1) A clear title with the material type, main feature, and use. 2) A description with an inspiring opening (colors, texture, how it's made and who makes it), exact specifications, which projects it suits and which it doesn't, and usage or care tips. 3) 5 project ideas using this material, for the description or social media. 4) A note about natural variation between batches, so buyers know what to expect.
Criteria: Specifications must be exact, because supply buyers calculate quantities for their projects. If you mention the relaxing side of crafting, describe the experience without making health claims.
Format: Title; description with short headings and bullets, 180-280 words; project ideas as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
