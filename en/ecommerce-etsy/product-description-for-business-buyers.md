Context: I sell [your product] (for example, logo stamps, custom labels, or templates) to small businesses such as [type of client business]. The customization options are: [customization options].
Goal: I want a description that shows someone buying for their business how my product helps them look more professional or save time, and that clears up any doubts about the customization process.
Deliverable: A ready-to-paste description with: an opening tied to a real problem that kind of business has, 3 to 5 concrete benefits for their operations, how customization works step by step (what they send me, design proof, included revisions, timelines), and bulk order options if they apply. Add 3 FAQs from business buyers with answers.
Criteria: Talk about results the customer will recognize in their day-to-day work, without promising sales increases in numbers. Professional and approachable language.
Format: Short headings and bullets; 200-300 words not counting the FAQs.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
