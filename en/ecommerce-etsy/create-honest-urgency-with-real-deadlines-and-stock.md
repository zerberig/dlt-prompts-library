Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform]. The real reason it makes sense to buy soon is: [real reason for urgency] (a shipping cutoff before a holiday, a limited run with a set number of pieces, a deadline for custom orders, seasonal pricing with an end date).
Goal: I want to encourage people to buy now using only genuine urgency, because fake countdowns and invented scarcity erode trust and can break platform policies and consumer protection laws.
Deliverable: 1) 5 lines for the listing description. 2) 3 versions of text for the shop announcement or a banner. 3) 3 short social media posts. 4) A reminder to send on the last day. Every piece should mention the concrete fact (date, quantity, or lead time).
Criteria: A friendly tone that informs rather than pressures; explain what the customer gains by deciding in time (it arrives before the date, they lock in their customization). No lines like "almost gone" unless it's true.
Format: Four sections with headings and bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
