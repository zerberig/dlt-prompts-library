Context: I run an Etsy shop selling [niche or product type] with these products: [list of products]. These are the keywords I've collected: [list of keywords].
Goal: I want to organize the shop into sections that help buyers find what they want quickly, using the same words they search with.
Deliverable: 1) A proposal of 5 to 10 shop sections with a name, which products belong in each, and which of my keywords fit it. 2) For each product, the most specific Etsy category that applies. 3) The keywords that don't fit any section, with a suggestion of where to use them instead (title, tags, or description).
Criteria: Short, clear section names a buyer understands with no context. Group by how people shop (occasion, recipient, style, or product type), not by how I make things. If you have web search, check Etsy's current limit on sections and characters per name; don't claim section names affect ranking unless you can confirm it.
Format: A table with the columns Section, Products, Assigned keywords, and Grouping logic; then the category list and the unassigned keywords.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
