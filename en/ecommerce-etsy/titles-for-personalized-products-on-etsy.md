Context: I sell [your personalizable product] on Etsy. Buyers can customize: [personalization options] (name, initials, date, color, or phrase). They mostly buy it for [buying occasions].
Goal: Titles that make it clear from search results that the product is personalized and that match how people search for this kind of gift.
Deliverable: 1) A reusable title template with the order of elements (product, personalization type, occasion, recipient, and style). 2) 8 titles applying the template with different combinations. 3) 5 search phrases my buyers are likely to use, so I can check them in Etsy's search bar.
Criteria: Put the words that describe what the product is at the start, because that's what shoppers read first when scanning results. Use natural variants ("personalized", "custom name", "engraved") without repeating words. Stay within Etsy's current character limit and avoid all caps and filler symbols.
Format: The template on one line; then the titles as a numbered list with their character counts and, at the end, the search phrases.
