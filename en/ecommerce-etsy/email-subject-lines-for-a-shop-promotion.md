Context: I run a shop selling [your product or shop], and I'm emailing my subscribers to announce [your promotion] (discount, dates, and terms). My subscribers are [type of subscribers].
Goal: I want more people to open the email without sounding like spam or promising something the email doesn't deliver.
Deliverable: 10 subject lines with preview text, grouped into 5 approaches: curiosity, direct benefit, real urgency, personalization, and a friendly tone. At the end, recommend 2 for an A/B test and explain what I'd learn from each.
Criteria: Subject lines of 45 characters max so they display in full on a phone, and preview text of 90 characters max that adds to the subject line instead of repeating it. Urgency must be based on the promotion's real end date. Avoid all caps, stacked exclamation marks, and typical spam words, because they hurt deliverability.
Format: A table with the columns Approach, Subject line, Preview text, and Characters.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
