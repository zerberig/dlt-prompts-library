Context: I sell [product type] on [platforms you sell on] and my team is [how your team is set up] (just me, two people, a small team). What takes up most of my time today is [tasks that eat most of your time].
Goal: I want a set of tools that saves me hours every week without paying for subscriptions I won't use.
Deliverable: Recommendations organized by need: keyword and trend research, product design and photos, listing management, customer service, inventory and shipping, bookkeeping, and tracking platform policy changes. For each need, one free option and one paid option, what it solves, and roughly how much time it could save me. Close with the 3 tools you'd start with in my case.
Criteria: If you have web search, check that each tool is still active, its current price, and whether it integrates with my platforms; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Prefer tools that connect through official integrations, because tools that scrape data without authorization can break the platform's terms.
Format: A table with the columns Need, Tool, Free or paid, Approximate price, and What I'd use it for; then the final recommendation as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
