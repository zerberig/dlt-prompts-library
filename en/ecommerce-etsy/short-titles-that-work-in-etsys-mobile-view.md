Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy. Its main keyword is [main keyword] and its most eye-catching quality is [most appealing trait].
Goal: Short titles that display in full on a phone and make shoppers stop in search results.
Deliverable: 5 titles under 60 characters, each from a different angle: sensory, occasion, style, benefit, and recipient. For the one you recommend, an extended version that adds useful information after the first 60 characters.
Criteria: The main keyword goes at the start of every title, because it's the first thing shoppers see and it confirms they found what they were looking for. Use concrete, evocative words instead of empty adjectives like "amazing" or "perfect". No all caps, emojis, or filler symbols.
Format: A numbered list with the angle and character count for each title.
