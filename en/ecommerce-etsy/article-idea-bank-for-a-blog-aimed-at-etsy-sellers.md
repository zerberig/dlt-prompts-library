Context: I run a blog for sellers on Etsy and other marketplaces for handmade or print-on-demand products. My readers are [your readers' profile] and I want to focus on [main topic] (for example, time management, customer service, creative process, or email marketing).
Goal: Build a bank of article ideas that solve real problems for these sellers and bring me recurring traffic.
Deliverable: 12 article ideas grouped into 3 content pillars. For each: working title, the problem it solves, 3-4 points it would cover, and the type of piece (practical guide, real case, checklist, downloadable template, or interview).
Criteria: Specific, actionable ideas, not generic topics like "tips to sell more". Include at least 2 ideas on seller well-being (priorities, boundaries, avoiding burnout) and 2 behind-the-scenes ideas. If you mention Etsy features or policies, use web search if you have it to confirm they're still current.
Format: A table with the columns Pillar, Title, Problem it solves, Key points, and Type of piece. Then recommend in 2-3 sentences which 3 articles to start with and why.
