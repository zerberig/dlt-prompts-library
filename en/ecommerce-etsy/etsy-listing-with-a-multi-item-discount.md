Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy as part of a line of [your shop's product types]. I want to offer this multi-item discount: [discount structure] (for example, 10% off 2 pieces and 15% off 3).
Goal: A complete listing that sells the product on its own and also encourages adding more pieces to the cart.
Deliverable: 1) Listing title. 2) Full description: opening, benefits, details and specs, and a section explaining the discount with an example of how much you save. 3) 3 suggested combinations with other products in the line and who each is for. 4) A short shop announcement communicating the promotion.
Criteria: The discount should be explained in one easy line and framed as a perk for gift buyers and collectors, not as pressure. If you have web search, confirm how quantity-based promotions are currently set up on Etsy and tell me where to turn them on, because the copy has to match the actual promotion.
Format: Sections with headings; the description at 180-250 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
