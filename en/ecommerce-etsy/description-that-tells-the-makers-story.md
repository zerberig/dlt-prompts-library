Context: I sell handmade [your product]. Who makes it and how they started: [maker or workshop story]. The inspiration behind this design: [design inspiration].
Goal: I want buyers to feel they're taking home a piece with a name and an origin, not a factory product, and for that to justify the price.
Deliverable: A description with three beats: the person or workshop behind the piece, the design's inspiration, and what makes this piece one of a kind (natural variations, time to make, technique). Include a short first-person quote from the maker, drafted from the details I gave you, and note that I should confirm it with them before publishing.
Criteria: Concrete and human: names of places, techniques, and materials rather than adjectives like "unique" or "exclusive". Present natural variations as part of the value, so buyers know what to expect. Don't invent biographical details I didn't give you.
Format: A 120-180 word story; then bullets with dimensions, materials, time to make, and care, plus a call to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
