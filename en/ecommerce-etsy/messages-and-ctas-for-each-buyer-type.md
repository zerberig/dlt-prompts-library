Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform]. I think my main buyers are [buyer types], and the biggest difference between them is why they buy.
Goal: I want to tailor my value proposition and calls to action to each buyer type, so my listings, shop page, and social media speak to what each one cares about.
Deliverable:
1. A short profile of each buyer: main motivation, underlying desire, typical doubt or objection, and when they buy.
2. For each one, a one-sentence value proposition stating what makes my product special for that person.
3. 3 calls to action per profile: for the listing, for social media, and for email.
4. One website or description section per profile (headline plus 2 or 3 sentences) focused on the benefit they care about most.
5. What message to use when I don't know which profile is reading.
Criteria: Base the profiles on the information I gave you and label anything you're assuming as a hypothesis, so I can validate it with real customers. Every message must be specific to its profile: if a line works for everyone, it isn't specific enough.
Format: One section per profile with parts 1 to 4; part 5 at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
