Context: My shop sells [your product or shop]. These are pieces of writing that feel like my brand: [samples of your brand's writing]. And this is a description I want to bring in line: [current description].
Goal: I want all my descriptions, ads, and posts to sound like the same brand so customers recognize it and trust it.
Deliverable: 1) A mini voice guide: 3 personality traits with an explanation of each, words and expressions we use, ones we avoid, and how we address the customer (level of formality, humor). 2) The description rewritten using the guide. 3) A before-and-after table with 3 key changes and the reason for each.
Criteria: Base the guide on real patterns in my samples, not on generic adjectives like "friendly" or "authentic" without defining them. Keep all the practical information from the original description (dimensions, materials, shipping).
Format: Three numbered sections; the guide in bullets, 200 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
