Context: I need to present [your product] to [presentation audience] (a wholesale retailer, a partner, an investor, or a distributor). Key data: [product and business data] (price, margins, sales, reviews, production capacity).
Goal: A presentation that clearly explains the product's value for that audience and ends in a concrete decision: [what you want them to decide].
Deliverable: A script for 8-10 slides. For each: title, main message in one sentence, supporting content (data, suggested image, or chart), and what I'd say while presenting it.
Criteria: Translate every feature into the benefit that audience cares about: for a retailer, margin and turnover; for an investor, growth and scalability; for a partner, complementarity. Use only the data I gave you and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Include a slide with the tough questions they'll likely ask and how to answer them.
Format: A numbered list by slide, with the 4 elements as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
