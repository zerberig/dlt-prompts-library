Context: I sell [your product] through my shop [your shop name]. Use and care details: [use and care instructions].
Goal: I want to include a card in every package that improves the unboxing experience, prevents questions and returns caused by misuse, and inspires new ways to use the product.
Deliverable: Copy for a two-sided card. Front: a 2-3 sentence welcome and thank-you in the brand's voice. Back: a quick-start guide in 3-5 steps, care tips in bullets, safety warnings if relevant, 2 inspiring ideas for using or pairing the product, and a closing line inviting customers to share a photo on social media and leave an honest review.
Criteria: It has to fit on a postcard-size card: 60 words max on the front and 120 on the back. Clear, friendly language. The review request must be neutral, with no incentives in exchange for positive ratings, because that breaks most marketplaces' policies.
Format: Front and Back as separate sections, with visual hierarchy suggestions (what goes large and what goes small).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
