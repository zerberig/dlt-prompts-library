Context: I'm putting together a [holiday or season] gift guide for [guide audience], featuring these products from my shop: [products with price and one key feature].
Goal: A guide that helps people decide quickly what to give, which I can use as a shop section, blog post, or email.
Deliverable: 1) 3 title options for the guide. 2) A 60-90 word introduction. 3) A 25-40 word blurb per product saying who it's perfect for. 4) A way to group the products (by recipient, budget, or personality) and why you recommend it. 5) A closing with shipping deadlines and personalization options.
Criteria: Write for the gift giver: what the recipient will feel and why this is a thoughtful gift, not a generic one. Warm, festive tone without overdoing it. Use only the prices and details I gave you, and leave shipping deadlines marked as «to be completed».
Format: Sections with headings; the blurbs as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
