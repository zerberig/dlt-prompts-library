Context: I sell, or want to sell, [your product] in the [your niche] niche through [marketplace or channels]. Some competitors I know of: [known competitors].
Goal: I want to understand the market and find real gaps (products, prices, styles, or underserved customers) where I can stand out.
Deliverable: 1) A map of 6-10 relevant competitors, with their value proposition, price range, strengths, weaknesses, and what their reviews say. 2) Market patterns: what almost everyone does and what almost no one offers. 3) 5 differentiation opportunities, each with the supporting evidence, the effort required, and the risk. 4) Your pick of the 2 opportunities with the best effort-to-impact ratio and the first steps to test them.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to review current public listings, prices, and reviews; cite where each data point comes from, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source, such as sales or market share. Base the opportunities on evidence, not assumptions.
Format: A competitor table with the columns Competitor, Positioning, Price, Strengths, Weaknesses, and Reviews; then numbered sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
