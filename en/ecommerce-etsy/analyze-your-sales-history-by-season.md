Context: I sell [product type] on [marketplace]. Here is my exported sales data for the last [data period]: [sales data] (date, product, quantity, price, and, if I have them, views and reviews).
Goal: I want to understand what sells best and when, so I can decide launch dates, inventory, and pricing for next season based on my own history instead of hunches.
Deliverable:
1. Seasonal patterns: the highest and lowest sales months or weeks, by product or category.
2. Products that are growing, flat, or dependent on a single date.
3. The best time to launch or relaunch each line, accounting for production time and shipping lead times.
4. Pricing observations: whether price changes, discounts, and volume are related.
5. 5 concrete actions for next season, ranked by impact.
Criteria: Separate what the data shows from your interpretation; if the volume is small or the period short, warn that conclusions are tentative. If you can run code, include a chart of sales by month. If you use outside market data, say so and cite the source.
Format: A 3-line executive summary first, then numbered sections, with tables wherever they help comparison.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
