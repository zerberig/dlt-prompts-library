Context: I sell vintage items on Etsy. This piece is [description of the vintage piece] and this is what I know about its origin and condition: [era, provenance, and condition].
Goal: I want a description that conveys the piece's charm and history to collectors and retro lovers while being fully transparent about its condition, to avoid returns and bad reviews.
Deliverable: 1) An evocative opening paragraph about the era and style. 2) What I know about its provenance, separating what's confirmed from what's likely. 3) A detailed condition report: wear, repairs, marks, and missing parts. 4) Measurements and how to use or display it today. 5) A short blurb introducing the full collection in its shop section.
Criteria: Don't attribute dates, makers, or collector value that aren't in my data; use "likely" or "in the style of" when you're unsure, because false attribution is one of the main causes of complaints in vintage. If you have web search, check the minimum age Etsy requires for an item to count as vintage.
Format: Sections with short headings; 180-280 words not counting the collection blurb.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
