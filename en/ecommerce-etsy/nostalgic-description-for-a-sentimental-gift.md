Context: I handmake [your product] (for example, family recipe books, albums, keepsake boxes, or pieces personalized with names and dates). My buyers usually give it for [gifting occasions].
Goal: I want a description that evokes nostalgia and warmth and gets buyers thinking about their own memories and the person they're giving it to.
Deliverable: 1) A full description that opens with a familiar everyday scene (grandma's kitchen, a box of old photos), connects it to the product, and explains how personalization works. 2) 2 alternate openings built on other memories. 3) A short gift card line the buyer can choose.
Criteria: Use concrete, universal sensory details without getting sappy. Keep the practical information (dimensions, materials, personalization, lead times) clear and easy to find after the emotional part. Don't invent a family story of mine that I haven't told you.
Format: A 180-250 word description with short paragraphs and bullets for the practical details; the openings and gift card line separately.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
