Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform]. Besides its main use, it works for: [uses or occasions you know of].
Goal: I want buyers to see every way they can use it, to broaden the audience and better justify the price.
Deliverable: 1) A full description organized by use scenario (at home, as a gift, seasonal, or in a business, as relevant), with one concrete scene for each. 2) Short answers to compatibility or size questions that could limit a use. 3) 3 photo ideas with captions showing different uses. 4) A short social post with the angle "one product, five uses".
Criteria: Include only uses that are realistic and safe for the material and design; if a use needs special care, say so. Practical, enthusiastic tone without exaggeration.
Format: One heading per scenario with 2 or 3 sentences each; total description 180-280 words; sections 2 to 4 as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
