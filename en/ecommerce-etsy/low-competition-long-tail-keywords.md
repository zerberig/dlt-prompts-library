Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy. Its main attributes are [product attributes] (material, style, color, size, recipient, occasion).
Goal: I want to find long, specific search phrases with less competition, used by buyers with clear purchase intent, so my listings show up where I can actually compete.
Deliverable: 1) A matrix that combines attributes to generate 25 to 30 long-tail phrases (for example, "minimalist silver necklace for bridesmaid"). 2) For each phrase: the buyer's likely intent and a qualitative competition estimate (low, medium, or high) with your reasoning. 3) The top 10, noting whether each goes in the title, tags, or description. 4) A method for checking competition myself with Etsy's search bar.
Criteria: Every phrase must honestly describe my product and sound like something a person would type. If you have web search or keyword tool data, use it; if not, make clear that competition is an estimate and don't invent volumes.
Format: A table with the columns Phrase, Intent, Estimated competition, and Where to use it; then section 4 as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
