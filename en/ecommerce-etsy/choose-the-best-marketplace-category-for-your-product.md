Context: I sell [your product] on [marketplace]. It's currently in the [current category] category, and people mostly buy it for [main use or occasion].
Goal: I want to place my product in the category and subcategory that give it the most visibility with buyers ready to purchase, and align the title and tags with that choice.
Deliverable: 1) The 3-4 possible categories or category paths for my product, with pros and cons of each (relevance, competition, and the attributes it unlocks). 2) Your recommendation and why. 3) The attributes I should fill in for that category. 4) Suggested tweaks to the title and tags so they're consistent with the category, without repeating what the category already conveys.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to check the marketplace's current category tree and how top-ranking listings for similar products are categorized; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Prioritize accuracy, because the wrong category can reduce visibility or get the listing flagged.
Format: A comparison table of categories, followed by the recommendation and the tweaks in bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
