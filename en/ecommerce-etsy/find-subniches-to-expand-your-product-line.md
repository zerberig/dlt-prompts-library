Context: My business sells [your current product or line] to [your current customer]. My strengths are [your strengths] (techniques, materials, suppliers, audience, available time).
Goal: I want to identify subniches close to what I already do where I can launch new products with low risk, building on skills I already have.
Deliverable:
1. A step-by-step research process, with the sources I'd use at each stage (marketplace internal search, search trends, communities, competitor reviews).
2. 8 to 10 candidate subniches, each with its buyer, the need it meets, and an example product.
3. An evaluation matrix scoring each from 1 to 5 on demand, competition, fit with my strengths, expected margin, and ease of testing.
4. The 3 top-scoring subniches, each with a low-cost test to validate it in under 30 days.
Criteria: Favor subniches I can test with a few units or a single design. If you have web search, use it to check real demand and competition; flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate, and don't invent search volumes.
Format: Numbered sections matching the deliverable; the matrix as a table sorted from highest to lowest score.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
