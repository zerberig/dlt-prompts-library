Context: I sell [your products] on [your shop or platform]. These are the products I could combine, with their current price and total cost per unit: [products, prices, and costs].
Goal: I want bundles that raise order value and stay profitable, with real savings that are easy for the customer to understand.
Deliverable:
1. 3 to 5 bundle proposals with clear logic (complementary items, by occasion, starter kit, or full collection).
2. For each bundle: suggested price, discount versus buying separately, resulting margin, and why customers would see it as good value.
3. A comparison of bundle pricing strategies (fixed price, quantity tiers, "build your own bundle") and when each makes sense.
4. How to adjust prices for season and inventory without confusing customers.
5. How to present the savings in the listing.
Criteria: Calculate margins with full costs, including platform fees and shipping, and show your math. If you have web search, check current fees and prices of similar competitor bundles; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. The "bought separately" reference price must be the real one, not inflated.
Format: Numbered sections; the proposals in a table with the columns Bundle, Contents, Price separately, Bundle price, Customer savings, and Margin.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
