Context: I sell [your product] on [marketplace]. These are the 3 best-selling competitor listings in my category: [competitor listings] (title, price, description, number of reviews, and a few negative reviews). This is my listing: [your listing].
Goal: Understand what my competitors do well and where I can beat them, without copying them.
Deliverable: 1) An element-by-element analysis: title, photos (based on what I describe), price and shipping, description, variations, and reviews. 2) Recurring complaints in their negative reviews and how my product or listing can solve them. 3) The 5 concrete improvements for my listing, ranked by impact.
Criteria: Separate practices worth adopting because they're the category standard from real opportunities to stand out. Don't suggest copying text, photos, or designs, because it infringes rights and can get the listing removed. If you have web search, use it to fill in missing data and flag as an estimate anything you can't confirm.
Format: A comparison table with the columns Element, Competitor 1, Competitor 2, Competitor 3, My listing, and Opportunity; then points 2 and 3 as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
