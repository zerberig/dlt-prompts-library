Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy in a [your product's style] style, and I want to update my listings for [season or period].
Goal: I want to identify trending keywords and searches that fit my product so I can add them before demand peaks.
Deliverable: 1) A list of 15 to 20 trending or rising terms related to my product, grouped by type (style, occasion, recipient, theme, or color). 2) For each term: why you think it's trending, the source, and how well it fits my product. 3) How to verify them myself using Etsy's search bar, my stats, and trend tools. 4) Where to use the 5 most promising ones (title, tags, attributes, or description).
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and cite the source for each trend; flag anything you can't verify as an estimate. Don't invent search volumes. Drop trendy terms that don't honestly describe my product, because they bring visits that don't convert.
Format: A table with the columns Term, Type, Evidence or source, Fit (high, medium, or low), and Where to use it; then section 3 as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
