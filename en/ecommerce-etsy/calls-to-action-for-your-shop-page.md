Write calls to action for my shop selling [your product or shop], aimed at [target audience]. What makes my shop special: [your differentiator].
Deliverable: 12 calls to action of 12 words max, in four groups of 3: for the shop banner or title, for the shop announcement, for the end of a product description, and for encouraging shoppers to combine several items in one order (sets, combined shipping, or a multi-item discount, only if I offer it).
Criteria: Start with action verbs, focus on the outcome for the customer (how their home will look, how the gift recipient will feel), and use urgency only when there's a real date or limited stock. Warm, direct tone.
Format: Four lists with headings.
