Context: I sell [your product] on [marketplace] (Amazon, Mercado Libre, Etsy, or another). Its features and advantages are: [features and advantages]. The typical buyer is [target audience].
Goal: Benefit bullets that can be read in seconds on a phone and win over someone comparing options.
Deliverable: 5 bullets ready for the listing's key points section, plus an alternative version of the first one.
Criteria: Each bullet opens with a 2-4 word benefit, followed by the feature that backs it up. Order them from most to least important to the buyer. Work in, naturally, the words someone would use to search for this product. Stay within the marketplace's current character limit (if you have web search, check it; if not, keep each bullet under 200 characters). No claims I can't prove, like "the best on the market".
Format: A numbered list with the character count at the end of each bullet.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
