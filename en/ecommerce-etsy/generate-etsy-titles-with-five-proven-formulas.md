Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy. Its main advantage is [unique selling point], and it's made for [target audience].
Goal: I want to test several title styles to find the one that earns the most clicks without losing search visibility.
Deliverable: 10 titles, two for each of these formulas: product + unique selling point; product + who it's for + what need it solves; product + occasion or season; product + material or style; product + personalization option.
Criteria: Put the most important search phrase first, since that's what buyers read first in a results list. Each title should be clear and easy to read, with no repeated words or stacked synonyms. Stay within Etsy's current character limit (check it with web search if you have it).
Format: A table with the columns Formula, Title, and Why it works (one line). At the end, recommend which one to test first and why.
