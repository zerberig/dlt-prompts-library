Context: I'm publishing or improving an Etsy listing for [your product]. It's mostly bought by [target audience] for [main occasion or use].
Goal: Etsy doesn't use hashtags, it uses tags, and I want a repeatable method for choosing the ones that match what people actually search for instead of guessing.
Deliverable: 1) A research method I can repeat for every listing: search bar suggestions, competitor listings, my shop stats, and the searches already bringing me visits. 2) A starter set of tags grouped by search type: what the product is, who it's for, occasion, style, and material. 3) How to tell, after a few weeks, which tags to change.
Criteria: Favor multi-word phrases that describe the product precisely over highly competitive single words. Avoid repeating what the category or attributes already cover, and don't use protected brand or character names. If you have web search, confirm Etsy's current limits on the number of tags and characters.
Format: The method as a numbered list; the tags in a table with the columns Tag, Search type, and Why it works.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
