Context: I sell [your product] and I have these real customer reviews: [real reviews copied verbatim].
Goal: Use these reviews to build trust with new buyers in the product description, photos, and social media.
Deliverable: 1) The 3-5 most useful reviews and why: which answer common questions, which describe a concrete benefit, and which tell a story. 2) A paragraph for the description that works 1-2 excerpts into a short story. 3) 3 ideas for showing them in photos, video, or social posts. 4) A short message asking the customer's permission before featuring their review outside the platform.
Criteria: Quote reviews without changing their meaning and without inventing or blending opinions, because altered or fake reviews mislead buyers and break platform policies and consumer protection laws. You may trim excerpts as long as the meaning holds. Use only the name or initials the customer approves.
Format: Numbered sections with headings.
