Context: I sell, or plan to sell, [your products] on Amazon in [country or Amazon marketplace], using this model: [business model] (private label, resale, handmade, self-fulfilled or fulfilled by Amazon).
Goal: Identify the risks that could cost me money, get my account suspended, or hurt my reputation, and have a plan to reduce them before they happen.
Deliverable:
1. A risk matrix by category: account and policies, inventory and logistics, finances and fees, competition and pricing, intellectual property, reviews and customer service, and product compliance (certifications and labeling).
2. For each risk: likelihood, impact, and 1-2 concrete mitigation actions.
3. The top 5 risks for my case and an action plan for the first 90 days.
4. Warning signs I should monitor every week.
Criteria: If you have web search, check the current policies, fees, and requirements for the marketplace I named and cite your sources; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. For tax, legal, or intellectual property matters, recommend I confirm with a professional.
Format: The matrix as a table with the columns Risk, Category, Likelihood (low, medium, high), Impact (low, medium, high), and Mitigation; everything else as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
