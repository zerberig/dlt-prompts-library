Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform] to [target audience]. My brand's tone is [your brand tone].
Goal: I want varied calls to action for every touchpoint, so I'm not always repeating "buy now" and can guide customers to the next step that makes sense in each place.
Deliverable: 4 calls to action for each of these places: the end of the product description, the shop announcement, a social media post, the post-purchase thank-you message, and the email newsletter. For each place, state the action we're after (buy, favorite, follow the shop, subscribe, leave a review) and why.
Criteria: Short lines (12 words max) with an action verb and a concrete benefit or reason. Review requests must be neutral and incentive-free, because offering something in exchange for reviews breaks most platforms' policies.
Format: A table with the columns Place, Target action, and Calls to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
