Context: I run an Etsy shop selling [niche or product type] and want to get ready for [season or shopping event] to promote [products you'll feature]. I post on [channels you use] and have [time available per week] for content.
Goal: I want a calendar that coordinates listings, social media, email, and collaborations so I reach the season with products ready, visible, and with an audience waiting for them.
Deliverable:
1. An 8-week calendar in three phases: prep (new listings and photos), warm-up (process content and sneak peeks), and sales (launch, reminders, and shipping cutoffs).
2. For each week: what to post on each channel, with topic, format, and call to action.
3. Ideas for one or two collaborations with content creators or complementary shops.
4. How to repurpose each main piece into at least three formats.
Criteria: Scale the volume to the time I have; a plan I can stick to beats an ideal one. If you have web search, confirm the season's key dates and typical shipping cutoffs; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Phase, Channel, Topic, Format, and Call to action; then sections 3 and 4 as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
