Context: I sell [your product]. These are public reviews of similar competitor products: [competitor reviews]. What my product does differently: [how your product differs].
Goal: I want to understand what frustrates and what delights customers in this category, in their own words, so my listing addresses those points.
Deliverable: 1) The 5-7 most frequent pain points or complaints, with approximate frequency and 1-2 verbatim example quotes. 2) What customers praise most, which I need to match at a minimum. 3) A table linking each pain point to how my product solves it, only where my details support it, or to an improvement I could make. 4) A list of listing benefits written in the customers' own vocabulary. 5) A short 120-180 word description that opens with the main pain point.
Criteria: Don't name or disparage competitors in the listing copy. If my product doesn't solve a pain point, say so clearly instead of forcing it.
Format: Five numbered sections; tables for points 1 and 3.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
