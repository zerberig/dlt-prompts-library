Context: I sell [your product]. This is my production process: [stages, techniques, and materials]. Quality checks I perform: [quality checks].
Goal: I want to show buyers the work behind each piece so they understand its quality and price, and trust what they'll receive.
Deliverable: 1) A 150-220 word "How it's made" section that walks through the stages in order, in clear, engaging language. 2) A materials table with the columns Material, What it's used for, and What it adds to quality or durability. 3) A short "Our quality checks" block in bullets. 4) 3 photo or short video ideas to illustrate the process.
Criteria: Explain technical terms in plain words. Highlight only checks and properties that are in my details; don't invent certifications or lab tests.
Format: Four numbered sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
