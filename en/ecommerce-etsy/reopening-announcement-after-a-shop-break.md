Write a reopening announcement for my shop selling [your product or shop] after a break for [reason for the break]. To celebrate, I'm offering [reopening promotion] (discount, dates, and terms). I need three versions: the shop announcement (60-100 words, warm and grateful, mentioning what's new and when orders will ship), a social media post of 50 words max, and an email subject line with preview text for my subscribers. Use only the promotion's real dates and terms, to avoid misunderstandings and complaints, and end each version with a clear invitation to visit the shop.
