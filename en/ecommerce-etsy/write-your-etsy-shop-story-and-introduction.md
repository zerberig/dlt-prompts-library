Context: My Etsy shop is called [your shop name] and sells [your products]. Here's how it started and how I work: [your story, process, and values].
Goal: I want copy for the About section and the shop's front page that connects with [target audience] and explains why it's worth buying from a person rather than a factory.
Deliverable: 1) A short shop headline. 2) An About section of 200-300 words. 3) A 2-3 sentence welcome announcement for the front page.
Criteria: Write in first person with concrete details about my process, not generic lines like "made with love". Mention sustainable or ethical practices only if they appear in what I told you, so I'm not making claims I can't back up. Warm, professional tone.
Format: The three pieces separately, each under its own heading.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
