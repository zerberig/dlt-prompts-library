Context: I sell [your digital product] as a digital download on [your shop or platform]. Technical details: [formats, sizes, and compatibility]. Usage license: [personal or commercial use allowed].
Goal: I want a description that conveys how useful the product is while preventing misunderstandings like expecting a physical item or not knowing how to open the files.
Deliverable: A description with: 1) a 2-3 sentence opening about the outcome the customer gets, 2) what's included in the download, 3) technical specs, 4) how to download and use the files in 3-5 steps, 5) the usage license in plain words, and 6) a clear notice that no physical item will be shipped.
Criteria: Plain language for non-technical buyers. Mention compatible apps or devices only if they're in my details. Note that colors may vary by screen or printer.
Format: Sections with subheadings and bullets, 200-300 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
