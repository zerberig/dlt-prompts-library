Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. The typical situation in which someone needs it is: [customer situation or problem].
Goal: I want a description that makes buyers picture themselves using the product instead of reading a spec list.
Deliverable: A description with a three-part narrative structure: 1) the real-life moment when the need shows up, 2) how the product enters the scene and what makes it different, 3) what the day looks like afterward. Then a short section of practical details. Add 2 alternative openings with different scenarios.
Criteria: The customer is the hero, not the product or the brand. Use concrete scenes with sensory, everyday details, without overdramatizing. Every claim about the product must be true based on the details I gave you; don't invent results or numbers.
Format: A 120-180 word story with no subheadings, then "Details" in bullets (dimensions, materials, care, shipping) and a closing call to action.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
