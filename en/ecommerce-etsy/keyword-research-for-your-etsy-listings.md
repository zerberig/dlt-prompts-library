Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy to [target audience]. The tools I have available are: [available tools] (for example, only Etsy's search bar, my shop stats, or a free or paid external tool).
Goal: A repeatable method for finding the search phrases with the most potential for my titles, tags, and descriptions, instead of guessing.
Deliverable: 1) A step-by-step process: brainstorming seed words, expanding with autocomplete and related searches, reviewing listings that already sell, using my stats, and final filtering. 2) Criteria to prioritize each phrase: relevance, purchase intent, approximate competition, and specificity (short phrases versus long niche phrases). 3) A first list of 20 candidate phrases for my product, rated on those criteria. 4) How often to repeat the process.
Criteria: External tools give estimates, not official Etsy data; say so where relevant and flag any search volume as an estimate. If you have web search, use it to validate the candidate phrases. Favor phrases that describe the product precisely over popular but vague ones, because vague phrases bring visitors who don't buy.
Format: Numbered sections; the candidates as a table with the columns Phrase, Intent, Estimated competition, and Where to use it (title, tag, or description).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
