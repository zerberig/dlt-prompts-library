Context: I sell [type of products] on [marketplace] (eBay, Amazon, Etsy, or another). My current strategy is: [summary of your current strategy].
Goal: Understand what's changing on the platform and in buyer behavior, and adjust my strategy before my competitors do.
Deliverable: 1) The most relevant platform changes from the last 6-12 months: fees, new features, policies, advertising, AI tools, and shipping. 2) Demand trends in my category. 3) For each change or trend, the likely impact on my business (high, medium, or low) and one concrete action. 4) What to keep an eye on over the coming months.
Criteria: Use web search and give the source and date for each data point; if you don't have search access, say so up front and limit the analysis to what you can support. Separate confirmed facts from interpretation and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Prioritize actions a small seller can carry out.
Format: A table with the columns Change or trend, Source and date, Impact, and Recommended action; then the watch list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
