Context: I sell [type of products] on [marketplace], and my current situation is: [your shop's situation] (new, few reviews, a recent dispute, etc.).
Goal: I want a solid reputation that earns new buyers' trust and protects me when something goes wrong.
Deliverable: 1) The 5 practices with the biggest impact on reputation for my case, each with concrete actions. 2) Short message templates for order confirmation, shipping delay, a post-delivery review request, and a reply to an upset customer. 3) A protocol with concrete steps for resolving disputes and returns before they escalate.
Criteria: Review requests should be neutral and go to every customer, with no incentives in exchange for positive ratings, because that breaks most marketplaces' policies. If you have web search, check the marketplace's current policies on messaging, reviews, and disputes, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: Sections with headings, bullets, and each template in its own block. 600 words max.
