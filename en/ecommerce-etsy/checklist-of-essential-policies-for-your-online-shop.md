Context: I run [type of shop] selling [type of products] to customers in [countries you sell to], through [sales platforms].
Goal: Know which policies my shop needs to operate clearly, avoid disputes, and follow platform rules.
Deliverable: A checklist of the essential policies for my case (for example, shipping, returns and exchanges, personalized items, cancellations, data privacy, payments, and intellectual property of designs). For each: why I need it, the minimum points it must cover, and one common mistake when writing it.
Criteria: Prioritize based on my product type, because a digital download isn't the same as a personalized piece. If you have web search, check each platform's current requirements and the consumer protection rules in those countries, and flag as an estimate anything you can't confirm. This is general guidance: tell me which points are worth having a lawyer review in the final text.
Format: A table with the columns Policy, Priority (high, medium, or low), Why I need it, What it must cover, and Common mistake.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
