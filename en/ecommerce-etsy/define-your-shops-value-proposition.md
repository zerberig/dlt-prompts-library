Context: I sell [your product or shop] to [target audience]. My main competitors or alternatives are [competitors or alternatives], and what I think sets me apart is [your differentiator].
Goal: I want a clear value proposition I can use consistently across my shop, ads, and social media, so customers understand in seconds why they should buy from me.
Deliverable: 1) A brief analysis: what the customer is trying to achieve, the frustrations they have, and what they gain from my product. 2) 3 one-sentence versions of the value proposition (20 words max), each with a different angle. 3) For the version you recommend: a headline for the shop banner, a 1-2 sentence subheading, 3 proof bullets, and a call to action.
Criteria: The differentiator should be something competitors can't easily claim; if mine is weak, tell me and suggest another. Use the customer's language, not marketing jargon.
Format: Three numbered sections, with the final recommendation highlighted.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
