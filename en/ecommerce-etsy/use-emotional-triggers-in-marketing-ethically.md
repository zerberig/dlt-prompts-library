Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. The emotions I think drive purchases are [relevant emotions] (nostalgia, belonging, pride, relief, the joy of gifting).
Goal: I want campaigns that connect emotionally with customers without manipulating them, to build a relationship of trust that outlasts a single sale.
Deliverable:
1. For each emotion: why it's relevant to my product, what real need sits behind it, and 2 example messages for a listing, email, and social media.
2. A short 3-email sequence for new subscribers that uses those emotions progressively.
3. An ethics check for reviewing any message: Is it true? Would the customer feel fine knowing why it was written this way? Does it respect their freedom to decide?
4. Practices to avoid (guilt, exaggerated fear, false scarcity, pressuring vulnerable people), each with an example of how to reframe it.
Criteria: Every message must rest on real product benefits. Emotion strengthens a good offer, but it doesn't replace clear information about price, timing, and terms.
Format: Numbered sections; the section 1 messages in a table with the columns Emotion, Channel, and Message.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
