Context: Over [time period] I optimized these listings in my Etsy shop: [listings and changes made]. Here is their before-and-after data: [before and after data] (visits, impressions, search terms, favorites, sales, and revenue).
Goal: A report showing whether the optimization worked, which changes had the most effect, and what to do next.
Deliverable: 1) A 3-4 sentence executive summary. 2) A before-and-after comparison per listing, with percentage change. 3) Which changes seem to have worked and which didn't, with a confidence level for each conclusion. 4) External factors that may have played a role (season, promotions, price changes). 5) Prioritized next steps.
Criteria: Be careful with conclusions: with little data or short periods, say the evidence is weak instead of claiming causes. Don't attribute results to changes in Etsy's algorithm without evidence. If any data needed for the comparison is missing, point it out.
Format: A report with numbered sections and a table with the columns Listing, Metric, Before, After, and Change.
