Context: I run [your business] and sell [your product] to [target audience]. My per-unit costs are roughly: [materials, production, shipping, and fee costs]. My current price is [current price in your currency].
Goal: I want to pick a pricing strategy that improves my margin without losing sales, and know how to test it before changing everything.
Deliverable:
1. My current per-unit margin and my break-even price.
2. A comparison of 4-5 strategies that fit my case, for example: cost-plus, value-based, bundles, basic and premium tiers, subscription, or seasonal pricing.
3. Your main recommendation and why.
4. A 4-6 week test plan: what to change, what to measure, and what result decides it.
Criteria: Base the math on my numbers and show the formulas so I can adjust them. If you have web search, check the current fees of the platform I sell on. Flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate. If a decision has tax implications, recommend I confirm it with an accountant.
Format: The math in a short table; the comparison in a table with the columns Strategy, How it works, Pros, Risks, and Fit for my business; the recommendation and plan as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
