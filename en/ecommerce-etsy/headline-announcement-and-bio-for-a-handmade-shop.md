Context: My shop is called [your shop name] and sells [your handmade products]. What sets it apart is [what sets your work apart] (technique, materials, origin, time spent on each piece).
Goal: I want short copy that shows within seconds that each piece is unique and made by a person, so visitors stay to explore instead of comparing on price alone.
Deliverable: 1) 5 shop headline options (the short line under the shop name). 2) 2 versions of the shop announcement: a permanent welcome and one for new arrivals. 3) 3 social media bios with a call to action pointing to the shop. 4) 3 captions for introducing new pieces that invite people to discover them.
Criteria: Concrete over poetic: swap "made with passion" for a real detail from the process. If you have web search, check each field's current character limits and stay within them.
Format: Four numbered sections with bullets and the character count for each piece.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
