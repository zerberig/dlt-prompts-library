Goal: I want to comply with Etsy's policies from the start and learn about changes before they affect my listings or account.
Deliverable: 1) A summary of the policies most relevant to my case: what can be sold, rules on handmade items and production partners, intellectual property, photos and descriptions, shipping and returns, and tax or legal requirements in my country. 2) A checklist for reviewing every listing before publishing it. 3) A 20-minute monthly routine for monitoring changes: which official sources to check and what to look for. 4) What to do if I get a warning or a listing is deactivated.
Criteria: Use web search to check the current policies on Etsy's official pages, name the policy that supports each point, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. This is general guidance: for legal or tax questions, recommend that I consult a professional.
Format: Four numbered sections; the checklist with checkboxes.
