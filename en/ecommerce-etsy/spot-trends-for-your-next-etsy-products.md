Context: I run an Etsy shop selling [your niche or type of products], and I want to launch new designs for [season or period].
Goal: I want product ideas grounded in real trends, not hunches, plus a cheap way to check demand before I produce anything.
Deliverable: 1) 6 to 10 trends (aesthetics, themes, colors, occasions, or formats) relevant to my niche, split into "established" and "emerging". 2) For each one: the signal you're relying on, why you think it's growing, 2 concrete product ideas for my shop, and its risk level. 3) A one-week validation plan for the 3 most promising ideas (Etsy searches, competitor listings, social polls, pre-orders, or mockups).
Criteria: Use web search if you have it, cite the source of each signal, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Rule out trends that depend on protected characters, brands, or designs, because they lead to intellectual property claims.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, Type, Signal and source, Product ideas, and Risk; then the validation plan as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
