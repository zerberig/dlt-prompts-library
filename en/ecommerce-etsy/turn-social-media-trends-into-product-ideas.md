Context: I run a shop selling [type of products] on [sales platform] and my audience is [target audience].
Goal: Spot trends on social media and online communities that I can turn into products before the niche gets saturated.
Deliverable: 1) 8 current trends or topics relevant to my audience, with where they're showing up and why they're growing. 2) For each, 2 concrete product ideas. 3) An assessment of each idea: staying power (passing fad or steady trend), likely competition, and intellectual property risk. 4) A 30-minute weekly routine to keep spotting trends.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and cite where each trend comes from; flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Drop ideas that rely on characters, trademarked phrases, copyrighted memes, or brands, because they can get listings removed or the shop suspended.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, Source, Product idea, Staying power, Competition, and IP risk; then the routine as numbered steps.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
