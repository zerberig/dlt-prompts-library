Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform]. My brand stands out for [your brand's personality or values].
Goal: I want first-time buyers to feel from the very first touch that they've found a thoughtful place with personality, so they come back and recommend me.
Deliverable: Ready-to-use copy for each moment: 1) the shop announcement for first-time visitors, 2) the order confirmation message, 3) the shipping notice, 4) the card or insert inside the package, and 5) a post-delivery follow-up with a neutral invitation to leave a review. For each moment, 2 creative ideas (a packaging touch, a sample, a handwritten note, a care guide) with an approximate cost.
Criteria: Ideas should fit my industry and price point and stay workable as orders grow. Review invitations offer nothing in return, because that breaks most platforms' policies.
Format: One section per moment with the ready copy and the ideas as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
