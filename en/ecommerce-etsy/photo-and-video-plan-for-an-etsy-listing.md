Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy. I shoot with [available equipment] (for example, a phone, window light, or a lamp) and my shop's visual style is [your shop's visual style].
Goal: A photo and video plan that shows the product clearly, answers buyers' questions before they ask, and looks consistent with the rest of the shop.
Deliverable: 1) A shot list in the order the images will appear in the listing: what's shown, angle, background, props, and which question each shot answers (size, texture, use, details, packaging). 2) A short video idea for the listing. 3) A low-budget lighting setup for my equipment.
Criteria: The first photo has to work as a thumbnail in search results: clear product, clean background, good contrast. Include at least one shot with a scale reference and one in a real-use setting. If you have web search, confirm Etsy's current maximum number of photos and video requirements.
Format: A table with the columns No., Shot, Angle and framing, Background and props, Question it answers; then the video and lighting as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
