Context: My shop is called [your shop name] and sells [your products]. My story as the founder: [your story]. My inspiration is [your inspiration] and this is how we give back to our community: [your community commitment].
Goal: I want a complete About section that builds trust: who's behind the shop, how the products are made, and what values drive it.
Deliverable: 1) A headline for the section. 2) The founder's story (150-200 words), told from the moment that changed everything. 3) An explanation of the production process that makes clear what's done in-house and what's done with partners or suppliers. 4) A block on community or sustainability commitments, with concrete actions. 5) Short introductions for team members (1 or 2 sentences each), with "to be completed" where details are missing.
Criteria: Use only what I told you; don't invent impact figures, awards, or certifications. Be transparent about who makes the products, because buyers of handmade goods value it and platforms often require production partners to be disclosed. Warm, first-person tone.
Format: Five blocks with headings, 350-500 words total.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
