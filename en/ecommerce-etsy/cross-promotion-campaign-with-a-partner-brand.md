Context: My shop sells [your product] and I want to run a cross-promotion campaign with [partner brand and its product], which sells to a similar audience. We have [budget and resources] and these channels: [available channels].
Goal: Help both brands win new customers by leveraging how our products complement each other, with a campaign we can run in 4-6 weeks.
Deliverable: 1) The campaign concept and one line explaining why the products belong together. 2) Tactics per channel (for example, a joint bundle, a shared giveaway, co-created content, packaging inserts, a cross-email). 3) A week-by-week calendar. 4) How tasks, costs, and revenue are split between the two brands. 5) Metrics to measure each brand's results.
Criteria: Suggest realistic tactics for small businesses and prioritize low-cost ones. Include the agreements worth putting in writing before starting (who pays for what, image use, handling of customer data). Any giveaway must follow the rules of the platform where it runs.
Format: Numbered sections; the calendar as a table with the columns Week, Action, Channel, and Owner.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
