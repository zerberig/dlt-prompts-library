Context: I work with [traditional technique] and want to create [product type] to sell online to [target audience].
Goal: I want to develop an authentic product that respects the technique and its origins while looking current and fitting into my buyers' lives.
Deliverable:
1. A staged development guide: researching the technique and its references, defining the concept, sketches and prototypes, buyer testing, production, and launch.
2. 5 product concepts that pair the technique with a present-day need, each with materials, palette, and use.
3. How to tell the technique's origin story in the listing respectfully and accurately, including credit to communities or master artisans where appropriate.
4. Risks to manage: cultural appropriation, use of symbols with special meaning, and production costs.
5. A pre-launch checklist.
Criteria: Favor concepts I can produce with my current resources. If the technique belongs to a specific community, suggest fair collaboration rather than just drawing inspiration from it. If you have web search, use it to verify historical facts about the technique and flag anything you can't confirm.
Format: Numbered sections; the concepts in a table with the columns Concept, Materials, Palette, Use, and Difficulty.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
