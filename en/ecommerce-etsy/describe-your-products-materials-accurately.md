Context: I sell [your product], made from [material or materials]. What I know about the material: [properties, origin, treatments, or certifications].
Goal: Explain the material so buyers understand the quality they're paying for, know what to expect with use, and how to care for it.
Deliverable: 1) A paragraph about the material for the description: what it is, where it comes from, and what it brings to the product (durability, feel, weight, how it ages). 2) A list of technical specifications. 3) Care instructions. 4) 2 FAQs about the material with answers.
Criteria: Connect each property to something the buyer notices in real life ("linen softens with every wash"). Be precise with measurements, composition, and percentages. Don't attribute properties that aren't in the data (hypoallergenic, waterproof, antibacterial), because those are claims a buyer can dispute. If you have web search, use it to confirm the material's general properties.
Format: The 4 parts with headings; specs and care as bullets. 300 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
