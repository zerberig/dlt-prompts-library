Context: This is the current description of my product [your product]: [current description]. I want to adapt it for [holiday or season].
Goal: A seasonal version that connects with the feeling of that occasion and with people buying it as a gift, without losing the information or keywords of the original.
Deliverable: 1) The adapted description. 2) 2 alternative openings to test. 3) A short "Perfect gift for" section with ideas of who to give it to. 4) A line about shipping deadlines, with the dates marked «to be completed».
Criteria: Keep the technical details (dimensions, materials, production times) and main keywords intact. The feeling of the occasion should come through in concrete scenes of use or gift-giving, not exclamation marks. So it's easy to switch back after the season, tell me which paragraphs you changed.
Format: A 150-250 word description; then pieces 2 to 4 and the list of changes.
