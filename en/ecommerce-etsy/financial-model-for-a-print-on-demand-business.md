Context: I want to sell [your print-on-demand products] through [sales platforms] using a print-on-demand supplier. My starting budget is [starting budget] and I can put in [time available per week].
Goal: I want to know whether the business is viable, how much I need to sell to cover costs, and how long it could take to recover my investment.
Deliverable:
1. Unit economics for each product: sale price, production cost, shipping, platform and payment processing fees, ad spend per sale, and net margin.
2. Monthly fixed costs (subscriptions, tools, designs, samples).
3. Three 12-month scenarios (conservative, base, and optimistic) with monthly sales, revenue, costs, and profit.
4. The break-even point in units per month.
5. The 3 variables that most affect profitability and what to do about each.
6. A spreadsheet structure to rebuild the model with my own numbers.
Criteria: List each scenario's assumptions separately at the start so I can change them. Express amounts in [currency]. If you have web search, look up current platform fees and supplier costs; flag any figure you can't back with a source as an estimate. This is planning, not financial advice: for taxes, recommend that I consult an accountant.
Format: Numbered sections with tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
