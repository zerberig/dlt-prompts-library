Context: I sell [your product] on Etsy. These are my current titles, which read like keyword lists: [current titles].
Goal: I want titles that keep the important search terms but that a person can read in one go and understand instantly, because the title is also the first thing buyers see in search results.
Deliverable: For each title: the rewritten version, which keywords you kept and which you moved to tags, attributes, or the description, and a variant that reflects my brand's personality, which is [your brand personality]. At the end, 5 techniques I can apply to future titles on my own, each with a before-and-after example.
Criteria: Open with what the product is, followed by the trait that sets it apart most; cut repetition and unnecessary separators. If you have web search, check Etsy's current guidance on title length and structure.
Format: A table with the columns Original title, Rewritten title, Brand-voice variant, and Words moved (and where); then the techniques as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
