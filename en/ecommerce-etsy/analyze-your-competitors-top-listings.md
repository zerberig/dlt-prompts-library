Context: I sell [your product] on [marketplace]. These are standout competitor listings (by sales, reviews, or search position) with their title, visible tags, description, price, and a description of their photos: [competitor listings].
Goal: I want to understand what they do well so I can improve my own listings, without copying text or designs.
Deliverable: 1) For each listing, a short analysis of title and keywords, photos, price and offer, description, and trust signals (reviews, policies, shipping times). 2) The patterns the successful ones share. 3) What none of them do that could set me apart. 4) The 5 changes with the most potential for my listing, ranked by impact and effort.
Criteria: Base the analysis only on the data I gave you; don't invent sales figures or examples from other shops. Separate what's observed from what's a hypothesis. Recommendations should adapt ideas to my product, not imitate another seller.
Format: A comparison table with one row per listing and the columns Title and keywords, Photos, Price and offer, Description, and Trust; then sections 2 to 4 as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
