Context: I sell [your product] on Amazon [marketplace country] to [target audience]. My current situation: [product stage and current results] (launch, few reviews, steady sales, etc.). Monthly marketing budget: [monthly budget].
Goal: I want a 90-day plan that combines channels on and off Amazon to grow sales profitably, not just traffic.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of what needs to be in place before spending (listing, images, price, reviews). 2) A strategy for each channel: Amazon advertising, social media, content (blog, video, email), and influencers. For each: the objective, concrete tactics, share of the budget, and 2-3 KPIs with a starting target. 3) A phased timeline (weeks 1-4, 5-8, and 9-12). 4) Decision rules: when to scale, adjust, or pause each channel.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to review Amazon's current advertising options and policies, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source, such as cost per click or conversion rates. Any review tactic must follow Amazon's policies: no reviews in exchange for incentives.
Format: Numbered sections, one table per channel, 900 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
