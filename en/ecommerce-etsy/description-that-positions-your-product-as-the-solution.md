Context: I sell [your product]. Customers look for it because they have this problem or need: [customer problem or need]. Their main hesitation before buying is usually [main hesitation before buying].
Goal: A description that presents the product as the concrete answer to that problem, so shoppers recognize themselves in the first paragraph and move toward buying.
Deliverable: One complete description and 2 alternative headlines for the opening line.
Criteria: Open by describing the customer's situation in the words they'd use, without overdramatizing. Then show how the product solves it with 3 concrete details. Answer the main hesitation with facts (real materials, dimensions, warranty, or return policy). Don't promise results or guarantees I haven't mentioned, because they lead to returns and complaints. Close with a clear call to action.
Format: Problem, solution, proof, and close. 150-220 words, short paragraphs, with bullets in the solution section.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
