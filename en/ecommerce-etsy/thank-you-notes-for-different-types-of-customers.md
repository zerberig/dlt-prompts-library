Goal: I want every customer to feel valued and have a reason to come back or recommend me, without it sounding like a generic template.
Deliverable: Three thank-you notes of 50-80 words: 1) first purchase, 2) repeat customer, 3) customer who referred someone. Each with placeholders in quotes like «name» or «product» to personalize, plus a short 1-2 sentence version to handwrite.
Criteria: Mention something specific about the order so it doesn't feel generic. If I include an offer, present it as a thank-you gift with clear terms and no pressure. Don't make the perk conditional on a positive review, because that breaks most marketplaces' policies.
Format: Three sections with headings; in each, the full note and the short version.
