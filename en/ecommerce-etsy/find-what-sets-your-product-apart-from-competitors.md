Context: I sell [your product] and my main competitors are [competitors and their products]. Here's what I know about my product: [features, price, and key data].
Goal: Find out what truly sets me apart so I can use it in titles, descriptions, and ads.
Deliverable: 1) A comparison table of key features across my product and the competitors. 2) My 3 strongest differentiators, each with a supporting line based on data. 3) Where I'm at a disadvantage and how to address it honestly.
Criteria: A differentiator counts if shoppers care about it and competitors don't offer it or do it worse; drop anything everyone has. Use only the information I gave you or what you find with web search, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Avoid lines that attack competitors by name.
Format: A table with a Feature column, one column for my product, and one per competitor; then numbered lists for points 2 and 3.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
