For each: primary text (125 characters max), headline (40 characters max), call to action, and an image or video suggestion.
Criteria: Use only true claims and the details I gave you; if an angle needs a review, leave the placeholder as «fill in with a real review». Since ads inside Etsy are generated from each listing's photo and title, add 2 suggestions for improving both. If you have web search, check the platform's current character limits.
Format: A table with the columns Angle, Primary text, Headline, Call to action, and Image or video; the Etsy suggestions at the end.
