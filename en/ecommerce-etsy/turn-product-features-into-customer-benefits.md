Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. These are its main features: [list of features].
Goal: I want to rewrite my listing and ads so they talk about what the customer gains, not just what the product has.
Deliverable: For each feature, the practical benefit, the emotional benefit, and one line ready to use in the listing. Then 3 different angles for presenting the product (by use case, buyer type, or situation), each with a sample headline.
Criteria: Every benefit should answer "so what does that do for me?" from the customer's real life. Use concrete, verifiable language; skip empty superlatives like "the best", because they cost credibility.
Format: A table with the columns Feature, Practical benefit, Emotional benefit, and Listing line; then the 3 angles as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
