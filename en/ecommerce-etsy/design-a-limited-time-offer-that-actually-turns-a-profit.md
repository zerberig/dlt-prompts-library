Context: I want to run a limited-time offer on [your shop or platform] for [products included]. My current margin on those products is [approximate margin] and the goal of the offer is [offer goal] (clearing inventory, getting new customers, raising average order value, or tying into a date).
Goal: Design an offer that actually generates revenue, not just sales at a loss.
Deliverable: 1) 3 possible offer formats (percentage off, gift with purchase, free shipping over a threshold, or a bundle) with their calculated effect on my margin. 2) The recommended format, with its ideal duration and why. 3) A communication plan for before, during, and at the close of the offer. 4) How to measure whether it was worth it.
Criteria: Show the margin and break-even math: how many extra units I need to sell to earn the same as without the discount. Urgency must rest on a real deadline that's honored, because extending "last day" offers undermines the next ones. If you have web search, check the platform's current rules on discounts.
Format: A comparison table for the 3 formats; the rest in sections with bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
