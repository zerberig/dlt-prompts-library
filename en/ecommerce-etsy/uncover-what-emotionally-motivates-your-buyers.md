Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. What I know about why people buy from me: [what you know about your customers].
Goal: I want to understand the real emotional motivations behind the purchase (gifting, treating themselves, belonging, feeling capable, remembering someone, etc.) so I can write messages that genuinely connect.
Deliverable: 1) 10 open-ended questions to interview or survey current customers, with no leading questions. 2) A hypothesis of the 5 most likely emotional motivations for my product, each with the signal that would confirm it and a sample message for listings or social media. 3) How to test which one matters most on a small budget.
Criteria: Messages should connect with customers' real desires without exploiting fears, insecurities, or false pressure, because that damages trust and the shop's reputation. Clearly separate what's a hypothesis from what comes from my data.
Format: Three sections with headings; the motivations in a table with the columns Motivation, Confirming signal, and Sample message.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
