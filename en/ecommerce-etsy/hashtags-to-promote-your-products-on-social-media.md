Context: I sell [your product] through an online shop and promote it on [social networks you use]. My audience is [target audience] and I sell mainly in [country or region].
Goal: I want hashtags that bring interested people from my social posts to my listings. I know Etsy listings use tags, not hashtags, so this is for social media only.
Deliverable: For each network: 1) a hashtag set split into broad, niche, and low-saturation-but-relevant; 2) how many to use per post based on that network's current practices; 3) 2 ready-to-copy combinations by post type (finished product, process, gift). Add local or regional-language hashtags if they make sense.
Criteria: If you have web search, check that the hashtags are still in use, aren't tied to unrelated or restricted content, and what each network's current practice is; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Skip trending hashtags unrelated to the product, because they attract people who don't buy.
Format: One section per network with a table of the columns Hashtag, Type, and Why it's relevant; the ready-made combinations in separate blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
