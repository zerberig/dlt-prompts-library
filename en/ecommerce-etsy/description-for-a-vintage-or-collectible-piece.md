Context: I sell [your vintage piece] on [your shop or platform]. Here's what I know about it: [era, origin, and condition] (maker if known, materials, wear, repairs).
Goal: I want a description that conveys the piece's character and why it's worth owning, whether to collect, use, or give as a gift, and that tells buyers exactly what they'll receive.
Deliverable: One description ready for the listing, plus a short line to introduce the piece on social media.
Criteria: Open with the story or style of its era, using concrete details. Describe the condition honestly, including signs of wear, because that prevents returns and builds trust. Mention rarity or collectible value only if my details support it; if something isn't confirmed (exact date, attribution), present it as "approximate" or "attributed". If the piece makes a good gift for a particular season, suggest it naturally.
Format: A 2-3 sentence opening paragraph, a "Details" section with dimensions, materials, and era, a "Condition" section in bullets, and a closing call to action. 150-250 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
