Context: I make [your product] by hand. This is my process: [steps, techniques, and production times]. The materials I use are [materials].
Goal: Help buyers understand why this piece is different from a mass-produced one and value the work behind its price.
Deliverable: 1) A "How it's made" section for the product description, 120-180 words. 2) A 3-4 sentence version for the listing's opening paragraph. 3) 3 ideas for photos or short clips that show the process.
Criteria: Tell the process with concrete, verifiable details (hours of work, number of steps, tools, finishes), not adjectives like "exquisite" or "unique". Explain how each stage shows up in the result: durability, texture, small variations between pieces. Present those variations as part of the handmade character, so buyers know what to expect. First person, warm tone.
Format: The 3 pieces with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
