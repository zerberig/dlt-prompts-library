Context: I sell [your product] to [target audience]. A surprising fact, tradition, or story related to the product: [product fact or story].
Goal: Many shops and search engines only show the first lines of a description, so I want that opening to hook buyers and make them want to keep reading.
Deliverable: 8 openers of 1-2 sentences (160 characters max each), two per technique: a curiosity-sparking question or fact, an unexpected twist, a mini scene with a character who represents my customer, and a tradition or story. For each opener, a second sentence that ties it to the main benefit.
Criteria: The mystery must be resolved in the next sentence with real product information; no clickbait hooks that promise something the product doesn't deliver. Work [main keyword] in naturally in at least half of the openers.
Format: A table with the columns Technique, Opener, Second sentence, and Characters. At the end, tell me which one you'd test first and why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
