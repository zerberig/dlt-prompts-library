Context: I sell [your product] on [your shop or platform]. Each piece is unique because [what makes it unique] (personalization, a limited run, a handmade process, or something else). My buyers are usually [target audience].
Goal: I want a description that makes buyers feel they're getting something made for them, or for the person they're gifting it to, and that this moves them to buy.
Deliverable: One description ready to paste into the listing, plus 2 alternative opening paragraphs to test which works better.
Criteria: Open with the moment someone receives or uses the piece, not with specs. Explain with concrete details what makes it one of a kind (materials, process, story). Mention limited availability only using the real numbers I gave you, because invented scarcity erodes trust and can break platform policies. Warm, confident tone without exaggeration.
Format: A 2-3 sentence opening paragraph, bullets on what makes it unique, personalization options and lead times, and a closing call to action. 150-250 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
