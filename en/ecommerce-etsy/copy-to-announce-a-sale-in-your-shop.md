Context: My shop [your shop name] is running this promotion: [promotion details] (real discount, products included, start and end dates). My brand's tone is [your brand tone].
Goal: Eye-catching, consistent copy to communicate the promotion across every part of the shop, so shoppers understand the offer in seconds.
Deliverable: 1) 3 headline options for the shop banner (6 words max) with a subheadline. 2) The shop announcement text. 3) A line to add at the top of the included listings. 4) 3 short social media captions. 5) Visual direction for the banner: text hierarchy, colors, and which product to show.
Criteria: The offer must be clear immediately: what, how much, and until when. Use only real discounts and dates, because inflating prices to fake a discount or creating false urgency breaks marketplace policies and damages trust. Keep my brand's tone across every piece.
Format: The 5 pieces numbered with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
