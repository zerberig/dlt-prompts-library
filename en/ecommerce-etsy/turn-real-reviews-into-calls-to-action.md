Context: I run a shop selling [your product or shop]. These are real reviews my customers have left: [real customer reviews].
Goal: I want to use that social proof on my shop page, listings, and social media so new visitors trust me and buy.
Deliverable: 1) The 5 most persuasive excerpts, quoted word for word, each with the benefit it highlights or the doubt it resolves. 2) 6 calls to action that work in that social proof: 2 for the shop banner or announcement, 2 for the end of a product description, and 2 for social posts. 3) The 3 themes that come up most in the reviews and that I should reinforce in my listings.
Criteria: Use only what the reviews I gave you actually say: you can trim them, but don't rewrite them or invent quotes, numbers, or ratings, because made-up testimonials mislead buyers and break marketplace policies. Don't include customers' full names, to protect their privacy. Keep calls to action to 15 words max.
Format: Three sections with headings; excerpts and calls to action as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
