Context: I run a shop selling [type of products] to [target audience]. Here are 2-3 descriptions I've already written: [current descriptions]. I want my brand to sound [desired brand personality].
Goal: A short voice guide so all my descriptions, captions, and messages sound the same, even when I write in a hurry, someone else writes, or I use AI.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of my current descriptions: what's consistent and what shifts between them. 2) The voice guide: 3-4 personality traits with "do" and "don't" examples, preferred vocabulary and words to avoid, formality level, use of emojis and punctuation, and the standard structure of a description. 3) One of my descriptions rewritten using the guide.
Criteria: The guide should balance information and emotion: concrete fact first, feeling second. Examples should come from my product type, not be generic. Keep it to one page, so I'll actually use it.
Format: Sections with headings; the traits as a table with the columns Trait, Do, and Don't.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
