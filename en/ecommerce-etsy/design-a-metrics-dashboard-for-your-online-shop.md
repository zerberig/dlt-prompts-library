Context: I sell [type of products] on [platforms you sell on]. Right now I track my numbers like this: [how you track your data today] (spreadsheet, platform stats, nothing yet, etc.). My goal for the next 6 months is [your main goal].
Goal: I want to stop deciding on gut feeling and have a simple dashboard that tells me what's working, what isn't, and what to do about it.
Deliverable: 1) 8 to 12 key metrics grouped into traffic, conversion, profitability, and customers, each with its formula, data source, and review frequency. 2) The dashboard layout in a spreadsheet: tabs, columns, and recommended charts. 3) A 30-minute weekly review routine and a monthly one, with guiding questions. 4) An "if this happens, try this" table that links metric changes to concrete decisions (pricing, photos, titles, ads, inventory).
Criteria: Prioritize metrics I can get with the tools I already have, without expensive software. Explain formulas in plain language. If you cite industry benchmarks, such as typical conversion rates, use web search if you have it and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections, tables where useful, 800 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
