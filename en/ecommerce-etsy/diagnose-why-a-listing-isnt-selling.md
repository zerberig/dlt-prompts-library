Context: This listing on [sales platform] isn't selling as I expected: [listing text or summary]. Its data for the last [time period] is: [impressions, visits, favorites, and sales].
Goal: Find where in the buyer's journey the sale is being lost and what to change first.
Deliverable: 1) The key funnel metrics (impressions, click-through rate, favorites or add-to-carts, conversion rate) calculated from my data, and what each one indicates. 2) A diagnosis: whether the problem is visibility, appeal in search results, or the listing page itself. 3) The 5 top-priority fixes based on that diagnosis, each with the metric it should improve. 4) How long to wait before evaluating the changes.
Criteria: Follow the data: lots of impressions and few visits usually point to the main photo, title, or visible price; visits without sales point to the listing page. If you can't calculate a metric from what I gave you, tell me what's missing and where to find it. Flag any reference to industry average rates as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections; the fixes as a table with the columns Fix, Problem it addresses, Metric to watch, and Effort.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
