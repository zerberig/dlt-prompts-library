Context: I'm launching a print-on-demand shop selling [product types and theme] for [target audience]. I sell on [your shop or platform] and have no physical inventory, just mockups and the occasional sample.
Goal: I want to build anticipation before launch, land my first sales, and take advantage of being able to test designs without producing them upfront.
Deliverable: 1) A 4-week plan: the week before launch, launch week, and the 2 weeks after. 2) For each week, 3-4 post ideas with a suggested platform (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, or Facebook), format (carousel, short video, pin, story), opening hook, and call to action. 3) 3 ways to let the community vote on or pick designs before I publish them. 4) How to turn customer orders and photos into new content, with their permission.
Criteria: Ideas one person can pull off with mockups and a phone. Mockups should look like the real product, to avoid complaints about false expectations. Adapt the format to each platform instead of cross-posting the same thing.
Format: One table per week with the columns Platform, Format, Idea, Hook, and Call to action; then points 3 and 4 as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
