Context: My business [your business name] sells these products, with price and cost: [products with price and cost]. My operational constraints are: [inventory or shipping constraints].
Goal: Choose the bundling strategy that raises average order value without eating into my margin or complicating operations.
Deliverable: 1) The bundle types that apply to my case (pure, mixed, occasion-based, "build your own set", cart add-on, or subscription), with the upside and risk of each. 2) 3 concrete bundles using my products, with suggested price and calculated margin. 3) The challenges of each bundle in inventory, shipping, and perceived value, and how to handle them. 4) Metrics to evaluate them after 60 days: average order value, margin per order, units per order, and cannibalization of individual sales.
Criteria: Show the math. The bundle savings must be real compared to the sum of individual prices. Avoid bundles that force me to stock slow-moving inventory without a clear benefit to the customer.
Format: Numbered sections; the 3 bundles as a table with the columns Bundle, Products, Sum of individual prices, Bundle price, and Margin.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
