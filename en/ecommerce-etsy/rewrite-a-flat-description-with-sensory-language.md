Context: I sell [your product] and this is its current description, which feels flat: [current description]. I also want to use it for [season or holiday].
Goal: I want to transform it so readers imagine how the product feels, smells, sounds, or looks in their life, without losing the practical information.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis: which parts can become sensory and which details must stay as they are. 2) The rewritten description. 3) An alternate opening set in the season or holiday. 4) A table of 10 swaps from generic phrases to sensory images (for example, "good quality" to "feels solid in your hand and doesn't bend"), reusable for other products in my shop.
Criteria: Use at least three senses, but only with the product's real attributes; don't invent scents, textures, or materials that aren't in the original description. Balance it: vivid imagery up front, clear facts after.
Format: Numbered sections; the rewritten description 150-250 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
