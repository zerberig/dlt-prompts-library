Context: My business is called [your business name] and sells [what you sell]. This is how it started and what drives me: [your story and motivations]. My customers value [what your customers value].
Goal: I want a consistent brand narrative I can use across all my channels, so people quickly understand who's behind it and why they should buy from me rather than another shop.
Deliverable: 1) The core message in a single sentence. 2) A 50-word version for bios and profiles. 3) A 150-word version for the shop page or website. 4) A 300-word story with a beginning, obstacle, and transformation, for a newsletter or long post. 5) 3 short reusable lines for packaging, product listings, or social media.
Criteria: Use only facts from my story; where a detail is missing, write "to be completed" instead of inventing it. Connect the story to what the customer gains, not just my experience. Warm, genuine tone, without stock phrases like "made with love" unless a concrete detail backs them up.
Format: Five numbered sections with headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
