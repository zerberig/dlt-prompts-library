Context: I sell [your product] on Amazon. My current images are: [description of your current images]. Buyers' most common questions or complaints are: [frequent questions or complaints].
Goal: I want the main image to earn more clicks in search results and the rest of the gallery to answer questions and convince people to buy.
Deliverable: 1) A 7-image plan in order: main image, key benefits, in-context use, size and scale, material details, comparison or what's in the box, and one that answers the most common question. 2) For each image: the objective, what's shown, overlay text (6 words max), and notes for the photographer or designer. 3) 3 A/B test ideas for the main image and which metric to check to pick the winner.
Criteria: The main image must meet Amazon's technical and style requirements; use web search if you have it to confirm the current rules, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Think mobile first: overlay text must be readable on a small screen.
Format: A table with the columns Image, Objective, What's shown, Overlay text, and Notes; the A/B tests as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
