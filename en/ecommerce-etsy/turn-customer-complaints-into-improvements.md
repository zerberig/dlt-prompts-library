Context: I sell [your product or type of products] on [sales platform]. These are customer reviews, messages, and complaints from the last few months: [reviews, messages, and complaints].
Goal: Turn this feedback into concrete improvements to the product, listings, and service, instead of answering each one without learning from it.
Deliverable: 1) Recurring themes, grouped into product, expectations that don't match the listing, shipping and packaging, and customer service. 2) The frequency and severity of each theme. 3) One concrete action for each and where it gets fixed (product, description, photos, policy, or an automated message). 4) A simple monthly process to keep collecting and analyzing feedback.
Criteria: Quote excerpts from the feedback as evidence for each theme. Distinguish isolated cases from patterns. Also point out what customers praise, because those are selling points worth highlighting in listings.
Format: A table with the columns Theme, Examples, Frequency, Severity, and Action; then the positives and the process as bullets.
