Context: I make [your product] using [main materials or technique] and sell it on [your shop or platform]. Right now I only offer fixed versions.
Goal: I want to offer customization that customers value and will pay for, without making my production unmanageable.
Deliverable: 1) 10 to 12 customization ideas grouped into three tiers: simple (text, color, size), mid-level (combinations, packaging, messages), and premium (made-to-order designs or collector pieces). 2) For each idea: which type of customer would value it, how much extra time or cost it adds, and a suggested upcharge range. 3) The 3 ideas with the best ratio of perceived value to effort, with how to present them in the listing. 4) Unexpected uses of the product that customization can open up.
Criteria: Account for common mistakes in custom orders (typos, misspelled names) and suggest how to prevent them with a confirmation step before production. Flag the upcharges as estimates.
Format: A table with the columns Idea, Tier, Ideal customer, Extra effort, and Suggested upcharge; then sections 3 and 4 as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
