Goal: Build income I can earn from anywhere, without relying on a single source.
Deliverable: 8 to 10 income options that use my skills, mixing remote employment, freelancing, productized services, online teaching, and digital products. For each: what it involves, a concrete first step, estimated time to first income, earning potential, risk level, and whether it works well across time zones. Then the combination of 2 or 3 sources you recommend and why.
Criteria: Realistic, sustainable options, with no easy-money promises or pyramid schemes. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Option, First step, Time to first income, Potential, Risk, and Travel-friendly; then the recommended combination in one paragraph.
