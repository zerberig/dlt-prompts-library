Context: I work as [your profession or service] and travel while I work. I post on [social networks], want to attract [your ideal client or audience], and can post [posting frequency].
Goal: Make my travel content do more than look nice: show my expertise and bring in clients or opportunities.
Deliverable: A 4-week calendar balancing four pillars: behind the scenes of remote work, useful lessons from my field, life at the destination, and client stories or results. For each post: date, network, format (carousel, short video, text post, story), pillar, first-line hook, and call to action.
Criteria: Someone who doesn't know me should understand within a week what I do and who I do it for. Match formats to each network. Include how to repurpose each video into at least one other format.
Format: A table with the columns Date, Network, Format, Pillar, Hook, and Call to action; at the end, 3 metrics to review each week.
