Context: I'm designing [type of product] (an app, a bot, or a feature on a travel site) that recommends the cheapest days to fly on a route and date range the user picks. My team works with [available stack or tools] and the audience is [target audience].
Goal: Have a clear spec for building a first prototype that gives useful, honest recommendations on which dates to fly and when to book.
Deliverable: 1) Possible data sources (fare APIs and historical data providers), with the data each provides, its cost, and its usage restrictions. 2) Analysis methodology: seasonality, day of week, booking lead time, events and demand, and the model or heuristic that makes sense for a prototype. 3) How results are shown: a price calendar, a confidence level, and a short explanation. 4) Metrics to evaluate whether predictions are accurate. 5) Risks and limitations.
Criteria: Access to flight APIs changes often; use web search if you have it to confirm which services are available today and on what terms, and flag as an estimate anything you can't confirm. Prefer a simple solution that works over a complex one that's hard to maintain. Present every prediction as a probability, never as a price guarantee.
Format: A document with the 5 numbered sections, bullets, and a table for the data sources. 800 to 1,200 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
