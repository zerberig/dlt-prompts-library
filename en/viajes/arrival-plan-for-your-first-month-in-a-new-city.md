Context: I'm arriving in [city] on [arrival date] to stay [length of stay] and work remotely. My monthly budget for housing and a workspace is [monthly budget], and for work I need [work requirements] (for example, stable internet for video calls).
Goal: Settle in quickly and well, without losing workdays or signing a long lease in the wrong neighborhood.
Deliverable: 1) 2 or 3 recommended neighborhoods for my profile, with pros, cons, and approximate price. 2) A week-by-week plan for the first month: week 1 in flexible housing while I try out neighborhoods and coworking spaces; week 2 to lock in long-term housing; weeks 3 and 4 settled into a routine. 3) How to get around the city (public transport, apps, transit cards). 4) First-day errands: SIM or eSIM, cash, groceries, pharmacy, and the nearest clinic.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it for current coworking and transport options and prices, and flag as an estimate anything you can't confirm.
Format: A neighborhood table with the columns Neighborhood, Pros, Cons, and Approximate price; the monthly plan as a list by week.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
