Context: I work as [your profession] and rely on [your main gear]. I mostly travel to [type of destinations] (big cities, small towns, or rural areas) and often cross time zones.
Goal: Make sure an equipment, internet, or power failure doesn't stop me from working or cost me data.
Deliverable: A checklist organized into connectivity (eSIM, travel router or hotspot, backup options), power (chargers, adapters, power bank), data protection (cloud and local backup, encryption, password manager, two-step verification), physical security (anti-theft, gear insurance), and work accessories. For each item: why it matters, its priority, and a budget alternative. At the end, a plan for the first 24 hours if my laptop is stolen or breaks.
Criteria: Redundancy for what's critical and lightness for everything else. Recommend product types with specs rather than brands, unless one is clearly the standard.
Format: A checklist by category and the emergency plan as a numbered list.
