Context: I work remotely as [your profession] and travel often. These are the tools I use today and what I pay for them: [list of tools and costs].
Goal: Use fewer apps, pay less, and not lose anything important when I change countries or connections.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of my stack: overlapping functions, gaps (backup, security, offline work, international payments), and tools I could drop. 2) A simplified stack by function, with a free or cheaper alternative where one exists. 3) A step-by-step migration plan so I can switch without losing data.
Criteria: Prioritize tools that work well on unstable internet and from multiple countries. Prices and plans change; use web search if you have it and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Function, Current tool, Recommendation, and Estimated savings; then the migration plan as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
