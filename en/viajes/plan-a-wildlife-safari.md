Context: I want to go on safari in [region or country] around [possible dates or month], for [number of days] days. I'm most interested in [animals or experiences you're interested in] and my budget is [budget level].
Goal: Choose well when to go, where, and with what kind of operator, so I have a good chance of seeing what I care about, responsibly.
Deliverable: 1) The best season for my interests and what changes if I go on my dates. 2) 2 or 3 recommended parks or reserves, with the wildlife I'm likely to see, the landscape, and access logistics. 3) A comparison of safari types (self-drive, group, private, walking) with approximate costs. 4) Criteria for choosing an ethical, responsible operator. 5) A gear and clothing list, plus the health and document requirements I should check.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it for current seasons, park fees, and requirements, and flag as an estimate anything you can't back up with a source. For vaccines and preventive medication, recommend that I see a travel medicine doctor.
Format: Numbered sections; the safari type comparison as a table.
