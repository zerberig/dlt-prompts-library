Context: I work remotely as [your profession], about [weekly work hours] hours a week, with standing meetings at [your meeting times]. I do my best work in [time of day when you have the most energy].
Goal: A routine that protects my most important work without giving up the flexibility to travel.
Deliverable: Three versions of my day: 1) a normal workday, 2) a travel day (flight, bus, or moving to a new place), and 3) a day with unstable internet. Each with time blocks, the type of task for each block, breaks, and a short start-of-day and end-of-day ritual.
Criteria: Put deep-focus work in my highest-energy hours and leave room for the unexpected. Include which tasks to prepare in advance so I can work offline.
Format: One table per version with the columns Time, Block, and Notes; at the end, 3 rules for deciding which version to use each day.
