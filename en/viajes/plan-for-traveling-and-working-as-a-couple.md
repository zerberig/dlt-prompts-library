Goal: Make both our jobs work while keeping the trip quality time together, not just sharing an apartment.
Deliverable: 1) A weekly schedule that coordinates our work blocks, time together, and time apart. 2) Criteria for choosing destinations and accommodation that work for both of us (separate workspaces, noise, connection, time zone). 3) 5 practical agreements for handling our friction point and other common ones (calls, shared expenses, route decisions). 4) A 15-minute conversation guide for a weekly check-in on how we're doing.
Criteria: Fair to both, specific, and easy to adjust. Practical, warm tone.
Format: A table for the weekly schedule and bullets for the rest. 600 words max.
