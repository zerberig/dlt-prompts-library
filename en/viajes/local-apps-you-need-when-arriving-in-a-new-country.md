Context: I'm arriving in [destination country or city] from [your country] and staying [length of stay]. My phone runs [phone operating system].
Goal: Get around, pay, and communicate from day one the way locals do, instead of relying on apps hardly anyone uses there.
Deliverable: The most widely used apps at that destination, grouped into transport and maps, payments, messaging, food and shopping, language and translation, and safety. For each: what I'd use it for, whether it needs a local phone number or bank account, and an alternative if I can't sign up. At the end, what to download and set up before leaving (offline maps, eSIM, offline translator).
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to confirm which apps are current and available in that country, and mark anything you can't verify. Note whether any common service is blocked or restricted there.
Format: A table with the columns Category, App, What it's for, and Requirements; then the pre-trip list as a checklist.
