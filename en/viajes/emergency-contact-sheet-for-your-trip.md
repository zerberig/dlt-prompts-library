Context: I'm traveling to [destination] from [your country of residence] on a [your nationality] passport. My travel insurance is [your insurance details].
Goal: Have everything I'd need in an emergency on a single page I can save on my phone, print, and share with someone I trust.
Deliverable: A sheet with local emergency numbers (police, ambulance, fire), my country's embassy or consulate at the destination, reference hospitals in the areas I'll visit, my insurer's assistance line, space for my personal contacts, and what to do if I lose my passport or wallet.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to confirm numbers and addresses, and mark any you couldn't verify so I can check them. Short, clear lines meant to be read in a hurry.
Format: Short sections with headings that fit on one page.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
