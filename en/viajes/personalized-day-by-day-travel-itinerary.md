Context: I'm traveling to [destination] for [number of days] days. My travel style is [travel style] (relaxed, adventurous, cultural, family), I'm into [your interests], and my budget is [budget level].
Goal: A realistic plan that makes the most of each day without wearing me out or spending hours in transit.
Deliverable: A day-by-day itinerary split into morning, afternoon, and evening: activity or place, why it's worth it, estimated time, and approximate travel from the previous stop. Include a theme for each day, one local food recommendation per day, and a backup for rainy or low-energy days. Arrival and departure days should be light.
Criteria: Group activities by area to cut down on transit. Point out what needs advance booking. If you have web search, confirm current hours and prices, and flag as an estimate anything you can't verify.
Format: One block per day with the heading "Day N: theme" and bullets for morning, afternoon, and evening; at the end, a list of pending bookings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
