Context: I just arrived in [city] for [length of stay]. I work as [your profession], outside work I'm into [your interests], and my level in the local language is [language level].
Goal: Build a network of contacts and friends within a month, with other remote workers as well as locals and people in my field, instead of staying isolated.
Deliverable: A 4-week plan with 2 or 3 actions per week: types of events and places (coworking spaces, professional meetups, classes, volunteering, group sports, language exchanges), how to find them, a natural way to start a conversation in each setting, and an idea for hosting a small get-together myself in week 4.
Criteria: Mix online and in-person activities, and alternate work-related plans with social ones. If you have web search, suggest specific active communities or events and mark any you can't confirm.
Format: A table per week with the columns Action, Where to find it, and First step; at the end, 3 conversation openers.
