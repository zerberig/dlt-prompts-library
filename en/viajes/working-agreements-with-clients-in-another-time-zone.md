Context: I work as [your role] for [clients or team] based in [your clients' time zone], and I'll be in [your current time zone]. We communicate through [tools you already use].
Goal: Keep the time difference from slowing projects down or forcing me to be available around the clock.
Deliverable: 1) Recommended overlap hours and what kind of work fits inside and outside them. 2) Async communication rules: response times, what goes in chat vs. a document, how to leave status updates, and how to escalate something urgent. 3) A short, professional message proposing these agreements to my client or team.
Criteria: Concrete practices that are easy to keep up and that protect both the quality of the work and my rest. Account for daylight saving changes if they apply.
Format: A table with my hours and theirs side by side, then the rules as bullets and the message in its own block. 450 words max.
