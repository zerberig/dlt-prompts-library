Deliverable: 1) A template with 4 to 6 recurring sections (for example: lesson of the week, remote job opportunity, money tip, destination or tool, community question), with the purpose and length of each. 2) 5 subject line options with their preview text. 3) A complete first issue using the template, about [topic of the first issue].
Criteria: Practical, specific advice in a warm tone. If you include figures or platform data, flag as an estimate anything you can't back up with a source.
Format: The template as bullets, the subject lines as a list, and the full issue with headings, 400 to 600 words.
