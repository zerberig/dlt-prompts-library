Context: I work remotely and I'm looking for a base for the next [how long you plan to stay]. My monthly budget is [monthly budget and currency], I'm considering [candidate cities or regions], and my priorities, in order, are [your top 3 priorities].
Goal: Decide where to settle based on data, not on what's trending.
Deliverable: A matrix comparing up to 6 cities (the ones I gave you plus 1 or 2 you think fit better) on cost of living, internet quality, ease of immigration for my case, remote worker community, safety, weather on those dates, and time difference with my work. Weight each criterion by my priorities and calculate a final score.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it for current costs and requirements, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Explain in one line why you suggest each extra city.
Format: A table with cities as rows and the criteria plus the weighted score as columns; then one paragraph with your recommendation and the biggest drawback of the winning city.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
