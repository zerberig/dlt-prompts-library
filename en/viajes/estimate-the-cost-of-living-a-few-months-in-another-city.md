Context: I want to live in [city] for [number of months] months while working remotely. My lifestyle is [lifestyle] (frugal, comfortable, or not very limited) and I'm traveling [alone or with others].
Goal: Know how much money I need before deciding, including the costs people tend to forget.
Deliverable: 1) One-time upfront costs: flight, housing deposit, paperwork, insurance, SIM or eSIM. 2) A monthly budget at three levels (frugal, mid-range, comfortable) for housing, food, transport, coworking or internet, health, leisure, and other. 3) The total for the whole stay plus 10 to 15% for the unexpected. 4) 5 specific ways to spend less in that city.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it for current prices; show the local currency and its equivalent in [your currency], and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Note which neighborhoods fit each level.
Format: Tables for upfront costs and the monthly budget; bullets for the rest.
