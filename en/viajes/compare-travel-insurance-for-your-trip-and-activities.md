Context: I'm traveling to [destination] for [trip length] from [your country of residence]. I plan to [planned activities] and I'm bringing work gear such as a laptop or camera. My insurance budget is [budget].
Goal: Choose a policy that covers what could actually happen to me, without paying for coverage I don't need.
Deliverable: 1) The factors to compare, ranked by importance for my trip: medical costs and evacuation, cancellation, luggage and electronics, adventure activities, long stays, deductibles, and exclusions. 2) Recommended minimum coverage amounts as a starting point. 3) A blank table for comparing up to 3 policies. 4) Questions to ask the insurer before paying.
Criteria: Point out the exclusions that tend to surprise people (pre-existing conditions, alcohol, extreme sports, theft without a police report). If you have web search, check whether the destination requires insurance for entry, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Remind me to read the full policy terms or talk to an insurance agent.
Format: Numbered sections; the table with the columns Criterion, Policy 1, Policy 2, and Policy 3.
