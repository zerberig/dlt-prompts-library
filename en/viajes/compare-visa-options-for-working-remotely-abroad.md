Deliverable: 1) For each country, the possible routes (tourist entry, digital nomad visa, temporary residence, or other), with main requirements, minimum income, approximate cost, processing time, and allowed length of stay. 2) What counts as working legally in each case and the risks of doing it on tourist status. 3) A reasoned recommendation of the best option for my profile and the list of documents I need to gather.
Criteria: Immigration rules change often: use web search if you have it, prioritize official sources (consulates and government sites), and note the date of what you check. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Recommend that I confirm everything with the consulate or an immigration lawyer before starting any application, because a mistake could cost me entry to the country.
Format: A comparison table with the columns Country, Route, Key requirements, Minimum income, Cost, Processing time, and Length of stay; then the recommendation in one paragraph and the document list as bullets.
