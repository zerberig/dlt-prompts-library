Context: We need to pick software for [what you need it for] at [type and size of organization]. We're considering [options you're comparing], and our main requirements are [key requirements].
Goal: Make a well-justified decision instead of choosing on the strength of a good demo or the entry price.
Deliverable: 1) An evaluation matrix with 12 to 20 criteria grouped into functionality, integration, security and privacy, usability, accessibility, support, and total cost. 2) A suggested weight for each group based on my requirements. 3) Concrete questions for each vendor and test scenarios for the trial period. 4) A preliminary assessment of the options with whatever information you have.
Criteria: Every criterion should be checkable with a test or a question, not an impression. Include hidden costs (implementation, training, data migration, vendor exit). If you have web search, look up current pricing and features and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: The matrix as a table with the columns Criterion, Weight, How to verify, and one score column per option; then the questions and scenarios as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
