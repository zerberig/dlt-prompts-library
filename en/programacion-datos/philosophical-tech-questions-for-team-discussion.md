Context: I run conversation sessions for [team or community] and want to open a debate on [technology topic] (for example, generative AI, privacy, technical debt, or automation of work). The format will be [session format] (a 15-minute meeting, the start of a pair programming session, or a monthly talk).
Goal: Get the team thinking beyond the code and talking about the implications of what they build.
Deliverable: 8 philosophical or ethical questions on the topic, along the lines of "if an AI writes code with a serious bug, who is responsible?". For each: 1-2 sentences of context, two well-argued opposing positions, and a follow-up question to go deeper.
Criteria: Questions with no obvious answer that connect to everyday development work, so everyone can contribute from their own experience. Present both positions fairly.
Format: A numbered list; each question in bold followed by its parts as bullets.
