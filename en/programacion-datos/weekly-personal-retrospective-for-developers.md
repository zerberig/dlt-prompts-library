Context: I work as [your role] and this quarter I'm focusing on [your main goal]. I want to spend 20 minutes every Friday reviewing my week.
Goal: Spot early what's holding me back and what's worth repeating, instead of reaching the end of the month not knowing whether I made progress.
Deliverable: 1) A weekly retrospective template with 8 to 10 questions grouped into wins, blockers, learnings, energy, and next week. 2) 3 simple metrics I can log each week to see my trend. 3) A guide for a monthly review that builds on the 4 retrospectives.
Criteria: Specific questions I can answer in 1 or 2 lines ("Which task took twice as long as estimated, and why?"), not generic ones. Include at least one question about collaborating with the team and one about rest, because a sustainable pace is part of progress too.
Format: The template in Markdown, ready to paste into my notes, with blanks marked as "to fill in"; then the metrics and the monthly guide as bullets.
