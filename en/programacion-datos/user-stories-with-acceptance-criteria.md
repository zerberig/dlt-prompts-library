Context: We're building [module or feature] in [your product]. It will be used by [user types]. Business rules and constraints: [business rules].
Goal: Get product, engineering, and QA to share the same definition of "done," and let users validate the feature during user acceptance testing (UAT).
Deliverable: The user stories needed, in the format "As a… I want… so that…", each with acceptance criteria in Given-When-Then format, including error and permission cases. Add UX and accessibility criteria where they apply, plus a list of open questions for the team.
Criteria: Every story must meet INVEST (independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small, testable); split any story that's too big. Criteria must be verifiable without knowing the code, with no vague terms like "fast" or "easy" unless they come with a measure.
Format: One section per story with its title, the story, numbered criteria, and a suggested priority.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
