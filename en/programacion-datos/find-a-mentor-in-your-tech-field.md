Context: I work in [your field or specialty] with [years of experience] in the field, and I want a mentor to help me with [what you want from mentorship]. I live in [your city or country] and can meet online or in person.
Goal: Find 2 or 3 good-fit people and start a mentoring relationship that's valuable for both sides.
Deliverable: 1) The ideal mentor profile for my goal. 2) 6 concrete places to look (communities, meetups, open-source projects, professional networks, mentorship programs) and how to stand out in each. 3) A short first-contact message in two versions: one for someone I barely know and one for someone who doesn't know me. 4) An agenda for the first conversation and a simple way to follow up.
Criteria: The first contact should ask for something small and specific (a 20-minute call or one concrete question), because asking "will you be my mentor?" outright usually gets a no. Include ideas for offering value in return. If you have web search, suggest active communities in my region.
Format: Sections with headings; the messages in separate blocks of 90 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
