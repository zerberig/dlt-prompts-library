Context: I work as [your role] and collaborate with [who you work with] (developers, product, design, clients, leadership). The situation I struggle with most is [difficult communication situation] (for example: giving feedback in code reviews, explaining delays, saying no to a request).
Goal: Identify where my communication needs work and practice concrete ways to handle it.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnostic with 10 self-assessment questions on clarity, listening, feedback, handling disagreement, and written communication. 2) For my difficult situation: why it usually goes wrong, a sample script, and 2 alternative phrasings. 3) 3 weekly practices to improve, each with a way to measure progress (for example, asking a teammate for feedback every two weeks).
Criteria: Realistic examples from a software team's day-to-day, with phrasing that sounds natural rather than like an HR manual. Focus on behaviors within my control.
Format: Numbered sections; the script as a short dialogue.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
