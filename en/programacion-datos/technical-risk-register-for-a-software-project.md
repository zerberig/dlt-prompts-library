Context: Here's the project: [project description]. It depends on these integrations and external systems: [integrations and dependencies]. Team and timeline: [team and timeline].
Goal: Anticipate what could derail the project and decide up front how to prevent it, so I can present it at kickoff to the team and stakeholders.
Deliverable: 1) 10-15 technical risks (third-party integrations, architecture, data, security, performance, dependencies, knowledge concentrated in one person, estimates). 2) For each: likelihood, impact, early warning sign, mitigation, contingency plan, and suggested owner. 3) The top 3 risks, each with an action for this week. 4) The project milestones at which the register should be reviewed.
Criteria: Risks specific to this project, not generic ones. Prioritize by likelihood times impact and explain the rating for the highest ones.
Format: A table with the columns Risk, Category, Likelihood (1-5), Impact (1-5), Score, Early warning, Mitigation, and Contingency, sorted by score.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
