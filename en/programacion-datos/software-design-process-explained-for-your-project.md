Answer as a tech lead coaching a small team through its first serious product.
Context: We're going to build [your product]. Team: [team size and experience]. Current stage: [current stage] (idea, prototype, or first version in production).
Goal: Understand which design steps to follow before and during development, so we don't skip decisions that get expensive later.
Deliverable: The phases of the design process (requirements, domain modeling, architecture, module and interface design, security, test planning, and documentation) applied to my product. For each phase: which decisions get made, what deliverable it produces, useful tools, best practices, the most common mistake, and how much effort to invest given our stage. At the end, a short design document template with its sections.
Criteria: Match the level of formality to the team size and stage, because a heavy process slows a small team down. Use concrete examples from my product in every phase.
Format: One section per phase with bullets; the template at the end as a list of headings.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
