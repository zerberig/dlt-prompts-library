Context: I work as [your role] in [your work situation] (company, freelance, remote, career change) and I feel like I'm going it alone. I'd like support with [what you need support with] (learning, technical questions, staying consistent, career decisions).
Goal: Over the next 3 months, build a small but active support network that pushes me forward and that I contribute to as well.
Deliverable: 1) A map of the ideal network with 4 relationship types (peers, more experienced people, community, and an accountability partner) and what each one offers. 2) Concrete monthly actions to build it. 3) A simple accountability system with a partner: frequency, check-in format, and what to do when someone falls behind. 4) Ideas for asking for help without feeling like a burden.
Criteria: Realistic actions for someone short on time (2 hours a week max). Include how to give value to others, because networks run on reciprocity. Add brief self-compassion practices for tough weeks.
Format: Sections with headings and a monthly plan in a table with the columns Month, Action, and Expected result.
