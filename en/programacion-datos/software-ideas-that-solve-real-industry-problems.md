Context: I want to build a software product for [industry or niche]. I know this sector because [your experience or connection to the industry], and I can build with [your technical skills or resources].
Goal: Find software ideas that solve real, recurring problems in the sector and pick one to validate with customers.
Deliverable: 1) The 6 most costly or frustrating operational problems in the sector and who experiences them. 2) One software idea per problem: what it does, the main user, and how the problem is handled today. 3) For the 2 most promising ideas: the scope of an MVP I could build in 6 to 8 weeks and 3 questions for interviewing potential customers before writing code.
Criteria: Start from the problems, not the technology; use AI or automation only where it adds a clear advantage. If you have web search, check which competitors exist and mention them by type. Flag as an estimate any market size or figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Problem, Who has it, Idea, Current alternative, and Build difficulty (low, medium, high); then the 2 finalist ideas in their own sections.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
