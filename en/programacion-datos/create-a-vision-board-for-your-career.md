Help me create a vision board for my career in [your professional field] over the next [time horizon]. First, ask me 5 questions to clarify what I want to achieve, how I want my work to feel, and what matters to me outside of it. Based on my answers, propose 4 to 6 sections for the board, what image, phrase, or symbol to put in each, and one concrete action tied to each section, because the board works better as a reminder of what I'll do than as a wish. Finish with ideas for building it digitally or on paper and a way to review it every month.
