Context: I'm [your role and experience] and I want to learn [technology or skill]. I can put in [hours per week], and my biggest challenge is [your main obstacle] (little time, not knowing where to start, losing consistency, etc.).
Goal: Go from the basics to using this technology in a real project, with a plan that fits my life.
Deliverable: A 12-week plan split into 3 phases, with weekly goals, a mini project at the end of each phase, and a final project I can show in my portfolio.
Criteria: Every week needs a checkable goal ("deploy an API with 3 endpoints"), not just topics to study, so I'll know whether I made progress. Favor practice over consuming content. Include a concrete strategy for my obstacle and a review or buffer week in case I fall behind. Suggest types of resources (official docs, courses, exercises) rather than links.
Format: A table with the columns Week, Goal, Activities, and Deliverable; then each phase's projects and the obstacle strategy as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
