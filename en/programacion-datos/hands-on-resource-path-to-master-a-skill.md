Goal: A short selection of practical resources, without wasting time on generic or repetitive content.
Deliverable: A 3-stage path (fundamentals, practice, and going deeper) with 2 or 3 resources per stage: books, courses, podcasts, documentation, communities, or tools. For each resource: what it is, why it belongs in that stage, roughly how much time it takes, whether it's free or paid, and what exercise to do after using it.
Criteria: Favor resources that lead to action and are still current. Use web search if you have it to confirm they exist and are still available; don't invent titles or authors, and mark anything you can't confirm as unverified.
Format: One table per stage with the columns Resource, Type, Why, Time, Cost, and Exercise.
