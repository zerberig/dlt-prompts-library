Context: I'm part of a team that works with [your tech stack] in the [your industry] sector. We're setting our technical priorities for the next 12 months.
Goal: Understand which software development trends actually affect us, separate signal from noise, and decide what to explore.
Deliverable: The 10 trends most relevant to my context (for example: AI agent-assisted development, platform engineering, software supply chain security). For each one: what it is in 2 lines, maturity level, expected impact on my team, and one concrete action (adopt, trial, watch, or ignore).
Criteria: Use web search if you have it so you're working from current information, and cite a source for each trend. Flag as an estimate any adoption or growth figure you can't back up with a source. Prioritize by impact on my stack and industry, not by general popularity.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, What it is, Maturity, Impact for us, Action, and Source; at the end, a summary with 3 recommendations for next quarter.
