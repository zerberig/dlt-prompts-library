Context: [company name] is a family business in [industry and company size]. It's currently led by [who leads it today], and the potential successors are: [potential successors and their current roles]. We want to complete the transition within [target succession timeline].
Goal: I want an orderly transition that protects both business continuity and family relationships.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of the issues to resolve: leadership, ownership, and the roles of family members who do and don't work in the business. 2) Objective criteria for choosing and preparing the successor, with a staged development plan. 3) Options for transferring ownership (gradual, during life, by inheritance, through a trust or shareholder agreement), with the pros and risks of each. 4) A governance proposal: board, family constitution, and rules for resolving conflicts. 5) A roadmap with quarterly milestones.
Criteria: Keep business, family, and ownership issues separate, because mixing them is the most common source of conflict. Raise the hard questions instead of assuming agreement. Flag which decisions require a lawyer, accountant, or tax adviser in my country.
Format: Sections with headings, a transfer options table with the columns Option, Pros, Risks, and Adviser needed, and the roadmap as a quarterly table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
