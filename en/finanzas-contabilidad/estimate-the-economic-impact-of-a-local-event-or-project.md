Context: I'm preparing a report on [event or project] in [city or region]. Available data: [event or project data] (expected attendance or investment, duration, jobs, local suppliers, where visitors come from). The report is for [report audience] (local government, sponsors, investors).
Goal: I want a credible, transparent estimate of the economic impact that holds up to a skeptical reader's questions.
Deliverable: 1) Direct impact: visitor spending or investment that stays in the area. 2) Indirect (suppliers) and induced (wage spending) impact, with the multiplier used and its justification. 3) Estimated jobs (temporary and permanent) and approximate local tax revenue. 4) Costs or negative effects to consider (displaced local spending, public services, congestion). 5) A one-page executive summary.
Criteria: State every assumption and separate new spending from spending that just shifts within the area, because mixing them up inflates the result. If you have web search, look for comparable studies or regional multipliers and cite the source. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Give ranges instead of single figures.
Format: An assumptions table, a results table with the columns Impact type, Low range, High range, and Source or assumption, and the executive summary in paragraphs.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
