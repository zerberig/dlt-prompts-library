Context: I'm analyzing [company or ticker], and my investment horizon is [investment horizon]. My main question is: [what you want to decide or understand].
Goal: I want an objective analysis that helps me judge whether the current price makes sense relative to the business, not a buy or sell recommendation.
Deliverable: 1) Business summary: how it makes money, competitive advantages, and key risks. 2) A discounted cash flow valuation with explicit assumptions (growth, margins, discount rate, terminal value). 3) A relative valuation with 3–5 comparables and the multiples used (P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B). 4) Three scenarios (bear, base, bull) with a per-share value and what would have to happen in each. 5) The 3 indicators I should watch to know if my thesis breaks.
Criteria: If you have web search, use the latest financial statements and price, and cite the date and source of each data point. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Clearly separate facts from your assumptions. Include a sensitivity table of value by discount rate and growth. This is analysis material: remind me the final decision is mine or one to make with a registered adviser.
Format: Sections with headings, tables for assumptions, comparables, scenarios, and sensitivity, and a conclusion of no more than 5 bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
