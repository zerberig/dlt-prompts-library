Context: These are my assets and debts, with their approximate value: [list of your assets and debts]. I've never calculated my net worth.
Goal: I want to know what I'm worth financially today and have a simple way to see whether I'm making progress.
Deliverable: 1) An explanation of net worth in 2 sentences. 2) My calculation organized into liquid assets, investments, personal-use assets (home, car), and short- and long-term debts. 3) Two readings of the result: total net worth and net worth excluding home and car. 4) A spreadsheet template to update it every quarter, with the change formula. 5) Three actions that would move my net worth the most over the next year.
Criteria: Value assets at a realistic sale price, not what I paid for them, and explain why. Use plain language.
Format: An assets table and a debts table with the columns Item and Value, a total row, and bullets for the reading and actions.
