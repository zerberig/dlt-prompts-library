Context: We want to launch [product name], an AI-powered financial news aggregator for [target users] (beginner investors, analysts, business owners, etc.) in [market or region]. What sets us apart is [your differentiator] (portfolio-based personalization, summaries, sentiment analysis, alerts).
Goal: I want a clear product document so design and engineering can build an MVP within [MVP timeline], and so we know how to measure whether it works.
Deliverable: 1) Two or three personas with their jobs to be done, current frustrations, and usage frequency. 2) 12–15 user stories with acceptance criteria. 3) Prioritized MVP features versus later ones, with the reasoning. 4) Content requirements: sources, usage rights, refresh frequency, and handling of errors and unverified news. 5) How to present summaries and sentiment signals so they don't read as investment advice. 6) Success metrics (activation, retention, alert engagement) and initial targets.
Criteria: Tie every MVP feature to a specific persona's problem. Include source attribution and clear notices about AI-generated content, because in finance trust and regulatory compliance matter as much as features.
Format: Sections with headings; user stories as a table with the columns As a, I want, So that, and Acceptance criteria; prioritization as an MVP vs. Later table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
