Context: I live in [your country] and I'm filing my annual personal tax return. My situation: [your income sources], and these are my relevant expenses for the year: [relevant expenses for the year] (medical, tuition, mortgage interest, voluntary retirement contributions, donations, etc.).
Goal: I want to identify every deduction and tax benefit that could genuinely apply to me, so I don't overpay or claim something I'm not entitled to.
Deliverable: First, ask me up to 5 targeted questions about my situation. Using my answers, build a checklist of possible deductions, each with its key requirement, approximate cap, the receipt I need, and whether it applies, may apply, or doesn't apply.
Criteria: If you have web search, check the current rules from my country's tax authority and cite the source; flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Explain each deduction in plain language. Close by pointing out which items I should confirm with an accountant before filing.
Format: A table with the columns Deduction, Requirement, Approximate cap, Receipt, and Applies to me?; then a to-do list sorted by deadline.
