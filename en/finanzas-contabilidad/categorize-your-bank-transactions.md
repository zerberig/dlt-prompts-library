Context: These are my transactions for [period covered], copied from my bank statement or banking app: [your transactions].
Goal: I want to see clearly where my money goes so I can build a realistic budget.
Deliverable: 1) Every transaction assigned to a category (housing, groceries, transportation, eating out, utilities, subscriptions, health, entertainment, debt, other). 2) Totals by category with their percentage of spending. 3) A list of subscriptions and recurring charges. 4) The most frequent small purchases and how much they add up to per month.
Criteria: If a transaction is ambiguous, mark it "to confirm" instead of guessing. Use the same categories throughout so I can repeat this every month.
Format: A table with the columns Date, Description, Amount, and Category; then a summary table by category and bullets for subscriptions and small purchases.
