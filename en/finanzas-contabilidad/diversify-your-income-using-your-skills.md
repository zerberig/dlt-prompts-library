Deliverable: 1) Two ways to increase my current income (negotiating, changing arrangements, raising rates) with concrete arguments. 2) Five ideas for new income streams that use my skills, each with upfront cost, hours per week, estimated time to first income, possible income range, and main risk. 3) The idea I should test first and a 30-day plan to validate it.
Criteria: Prioritize ideas I can validate quickly and cheaply. Be honest about so-called "passive" income: say how much upfront work it takes. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Idea, Upfront cost, Hours per week, First income, Income range, and Risk; then the 30-day plan as a week-by-week list.
