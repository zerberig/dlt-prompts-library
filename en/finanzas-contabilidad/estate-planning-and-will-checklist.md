Context: I live in [your country]. My family situation is [your family situation] (marital status, minor children, dependents), and my main assets are: [your main assets] (home, accounts, insurance, business, retirement accounts).
Goal: I want my affairs in order so that, if something happens to me, my family doesn't face long procedures, conflicts, or assets with no clear owner.
Deliverable: 1) A checklist of the documents and decisions I should have: will, beneficiary designations on accounts and insurance, powers of attorney for financial and medical decisions, guardianship for minors, and trusts if relevant. 2) For each item: what it is in one sentence, why it matters in my situation, and who handles it (notary, lawyer, bank, insurer). 3) An inventory of what my family would need to find (passwords, policies, contacts) and how to organize it securely.
Criteria: Explain without jargon and adapt the terms to my country's legal framework; if you have web search, check what these instruments are called and how they work there. This is a guide to prepare for my appointment: point out which decisions require a lawyer or notary.
Format: A checkbox list grouped by priority (do this month, this quarter, review every year).
