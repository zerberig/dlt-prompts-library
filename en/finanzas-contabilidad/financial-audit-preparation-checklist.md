Context: [your company name] is a [industry and size] company facing a [type of audit] (external financial statement audit, tax audit, investor review, etc.) starting on [audit start date]. Our accounting team is [your accounting team].
Goal: I want to walk into the audit with complete, organized information to cut down on last-minute requests and potential findings.
Deliverable: 1) A checklist of documents and information: financial statements, bank reconciliations, AR and AP subledgers, inventory, fixed assets, contracts, payroll, tax returns, and board minutes. 2) For each item: period covered, suggested owner, and red flags auditors usually look for. 3) A week-by-week prep schedule up to the start date. 4) The 5 most common findings in companies like mine and how to prevent them.
Criteria: Prioritize whatever takes longest to gather. Use practical language so anyone on the team knows what to hand over.
Format: A table with the columns Document, Period, Owner, Status, and Red flag; then the weekly schedule and the list of findings.
