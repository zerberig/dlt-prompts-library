Context: At [your company name], a [industry and size] company, we've spotted this possible irregularity: [description of the suspected irregularity] (for example, payments to an unknown vendor, inventory shortfalls, or duplicate expenses). We found it through [how it was detected], and the period involved is [period involved].
Goal: I want an orderly investigation plan that confirms or rules out the irregularity without destroying evidence or accusing anyone without grounds.
Deliverable: 1) The first 48 hours: what to do and what to avoid (preserving records, restricting access, confidentiality). 2) Scope definition: questions to answer, areas, accounts, and periods. 3) Evidence to collect (documentary, digital, and testimonial) and how to maintain chain of custody. 4) Applicable analysis techniques (trend and ratio analysis, Benford's law, vendor-employee master file matching, duplicate testing) and what each would look for. 5) The final report structure. 6) Controls to prevent a repeat.
Criteria: Keep the tone neutral and fact-based. Say at what point to bring in a forensic accountant, a lawyer, or the authorities, because mishandling can invalidate evidence.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets; the evidence as a table with the columns Type, Source, Owner, and How to preserve it.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
