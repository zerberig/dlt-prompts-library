I want to achieve this: [your financial aspiration]. My monthly take-home income is [monthly take-home income], and right now I can put about [amount available per month] toward it.
Help me turn it into a SMART goal (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound). First, ask me 2 or 3 questions to understand what achieving it means to me. Then give me: the goal written as one sentence with an amount and a date, why it meets each SMART criterion, 3 intermediate milestones with dates, and the 3 actions I should take this week.
If the goal isn't achievable with what I can contribute, tell me and suggest adjusting the amount or the timeline. Answer with bullets, 250 words max.
