Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis: monthly savings capacity, savings rate, and debt health. 2) My goals ranked with an explicit criterion (safety first, cost of debt, timeline, importance). 3) The monthly allocation to each goal and the estimated date I'd reach it. 4) The right type of instrument for each goal given its timeline and risk, without naming specific products. 5) Which goals aren't feasible with my current numbers and options to adjust them. 6) A quarterly review plan with indicators.
Criteria: State return and inflation assumptions in a table. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. If two goals compete, explain the trade-off rather than silently deciding for me. This is for planning: point out which parts would be worth reviewing with a certified financial planner.
Format: Sections with headings; a goals table with the columns Goal, Amount, Timeline, Priority, Monthly contribution, Instrument, and Estimated date; bullets for adjustments and review.
