Context: I live in [your country] and want to give about [annual giving amount] a year to charitable causes. The causes I care most about are: [causes you care about]. I can also offer [time or skills you can donate].
Goal: I want my giving to have real impact and reflect my values, instead of scattering it.
Deliverable: 1) A short giving mission statement based on my causes. 2) Ways to give, compared: direct gifts, recurring gifts, volunteering, in-kind donations, and vehicles like donor-advised funds if they exist in my country. 3) Criteria for evaluating organizations (transparency, measurable results, use of funds) and where to check whether they're authorized to receive tax-deductible donations. 4) A suggested split of my annual amount by cause and way of giving. 5) A simple calendar for the year.
Criteria: If you have web search, check the current rules on deductible donations in my country and cite the source; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. The tax benefit is secondary to impact; mention it without making it the focus. For large amounts, suggest confirming the tax treatment with an accountant.
Format: Sections with headings, a ways-to-give table with the columns Method, Advantages, Considerations, and Possible tax benefit, and the split as a table.
