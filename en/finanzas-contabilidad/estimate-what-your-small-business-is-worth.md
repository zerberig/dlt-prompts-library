Context: I run a [type of business] that has been operating for [years in business]. Here are my figures for recent years: [business financial data] (revenue, profit, owner's pay, assets, and debts). I need the valuation to [why you need the valuation] (sell, bring in a partner, raise money, plan succession).
Goal: I want a reasonable value range and an understanding of what it's based on, so I can negotiate or plan from solid ground.
Deliverable: 1) Normalized earnings: adjustments for owner's pay at market rate and personal or one-off expenses. 2) A valuation using an earnings multiple, with the multiple range used and why. 3) A net asset valuation. 4) A final value range and how the methods reconcile. 5) The factors that raise or lower the multiple in my case (owner dependence, customer concentration, recurring revenue, growth) and 3 actions to increase value in 12–24 months.
Criteria: Show every calculation. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Present the result as a preliminary estimate: for a real transaction, recommend a formal valuation from a specialist.
Format: An adjustments table, a table with the columns Method, Assumptions, and Value, and bullets for factors and actions.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
