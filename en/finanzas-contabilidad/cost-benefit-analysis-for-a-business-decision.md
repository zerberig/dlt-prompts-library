Context: My company is evaluating [decision to evaluate] (for example, launching a product, hiring, or investing in software). The upfront investment would be [upfront investment], and we expect [expected benefits]. The evaluation horizon is [evaluation horizon].
Goal: I want to decide with numbers whether it's worth doing, and present the analysis to [who makes the decision].
Deliverable: 1) A complete list of costs (upfront, recurring, hidden, and opportunity costs) and benefits (tangible and intangible), quantified where possible. 2) Payback period, net present value, and ROI, using a discount rate you propose and justify. 3) Three scenarios (pessimistic, base, optimistic). 4) The assumptions that move the result the most. 5) A clear recommendation and the conditions that would change it.
Criteria: Ask me questions to estimate hidden costs before calculating. Keep measurable benefits separate from qualitative ones and don't add them into the same number. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table of costs and benefits by year, a metrics table by scenario, and an executive summary of 150 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
