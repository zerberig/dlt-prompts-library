Context: This is my portfolio with each holding, amount invested, current value, and purchase date: [your portfolio]. My goal is [your investment goal] over [investment horizon], and my risk profile is [your risk profile].
Goal: I want to know whether my portfolio is on track for my goal and whether I should adjust anything.
Deliverable: 1) Total and annualized return for each holding and for the portfolio. 2) A comparison against an appropriate benchmark for each asset class. 3) Current allocation by asset class, sector, currency, and country versus what fits my profile. 4) Concentration risks and costs (fees) dragging down returns. 5) Two or three rebalancing options with pros and cons.
Criteria: Show how you calculated each return. If you have web search, use up-to-date prices and benchmarks with their dates. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Don't recommend buying or selling specific securities: describe adjustments by asset class. Close by suggesting I review major changes with a registered investment adviser.
Format: A holdings table with the columns Holding, Current value, Total return, Annualized return, and Weight; then a current vs. target allocation table and bullets for risks and options.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
