Context: I'll share an article about [article topic]. The video is for [target audience], will be published on [platform], and should run about [video length].
Goal: Repurpose the article as a video that gets its key ideas across and holds attention to the end, without sounding like text being read aloud.
Deliverable:
1) The article's 3–5 key ideas and which ones get cut for length.
2) A full script: a 10–15 second hook, an introduction with the video's promise, the body by section, and a close with a call to action.
3) For each section, visual suggestions (B-roll, charts, on-screen text, animations) and a retention hook leading into the next one.
4) 3 title options and a thumbnail idea.
Criteria: Rewrite in spoken language, with short sentences, questions, and concrete examples. Keep the article's data exactly as written and point out anything worth verifying before publishing. Match the amount of text to the length (about 150 words per minute of narration).
Format: Key ideas as a list, then the script in a table with the columns Time, Narration, Visual.
Article: [article text]
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
