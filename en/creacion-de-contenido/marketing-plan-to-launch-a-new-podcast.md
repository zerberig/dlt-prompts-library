Context: I'm launching a podcast about [podcast topic] for [target audience] on [launch date]. These are my resources: [available resources] (existing audience, budget, contacts).
Goal: Launch with an initial base of listeners and get reviews and recommendations from the first week.
Deliverable:
1) Pre-launch (4 weeks out): how to build anticipation, a waitlist, a trailer, and how many episodes to release on day one.
2) Launch week: day-by-day actions across social, email, communities, and personal contacts.
3) First 90 days: growth tactics, partnerships with niche creators and media, guest appearances, and paid promotion options if the budget allows.
4) Social content ideas and suggested hashtags.
5) Targets and metrics for each stage.
Criteria: Review requests should be honest invitations to real listeners, with no incentives in exchange for ratings. Prioritize tactics that fit my resources. Include what to adjust if first-week results fall short of the targets. Clearly flag any expected results as estimates.
Format: Sections by stage with action lists; targets in a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
