Context: I have a podcast about [podcast topic] in [language and region] and want to stay current.
Goal: Spot industry shifts early that could hurt or help my show, and decide which ones to act on.
Deliverable: 1) The 6–8 most relevant trends in podcasting today: formats, video consumption, platforms, discovery, monetization, and AI in production. 2) For each, the evidence or source, how mature it is, and what it means for a podcast like mine. 3) The 3 clearest opportunities for my niche, with one concrete action each. 4) Events, conferences, or communities where I can keep learning and network.
Criteria: Use web search and cite sources with their dates; separate trends confirmed by data from predictions, and flag anything you can't back up as an estimate. Prioritize what applies to an independent creator or small team.
Format: A table with the columns Trend, Evidence, Maturity, What it means for me; then opportunities and events as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
