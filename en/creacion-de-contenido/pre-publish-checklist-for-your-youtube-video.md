Context: I'm about to publish a YouTube video about [video topic] for [target audience]. Here's what I have: [title, description, thumbnail idea, and length].
Goal: Catch anything that could hold back reach or retention and fix it before publishing.
Deliverable: A best-practices checklist applied to my video: title, thumbnail, first 30 seconds, description, chapters, captions, end screen, cards, playlist, and pinned comment. For each item, say whether it passes, what's off, and the specific fix.
Criteria: Prioritize what most affects whether someone clicks and keeps watching. If my title can be improved, suggest 3 alternatives.
Format: A table with the columns Element, Status, Fix, ordered from highest to lowest impact.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
