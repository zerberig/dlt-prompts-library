Context: My podcast is about [podcast topic], reaches [approximate audience size], and I want to collaborate with guests, experts, and other podcasts.
Goal: Grow through collaborations that add value for both audiences, not just shout-out swaps.
Deliverable: 1) 3 ideal collaborator profiles and where to find them. 2) 5 collaboration formats (guest swap, joint episode, promo swap, panel, shared series) with what each side gains. 3) A short, customizable invitation email for a guest and another for a partner podcast. 4) A simple process for following up and measuring results.
Criteria: Emails should be short, show I know the other person's work, and make clear what's in it for them. Friendly tone, no pressure.
Format: Numbered sections; emails in separate blocks, each with its subject line.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
