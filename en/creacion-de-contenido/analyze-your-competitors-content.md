Context: I run [your business or site] in the [your industry] space and compete with [main competitors]. My current content: [channels and type of content you publish].
Goal: Understand which content works for my competitors and why, so I can create better, more useful content rather than copies.
Deliverable:
1) For each competitor: main topics, formats, frequency, tone, and the pieces that seem to perform best (by ranking, engagement, or mentions).
2) Patterns in what works: hooks, structures, depth, and recurring keywords.
3) Gaps: questions my audience has that nobody answers well, and topics where I can bring more expertise or original data.
4) 10 prioritized content ideas, each with format, target keyword, and differentiating angle.
5) A method for repeating this analysis every quarter.
Criteria: Use web search to review their recent content and cite the pieces you analyze. Don't invent traffic metrics or rankings; flag any indicator you infer as an estimate. Each idea must beat what already exists in some concrete way.
Format: One table per competitor, gaps as a list, and ideas in a table with the columns Idea, Format, Keyword, Angle, Priority.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
