Context: I'm a YouTube creator covering [channel topic]. These are the tasks I do every week and how long each takes: [weekly tasks and time]. My monthly tool budget is [monthly budget].
Goal: I want to win back hours each week to spend on creating, by automating or simplifying whatever doesn't need my judgment.
Deliverable: 1) My tasks sorted into: fully automate, speed up with AI or templates, delegate, and keep. 2) For the tasks that can be automated or sped up: the specific shortcut or tool, roughly how much time it saves, and the effort to set it up. 3) The 5 actions with the most time saved per effort, to start this week. 4) An estimate of hours recovered per week.
Criteria: Fit the suggestions to my budget and include free options. If you have web search, check that the tools are still available and their prices in [currency]. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Task, Current time, Category, Solution, Estimated savings, and Effort; then the list of 5 actions.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
