Context: I have a podcast called [podcast name] about [podcast topic], aimed at [target audience]. My current visual identity is: [existing colors, fonts, or logo].
Goal: I want cover art for audio platforms and a YouTube banner that are instantly recognizable, even as thumbnails, and that say what the show is about in one second.
Deliverable: 3 distinct visual concepts. For each one: the core idea in one sentence, composition, palette, typography, a tagline of 6 words max, and a natural-language image prompt to generate the base with an image model.
Criteria: The show name must be readable at thumbnail size, so keep words few and contrast high. Keep the banner's key text inside the safe area that shows on phones. If you have web search, confirm the current cover and banner dimensions for each platform and flag anything you can't confirm.
Format: One block per concept with subheadings, then a table with dimensions and file formats per platform.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
