Context: This is the episode [episode title] of my podcast, aimed at [target audience]. Here is the transcript: [episode transcript].
Goal: I want to get more out of every episode by publishing its best ideas in other formats, to reach people who don't listen to podcasts.
Deliverable, fully written:
1) A 600-800 word blog post built on the episode's central idea, with subheadings.
2) 3 posts for LinkedIn or Instagram, each with a different angle.
3) A 5-7 post thread for X or Threads.
4) 3 moments to cut as vertical clips, with the exact start and end quote and suggested on-screen text.
5) A short email to my list inviting people to listen.
Criteria: Use only ideas and facts that appear in the transcript; if something needs extra context, flag it instead of making it up. Each piece should stand on its own for someone who hasn't heard the episode. Keep my way of speaking.
Format: One section per piece with a clear heading.
