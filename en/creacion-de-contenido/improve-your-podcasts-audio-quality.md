Goal: I want the podcast to sound clear and pleasant on headphones, speakers, and in the car, so audio quality is never a reason to stop listening.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis of the likely cause of each problem. 2) Free improvements to the space and recording technique (mic distance, room treatment, levels). 3) A recommended editing chain in order (noise cleanup, EQ, compression, normalization to a loudness level suited to podcasts), with starting values. 4) Which gear to replace first if I invest, prioritized for my budget. 5) A pre-recording checklist.
Criteria: Start with what improves the sound most for the least money, because the room and technique usually matter more than the mic. If you mention recommended loudness levels per platform, check them with web search if you have it. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets; the editing chain as a table (Step, Setting, Starting value, What it does).
