Context: I make videos for [platform] about [product category] for [target audience]. I want to review these trending products: [products to review]. My relationship with the brands is [relationship with the brands] (I bought them, they were sent to me, it's paid content).
Goal: I want a video that rides the interest in these products and earns the audience's trust by being clear about the good and the bad.
Deliverable: A 45-90 second script with: a hook that asks the buyer's question, an intro to each product, one concrete test or demo per product, pros and cons, who it's for and who it isn't, a final verdict, and a call to action. Include on-screen text with key data and the sponsorship or gifting disclosure at the start if it applies.
Criteria: Base claims on what I can show or verify, without exaggerating results or passing off other people's testimonials as my own. The sponsorship disclosure goes at the start, because platforms and advertising law in many countries require it.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Visual, Voice, and On-screen text.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
