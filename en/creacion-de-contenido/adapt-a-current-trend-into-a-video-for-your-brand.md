Context: My brand is [your brand or business]; it sells [your product or service] and speaks to [target audience] in a [brand tone] tone. I want to post on [platform].
Goal: I want to join current trends on the platform in a way that feels natural for my brand and draws in new people, not just copy what everyone else is doing.
Deliverable: 1) 3 current trends, formats, or topics on the platform that fit my brand, explaining what they are and why they fit. 2) For each, a 20-45 second script with a hook in the first 2 seconds, development, closing, and on-screen text, adapted to my product. 3) What kind of audio or music to use, with the recommendation to pick it from the platform's licensed library.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to identify current trends, because they change every week; if you can't verify them, say so and suggest evergreen formats instead. Keep the brand voice and avoid trends that are controversial or unrelated to what I sell.
Format: One section per trend with its script as a table (Time, Visual, Audio, On-screen text).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
