Context: I'm interviewing [guest name and expertise] on my podcast about [podcast topic]. Here's what I know about their background: [what you know about the guest]. The episode's goal is [episode goal].
Goal: A conversation that reveals the guest's real experience, leaves my audience with useful ideas, and doesn't repeat what they've said in other interviews.
Deliverable: 1) A summary of what they're known for and the less-explored angles (if you have web search, review recent interviews and flag anything you can't confirm). 2) An interview guide in blocks: opening, personal story, core expertise, debatable topics, practical advice, and close. 3) 15–20 open-ended questions spread across those blocks, with 1–2 follow-ups for the most important ones. 4) A short message to send the guest with the topics before recording.
Criteria: Favor questions that ask for examples, decisions, and specific moments over generic opinions. Avoid yes-or-no questions.
Format: Numbered sections; questions listed by block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
