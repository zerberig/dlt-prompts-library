Context: I want a demo video for [your product] aimed at [target audience]. Its main features are [main features] and the problem it solves is [customer's main problem].
Goal: Within a few minutes, viewers should understand how to use it and what they gain, and take the next step (try it, buy it, or book a call).
Deliverable: A 2–3 minute script with: a hook that shows the problem in action, a one-line product introduction, a demo of 3 features applied to real cases (each with situation, on-screen action, and result), an answer to the most common objection, and a specific call to action. Add 3 title options for the video.
Criteria: Show, don't tell: every feature should be seen working. Use concrete, verifiable benefits, with no empty superlatives like "revolutionary". Natural language for narration.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Narration, On screen.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
