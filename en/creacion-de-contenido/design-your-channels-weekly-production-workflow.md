Context: I run the YouTube channel for [your business or project] with this team: [people and roles]. We publish [publishing frequency] and currently use [current tools].
Goal: A clear, repeatable weekly process that cuts bottlenecks and delays, and automates whatever doesn't need human judgment.
Deliverable:
1) A stage-by-stage workflow: ideas, keyword research, script, recording, editing, thumbnail, captions, metadata, scheduling, promotion, comment replies, and analytics.
2) For each stage: owner, deliverable, estimated time, tool, and whether it's automated, AI-assisted, or manual.
3) A typical weekly calendar with production and publishing days.
4) A 30-minute weekly metrics review routine with the 5 questions that drive decisions.
5) The 3 highest-impact changes to start with.
Criteria: Design for my team's real size. Every automation should save time without lowering quality; point out where human review is needed before publishing. Where one stage depends on another, note the dependency so delays become visible early.
Format: A table with the columns Stage, Owner, Deliverable, Time, Tool, Type; then the calendar and routine as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
