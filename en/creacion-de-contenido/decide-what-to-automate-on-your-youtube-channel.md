Context: I manage the YouTube channel for [your business or project]. These tasks take up most of my time: [tasks that take most of your time]. I'm considering automating part of the process with tools or AI.
Goal: Decide carefully what to automate and what not to, without putting quality, my relationship with the audience, or the channel's monetization at risk.
Deliverable: 1) For each task, an assessment of benefit, risk, and recommended level of automation (full, assisted, or manual). 2) The main risks of over-automating, including those tied to YouTube's policies on repetitive or mass-produced content. 3) A final recommendation with the first 3 automations to try and how to measure whether they work.
Criteria: If you have web search, check YouTube's and its Partner Program's current policies and cite what you find; flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Be honest about the limits: if something shouldn't be automated, say so and explain why.
Format: A table with the columns Task, Benefit, Risk, Recommendation; then the risks and recommendation as short lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
