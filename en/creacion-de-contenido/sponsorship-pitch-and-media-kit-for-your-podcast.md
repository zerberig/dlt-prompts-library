Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic]. Here is my real data: [podcast metrics] (downloads or plays per episode, audience profile, social accounts, engagement). I want to pitch a sponsorship to [brand or type of brand], which sells [what the brand sells].
Goal: I want to land sponsors that fit my audience, with a professional pitch that makes clear what they get.
Deliverable:
1) A one-page media kit: show description, who it reaches, key metrics, available ad formats, and testimonials or past results if I have them.
2) 3 sponsorship packages (basic, standard, and premium) with what each includes and a suggested price range in [currency].
3) A pitch email tailored to the brand: why it fits my audience, a concrete integration idea, and the next step.
4) How to measure and report results to the sponsor (codes, tracked links, surveys).
5) Criteria for deciding which brands to accept or turn down.
Criteria: Use only the metrics I gave you, without inflating them, because a sponsor who finds exaggerated data won't come back. Include disclosure to the audience about sponsored content. If you have web search, look up current rate benchmarks. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: Numbered sections; the media kit and the email ready to copy.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
