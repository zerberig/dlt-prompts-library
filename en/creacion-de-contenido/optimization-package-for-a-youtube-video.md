Context: I'm about to publish a YouTube video about [video topic] for [target audience]. Here is the script, transcript, or summary: [video script or summary].
Goal: I want the video to surface in search and recommendations, and give people who see it in their suggestions a clear reason to click.
Deliverable: 1) The main search phrase and 4 secondary ones, with the intent behind each. 2) 5 title options (60 characters max) with different angles: benefit, curiosity, number, question, and result. 3) 3 thumbnail concepts with a main image and text of 4 words max that complements the title. 4) A description whose first 2 lines are written to hook, a summary, timestamped chapters if the material allows it, and a call to action. 5) 8-10 tags.
Criteria: The video has to deliver what the title and thumbnail promise, because false promises sink retention and recommendations. If you have web search, check how my audience phrases the topic and flag any search-volume figure as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections; titles and description ready to copy.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
