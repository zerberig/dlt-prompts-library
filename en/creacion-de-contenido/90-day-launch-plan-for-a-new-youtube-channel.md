Context: I'm launching a YouTube channel about [channel topic] for [target audience]. These are the resources I already have: [current resources] (for example, followers on other platforms, an email list, contacts with other creators, or nothing yet).
Goal: I want a launch plan for the first 90 days that helps me win my first quality subscribers and learn which kind of video works with my audience.
Deliverable:
1) The channel's positioning in 2 sentences: who it's for and what promise every video delivers.
2) A 3-phase plan (days 1-30, 31-60, 61-90) with each phase's goal, how many long videos and Shorts to publish, and which promotion actions to take on social media, email, and collaborations.
3) A sample of 5 social posts announcing the channel or a video, adapted to [main social platforms].
4) The 4 metrics I should check at the end of each phase and what decision to make depending on the result.
Criteria: Be realistic about what one person can produce. Center promotion on giving value rather than asking for subscriptions, because value is what makes people stay. If you have web search, check YouTube's current practices for new channels and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: Numbered sections with headings, phase 2 as a table (Week, Content, Promotion, Target). 900 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
