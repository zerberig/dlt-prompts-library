Context: I'm interviewing [guest name], who works in [what the guest does], on my podcast about [podcast topic]. This is what I know about their work: [guest information] (bio, summarized links, publications). The interview will run [interview length].
Goal: I want a conversation that draws out stories and lessons the guest hasn't told a thousand times, and leaves my listeners with something they can apply.
Deliverable: 1) A summary of the topics where the guest adds the most value for my audience. 2) A prep email for the guest with logistics, topics, and technical tips. 3) A guide of 12-15 questions in blocks (opening, backstory, key ideas, practical application, lightning round), marking the 5 must-asks and 3 possible follow-ups for each. 4) How to handle short answers, tangents, or sensitive topics. 5) A follow-up plan: thank-you message, assets for them to share, and release date.
Criteria: Use only the information I gave you about the guest; if something isn't confirmed, frame it as a question rather than a statement. Favor open, specific questions about experiences, because they get better answers than generic ones.
Format: Numbered sections; the questions as a list grouped by block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
