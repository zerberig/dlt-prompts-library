Context: I'm launching [your product or service] for [target audience]. The problem it solves and its 3 key benefits are: [problem and main benefits]. The video will run [video length] and be published on [platform].
Goal: I want a launch video people watch to the end because it follows a story, not because they're being sold to, and that leaves them wanting to try the product.
Deliverable: A script with a character who lives the problem, the moment they discover the solution, a transformation that shows the benefits in action, and a closing with a call to action. Include 2 hook options for the first 3 seconds.
Criteria: Show the benefits in concrete scenes instead of listing them. Use only claims the product can deliver, with no exaggerated promises. Match the pacing to the length: in short videos, one scene per stage.
Format: A table with the columns Scene, Time, Visual, Narration or dialogue, and On-screen text.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
