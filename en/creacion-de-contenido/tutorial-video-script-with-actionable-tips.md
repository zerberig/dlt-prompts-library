Context: I want a YouTube video that teaches [target audience] how to [what the viewer will learn to do]. Their current level: [viewer's knowledge level]. Target length: [video length].
Goal: Viewers should finish able to apply what they learned and eager to watch more from the channel.
Deliverable: A full script with: a 10–15 second hook that shows the end result, the video's promise, the tips or steps with one concrete example each, a common mistake and how to avoid it, a quick recap, and a call to action pointing to another video.
Criteria: Every tip should be something viewers can apply today. Before each section, add a retention hook (a question or a teaser of what's next). Spoken language, short sentences, no filler.
Format: Script by section with approximate timestamps, narration text, and on-screen suggestions in parentheses.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
