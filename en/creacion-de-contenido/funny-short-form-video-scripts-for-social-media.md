Context: I create content about [your niche] for [target audience] on [platform].
Goal: Publish short videos that entertain, make viewers feel seen, and get shared naturally.
Deliverable: 3 sketch scripts of 30–45 seconds based on everyday situations my audience lives through. Each with: a one-line premise, the main character, a hook in the first 2 seconds, a build-up with a punchline, and on-screen text. Note what I need to shoot it (people, location, props).
Criteria: The humor should come from real situations in the niche, never from mocking groups of people. One main joke per video, with a clear escalation. Short, natural dialogue.
Format: One block per script with numbered scenes.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
