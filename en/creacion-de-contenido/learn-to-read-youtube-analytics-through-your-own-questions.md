Context: I run a YouTube channel about [channel topic], and my experience with analytics is [experience level]. These are my questions about the channel: [your questions about the channel] (for example, why people stop watching, where my views come from, which videos bring in subscribers).
Goal: I want to learn to use YouTube Analytics to make content decisions, not just to look at numbers.
Deliverable: A guide organized by question. For each question: which YouTube Studio report or metric answers it, how to interpret it (what counts as low or high in context), one common mistake when reading it, and 2 concrete actions depending on what I find. Cover long videos and Shorts separately. Close with a 30-minute monthly review routine.
Criteria: Explain in plain language with examples. YouTube Studio sections change over time; if you have web search, check current names and locations. Don't present industry averages as rules; if you mention them, flag them as estimates.
Format: One section per question with bullets; the monthly routine as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
