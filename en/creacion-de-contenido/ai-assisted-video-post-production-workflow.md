Context: I produce YouTube videos about [channel topic]. A typical video runs [typical length], and editing it currently takes me [editing hours] hours. I edit in [editing software] and want to add AI tools.
Goal: I want to cut post-production time without lowering quality, so I can publish more consistently.
Deliverable: A stage-by-stage workflow: organizing footage, transcription, rough cut (removing silences and bad takes), fine edit, captions, music and effects, color and audio, thumbnail, and export for horizontal and vertical formats. For each stage: what the AI can do or speed up, what kind of tool works, what I should check by hand, and the estimated time before and after. Add a folder structure and file-naming template.
Criteria: Recommend tool types and, if you name any, mention free and paid alternatives. If you have web search, check that the tools are still available and what they do today. Use only licensed music and assets, because a copyright claim can block monetization. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Stage, What the AI does, Tool, Human review, and Time before/after; then the folder structure.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
