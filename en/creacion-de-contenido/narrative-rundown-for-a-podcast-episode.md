Context: I'm about to record an episode of my podcast about [episode topic], aimed at [target audience]. It will run about [episode length], and the format is [episode format] (solo, interview, co-host conversation, narrative).
Goal: I want a structure that holds attention from start to finish and leaves listeners with something they can apply.
Deliverable: A rundown with: a 30-60 second opening hook (anecdote, question, or surprising fact), the episode intro, 3-4 segments each with its goal, key ideas, one story or example, and transitions; a listener participation moment; and a closing with a recap, a memorable takeaway, and a call to action. Give the approximate time for each part.
Criteria: Use narrative techniques like open questions that get resolved later, before/after contrast, and concrete examples, because they keep people listening better than a list of tips. Leave room to improvise: bullets, not text to read word for word.
Format: A table with the columns Block, Time, Content, and Technique; then 3 title options for the episode.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
