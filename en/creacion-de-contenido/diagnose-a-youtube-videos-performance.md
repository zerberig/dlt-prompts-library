Context: I published a video about [video topic] [time since publishing] ago. These are its YouTube Studio metrics: [video metrics] (impressions, CTR, views, average view duration, retention by minute, traffic sources, subscribers gained). My channel averages are: [your channel averages].
Goal: Understand what worked, what didn't, and what to change in this video and the next ones.
Deliverable: 1) A diagnosis by stage: discovery (impressions and CTR), retention (where and why people leave), and action (subscribes, clicks, comments). 2) The 3 most likely causes of the result, with the supporting evidence from the data. 3) Actions for this video (title, thumbnail, cards, pinned comment) and lessons for future ones.
Criteria: Compare against my averages, not general benchmarks. Separate what the data shows from your own hypotheses. If a data point is missing to draw a conclusion, tell me which.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets, and a final table with the columns Action, Expected impact, Effort.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
