Context: My podcast about [podcast topic] has been publishing for [time on air] and currently has [current metrics]. The channels I already have are: [channels you already have].
Goal: Grow returning listeners over the next 90 days by combining social media, email, and cross-promotion.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis of where the biggest opportunity is. 2) A month-by-month plan with 3–5 concrete actions per channel (social, email list, cross-promotion with other shows, guest appearances, and presence in niche communities). 3) Measurable monthly targets and how to track them.
Criteria: Prioritize actions that make use of what I already have before opening new channels. Fit the plan to a team of one or two people. Flag any expected results as estimates.
Format: The diagnosis in one paragraph; the plan in a table with the columns Month, Channel, Action, Target; close with 3 actions to start this week.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
