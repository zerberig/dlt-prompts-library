Context: I offer [your product or service] and have a real customer story the customer agreed to share: [case summary] (starting point, what they did, and results). The video will run on [platform].
Goal: Show a real person's transformation so my audience sees themselves in it and sees how they could achieve something similar.
Deliverable: A 60–90 second script with a story structure: the initial moment of tension, the turning point, how the product or service came in, the result, and a soft call to action. Include 2 versions of the first-3-seconds hook and a suggested shot list (interview, B-roll, on-screen text).
Criteria: Use only the real results I gave you, without inflating them, because the story loses its value if it sounds like an ad. The customer is the hero, not the brand. Cinematic but natural tone.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Audio, Visual.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
