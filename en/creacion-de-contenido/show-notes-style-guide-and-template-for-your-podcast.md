Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic] for [target audience]. I publish show notes on [where you publish the notes] (podcast apps, blog, YouTube). Here is an example of my current notes: [example of current notes].
Goal: I want consistent show notes that give useful context and resources, help the episode get found in search, and that anyone on my team can write the same way.
Deliverable: 1) A style guide with 8-10 rules (tone, length, how to credit guests and sources, keyword use, calls to action, accessibility). 2) A template with sections: hooky summary, what you'll learn, timestamped chapters, resources mentioned, about the guest, call to action, and credits; say what goes in each and how long it should be. 3) A complete example applying the template to a made-up episode on my topic. 4) What to change in my current notes.
Criteria: Prioritize what helps the listener, because good notes get shared too. Resources should have a "to be filled in" placeholder for the real link, with no invented URLs.
Format: Numbered sections; the template as a copy-ready text block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
