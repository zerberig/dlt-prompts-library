Context: I want to launch or reposition a YouTube channel about [your niche] for [target audience]. Channels I already know: [channels you already know].
Goal: Understand the niche landscape so I can differentiate my channel and spot opportunities, without copying anyone.
Deliverable: 1) A list of 8–12 relevant channels in the niche (large, mid-sized, and emerging) with their focus. 2) For each: main formats, approximate posting frequency, title and thumbnail style, and what seems to work best for them. 3) Common patterns across the niche. 4) 5 gaps or opportunities no channel covers well, with a video idea for each.
Criteria: Use web search to identify active channels and review their recent videos; don't invent channels or numbers, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. If I later want to collect this data on a recurring basis, recommend doing it through the official YouTube API rather than scraping, which goes against its terms of service.
Format: A table with the columns Channel, Focus, Formats, What works; then patterns and opportunities as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
