Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic] with an audience of [audience size], mostly [description of your audience].
Goal: I want to find partners with complementary audiences so I can grow through cross-promotion and swapped appearances, without competing head-on.
Deliverable: 1) 5 types of ideal partners (podcasts in neighboring niches, creators in other formats, brands, communities or organizations, media outlets), with the mutual benefit of each. 2) Criteria for vetting a potential partner (audience fit, similar size, values, consistency) as a checklist. 3) 5 collaboration formats (promo swap, joint episode, guest appearance, shared content, event). 4) A short, customizable first-contact message. 5) Where and how to find these partners.
Criteria: Don't invent podcast names or contact details; describe profiles and places to search. If you have web search and want to suggest real examples, check that they exist and are still active. Focus the pitch on what the partner gains, because that raises the reply rate.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets; the message in its own block.
