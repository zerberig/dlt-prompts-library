Context: My brand is [your brand or business], and I speak to [target audience] on [platform]. I want to launch an activity tied to [topic or product].
Goal: I want a video that doesn't just get watched but asks people to do something: reply, vote, recreate, or tag, so the audience gets involved and the activity spreads beyond my account.
Deliverable: 1) 3 activity ideas (a challenge to recreate, an in-video poll or quiz, a question answered through duets or video replies) and which one you recommend. 2) The 20-45 second script for the chosen idea with a hook, a demo of how to take part, a clear rule, and a closing. 3) The post caption with participation instructions and a short branded hashtag. 4) A 7-day plan to keep it going: how to feature participants and what to post next.
Criteria: The challenge should be safe, doable in under a minute, and open to everyone without buying anything. If there's a prize, suggest checking the platform's promotion rules. If you have web search, check the interactive formats currently available on the platform.
Format: Numbered sections; the script as a table (Time, Visual, Voice, On-screen text).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
