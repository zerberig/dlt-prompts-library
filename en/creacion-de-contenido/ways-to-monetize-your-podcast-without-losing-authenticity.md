Context: I have a podcast about [podcast topic] with [your audience size] and this listener profile: [your listener profile]. This is what I already sell or offer: [what you already sell or offer].
Goal: Generate income from the podcast without losing the trust and authenticity I have with my audience.
Deliverable: 1) The monetization options (sponsorships, programmatic ads, subscriptions or exclusive content, direct listener support, affiliates, own products, services, events, merch) assessed for my case: requirements, potential, effort, and risk to the audience relationship. 2) The 2 best options right now and the one worth preparing for later. 3) A 30-day plan to activate the first one, including how to introduce it to listeners.
Criteria: If you have web search, confirm current platform requirements and flag any revenue or rate figures as estimates, because they vary widely. Recommend being transparent with listeners about sponsored content.
Format: A table with the columns Option, Requirements, Potential, Effort, Risk; then the recommendation and plan as lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
