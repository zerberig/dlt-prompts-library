Context: I work at [your company and its industry], and our main topic is [niche or topic]. Our audience is [target audience], and their main pain points are [audience pain points]. The core piece I want to make the most of is [core content piece] (long-form article, webinar, case study, report, episode).
Goal: I want to multiply that piece's reach and return by turning it into content adapted to each platform, instead of creating everything from scratch.
Deliverable: A matrix with at least 5 derivative pieces. For each: a title optimized for the platform, format (short video, carousel, LinkedIn article, thread, infographic, email, episode), main platform, audience segment it targets (if different), key message in one sentence, a specific call to action, and a short production brief with the angle that sets it apart from the original. Then a suggested publishing order over 2-4 weeks.
Criteria: Each piece needs its own angle, not a trimmed summary, because the same message repeated wears out people who follow you on several platforms. Calls to action should lead to a logical next step (subscribe, download, book a call). Use only information contained in the core piece.
Format: Plain text without markdown, with one numbered card per derivative piece and the schedule at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
