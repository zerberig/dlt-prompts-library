Context: My brand offers [your product or service] to [target audience]. I want a short video for [platform] that people will want to share.
Goal: Grab attention with a story that seems ordinary and then surprises, so the product shows up as a natural part of the twist rather than as an ad.
Deliverable: 3 distinct story concepts (one funny, one heartfelt, one intriguing), each in 3–4 lines. Then the full script for the strongest concept: 45 to 90 seconds, with setup, tension, twist, and ending, including dialogue, actions, and on-screen text.
Criteria: The twist should surprise but still make sense on a second viewing. Show emotions through actions, not explanations. The product should solve or reveal something within the story, without promises it can't keep.
Format: Concepts as a numbered list; the script scene by scene with approximate timestamps.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
