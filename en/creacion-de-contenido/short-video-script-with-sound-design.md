Context: I want to make a short video for [platform] about [video topic], aimed at [target audience]. The feeling I'm after is [mood or emotion].
Goal: I want the sound to do part of the work: music, effects, and silences that set the rhythm and make the video feel polished and worth rewatching or sharing.
Deliverable: A 20-40 second script with, for each moment: visual, voice or dialogue, music (style, tempo, when it comes in, swells, or cuts), sound effects, and on-screen text. Mark the cuts synced to the beat and at least one intentional silence or pause. Add 2 alternative music styles and how each would change the video.
Criteria: Describe the music by style and function without naming copyrighted songs; recommend picking it from the platform's licensed library or a licensed stock library, because unlicensed audio can get the video muted or blocked. The on-screen text should let people get the message with the sound off.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Visual, Voice, Music, SFX, and On-screen text.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
