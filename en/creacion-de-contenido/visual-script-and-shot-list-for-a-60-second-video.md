Context: I want a 60-second video for [platform] about [video topic or story], aimed at [target audience]. I'll produce it this way: [production method] (filming, AI video generators, or a mix of both).
Goal: Grab attention in the first seconds and hold it to the end with striking visuals and a clear narrative.
Deliverable: 1) The core idea in one sentence and the story arc (opening, development, ending). 2) A narration or on-screen text script timed to 60 seconds. 3) A list of 6–10 shots with timing, visual description, camera movement, and sound. 4) For each shot that will be AI-generated, a natural-language prompt covering cinematography, subject, action, setting, style, and audio. 5) 3 caption options for the post.
Criteria: Every shot should add new information or emotion, no filler. Keep visual consistency across shots (palette, lighting, style). In the prompts, avoid brands, logos, and copyrighted characters.
Format: The idea and arc at the top; then a table with the columns Time, Visual, Camera, Audio, AI prompt.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
