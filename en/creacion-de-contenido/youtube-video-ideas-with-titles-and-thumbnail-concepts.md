Context: I run a YouTube channel about [channel topic] for [target audience]. These are some of my best-performing videos: [best-performing videos].
Goal: I want a list of ideas that attracts new people without drifting from what my audience already values.
Deliverable: 12 video ideas split into three types: 4 that answer my audience's frequent searches, 4 on current topics in the niche, and 4 evergreen ones that keep adding views over the months. For each idea: a title, the video's promise in one sentence, the thumbnail concept (main image, expression or element, and text of 4 words max), and why my audience would care.
Criteria: The title and thumbnail should complement each other rather than repeat the same thing, and the video should deliver what they promise, because clickbait hurts retention. If you have web search, use it to identify current topics and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: A table with the columns Type, Title, Promise, Thumbnail, and Why it works.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
