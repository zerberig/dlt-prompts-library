Context: I offer [your product or service] and speak to [target audience] on [platform]. A topic my audience often has opinions or questions about is [conversation-starting topic].
Goal: I want a video people feel like commenting on and sending to someone, so it spreads organically and steers the conversation toward what I offer.
Deliverable: 1) 3 possible angles (myth vs. reality, a clear opinion with arguments, a situation everyone relates to) and which one you recommend. 2) The 30-60 second script for the chosen angle: 2-second hook, development, a specific question for the audience, and closing. 3) The post caption and a pinned comment that opens the conversation. 4) 5 model replies for the most likely kinds of comments, including one that disagrees.
Criteria: Spark conversation with a clear stance and honest arguments, not misinformation, attacks, or manufactured controversy, because that attracts the wrong audience and hurts the brand. The product mention should feel like a natural consequence of the topic.
Format: Numbered sections; the script as a table (Time, Visual, Voice, On-screen text).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
