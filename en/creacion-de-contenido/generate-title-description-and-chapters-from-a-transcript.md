Context: Here's the timestamped transcript of my YouTube video about [video topic], aimed at [target audience]: [timestamped transcript].
Goal: Prepare all the upload metadata in minutes, optimized for search and clicks, in a format I can reuse for every video.
Deliverable: 5 title options (60 characters max each); a description whose first 2 lines are written for search, followed by a video summary and a call to action; chapters with exact timestamps taken from the transcript; 10–15 tags; 3 hashtags; and a pinned comment that invites participation.
Criteria: Titles should promise only what the video delivers, because clickbait hurts retention. Chapters start at 0:00 and have descriptive names. Use the words my audience would search for, naturally.
Format: One block with a subheading per element, ready to copy and paste.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
