Context: I have a podcast about [podcast topic] with this audience: [your audience size and channels]. I can give community work [time available per week].
Goal: Make listeners feel part of something, take part, and recommend the show, beyond just listening to each episode.
Deliverable: 10 community initiatives (for example, virtual or in-person meetups, exclusive content, listener-question segments, challenges, groups, or polls that pick topics). For each: how it works, effort required, expected impact, a concrete first step, and how to measure whether it works. Then an 8-week plan with the 3 initiatives that best fit my time.
Criteria: Prioritize initiatives I can sustain with the time I really have. Favor the ones that turn listeners into participants in the content.
Format: A table with the columns Initiative, How it works, Effort, Impact, First step; then the week-by-week plan.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
