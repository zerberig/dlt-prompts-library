Context: I want to create my first podcast, about [podcast topic]. I have no prior experience, [time available per week], and a budget of [budget in your currency].
Goal: Publish my first episodes within 30 days, with good audio quality and a format I can sustain.
Deliverable: A 4-week plan: week 1, concept, audience, name, and show format; week 2, minimum gear, recording space, and sound check; week 3, run sheet, recording, and editing of the first episodes; week 4, cover art, description, hosting, and publishing. For each week: concrete tasks, estimated time, and how to know it's done.
Criteria: Favor finishing over perfection: simple gear, basic editing, and a repeatable episode structure. Include recording and post-production techniques that improve audio without expensive equipment. If you have web search, confirm current hosting options.
Format: One section per week with a checklist and, at the end, a run-sheet template for episode 1.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
