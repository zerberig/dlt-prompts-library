Context: I create YouTube content about [channel topic] as part of the marketing strategy for [your brand or business]. This is my current process, from idea to publishing: [your current process].
Goal: I want to bring automation and AI into that process so I can publish more consistently, without the channel losing the voice and authenticity that make people trust it.
Deliverable: 1) My process broken into stages, showing for each what to automate, what to assist with AI, and what should stay human, with the reason. 2) A best-practice guide of 8-10 rules for using AI and automation responsibly on the channel (fact-checking, brand voice, transparency with the audience, usage rights). 3) How to connect the channel with the rest of my content strategy (blog, email, social) without duplicating work.
Criteria: Prioritize whatever frees up creative time. If you have web search, review YouTube's current policies on altered or synthetic content and reused content, and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate.
Format: A table for the process (Stage, Automate, AI-assisted, Human, Reason), a numbered list for the guide, and a short paragraph for the integration.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
