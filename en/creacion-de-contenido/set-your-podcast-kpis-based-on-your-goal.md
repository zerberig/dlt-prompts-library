Context: I have a podcast about [podcast topic]. My main goal for the show is [podcast goal] (personal brand, lead generation, monetization, or community). I use [hosting and analytics platforms].
Goal: Know what to measure, where to find it, and how to read it, so I make decisions based on data rather than gut feeling.
Deliverable: 1) 5 to 8 KPIs aligned with my goal, explaining why they matter. 2) For each: where to find it, how often to check it, and what signals mean it's going well or badly. 3) How to fold in qualitative listener feedback (reviews, comments, surveys). 4) A monthly report template.
Criteria: Separate reach, engagement, and business metrics. Explain the limits of download counts, which aren't measured the same way across platforms. If you have web search, confirm which metrics each platform currently offers and flag any industry benchmarks as estimates.
Format: A table with the columns KPI, Why it matters, Where to measure it, Frequency, Warning sign; then the report template.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
