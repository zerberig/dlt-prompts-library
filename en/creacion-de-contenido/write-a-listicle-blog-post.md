Context: I run a blog about [blog topic] for [target audience]. I want a list article about [article topic] with [number of points] points.
Goal: Attract search traffic and leave readers with ideas they can apply, not a shallow list.
Deliverable: 3 numbered headline options; a 60–90 word introduction that frames the problem; each point with a clear subheading, an 80–120 word paragraph, and a concrete example or data point; and a conclusion with the reader's next step. Add a meta description of 155 characters max.
Criteria: Order the points logically (basic to advanced, or by impact). Each point should bring something different. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source.
Format: Markdown, with an H2 heading for each point.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
