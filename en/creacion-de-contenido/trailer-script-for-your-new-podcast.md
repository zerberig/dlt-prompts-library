I'm launching a podcast called [podcast name] about [podcast topic] for [target audience]. Write the script for a 60-90 second trailer that opens with a hook (a question, a scene, or a bold line), explains in one sentence what listeners get, introduces who's hosting and why, previews 2-3 topics from the first episodes, and invites people to follow the show. [podcast tone] tone, written to be spoken aloud, with music or sound-effect cues in parentheses. Add 2 alternative hooks and a show description of 300 characters max for the platforms.
