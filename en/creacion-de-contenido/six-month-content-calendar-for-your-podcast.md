Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic] for [target audience] and publish [publishing frequency], starting on [start date]. Dates or events that matter to my audience in this period: [key dates].
Goal: I want to plan ahead so I can book guests in time, line up episodes with important dates, and stop improvising promotion.
Deliverable: 1) A 6-month calendar with each episode: release date, topic, format, guest or collaboration profile, deadline to confirm the guest, and 2 promotion actions. 2) A balanced spread of topics across the period, showing which theme each month leans on. 3) 2-3 evergreen backup episodes I can record ahead of time to cover surprises.
Criteria: Leave room between recording and release for editing. Use the key dates without forcing unrelated topics. Describe guest profiles without inventing names of real people.
Format: A table with the columns Date, Episode, Topic, Format, Guest or collaboration, Guest deadline, and Promotion; then the backup episodes as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
