Context: My brand is [your brand or business] and it sells [your product or service]. I have this material created by real customers: [available customer content] (videos, photos, reviews, messages). I'll publish the video on [platform].
Goal: I want a video that shows real customers using what I sell, to build trust and encourage others to share their own experience.
Deliverable: A 60-90 second script with: a 3-second hook, the sequence of customer clips in order (what's seen and what's heard), on-screen text, my input as the brand (if any), and a closing that invites the community to join in with their own content. Add a list of the permissions I need from each customer and a short message to request them.
Criteria: Use only real testimonials, exactly as they were said; don't invent quotes or results, because a fake testimonial destroys trust and can carry legal consequences. If a clip isn't enough to cover a point in the script, suggest what kind of material to request.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Visual, Audio, and On-screen text; then the permissions list and the message.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
