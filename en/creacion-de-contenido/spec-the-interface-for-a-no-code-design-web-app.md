Context: I'm designing [app name], a web app for customizing [template type] (infographics, presentations, social posts, or others) with your own data, copy, and brand elements. The users are [target user] with no design experience.
Goal: A UI and UX specification that design and engineering can use to prototype and build.
Deliverable:
1) The 3 core flows (choose a template, edit content and branding, export or share), step by step.
2) Key screens with their purpose, components, and visual hierarchy.
3) Editing features: text, data-driven charts, brand colors and fonts, images, undo, and autosave.
4) Empty, loading, error, and success states, with their messages.
5) Accessibility and small-screen requirements.
6) How to measure whether the experience works (time to first export, drop-off per step).
Criteria: Prioritize getting someone with no experience to finish their first design within minutes; keep advanced features hidden until they're needed. Describe the interface precisely enough to sketch wireframes from it.
Format: Numbered sections; screens in a table with the columns Screen, Purpose, Components, Main actions.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
