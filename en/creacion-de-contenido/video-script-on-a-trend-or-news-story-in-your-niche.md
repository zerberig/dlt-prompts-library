Context: I run a YouTube channel about [your niche] for [target audience]. I want to cover this trend or news story: [trend or news story].
Goal: Publish a timely video that offers my own perspective rather than another recap, so it catches current search interest and earns subscribers who come back for my judgment.
Deliverable: A 6–8 minute script with: 3 title-and-hook options, a 15-second opening, brief context, my position with 3 arguments, the strongest objection and my response, what it means for the viewer, and a closing call to action.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to confirm dates, figures, and quotes; cite the source in a note and flag anything you can't verify as an estimate. Keep facts clearly separate from opinion, because credibility is what brings viewers back. Spoken language, short sentences.
Format: Script by section with approximate timestamps and visual notes in parentheses.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
