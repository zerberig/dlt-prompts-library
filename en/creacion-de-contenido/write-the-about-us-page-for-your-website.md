Context: My company is [company name] and offers [what you offer] to [target audience]. Here's how it started: [origin story in a few lines]. Team: [team names and roles].
Goal: A visitor should understand within seconds who we are, why we exist, and why they can trust us, and that should move them closer to contacting us or buying.
Deliverable: Ready-to-publish copy with: a headline and subheadline, a 1–2 sentence mission, the founder's story (120–180 words), 3–4 values each with a line showing how we live it day to day, short team bios (2 sentences each), and a closing call to action.
Criteria: Use the facts I gave you and concrete details instead of generic phrases like "passion for excellence", because specifics build trust. Warm, professional tone. Don't invent numbers, awards, or clients.
Format: Sections with subheadings and short, web-friendly paragraphs.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
