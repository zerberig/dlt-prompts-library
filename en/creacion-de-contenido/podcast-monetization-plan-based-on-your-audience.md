Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic] with about [monthly downloads] downloads or plays a month. My audience is [description of your audience], and I publish on [platforms you publish on].
Goal: I want to earn sustainable income from the podcast without breaking my listeners' trust.
Deliverable:
1) An assessment of 6-8 revenue streams (sponsorships, programmatic ads, affiliates, premium content or subscriptions, memberships, my own products, services, events, merchandise), showing which are viable with my current audience and at what size the others become viable.
2) For the 3 best fits: how they work, what I need to get started, an expected income range in [currency], and the risks.
3) A 3-stage plan (now, as the audience grows, and long term) with concrete actions.
Criteria: Be honest about what's realistic at my size. Recommend disclosure for sponsored and affiliate content, because listeners and the law expect it. If you have web search, check current rates and programs. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table for the assessment (Stream, Viable now, Requirement, Effort, Potential), short cards for the 3 chosen ones, and a list per stage. 800 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
