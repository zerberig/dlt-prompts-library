Context: I'm launching a podcast about [podcast topic] for [target audience]. What sets me apart is [your angle or expertise], and I want to publish [publishing frequency].
Goal: Before recording, I want to be clear on what the show will cover over the long run and reach launch day with a planned first season.
Deliverable: 1) 3-5 content pillars: each one's name, what it covers, why my audience cares, and 3 sample topics. 2) A 10-episode first season with a through-line that gives people a reason to listen in order: working title, pillar, central idea, format (solo, interview, or mixed), and the ideal guest profile where relevant. 3) Which episodes to release on launch day so a newcomer understands the show.
Criteria: Balance beginner-friendly entry topics with topics that show depth. Describe guest profiles rather than naming real people with contact details, because those could be inaccurate.
Format: Pillars as a numbered list; the season as a table with the columns Episode, Title, Pillar, Central idea, Format, and Ideal guest.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
