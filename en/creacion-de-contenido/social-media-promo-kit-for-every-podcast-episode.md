Context: I publish a podcast about [podcast topic]. Here's the transcript or summary of the latest episode: [episode transcript or summary]. My active social networks are [social networks you use].
Goal: Promote every episode on social without starting from scratch each week, with pieces that work on their own even if people haven't heard the show.
Deliverable: 1) The 3–5 strongest moments for video clips, with approximate timestamp, why they work, and the on-screen hook text. 2) For each network, the post copy adapted to its style and length, with a call to action. 3) A thread or carousel with the episode's main ideas. 4) A 5-step plan for rolling out the pieces during release week.
Criteria: Every piece should deliver value on its own, not just announce "new episode". Use the guest's or host's own words when they're stronger than a summary.
Format: Numbered sections; copy for each network in a separate block with the network's name as a subheading.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
