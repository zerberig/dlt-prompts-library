Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic] for [target audience]. Right now I get around [listeners per episode] listeners per episode, and I can spend about [hours per week] hours a week promoting it, with little or no budget.
Goal: I want more people in my audience to discover the show through channels other than posting on my own social accounts, which I already do.
Deliverable: 10 creative promotion tactics that mix collaborations with other creators, guest appearances, other people's newsletters, online communities, in-person events, and local partnerships. For each: what it is, a concrete example applied to my topic, the first step I can take this week, and how I'll know it worked.
Criteria: Prioritize tactics that reach people who don't know me yet and fit the time I have. Everything should be honest and follow each community's rules; no unsolicited mass messages, because they burn the show's reputation.
Format: A table with the columns Tactic, Example, First step, Indicator, Impact (high/medium/low), and Effort (high/medium/low), sorted from highest to lowest impact. Close with the 3 tactics you'd start with and why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
