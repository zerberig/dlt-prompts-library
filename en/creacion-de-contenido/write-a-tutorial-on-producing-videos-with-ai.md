Context: I want to publish a tutorial for [creators' niche] creators who want to use AI to produce their YouTube videos. My audience is at a [audience level] level, and the tutorial will be a [tutorial format] (blog post, video script, or course lesson).
Goal: By the end, I want readers to be able to produce a complete video with AI's help and understand at which stages it makes sense to use it and at which it doesn't.
Deliverable:
1) A title and an intro explaining what readers will be able to do by following the tutorial.
2) The tutorial split by stage: topic research, script, voice or recording, editing, music and effects, thumbnail, title, description and tags, and publishing. For each stage: what the AI does, a sample prompt readers can copy, what to check by hand, and a common mistake.
3) A best-practices section: transparency with the audience about AI-generated or altered content, usage rights for voices, images, and music, and why your own judgment is still what sets a channel apart.
4) A closing with a hands-on exercise.
Criteria: Describe tool types and, if you name any, check with web search that they're still available. If you mention YouTube policies, review the current ones and flag anything you can't confirm as an estimate. Match the language to the audience's level.
Format: Sections with subheadings, sample prompts in separate blocks. 1,200-1,800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
