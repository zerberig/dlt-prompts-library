Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic]. Here is the data from my recent episodes, exported from the platforms: [episode data] (title, date, length, 7- and 30-day plays or downloads, average listen-through rate, and any other data available). I'm also sharing the comments or reviews I've received: [listener comments].
Goal: I want to understand what's working and what isn't, so I can decide what to do differently next quarter.
Deliverable:
1) A summary of 5 key findings about performance, each backed by the data.
2) The best- and worst-performing episodes and the patterns they share (topic, length, guest, title, release day).
3) Where listeners drop off, if the data allows it, and likely causes.
4) What the comments reveal that the numbers don't.
5) 3 concrete experiments for next quarter, each with a hypothesis, what to change, how to measure it, and how long to run it.
Criteria: Separate what the data shows from your interpretation. If the sample is too small to conclude something, say so. Don't recommend changing everything at once, because then I won't know what worked.
Format: Numbered sections; the patterns in a table and the experiments as short cards. 500-800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
