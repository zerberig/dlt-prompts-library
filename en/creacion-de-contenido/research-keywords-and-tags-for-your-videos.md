Context: I make YouTube videos about [your video topic] for [target audience] in [main language and country].
Goal: Pick the words and phrases my audience actually searches with, and use them in titles, descriptions, and tags.
Deliverable: 1) 20 likely search queries grouped by intent (learn, compare, solve a problem, buy). 2) The 5 with the best opportunity for a channel my size, and why. 3) For the best one, a proposed title, the first 2 lines of the description, and 10–15 tags. 4) The most suitable YouTube category.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it to check which videos currently rank for those queries; don't invent search volumes, and flag any demand indicator as an estimate. Tags carry less weight than the title, thumbnail, and description, so prioritize those.
Format: A table with the columns Query, Intent, Opportunity; then the proposal in blocks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
