Context: My podcast is called [podcast name], it's about [podcast topic], and it's for [target audience]. Format and frequency: [format and frequency]. What sets it apart: [what makes your podcast unique].
Goal: Convince a new listener to subscribe from the first listen and show up in relevant searches inside audio apps.
Deliverable: 1) A 60–90 second trailer script with an opening hook, a clear promise, 2–3 teasers of topics or moments, the show's tone, and an invitation to subscribe; mark where music or sound effects go. 2) A 2-paragraph show description using the words my audience would search for. 3) 3 short lines for social media.
Criteria: It should sound like a conversation, not a radio ad. The description's first sentence must make clear what the show is about and who it's for, because that's what platforms display.
Format: Script with audio cues in parentheses; then the description and the lines.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
