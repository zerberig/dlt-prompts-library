Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic] with an audience of [audience size]. Today I talk with my listeners through [current channels], and I can give [available time] to the community.
Goal: I want listeners to go from listening silently to feeling part of the show, so they come back every episode and recommend it.
Deliverable: 1) 3 recurring in-show segments that bring the audience in (listener questions, challenges, submitted stories, polls). 2) The community space or spaces that fit my size (group, server, comments, livestreams, in-person meetups), with pros and cons. 3) An 8-week plan to activate the community, with weekly actions. 4) Basic house rules and how to moderate without it eating my time.
Criteria: Prioritize actions I can sustain with the time I have, because an abandoned community does more harm than none. Give real value to people who take part (recognition, access, bonus content) without relying on giveaways.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets; the 8-week plan as a table (Week, Action, Channel, Target).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
