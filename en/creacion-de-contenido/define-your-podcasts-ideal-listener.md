Context: I have (or am about to launch) a podcast about [podcast topic]. What I know about my audience today: [data or hunches about your audience].
Goal: Pin down who the show is for, so every decision about topics, length, and promotion points at the same person.
Deliverable: 2 listener profiles covering: life or work situation, the problem or curiosity that makes them listen, when and where they listen, what other podcasts or creators they follow, and what would make them recommend the show. Then, for each profile, the concrete implications for episode length, format, tone, and promotion channels. Finally, 5 questions to validate these profiles with real listeners.
Criteria: Build the profiles on behaviors and motivations, not just age and gender, because that's what drives content decisions. Point out which parts are assumptions I should test.
Format: One block per profile and an implications table with the columns Decision, Profile 1, Profile 2.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
