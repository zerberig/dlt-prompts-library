Context: I run [your business], I sell to [ideal customer], and for marketing I use [tools you use today] (CRM, email, social). My YouTube channel's current state: [current channel status].
Goal: I want YouTube to grow views and engagement, but above all to generate measurable leads over the next 6 months.
Deliverable:
1) 3–4 content pillars tied to the questions my customer asks before buying, with 3 video ideas per pillar.
2) A posting frequency and format mix (long-form, Shorts, live streams) that a small team can realistically sustain.
3) A distribution and tracking workflow: which tasks to automate (publishing, UTM links, logging leads in the CRM, reports) and which to keep in human hands.
4) A KPI dashboard: metric, how it's measured, review frequency, and the decision it triggers.
Criteria: Prioritize business metrics (leads, conversions) over vanity metrics and connect each pillar to a funnel stage. Targets should be reasonable ranges for a channel my size; flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source.
Format: Numbered sections; KPIs in a table with the columns Metric, How to measure, Frequency, Decision. 900 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
