Context: I host a podcast about [podcast topic] that I publish on [platforms you publish on]. My main goal for the show is [podcast goal] (for example, generating clients, building a personal brand, landing sponsorships, or creating a community).
Goal: I don't know which numbers to look at to tell whether the podcast is doing well. I want a simple measurement system I can review once a month without losing hours.
Deliverable: 1) The 6-8 most useful metrics for my goal, grouped into reach, retention, engagement, and business outcome. 2) For each: what it measures, where I find it (each platform's dashboard, a survey, a tracked link, etc.), and what change in the number should worry me. 3) A monthly spreadsheet template with those metrics.
Criteria: Prioritize indicators that help me make a decision, not vanity metrics. Platforms change their dashboards often; if you have web search, check what data each one offers today. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: A table with the columns Metric, What it measures, Where to find it, Warning sign; then the monthly template columns as a list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
