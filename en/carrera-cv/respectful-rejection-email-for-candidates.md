Write an email template for candidates who weren't selected for the [role name] position at [company name]. I need two versions: one for people who didn't get past resume screening and one for people who reached the interview stage.
The email should sincerely thank them for their time, state the decision clearly in the first paragraph, avoid promises we won't keep and, in the interview version, offer a line of general feedback if relevant. Professional, human tone. 120 words max per version, with a subject line and gaps marked with «» for personalizing.
