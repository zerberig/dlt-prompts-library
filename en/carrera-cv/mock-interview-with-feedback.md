Role: Act as the person who will interview me for the [target job title] role at [type of company].
Context: My experience level is [experience level]. If I have the posting, here it is: [job posting text].
Goal: Practice under conditions close to a real interview and sharpen my answers before the day.
How it works: Ask me 8 questions, one at a time, and wait for my answer before moving on. Mix technical questions for the role with questions on communication, teamwork, and problem-solving. After each answer, give brief feedback: what worked, what was missing, and an improved version of my answer in 2 or 3 sentences. If my answer is vague, ask a follow-up the way a real interviewer would.
Wrap-up: When we're done, rate me 1-5 on clarity, concrete evidence, and fit for the role, and give me my top 3 areas to improve.
Start with a brief introduction as the interviewer and the first question.
