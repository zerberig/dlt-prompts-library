Context: I'm going after [target job title] roles in [industry], a very competitive market. Here's my background: [your background, skills, and achievements].
Goal: Understand what makes me different from other candidates with a similar profile and use it consistently across my resume, LinkedIn profile, and interviews.
Deliverable: 1) 3-5 real differentiators, each with the evidence from my background that backs it up. 2) My value proposition in one sentence. 3) How to express each differentiator on a resume (one bullet), on LinkedIn (a headline or About line), and in an interview (a short story). 4) Two concrete ideas to strengthen the weakest differentiator over the next 3 months.
Criteria: Look for uncommon combinations (for example, a technical skill plus experience in a specific industry) rather than generic qualities everyone claims. Be honest if something isn't as distinctive as it seems.
Format: Numbered sections; part 3 as a table with the columns Differentiator, Resume, LinkedIn, and Interview.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
