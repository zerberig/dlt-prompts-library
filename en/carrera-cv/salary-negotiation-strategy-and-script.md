Context: I'm in the process for a [target job title] role in [city or country]. I have [years of experience] years of experience and my situation is: [your situation] (type and size of the company, current pay or latest offer, other offers, benefits I care about).
Goal: Negotiate a fair package with solid arguments, without putting the offer or the relationship at risk.
Deliverable:
1) Estimated market range for the role in [currency], with sources and confidence level.
2) My range: ideal number, target, and walk-away minimum, and how to calculate them.
3) What to say if I'm asked for my expectations before I have an offer.
4) A script for responding to an offer: lines to say thanks, counter with value-based arguments, and handle common objections ("there's no budget", "it's our policy").
5) Negotiable items beyond base pay (bonuses, vacation days, remote work, training, start date), prioritized for my case.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to check current information, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. Arguments based on value and market, not personal needs. This is general guidance; for questions about contracts, taxes, or statutory benefits, suggest I consult an employment or tax professional.
Format: Numbered sections; the script as a short dialogue with ready-to-use lines.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
