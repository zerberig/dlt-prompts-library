Context: I'm going to interview candidates for the [role name] position at [type of company]. The key technical skills are [key technical skills] and the team's values are [team values].
Goal: A structured interview that assesses every candidate with the same questions and criteria, so I can decide on evidence and reduce bias.
Deliverable:
1) A 45-60 minute interview structure with blocks and timings.
2) 10-12 questions: technical, problem-solving (with a short practical case), and behavioral tied to the team's values.
3) For each question, the signals of a strong, acceptable, and weak answer.
4) A 1-4 scoring rubric per competency and a summary sheet to compare candidates.
Criteria: Assess culture fit as alignment with observable values and ways of working, not personal affinity, because that favors people similar to the interviewer. Avoid questions about age, marital status, family plans, religion, or other topics unrelated to the job that may be illegal.
Format: Numbered sections; questions and signals in a table with the columns Question, What it assesses, Strong answer, Weak answer.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
