Context: I'm a [your profession] with experience in [your specialty], looking for opportunities as a [target job title]. I have [time available per week] to spend on social media.
Goal: Get recruiters and people in my field to find me and remember me for my specialty, instead of relying only on applying to postings.
Deliverable: 1) Concrete changes to my LinkedIn profile so I show up in recruiter searches. 2) A 4-week content plan with 3 post ideas per week based on my real work. 3) What to do on X and in industry communities or groups, with examples of the kinds of communities worth joining. 4) A weekly engagement routine that fits my time.
Criteria: Ideas that show what I can do (cases, lessons, processes), not motivational quotes. Don't state how each platform's algorithm works as fact; if you have web search, verify each platform's current features.
Format: Four sections with bullets; the content plan as a table with week, idea, and format.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
