Context: Here's my current resume: [your resume]. And here's the job posting I want to apply to: [job posting text].
Goal: I want a version of my resume tuned to this posting so that both the applicant tracking system (ATS) and the recruiter see quickly that I meet what they're asking for.
Deliverable:
1) Match table: the posting's requirements and keywords, whether they appear in my resume (yes, partial, no), and where.
2) My bullets and summary rewritten to work in the missing keywords that my experience actually supports.
3) A list of requirements I don't meet, with an honest suggestion for each (point to adjacent experience, a course in progress, or leave it out).
Criteria: Use the posting's exact wording when it describes something I really did, because ATS tools look for literal matches. Don't add skills or achievements that aren't in my resume: an inflated resume falls apart in the interview. Keep each bullet to one or two lines, starting with an action verb.
Format: The three sections with headings; in section 2, show each change as "Before" and "After".
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
