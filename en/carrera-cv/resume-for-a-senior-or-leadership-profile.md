Context: I have [years of experience] years of experience as a [your current role] and I'm targeting a [target job title] position. My background, including teams led, budgets, projects, and results: [your background].
Goal: A resume that positions me for a bigger role, showing business impact and leadership ability, not a list of duties.
Deliverable:
1) A 4-line executive summary.
2) A key achievements section with the 3-4 highest-impact results of my career.
3) The last 10 years of experience in detail (role scope in one line: team, budget, markets; then 3-5 achievements) and earlier roles condensed to one line each.
4) Core competencies and education.
Criteria: Prioritize business results (revenue, costs, growth, efficiency, talent developed) and decisions I made, not operational tasks. List roles in reverse chronological order. Use only the figures I gave you; where an important number is missing, write "add figure". Two pages max.
Format: Resume as text with Markdown headings and bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
