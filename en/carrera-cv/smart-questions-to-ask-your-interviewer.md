I have an interview for a [target job title] role at [company name]. Prepare 10-12 questions for me to ask at the end, grouped into: the role and how success is measured, the team and how it works, the company and its current challenges, and career growth.
The questions should show that I understand the role, not have obvious answers on the company website, and help me spot red flags. For each, say in one line what the answer reveals. Mark the 3 I should ask if there's only time for a few, and adapt 2 of them to the person interviewing me: [who's interviewing you] (recruiter, future manager, executive).
