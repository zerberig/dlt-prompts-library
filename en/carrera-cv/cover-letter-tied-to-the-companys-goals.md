Context: I'm applying for a [target job title] role at [company name]. Here's the job posting: [job posting text]. My relevant experience and achievements: [your experience and achievements].
Goal: I want a letter that shows I understand where the company is heading and how I can help it get there, not a generic letter I could send anywhere.
Deliverable: 1) A short summary of the company's goals, values, and current challenges. 2) The cover letter.
Criteria: If you have web search, research the company (official site, recent news, reports) and cite where each fact comes from; if not, work only from the posting and tell me so. Connect 2 or 3 of my achievements to specific company challenges. Use only the facts I gave you. Professional, warm tone with no over-the-top flattery.
Format: A 250-350 word letter: an opening hook specific to the company, two paragraphs of evidence, and a close that proposes a conversation.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
