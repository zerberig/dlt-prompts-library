Context: I have an interview for a [target job title] role at [company name] in [days available] days. The format will be [interview format] (in person, video call, panel, technical test).
Goal: Show up prepared and calm, without leaving important things to the last minute.
Deliverable: A day-by-day plan up to the interview covering: company research (what to look for and where), reviewing the posting and my achievement stories, practicing answers out loud, technical prep if relevant, questions for the interviewer, logistics (route or video-call setup, attire that fits the company culture, documents), and a checklist for interview day and for follow-up afterward.
Criteria: Concrete tasks with estimated time, prioritized by impact. Tailor the advice to norms in [industry].
Format: A list per day with checkboxes; at the end, the interview-day checklist and a short thank-you email template.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
