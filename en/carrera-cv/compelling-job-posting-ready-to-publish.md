Context: I'm hiring for the [role name] position at [company name], which does [what the company does]. Role details (responsibilities, requirements, work model, location, salary range, and benefits): [role details].
Goal: Attract qualified candidates who connect with the team's culture and cut down on applications that don't fit.
Deliverable: 1) The full posting for job boards. 2) An 80-word short version for LinkedIn or other social media. 3) Three title options for the posting.
Criteria: Open with what the person will achieve in the role, not the company's history. Describe the culture with concrete examples (how the team works, how decisions get made) instead of adjectives like "dynamic". Include the salary range because it improves application quality. Separate must-have and nice-to-have requirements, and use inclusive language.
Format: A posting with sections (The role, What you'll do, What we're looking for, What we offer, How to apply), 300-450 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
