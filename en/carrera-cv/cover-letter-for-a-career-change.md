Context: I worked as a [your previous role or field] and I'm now pursuing a [target job title] role at [company name]. My reason for the change and what I've done to prepare (courses, projects, volunteering): [your reason and preparation]. Here's the posting: [job posting text].
Goal: Have the recruiter see my switch as an asset rather than a risk, and give me a shot at an interview.
Deliverable: 1) A short table connecting 3-4 skills from my previous experience to requirements in the posting. 2) The cover letter.
Criteria: The letter addresses the change in the first paragraph with a clear, positive reason, presents concrete evidence of transferable skills and recent preparation, and closes by showing the different perspective I bring. Don't downplay or hide the lack of direct experience; frame it with what I've already done to close the gap. Use only the facts I gave you.
Format: First the table with the columns Previous experience, Transferable skill, and Posting requirement; then a 250-350 word letter.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
