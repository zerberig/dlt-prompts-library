Context: I work in [professional field] and my goal for the next 12 months is [your career goal]. I can invest up to [budget] and about [hours per week] hours a week.
Goal: Choose the training that moves me closest to that goal, instead of piling up courses nobody values.
Deliverable: 6-8 options across certifications, courses, and programs. For each: who issues it, approximate cost, duration, format, how well recognized it is by employers in my field, and what concrete skill it gives me. Then my recommendation of the top 2 for my case and in what order to take them.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to check current information, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. Distinguish credentials that job postings ask for from training that's useful but carries little weight on a resume. Include free or low-cost options if they exist.
Format: A comparison table with the columns Option, Issuer, Cost, Duration, Recognition, and Skill gained; then the recommendation in one paragraph.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
