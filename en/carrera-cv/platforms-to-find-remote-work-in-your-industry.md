Context: I work in [industry] at the [experience level] level and live in [country]. I want to find remote work, either with local companies or companies abroad.
Goal: Focus my search on the platforms and channels that actually post remote openings for my profile.
Deliverable: 1) 8-12 platforms or channels for remote work (specialized boards, general portals with a remote filter, communities, international hiring platforms), with which profile and level each suits and whether they accept candidates from my country. 2) How to set up searches and alerts so I don't miss new openings. 3) Red flags for spotting fraudulent offers (upfront payments, chat-only interviews, bank details requested early). 4) What to check before accepting: contract type, payment currency, time zone.
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to check current information, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. Include only active platforms. For questions about taxes or international contracts, suggest I consult an accountant or lawyer.
Format: A table with the columns Platform, Type, Profile and level, Accepts candidates from my country, and Tip; then sections 2-4 as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
