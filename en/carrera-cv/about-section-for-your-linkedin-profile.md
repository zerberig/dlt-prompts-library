Context: I'm a [your profession] with [years of experience] years of experience in [industry]. My main achievements and skills: [your achievements and skills]. I'm looking for [the kind of opportunities you want].
Goal: Text for the About section that lets a recruiter or potential client understand in seconds what I do, what I'm good at, and why to reach out.
Deliverable: A main version and an alternative with a different tone (one more formal, one more personal).
Criteria: Write in first person with a natural tone. The first 2 lines must work on their own, since that's what shows before "see more". Include 2 or 3 concrete achievements, my specialty's keywords worked in naturally, and a close that says what kind of contact I'm open to. Avoid clichés like "passionate" without evidence.
Format: Short paragraphs, 150-250 words per version; optionally, a final line listing my specialties separated by dots.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
