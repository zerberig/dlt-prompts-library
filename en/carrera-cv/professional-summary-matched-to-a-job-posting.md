Context: I want the professional summary on my resume for this posting: [job posting text]. My details:
- Relevant experience and roles: [your relevant roles]
- Achievements with measurable results: [your quantified achievements]
- Skills, certifications, and education: [your skills and education]
Goal: Within the first seconds of reading, the recruiter should see that my profile answers what the posting asks for.
Deliverable:
1) The posting's 5 core keywords or requirements.
2) Two versions of the summary, 4-5 lines each: one that opens with my specialty and one that opens with my strongest achievement.
3) If I'm light on experience for the role, a third objective-style version that highlights potential and transferable skills.
4) A short note on which requirement each sentence covers.
Criteria: Work in the posting's keywords only where my details support them. Include at least one achievement with a number. No stock phrases ("team player", "results-oriented") unless backed by evidence. Confident, professional tone, without first person.
Format: Numbered sections with headings; each summary version in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
