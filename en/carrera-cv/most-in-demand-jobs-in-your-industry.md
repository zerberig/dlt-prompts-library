Context: I work, or want to work, in the [industry] industry in [country or region]. My current profile is: [your current profile].
Goal: Know which roles are most in demand right now so I can decide where to focus my job search or training.
Deliverable: The 10 most in-demand roles in that industry and region. For each: what it does in 2 lines, estimated monthly salary range in [currency], the skills most often required, and how close it is to my current profile (close, medium, far).
Criteria: If you have web search, use it to check current information, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. State the date or period of the data and its sources, because the market moves fast. If a salary range varies a lot by city or company size, say so.
Format: A table with the columns Role, What it does, Salary range, Key skills, and Fit with my profile; then 3 recommendations on which to explore first and why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
