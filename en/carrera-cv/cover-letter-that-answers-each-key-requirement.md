Context: I'm applying for the [target job title] role at [company name]. Here's the posting: [job posting text]. Here's my resume: [your resume].
Goal: A letter that makes the recruiter's decision easy by showing, requirement by requirement, that I meet what they're asking for.
Deliverable: 1) The posting's 4 most important requirements and the strongest evidence from my resume for each. 2) The cover letter.
Criteria: The letter opens by introducing me in one sentence with my value proposition, then includes a short two-column section: What you need / What I bring, with one line per requirement and a concrete achievement, and closes with a short paragraph inviting a conversation. If I don't meet a requirement, leave it out of the table; tell me separately how I could address it in the interview. Use only facts from my resume.
Format: First the analysis as a list, then the full letter at 250-350 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
