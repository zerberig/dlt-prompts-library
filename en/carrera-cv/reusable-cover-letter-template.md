Context: I'm applying to several [type of role] positions in [industry]. My strongest skills and achievements are: [your skills and achievements].
Goal: Have a base letter I can adapt to each posting in 10 minutes without it sounding generic.
Deliverable: 1) A letter template with fixed paragraphs and gaps marked with «» for what changes per application (company, role, key requirement, reason for interest). 2) Three alternative versions of the achievements paragraph, each focused on a different skill, to pick from depending on the posting. 3) A 5-point checklist for personalizing it before sending.
Criteria: The fixed part should stand on its own with my real achievements; the variable part should force me to mention something specific about each company. Final length of 250-300 words. Confident, direct tone.
Format: The three parts with headings; the template in a text block ready to copy.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
