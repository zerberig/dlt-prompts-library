Context: I'm preparing for [target job title] interviews. My situation: [your situation] (for example, why I'm leaving my job, a period without work, a change of field, or limited experience in one requirement).
Goal: Answer the questions that make me most nervous calmly and honestly, without sounding defensive.
Deliverable: The 8 most likely tough questions for my case (include "What's your biggest weakness?", "Why are you leaving your current job?", and "What are your salary expectations?"). For each: the interviewer's real intent, the answer strategy in one line, a sample answer tailored to my situation, and what to avoid saying.
Criteria: Honest answers that acknowledge the situation and steer toward what I bring; no weaknesses dressed up as strengths ("I'm too much of a perfectionist"), because interviewers see through them. Answers of 45-90 seconds spoken.
Format: Numbered list with the four elements per question.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
