Role: Review my resume the way a recruiter who uses an applicant tracking system (ATS) to filter hundreds of applications would.
Context: Here's my resume text: [your resume text]. I'm targeting [target job title] roles. Optional reference posting: [job posting text].
Goal: Find out what's hurting my visibility in automated filters and what to fix first, without rebuilding the whole resume.
Deliverable:
1) Overall diagnosis: low, medium, or high compatibility, with 2 sentences on why.
2) Format and parsing: automated-reading risks (columns, tables, non-standard headings, inconsistent dates, info in headers or footers).
3) Keywords: which role keywords already appear, which are missing, and where they'd best go; with no posting, use the ones most common in postings for this role.
4) Content: unexplained acronyms, bullets without results, weak verbs.
5) The 5 highest-impact fixes, in priority order, with an example of how each would look.
Criteria: Recommend adding keywords only where they describe real experience, because a person will read the resume too. Don't invent compatibility percentages; explain your reasoning in words.
Format: The five sections with headings and bullets; section 5 as a table with Issue, Fix, and Example.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
