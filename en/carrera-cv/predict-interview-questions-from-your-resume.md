Role: Read my resume the way the hiring manager who's interviewing me would.
Context: Here's my resume: [your resume]. Here's the posting: [job posting text]. The industry is [industry].
Goal: Anticipate the questions that come straight out of my own resume, including the awkward ones, so I don't have to improvise.
Deliverable: 12 questions grouped into technical, behavioral, and situational. For each: which part of my resume or the posting triggers it, what the interviewer wants to confirm, and recommendations for answering (key points, which example from my experience to use, and what to avoid). Include at least 2 questions about visible weak spots in my resume (frequent job changes, gaps, requirements I only partly meet).
Criteria: Questions specific to my profile, not generic ones. Recommendations should rest on facts from my resume.
Format: Three sections with headings; each question numbered with its three elements as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
