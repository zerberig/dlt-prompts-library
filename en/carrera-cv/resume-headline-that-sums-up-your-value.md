Write headline options to place under my name on my resume. My specialty is [your specialty] and the result I'm best at delivering is [the result you deliver] (for example, cutting costs, growing sales, launching products).
Give me 8 options of 12 words max: 4 that pair specialty and result, 2 that include a figure or concrete fact from my background if I provide one, and 2 more understated ones for conservative industries. No empty adjectives or emojis. Finish by recommending the best one and explaining why in one line.
