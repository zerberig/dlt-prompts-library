Context: I need to document the [role name] role at [type and size of company]. What I know so far: [notes about the role].
Goal: Have an internal job description that serves as a base for hiring, performance reviews, and clarifying responsibilities with the team.
Deliverable: A job description with: the role's purpose in 2 sentences, who it reports to and works with, 6-8 main responsibilities with approximate share of time, must-have requirements, nice-to-have requirements, key competencies, and 3-5 success indicators.
Criteria: Clearly separate must-haves from nice-to-haves, because mixing them discourages good candidates. Write responsibilities as outcomes, not loose tasks. Inclusive language with no requirements about age, gender, or appearance.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets, one page max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
