Context: I want to apply for [target job title] roles. Here are my unordered notes on jobs, dates, tasks, achievements, education, and tools: [your notes].
Goal: Turn those notes into a professional, well-organized resume I can paste into my editor and fine-tune.
Deliverable: The full resume: contact details, a 3-line summary, experience (3-5 bullets per role), education, skills and, if relevant, certifications or projects.
Criteria: Each bullet starts with an action verb and, where my notes allow, shows a measurable result. Don't invent figures: if an achievement needs a number I didn't give you, write "add figure" so I can fill it in. Prioritize what's most relevant to [target job title] and condense the rest. Two pages max.
Format: Text with Markdown headings and simple bullets. At the end, a list of the details still missing to complete the resume.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
