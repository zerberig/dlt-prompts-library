Context: I'm looking for work as a [target job title] in [city or work model]. My main skills are [your key skills] and my career goal is [your career goal]. I can spend [hours per week] hours a week on the search.
Goal: Go from sending resumes aimlessly to an organized search that gets me interviews within the next 4 weeks.
Deliverable:
1) Quick diagnosis: which types of companies and roles fit me best, and a list of 15-20 target companies with the criteria for choosing them.
2) Channel mix with share of time: job-board applications, direct outreach, networking, referrals, events, and LinkedIn profile.
3) A 4-week plan with measurable weekly goals (tailored applications, messages, conversations, events).
4) A 30-second elevator pitch for events and calls.
5) A simple tracking system (spreadsheet columns) and what to adjust if I get no responses after 2 weeks.
Criteria: Prioritize quality over volume: a few well-tailored applications beat many generic ones. Realistic goals for the time I have. If you have web search, use it to check current information, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source.
Format: Numbered sections; the 4-week plan as a table with Week, Goals, and Key actions.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
