Context: I studied [degree or program] and I'm looking for my first job as a [target job title] in [industry]. Here's what I have: [internships, projects, volunteering, part-time jobs, and skills].
Goal: A resume that makes up for limited formal experience by showing what I can already do and how fast I learn.
Deliverable: A one-page resume with: a 2-line career objective, education with relevant projects or courses, experience (internships, projects, volunteering, or jobs) with 2 or 3 bullets each, hard and soft skills, and languages or certifications if relevant.
Criteria: Each bullet starts with a strong action verb and shows a concrete result or takeaway, even from school projects ("Designed…", "Coordinated a team of 4…"). Translate non-work experience into skills the role values. Don't invent details; write "add detail" where something is missing. Below the resume, suggest 3 things I could do in the next month to strengthen it.
Format: Resume as text with Markdown headings and bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
