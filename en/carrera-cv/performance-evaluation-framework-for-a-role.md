Context: I lead or support a team at [type of company] and need to evaluate the performance of the person in the [role to evaluate] role. Their main responsibilities are: [role responsibilities].
Goal: Have a fair, clear evaluation framework that measures results, drives the person's growth, and supports regular feedback conversations.
Deliverable:
1) 4-6 key performance indicators (KPIs) with a definition, formula or way to measure, data source, and suggested target.
2) 3-5 behavioral competencies with an observable description for each level on a 1-4 scale.
3) An evaluation cycle: frequency, who takes part, self-assessment, and how it's documented.
4) A guide for the feedback conversation with suggested questions and how to close with a development plan.
Criteria: Each indicator should depend mainly on the person's own work, not on factors outside their control, so the evaluation is fair. Observable, unambiguous language ("delivers the report by the 5th" instead of "is punctual"). Suggested targets are reference points and should be adjusted to the team's reality.
Format: Indicators and competencies in tables; cycle and guide as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
