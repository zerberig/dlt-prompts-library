Write the professional summary that goes at the top of my resume. I'm targeting a [target job title] role in the [industry] industry. My background in a nutshell: [your experience and key achievements].
Give me 3 versions of 3-4 lines each: one focused on results, one on technical expertise, and one on leadership or working with people. Use concrete language with at least one verifiable fact or achievement per version, and skip empty phrases like "self-starter" or "results-driven" without proof, because recruiters skim past them. Use implied first person, without "I".
Finish with one line on which kind of job posting each version suits best.
