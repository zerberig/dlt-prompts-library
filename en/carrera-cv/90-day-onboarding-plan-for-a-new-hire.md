Context: Someone is joining as a [role name] at [type of company and team]. Their main responsibilities will be [role responsibilities], and they'll use these tools or systems: [tools or systems].
Goal: Help them ramp up fast, understand the business and the team, and work independently on their core tasks within 90 days.
Deliverable:
1) A pre-day-one checklist (access, equipment, materials, welcome message).
2) A day-by-day first-week agenda.
3) A phased plan (days 1-30, 31-60, 61-90) with learning objectives, training modules, key meetings, and concrete deliverables per phase.
4) A mentoring setup: who supports them, how often, and meeting topics.
5) Check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days with guiding questions and criteria for knowing it's going well.
Criteria: Balance learning with contributing: within the first month they should have at least one small visible win, because that boosts confidence and retention. Realistic workloads and a clear owner for each activity.
Format: Numbered sections; the phases as a table with Phase, Objectives, Activities, Deliverables, and Owner.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
