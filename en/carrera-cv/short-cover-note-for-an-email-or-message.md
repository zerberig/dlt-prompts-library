I'm sending my resume for the [target job title] role at [company name] by email or direct message to the recruiter. My 2 or 3 strongest points for this role are: [your strongest points].
Write an intro of 150 words max that says which role I'm interested in, why I'm a fit with one concrete fact, why this company in particular, and a clear invitation to talk. Include an email subject line and a 60-word version for a LinkedIn message. Direct, friendly tone, without repeating the resume.
