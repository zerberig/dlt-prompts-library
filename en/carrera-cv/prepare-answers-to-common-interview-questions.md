Context: I have an interview for a [target job title] role in [industry]. My main skills are [your key skills], and this is my relevant experience: [your relevant experience].
Goal: I want to walk in with rehearsed answers to the questions I'm almost certain to get, without sounding memorized.
Deliverable: The 10 most likely questions for this role (a mix of general, behavioral, and technical) and, for each, a sample answer of 60-120 words built from my experience.
Criteria: Behavioral answers follow the STAR method (situation, task, action, result). Use only facts I gave you; where something is missing, write "to fill in" instead of inventing it, because the interviewer may ask for details. Natural tone, the way I'd say it out loud.
Format: Numbered list; for each item, the question, one line on what the interviewer is assessing, and the sample answer.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
