Context: I work in [technical or digital field] and I'm targeting [target job title] roles. My experience, projects, and the tools or technologies I know (with approximate level): [your experience, projects, and tools].
Goal: A resume that shows not just which tools I know, but what I've built or solved with them.
Deliverable: A resume with a 3-line summary, a technical skills section grouped by category, experience with bullets that name the tool and the result in the same line, a featured projects section (problem, solution, technologies, and result), and education.
Criteria: Order tools by relevance to the role, not alphabetically. Skip skill bars or proficiency percentages, because they mean nothing to a recruiter; use levels in words or years of use. Use only the details I gave you and write "add figure" where a result is needed. Two pages max.
Format: Resume as text with Markdown headings and bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
