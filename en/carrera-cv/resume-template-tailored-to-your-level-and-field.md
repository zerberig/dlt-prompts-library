Context: I'm looking for work as a [target job title] in [field or industry], and my experience level is [experience level] (for example, recent grad, 5 years, management). I'll mostly apply in [country or market].
Goal: I want a resume template I can fill in and reuse myself, compatible with applicant tracking systems (ATS) and with that market's conventions.
Deliverable:
1) The recommended section order for my profile and the ideal length (1 or 2 pages), with one line on why.
2) The full template, with standard headings and, inside each section, brief instructions on what to write plus a one-line example tailored to my role.
3) What to include or avoid in that market (photo, age, marital status, personal details), noting whether it's custom or a requirement.
Criteria: Simple single-column format with no tables, icons, or graphics, because several ATS tools don't read them well. Mark fill-in spots with guillemets, for example «achievement with a number». Realistic examples, no inflated figures.
Format: The three parts with headings; the template in one text block I can paste into a document.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
