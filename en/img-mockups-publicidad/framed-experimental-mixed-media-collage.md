An experimental mixed media collage presented as a framed print mockup on a white studio wall. The composition combines cut-outs of vintage black-and-white photographs, a classical statue's face sliced into offset strips, torn old maps, splashes of orange and ultramarine acrylic, oil pastel strokes, yellowed masking tape, and misregistered screen-print texture. Geometric gold paper shapes float over the scene and give it a surreal reading. Thin oak frame, soft side window light, and a subtle shadow on the wall. Contemporary gallery mood.
