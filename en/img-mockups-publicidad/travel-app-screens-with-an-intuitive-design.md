UI/UX design presentation: three phone screens floating side by side in slight perspective over a peach-to-lilac gradient background. The first is a welcome screen with a paper airplane illustration and a large button reading "Get started"; the second is the home screen with a search bar, destination cards with rounded photos, and a four-icon bottom navigation bar; the third is a destination detail with a gallery, star rating, and a day-by-day itinerary. Clean interface with lots of white space, rounded corners, a friendly sans serif, and a coral accent. Crisp digital design.
