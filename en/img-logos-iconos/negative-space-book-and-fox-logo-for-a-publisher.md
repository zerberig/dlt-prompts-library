Negative space logo for an independent publisher, isolated on a plain white background and centered. An open book seen from the front in solid black, whose cut-out white pages form a fox's head in the negative space: the top corners of the pages become the ears and the central spine draws the snout. Below, the text "Fox Books" in a classic editorial serif, black. One-color vector illustration, pure black on white, no gradients or shadows, with shapes precise enough that the illusion reveals itself on a second look. Clever, literate, memorable mood.
