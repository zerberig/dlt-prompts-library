Wide shot with a slow, continuous push-in from the shore of a lagoon in Torres del Paine National Park, Chilean Patagonia, at sunrise. In the foreground, a guanaco lifts its head among golden grasses bent by the wind; behind it, the three granite towers turn from gray to blazing red as the first sun hits them, and lenticular clouds blush pink. The lagoon ripples with every gust. Photorealistic, moderate telephoto lens, warm raking light against blue shadows, travel-documentary style. Remote, cold, majestic mood.
SFX: strong gusts of wind and the guanaco's snort.
Ambient: constant Patagonian wind and a deep string chord that opens up.
