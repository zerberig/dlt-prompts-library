Low-angle shot from behind an engineer in an orange safety suit and harness, standing on a platform hundreds of meters above a futuristic city at sunrise. In front of her, a colossal dark-fiber cable rises straight up from the base of the tower and disappears through the clouds toward space, while robotic arms adjust anchors around it. She speaks into her helmet radio, a silver capsule with glowing windows begins climbing the cable and the camera tilts up to follow it.
