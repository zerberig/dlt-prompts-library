Documentary interview sequence in a coastal fishing village.
0–5 s: static medium shot of a fisherwoman in her mid-sixties sitting in a boat pulled up on the sand, a net in her hands, looking just off camera as she speaks; behind her, other brightly painted boats and the sea at dawn.
5–8 s: transition to a black-and-white archive photograph of the same beach decades ago, slowly pushing in on a group of fishermen hauling a net.
Photorealistic, soft natural light, fine grain, social-documentary style. Dignified, moving mood.
The woman says: "When the cannery closed, all we had left was the sea and each other."
Ambient: gentle waves, gulls and a melancholic piano.
