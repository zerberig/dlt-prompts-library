Dashboard hyperlapse from a classic van driving the Transpeninsular Highway in Baja California, Mexico, with the windshield and one hand on the steering wheel in the foreground. In 8 seconds the trip runs from sunrise to night: the straight road cuts through a desert of giant cardón cacti and round boulders, the turquoise Sea of Cortez appears around a bend, the sun arcs across the sky as shadows swing, and finally the road lies under a sky full of stars and the Milky Way. Photorealistic, wide-angle lens, warm colors shifting to deep blue. A mood of adventure and freedom.
