Isometric 3D animation in an infographic style; the camera slowly circles a gray office building pouring out smoke with every light on, linked by a yellow energy line to a large meter. Suddenly the building transforms piece by piece: solar panels appear on the roof, then a green roof with plants, double-glazed windows and sunshades, and the smoke fades away. The meter's needle drops and the text "LESS ENERGY" appears above it in bold white sans serif letters. Flat colors in greens, sky blue and soft gray on a clean cream background. Optimistic, educational mood.
SFX: soft clicks with each transformation and a chime as the needle drops.
Ambient: light, upbeat electronic music with marimba.
