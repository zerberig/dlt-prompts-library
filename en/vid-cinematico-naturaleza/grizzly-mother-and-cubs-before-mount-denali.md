Telephoto shot with a slow pan following a grizzly bear and her two cubs as they cross the autumn tundra of Denali National Park in Alaska. The mother stops to dig among red blueberry bushes and the cubs stumble along behind her; in the background, the snowy mass of Mount Denali is reflected in a still lake under a clear sky. Photorealistic, 400 mm lens with compression that pulls the mountain closer, low morning light, reds, ochres and golds of the tundra, wildlife-documentary style. Wild, majestic mood.
SFX: footsteps on twigs and a short grunt from the mother bear.
Ambient: cold wind over the tundra, a distant raven's call and soft strings.
