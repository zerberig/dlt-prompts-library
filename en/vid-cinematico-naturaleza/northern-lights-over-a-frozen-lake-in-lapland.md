Slow left-to-right pan across a frozen lake in Finnish Lapland at night. An intense green aurora borealis with violet edges ripples like curtains across the starry sky and reflects off sheets of polished ice; on the shore stand a red wooden cabin with one lit window and snow-laden pines. Toward the end the aurora flares up and forms a corona pulsing directly above the camera. Photorealistic, fast wide-angle lens, high dynamic range, snow glowing in the green light. Silent, magical mood.
SFX: deep cracks and booms from the lake ice.
Ambient: night silence with a very soft wind and an ethereal synth pad.
