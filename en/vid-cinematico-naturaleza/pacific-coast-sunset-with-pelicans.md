Static camera at water level, resting on algae-covered volcanic rocks on Mexico's Pacific coast at sunset. A wave rolls in slowly, covers the foreground rocks with white foam and pulls back, leaving shimmering threads of water; behind, the orange sun sinks into the horizon and a line of pelicans glides past in silhouette over the sea. Photorealistic, 24 mm wide-angle lens, sky fading from orange to violet, golden reflections on the wet sand. Calm, contemplative mood.
SFX: the breaking wave and the fizz of retreating foam.
Ambient: the steady murmur of the sea and distant gulls.
