Slow tracking shot starting at the lens of a running 35 mm film projector and moving along its beam of light through an old, empty movie theater, with red velvet seats and dust floating in the ray. An indistinct black-and-white scene flickers on the screen; the camera stops behind a single viewer seated in the middle row, in silhouette. Photorealistic, high contrast, film grain, warm tones and deep shadows. Reflective, intriguing mood.
A male narrator says: "Why do we keep coming back to this film after so many years?"
SFX: the clatter of the reel in the projector.
Ambient: the echo of an empty theater and a slow piano.
