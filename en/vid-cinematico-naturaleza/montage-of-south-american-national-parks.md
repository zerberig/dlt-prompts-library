Cinematic three-shot montage cut to the beat of the music, photorealistic, in a nature-documentary style.
0–3 s: drone shot pushing over a turquoise lake toward the Torres del Paine in Chile, the granite spires glowing pink at sunrise.
3–6 s: low shot beside the Devil's Throat at Iguazú Falls; the camera rises through the mist as swifts dart in front of the water.
6–8 s: top-down drone shot over the white dunes of Lençóis Maranhenses in Brazil, dotted with blue lagoons, as the camera slowly rotates.
SFX: roaring water in the second shot and wind over sand in the third.
Ambient: a rising orchestral piece with percussion hitting each cut.
