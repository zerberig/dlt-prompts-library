Slow drone orbit around a stretch of river in a farming valley as years pass in a continuous time-lapse.
0–3 s: the river runs gray, with foam and trash trapped against a small concrete dam.
3–6 s: machinery removes the dam, volunteer crews plant willows and reeds along the banks, and the water starts to clear.
6–8 s: the river flows clear between green banks and a white egret lands in the shallows.
Photorealistic, vegetation shifting with the seasons, natural light moving from dull gray to golden. Hopeful mood.
SFX: the egret's wingbeats at the end.
Ambient: water running cleaner and cleaner, birds gradually joining in, and a swelling piano.
