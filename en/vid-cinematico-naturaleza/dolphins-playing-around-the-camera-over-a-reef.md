Underwater shot with an almost still camera hovering in midwater over a shallow coral reef full of purple sea fans and colorful fish. A pod of five bottlenose dolphins enters the frame and spirals around the camera; one blows a perfect bubble ring that rises toward the surface while another playfully swims through it. Photorealistic, rippling sunbeams from the surface, crystal-clear turquoise water, marine-documentary style. Joyful, playful mood.
SFX: dolphin clicks and whistles and the burble of the ring.
Ambient: the muffled sound of the sea and a soft kalimba melody.
