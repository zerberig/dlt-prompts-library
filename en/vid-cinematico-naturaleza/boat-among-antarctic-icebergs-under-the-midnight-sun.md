Camera mounted on the bow of an inflatable boat moving slowly between turquoise-blue icebergs in a channel of the Antarctic Peninsula under the midnight sun. To the left, a colony of gentoo penguins huddles on an ice floe; suddenly, a few meters away, a humpback whale surfaces, blows a plume of vapor and shows its back before diving. In the distance, snowy peaks and a glacier calving into the sea. Photorealistic, low golden light, dark mirror-like water, nature-documentary style. Mood of quiet awe.
SFX: the whale's powerful blow and penguin calls.
Ambient: a soft outboard motor, lapping water and the distant creak of ice.
