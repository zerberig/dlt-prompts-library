Slow forward tracking shot down a narrow alley of a Marrakech souk in the late afternoon. The camera enters a lantern shop: hundreds of pierced-brass and colored-glass lanterns hang from the ceiling, casting stars of amber, ruby, and emerald light across the walls and the face of the shopkeeper, an older man in a djellaba who gently spins a lantern to show it off. Around him, stacked rugs, cones of spices, and metal teapots. Photorealistic, low warm light, dust floating in the beams, saturated colors. Immersive, spellbinding mood.
SFX: the soft jingle of brass as the lantern turns.
Ambient: the murmur of the souk, a distant call to prayer, and a gentle oud.
