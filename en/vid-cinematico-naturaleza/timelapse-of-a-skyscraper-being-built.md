Timelapse from a fixed camera on the roof of a neighboring building, with a very slow optical zoom, compressing months of construction into eight seconds. An excavated lot fills with formwork and concrete, two yellow tower cranes swing nonstop, the steel frame rises floor by floor and a glass curtain wall climbs up it from bottom to top, while the sky cycles through sunrises, storms and nights lit by work lights. It ends with the finished skyscraper reflecting an orange sunset. Photorealistic, wide angle, fast-moving clouds. Mood of achievement and progress.
SFX: sped-up metallic clanks and construction horns.
Ambient: an optimistic orchestral piece with piano and strings that builds to the end.
