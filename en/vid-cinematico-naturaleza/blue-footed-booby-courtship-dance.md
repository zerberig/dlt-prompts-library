Ground-level shot, static camera with a slight push-in, on black volcanic rock on Española Island in the Galápagos. A male blue-footed booby lifts one turquoise foot and then the other in a slow dance in front of a female, spreads his wings, points his beak to the sky, and whistles; she watches with her head tilted. In the background, a marine iguana basks in the sun and the turquoise sea breaks against the rocks. Photorealistic, telephoto lens, clear morning light, vivid colors, nature-documentary style with a touch of humor. Curious, charming mood.
SFX: the male's high whistle and the patter of his feet on the rock.
Ambient: waves, sea breeze, and calling seabirds.
