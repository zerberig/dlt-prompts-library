Slow vertical tilt that starts on the branches of a sacred fir covered in dense clusters of sleeping monarch butterflies and moves down toward the forest floor, in a mountain sanctuary in Michoacán, Mexico, in mid-morning. The first sunbeams cut through the mist between the trunks; as they warm up, the butterflies open their orange wings and thousands take off at once, filling the air with flickers that swirl in the light. Photorealistic, soft backlight, mist pierced by shafts of light, vivid orange against the dark green forest. Serene, wondrous mood.
SFX: the soft fluttering of thousands of wings, like light rain.
Ambient: forest silence, a distant woodpecker, and a delicate violin.
