Slow forward tracking shot pushing through a curtain of vines and large leaves deep in a dark, humid jungle, revealing a hidden waterfall pouring into a clear turquoise pool. Sunlight enters through a gap in the canopy and casts bright patches that shimmer on the water; a kingfisher launches from a branch, dives into the pool, and comes up with a small fish in its beak. Photorealistic, wide-angle lens, fine waterfall mist lit by the sun, deep greens and turquoise. Secret, magical mood.
SFX: leaves brushing past the camera and the bird's splash.
Ambient: the steady roar of the waterfall, cicadas, and tropical birdsong.
