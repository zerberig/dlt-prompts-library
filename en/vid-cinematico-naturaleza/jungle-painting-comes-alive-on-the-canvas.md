Slow forward tracking shot through a painter's studio lit by a window, heading straight for a large canvas on an easel showing a tropical jungle in thick oil brushstrokes. As the camera crosses the edge of the frame, the painted leaves begin to sway, a waterfall flows in strokes of paint, and a tiger with heavy impasto texture slowly steps out of the ferns and looks at the camera. Transition from realistic footage to animated oil painting, with visible canvas texture and a palette of deep greens and warm oranges. Magical, surprising mood.
