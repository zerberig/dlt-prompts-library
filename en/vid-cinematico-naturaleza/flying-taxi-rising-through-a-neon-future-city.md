Crane shot that starts at the level of a wet street in a futuristic city at night and rises to follow a yellow flying taxi as it lifts off a landing pad and climbs between colossal skyscrapers. The facades are covered in holographic ads in several languages, lit skybridges connect the towers, and streams of flying vehicles cross at different levels; below, pedestrians carry glowing umbrellas. Photorealistic science fiction, magenta, cyan, and amber neon reflected on the pavement, fine rain, and steam rising from street grates. Vibrant, dizzying mood.
SFX: the electric whine of the taxi's thrusters and the whoosh of passing vehicles.
