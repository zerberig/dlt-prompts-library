2D animation in a paper-cutout style with cardstock texture. Slow lateral tracking shot following a young woman as she walks through a polling station and drops her ballot into a clear box. As the ballot falls, her shadow on the wall transforms into silhouettes of early 20th-century women marching with banners, and then into a diverse crowd moving forward together toward the light. At the end, the text "YOUR VOTE COUNTS" appears centered in bold sans serif letters on a cream background. Limited palette of terracotta red, navy blue and cream. Inspiring, nonpartisan mood.
A female narrator says: "Someone fought so you could do this today."
Ambient: piano and strings that slowly build.
