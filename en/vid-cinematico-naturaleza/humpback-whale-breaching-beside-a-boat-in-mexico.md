Handheld slow-motion shot from the bow of a small boat in Banderas Bay, on Mexico's Pacific coast. About 50 meters away, a humpback whale launches almost entirely out of the water, rolls onto its side showing its white pectoral fins, and crashes onto its back in an explosion of foam; the spray glitters in the sun and a seagull crosses the frame. Photorealistic, telephoto lens, deep blue sea, green coastal mountains in the background, marine-documentary style. A mood of awe.
A tourist whispers: "No way..."
SFX: the enormous slap of water and spray hitting the boat.
Ambient: idling engine, waves against the hull, and distant cheers.
