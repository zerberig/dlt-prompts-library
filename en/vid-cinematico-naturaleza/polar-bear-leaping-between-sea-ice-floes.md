Top-down drone shot descending to follow a polar bear as it crosses a sea of broken ice floes in the Arctic. The bear walks to the edge of a floe, sizes up the gap, leaps over the dark blue water, and lands on the next sheet with all four paws splayed, splashing; then it keeps going, leaving wet paw prints in the snow. Photorealistic, low midnight-sun light, contrast between white ice and near-black water, wildlife-documentary style. A mood of solitude and survival.
SFX: the splash of the landing and the creak of the ice.
Ambient: Arctic wind and a minimalist theme of low strings.
