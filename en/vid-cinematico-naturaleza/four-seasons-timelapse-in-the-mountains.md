Fixed-camera timelapse with a very slow push-in toward a lone aspen on the shore of a mountain lake, with rocky peaks behind, showing an entire year.
0–3 s: snow melts on the slopes, wildflowers sprout and the tree fills with green leaves as clouds race past.
3–6 s: the leaves turn gold, the forest blazes orange and leaves drift onto the water.
6–8 s: a snowfall covers the peaks, the lake freezes and the tree stands bare under a gray sky.
Photorealistic, symmetrical framing with the tree reflected in the lake, shifting natural light. Contemplative mood.
SFX: melting snow, wind through dry leaves and cracking ice, each in its season.
Ambient: a minimalist piano piece that changes key with each season.
