Stylized 3D animation for kids with bright colors and friendly characters. The camera dives into a canal in Xochimilco, passes the hull of a colorful trajinera boat and finds a pink axolotl with fluffy gills waving a little foot. As it talks, it swims between water plants and roots, and small bubbles pop up around it with simple drawings of what it eats: worms and insects. Soft, warm lighting, clear green water with sunbeams, children's educational show style. Cheerful, curious mood.
The axolotl says in a cheerful voice: "Hi! I'm an axolotl, and I live right here in the canals of Xochimilco."
SFX: bubbles and a soft "pop" as each drawing appears.
