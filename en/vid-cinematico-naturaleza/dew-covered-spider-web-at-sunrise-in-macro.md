Macro video on a slider moving very slowly along an orb web covered in dew, strung between grass stems in a meadow at sunrise. Each droplet acts as a tiny lens reflecting the rising sun; focus jumps from drop to drop until it reaches a yellow and black garden spider at the center, adjusting a thread with its front legs. A droplet falls and the web trembles. Photorealistic, 100 mm macro lens, golden backlight, circular bokeh over the green background. Delicate, quiet mood.
SFX: the faint vibration of the thread and the falling drop.
Ambient: crickets fading out, the first birdsong and soft celesta notes.
