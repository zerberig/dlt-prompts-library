Time-lapse from a camera fixed high on a wall, looking down on a vacant lot between brightly painted houses in a Latin American neighborhood; shadows race across the ground as the weeks go by.
0–3 s: neighbors of all ages clear rubble and build raised beds from reclaimed boards.
3–6 s: rows of lettuce, corn, and tomatoes sprout; a painted water barrel and a mural appear.
6–8 s: time slows to real speed as a girl lifts a basket of red tomatoes while everyone else applauds.
