Water-level camera mounted on the bow of a wooden boat gliding slowly through a floating market in Vietnam's Mekong Delta at dawn. Boats loaded with pineapples, watermelons and dragon fruit pass on both sides; a vendor in a conical hat rows standing up and holds out a bunch of rambutans to the camera, while on another boat someone ladles steaming soup. In the background, wooden stilt houses and coconut palms in the haze. Photorealistic, soft golden light, reflections on the brown water, vivid colors, travel-documentary style. Lively, warm mood.
SFX: oars dipping into the water and the soft bump of boats touching.
