Low-altitude drone flight over the flat, rocky summit of a tepui in Venezuela's Gran Sabana, past dark pools, bromeliads, and carnivorous plants. The camera moves toward the edge, crosses the cliff, and tilts down to follow a waterfall hundreds of meters tall that dissolves into mist before reaching the rainforest canopy, as a rainbow appears in the spray. Photorealistic, wide-angle lens, clouds grazing the plateau, deep greens and black rock, nature-documentary style. Dizzying, primeval mood.
SFX: a gust of wind as the drone crosses the edge and the growing roar of water.
Ambient: distant jungle birds and a rising orchestral piece.
