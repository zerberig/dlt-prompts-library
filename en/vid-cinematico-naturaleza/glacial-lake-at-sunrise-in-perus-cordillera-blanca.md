Drone shot skimming half a meter above the water, moving slowly across Laguna 69 in Peru's Cordillera Blanca at sunrise. The turquoise surface is mirror-still, reflecting an ice-walled snowy peak with a waterfall spilling from its glacier; the camera gradually rises to reveal a valley of high Andean grassland and boulders. At the end, a trout jumps and its ripples break the reflection. Photorealistic, crisp 4K image, first pink light on the summits while the valley is still in blue shadow, mountain-documentary style. Pure, silent mood.
SFX: the trout's small splash.
