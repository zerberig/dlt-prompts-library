Time-lapse from a camera locked on a tripod on a hilltop, facing blue agave fields in Jalisco, Mexico, at sunset. A supercell rotates and advances toward the camera: the cloud base spirals, gray curtains of rain sweep across the rows of agave, and several branching lightning bolts strike the horizon, lighting the clouds from within in violet flashes. In the final second the rain reaches the foreground and drops hit the lens. Photorealistic, wide-angle lens, dramatic contrast between the dark sky and a last strip of orange light. Imposing mood.
SFX: rolling thunder and the pounding of heavy rain.
Ambient: rising wind and a deep, tense orchestral piece.
