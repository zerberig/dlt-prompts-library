Slow, low forward tracking shot through an ancient limestone cave on the Yucatán Peninsula. Age-old stalactites and stalagmites frame the path, tree roots hang from cracks in the ceiling and drops fall into puddles; ahead, the cave opens onto a cenote where a beam of light pours through an opening above onto clear turquoise water, as swallows dart through the beam. Photorealistic, rich detail in the wet rock, strong contrast between shadow and light, dust floating in the beam. Mysterious, sacred mood.
SFX: echoing drips and the flutter of swallows.
Ambient: the cave's low hum and a soft wordless choir.
