Nighttime underwater timelapse with the camera at reef level, slowly tilting up toward the surface. Brain and elkhorn corals release thousands of tiny pink and orange egg bundles all at once, rising like upside-down snowfall; the water column fills with specks that glow in the flashlight of a distant diver and in the moonlight piercing the surface. Small fish dart in to feed through the cloud. Photorealistic, nature-documentary style, deep contrast between black water and warm colors. Mood of a natural miracle.
SFX: bubbles from the diver's regulator.
Ambient: a low ocean hum and an ethereal wordless choir.
