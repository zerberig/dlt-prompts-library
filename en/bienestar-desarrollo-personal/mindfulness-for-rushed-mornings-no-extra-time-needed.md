Context: My mornings are chaotic: [what your mornings look like] (getting kids ready, a long commute, early meetings). I don't have time to sit and meditate.
Goal: Start the day calmer and more focused by using moments that already exist in my morning.
Deliverable: 5-7 micro-practices of 30 seconds to 2 minutes, each tied to a specific moment of my morning (turning off the alarm, in the shower, while the coffee brews, on the commute), with one- or two-line instructions.
Criteria: Discreet practices I can do around other people without interrupting what I'm doing. Include a short intention phrase to say as I leave the house.
Format: A list in the order of my morning: Moment, Practice, and Duration.
