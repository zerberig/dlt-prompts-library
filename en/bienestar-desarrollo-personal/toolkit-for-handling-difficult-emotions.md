Context: The emotions I struggle with most are [most frequent difficult emotions], and they usually show up when [situations that trigger them]. Right now I tend to react by [what you currently do to cope].
Goal: Have healthy alternatives ready for each emotion so I don't have to figure out what to do in the hard moment.
Deliverable: For each emotion, strategies at three levels: high intensity (something that works in 1-3 minutes to bring arousal down), medium intensity (10-20 minutes), and prevention (habits during the week). Add a section on how to gradually replace my current reactions that aren't helping.
Criteria: Concrete strategies with general backing in psychology (breathing, movement, social contact, writing, questioning thoughts), each explained in one line, without judging my current reactions. No diagnoses. Close by noting that if emotions overwhelm me often, it's worth seeing a mental health professional, and that if I have thoughts of harming myself I should contact my country's emergency line right away.
Format: One table per emotion (Level, Strategy, How to do it) and a short list at the end. It should fit on one printable page.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
