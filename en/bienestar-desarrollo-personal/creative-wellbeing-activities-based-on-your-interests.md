Context: I enjoy [your interests and hobbies]. I have around [free hours per week] free hours a week and a [budget level] budget for materials.
Goal: Find creative activities that help me release tension, lift my mood, and get away from screens, without turning them into another obligation.
Deliverable: 10 activities grouped into four types: artistic expression, hands-on crafts, nature and movement, and play or music. Mark the 3 that fit me best and explain why.
Criteria: Connect each activity to my interests in a concrete way ("if you like cooking, try…"), not a generic one. Include approximate cost, time, and whether it can be done alone or with others. Prioritize activities where the process matters more than the result, because pressure to do it well cancels out the relaxing effect.
Format: A table with the columns Activity, Type, Why you might like it, Time, Cost, and Solo or group; then a plan to try 3 activities over the next 2 weeks.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
