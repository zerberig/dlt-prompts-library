Context: I usually go to bed at [your usual bedtime], and in the last hour before sleep I usually [what you do before bed] (scroll my phone, work, watch shows). I have trouble switching my mind off.
Goal: A wind-down routine that signals to my body and mind that the day is over.
Deliverable: A 60-minute routine split into blocks (closing out tasks, screen-free transition, relaxation, and in bed), with 2 alternatives per block so it doesn't get boring.
Criteria: Include one technique for offloading worries, such as writing down tasks, and one for physical relaxation. Explain in one sentence the reason behind each block (light, temperature, mental arousal). It has to be realistic for someone short on time, so also include a minimal 15-minute version. If I've been sleeping badly for weeks or insomnia is affecting my days, recommend that I see a doctor.
Format: A table with the columns Time, Block, Activity, and Why it works; then the 15-minute version as bullets.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
