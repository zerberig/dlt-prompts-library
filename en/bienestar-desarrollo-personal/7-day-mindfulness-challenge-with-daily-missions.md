Context: I want to build a mindfulness habit and can give it [minutes per day] a day. My main goal is [your goal] (more focus, less stress, better sleep).
Goal: Try mindfulness for a week with a motivating challenge that leaves me wanting to keep going.
Deliverable: 7 days, each with a mission name, a formal practice with step-by-step instructions, a mini informal challenge for during the day, and an evening reflection question. Add a day 8 with suggestions for continuing based on how it went.
Criteria: Difficulty should rise gradually (from 3 minutes of breathing on day 1 to a longer practice on day 7), and each day should explore a different angle: breath, body, senses, emotions, thoughts, kindness, and integration. If I miss a day, tell me how to pick back up without starting over.
Format: One block per day with a heading, and at the end a tracking table with checkboxes.
