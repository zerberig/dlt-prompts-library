Context: I'm going to facilitate a workshop on [workshop topic] for [participants] (company employees, students, a community group). Length: [workshop length]. Format: [in person or online].
Goal: Participants leave with at least two practical tools they can apply that same week and feel safe enough to take part.
Deliverable: 1) Learning objectives. 2) A minute-by-minute agenda with an opening, short content segments, hands-on activities, pair or group discussion, and a close. 3) Instructions for each activity and questions to guide discussion. 4) Facilitator notes: confidentiality agreements, how to respond if someone shares something sensitive, and how to refer people to professional support. 5) A one-page follow-up handout for participants. 6) A 4-question survey to measure impact.
Criteria: Evidence-based content explained in everyday language; flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. Participation is voluntary, and nobody should feel pressured to share personal experiences. Make clear the workshop is educational and doesn't replace psychological care.
Format: A table for the agenda (Time, Block, Activity, Materials) and sections with headings for everything else.
