Context: A belief that holds me back: [limiting belief] (for example, "I'm bad at public speaking"). It shows up mostly in [situation where it shows up] and has kept me from [what it has kept you from doing].
Goal: Question that belief honestly and replace it with a more realistic one that lets me act.
Deliverable: 1) Questions to examine the belief: where it comes from, the evidence for and against it, and how someone who cares about me would see it. 2) A realistic alternative belief, not forced optimism. 3) Three small experiments of increasing difficulty to test it this week, with what to watch for in each. 4) What to do when the old belief comes back.
Criteria: A direct, empathetic tone with no empty motivational lines. Ground everything in observable actions, because beliefs change more through new experiences than through arguments. If the belief is tied to intense or persistent emotional distress, recommend I talk it through with a mental health professional.
Format: Numbered sections; the experiments in a table with Experiment, Difficulty, and What to watch for.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
