Context: My current routine: [your current routine]. The area I feel I neglect most is [area you neglect most].
Goal: A balanced personal development program where no area grows at the expense of the others.
Deliverable: 1) A quick 1-10 self-assessment for 6 areas (mind, body, relationships, learning, work, and personal finances), with a guiding question for each. 2) One keystone habit per area, explaining how it supports my growth and its 2-minute minimum version. 3) How to stack each habit onto something I already do. 4) The order for adding them, one every two weeks.
Criteria: Choose habits with reasonable backing rather than "habits of successful people" lists, and explain the why behind each in plain language. Start with the most neglected area.
Format: A table with the columns Area, Habit, Why it helps, Minimum version, and Anchor in my routine; then the rollout calendar.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
