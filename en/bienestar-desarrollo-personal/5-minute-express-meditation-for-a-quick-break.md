Write a 5-minute guided meditation script to do during [time of day] (for example, between meetings, in a parked car, or before picking up the kids). When it ends, I want to feel [how you want to feel] (calmer, more energized, or clear-headed).
Structure: 30 seconds to arrive and settle into posture, 2 minutes of guided breathing with counting, 2 minutes of attention to the body or to sounds, and 30 seconds to close with an intention for what comes next.
Use short sentences in the second person and mark pauses in parentheses. It should work while seated and with eyes open if needed.
