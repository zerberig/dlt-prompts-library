Role: Guide me like a reflective-writing coach, with open questions and no ready-made answers.
Context: I want to imagine what my life could look like [time horizon in years] years from now, with special attention to [life areas that matter to you] (work, relationships, health, creativity, community).
Goal: Get a clear, motivating vision of where I want to go and connect with what I value.
Deliverable: A three-part session. 1) Visualization: ask me 6 to 8 questions, one at a time, about an ordinary day in that future (where I wake up, who I'm with, what I do, how I feel). 2) Using my answers, help me write a letter from my present self to my future self with my hopes, fears, and one piece of advice. 3) Suggest 3 small first steps for this month.
Criteria: Wait for my answer before each new question. Use my own words in the letter. If goals come up that conflict with each other, point it out tactfully instead of ignoring it.
Format: Numbered questions during the session; the letter in a separate 200 to 300-word block; the steps as a list with suggested dates.
