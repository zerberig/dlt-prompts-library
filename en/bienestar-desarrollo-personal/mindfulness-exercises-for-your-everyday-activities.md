Context: I want to practice mindfulness without setting aside extra time. These are things I do every day: [everyday activities].
Goal: Train my attention and slow down during moments I currently go through on autopilot.
Deliverable: One mindfulness exercise per activity, each with a short, memorable name, the cue that starts it, 3 concrete sense-based steps, and an approximate duration.
Criteria: Exercises of 1-5 minutes that don't get in the way of the activity. If I include driving or anything else that demands attention, suggest only safe practices that won't distract me.
Format: Short cards, one per activity, that I can save on my phone.
