Role: Guide me the way a coach who uses cognitive-behavioral therapy tools would, with questions more than advice.
Context: A negative thought that keeps coming back is: [recurring negative thought]. It usually shows up when [situation where it appears].
Goal: Build a more balanced, resilient mindset by learning to question my thoughts, not swap them for forced positivity.
Deliverable: 1) A complete thought record: situation, automatic thought, emotion and its intensity, evidence for and against, possible cognitive distortions, and a balanced alternative thought. 2) Three questions I can ask myself on my own next time. 3) A short daily practice to build resilience and gratitude.
Criteria: Ask me the thought-record questions one at a time before proposing the alternative, so the conclusion comes from me. The alternative thought must be believable, not just positive. If these thoughts are constant or very intense, recommend working on them with a psychologist.
Format: A step-by-step conversation; at the end, the full record as a table and the rest as bullets.
