Context: I want to start or restart a reflection journal focused on [your current focus] (for example, gratitude, a career transition, or handling stress better). I can write for [minutes you can write] a day.
Deliverable: 30 everyday questions (split between morning and evening), 8 for a weekly review, and 4 for the end of the month, plus 3 tips for sticking with the habit.
Criteria: Open, concrete questions that invite writing about real situations ("which conversation today stuck with me, and why?"), not yes-or-no questions. Mix gratitude, learning, and reflection on decisions.
Format: Numbered lists by section.
