Context: Here's what I want to achieve personally and professionally: [what you want to achieve]. Timeframe: [timeframe]. Resources and limits (time, money, support): [resources and limits].
Goal: Move from general wishes to goals I can start this week.
Deliverable: 1) My aspirations rewritten as 3-5 specific, measurable goals, with a note if any of them compete for time or energy. 2) For each goal: why it matters to me, the 3 most likely obstacles with an "if X happens, I'll do Y" plan, progress indicators, and a first step that takes under 30 minutes. 3) A suggested order: what to start now and what to postpone.
Criteria: Be realistic about my resources, because chasing too many goals at once is the most common reason people drop all of them. Distinguish outcome goals from habit goals.
Format: One section per goal with bullets; at the end, a summary table with Goal, Indicator, Date, and First step.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
