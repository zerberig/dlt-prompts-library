Write a [length in minutes]-minute guided meditation script for the moments when I'm hard on myself, especially when [situation where you're hard on yourself] (for example, when I make a mistake at work).
Structure: arriving and breathing; acknowledging the critical voice without fighting it; a three-step self-compassion practice (noticing the discomfort, remembering that making mistakes is part of being human, and speaking to myself as I would to a good friend); loving-kindness phrases toward myself and then toward others; and a close with three things I can be grateful for today.
Criteria: Warm, simple language without forced positivity: it's okay to acknowledge that something hurt. Short sentences in the second person, with pauses marked in parentheses. At the end, add a brief note saying that if the self-criticism is constant or comes with persistent low mood, it's worth talking to a mental health professional.
