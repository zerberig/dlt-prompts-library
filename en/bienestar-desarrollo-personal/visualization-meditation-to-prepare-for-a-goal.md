Write a guided meditation script of about [length in minutes] minutes that helps me prepare mentally for [your goal or challenge] (for example, a big presentation, a 10K race, or my first month at a new job).
Structure: 1) slow breathing with counting to calm the body; 2) visualizing the process, not just the outcome: picturing the steps in sensory detail, a likely obstacle, and how I handle it calmly; 3) visualizing the moment I achieve it and how it feels in the body; 4) three believable present-tense affirmations; 5) a close with one small action I'll take today.
Criteria: Frame the visualization as mental rehearsal and motivation, not as something that guarantees the outcome, because the goal also depends on what you do. Short sentences in the second person and pauses marked in parentheses so I can record it.
