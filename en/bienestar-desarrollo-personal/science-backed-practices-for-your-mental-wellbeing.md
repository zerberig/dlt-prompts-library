Context: I want to improve my mental wellbeing, especially [area you want to improve] (mood, stress, energy, or focus). My day-to-day looks like this: [your routine and constraints] (schedule, physical activity, sleep, time or health limitations).
Goal: Know which practices have real support so I can focus my effort where it pays off most.
Deliverable: 8 to 10 evidence-based practices covering sleep, physical activity, nutrition, social connection, time in nature, and emotional regulation, ranked from highest to lowest expected impact for my case.
Criteria: For each practice, give the strength of evidence (high, moderate, or preliminary) and the minimum effective dose (for example, minutes of walking per week). If you have web search, draw on clinical guidelines or systematic reviews, and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. No miracle supplements. Recommend that I check with a doctor or mental health professional before making major changes or if I have persistent symptoms.
Format: A table with the columns Practice, Why it helps, Strength of evidence, Minimum dose, and How to start this week; then the 3 priorities for my first month.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
