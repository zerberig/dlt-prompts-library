Write 30 short mindfulness reminders to schedule as alarms or notifications on my phone throughout the day. My routine includes [your main daily activities], and what I most want to work on is [what you want to work on] (stress, patience, gratitude, posture, or unplugging from screens).
Each reminder should be 12 words or fewer, invite a concrete action that takes under a minute (breathe, notice the body, look around, appreciate something), and sound kind, not scolding.
Organize them into three blocks of 10 (morning, afternoon, and evening) and suggest a time window for each block. At the end, give me 3 tips so they don't turn into noise I end up ignoring.
