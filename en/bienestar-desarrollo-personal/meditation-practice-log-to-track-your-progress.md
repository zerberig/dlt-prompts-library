Context: I practice meditation or mindfulness [frequency and length of your practice], and my biggest challenge is [your biggest challenge when meditating] (getting distracted, getting sleepy, forgetting to practice, etc.).
Goal: See my progress with simple data and understand which conditions help me focus.
Deliverable: 1) A daily log template with useful columns (date, time, duration, type of practice, distraction level 1-5, mood before and after, short note). 2) A 5-question weekly review to spot patterns. 3) Five concrete tips for my main challenge. 4) Six types of background sound or music that pair well with practice, with the best moment for each.
Criteria: Filling in the log should take under a minute, because heavy logs get abandoned. Explain how to read the data without judging the "bad" days.
Format: The template as a table ready to copy into a spreadsheet, and everything else as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
