Context: I'm designing [type of wellbeing app] for [target audience]. My team and budget: [available resources].
Goal: Have a solid product concept to validate with users and pitch to partners or investors before building.
Deliverable: 1) Problem and value proposition in 3 sentences. 2) Prioritized MVP features (guided practices, progress tracking, personalized recommendations, community, or others) with the reasoning for each. 3) Personalization logic: what data is requested, what is optional (for example, calendar or wearable data), and how recommendations change. 4) Privacy and ethics: handling sensitive health data, consent, and what the app must not promise. 5) Safety protocol: how to detect crisis signals and route users to helplines or professionals. 6) Success metrics and 3 hypotheses to validate with users.
Criteria: Ground features in evidence-based approaches (mindfulness, cognitive-behavioral techniques, psychoeducation) and flag as an estimate any figure you can't back with a source. If you have web search, check current health-data regulation in [country or market] and similar apps already available. The app complements professional care rather than replacing it; reflect that in the design.
Format: A document with numbered headings; tables for features (Feature, Problem it solves, Priority) and metrics. 1,200 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
