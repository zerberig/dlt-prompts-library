Context: I want to pause and check in on how my wellbeing is doing at this point in my life: [your current situation].
Goal: Identify which areas are strong and which need attention, so I can decide where to focus my energy next month.
Deliverable: Give me an interactive 12-question questionnaire, one question at a time, covering sleep, energy, stress management, relationships, sense of purpose, and self-care, answered on a 1-5 scale. When we're done, give me a summary with my 2 main strengths, my 2 areas to care for, and 3 concrete actions for next month.
Criteria: This is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical assessment, so no diagnoses or labels. Use a warm, non-judgmental tone. If my answers show intense or persistent distress, recommend that I talk to a mental health professional; if I mention thoughts of harming myself, tell me to contact my country's emergency line right away.
Format: One question per message; the final summary with short headings and bullets.
