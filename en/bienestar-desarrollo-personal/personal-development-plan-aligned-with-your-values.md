Context: [your role and situation]. In the medium term I want [your three-year goals]. What I value most is [what you value most]. My strengths and growth areas, in my own view and from feedback I've received: [strengths and growth areas].
Goal: A personal development plan for the next 12 months that I can review every quarter and that doesn't stay at good intentions.
Deliverable:
1. A summary of where I am and where I'm heading, with the 2 or 3 gaps that matter most.
2. No more than 3 development areas, each with a measurable objective, why it matters for my goals and values, and learning actions (on-the-job practice, mentoring, courses).
3. A quarter-by-quarter calendar with milestones.
4. A quarterly review ritual with questions for adjusting the plan.
Criteria: A few well-chosen priorities beat a long list, because the plan has to fit a busy life. At least half the actions should be learned by doing, not just studying. Point out if any goal clashes with my values.
Format: Numbered sections; areas in a table with the columns Area, Measurable objective, Actions, Evidence of progress, and Date.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
