The script should follow the activity in the actual order I do it and guide my attention to the senses: textures, temperature, sounds, smells, and movements. Include how to come back gently when my mind wanders, and close with a short question to notice how I feel.
Format: short sentences in the second person, with pauses marked in parentheses, for example (10-second pause), so I can record it or read it.
