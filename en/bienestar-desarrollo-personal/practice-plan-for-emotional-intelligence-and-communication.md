Context: I want to get better at [skill you want to develop] (for example, managing my emotions in conflict, listening better, or giving difficult feedback). Where I struggle most is [situation where you struggle most]. My context: [your context].
Goal: Build the skill through deliberate practice in my daily life, not just understand the theory.
Deliverable: 1) What the skill involves, in a few lines, with observable signs that I'm applying it. 2) A 6-week plan with one small habit per week, tied to a specific moment in my day. 3) Two scripts or model phrases for the situation where I struggle most. 4) A 5-minute reflection exercise for after each important interaction. 5) How to ask someone I trust for feedback to measure my progress.
Criteria: Concrete, realistic exercises for my context, because the skill is built in real situations. If my emotional difficulties are seriously affecting my daily life, recommend I seek support from a mental health professional.
Format: Numbered sections; the 6-week plan in a table with Week, Habit, Time of day, and Sign of progress.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
