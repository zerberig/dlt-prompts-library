Context: I have a side project: [your project], and I started it because [why you started it]. I haven't made progress in [time without progress], and what holds me back most is [what's stopping you] (not knowing what's next, perfectionism, being tired after work, fear it won't be useful).
Goal: Get back into the project and keep making steady progress, even if it's small.
Deliverable: 1) A short diagnosis of the type of procrastination my situation describes and why it happens. 2) A 7-day restart plan with one 15-to-30-minute task per day, starting with the easiest. 3) 5 concrete techniques for my main obstacle. 4) A reminder of why I started, in 2 or 3 sentences, based on what I told you.
Criteria: Tasks so small and concrete they're hard to say no to ("open the repo and write the README for the next feature"). No guilt and no empty motivational lines. Include how to shrink the scope if the project is too big, because finishing a small version is sometimes worth more than planning the big one.
Format: Numbered sections; the 7-day plan as a table with Day, Task, and Time.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
