Context: I work at [your company or department], which has about [number of employees] employees working [work arrangement]. The main wellbeing challenges we see are [team wellbeing challenges], and the available budget is [budget in your currency].
Goal: Launch a wellbeing pilot that combines mindfulness and access to mental health support, and measure whether it's worth scaling.
Deliverable: 1) Pilot objectives and how to measure them (participation, short stress or wellbeing surveys, absenteeism), with a baseline. 2) An 8-week calendar of activities: workshops, short practices during work hours, break spaces, and training for managers. 3) Options for providing access to professional support (employee assistance program, partnerships, content) based on the budget. 4) An internal communications plan with a launch message. 5) Risks and how to mitigate them.
Criteria: Voluntary participation and confidential health data, because without trust nobody joins. The program complements, rather than replaces, changes to workload and culture; point out which organizational causes are worth reviewing. If you have web search, check current local regulations on psychosocial risks at work and flag what I should validate with a specialist.
Format: Numbered sections; the calendar as a table with the columns Week, Activity, Owner, Duration, and Estimated cost.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
