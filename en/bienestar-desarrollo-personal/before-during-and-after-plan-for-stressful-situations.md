Context: The everyday situations that cause me the most stress or anxiety are: [situations that stress you out] (meetings with my boss, traffic, public speaking, difficult conversations). When they come up, I notice [how you react physically and mentally].
Goal: Stop improvising in those moments and have a practical plan for each situation.
Deliverable: For each situation, one strategy to prepare beforehand, one discreet technique to use during it (breathing, grounding, pausing), and one to release tension afterward. At the end, 3 daily habits that lower my baseline stress.
Criteria: Techniques I can use at work or out in public without drawing attention, explained in one or two lines. If stress or anxiety keeps me from functioning normally, suggest seeing a mental health professional.
Format: A table with the columns Situation, Before, During, and After, followed by the habits as a numbered list.
