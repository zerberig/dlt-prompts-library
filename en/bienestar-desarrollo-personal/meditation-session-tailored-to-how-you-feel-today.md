I want you to design a meditation session tailored to how I'm doing today. My overall intention for this practice is [your intention for the practice].
First, run a quick check-in with these questions, all at once: my energy level (1 to 10), the main emotion I'm feeling, where I feel tension in my body, how much time I have, and where I am (home, office, commuting).
Based on my answers, choose the most suitable type of practice (breathing, body scan, listening to sounds, loving-kindness, mindful walking, or another) and explain in one sentence why you chose it.
Deliverable: the full session script fitted to the time I have, with short second-person sentences and pauses marked in parentheses, ending with a question to notice what shifted.
Criteria: If my energy is very low, avoid long silent practices; if I'm very wound up, start with something physical before stillness. If my answers suggest intense distress, suggest I talk to someone I trust or a professional before continuing.
