Context: This is what a typical weekday looks like for me: [your daily routine hour by hour]. What drains my wellbeing most right now is [what drains you most during the day].
Goal: Weave mindfulness into what I already do instead of adding another task to a full schedule.
Deliverable: 1) A short analysis of my routine: peak-stress moments, transitions I can use, and habits that can already work as anchors. 2) 5-7 practices tied to things I already do (for example, three mindful breaths when I open my laptop), each with its duration. 3) The one change to my routine that would have the biggest effect on my wellbeing, and why.
Criteria: Start with the easiest practices so the habit sticks. Be specific about the time and trigger for each practice, because vague intentions don't get done.
Format: A timeline of my day with the practices inserted, followed by the main recommendation in 3-4 sentences.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
