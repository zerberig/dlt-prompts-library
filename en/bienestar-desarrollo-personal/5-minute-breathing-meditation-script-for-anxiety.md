Context: I need a 5-minute breath-focused meditation script for anxious moments. I'll use it by [how you'll use it] (reading it silently, recording it in my own voice, or guiding someone else).
Goal: Have a reliable practice that helps me calm my body down and come back to the present when I feel wound up.
Deliverable: The full script with settling in and posture, a breathing technique with a longer exhale than inhale, anchoring in body sensations, what to do when the mind wanders, and a gentle close to return to what I was doing.
Criteria: Short sentences, second person, simple and kind language. Offer an alternative to counting breaths in case that makes me more anxious. No promises to "eliminate" anxiety. If anxiety is frequent or intense, recommend at the end seeing a mental health professional.
Format: Continuous text with pauses marked as "(pause 5 s)", at roughly 120 words per minute of practice.
