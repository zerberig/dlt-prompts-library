Context: Here's what a typical day looks like for me, morning to night: [your daily routine].
Goal: Practice mindfulness without adding extra time to my day, by building on habits I already have.
Deliverable: 8 mindfulness "anchors". For each one: the existing habit that acts as the trigger (for example, pouring coffee, opening the laptop, washing my hands, getting on the bus), a micro-practice of 30 seconds to 3 minutes, and a short cue phrase to remind me.
Criteria: Spread the anchors across the day and vary the type of practice (breath, senses, body, gratitude) so it doesn't become automatic. Start with the 3 easiest and suggest when to add the rest, because adding everything at once usually leads to dropping it.
Format: A table with the columns Moment, Trigger habit, Micro-practice, Duration, and Cue phrase; below it, a 3-week plan to phase them in gradually.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
