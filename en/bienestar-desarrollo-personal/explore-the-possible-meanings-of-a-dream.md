Context: Here is my dream, with every detail I remember: [description of your dream]. When I woke up I felt [how you felt when you woke up]. Lately this has been going on in my life: [relevant recent events].
Goal: Use the dream as a starting point to reflect on what I'm living through and feeling.
Deliverable: 1) The 3-5 main symbols or scenes and what they might represent from different angles (psychological, emotional, and personal). 2) The dream's emotional tone and what it might say about my current state. 3) Possible links to my recent life. 4) Four questions for further reflection.
Criteria: Present everything as possibilities, not truths; I decide the final meaning, because symbols vary from person to person. Empathetic, non-judgmental tone, with no predictions about the future. If the dream is recurring and distressing or affects my sleep, suggest raising it with a mental health professional.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets, 300-450 words.
