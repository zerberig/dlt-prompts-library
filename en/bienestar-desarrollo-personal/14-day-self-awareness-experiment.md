Context: I want to understand myself better in [area you want to understand better] (for example, how I handle stress, when I do my best work, or why I put certain things off). My current routine: [your current routine]. I can give this [time available per day].
Goal: Run a 14-day experiment that shows me real patterns about myself instead of assumptions.
Deliverable: 1) A daily log template with no more than 5 fields, plus the best time of day to fill it in. 2) A short reflection practice for each week. 3) A guide for analyzing my logs at the end: which patterns to look for and which questions to ask myself. 4) How to turn what I find into 1 or 2 concrete changes.
Criteria: Everything has to fit the time I have, because a log that feels like work gets dropped by day three. Use concrete, observable questions ("what was I doing right before?") instead of abstract ones.
Format: Numbered sections, with the template in a copy-ready table. 450 words max.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
