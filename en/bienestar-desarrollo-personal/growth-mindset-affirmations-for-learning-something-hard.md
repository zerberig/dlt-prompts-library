Context: I'm learning [what you're learning], and what frustrates me most is [what frustrates you most].
Goal: Keep a growth mindset through the hard part of learning.
Deliverable: 7 affirmations, one for each day of the week. Each with: the affirmation in first person (15 words max), the fixed-mindset thought it replaces, and a 10-minute micro-challenge for that day that puts it into practice.
Criteria: Believable affirmations focused on effort, strategy, and progress ("I haven't mastered it yet, and every attempt gets me closer"), not promises of success, because unrealistic statements trigger pushback.
Format: A table with the columns Day, Affirmation, Thought it replaces, and Micro-challenge.
