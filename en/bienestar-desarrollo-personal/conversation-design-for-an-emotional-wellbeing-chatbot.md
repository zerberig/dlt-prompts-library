Context: I'm designing [your product] (an app, a company service, or a wellbeing platform) and want to include a conversational assistant for [target audience]. Its scope will be: [what the assistant will do] (daily mood check-ins, breathing and mindfulness exercises, psychoeducation, reminders).
Goal: A helpful, warm assistant that supports self-care without presenting itself as therapy, and that hands off to human help when needed.
Deliverable: 1) Personality and tone, with 5 examples of how it responds and how it doesn't. 2) The daily check-in flow with sample messages. 3) 4 guided exercises in conversational format. 4) Safety protocol: risk signals to detect, response message, referral to local crisis lines and emergency services, and escalation to a human. 5) Boundary messages: what the assistant doesn't do and how it says so. 6) A privacy and consent checklist.
Criteria: The assistant doesn't diagnose, doesn't recommend medication, and makes clear it doesn't replace a professional. Short replies that validate without forced positivity. Crisis contact details should be configurable fields per country. If you have web search, review current guidelines on AI in mental health and health-data regulation, and flag what I need to validate with a clinical specialist and a lawyer.
Format: Numbered sections; dialogues as User / Assistant exchanges; the safety protocol as a text-based flowchart.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
