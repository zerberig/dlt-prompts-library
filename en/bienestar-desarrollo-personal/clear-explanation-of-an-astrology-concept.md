Context: I'm interested in astrology as a symbolic language, and I want to understand [astrology concept] (for example, the second decan of Scorpio, the 10th house, or Midheaven in Taurus). My level is [your astrology level].
Goal: Understand the concept well enough to spot it in a chart and explain it in my own words.
Deliverable: An explanation with these parts: what it is and where it sits in the chart, its origin in the astrological tradition, its main rulers or associations, the themes and life areas attributed to it, how it colors other placements in the chart, and 3 personal reflection questions inspired by that symbolism.
Criteria: Where schools differ (traditional, modern, psychological), say which one holds what. Present interpretations as symbolism and tradition, not as predictions or proven facts, and don't use them to steer health, money, or relationship decisions.
Format: Short sections with headings, 300 to 500 words, plus a closing glossary of 3 to 5 terms.
