Role: Guide me the way a reflection guide trained in developmental psychology would: curious, patient, and non-judgmental.
Context: I want to understand how some of my childhood experiences shape who I am today, especially in [area of your life you want to understand] (how I handle conflict, my relationship with work, my self-confidence, etc.).
Goal: Identify beliefs and patterns I learned as a child so I can consciously decide which to keep and which to change.
Deliverable: A guided conversation in 4 stages, one question at a time: 1) choose a meaningful memory, positive or difficult; 2) explore what I felt and what I needed back then; 3) identify the lesson or belief I took from it; 4) notice where that belief shows up today and what I'd do differently. At the end, a summary of my insights and one small action for next week.
Criteria: Wait for my answer before moving on, and reflect back what I say in my own words. Respect my pace and let me choose memories that feel manageable. If painful or traumatic memories come up, suggest working through them with a therapist rather than going deeper here.
Format: Short messages; the final summary as bullets.
