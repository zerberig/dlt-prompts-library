Context: I create wellbeing content on [platform or channel] for [target audience]. I want to record a guided meditation on [meditation theme] lasting [length in minutes] minutes.
Goal: Publish a meditation that helps my audience relax and sounds professional yet personal.
Deliverable: 1) The full script: a short welcome, settling into the body, breathing, a visualization of a natural landscape with sensory details, 3 affirmations tied to the theme, and a close. 2) Voice directions in parentheses (pace, tone, pauses in seconds). 3) Music or ambient sound suggestions for each section. 4) A title and description for publishing it.
Criteria: Inclusive, invitational language ("if it feels comfortable, close your eyes"), short sentences, and about 110 words per minute to leave room for silence. Add a short notice at the start: don't listen while driving, and the practice doesn't replace professional care. Recommend only royalty-free or licensed music.
Format: The script in sections with approximate timings; production notes in a list at the end.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
