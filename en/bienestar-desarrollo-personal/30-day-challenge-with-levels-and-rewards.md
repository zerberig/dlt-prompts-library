Context: I want to do a 30-day challenge on [challenge topic]. My current level: [your current level]. I can give it [minutes per day].
Goal: Make visible progress in one month and stay motivated with a game-like system.
Deliverable: The full challenge split into 4 levels (one per week) with a concrete daily task, a final test at the end of each level that puts what I learned to work, a points system with small rewards, rest days or wildcards, and what to do if I miss a day.
Criteria: Difficulty ramps up gradually and every task fits the time I have, because challenges fail when the first week is too demanding. Rewards should be healthy and not undercut the challenge's goal.
Format: A table with the columns Day, Level, Task, Points, and How I know I did it; then the game rules as bullets.
