Context: A high-pressure stretch is coming up: [stressful situation and how long it lasts] (a project deadline, exams, a move, caring for a family member). When I'm under pressure I usually notice [your stress signals]. My fixed responsibilities are [responsibilities you can't drop].
Goal: Reach the end of this stretch with my mental health intact, instead of just surviving it and ending up exhausted.
Deliverable: 1) My warning signs at three levels (green, yellow, red) with what I'll do at each. 2) Non-negotiable minimum habits for sleep, food, movement, and rest, in a realistic version for this period. 3) Strategies for the hardest days, including relaxation techniques and how to challenge anxious thoughts. 4) What I can delegate, postpone, or simplify. 5) My support network for this stretch: who to ask for what. 6) A plan to recover once it's over.
Criteria: Everything has to fit around my real responsibilities; favor the sustainable minimum over the ideal. At the red level, include seeing a mental health professional and, if thoughts of harming myself appear, contacting my country's emergency line right away. No diagnoses.
Format: A one-to-two-page document with headings; the traffic-light levels as a table (Level, Signs, What I do).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
