Context: I want to support [person and your relationship to them], who is going through [what they're dealing with]. Some real qualities I admire in them: [qualities and concrete examples].
Goal: Help them feel seen and valued, without my words sounding like clichés or minimizing what they feel.
Deliverable: 6 short messages to send on different days (by text or as a note), each based on a specific quality or memory, plus 3 things to say in person.
Criteria: Warm, natural tone that fits our relationship. Acknowledge their experience before encouraging them, and avoid lines like "just stay positive" or "everything happens for a reason." If what they're facing seems serious, end with a gentle way to encourage them to seek professional support.
Format: A numbered list of messages and another for the in-person lines.
