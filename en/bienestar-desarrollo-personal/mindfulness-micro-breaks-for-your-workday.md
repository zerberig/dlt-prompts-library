Context: My typical day: [your typical day]. My most stressful moments are usually [most stressful moments].
Goal: Bring mindfulness into my day without adding a long meditation session, to lower stress and notice my thoughts and feelings better.
Deliverable: 6 micro-breaks of 1-3 minutes, each tied to a moment that already exists in my day (first coffee, before opening email, after a meeting, the commute). For each: the trigger, step-by-step instructions, and what to notice.
Criteria: Simple practices I can do in an office or on public transit without anyone noticing. If the stress is constant or affects my sleep or health, include a note recommending I see a health professional.
Format: A numbered list with the trigger in bold.
