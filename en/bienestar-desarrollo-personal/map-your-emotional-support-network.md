Context: I live in [your city or living situation], and my support network currently looks like this: [people and groups you can count on today]. What makes connecting hardest for me is [your main obstacle to connecting] (shyness, little time, a recent move, etc.).
Goal: I want a stronger support network so I can get through hard times without relying on a single person.
Deliverable: 1) A map of my network by type of support: emotional, practical, advice, and companionship, showing where the gaps are. 2) A 4-week plan with one concrete action per week to reconnect with someone or add someone new. 3) Three short sample messages to get back in touch with someone I've lost contact with.
Criteria: Small, realistic actions that fit my obstacle, because big goals get abandoned. Include options beyond my personal circle (interest groups, volunteering, moderated online communities) and mention when it makes sense to add a mental health professional to the network.
Format: A table for the map (Type of support, Who provides it today, Gap, Idea to fill it), a numbered list for the plan, and each message in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
