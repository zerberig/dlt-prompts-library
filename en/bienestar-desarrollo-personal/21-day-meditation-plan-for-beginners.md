Context: I've never meditated consistently. I can spend at most [minutes per day] minutes a day, and the best time for me is [time of day].
Goal: Build a daily meditation habit over 21 days and finish knowing which type of practice works for me.
Deliverable: A 21-day plan in three weeks: week 1, foundations (breath and posture); week 2, body and sensations; week 3, emotions and loving-kindness. For each day: practice, duration, and an instruction in 2 or 3 sentences.
Criteria: Start with short sessions and build up gradually without exceeding my maximum. Include one review day per week and what to do if I miss a day, because consistency matters more than perfection. Add 3 questions to log how each week went.
Format: One table per week with the columns Day, Practice, Duration, and Instruction; at the end, how to keep going after day 21.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
