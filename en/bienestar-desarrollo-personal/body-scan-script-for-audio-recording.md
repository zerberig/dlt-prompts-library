Context: I'm going to record a body scan meditation for [where it will be published] (a podcast, video channel, app, or yoga class). My audience is [your audience], and the total length should be about [length in minutes] minutes.
Goal: An audio track that relaxes the body and trains attention, and that sounds natural when read aloud.
Deliverable: The full script with an introduction and posture, a sweep through the body from feet to head, a whole-body integration, and a closing to come back slowly.
Criteria: Simple, sensory language without jargon that invites noticing without changing anything. Include inclusive options (lying down or seated, eyes open or closed, skipping an uncomfortable area). Mark pauses with their length in parentheses, for example (15-second pause), and add a timestamp every minute to keep the pace. Assume about 110 to 130 words per minute of slow speech, not counting pauses.
Format: Script in sections with headings; at the top, a 3-line note with voice direction and suggested background music; at the end, a short disclaimer for the episode description stating that the practice doesn't replace care from a health professional.
