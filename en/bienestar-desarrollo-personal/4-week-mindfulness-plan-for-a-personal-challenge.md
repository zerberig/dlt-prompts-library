Context: I want to work on [personal challenge] (for example, impatience, constant worrying, or trouble switching off from work). I have [minutes available per day] minutes a day, and my meditation experience is [your prior experience].
Goal: Build a sustainable mindfulness practice over 4 weeks that helps me respond better to that challenge.
Deliverable: A 4-week plan with three short daily practices (morning, during the day, and night), one affirmation per week, and a progress log.
Criteria: Increase the difficulty or duration slightly each week. Affirmations should be believable and in the present tense ("I can pause before I react"), not over-the-top promises, because statements you don't believe tend to backfire. Include what to do if I miss a day. If the challenge involves persistent anxiety or low mood, remind me that this plan complements, rather than replaces, care from a mental health professional.
Format: One table per week with the columns Moment, Practice, Duration, and Short instruction; below it, the week's affirmation and 3 log questions to answer each night.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
