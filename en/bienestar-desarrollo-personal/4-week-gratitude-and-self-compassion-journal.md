Context: I want to start a gratitude journal, but whenever I've tried, I end up writing the same things every day. I have [minutes per day] a day and prefer to write [time of day].
Goal: Build positive emotions and treat myself more kindly, without forcing an optimism I don't feel.
Deliverable: 28 prompts, one per day, organized into 4 themed weeks: people, small everyday things, my body and abilities, and hard moments I learned from. Include two self-compassion exercises each week (for example, writing to myself the way I'd write to a good friend) and a closing reflection.
Criteria: Specific prompts that push me to find new answers. Leave room to acknowledge difficult emotions alongside gratitude, because forced gratitude backfires.
Format: One section per week with prompts numbered day 1 through 28.
