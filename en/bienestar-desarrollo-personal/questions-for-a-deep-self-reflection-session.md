Context: I want to spend an afternoon reflecting deeply on [area or topic] (for example, my career, how I lead, or my relationships). I'm doing it now because [why you want to reflect now].
Goal: See my strengths, weak spots, and the assumptions I take for granted more clearly.
Deliverable: A 3-round session with 5 questions each: 1) Where I am (facts and patterns). 2) What I'm taking for granted (assumptions and perspectives I haven't considered). 3) Where I'm heading (decisions and next steps). Close with one question for choosing a concrete action.
Criteria: Questions that are a little uncomfortable and push for concrete examples, because vague answers reveal nothing new. Avoid yes-or-no questions.
Format: Three numbered sections, each with a short instruction at the start.
