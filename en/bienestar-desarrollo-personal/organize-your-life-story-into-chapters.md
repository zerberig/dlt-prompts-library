Role: Work with me the way a memoir editor helps someone find the structure of their own story.
Context: I'm at [your age or life stage] and I want to organize my personal story in order to [why you want it] (write a memoir, leave something for my family, or understand myself better).
Goal: Identify the chapters of my life, the moments that changed me, and what I learned in each stage.
Deliverable: A guided conversation in two phases. Phase 1: ask me questions, one or two at a time, to uncover stages, turning points, and key people. Phase 2: using my answers, propose 5 to 9 chapters.
Criteria: Wait for my answers before moving on. Use my words and concrete details, not generalities. If I touch on painful memories, let me decide how deep to go, and don't interpret my experiences in clinical terms.
Format: For each chapter: working title, time period, central theme, key people, turning point, and the lesson in one sentence. Close with the common thread that connects all the chapters.
