Context: These are my current sleep habits: [your sleep habits] (bedtime and wake time on weekdays and weekends, caffeine, alcohol, screens, naps, exercise). My bedroom is like this: [what your bedroom is like] (light, noise, temperature, who I sleep with). What affects me most is [your main sleep problem].
Goal: Improve the quality and regularity of my sleep with changes I can sustain.
Deliverable: 1) A brief assessment: which habits are probably affecting me and why. 2) A 2-week plan that introduces no more than 2 changes per week, starting with the highest-impact ones (consistent schedule, light, caffeine, environment). 3) A simple sleep diary to fill in each morning. 4) How to read the diary on day 14 to decide what to keep.
Criteria: Base the recommendations on clinically supported sleep hygiene and explain the reason for each in one sentence. Gradual changes, because changing everything at once usually fails. Don't diagnose sleep disorders; if I mention loud snoring, pauses in breathing, severe daytime sleepiness, or insomnia lasting more than a month, recommend that I see a doctor.
Format: Sections with headings; the plan as a table per week with the columns Change, How to apply it, and Why; the diary as a table with the columns Date, Bedtime, Time to fall asleep, Awakenings, Wake time, and How I feel (1 to 5).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
