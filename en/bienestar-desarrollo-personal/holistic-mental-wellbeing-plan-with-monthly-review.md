Context: My mental wellbeing goals are: [your wellbeing goals]. The challenges I'm facing right now are [your current challenges], and what already helps me is [what already works for you] (activities, people, apps, therapy).
Goal: A personal wellbeing plan that brings together habits, support, and resources, and that I can review and adjust every month.
Deliverable: 1) My goals reframed as concrete, measurable goals. 2) Baseline routines in five areas (sleep, movement, nutrition, social connection, and mental rest), with a minimum daily action and a weekly action for each. 3) My support network: people, professionals, and resources, and when to turn to each. 4) A plan for hard days: early signs that I'm not okay and what I do in each case. 5) Optional digital tools for tracking. 6) A monthly review template.
Criteria: Build on what already works for me instead of starting from scratch. Small, specific actions. This plan complements, rather than replaces, professional care: if I mention persistent symptoms or ongoing treatment, suggest reviewing the plan with my therapist or doctor. If you recommend apps and have web search, check that they're still available.
Format: Six sections with headings; the routines as a table with the columns Area, Minimum daily action, Weekly action, and Indicator.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
