Context: I want to set up a meditation space in [available space] (a bedroom corner, a balcony, a small apartment, etc.) of about [space dimensions]. My budget is [budget in your currency], and I practice [type and length of your practice].
Goal: A place that invites me to sit and practice every day and feels calming the moment I see it.
Deliverable: 1) Layout of the space and recommended seating (cushion, bench, or chair) for my practice. 2) A plan for natural and artificial lighting, color palette, scents, and sound. 3) A shopping list at three levels: using what I already have, basic budget, and full budget. 4) Three habits to keep the space tidy and intentional.
Criteria: Favor simple, movable solutions in case the space is shared or rented. Suggest gentle scents with good ventilation, and candle-free alternatives if there are pets or kids.
Format: Sections with headings and bullets; the shopping list as a table (Item, Level, Approximate cost), with costs marked as estimates.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
