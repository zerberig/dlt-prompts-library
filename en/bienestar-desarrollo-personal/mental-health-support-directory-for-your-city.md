Context: I live in [your city and country] and I'm looking for mental health support for [who it's for] (myself, a family member, a group of students, or a team at work). What I need most is [type of support] (crisis care, low-cost therapy, support groups, addiction support, etc.).
Goal: Have a practical guide to know where to go depending on how urgent the need is.
Deliverable: A directory organized into three tiers: 1) emergency and crisis, 2) professional care (public, university clinics, nonprofits, and low-cost private), 3) support groups and online resources.
Criteria: Use web search if you have it and prioritize official sources. For each resource, list services, who it serves, requirements, approximate cost, hours, and how to contact them. Flag any detail you can't confirm as "to verify", because phone numbers and hours change. Always start with the local emergency number and an available crisis line.
Format: One table per tier with the columns Resource, What it offers, Who it's for, Cost, Hours, and Contact. End with 3 tips for choosing between the options.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
