Criteria: Participation must be voluntary and respect everyone's privacy, because imposed wellbeing backfires. Include at least two initiatives that change how work gets done (workloads, meetings, schedules), not just activities. Flag costs and any figure you can't back with a source as estimates.
Format: A table (Initiative, Rollout, Cost, Metric, Risk) and a final recommendation on which to start with.
