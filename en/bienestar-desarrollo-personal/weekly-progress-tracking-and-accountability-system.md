Context: These are my current development goals: [your current goals]. To stay organized I use [tool you use] (for example, a notebook, Notion, or a spreadsheet). I can lean on [support person or group].
Goal: A simple system to track progress every week, notice early when I'm drifting, and not rely on willpower alone.
Deliverable: 1) A weekly tracker adapted to my tool, with monthly milestones. 2) A 15-minute weekly review with guiding questions (what moved forward, what held me back, what I'll adjust). 3) An accountability setup with my support person or group: what to share, how often, and how to ask for help. 4) A protocol for getting back on track after a bad week.
Criteria: Track actions within my control, not just outcomes, because that keeps motivation alive when results are slow. Everything should take little time each week.
Format: Numbered sections; the tracker in a copy-ready table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
