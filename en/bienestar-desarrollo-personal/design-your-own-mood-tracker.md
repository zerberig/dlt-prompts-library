Context: I want to track my mood in [tool you'll use] (a spreadsheet, Notion, a notes app, or a notebook). I'm mostly interested in seeing [what you want to observe] (how sleep, work, certain people, or exercise affect my mood).
Goal: Spot patterns with simple data, without tracking taking more than 2 minutes a day.
Deliverable: 1) The structure of the daily entry: fields, scale, and examples of how to fill it in. 2) A short list of emotions to choose from, more precise than "good" or "bad". 3) A 10-minute weekly review with 5 questions for finding patterns. 4) If I use a spreadsheet, the basic formulas or charts to see trends.
Criteria: Few fields that are easy to fill in, because a complicated tracker gets abandoned within a week. The goal is to observe, not to judge myself. Include a note saying that if low mood, anxiety, or insomnia dominate for two weeks or more, it's worth sharing the log with a mental health professional.
Format: The daily entry as a sample table with 3 days filled in; the weekly questions as a numbered list.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
