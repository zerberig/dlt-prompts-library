Context: I have [important moment] coming up (a presentation, a negotiation, or an interview). Real achievements related to it: [related real achievements]. My values: [your values].
Goal: Walk in confident and clear-headed, remembering what I know I can do.
Deliverable: 5 personalized first-person affirmations, each backed by an achievement or value I gave you, plus a 2-minute routine to do right before (breathing, posture, repeating 2 affirmations, and setting an intention for the moment).
Criteria: Believable, specific affirmations anchored in real evidence, because generic lines like "I'm the best" don't even convince ourselves. 15 words max per affirmation.
Format: A numbered list of affirmations with the supporting achievement in parentheses; then the routine as steps.
