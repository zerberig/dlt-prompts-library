Context: I want to work on [area you want to grow in] (confidence speaking in public, consistency, self-esteem, etc.). Some things I've already achieved or do well: [real achievements or qualities].
Goal: Get affirmations that genuinely motivate me; overly optimistic lines sound fake to me and don't help.
Deliverable: 8 first-person affirmations: 4 "bridge" statements about progress (like "I'm learning to…") and 4 backed by my achievements. For each one, the evidence from my life that supports it and the best time of day to say it.
Criteria: Lines of 15 words max, present tense, positive, and in words I'd actually use. Avoid absolute promises like "I never fail."
Format: A table with the columns Affirmation, Evidence, and Time of day.
