Photorealistic concept art of an underwater city on the ocean floor, about thirty meters deep. Translucent domes of different sizes, linked by glass tunnels, sit among coral reefs; the towers have shapes reminiscent of jellyfish and seashells, and their edges glow with cyan and violet bioluminescent light. Schools of silver fish and a manta ray pass a window where people can be seen in an indoor garden. Shafts of sunlight fall from the surface and mix with the city's glow. Suspended particles, wide perspective. Serene, wondrous mood.
