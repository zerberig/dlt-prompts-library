Photorealistic architectural render of the interior of a fractal pavilion in a park. The CNC-cut pale-wood structure repeats the same branching form at ever-smaller scales: large arches split into smaller arches, and those into smaller ones still, until they form an ultra-fine lattice that covers the ceiling like a canopy of leaves. Sunlight passes through the gaps and casts an equally intricate shadow on the floor. A couple of visitors look up. 16 mm lens pointed at the ceiling in radial symmetry, midday light. Hypnotic, mathematical, organic mood.
