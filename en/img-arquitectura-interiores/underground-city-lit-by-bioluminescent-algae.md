Digital concept art of a city built inside an immense underground cavern. From the mouth of a tunnel in the foreground, the view opens onto tiers of homes carved into the rock, linked by suspension bridges and round tunnels. The tunnel walls are coated in bioluminescent algae glowing blue and mint green, and gardens of luminous fungi hang from the stalactites, feeding small turbines. An underground river at the bottom of the cavern reflects all the light. Detailed digital painting with deep perspective, atmospheric haze, and contrast between dark rock and points of light. Serene, mysterious mood.
