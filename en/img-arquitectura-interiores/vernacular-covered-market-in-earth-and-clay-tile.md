Photorealistic architectural photograph of a newly built farmers market using vernacular techniques. Thick rammed-earth walls in ochre layers, a gabled clay-tile roof supported by hand-lashed trusses of local timber, and reed lattice screens that let air flow through. Long stone tables await the vendors; at the back, a courtyard with a mesquite tree and a water cistern. Early in the morning, the space is nearly empty, with just one farmer unloading crates of corn and squash. 24 mm lens at f/8, low sunrise light slanting through the screens. Simple, dignified mood rooted in its place.
