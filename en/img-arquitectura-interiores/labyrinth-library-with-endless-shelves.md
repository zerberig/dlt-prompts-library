Fantasy digital painting of a labyrinthine library seen from below: dark wooden shelves packed with old books rise on every side until they vanish into a golden haze, linked by wrought-iron bridges, balconies, and spiral staircases that cross at different levels. Between two bookcases, a secret door stands ajar with warm light beyond it. Brass lamps with green shades light reading nooks, and a few loose pages float in the air. Detailed painterly style, dizzying vertical perspective, a palette of mahogany, bottle green, and gold. Mood of mystery, wisdom, and intimacy.
