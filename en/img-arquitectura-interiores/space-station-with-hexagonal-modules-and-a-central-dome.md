Photorealistic sci-fi render of a space station orbiting a ringed gas giant. The structure is a honeycomb of white hexagonal modules with gold panels, linked by short corridors and clustered around a large central glass dome that houses a zero-gravity tree garden. Solar panel arms stretch out to the sides, and a small ship approaches a docking port. Hard light from the distant sun from one side, deep black shadows, and the planet filling the lower half of the frame. Calm, isolated mood.
