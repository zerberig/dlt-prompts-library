Fantasy concept art of a city carved straight into the sheer face of a granite mountain. From a valley with a river, hundreds of windows, balconies, and columns cut into the rock are visible, stepped across dozens of levels and linked by exterior staircases. Through every opening glows the amber light of huge crystals embedded in the mountain, and a waterfall pours beside the main gate, guarded by two giant statues. Blue hour at dusk, the cold sky contrasting with the city's warm glow. Epic digital painting with vast scale and mist in the valley. Ancient, mysterious mood.
