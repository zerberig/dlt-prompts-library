Surreal concept art of an endless white salt flat reflecting the sky like a mirror. Above it float a dozen buildings of pale stone and glass at different heights, with rocky roots hanging beneath each one, like islands torn from the ground. Thin staircases and walkways connect them in midair with no visible supports, and a small human figure crosses one of them. Low clouds drift between the structures. Clean, highly detailed digital painting in a pastel palette of peach, lavender, and pale blue, with soft dawn light and long shadows mirrored on the salt flat.
