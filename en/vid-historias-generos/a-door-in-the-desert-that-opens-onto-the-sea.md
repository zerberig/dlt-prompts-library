Slow forward tracking shot over a desert of gray sand under a bone-white sky. In the middle of nowhere stands an old wooden door with no walls. A woman in a long dress holding a closed umbrella stops before it and opens it: on the other side there is no desert but a calm sea at night under a full moon, and a gentle wave spills onto the dry sand, wetting her feet as she stands unmoved. Pocket watches drift slowly through the air. Animated surrealist painting style, muted palette of grays, ochres, and desaturated blues, long shadows. Mysterious, ambiguous mood.
SFX: the creak of the door and the murmur of a wave.
