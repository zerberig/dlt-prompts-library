Fixed frontal shot with a very slow push-in on an animated mixed-media collage. A sepia vintage photograph of a woman in profile is glued over yellowed newspaper clippings. Black ink lines start drawing themselves from her hair and turn into branches; from them bloom acrylic flowers in thick strokes of red, cobalt, and yellow that spill beyond the photo, while the newspaper clippings peel away and fly off like paper birds. Mixed-media animation with real paper, tape, and paint textures, with a Dada avant-garde feel. Dreamlike, mysterious mood.
SFX: paper tearing and the scratch of an ink pen.
Ambient: a distorted music-box waltz.
