Slow arcing tracking shot around two people on the rooftop of an office building at night. An executive in her mid-thirties, in a tailored suit with her phone buzzing in her hand, steps out for air and finds a muralist in paint-stained overalls finishing a giant hummingbird mural on the water tank wall.
