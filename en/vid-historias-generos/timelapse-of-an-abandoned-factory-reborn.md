Fixed wide-angle timelapse from a high balcony inside an abandoned textile factory. 0–3 s: graffiti, broken glass, and weeds; volunteers in gloves sweep debris as sunlight shadows race across the floor. 3–6 s: scaffolding appears, the walls are painted white, skylights go in, and planters with trees are added. 6–8 s: time slows to reveal a bustling community market with wooden stalls, string lights, and children running between the plants. Photorealistic, shifting from cold gray tones to warm, bright ones. Inspiring mood.
