Medium shot with a very slow push-in on the living room of a wooden cabin in the woods at night, lit only by candles. Four friends laugh around an antique record player they found in the basement, and one drops the needle on an unlabeled record. A chant in an unknown language plays; the laughter dies, the candles go out one by one from left to right, and in the dark window's reflection a tall silhouette appears behind them for an instant. Supernatural horror with no gore, warm candlelight giving way to cold blues, deep shadows. Unsettling mood.
One says quietly: "Who else is listening to this?"
SFX: vinyl crackle and a soft puff as each candle dies.
Ambient: wind through the pines and the distorted chant.
