Slow circular tracking shot around a middle-school student seated in a simple small-town classroom as she puts on a lightweight VR headset. As the camera circles, the walls, desks, and chalkboard break apart into thousands of light particles and rebuild as a deep-ocean reef; a school of glowing fish swims through where the window used to be, and she reaches out to touch a jellyfish. Stylized 3D animation with soft lighting, a smooth shift from warm tones to bioluminescent blues. Mood of wonder and tech optimism.
She whispers: "It's like being there."
SFX: a crystalline shimmer as the headset activates.
Ambient: underwater sound and a rising synthesizer.
