Slow arcing tracking shot around a brick-walled warehouse with a huge upright canvas. A seated cellist plays with his eyes closed while a painter in overalls throws broad strokes of blue and orange in time with the music: when he builds intensity, she flicks paint from the brush; when he softens, she draws a slow, thin line. At the end they look at each other and smile. Photorealistic, warm light from hanging bulbs, dust in the air, paint droplets in slow motion. Creative, intimate mood.
SFX: the wet slap of the brush on canvas.
