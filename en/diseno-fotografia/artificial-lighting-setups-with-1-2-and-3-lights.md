Context: I shoot [type of photography] in [space where you shoot] and have this lighting gear: [your lighting gear] (flashes, continuous LED lights, modifiers).
Goal: Master a handful of lighting setups and know what story each one tells, so I stop improvising at every session.
Deliverable: 6 setups ordered from 1 to 3 lights (for example, key light with reflector, Rembrandt, rim light, high key, low key, and silhouette against a lit background). For each: a text diagram with each light's position (angle, height, and approximate distance from the subject), modifier, key-to-fill ratio, starting camera settings, the composition and framing that suit it best, the emotion or kind of story it conveys, and a common mistake. If my gear falls short for any setup, suggest an alternative using what I have.
Criteria: Measurable, repeatable instructions designed for my space.
Format: One block per setup with the subheadings Setup, Settings, Composition, Story, Common mistake.
