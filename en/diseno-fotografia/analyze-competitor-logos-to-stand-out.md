Context: My business is [your business] in [industry and country or city], and these are my direct competitors: [competitors and their logos] (names, links, or screenshots).
Goal: Find the open visual territory in my category so my logo and identity aren't confused with the competition's.
Deliverable: 1) A comparison table for each competitor: logo type, symbol, typography, colors, the personality it conveys, and who it seems to speak to. 2) Industry patterns and clichés (what everyone does). 3) A visual positioning map on 2 axes (for example, classic-modern and approachable-premium) with each competitor's position described in text. 4) 3 differentiation opportunities, each with a concrete logo direction (shape, typography, and color) for my brand. 5) Which industry codes are worth keeping so people still recognize me as part of the category.
Criteria: If you can browse or search the web, review the competitors' current identities; if not, work only with what I share and say so. No proposal should resemble an existing logo.
Format: Tables and numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
