Context: My company, [your company name], offers [product or service]. Here's what I know about my current customers: [information about your customers] (sales data, surveys, reviews, or team feedback). I sell through [sales channels].
Goal: Identify the segments with the most potential and understand each well enough to tailor messages, offers, and design.
Deliverable: 3 customer profiles (buyer personas), each with: a descriptive name, relevant demographics, situation and goals, problems and frustrations, how they make the purchase decision and who influences it, typical objections, channels and content they consume, a message that would speak directly to them, and the visual style that would connect with them (colors, image types, tone). At the end, how to prioritize the 3 profiles and what data to collect to validate them.
Criteria: Base every trait on the information I gave you; label anything assumed as a hypothesis to validate. Avoid stereotypes.
Format: One profile card per persona with subheadings; the final comparison as a table (Persona, Estimated size, Value, Ease of reach, Priority).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
