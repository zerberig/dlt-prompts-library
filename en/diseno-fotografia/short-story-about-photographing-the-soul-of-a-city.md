Write a short story of about [approximate length] words about someone who walks through [city] with a camera, trying to capture the essence of the place. Tone: [story tone] (nostalgic, bright, mysterious, or humorous).
Criteria: Make the city feel like a character, with concrete sensory details: sounds, smells, the light at different hours, trades, and real or believable corners. Build the story around 3 or 4 photographs the protagonist takes, describe each image with visual precision, and let the last one reveal something about her. Avoid tourist postcards and stereotypes; favor everyday life. An open ending that still lands.
Format: A title, the story in prose, and at the end a list of the story's photographs described in one line each, in case I want to recreate them.
