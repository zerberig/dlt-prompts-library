Context: I need a business card for [your name and title] at [your company or brand]. It must include: [contact details]. My brand's style is [your brand style] (colors, fonts, or adjectives that describe it).
Goal: A card that reads in 3 seconds, reflects the brand, and prints without issues.
Deliverable: 3 distinct concepts (for example, minimalist, type-driven, and one with a graphic element). For each: front and back layout, suggested fonts with minimum sizes, palette in HEX and approximate CMYK, recommended paper and finish, and why it works for my brand. At the end, print specs (standard size for my country, bleed, safe margin, and resolution) and an image-generator prompt so I can visualize the favorite concept.
Criteria: Clear hierarchy: name first, then title and one primary contact channel; no more than 2 typefaces. If you include a QR code, say where it should lead.
Format: One block per concept with the subheadings Front, Back, Color and type, Finish.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
