Context: I want to do a photo shoot about [product or theme] for [how the photos will be used] (campaign, portfolio, social media, or catalog). I have [available resources] (budget, location, gear, and people).
Goal: A concept with its own narrative that makes the photos memorable and that the team can execute in one day.
Deliverable: 1) 3 one-sentence concepts: a realistic one, one that blends natural and artificial elements with a surreal touch, and a minimalist one; pick one and explain why. 2) For the chosen concept: the story the series tells, location or set, styling and props, palette, lighting setup, a list of 10 shots in production order, and an editing look (color, contrast, grain). 3) A schedule for the day. 4) A list of what needs to be sourced before the shoot.
Criteria: Everything doable with my resources; if something exceeds them, suggest a DIY alternative. Favor in-camera effects over post-production ones.
Format: Numbered sections; the shot list and schedule as tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
