Context: My business is [your business] and I sell to [target audience]. My brand is defined by [your brand traits], and my campaigns use [type of photography you use] (my own photos, professional shoots, or stock images).
Goal: Make images across all my campaigns recognizably mine and always pick the ones that connect best, even when different people are deciding.
Deliverable: 1) A visual signature of 5 to 7 recurring photographic elements (light, color, framing, type of people, settings, gestures) that set me apart. 2) A 10-point checklist to approve or reject an image, with one yes-or-no question per point. 3) A scoring table to compare finalist options. 4) How to adapt the choice by channel and funnel stage. 5) Red flags: generic photos, lack of representation, or unclear usage rights.
Criteria: Observable criteria, not personal taste, each with its business reason.
Format: Numbered lists and the scoring table with columns Criterion, Weight, Option A, Option B, Option C.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
