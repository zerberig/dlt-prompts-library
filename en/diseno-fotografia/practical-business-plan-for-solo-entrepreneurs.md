Context: I run my business solo, and my idea is [your business idea] in [industry or market]. I'm at the [business stage] stage (idea, first sales, or growth), and I need the plan to [what you'll use the plan for] (get organized, apply for a loan, or find partners).
Goal: A short, realistic business plan that helps me make decisions, not a document that sits in a drawer.
Deliverable: A plan with these sections, filling in what you can from my information and marking as "to be completed" anything I need to provide: 1) Executive summary. 2) Problem, solution, and value proposition. 3) Ideal customer and market size. 4) Competition and differentiation. 5) Revenue model and pricing. 6) Marketing and sales plan with priority channels. 7) Operations: what I'll do, automate, and delegate. 8) A simple 12-month financial projection (revenue, costs, and break-even) in [currency]. 9) Risks and a plan B. 10) 90-day goals.
Criteria: Plain language, designed for one person with limited time. If you have web search, use current market data. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source, and recommend reviewing the financial and tax sections with an accountant.
Format: Headings per section and the projection as a monthly table; 1,200 to 2,000 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
