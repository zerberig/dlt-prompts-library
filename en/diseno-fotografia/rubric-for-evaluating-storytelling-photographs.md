Context: I need to evaluate [what you're evaluating] (student work, contest entries, or campaign images) whose goal is [communication goal]. The evaluation will be done by [who's evaluating].
Goal: A consistent, fair way to judge how well a photo or series tells its story, beyond "I like it."
Deliverable: 1) A rubric with 6 criteria: message clarity, composition, use of light, moment and emotion, coherence of the series or sequence, and originality of viewpoint. For each criterion: a definition, 4 performance levels with observable descriptors, and a weight. 2) Guiding questions for a constructive spoken critique. 3) If it's for marketing, complementary effectiveness indicators (recall, engagement, and action). 4) A sample of written feedback using the rubric.
Criteria: Descriptors concrete enough that two evaluators would apply them the same way. Language that encourages improvement.
Format: The rubric as a table (Criterion, Weight, Excellent, Good, Developing, Beginning); everything else as lists.
