Context: Here's [ad or piece to analyze] (image, screenshot, or detailed description) by [brand or creator]. I'm analyzing it to [why you're analyzing it] (get inspired, compare it to my competitors, or use it in a design class).
Goal: Understand which visual decisions make the piece work or not, so I can learn from them and apply them to my own work.
Deliverable: 1) First read: what you see first, second, and third, and what message sticks after 3 seconds. 2) Element-by-element analysis: composition and hierarchy, color, typography, photography or illustration (light, framing, casting, gestures), the text-image relationship, and symbols or cultural references. 3) Which emotions it aims to trigger and with what devices. 4) Who it seems to target and what it assumes about that audience. 5) Strengths, weaknesses, and 3 lessons I can apply to my case without copying the piece.
Criteria: Tie every claim to something visible in the piece and separate observation from interpretation.
Format: Numbered sections with bullets, about 600 words.
