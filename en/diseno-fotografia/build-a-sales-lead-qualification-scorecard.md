Context: My company, [your company name], sells [product or service] to [ideal customer]. Our typical sales cycle is [sales cycle length and steps], and leads come from [lead sources].
Goal: Help the sales team decide consistently which leads deserve their time, using objective criteria instead of hunches.
Deliverable: 1) A scorecard with 8 to 12 criteria grouped into categories (profile fit, need, budget and authority, timing, brand engagement). For each criterion: description, how to get the data, a point scale with examples, and a percentage weight. 2) Total score ranges with the matching action (immediate follow-up, nurture, or disqualify). 3) 5 discovery questions to gather the data on the first call. 4) How to review and adjust the weights each quarter using real closed-deal data.
Criteria: Weights add up to 100%. It can be filled out in under 5 minutes per lead. Make clear that the score guides but doesn't replace the salesperson's judgment.
Format: Main table with columns Category, Criterion, How to get it, Scale, Weight; everything else as numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
