Context: I'm defining the visual aesthetic for [your business or project], aimed at [target audience]. I want it to feel [3 brand adjectives], and I'm inspired by references like [references you like] (places, eras, materials, or other brands, purely as inspiration).
Goal: A mood board that acts as a compass for designers, photographers, and me whenever we create anything.
Deliverable: 1) The mood board concept in one sentence. 2) A 5-6 color palette with HEX codes and each color's role. 3) Textures and materials. 4) Type direction: a style for headings and one for body text, with 2 free font options. 5) Photo style: light, framing, people, and objects. 6) 9 reference images for a 3x3 grid, each described and paired with a ready-to-use image-generator prompt. 7) What the brand is not: 5 visual traits to avoid.
Criteria: Every element must reinforce at least one of the 3 adjectives; say which. References are inspiration: describe techniques and atmospheres instead of copying other brands' designs.
Format: Numbered sections; the grid as a table (Position, Description, Prompt).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
