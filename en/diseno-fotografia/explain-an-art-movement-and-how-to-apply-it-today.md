Context: I want to understand [art movement] because I'll use it for [what you need it for] (a class, inspiration for a design project, or image prompts).
Deliverable: 1) A 2-3 sentence definition. 2) Historical context: when and where it emerged, and what it was reacting against. 3) Core philosophy. 4) 5 defining visual traits (composition, color, technique, and themes). 5) 3 representative artists and one key work by each, with why it matters. 6) Its influence on today's design, advertising, or visual culture. 7) How to apply its principles to my purpose by describing techniques and traits instead of copying specific works.
Criteria: Verifiable facts: if you have web search, confirm dates and works, and flag anything you can't confirm. Clear language for someone with no art history background.
Format: Numbered sections, about 500 words.
