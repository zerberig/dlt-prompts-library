Context: My brand is [brand name]. Here's what I already have. Logo: [logo description]. Colors: [brand colors]. Fonts: [current fonts]. The brand's personality is [brand personality].
Goal: A single-page guide that anyone (a designer, a vendor, or a teammate) can follow to keep the brand consistent.
Deliverable: Sections: 1) Brand essence in one sentence. 2) Logo usage: versions, clear space, minimum size, and 4 incorrect uses. 3) Palette: primary and secondary colors with HEX, RGB, and CMYK, and usage proportions. 4) Typography for headings, subheadings, and body text, with sizes and weights. 5) Image style (photography or illustration) with 3 "do" rules and 3 "don't" rules. 6) Tone of voice with 2 examples.
Criteria: Short, concrete rules. If any detail is missing, suggest a consistent option and label it as a suggestion. Check that text-and-background pairs have enough contrast.
Format: Structured text that fits on one page (450 words max), ready for layout.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
