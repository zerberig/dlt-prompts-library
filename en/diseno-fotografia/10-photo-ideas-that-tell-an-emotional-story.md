Give me 10 photo ideas about [theme] that convey emotion and tell a story in a single image. I can shoot in [location or available resources].
For each idea: a short title, the scene described in detail (who or what appears, what's happening, and where), the emotion it aims for, a light and framing suggestion, and the story viewers should imagine beyond the frame.
Criteria: Vary the emotions (not all melancholy), the scale (from detail to wide shot), and the technique. Tell me the most common cliché for this theme so I can avoid it. Every idea must be doable with my resources.
Format: A numbered list, 3-4 lines per idea.
