Context: We're launching [product name], which [what it does and for whom]. Our ideal customer is [ideal customer profile], and the goal for the first 6 months is [revenue or customer goal]. We have [marketing and sales resources].
Goal: A go-to-market strategy that focuses limited resources on the segments and channels most likely to succeed.
Deliverable: 1) The initial segment and why to start there. 2) Positioning: problem, the customer's current alternative, differentiator, core message, and 3 supporting messages. 3) A suggested pricing model with options to test. 4) Channel mix (content, direct sales, partnerships, community, ads) and each channel's role. 5) A 90-day plan in two-week blocks. 6) A funnel with metrics and conversion hypotheses. 7) Main risks and signals that it's time to change course.
Criteria: If you have web search, review competitors and reference pricing in my category. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source. Prioritize actions a small team can execute.
Format: Numbered sections; the 90-day plan and the funnel as tables.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
