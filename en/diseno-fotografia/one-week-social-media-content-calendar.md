Context: My brand is [your brand name], in the [industry] sector, and I talk to [target audience]. I post on [social networks], and this month I want [monthly goal] (more engagement, more site visits, or a launch).
Goal: A week of content ready to produce, balanced between educating, showing the brand from the inside, involving the community, and selling.
Deliverable: 1) 4 content pillars tailored to my brand. 2) A 7-day calendar; for each day: network, pillar, format (carousel, short video, story, or text post), a concrete idea, visual direction (what's on screen and photo or design style), the full caption with a call to action, and 3-5 relevant hashtags. 3) What to measure at the end of the week to plan the next one.
Criteria: Ideas I can produce with a phone and free design tools. Captions adapted to each network, not copied across them. Don't promise reach results.
Format: A table with columns Day, Network, Pillar, Format, Idea, Visual direction; below it, the full captions numbered by day.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
