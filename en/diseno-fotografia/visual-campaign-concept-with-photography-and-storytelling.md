Context: My business is [your business] and I want a campaign for [campaign goal] (a launch, a season, or a repositioning). My audience is [target audience] and the brand's values are [brand values].
Goal: A campaign with a strong central idea, expressed mainly through images, that makes people feel the brand's values without spelling them out.
Deliverable: 1) 3 big ideas in one sentence each, with a working tagline; pick the strongest and explain why. 2) For the chosen idea: the 3-act story the campaign tells, 4 key images written as photo direction (scene, people, light, framing, color), palette and visual treatment, and a short line of copy for each image. 3) How it adapts to 3 channels (for example, social media, point of sale, and email). 4) A production list: locations, models or real people, and props.
Criteria: Images of people who look like my audience in believable situations, with real diversity. Avoid stock-photo clichés.
Format: Sections with headings; key images as a table (Image, Scene, Photo direction, Copy).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
