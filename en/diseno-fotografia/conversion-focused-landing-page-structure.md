Context: I need a landing page for [product or offer] aimed at [target audience]. Its only goal is [page goal] (sign up, buy, or book a call), and traffic will come mainly from [traffic source].
Goal: A structure that takes visitors from doubt to action and that I can hand straight to design or a page builder.
Deliverable: 1) A text wireframe, section by section from top to bottom: purpose, elements (headline, subheadline, image, social proof, call to action), draft copy, and image type. 2) What changes on mobile. 3) 3 likely audience objections and which section answers each one. 4) 2 A/B test ideas with hypothesis and metric.
Criteria: What appears above the fold must match the traffic source. One primary action, repeated throughout the page. Social proof only from real data or testimonials I can supply; mark where they go.
Format: A numbered list of sections with subheadings; A/B tests as a table (Element, Variant, Hypothesis, Metric).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
