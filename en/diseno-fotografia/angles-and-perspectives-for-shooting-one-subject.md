I want to photograph [subject or place] in ways nobody expects. I have [your gear].
Give me 12 different angles and viewpoints, ordered from familiar to experimental: high angle, low angle, overhead, ground level, through a foreground object, reflection, frame within a frame, extreme distance, fragment or detail, and the point of view of someone or something in the scene, among others. For each: where to position myself, which focal length works best, how it changes the image's meaning or emotion, and a concrete idea applied to my subject.
At the end, suggest how to combine 4 of them into a small series that tells a story.
Format: A numbered list with 2-3 lines per angle.
