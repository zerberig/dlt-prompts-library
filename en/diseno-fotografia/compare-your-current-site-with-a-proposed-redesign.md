Context: My website is for [type of business], and its main goal is [site goal] (sales, leads, or bookings). Here are the current site and the proposed redesign: [screenshots or links for both versions].
Role: Review this the way a UX consultant would if they were paid to keep a redesign from lowering conversions.
Goal: Decide whether to approve the redesign, what to fix before launch, and what to measure afterward.
Deliverable: 1) A criteria-based comparison: visual hierarchy, message clarity in the first few seconds, navigation, calls to action, readability and typography, accessibility (contrast and sizes), mobile version, and trust signals. 2) The 3 biggest improvements and the 3 biggest risks of the redesign. 3) Recommended changes, ranked by impact and effort. 4) What to measure during the first month after launch.
Criteria: Tie every comment to something visible and explain why it affects the user. If something can't be seen (for example, the mobile version), say so instead of assuming.
Format: A comparison table with columns Criterion, Current site, Redesign, Verdict; then numbered lists.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
