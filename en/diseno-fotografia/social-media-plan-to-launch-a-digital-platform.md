Context: I'm launching [your platform's name], which [what it does and its main benefit]. My target audience is [target audience], and I'll be on [social networks] (for example, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, or X). My goal for the first 90 days is [launch goal] (sign-ups, free trials, or demos).
Goal: A plan that adapts the message to how people consume content on each network instead of posting the same thing everywhere.
Deliverable: For each network: 1) its role in the funnel, 2) 3 content pillars, 3) 5 concrete post ideas with format (carousel, short video, document, poll), hook, and call to action, 4) suggested posting frequency, and 5) 2 metrics to track. Then a 4-week launch calendar (before, launch day, and after) and 3 collaboration ideas with creators or communities.
Criteria: Messaging centered on the user's problem, not the feature list. If you have web search, check each network's current formats and best practices; don't present claims about the algorithm as facts. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: One section per network with bullets, the calendar as a table (Week, Network, Piece, Goal), and a final summary of priorities.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
