Context: I run a [type of business] and my website is [your site URL]. I manage it myself, my technical level is [your technical level], and the site is built on [site platform] (WordPress, Shopify, Wix, or other).
Goal: Find out what's holding back my site's search visibility, speed, and conversions, and in what order to fix it without hiring an agency.
Deliverable: An audit across 4 areas: technical SEO (indexing, speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile, security), content and local SEO (titles, meta descriptions, search intent, map business profile), user experience (navigation, forms, calls to action), and tracking (analytics and conversion setup). For each item: what to check, which free tool to use, what a healthy result looks like, and what to do if it fails.
Criteria: If you can browse or search the web, review the site and confirm which tools are current; if not, turn the audit into a checklist I can complete. Explain without jargon and tailor fixes to my platform. Flag as an estimate any figure you can't back up with a source.
Format: One table per area (Item, Tool, Healthy result, Action) and, at the end, a 30-day plan with the 10 priority actions ranked by impact and effort.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
