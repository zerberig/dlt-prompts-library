Context: I'm posting this image or video on Instagram: [post description]. I'm speaking to [target audience] and want a [desired tone] tone.
Deliverable: 5 caption options that differ from one another: a short one (1 line), one with a mini story, an educational one, one with a question for the community, and one with a call to [desired action].
Criteria: The first line must hook on its own, since it's all people see before expanding the caption. Natural language, no more than 2 emojis per option, and 3 to 5 specific hashtags at the end of each. No overblown promises.
Format: A numbered list with the style name before each option.
