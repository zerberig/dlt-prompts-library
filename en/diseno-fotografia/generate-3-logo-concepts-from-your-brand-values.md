Context: My business is called [business name], works in [industry or field], and serves [target audience]. Its core values are [brand values].
Goal: Have clear logo directions so I can pick one and develop it with a designer or with AI tools.
Deliverable: 3 clearly different logo concepts (for example, symbol plus wordmark, type-only, and monogram or emblem). For each: the core idea and which value it represents, a description of the symbol or type treatment, font style, a 2-3 color palette with HEX codes and their meaning, how it holds up at small sizes and in one color, and one risk or limitation.
Criteria: Simple, memorable designs that work from an app icon to a storefront sign. Point out the visual clichés in my industry so I can avoid them, and steer clear of any resemblance to well-known brands.
Format: One block per concept with subheadings and, at the end, which one you recommend developing first and why.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
