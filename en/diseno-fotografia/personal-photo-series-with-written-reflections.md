Context: I want to create a personal photo series about [theme or place] from my own perspective. My connection to the theme is [your connection to the theme].
Goal: A series of 8 to 12 photos paired with short texts that convey what I think and feel behind the camera, to publish as a photobook, exhibition, or long-form post.
Deliverable: 1) 3 possible approaches for the series and which you recommend given my connection to the theme. 2) A series plan: central idea, mix of candid and staged photos, and the image list with what each should show. 3) For each image, a reflection question that helps me write its text, without writing it for me. 4) A 60-80 word sample text for one photo, in first person, to show the tone. 5) Sequence order and how to open and close.
Criteria: The series should reveal something personal and specific, not a touristy or generic take on the theme.
Format: Numbered sections; the image list as a table (No., What it shows, Candid or staged, Reflection question).
