Context: I want to create a photo essay about [social cause or issue] in [community or place]. It will be published in [publication outlet] (exhibition, website, social media, or magazine).
Goal: Help the audience understand the issue through real people's stories and want to act, without images that re-victimize or exaggerate.
Deliverable: 1) A central idea and a working title. 2) A narrative arc of 10 to 15 photos split into opening, development, and closing. 3) A shot list stating, for each photo, what it shows, shot type, light and time of day, and the emotion it aims for. 4) A guide for writing captions with verifiable context. 5) Steps for getting informed consent and protecting vulnerable people.
Criteria: Prioritize the dignity and voice of the people photographed. Include at least 2 photos showing solutions or resilience, not just the problem, because that moves people to act. Use data only from sources I can cite.
Format: Numbered sections; the shot list as a table with columns Photo, What it shows, Shot, Light and time, Emotion.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
