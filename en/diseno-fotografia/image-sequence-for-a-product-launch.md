Context: I'm launching [your product] on [launch date] for [target audience]. What makes it special is [product differentiator].
Goal: A series of images that tells the launch story from start to finish and keeps anticipation high on social media and the website.
Deliverable: 8 images in story order: 2 teasers (details or silhouettes without revealing it), 1 reveal, 2 of the product in use in real situations, 1 close-up of materials or process, 1 of the people behind it, and 1 closing image with a call to action. For each image: what it contributes to the story, scene and photo direction (framing, light, background, props, people), format and aspect ratio for where it will be posted, a short line of copy, and an image-generator prompt in case I want to sketch it before the shoot.
Criteria: A consistent visual style across the series (same palette, light, and treatment). Show the product as it really is, without implying features it doesn't have.
Format: A table with columns No., Stage, Scene and direction, Format, Copy; the prompts below, numbered.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
