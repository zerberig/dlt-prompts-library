Context: I teach writing classes or workshops for [student level] and want a style imitation exercise based on [author or reference text]. Here's a 200 to 300 word excerpt: [text excerpt]. The session lasts [session length].
Goal: Help students understand how a style works from the inside by imitating it, so they can later use those tools in their own voice.
Deliverable: 1) Why this excerpt works for the exercise and which style traits to highlight. 2) An analysis guide for students: sentence structure and length, word choice, rhythm, punctuation, point of view, imagery, and rhetorical devices, with one concrete question per trait. 3) 3 writing prompts of increasing difficulty, from rewriting a scene to creating a new one on a different topic. 4) Reflection questions for afterward. 5) A short feedback rubric.
Criteria: Prompts that can be completed within the session. Include a note for students on the difference between imitating to practice and passing off someone else's text or style as their own.
Format: Numbered sections ready to hand out as a worksheet; the rubric as a table.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
