Context: My business, [business name], has this mission: [your business mission]. I want the logo to convey [key message] and evoke [emotion it should evoke] in anyone who sees it.
Goal: Explore logo directions with an image generator (Nano Banana, GPT Image, or Midjourney) before investing in the final design.
Deliverable: 1) 3 visual metaphors that link the mission to a symbol, each explained in one sentence. 2) 6 copy-ready prompts: 2 symbol marks, 2 wordmarks, and 2 emblems or monograms. Each one a natural-language paragraph describing shape, flat vector style, palette, typography if relevant, and "isolated on a plain white background," with the brand name in quotes. 3) A checklist for judging the results: legibility at small sizes, one-color performance, and originality. 4) Steps to turn the chosen concept into a vector file with a designer.
Criteria: Simple, scalable shapes with no resemblance to existing logos. Image generators struggle with long text, so use only the brand name.
Format: Numbered sections; each prompt in its own block.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
