Context: I shoot with [your gear], my level is [your level], and I have about [minutes per day] minutes a day to practice.
Goal: Break my visual habits and discover new ways to tell stories through creative constraints.
Deliverable: A 30-day challenge in 4 themed weeks: composition, perspective and point of view, light and camera settings, and narrative (sequences and diptychs). Each day: a one-line prompt with a concrete constraint (for example, "everything from one meter off the ground" or "the whole series at a single aperture"), the learning goal, and a question for reviewing the result. End each week with a review session: pick the 3 best photos, edit them with one consistent approach, and note what I learned.
Criteria: Prompts I can do at home or nearby, with no travel or extra gear, increasing in difficulty.
Format: One table per week with columns Day, Prompt, Goal, Review question.
