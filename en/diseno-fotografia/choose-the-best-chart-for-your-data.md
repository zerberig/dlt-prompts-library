Context: I have this data: [description of your data] (each field's name, data type, and what it represents, or a sample of rows). I want to answer this question: [question you want to answer]. I'll present it to [audience] as [presentation format] (report, dashboard, slide deck, or social post).
Goal: Pick the visualization that makes the answer obvious for that audience, not the flashiest one.
Deliverable: 1) A quick diagnosis: data types, number of variables, and the main relationship (comparison, trend, composition, distribution, or correlation). 2) The 3 best chart options in priority order, each with why it works, which variable goes on each axis or visual channel, and its limitations. 3) For the winning option: a title that states the finding, colors (one accent plus grays), labels, category order, and annotations. 4) 2 mistakes to avoid with this data.
Criteria: Prefer simple charts when they tell the story just as well. Keep it accessible: don't rely on color alone and use enough contrast. If the data can't answer the question, say so.
Format: Numbered sections; the option comparison as a table (Chart, Why, Variables, Limitations).
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
