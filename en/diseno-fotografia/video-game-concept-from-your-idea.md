Context: I have an idea for a [genre] video game with these elements: [keywords or ideas]. It would be for [platform] and for [target audience].
Goal: A clear, compelling concept I can use to pitch the idea to a team or start a prototype.
Deliverable: 1) 3 possible titles. 2) A one-sentence pitch. 3) World, story, and protagonist in 2 paragraphs. 4) Core gameplay loop: what the player does every minute, every session, and over the long run. 5) 3 core mechanics and what makes them distinct. 6) Art and sound direction described through techniques and atmosphere. 7) A business model suited to the platform. 8) Scope for a playable prototype in 4 weeks.
Criteria: Mechanics that reinforce the theme. Fair monetization, with no pay-to-win mechanics or loot boxes aimed at minors. An original concept: you may cite existing games only as mechanical references.
Format: Numbered sections with subheadings; 500 to 800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
