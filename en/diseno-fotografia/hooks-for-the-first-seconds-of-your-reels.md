Context: I'm posting a short Instagram video about [video topic] for [target audience]. I want viewers to [video goal] (watch to the end, save it, comment, or visit my profile).
Goal: Get people to stop scrolling in the first 2 seconds, without overblown promises that disappoint.
Deliverable: 10 hooks in different styles (question, common mistake, before and after, surprising fact, contradiction, personal story, list, challenge, behind the scenes, and "what nobody tells you"). For each: the spoken line (12 words max), the on-screen text (6 words max), and the video's first shot or visual action.
Criteria: Every hook must deliver on what the content promises. If you use a fact, make it verifiable or flag it for me to confirm. Point out your 3 favorites and why.
Format: A table with columns Style, Spoken line, On-screen text, First shot.
