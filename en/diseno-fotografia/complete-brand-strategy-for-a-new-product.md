Context: I'm building the brand for [your product], which solves [problem it solves] for [target audience]. My main competitors are [competitors].
Goal: A brand strategy that guides design, messaging, and experience so everything we do feels consistent from day one.
Deliverable: 1) Brand platform: purpose, vision, 3-5 values, and brand promise. 2) Positioning: a positioning statement and how we differ from the competitors. 3) Audience: primary segment, needs, and what they expect from the brand. 4) Personality and tone of voice, with examples. 5) Message architecture: core message and 3 supporting messages with proof points. 6) Visual identity direction: concept, colors, typography, and image style, each with its rationale. 7) Key touchpoints and how the brand should feel at each one. 8) Inclusion and diversity guidelines for language and imagery. 9) How to measure brand health.
Criteria: Connect every decision to the audience and the differentiation; avoid generic values any brand could claim. If you have web search, review how competitors communicate and flag anything that's an assumption.
Format: Numbered sections with headings; 1,200 to 1,800 words.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
