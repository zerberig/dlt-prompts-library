Context: I work in [your creative discipline] and I've been putting off [pending project] for a while. I have [time available per day] a day, and what holds me back most is [what usually stops you] (perfectionism, not knowing where to start, distractions, or fear of showing my work).
Goal: Rebuild a steady creative routine within a week and make visible progress on that project.
Deliverable: A 7-day plan. Each day includes: a theme, one key idea explained in 2-3 sentences (for example, why starting with a tiny task lowers resistance), 1-2 concrete tasks applied to my project, a short exercise, and how I'll know I completed the day. At the end, a minimum routine to keep after the week and what to do if I miss a day.
Criteria: Each day fits my available time. Strategies grounded in behavioral psychology, explained without jargon or guilt. If the procrastination seems tied to anxiety, low mood, or ongoing burnout, mention it gently and recommend talking to a mental health professional.
Format: One block per day with subheadings; short, direct text.
If any essential information is missing, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
